# Fearless
> Type: class | Also called: [Fearless Warrior]; [Fearless Mage] | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.02C
> See also: Demons, Tom, Alternate Tom
## Overview
- The Fearless are a type of Demon who carry a class that is more an addition to their normal class, such as [Fearless Warrior] or [Fearless Mage], and the class means they cannot feel fear at all. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L237)
- The Fearless are used as shock troopers: each is a dangerous fighter who will charge through killing grounds and is willing to die to take you with him, and the Demon King uses them when he wants to win. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L247)
- A captured Fearless sits unmoving in a cage while a crowd throws stones at him, and his body is scarred from earlier stonings. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L236,L238)
## Against Tom's alternate
- {event} The Fearless feel unease and disturbance, though not fear, when the laughing [Clown] strolls towards them, and they loose arrows and magic at him as he vanishes and reappears. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L234-236)
- {event} The Fearless break and run from an enemy they cannot kill, because without fear they also cannot understand it. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L236-237)
