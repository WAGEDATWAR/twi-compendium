# Runners
> Type: class | Also called: Messengers; Travelers; Sealers; Carriers; [Runner] | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.01R
> See also: Messenger Seals, Healing potions
## Overview
- Runners, also called Messengers, are people who deliver letters and goods for pay. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L87; Book 1, Ch1.20 L160)
- Other names for Runners include Travelers, Sealers (after the Seals they use) and Carriers, plus a host of rude words. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L87)
- Krshia says Runners are quick people who run long distances to earn money, delivering letters, valuable goods, magical items and messages meant to be spoken to one person only. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L160)
- Krshia says the expensive requests such as magical items or spoken messages go only to the best Runners, while most simply deliver goods cheaply. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L160)
## Ranks and guilds
- Ryoka Griffin, a City Runner herself, divides Runners into three rough categories: Street Runners, City Runners and Couriers. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L110,L112)
- According to Ryoka, Street Runners stay within one city, take the safest and easiest jobs for the least money, and rank low in the Runner's Guild. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L111)
- According to Ryoka, City Runners run from city to city in a general area, take more dangerous jobs, and mainly run away from trouble rather than fight it. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L112)
- Ryoka writes that Couriers are long-distance Runners who can cross the continent or even the sea, are quick and efficient, and cost incredible amounts of money. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L114)
- Ryoka reports that Garia told her each Courier is at least Level 30 and has levels in other classes as well, so they are not easy to ambush. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L115)
- Ryoka also observes that each Runner's Guild is a mess of politics, friendships and infighting over territory. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L113)
## Life and work
- Fals tells Garia that there are few experienced Runners because no one who does the job lives very long, and most people change classes after earning enough. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L88)
- A Runner tells Ryoka that most City Runners in the region make short runs between Celers, Remendia, Ocres, Celum and Esthelm, almost never to Liscor, while the best Runners travel across the continent. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L167-168)
- Val tells Ryoka that Runners level up based on how far they have run and how challenging the run is, and doubts she would reach Level 10 by the end of the year. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L153-155)
- A Runner's reputation for honesty matters as much as speed in winning contracts, because messengers who take goods and give false Seals appear every year. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L85)
