# Goblin [Shaman]
> Type: class | Also called: Goblin Shaman; Goblin Shamans | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.29
> See also: Goblin Lord, Toren, Hobgoblin
## Overview
- Goblin [Shamans] serve in the Goblin Lord's army, and one blasts the Stone Spears tribe's Gnoll [Shaman] apart with magic, after which the dead Gnoll's body rises. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L384)
- The Goblin Lord's army includes Goblin [Necromancers] and [Shamans] who chant to move undead. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L171)
## The [Shaman] who ordered Toren
- {event} An old Goblin [Shaman] with a twisted staff of ivory bones and darker crimson eyes speaks a word in the Goblin tongue that commands Toren to come to him. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L293-295)
- Toren finds the [Shaman]'s spoken words weaker than Erin's orders, does not struggle against the command, and marches straight up to him. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L297,L300)
- {event} Toren runs the [Shaman] through with his sword while the [Shaman] keeps trying to recast the spell. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L304-307)
