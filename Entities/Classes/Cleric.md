# [Cleric]
> Type: class | Also called: [Clerics] | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.04R
> See also: Healing potions, Tier Magic
## Overview
- A mage tells Ryoka Griffin that she needs a high-level [Healer], or better yet a [Cleric], to heal her leg, since [Healers] use only herbs and minor spells. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L217,L221)
- Ceria says treatment of that kind would cost at least a few hundred gold coins, and high-level healers are very rare. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L224)
- Pisces says that once upon a time a [Cleric] in their earliest levels could do what an [Archmage] could not, because faith warped the bounds of reality. (Book 1, Ch1.34 L107-109)
- Pisces says the gods are dead and so are the ancient ways of faith and miracle. (Book 1, Ch1.34 L111)
- Pisces says [Clerics] are now simply [Healers] who are able to cast spells, the definition of the class having changed along with the skills and spells learnt. (Book 1, Ch1.34 L114)
