# Gravetender's Fist
> Type: org | Also called: Quallet's company | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.02D
> See also: Quallet Marshhand, United Nations company, Ken
## Overview
- Gravetender's Fist is a suppression company: a night-duty force of "corpse battlers" that does not fight veterans. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L40)
- The company's work is to clean a battlefield of corpses after each day of combat so that undead do not spawn. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L83)
## Book 5
- Quallet offers a silver piece for every night spent fighting and four copper for nights off, and supplies swords, armor and healing potions to those without. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L48)
- {belief} Quallet claims that eight out of every ten raw recruits survive their first month and promises first ten combat levels. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L45)
- {event} The company arrives at the battlefield, a wide valley where two armies meet from hilltop camps, and moves out for its first night duty. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L318,L390)
- The company's job is to find the dead, pile them and burn them so undead do not spawn, killing any already risen, then leave before fighting resumes. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L90,L103)
- On Ken's third night, the work is better than the first because the shock is gone, though the fear remains. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L91-92)
## Book 10
- Ken and others were part of Quallet's company before leaving it. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L83)
- {state} The revitalised Gravetender's Fist is still burgeoning but has earned enough for a holiday in town. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L526)
- The company is still small but gaining respect under Quallet Marshhand, and every soldier is armed with a crossbow they usually fire before switching to other weapons. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L726)
- Quallet and Quexa, the [Mercenary Captain] who is his second-in-command, meet Daly just outside Talenqual. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L731)
