# Adventurer's Guild
> Type: org | Also called: Adventurers' Guild; adventurer guilds; Guild | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.06R
> See also: Liscor Adventurer's Guild, Adventurers, Celum Adventurer's Guild
## Overview
- {belief} A speaker told Ryoka that the Adventurer's Guild offers a copper piece for every four pairs of Goblin ears. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L152)
- The Adventurer's Guild lets people report dangerous monsters in their area, post quests and offer rewards, while adventurers look at assignments on a large wooden board and collect their reward there. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L16-17)
- The Liscor branch is a large hall that Erin first mistook for an inn or a bar. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L15)
- The Liscor branch's patrons include grizzled Drake warriors, inhumanly tall Gnolls and several Antinium, with a few robed Drake and Antinium mages carrying staves or daggers. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L3,L19-20)
- Most Drake and Gnoll adventurers wear armour of varying quality, and one Gnoll wears real plate armour. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L19)
## Liscor branch
- {belief} Relc said the Liscor Adventurer's Guild does not have many members, because few locals bother becoming adventurers when there is little to do around the city and fighters join the army or the Watch. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L245)
- The guildmaster of the Liscor Adventurer's Guild is an elderly Drake with dusky purple scales, who is also Selys's grandmother. (Book 1, Ch1.45 L105)
- {event} When Erin asked for help for the adventurers trapped in the Ruins, Selys said no one in the Guild would go in after what had come out, and a Drake adventurer replied that the trapped should have saved themselves. (Book 2, Ch2.03 L179,L185-188)
- Selys offered Erin four silver for each Corusdeer antler and said the Adventurer's Guild sells them to [Alchemists] and [Blacksmiths]. (Book 2, Ch2.35 L448)
## Rules, fees and bounties
- The Adventurer's Guild demands a ten percent cut of the spoils from a dungeon raid or a particularly lucrative assignment; the Horns of Hammerad were surprised by it and paid rather than report the artifacts' value. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L44-45)
- The Guild also takes a chunk out of every assignment it offers and earns money from referrals and its own products, but membership is free. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L44-45)
- Adventurers are subject to the Guild's authority, and rules vary between Guilds, though a few are common to all of them. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L187)
- According to Selys, any city with an Adventurer's Guild can demand adventurers' help in its defence in a crisis; an adventurer who refuses without good reason can lose rank or be expelled, and the city must compensate them. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L188)
- Selys said a city collects a fee on artifacts recovered within its jurisdiction, and the Adventurer's Guild does as well. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L190)
- Selys said the Guild pays bounties on monsters and parts, including unbroken Shield Spider shells, Raskghar, Lurkersnatch Fish, enchanted armour, slime cores, Dropbat teeth and Corusdeer horns, and that the city had put a bounty on the Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L195-197)
- Ceria says the Adventurer's Guild forbids using people's bones for undead, and Yvlon recalls Selys giving a list of species whose bones may not be used. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L352,L357)
