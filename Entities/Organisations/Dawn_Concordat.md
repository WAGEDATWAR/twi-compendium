# Dawn Concordat
> Type: org | Also called: the traditional bulwark of Calanfer, Kaliv and Gaiil-Drome | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.08K
> See also: Bloodtear Pirates, Five Families
## Overview
- The Dawn Concordat is the traditional bulwark of Calanfer, Kaliv and Gaiil-Drome in Terandria, known for having broken many nations sent against it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L17)
- {belief} A predictor expected Taimaguros, which has a defensive alliance with Ailendamus, to side with Ailendamus, so that two major powers and their allies would fight the traditional Dawn Concordat plus Pheislant. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L4)
- Ailendamus had swelled in recent years and its ruler, King Itorin II, was looking for easy victories. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L17)
## Conflict with Ailendamus
- Ailendamus warships clashed twice with Pheislant vessels and blockaded trade to the Dawn Concordat, intercepting goods bound there. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L18)
- Wistram's Council argued three days over the blockade and did nothing, and Feor did not rank the Dawn Concordat's fate highly, thinking he could interfere when the war began. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L31)
- A general planned to land at Nadel with Lord Belchaus's permission and flank the Dawn Concordat when the war began in six days. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L81)
- The Elusive Lot was on the side of the Dawn Concordat. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L206)
- Anyone watching continental politics knew the Ailendamus war threatened the Dawn Concordat of three kingdoms. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L61)
- Ailendamus had swallowed other nations' territory and won several wars in the two decades since King Itorin II came to power, and was set to roll over the Dawn Concordat unless Pheislant, Noelictus or another nation helped. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L63)
- Lord Tyrion judged that the Dawn Concordat's best outcome was a defensive victory with significant casualties and loss of territory. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L66)
- Calanfer, the most political of the three members, and Ailendamus each asked Tyrion Veltras for aid when war began. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L67-68)
- {event} Ailendamus declared war on the Dawn Concordat as its armies crossed the border of Kaliv and its ships began blockading nations such as Pheislant. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L135)
- Abebi took her band north to avoid the war between Ailendamus and the Dawn Concordat, since Pheislant could be drawn in. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L788)
