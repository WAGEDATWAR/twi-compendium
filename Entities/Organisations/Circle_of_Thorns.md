# Circle of Thorns
> Type: org | Also called: the Circle; Thorns | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.41
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Assassin's Guild, Tyrion Veltras, Ieka Imarris, Rie Valerund, Coven, Belavierr
## Overview
- Magnolia wonders whether an attack on her mansion is the Circle of Thorns again, and says that the Circle's nightmare took her great grandmother's whole life to sort out. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L277)
- A robed mage who blows out a wall tells Magnolia Reinhart that the Circle of Thorns seeks her life. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L599)
- Regis, the Reinhart ancestor who guards the family armory, tells Magnolia that the Circle of Thorns haunted her grandmother and that she died with their curse still over the family. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L162)
- Eloise says the Circle of Thorns may endanger [Witches] again, and that [Witches] are fewer and weaker than in the past. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L434)
## Origin and nature
- The Circle of Thorns is as old as the Five Families and began as a reaction to their power. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L6)
- Few members of the Five Families join the Circle, yet individuals are invited, and it has been known to move on behalf of the Five Families. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L7)
- The Circle is made of the 'lesser' nobility, meaning anyone not of the original Five Families, and Lady Ieka understands it as meant to censure those with too much power. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L8)
- In the past the Circle was more public, a symbol of opposition and even a political group, and only in the last few hundred years has it become more secretive. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L14)
- Ieka recalls that an invitation to the Circle six years earlier was to a small social group of nobles that reminded the Five Families they were not unopposed, but the new Circle is different and more like the old times. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L40-41)
- Most nobles get an invitation from someone connected to the Circle, and being publicly known as a member is dangerous. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L4-5)
- The Circle asks only loyalty from members and does not care what they do in private so long as it is not public. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L66)
- Lady Ieka says the Circle is meant to defend Izril from within as much as from outside, but in practice moves out of self-interest, and she thinks it rivals one of the Five Families in scope. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L72,L81)
- Tyrion Veltras knows the Circle as an old group that most nobles know of, and had assumed it extinct. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L116)
## Its history
- {belief} Lady Rie says the Reinharts drove the Circle into hiding, that Lady Suvelta Reinhart battled it and lost, and that later Reinharts also fought and lost. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L24)
- {belief} Lady Rie says the Circle killed Lady Suvelta Reinhart, a Vampire, and Ieka recalls that she died screaming in sunlight after sixteen days and nights of torture. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L35-37)
- Ieka knows that past Circles fought off invasions from Chandrar and Baleros, humbled Khelt when it invaded, and threw back the Six Great Companies of Baleros two thousand years back. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L38)
## Ranks and councils
- Lady Rie Valerund is higher in rank than Lady Ieka Imarris in the Circle, though both are members. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L21)
- The inner council of the Circle is called the Petals of Death, and a ranking agent group is the Roots of Contempt, of which Rie is a part. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L42-43,L81)
- Rie is second only to the Petals of Death as one of their agents, and Ieka is a member of the Thorns of Contempt, two ranks up from a newly introduced member. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L46)
- Lady Rie uses a glass rose called the Thorn of Speaking, activated with blood, to contact the Petals of Death by voice alone. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L41,L47-49)
- The voices of the Petals of Death are scrambled to be flat, and only six or seven of them speak. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L51,L54)
- Membership of the Petals of Death depends on loyalty and commitment, not on power. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L57)
- An [Assassin] says the Circle has many ranks and offers Tyrion the title First of Thorns as commander of the Circle's assets. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L130)
## Role in Book 11
- Magnolia believes the Circle of Thorns sent the assassins who attacked the Ladies, while those who sent black roses are openly expressing contempt, and that at least one noble in Tyrion Veltras's camp is in the Circle. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L729,L735)
- Magnolia says she does not have the Assassin's Guild at her back and that the Circle of Thorns is one thing, but she does not want a political war. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L408)
- {event} Lord Erill, a [Merchant Lord] who had backed Tyrion, leaves a bag of holding with two hundred thousand gold pieces' worth of goods with Lady Ieka as a donation, wanting no part of her organization, and Ieka says the Circle of Thorns will remember this. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L628-636,L640-643)
## The Marshlands Coven and the witches
- {belief} Mavika says the people who killed the Marshlands Coven called themselves the Circle of Thorns, a secret name the witches had heard before. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L328-329,L346-347)
- Mavika says the killers demanded a curse upon Magnolia Reinhart and her kin, so powerful that she would die screaming and boiled by her own skin. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L349)
- Califor thinks those who slaughtered the Marshlands Coven died painfully, after Mavika repaid blood with blood. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L346-347)
- Mavika says she remembers the Circle once, two generations ago, when it slew members of the Five Families. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L417)
- {belief} Belavierr says she remembers the Circle of Thorns thrice, that it comes again and again, and that twice it culled more than its enemies, with witches dying and war coming to Izril from north and south. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L425)
- Belavierr says the war lasted thirty-four years and killed three thousand, three hundred and twelve Witches. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L427-429)
## Efforts against Magnolia and Tyrion
- Lady Rie is told by the Petals of Death that Magnolia has betrayed the North by going to the Drakes and must be removed. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L52)
- The Petals decide, five votes to two, to approach Tyrion Veltras to kill Reinhart, and say that if he refuses they may raise one of their own. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L91-92)
- The Petals ask Rie whether Emperor Godart is an ally and order her to make him an offer, or settle it herself if he refuses. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L97-100)
- {event} A [Messenger] of the Circle approaches Tyrion Veltras with an invitation, and Tyrion calls him an [Assassin]. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L98-99,L115)
- {reveal} The [Assassin] is blond and sable-eyed, and he says the Assassin's Guild belongs to the Circle and offers Tyrion the title First of Thorns. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L110,L125-130)
- {belief} The [Assassin] claims that the Circle backed Tyrion's call for aid and that so many flocked to his banner because the Circle believed in his cause. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L122)
- {belief} The [Assassin] says Magnolia is one of Izril's greatest traitors who will suffer today for her transgressions. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L138)
- {event} The Circle decides to kill Magnolia Reinhart, and countless nobles are told to watch the attack. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L190-192)
- {event} After Tyrion saves Magnolia, the Circle meets in fury at his act and swears he will be brought to heel and made to swear himself to the Circle as a servant and not an ally. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L431-441)
- The Circle has failed to kill Magnolia and considers its dealings with Tyrion its one mistake. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L441)
## Revenge on House Veltras
- {event} A masked [Assassin] tells Tyrion that the Circle of Thorns is displeased and later offers an exchange for his sons' lives and his family's safety for his cooperation. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L141,L170)
- The Circle has killed over two dozen local [Healers] and [Alchemists] in Veltras's region and delivered their heads to House Veltras's door. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L4-6)
- The contract the [Assassin] offered Tyrion is a fully binding magical contract with few avenues for escape. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L7)
- A volley of spells hits Tyrion's keep and city as a warning, and a masked [Assassin] says if he leaves his estates she cannot guarantee his sons will be there when he returns. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L12-14)
- Magnolia is on the Reinhart flagship, chased from her home, and people no longer take the Circle of Thorns as lightly as before. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L59)
