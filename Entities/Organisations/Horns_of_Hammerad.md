# Horns of Hammerad
> Type: org | Also called: Horns; the Horns | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.00H
> See also: Adventurer's Guild, Griffon Hunt, Liscor Adventurer's Guild
## Overview
- The Horns of Hammerad were formed two years before the Liscor expedition; Ceria, Gerial, Calruz, Sostrom and Hunt are five members who had worked up through the ranks for years and would never quit. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L11)
- All ten members of the Horns of Hammerad were sitting in the room together in one early scene, with Calruz and Gerial discussing the plan. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L4)
- Ryoka described the Horns as a twelve-member party, most above level 20, led by the Minotaur [Fighter] Calruz with an enormous battleaxe. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L4,L8)
- The Horns received official permission to search a set of ruins for a week, during which other adventuring groups could not interfere or look for treasure. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L6)
- Gerial, a tall mustached human warrior, is Calruz's second-in-command. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L34; Book 1, Ch1.04R L120,L260)
- Gerial said the Horns specialise in monster slaying as a close-combat group, closing in to smash the enemy while their mages provide backup. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L204)
- Gerial described the Horns' code as not breaking promises and not retreating, and repaying everything taken in advance if a job is abandoned; he murmured "Death before dishonor." (Book 1, Ch1.00H L221-223)
- Gerial said the Horns were a Silver-rank team and not the only one in the north, and that coastal cities had Gold-rank teams they could hardly compare with. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L201)
## Original party
- {event} A week before meeting Ryoka in an inn, the Horns were pinned down by a Lich at the Ruins; Ryoka ran past it and dropped off potions in the middle of the battlefield, drawing its fire, and the Horns killed the Lich and recovered many artifacts. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L133-139)
- Ceria wears a set of dark blue robes with golden sigils that she owes to that delivery, and liquid runs off the cloth. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L133-135)
- The Horns moved fast, hiring a carriage and horses within an hour of Ceria receiving a reply from a necromancer she knew, and paid for Ryoka's healing potions and the carriage. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L16,L62)
- Calruz said the Horns were not yet Gold-ranked, and that they would not explore Liscor's new ruins without a full fighting party. (Book 1, Ch1.29 L73,L80)
- {event} With Calruz away and the Horns understrength and without contracts, Ceria and Gerial lent Ryoka healing potions and dry rations, and offered to guard her belongings at the Adventurer's Guild. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L214-224)
- A speaker told Ryoka that the Horns were all out of the city, gathered by Calruz and heading for Esthelm to prepare to enter the ruins in Liscor. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L48)
- {event} The Horns arrived at Erin's inn at night, attacked her Goblin guests, and agreed to stay only after Erin stopped them; Calruz promised on his honour not to attack her guests unless provoked. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L339-356,L409-415)
- The Horns said they needed a place to stay because every tavern, inn and home in Liscor was full of adventurers. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L393)
- The Horns paid in advance, and handsomely, for their first night at Erin's inn. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L205)
- Before the dungeon descent Ceria handed out rations, water, four healing and four mana potions each, and explained that too many healing potions make the body quit. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L478-482)
## Reformation
- {state} After the others died, Ceria, Yvlon, Pisces and Ksmvr joined hands at Erin's inn, and Ceria declared that the Horns of Hammerad live again. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L421-437)
- Ceria said she was in the Horns for years and had formed it with Gerial and Calruz, that they were dead, and that all fought to the end except one who ran because her friends sacrificed themselves for her. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L421-423)
- Pisces noted that the name came from the Minotaur, and Ceria kept it. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L425-427)
- The new Horns, Ceria, Pisces, Yvlon Byres and Ksmvr (a former Prognugator), were on their eighth night, hungry, when Ceria recalled that Calruz had kept his old group well fed and outfitted. (Book 3, Ch3.02H L5-7,L14-15)
- The new Horns had one gold coin left, having spent nearly all they had to get food quickly. (Book 3, Ch3.02H L61-65)
- {event} In a dungeon the new team was teleported into a pit and Ceria looked at a flickering magical rune on the wall, after which she and Ksmvr began to misidentify one another. (Book 3, Ch3.02H L558-573)
- {event} They left the dungeon alive with a burned spellbook, damaged artifacts and nearly eighty pounds of gold and fused gemstones, though they were not yet a proper Silver-rank team. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L211,L217)
- {event} In Remendia a man took the Horns to be dead, and a crowd cheered the party's name when they left their inn. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L317-318,L365-371)
- The crowd in Remendia saw the Horns off with fanfare, but they were broke enough that they guarded a wagon in exchange for transport. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L2,L35)
- The Adventurer's Guild's ten percent cut of their spoils left the Horns broke again. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L44-46)
- Ceria, Pisces, Yvlon and Ksmvr guarded Termin's wagon, drawn by the horses Erma and Fox, and Ceria told Gold-rank teams they were a Silver-rank team. (Book 4, Ch3.30 L82,L93; Book 4, Ch3.33 L170)
- Yvlon told the Horns that she used to get sweets at the winter solstice growing up. (Book 4, Ch3.39 L375)
## Silver rank at Liscor
- Newly equipped with items from a haul, the Horns of Hammerad approached the next tier of adventuring, and Ulrien suggested the Guild might recertify them as Gold-rank. (Book 5, Ch4.10 L365,L369-373)
- {event} Ceria, Pisces, Yvlon and Ksmvr agreed to take contracts in Esthelm, which Yvlon said was close by and likely short of adventurers. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L470-474)
- {event} In a bear cave the Horns found Crelers eating the third bear; Ksmvr named them, and Yvlon's arm was broken in the fight. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L342-346,L386)
- Because the Adventurer's Guild banned Pisces from using people's corpses and said nothing of bears, Ceria and the others pulled a sled of undead bears into Liscor. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L529,L537)
- The Horns took a contract to hunt a large Shield Spider nest in Liscor's sewers. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L95)
- {event} After a cave of cockroaches, Yvlon said she had swallowed some, and Ceria's poisoned snow spell only agitated the insects. (Book 6, Ch4.37O L406-409)
- In a quiet period at Erin's inn the Horns kept busy with small hunts and extermination of small monster nests. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L31-33)
- {event} The Horns were hired to provide meat for Esthelm's low food supplies and were away four to five days, returning on a wagon pulled by undead bears. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L130,L268-269)
- {event} Erin hugged Ceria and the others goodbye, and the Horns left by boat toward the dungeon rift. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L232-236,L264)

## Gold rank and travels
- {state} Guildmistress Tekshia promoted the Horns to Gold-rank, since no Silver-rank team can slay an Adult Creler. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L194-196)
- The author's note says the Horns have always been among the author's favourite characters to write. (Book 14, ChMeta_B14_Authors_Note L5)
- The Horns took one of Erin's door-portals north to Invrisil about a week and a half before the scene in which Fals asks after them. (Book 15, Ch7.00 L383-385)
- {event} The Horns killed a Stone Golem infestation in a village's mines on the road north and planned to reach Invrisil, then The Wandering Inn, then Ryoka. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L5-8,L57)
- {event} The Horns arrived at Invrisil with a magic door on a wagon, and then burned the wagon for firewood, since the door would not open unless Erin set its stone. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L929-955; Book 17, Ch7.20 L9-10,L59)
- Ksmvr hung tripwire bells around the Horns' camp on the road north. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L129)
- {event} Gold-rank Ceria, Pisces, Yvlon and Ksmvr joined a Face-Eater Moth raid in a chariot drawn by undead horses. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L792-795)
