# Celestial Trackers
> Type: org | Also called: Wiskeria's adventurer team | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.35
> See also: Adventurers, Adventurer's Guild
## Overview
- A speaker advertised that hiring the Celestial Trackers would find a quarry or the coin back. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L213)
- Odveig, a [Macewoman], calls herself the leader of the Celestial Trackers, while Wiskeria, a [Witch], is her subordinate and in Laken's view the brains of the group. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L86)
- The Celestial Trackers are experienced at ambushes, tracking prey and finding hidden monsters, the opposites of the Windfrozen Riders in many ways. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L30)
- Wiskeria is the only truly specialised spellcaster in either group. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L31)
## At Riverfarm
- {event} The Celestial Trackers found the Mossbear's cave, and the bear woke up and attacked after an avalanche. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L331)
- Laken hired the Celestial Trackers and Windfrozen Riders through the Merchant's Guild. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L85)
- {event} Wiskeria tended Beniar's poisoned arrow wound while the Trackers patrolled with the Riders against Goblin harassing attacks. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L12,L141)
- {belief} Laken suspected Wiskeria of snooping in his cottage, and when he shouted that Odveig was the spy, Odveig ran. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L296,L348-350,L382)
- {event} In the Goblin assault, Wiskeria threw glowing blue fire and led the Trackers forward; one Tracker lost a hand to a Hob. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L240,L246)
- {state} Four of twelve Celestial Trackers survived the battle, and Wiskeria said she could not go back to Invrisil. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L369-372)
