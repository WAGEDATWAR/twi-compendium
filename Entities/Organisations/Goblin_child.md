# Goblin child
> Type: group | Also called: Goblin children | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.15
> See also: Law of Liscor, Liscor guards
## Overview
- Lady Bethal Walchaís had never seen a Goblin child before meeting a toddler-sized Goblin girl in Book 8. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L65-66)
## Book 1: the child at the grave
- While Erin Solstice buries slain Goblins, a small ragged Goblin in a grey stained loincloth and rags, holding a small knife, rushes her from the long grass. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L67-69)
- Erin slaps the Goblin to the ground and kicks it in the stomach, realising it is a child with murder in its eyes. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L71-72,L75-76)
- Erin sees tears running from the Goblin's red eyes, tells it she does not want to kill it, and weighs killing it for the level; she buries the last head instead and the Goblin is gone when she turns. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L73-74,L77-79)
## Book 8: the weeping girl
- Lady Bethal sees a tiny, lost-looking Goblin girl no bigger than a toddler wandering among the Goblin dead, shouting a single word and crying as she searches the bodies. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L65-67)
- {reveal} Bethal learns that Goblins can weep tears just like any other species. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L68-69)
- When Bethal asks why only the child is crying, a Hob answers that tears are a waste of water. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L155-157)
- The girl clings to a body until the Hob pulls her away; she wails once, beats him, and bites his hand as he carries her off without looking back. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L161-164)
## Book 13
- A narrator in Book 13 says a Goblin child could learn to control an iPhone with motion commands in thirty minutes, even using a feature, shake-to-shuffle, that its owner Laken Godart never used. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L658)
