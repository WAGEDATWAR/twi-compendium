# Runner's Guild Celum
> Type: org | Also called: Runner's Guild of Celum; Runner guild of Celum | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.04
> See also: Runners, Ryoka Griffin, Bloodfeast Raiders
## Overview
- The Runner's Guild in Celum has still not forgotten Ryoka's incident with the Frost Faeries, and its [Receptionist] frowns when Erin mentions Ryoka. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L169)
- The Celum guild's Runners are out on deliveries in other cities, and the [Receptionist] offers to ask Fals and Garia to call on Erin. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L168-170)
## After the raid
- {event} The Runner's Guild stands intact but ransacked after the Bloodfeast Raiders walked in, took all the packages, and left; that is the only reason it still stands. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L295-296)
- The guild shelters many homeless people while the woman at the desk writes letters asking for [Healers] and builders. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L296-297)
- The [Receptionist] sends a Street Runner with a sealed request for help to every village, starting with Cimin. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L298-299)
- The Raiders took some of the guild's people, and a list is being made of the missing. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L323-324)
