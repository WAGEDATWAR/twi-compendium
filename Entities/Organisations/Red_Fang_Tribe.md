# Red Fang Tribe
> Type: org | Also called: Redfang tribe; Redfangs | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00G
> See also: Flooded Waters tribe, Goblin tribe
## Overview
- Rags judged the Red Fang tribe the strongest tribe by pure power, spread around the entrance to the High Passes with six Hobs, dominating the other grassland tribes. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L161)
- Rags thought the Red Fang Tribe numbered nearly a thousand, not counting smaller tribes that paid it tribute. (Book 2, Ch2.01G L286)
## Conflict with Rags
- {event} Sixty Goblins on Carn Wolves, led by a Hob with a red sword, scouted Rags's tribe, with the Red Fang Tribe's main host of over a thousand Goblins, perhaps many thousands, marching after them according to a scout. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L67,L117)
- {state} The Red Fang Chieftain submitted to Rags, telling both sides to stop fighting because the Flooded Waters tribe was strongest, and Rags became Chieftain over the whole Red Fang tribe. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L281-284,L302)
- On meeting them, Rags counted about three hundred Goblins, nearly forty wolves and six Hobs with good armour and weapons. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L62)
- The Red Fang tribe's non-warriors were thin and scrawny and the tribe did not seem to carry much food. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L63-64)
- Garen Redfang took the biggest tribe around by challenging its Chieftain, a Hobgoblin who fled, and as Rags had observed he was a terrible Chieftain, so the tribe did not expand and instead established local superiority, rivalled only by the Broken Spear and Ghostly Hand Tribes to the south. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L115-117)
- {event} Garen Redfang secretly sent thirteen Goblins, twelve and a Hobgoblin, each over Level 10 in a combat class, to kill the [Innkeeper] without telling the Chieftain. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L71-73)
