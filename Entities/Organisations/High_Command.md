# High Command
> Type: org | Also called: Liscor's High Command | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.13
> See also: Liscor's army, Zevara, Embria Grasstongue
## Overview
- {belief} Relc says High Command has it wrong about how Liscor was saved, since the Antinium saved the city; his daughter answers that they did it to trick the Drakes. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L672-673)
- {event} High Command, through Wing Commander Embria, orders Zevara to release Wistram's [Mages] and return their belongings. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L2-5,L16-19,L81)
- High Command tells Zevara that Liscor's walls are enchanted and that the city and army rely on services from [Mages]. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L79)
- Wistram threatens to cut off services, including maintaining the walls against the spring rains. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L85)
