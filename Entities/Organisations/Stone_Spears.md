# Stone Spears
> Type: org | Also called: Stone Spears Tribe | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.23
> See also: Gnoll tribes, Goblin army
## Overview
- The Stone Spears Tribe was not the largest, wealthiest or most powerful Gnoll tribe in southern Izril, and occupied the northernmost lands below the Blood Fields, roaming the rocky slopes. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L2)
- The tribe lived and even prospered by prospecting for gemstones and valuable minerals in caves and on the slopes of the Grisrith range, which the Gnolls revere. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L3-4)
- Much of the tribe had to spend its time digging or prospecting away from the main camp, because it was too dangerous for the young and elderly to venture up the slopes. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L6)
- A Gnoll said that when Mrsha was lost the miners had not returned and were a few days' journey away, and the tribe lacked the numbers for a search. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L249,L253)
- A Gnoll said the tribe's [Shaman] knows no spells that would help, only healing and fighting. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L317)
- The Stone Spears camp lies at the foot of one of the mountains surrounding Liscor. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L15)
- {event} A report said the Stone Spears tribe was wintering in the region, with half of it camped while warriors and miners went higher for ores and gems. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L99,L101)
- {event} The same report said an [Assassin] who slipped past the Goblin Lord's army found no survivors at the mining site, and that the Gnolls had apparently been ambushed and slew over a hundred Goblins before being overwhelmed. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L105)
