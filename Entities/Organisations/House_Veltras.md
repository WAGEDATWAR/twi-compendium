# House Veltras
> Type: org | Also called: the Veltras family | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.01
> See also: Five Families, Tyrion Veltras, Byres family, Fierre
## Overview
- {belief} Fierre tells Ryoka that the Veltras family, along with the Byres and Terland families, are famous vampire slayers. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L286)
- {event} House Veltras sends another request for assistance with the poisoning of its two children, this time calling on the Five Families rather than local [Alchemists] and [Healers]. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L35-37)
- Calling on the Five Families is a sign of weakness that Lord Veltras must be reluctant to show his political rivals. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L36)
- The Five Families get a fuller picture that the children are sick, not yet poisoned, and Tyrion needs a cure. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L37-38)
- The other families agree to help after an hour of reflection, since they were children, and Magnolia Reinhart is absent from Izril. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L39-40)
