# Strongheart family
> Type: org | Also called: Stronghearts | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.01R
> See also: Runner's Guild
## Overview
- Garia Strongheart is a City Runner who introduced herself cheerfully to Ryoka. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L22; Book 1, Ch1.04R L161)
- Wailant and Viceria Strongheart are the married couple who raised Garia. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L52)
- The Strongheart family home near a farm looked well made and expensive for a medieval setting, with a bookshelf full of books. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L85)
- Wailant Strongheart gave Ryoka a hard look and met her eyes without backing down; he has green eyes. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L78)
- Ryoka taught Garia to stand and punch on a snowy hilltop near the Strongheart farm. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L250)
## Wailant Strongheart
- A speaker recalled that Wailant Strongheart runs a really productive farm around Celum. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L89)
- Wailant is a [Farmer] and one of the higher-level [Farmers] around Celum, whose farm is known for a specialty crop. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L658)
- Wailant has many colourful tattoos, including a sea monster and a fish that seems to swim when his muscles move, and is a former [Pirate], though he is usually on the right side of the law. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L654,L672-675)
- {event} Wailant shoulder-charged past a trader's cart and attacked the Hobgoblin Numbtongue in Celum, and the Watch banned him from the city for a month, then for a year. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L681,L750,L762)
- Wailant's cutlass seldom sees use and is mostly a prop for negotiations with [Traders]. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L674)
- Palt asked Erin whether she knew Viceria Strongheart, whose daughter was in the city, and Garia came forward. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1073-1077)
