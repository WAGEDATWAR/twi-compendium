# 4th Company
> Type: org | Also called: Liscor's 4th Company; Skywalker Company (ledger key) | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.13
> See also: Liscor 4th Company, Embria Grasstongue
## Overview
- The company crossing the flooded plains toward Liscor is a hundred and twenty odd Drakes and Gnolls, all armed, armored and carrying their gear. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L13-14)
- Wing Commander Embria Grasstongue leads it, with Captain Halliss. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L20-23)
- All ninety-seven of the 4th Company are quartered in Liscor's barracks and use the Adventurer's Guild's practice area. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L14-15)
- Four [Captains] are left in the 4th Company since the battle at Liscor. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L58)
