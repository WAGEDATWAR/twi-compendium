# Runner's Guild
> Type: org | Also called: Runner Guild; Runners' Guild | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.01R
> See also: Adventurer's Guild
## Overview
- Runner's Guilds post delivery requests on a bulletin board and hand them out first-come, first-served, like the Adventurer's Guilds. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L17-18)
- A Runner logs the seals she delivered with her name and their composition, and a receptionist witnesses the claim and signs the log. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L10-13)
- Runners can collect their pay whenever, but most take it in one lump sum at the end of the week, signing for it so the receptionist can validate it. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L125)
- Garia says Guild requests are a mix of fast, safe and heavy deliveries; fast ones go over long distances, safe ones suit messages no one should read, and heavy ones are small enough to beat a cart or wagon. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L20-21)
- Ryoka understands that the guildmasters of local Runner's Guilds are retired Runners, as in the Adventurer's Guilds, and that the staff are hired help or people too injured or old to do the jobs themselves. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L128)
## Rules and customs
- {belief} Garia says some Street Runners and City Runners belong to a group that enforces the Guild's rules, which are not real written rules but ones everyone obeys. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L175,L179)
- {event} Runners from three nearby cities' Guilds withheld requests from Ryoka and covered them with other Runners, according to Ryoka. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L61-62)
- Fals judges an unmarked request offering twenty gold for a delivery to the High Passes to be a death trap for any Runner, and it sits unclaimed. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L69-77)
- Rule 21 of the Runner's Guild states that a Runner affected by spell or Skill may refuse to provide services and cancel any request, and that anyone who attacks, hinders or alters a Runner on duty can be banned from receiving or requesting deliveries. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L329)
- Runner's confidentiality, an unspoken code, requires a Runner to keep client names and the nature of a delivery secret even from [Guardsmen], unless testifying about a crime. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L158-159)
- Each Guild has private rooms shielded against eavesdropping where clients can talk to Runners about personal deliveries. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L160)
## Business
- Fals said business was slow in early winter because the frozen roads made carts and wagons go slowly until they were sharp shod. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L39-41)
- Few City Runners were working because of the Goblins, and the City Runners kept in the city took the deliveries that normally went to the Street Runners, who sat idle at the Guild's tables. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L117-119)
- {event} Laken met Ryoka at a Runner's Guild when heavy snow threatened the roads, saying the Merchant's Guild could not deliver food for at least a week, and asked her to carry supplies in a bag of holding. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L577,L582-587,L608)
