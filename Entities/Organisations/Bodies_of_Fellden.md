# Bodies of Fellden
> Type: org | Also called: The Bodies of Fellden | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.22D
> See also: Calectus, Geneva Scala, Okasha, Idis, Selphids, Talenqual
## Overview
- The Bodies of Fellden is the Selphid-only company and is called the largest Selphid company in Baleros. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1161-1162)
- Calectus, an [Honor Guard] whom Geneva and Okasha had parted ways with earlier, leads over two hundred Selphids who arrive to give Geneva aid. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1152-1155,L1153)
- Many of the Selphids wear fresh bodies, and some are members of the company while many are young Selphids who will become [Doctors] if Geneva teaches them. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1156-1157)
- Calectus says the Selphids will help manage the plague, since Selphids do not grow ill from most disease, and that they hope Geneva will help them in turn. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1157)
- Fezimet worries when the company arrives, even though it is only a hundred and most are aspiring apprentice [Doctors] and aid workers. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1162)
## Role in Talenqual
- {event} The Bodies of Fellden, a small but elite company of Selphids, enter Talenqual to help Geneva and learn from her. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L134)
- The Selphids also safeguard Geneva so that she can help them, and no one knows that. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L134-135)
- There are over two hundred Selphids in Talenqual, all powerful, and Geneva thinks they are better allies than not. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L176)
- Idis reports to Calectus and the other senior Selphids, and Geneva takes no part in their debate. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L176,L283)
- Marian and Umina learn that the Bodies of Fellden came to help Geneva and conclude from this that she has ties to the Selphids, calling the company serious strength. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L210-212)
## Treatment of Okasha and Idis
- {event} In a private inn room, Calectus tells Okasha that she has gone too far, and that the Selphids see her. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1170-1178)
- {event} Calectus says Okasha has committed the sin for which the Selphids were destroyed, that the Minds will sentence her, and he confines her in a holding-jar. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1182-1183)
- {event} Calectus introduces Idis as Okasha's replacement, who says she is from the Minds, the Selphids' leaders, and offers to help Geneva move and to protect her. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1188-1204)
- Calectus tells Geneva that the company wants to learn from her, and that Idis will be their link and protector. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1204)
- Idis overrides Geneva's voice to cover for Geneva, because Calectus said word about Geneva must not get out. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L122-124)
