# Rustängmarder
> Type: org | Also called: Rustangmarder | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.02K
> See also: Flos, Parasol Stroll
## Overview
- A Rustängmarder is a soldier who ferries Flos' orders to the rest of the company. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L438)
- {event} A Rustängmarder soldier stands at attention beside the throne as Flos' words are heard when he ends his reign. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L438)
- The Rustängmarder are Flos' vanguard and hammer. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L53)
- {state} After the battle, the Rustängmarder are down to twelve; Ulyse is dead, two half-Giants have fallen and Zamea's leg is broken. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L53)
