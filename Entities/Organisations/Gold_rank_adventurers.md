# Gold-rank adventurers
> Type: org | Also called: Gold-ranks | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.31
> See also: Adventurer's Guild, Silver Swords, Halfseekers, Griffon Hunt
## Overview
- Gold-rank adventurers who die are stripped of their equipment and cremated, so that thieves cannot despoil their corpses. (Book 5, Ch4.31 L189)
- Jelaqua says Gold-rank adventurers swear an oath to fight and do not run when a city is at stake. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L49)
- {event} After the failed rescue in the dungeon, the adventurers retreat battered and exhausted, stalemated rather than beaten, while the Antinium survivors return to their Hive. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L641-642,L667)
