# Painted Soldiers
> Type: org | Also called: Painted Antinium | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.12
> See also: Pawn, Yellow Splatters, Klbkch, Antinium
## Overview
- Painted Soldiers is an informal phrase for Antinium Soldiers with unique markings, which are meant to foster trust. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L15,L32)
- Pawn takes over relieving Liscor's [Guardsmen] with the Painted Soldiers on the walls. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L15)
- Yellow Splatters is a [Sergeant] on command duty with Purple Smile while Pawn watches a play. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L50,L53)
- {event} On seeing the Drakes' light-ramp spell, the Soldiers ring a bell and form up shoulder to shoulder at the top of the ramp in under two seconds. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L53-56)
- Zevara recognises Yellow Splatters as a [Sergeant], a class she notes for her report. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L99)
## War
- {event} Marching against Reiss' army in ranks of five, about a hundred Painted Soldiers carry unique paint: a flower, raindrops, numbers, and words. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L538-539,L564-565)
- {event} Though arrows fall among them, the Soldiers keep running and none fall, and the arrows seem to miss. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L556-560)
- {event} The Painted Soldiers stand around Yellow Splatters' body and make their own decision. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L214-215)
## Later
- The Painted Antinium number nearly seven hundred, with a cafeteria and sermon hall in their barracks. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L31)
- Yellow Splatters, a giant among the Soldiers, has returned from death and seen heaven. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L28-29)
- Chesacre and Thaina chose their own names and are Soldiers who became Autonomous, perhaps Individual, and are already Pawn's [Acolytes]. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L123,L175-177)
