# Gnoll tribes
> Type: org | Also called: Gnolls of Issiysil | First appears: Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1
> See also: Stone Spears, Goblin army
## Overview
- The Gnoll tribes of the plains and swamps reacted to the Antinium threat faster than the Drake city-states, and over a hundred tribes sent warriors to form a massive army. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L89)
- {event} In its first major engagement an army of twenty thousand Gnolls routed a force of nearly eighty thousand Antinium in a battle lasting a day. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L90)
- The Antinium never penetrated further than fifty miles into Gnoll lands. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L92)
- Teriarch said the Gnoll tribes used to meet only once every hundred years, then every fifty, then every twenty, to stay connected. (Book 2, Ch2.39 L212)
- {state} After Zel Shivertail's death most Gnoll tribes withdrew from sight and mourned with massed howls across the plains. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L7)
- Elirr said the Woven Bladegrass tribe, whose new Chieftain is young and has fought Drakes before, was behind the Gnoll-Drake incidents. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L352-356)
- A much larger force of over a hundred thousand Gnolls assembled within a week of that battle, and with hunting packs of tens of thousands it fought off every Antinium advance, though the Gnolls never sent armies out to attack the Antinium in retaliation. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L91-92)
