# Centrists
> Type: org | Also called: Centrist faction | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3
> See also: Libertarians, Ullsinoi
## Overview
- {belief} A Wistram student explained that the Centrists want every mage in the world to be part of Wistram and the academy to declare itself a nation, and that they are always fighting with the Isolationists. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L438)
- Archmage Feor, an old half-Elf, leads the Centrists, who are one of the largest factions in Wistram. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L12,L28)
- The Revivalists, Centrists and Libertarians are the big three factions of Wistram, with thousands of members in the academy and across the world, the most voting power in the Council and the most active Archmages, so they are more akin to political parties. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L480)
## Members and actions
- Feor belongs to the Centrists but also keeps a personal group of half-Elf mages he trusts, including Teura, one of his best. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L44)
- {event} Feor brought six experienced Centrist [Mages] from the Council to cast a spell on Blackmage, and they stepped back and left when four hundred [Mages] watched. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L322,L352)
- A Revivalist said the Revivalists have the numbers to resist any faction, including Feor and the Centrists. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L380)
- Feor told Ylawes that the Revivalists back Montressa du Valeross's team and are at odds with his own faction, the Centrists. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L137)
- Elena, an Earther, belongs to Archmage Feor's Centrists faction, and the Centrists would loan her to the Revivalists if they needed to collaborate on something enriching both groups. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L520; Book 18, Ch7.29B L97,L265)
- {event} Feor tried to use the Centrists to persuade Wistram to go beyond a letter of condemnation and unite coastal nations against Ailendamus's blockade, but lost the Council vote when Viltach's Libertarians sided with the Revivalists. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L28-29)
- Teura, a half-Elf [Mage] on Wistram's Council, belongs to the Centrists under Archmage Feor. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L25)
- Most of the trusted mages allowed to attend Elena's Earth lectures were Centrists. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L26)
