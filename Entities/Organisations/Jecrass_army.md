# Jecrass army
> Type: org | Also called: Jecrass's army; Jecrass' army; the army of Jecrass | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.08K
> See also: Raelt, General Lael, Reim's army at Jecrass border, Medain, Belchan, River Wardens
## Overview
- General Lael is the high commander of Jecrass's armies, which are known for emphasis on cavalry and speed meant to guard the wide, open realm. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L135)
- Lael is a Level 27 [General], one of the highest-leveled assets of Jecrass, and young, in her early thirties. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L135)
- Raelt promoted Lael because his daughter Jecaina suggested her, hiring her for loyalty over levels, and he thinks she is a decent [General] but not brilliant. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L148-150)
- Lael had served in Belchan with distinction until she was disciplined for striking a [Politician]. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L148)
- Raelt's oldest retainer, Geril, is an old man who rides with the army in a hauberk, mail armor and a conical helmet, carrying a spear he can use from horseback. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L437-439)
## Facing Reim
- When asked how she would respond to an invasion by Reim, Lael says she would negotiate a favorable surrender of Jecrass's forces. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L167)
- {event} Raelt orders the army to muster, and an army of tens of thousands, all mounted for speed, waits at the border within five hours. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L346,L389-390)
- Lael has confided in Raelt that the army is all mounted so that it can fall back if worse comes to worst. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L390)
- At the border Jecrass's army musters nearly a hundred and seventy thousand [Soldiers], nearly half of them mounted, and a huge portion of its military might. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L402)
- The River Wardens come to the border, some with their entire armies. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L392)
- Jecrass's army cheers and blows horns as Reim's smaller army approaches, then falls silent under Flos's aura. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L416,L424,L442)
- General Lael watches the Rustängmarder uneasily, and later bellows as she rides down her ranks urging her forces back to their feet. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L446,L465)
## Invasion of Belchan
- Raelt takes all the forces he can spare across Belchan's border as soon as he has declared war, three days earlier, mostly horses, over a hundred thousand of them. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L455)
- The foot soldiers barely keep pace even with Skills boosting their movement. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L455)
- Jecrass's army storms through Belchan's capital and occupies it in the hour before Flos's army can get there. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L598)
- Jecrass's army is a substantial force, larger than the army that Flos Reimarch destroyed. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L454)
- Jecrass's army is led by General Lael and Raelt himself, and has not fought many battles, only a few skirmishes on the first day. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L461)
- {event} Raelt uses [Flicker Charge] and his army flickers, with Riders appearing among the Serpent Hunters. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1089-1090)
- {event} In a later battle Raelt's rapier goes through Flos's chest, and Jecrass's forces advance as Reim's army freezes. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L565-571)
## Against Medain
- Jecrass's tired army has raced across its nation and the border of Medain for nearly a day, using every movement Skill and potions to keep riders and mounts alive. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L2)
- Medain's heavy cavalry is fresh, having ridden from the capital for less than four hours. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L3)
- Jecrass's army is still riding toward its Princess, staggered, as Medain's army led by its High King marches at the conflict where Raelt is fighting. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L4)
- The surprise Minotaur attack kept Medain from taking Princess Jecaina away before the forces met, and Jecrass's riders are exhausted. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L5)
