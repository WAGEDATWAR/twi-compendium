# Ullsinoi
> Type: org | Also called: Ullsinoi faction | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.59
> See also: Centrists, Libertarians
## Overview
- The Ullsinoi faction is a small Wistram faction, a little under a hundred members, more like a school of magic than an ideology such as the Revivalists, but with clout where it matters. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L828)
- Palt belongs to the Ullsinoi faction, and was reminded of his duties to it after the events involving Montressa. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L827)
- Everyone in the faction is full of tricks, and a candidate cannot join without working out the joke in the faction's name within the first ten minutes of hearing it. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L830)
- The Ullsinoi faction wanted an Earther, hoping to learn things such as electricity, harnessing lightning and flying into space. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L338; Book 14, Ch6.64 L441)
- Palt told Erin that Ullsinoi is quite flexible and small but has some influence, that Montressa is of a major faction, the Revivalists, and asked her to be an ally to the faction by giving information. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L425-430)
## Dealings with Erin
- {event} Palt negotiated through the night with the Ullsinoi faction in heated conference and then told Erin it had convinced the Revivalists not to interfere with her inn in exchange for a few concessions, such as plays and use of her door as a hub. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L164-172)
- Montressa du Valeross's mission to pursue Pisces would be rescinded and his bounty mitigated, though not erased, thanks to communications within the Ullsinoi faction and a larger debate in Wistram. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L278)
- The Ullsinoi faction does not deal with criminals, explicitly, but welcomes anyone who can read the sign, and compensates Erin for help it asks of her; Erin may refuse to shelter people with trouble on their heels. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L73,L81)
- A [Green Mage] farmer said not everyone affiliated with the Ullsinoi faction is an [Illusionist], and that it is a practical group to associate with. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L919)
