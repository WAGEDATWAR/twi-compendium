# Griffon Hunt
> Type: org | Also called: Griffin Hunt | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.01T
> See also: Horns of Hammerad, Adventurers, Adventurer's Guild
## Overview
- Griffon Hunt is a Gold-rank team that rode through the snow to Liscor after Revi received a [Message] about the dungeon, travelling day and night to arrive before other teams. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L177,L199)
- Halrac was the team's lead [Scout] and [Marksman], Ulric (Ulrien) led the team with a greatsword and magical armour, Typhenous was one of its two mages, and Revi, a mage with stitch marks, was the newest addition. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L177,L182-184,L229)
- {event} Fighting Gnolls, Halrac loosed blunt arrows meant to break bones without killing, and Revi summoned three armed phantoms. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L117-119,L130)
- Griffon Hunt stayed in an expensive side room of an inn, as a mark of respect for their rank and the gold they paid, and Halrac complained the team was idle while others could get the drop on them. (Book 2, Ch2.26 L113-114)
- {event} Ulrien, the Captain, told Revi to put her strongest summons out at the first sign of danger and Typhenous to hang back with his best spells, then forced open the rusted dungeon doors. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L340-346)
- Halrac, Ulrien, Revi and Typhenous quarrelled over rooms at Erin's inn, saying they had paid good coin. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L119-121)
## Name
- Halrac explained that the name means a breed of dog used for hunting, which is what the team specialises in, and not the beast Griffin. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L395)
- Halrac and Ulrien used to hunt actual Griffins, and the name is a pun on that which another member of the group, now dead, came up with. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L398-405)
- Halrac told a Watch [Sergeant] that the name is spelled with an 'o', as in the dog. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L135)
- Lord Radivaek recalled selling dogs to Griffon Hunt, and Halrac said the team let them go to a [Hunter]'s team after the incident in the north; they had used them to ambush Griffins by baiting the flying monster. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L744-746,L754)
## Alliance with the Halfseekers
- {rel} Griffon Hunt and the Halfseekers had agreed to join forces on the dungeon. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L49)
- {reveal} Jelaqua told Ulrien and Revi the Halfseekers' former companion was a Hobgoblin named Garen Redfang, who betrayed the team after a treasure find; Ulrien suggested justice be done after the dungeon. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L305-315)
- {state} Ulrien died in Liscor at Erin's inn, and Halrac, Typhenous and Revi left the inn but stayed in the city, unable to stay where he fell. (Book 5, Ch4.31 L245-248)
- Typhenous told Revi that Halrac and Ulrien had been comrades for decades, while they had joined the party not two years earlier. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L120)
- {event} Griffon Hunt and the Halfseekers went into the dungeon by the trapped main entrance, with Jelaqua in the Heartflame Breastplate. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L123-125)
- Halrac pushed cursed ornaments into his bag of holding after Typhenous and Moore judged they would not explode. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L509-511)
- {event} Typhenous proposed taking the Silver Swords, the Halfseekers and the Horns of Hammerad in turn and switching groups after a few hours. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L493)
- Revi told the Horns they fought like Silver-rank because Gold-ranks are experts in a field, and said Pisces lacked a signature style. (Book 8, Ch5.38 L105,L120)
- {state} Revi said the team's formation was weaker without Ulrien, their front-line fighter, and the team wanted to work with the Horns. (Book 8, Ch5.38 L125-128)
- About three years earlier Halrac and Ulrien contracted Typhenous and Revi to deal with a Griffin colony in the Nameless Heights; they poisoned nests and killed hundreds of Griffins, but Typhenous used the wrong spells and the plague killed most livestock and sickened three cities, and the team was lucky to keep its Gold-rank status. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L485,L494-497)
- Erin thought of how the Halfseekers and Griffon Hunt had sacrificed a lot and lost Ulrien. (Book 9, Ch5.49 L582)
## Under Halrac's captaincy
- {event} Halrac, Revi and Typhenous agreed to pay eighteen thousand gold pieces, six thousand per former teammate, to square their former members' losses, after Typhenous quietly added nearly seven thousand gold to the bag of holding. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L690-692,L716-718)
- Revi, a Stitch-Woman and [Summoner], said her home nation in Chandrar is now part of the Empire of Sands and that Chandrar deserves respect. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L307,L311)
- {state} Halrac became Captain of Griffon Hunt after Ulrien was killed in a fight in Liscor's inn by Regrika Blackpaw, a Named Adventurer. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L120-121,L182)
- Halrac told Briganda that the team wanted to make things right, offering recompense of 6,000 gold pieces to each former member, with Elm able to claim his share if he asked, and Briganda accepted. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L687-691)
- {event} Halrac found a request from Emperor Laken Godart of the Unseen Empire seeking a Gold-rank team at Riverfarm for very low pay. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L598-603)
- Halrac, Revi, Typhenous and Briganda breakfasted with friends at Erin's inn and planned to go to Hedault's with the Horns to appraise treasure. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L257-269)
- Griffon Hunt's members are Halrac Everam, Revi Cotton, Typhenous and Briganda Rishaw, and Briganda's son Cade travels with them but would be left behind from real danger. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L213,L230)
- {event} Typhenous cast [Valmira's Comet] and Halrac fired enchanted arrows, and with Revi's Stitch-Warriors the team destroyed a Creler nest at range. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L343-356)
- Laken Godart asked Halrac to stay a week and destroy a nest of Crelers, and Halrac accepted, saying the team would have destroyed the nest even without pay. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L297-306)
- Griffon Hunt's members decided to go tell Emperor Laken Godart about the Goblins in his lands but to say nothing about Erin Solstice to him, and not to tell Erin either. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L626)
- {event} The loot from the escape room included a Belt of Strength for Briganda and Boots of Stability for Halrac. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L416-420)
