# Redfang Hobs
> Type: org | Also called: Redfang Hobgoblins | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.33
> See also: Redfang Warriors, Redfang Tribe, Cave Goblins
## Overview
- {event} Six Redfang Hobs, with no arrows left and no tribe, charge the twenty Eater Goats that remain, laughing. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L404-415)
- The Redfang Hobs have limited medical knowledge, mostly stitching bad cuts and keeping wounds clean, but far ahead of Cave Goblin medicine. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L294-296)
- Hundreds of Cave Goblins are crammed into the cave. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L324)
- The Hobs lead five mini-tribes of Cave Goblins and none has the [Chieftain] class; they all still consider themselves part of Garen's tribe. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L10-14)
- {state} Olesm confiscates Erin's door under military law, and a Gnoll of the Watch puts manacles on Numbtongue, arresting the five Redfang Goblins. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L578-580)
- Laken hears that two Hobs of the Redfang tribe travel in the caravan, famed for their skill in battle, and only one is male. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L66)
- {belief} In the same report, a Redfang Goblin killed the Goblin Lord, Garen Redfang is their Chieftain, and another Redfang slew the [Knight] Sir Vumat. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L66)
