# Players
> Type: org | Also called: Players of Celum; Players of Liscor | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.28
> See also: Wesle, Jasi, Temile, Emme, Grev, The Wandering Inn, Celum
## Overview
- The Players of Celum are a troupe of [Actors] that practices daily in Erin's inn, with ten or so veteran Human actors and a new cast of Drakes, Gnolls and Humans. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L130,L150)
- A Gnoll actor addresses Temile as Director Temile while worrying about the lines of a Gnollish Macbeth. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L130-131)
- A casting call for the Players at the inn attracted nearly a hundred applicants. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L150)
- Temile becomes a [Producer], a low-level one, and takes charge of the Players of Celum in Liscor with a new cast of Drakes, Gnolls and Humans. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L174)
- Erin has used up all the plays she knew from home. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L175)
## The troupe on the road
- {state} The original Players of Celum travel north, earning money by performing in every place they come to. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L44)
- Wesle Salkis is the lead [Actor] and one of the two original performers, and he was a [Guardsman] before acting. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L25,L44)
- Jasi Redigal is an [Actress], older sister of Grev Redigal, and Emme, a short part-Dwarf woman, manages the Players. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L9,L53,L234)
- Grev is the only child among the Players, and he is Emme's left hand, managing and assisting the [Actors]. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L101,L234)
- By the time it reaches Sovvex the troupe is over forty strong, including two teams of [Actors], people for dresses, props, makeup and stage setup, and Grev. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L100)
- Acting drew crowds in Celum for months and stole Wesle, a former [Guardsman], Kilkran, Pralcem, Rima and others from their jobs, becoming an obsession and a calling. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L44)
- Pralcem, a former [Huntsman], objects to the Players rewriting the Bard's original text. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L11-12,L45)
- Emme demands the Mayor of Sovvex let the Players rent the town square for an outdoor play, and sets up barricades for a large crowd. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L6-7)
## Two troupes
- {event} The Players of Celum meet the Players of Liscor in Liscor, a name chosen to avoid confusion, with over two dozen Gnolls, Drakes and a Garuda as new [Actors]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L152-155)
- Temile says the new [Lead Actors] are already over Level 15 and everyone else is leveling fast. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L155)
- Wesle, Jasi, Kilkran, Yimur and Pralcem shine with higher levels than the newer [Actors]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L158)
## Meeting Griffon Hunt
- Grev, who knows Griffon Hunt from the inn and was with the Players, says the cast must wear illusion spells to go out and that he wears a hood because of the autographs. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1042,L1049,L1061)
- Griffon Hunt sees posters of the Players of Celum in the commercial districts advertising plays such as Elisial at the Season Theatre and Macbeth. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1059,L1062)
- Grev tells Griffon Hunt that the Players are now in Liscor with Drake and Gnoll [Actors], that Erin was kicked out of Celum and the Players went with her. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1064)
- Grev tells Griffon Hunt about the Horns bringing down an Adult Creler, the Wyvern attack on Pallass, Erin's magic fire and her garden; he learned of them by [Message] from Temile. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1064,L1071-1073,L1104-1109)
- Wesle tells Griffon Hunt that the Players are holding a Solstice Booth for Erin and performing Lady Macbeth with Jasi as lead. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1111-1113)
## Fame
- The Players of Liscor appear on television, and Temile signs autographs with the breakfast crowd at the inn. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L190-196)
- Jasi says the Singer herself sent the Players a [Message] saying she is a huge fan and asking for copies of all their plays. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L235)
- Emme had the Mage's Guild send the entire play, which cost a bit, in return for a shipment of song-crystals. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L235)
