# Council
> Type: organisation | Also called: the Council; Wistram's Council; Liscor's Council | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2
> See also: Wistram factions, Feor, Liscor, Lism, Krshia Silverfang, Zevara
## Overview
- This entry covers two separate bodies called the Council: the governing council of Wistram Academy and the ruling Council of the city of Liscor. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L342; Book 11, Ch6.31 L161)
## Wistram's Council
- A student relays that the Council, made up of the leaders of all the mage factions in Wistram, scolded the mage in charge of a class and told them to start teaching right away. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L342)
- Pisces argues his case for necromancy before the Council, and mages around the room shout furiously against him. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L508-512)
- {state} After a debate in which the opinion swings against Pisces, Archmage Feor announces that necromancy is not taught in Wistram and that the Council revokes Pisces's scholarship, so that he must pay the full dues or leave. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L514-516)
- {state} Pisces offers his silver-belled rapier to Calvaron to be sold to cover the academy's fees, and Calvaron says it should be enough. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L529-534)
## Liscor's Council
- {event} The Liscor Council votes to hold an election, which is described as a referendum on the current Council, and one listener thinks the wording lets them avoid counting the votes. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L161-162)
- {event} The Council is offering a headhunter fee for every team that Ceria's group can recruit in Celum to build a safe trade route around the Bloodfields, and Yvlon has signed the Horns of Hammerad up. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L43-45,L66-67)
- {event} At a Council meeting, Krshia proposes sending the [Negotiator] Teliv with wine and a puppy or two from Elirr's litter to placate Selys's grandmother. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L159,L164-168)
- Liscor's Council has allocated funds for all the teams who stood and fought, and will pay a pension to the dead, though it had to go to the Antinium to pay it and keep on budget. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L413-414)
