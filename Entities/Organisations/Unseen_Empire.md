# Unseen Empire
> Type: org | Also called: Laken's territory (Riverfarm); Riverfarm empire | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.09E
> See also: Laken Godart, Riverfarm, Windrest villagers
## Overview
- The Unseen Empire is small, with only a few thousand people, mostly villagers from Riverfarm, Windrest and other villages raided by Goblins, and housing, feeding and protecting them is a heavy burden. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L68)
- {event} The town of Trottvisk declares itself part of the Unseen Empire. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L68)
- {event} [Witches] come to Riverfarm to make a deal with the [Emperor], among them Agratha, whose coven is large and who arrives with nearly a dozen [Witches]. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L129)
- [Witches] in the Empire are protected by the [Emperor]'s laws: they are punished for committing a crime, not for existing. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L130)
- {event} Eleven nobles have replied after Lady Imarris, none of them from the Five Families yet. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L50)
