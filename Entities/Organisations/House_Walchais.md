# House Walchaís
> Type: org | Also called: House Walchais | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.39G
> See also: Lady Bethal Walchaís, Rose Knights, Five Families
## Overview
- House Walchaís is one of the larger houses in Izril and not one of the Five Families; it came soon after the original noble families and traces its roots to Terandria. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L442)
- {belief} Lyonette calls the house mad as loons, fond of quests, training [Knights] and duelling over insults. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L444)
- Lady Bethal Walchaís is the current scion; she eloped with and married a famous [Duelist], then returned to establish her own branch of [Knights] and take control of her family. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L444)
- Lyonette says Bethal's husband has killed twelve people in honour duels. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L444-447)
- The people of Bethal's lands love her: a [Shepherd] says House Walchaís has always been good to them, paid for medicine in the Redvein Plague two decades back, and fought for them during the Sacrifice of Roses. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L327-328)
- Ryoka's dust storm damaged farms on those lands, and she apologised and offered payment; the people accepted once they learned it was for Bethal. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L326-327)
- {event} The Knights of the Petal went to war for their Lady's honour, and Bethal's people refused to do business with anyone who had sent her a rose. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L329)
