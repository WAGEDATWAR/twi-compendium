# Crafters
> Type: org | Also called: Crafters of Illivere; Magus-Crafter | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.31
> See also: Free Antinium
## Overview
- Esthelm's ruling body of Master Crafters replaced the military leader Erin had helped remove. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L335)
- In Illivere, Crafters are the elite who manufacture or manage Golems, voted into positions of authority by each state, and they elect the Magus-Crafter. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L201)
- Crafters are in charge, lay out plans and 'make' the Golems, with dozens or hundreds of workers helping; making Golems is an industry there and Crafters are often businesspeople. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L203-204)
- Magus-Crafter Femithain is of the state of Dellva, whose capital is Demarsel, so the head of Illivere shifts when the next Magus-Crafter is elected. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L279)
- Femithain resolved discipline among the Crafters, and young Crafters dedicated Golems to the former Empress Nsiia. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L188; Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L198,L228)
- No one in Illivere can craft Sentient-class Golems. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L480)
- Crafters looked down on the [Armsmaster] Dellic as an elevated [Guard]. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L520)
- First Crafters of each state debated declaring war on Reim, with Femithain promising to speak against. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L734,L836)
