# Liscor 4th Company
> Type: org | Also called: Liscor's 4th Company | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.12
> See also: 4th Company, Embria Grasstongue, Liscor's army
## Overview
- Liscor's 4th Company is part of Liscor's army, a group of hardened elites, and is made up of Liscor's sons and daughters. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L610)
- {event} Commander Grasstongue, a female Drake, returns home with the company, arriving a day or two late because of the rising water, and camps at the flooded plains. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L606,L610-612)
- She orders word sent to Liscor's High Command that the 4th Company is back and ready to reinforce the city against monster, Goblin or Antinium threats. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L609)
- The company marched day and night without resting in the Drake cities on the way. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L611)
- {event} 4th Company squads, with the City Watch, hunt Shield Spider nests on the Floodplains in a cull of nearly a thousand Drakes and Gnolls in total. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L26)
- The cull is a tradition of Liscor's City Watch, a gesture of goodwill. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L27)
