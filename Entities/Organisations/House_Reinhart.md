# House Reinhart
> Type: org | Also called: Reinhart; the Reinharts | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.11R
> See also: Five Families, House Byres
## Overview
- A speaker told Ryoka they were distantly related to the Reinharts. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L224)
- Selys said the Reinharts were among the first humans to settle Izril, and have so many connections that half a dozen cities would march their armies if they declared war. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L186,L191)
- Lady Magnolia Reinhart is the head of the Reinhart family, heroine of the First Antinium Wars, and one of the richest on the continent. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L80,L428)
- No one made an enemy of the Reinharts without paying for it. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L400)
- The Reinhart household employs a [Butler] and has an estate in Invrisil. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L517-520; Book 2, Ch2.30 L21)
- Maviola El called the Reinharts the richest and most powerful family, always snakes, loyal to each other, their only curse being Regis Reinhart. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L153-155)
- Wellfar alone refused to send aid to House Reinhart. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L832)
