# Flooded Waters tribe
> Type: org | Also called: Rags's tribe; Flooded Waters Tribe | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00G
> See also: Rags's tribe, Red Fang Tribe, Goblin army
## Early days
- Before a Rock Crab hunt, Rags had about sixty Goblins in her tribe, and afterward she had closer to forty, possibly forty-five, since Goblins came and died quickly. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L5,L26)
- The Flooded Waters tribe's single Hob had been its Chief until Erin killed him. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L146)
- Rags considered Erin terrifying for killing a Hob by herself, something even Silver-rank adventurers struggle with. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L211)
- {event} In less than a week the Flooded Waters Tribe overran the Jawbreaker, Sword Taker and Bloody Hand Tribes, slaying or defeating their Chieftains and absorbing their Goblins. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L6)
- Rags had defeated a tribe of over two hundred Goblins with virtually no casualties and led over eight hundred Goblins, having absorbed three, and now four, tribes in a week. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L47-48)
## Coalition leader
- Rags's Flooded Waters tribe was the largest and strongest tribe in a coalition of fifteen or so tribes. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L100)
- {state} Later, it had nearly eight hundred warriors, several thousand Goblins in total and over ten Hobs, the most of any tribe, though Rags did not count the Redfang tribe as part of it. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L101)
- Rags introduced herself to Humans as Chieftain of the Flooded Waters tribe. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L250)
## Forest by the lake
- The Flooded Waters Goblins camped for a long while in a forest by a lake near several small cities and towns, which served as their semi-permanent home. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L63-65)
- From there they raided caravans and farms nonviolently, aiming crossbows and chasing Humans away without killing unless attacked, and grew rich on the spoils. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L65-66)
- The tribe was originally small and barely surviving around Liscor, and was thousands strong with many Hobs and a mounted force of Carn Wolves, caught between the Goblin Lord and Tremborag's Mountain City Tribe. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L134)
- Rags's tribe is organised, with tents, cook stations, food supplies and latrines, which is Rags's most important innovation, and it fought off Lady Bethal and her Rose Knights in the forest. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L137)
- {event} After Humans hunted down the Frostfeeder Tribe for a week and killed over half of it, the remainder became part of Rags's tribe. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L56-57)
- Two Hobs, a male and a female, led Goblins guarding Lady Bethal's party, and the female spoke the common tongue, surprising Bethal. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L32)
## Growth and flight
- {event} Thousands of Goblins with crossbows and pikes and hundreds of Hobs fled in a stream across the grassland. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L25)
- The tribe's Goblins communicate through sign language and posture, not talk, and a few Goblins who had known Erin and been in her inn told the others about her. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L7,L21-23)
- {state} After Tremborag's fall, the tribe had grown elevenfold, with thousands of Hobs and Goblin warriors who had served Tremborag, along with Ulvama and a few [Shamans]. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L17-18)
- {event} Fighting began between the Flooded Waters tribe and the Goblin Lord's army. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L475)
- The Redfangs and the Cave Goblins had supplies of food while fleeing Reiss's army. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L53)
