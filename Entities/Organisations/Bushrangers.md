# Bushrangers
> Type: org | Also called: Daly's team | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.04D
> See also: United Nations company, Daly Sullivan, Rustless Guard
## Overview
- The Bushrangers are the Silver-rank adventuring team of the United Nations company, led by Captain Daly, who chose the name so that anyone from home who knew basic history would recognise it. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L650-651)
- They are Silver-rank through hard work rather than gear or levels; they all carry crossbows, mostly hunt, work in stealth and avoid melee. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L652)
- {state} Daly applies to become an adventurer because the company is not earning enough money, and signs for the Bushrangers. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L569,L650)
- They are the only active fighters among the Earthworlders, six of them, all adventurers. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L39)
- Daly hands out two silver coins to each as spending money. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L121)
## Members
- Dawson is a friend of Daly's from home, Ald (Aldenon) seeks Daly out in camp, and Kami is their best shot. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L227,L241,L741)
- Kami is an Australian girl, and Tofte is a young man from Norway, while Siri and Tofte both speak Swedish. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L49,L77,L518,L522)
- Siri, Dawson, Kami, Aldenon, Tofte and Tobi are named among the team in the hunt for giant snakes. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L18-21,L49,L77,L113)
## Activity
- {event} The Bushrangers hunt sleeping Stelbore, nine of them: two very big, three more adults and four piglets, though Daly says only five were adults. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L426,L641)
- {event} Daly tells Italians Lorenzo and Diana, and Blake, a New Zealander, that the Australians are friends and part of the United Nations company. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L520-525,L540)
- {event} Daly has the Bushrangers learn Swedish words and hand signals from Siri and Tofte as a secret code, preparing for the day they run up against hostile people from Earth. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L518,L525-531)
- Their tactics are ambush from trees, traps, nets, and bombs, and they have partnered with the Rustless Vanguard, making them one of the best non-Gold teams around. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L106-107)
- The Bushrangers are the division of the United Nations company that goes into combat, and few of the company volunteered for it. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L255)
