# Walled Cities
> Type: org | Also called: the Walled Cities of the Drakes | First appears: Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1
> See also: Liscor's army, Free Antinium
## Overview
- The Walled Cities are not all ruled by the same method: some elect an official, others are managed by a body of powerful individuals, and some have a hereditary leader. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L37)
- {belief} Pisces claimed the Walled Cities are built with Dragon bones, the skeletons of the Drakes' ancestors placed in the walls, and that this is why the Necromancer marched south. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L212)
- {event} The Walled Cities were highly suspicious of a package being sent by Courier, and would have blocked it, but let it pass under the peace treaty so as not to reveal to the Antinium what they knew. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L181-182)
- {event} Over the Drake attacks, the Walled Cities rejected an accusation and claimed ignorance, and Manus suggested unusual Wyvern migrations combined with freak weather. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L61)
- {event} After the Wyvern attack, Manus ordered an investigation and wall drills for Pallass, Oteslia asked for a hunting force from the other cities, and Manus needed support for secret operations, likely a strike against the Antinium. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L816,L828)
- {belief} Ilvriss reflected that Bird the Hunter's name was spreading through security briefings and [Strategist]'s notes as a new Prognugator, as dangerous as Xrn, Klbkch or Wrymvr the Deathless. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L619)
