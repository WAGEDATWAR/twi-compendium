# Wellfar family
> Type: org | Also called: House Wellfar; Wellfar | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.41
> See also: Five Families
## Overview
- The Wellfar family is one of the Five Families who first settled Izril from Terandria. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L186; Book 12, Ch6.45E L68)
- The Wellfar family kept much of its strength, though the Antinium Wars killed some scions. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L69)
- Maviola El said the Wellfars have coastal holdings, control trade, are rich, led the first navies to Izril, and are infertile and shrinking. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L117-119)
- The Wellfar family earns money in ships and trade, and Lady Hetessana Wellfar, a wealthy widowed [Dowager], inherited much from her husband, a [Ship Lord]. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L17-18)
- {event} Lady Hetessana had a two-year relationship with Lord Bein Terland before he fell for Lady Dealia. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L17,L23)
- Zedalien named Lady Hetessana Wellfar's claim as part of the scandal with Lady Dealia and Lord Bein that Wellfar pressed on the El family. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L114-115)
## Later
- {belief} A speaker said Ailendamus had been approaching the Wellfar family for naval aid and might offer a cousin's hand in marriage. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L601)
- Lady Gresaria of House Wellfar is the [Harbormistress] of First Landing and a [Sea Lady] over a century old. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L350,L413,L480)
- Only Wellfar refused to send aid to House Reinhart. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L832)
- Maviola told Erin that Hetessana Wellfar is a good person, kindly though lonely, despite her failed romances. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L616)
- {belief} Geram said the witch Califor broke the Wellfar dam at Lake Roisemead, and Rie said Wellfar is still hunting the perpetrator. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L86,L90)
