# Gentlemen Callers
> Type: org | Also called: Gentleman Callers; Wilovan and Ratici; the Callers | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.54
> See also: Wilovan, Ratici, Erin Solstice, Typhenous, The Wandering Inn
## Overview
- The Gentlemen Callers are Wilovan, a Gnoll [Thug], and Ratici, a Drake [Thief], who belong to the Brotherhood of Serendipitous Meetings. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L698,L707,L709,L730)
- Palt explains that the Gentlemen Callers are two big Faces of the Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings, a smaller but elite gang with big players in northern Izril. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L265)
- Fierre tells Ryoka that the Callers are two of the Brothers' best and that they will kill an enemy of the Brothers. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L476)
- The Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings have built a reputation on politeness, and are one of the two largest local underworld groups along with the Sisters of Chell. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L915-916)
- Fierre lists the Gentlemen Callers among the gangs that would take a request to kill someone, none of them based around Filk. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L375)
- Fierre tells Ryoka that the Brothers are all-male, do not do kidnapping or racketeering, and keep a code of honor, and that if they take off their hats one should run. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L361)
## Wilovan and Ratici
- Wilovan is a [Thug] who is also a [Reader] able to memorize a book in an hour, and he says he reads during the rest he takes with the women he romances. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L680,L730)
- Ratici is a [Thief] who can produce small objects such as a ketchup cup from nowhere and handle spells such as butterfly illusions. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L703,L729-730)
- Both were born in Invrisil, which is rare for a Drake and Gnoll among Humans. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L707)
- Wilovan speaks in measured formal tones while Ratici speaks faster and uses fanciful words. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L673)
- They wear hats, a floppy one for Ratici's neck-spines and a tall one for Wilovan. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L709)
## Code
- Wilovan says the Brothers follow a code in which they prey on those with coin, never on those down on their luck, children or decent sorts. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L274)
- Ratici warns Typhenous not to make them take off their hats, especially not with innocent children about. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L733)
- Ratici says the Brothers do not stand for kidnapping or anything that would hurt a child. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L315)
- Wilovan tells Typhenous that calling in a debt in the most final way is not their style and that repayment takes many forms. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L731)
- A Brother of Serendipitous Meetings who has just faced the Goblins says that if the Gentlemen Callers had taken the case it would not have ended as it did. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L974)
## Debt and the contract
- {event} In the inn, Wilovan and Ratici confront Typhenous, saying a debt is owed to the Brothers and that their Nose had learned he might have the answers. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L711-725)
- {event} Typhenous says he acted to prevent an inn and city from being sacked and to let a young innkeeper, her child and her friends escape, and that he gave their friends fair warning though Goblins were a threat. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L740-744)
- Wilovan says the Brothers might accept that, but Ratici says a debt remains, and they lean forward to collect. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L747-755)
- Wilovan and Ratici discuss taking a contract from the Tall Man, who pays well and is near their home, and they came north by Pallass's door. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L698-706)
- The Titan of Baleros receives a note that the Gentlemen Callers are in Liscor and awaiting further instructions. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L212-214)
- Niers, the Titan, complains that the Gentleman Callers are not being cooperative, when Foliana suggests paying the Brothers to find out more. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L127-128)
## Guarding Erin
- The Gentlemen Callers accepted the Tallman of Baleros' invitation to guard the inn, and learned the problem was Erin Solstice herself. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L75)
- The two Gentlemen Callers spend their days in the inn, where they are taken with the new games Erin introduces and earn a reputation as two of the best dart-board champions. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L105)
- {reveal} Wilovan and Ratici tell Erin they were hired to look out for her, call themselves a [Thug] and a [Thief], and do not name their client. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L267-269,L282-286)
- {reveal} The Callers also admit they were in Invrisil when the mob came, and Erin realizes they protected her. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L327)
- {reveal} The Callers say they stopped unscrupulous sorts from kidnapping a little Gnoll child after Bearclaw's gang was found unconscious. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L308-315)
- The Callers say they were not able to stop the beating of Relc. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L308-311)
- The Gentlemen Callers took off their hats while listening to Erin tell Numbtongue why she sought mercy for the Raskghar, then replaced them and bowed. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L1390,L1407-1409)
