# House Byres
> Type: org | Also called: Byres family | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00H
> See also: Five Families, House Reinhart
## Overview
- Yvlon's steel-and-silver alloy armour, sword and shield are a Byres specialty, with enough silver to ward off some monsters and aid beneficial magics. (Book 2, Ch2.00H L44)
- {rel} Yvlon said House Byres has always supported the Reinharts, and she calls Magnolia Reinhart "Aunt Magnolia," a close friend of her mother's. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L312)
- Ylawes told Yvlon that House Byres sent reinforcements to Lord Tyrion's levy and a smaller group to Lady Reinhart, since several assassinations of [Ladies] tied to Magnolia had called her authority into question. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L310,L316)
- Fierre said the Byres family are famous vampire slayers, as are the Veltras and Terland families. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L286)
## Traditions
- Yvlon said House Byres used to slay Vampires and, since they are dead, that she and a few other houses only keep up the traditions. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L741)
- Those pledged to House Byres traditionally tithed some of their earnings at a pedestal beneath a mural of battle between silver-tipped figures and beasts. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L742-744)
- Yvlon said the offering of silver nuggets is for the Silver Dragon Yderigrisel, whom House Byres befriended long ago; it returned during the Creler wars to fight them and died, the greatest tragedy of House Byres. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L1064-1071)
- Members of House Byres drop a tiny amount of silver dust into each well they pass, as a tradition for good luck, warding the land and scaring away some monsters; too much causes silver poisoning that turns the skin blue-grey. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L1088-1096)
