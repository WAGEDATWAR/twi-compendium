# Liscor's army
> Type: org | Also called: Liscorian army; Liscor army | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.23
> See also: Liscor Watch
## Overview
- {belief} Relc said the Liscorian army fights battles for other nations for pay, fielding two thousand Drakes and a few hundred Gnolls at any given moment with an average Level of 16, against 8 in other armies. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L162-165)
- Klbkch cautioned that Levels cannot replace tactics, numbers or equipment, but said the army's strength deters larger forces and lets it fight as a mercenary force without prolonged engagements. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L169)
- Relc said the army is almost always out on some campaign, and thought it had last been in the east near the Walled City. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L172)
## Relations with the city
- Liscor's army has never had a good relationship with the city; it brings chaos but does not stay long, so the citizens regard it as a nuisance. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L434)
- Selys said the army is the strictest group Liscor has, hating monsters and the Antinium, and that it refused to go near the city for four years after the treaty with the Antinium following the second Antinium War, returning only twice since. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L573)
## Structure
- Liscor's army has an officer for every five soldiers, which makes the rank of [Sergeant] worthless compared with other armies. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L724)
- Liscor fields a very small force, generally two or three thousand and up to six thousand when actively conscripting, whose strength lies in its officers, who buff the army enough to fight larger forces. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L728)
- Every citizen of Liscor is formally conscripted into the army at birth in a small ceremony mandated by law, though no one has to serve. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L109)
- Because every citizen is technically a soldier, the army can promote many [Sergeants], [Captains], [Lieutenants] and [Wing Commanders] over a few thousand regulars, turning low-level [Soldiers] into a force fighting under many Skills at once. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L110-113)
- General Sserys held the Antinium at Shivering Falls Pass in the first Antinium War using this approach. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L113)
- Olesm reflects that the army is not invincible: it has suffered defeats, and the Second Antinium War and Az'kerash's assault showed how weak it was against an enemy with powerful magical support. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L114)
