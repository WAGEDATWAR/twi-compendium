# Assassin's Guild
> Type: org | Also called: Guild of Assassins; Assassins' Guild | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.41
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Circle of Thorns, Lacel the Leaper, Tyrion Veltras
## Overview
- Magnolia holds an [Assassin] named Theofore captive, and notes that the Guild's magical wards prevent him from speaking about it. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L238,L242)
- Magnolia says her contract with the Assassin's Guild had not expired when two of its [Assassins] lay dead in her room. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L253-254)
- The Assassin's Guild in Izril is described as quite competent, and a man posing as a Guardsman warns Ryoka that a word to a Lord or Lady of the Wall might set it on Erin. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L295)
- Lady Zekyria says Rhir has no Assassin's Guild of its own because infighting of that sort is too harmful, and Lord Yebior suggests contracting the Assassin's Guild on another continent. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L70,L72)
- {belief} A history of the Second Antinium War says the Assassin's Guild in the south of Izril treated the Silent Antinium as a threat to its way of life and put a universal contract on the Antinium's assassination insects, starting a quiet war in the shadows. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L152)
- {belief} The same history says Magnolia Reinhart negotiated with the Assassin's Guild during the war so that her life and the Crown of Flowers would not be targeted, her first deal with the Guild, and that she employed [Assassins] widely afterwards. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L313)
## Size and ranks
- Rookie recruits are the lowest rank of the Assassin's Guild, which numbers in the thousands, and possibly tens of thousands if it still has bases in southern Izril as rumoured. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L676)
- A [Chevalier] judges the first assassins to attack Bethal's party as the first tier of Guild recruits, well armed because they were paid in advance. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L278)
- The Assassin's Guild is expensive even for the richest [Merchants], and Ressa guesses the Ladies' attackers cost tens of thousands of gold. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L715)
- At one attack on Couriers, the Guild sends its Faces rather than Ranks or expendable [Assassins], five in all. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L186-188)
## Attacks on the Ladies
- {event} The Assassin's Guild sends nearly a hundred of its weakest recruits against the Ladies as a message, and they die to guards and the Ladies' perfume and fans. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L674-676)
- Magnolia says she does not have the Assassin's Guild at her back, and that if she moved openly to destroy a Guild free to choose its targets she would have to kill them all. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L408,L415)
- {state} Magnolia reads the attack as the Guild's message that assassinations are open game and that it is not under her control, and she says Izril is now officially divided into sides. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L717-719)
## Role in Book 16
- {event} An [Assassin] disguised as the inn's bartender breaks Lacel the Leaper's legs with a hammer, saying the Guild has a contract on him and that Magnolia Reinhart holds no sway over the Guild any longer. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1216-1225)
- {event} The [Assassin] traces a sigil of the Guild in the air, and Typhenous and Eltistiman recognise it and lower their weapons. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1258-1260)
- {belief} The [Assassin] says this is a public statement and that the Guild is not abiding by Reinhart's laws. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1258,L1262)
- Typhenous says the Guild accepted the bounty on Lacel to prove it will take contracts on Runners and operate in Invrisil, and that it used to be under Magnolia's control or at least she dictated whom it avoided. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1270,L1273)
- Hawk learns that the Guild accepting contracts on Runners, in Magnolia Reinhart's own city, is known across Izril. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L455)
- The Couriers reason that the Guild will leave them alone once they deliver the amulet, but that making the matter personal would be war. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L584)
## The Circle of Thorns
- In Lady Ieka's view, the Circle of Thorns stole the Assassin's Guild back from Magnolia and used it to murder some of Magnolia's fellow Ladies. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L59)
- Tyrion Veltras knows Magnolia has lost control of the Assassin's Guild. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L123)
- {reveal} An [Assassin] tells Tyrion that the Assassin's Guild belongs to the Circle of Thorns and is only one part of it. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L125,L128)
- The Courier incident makes people aware that the Assassin's Guild is no longer Magnolia's. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L60)
- A Guild [Assassin] with a mask threatens Tyrion Veltras, saying the Guild will burn down a city if it must, since his sons are already poisoned. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L13-14,L19)
