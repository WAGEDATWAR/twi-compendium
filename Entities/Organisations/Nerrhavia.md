# Nerrhavia
> Type: org | Also called: Nerrhavia's Fallen; Nerrhavia's Fall; Nerrhavian; the Kingdom of Nerrhavia's Fall | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.14K
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Tiqr, Yisame, String People, Reim, Thelican
## Overview
- Nerrhavia's Fallen is one of the largest nations of Chandrar, a kingdom of String People made of silk, and the most powerful, wealthiest and most populated nation at the summit in Pomle. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L443-444)
- Queen Yisame rules Nerrhavia. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L445)
- Flos says Nerrhavia is not weak but that its ruler is not prone to war, lazy as rulers go or not as hateful of him as some nations. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L488)
- {belief} Flos says the land was once smaller String People states that unified about six thousand years earlier into an empire that ruled for thousands of years until it was overthrown into the Kingdom of Nerrhavia's Fall. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L488-490)
- Silk is manufactured only in nations like Nerrhavia and is an expensive fabric, and all of Yisame's escort is made of it. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L447)
- Nerrhavia is described as among the great String-folk powers, ahead of the newer Empire of Sands. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L20)
## Army and caste
- The Nerrhavian army arrives in the war on Tiqr with thousands of chariots driven by Stitch-warriors of silk, and Hemp foot-soldiers marching behind who have rough skin. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L238,L251)
- Silk officers shout insults at the Hemp soldiers, and Femithain explains that String-people society is caste-based. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L251,L254)
- The army that Nerrhavia sends into Tiqr is two hundred thousand strong, and the army is led by General Thelican. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L179,L191,L237,L344)
- General Thelican's forces include dreaded chariots, Hemp foot-soldiers and Stitch-mages who do not use fire, and String-people fear fire. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L346-347,L352)
- Octavia, who studied in Nerrhavia, says that learning there can involve sexual exchanges with masters and that it is mostly Silk-castes who get offered them. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L250,L254)
- Saliss recalls honor-duels between [Alchemists] in the streets of Nerrhavia, and [Alchemists] there usually steal each other's work. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L558,L627)
## History with Reim
- Queen Yisame says Nerrhavia sent no army against Reim with the coalition because she did not care to, and that Nerrhavia does not fear the King of Destruction. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L481-483)
- Yisame says Nerrhavia is an enemy of Reim, or at least no friend. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L496)
- Yisame says the only thing that has roused her ire is Flos's demands of her nation, in which he provoked her response. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L483)
- At the summit, after Nsiia walks away from the other monarchs, a String-person in Nerrhavia's delegation draws his sword, calls to arms for Yisame's honor and charges Nsiia. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L521-524)
- Twice, forces of Reim clash with Nerrhavia on its border in accidental skirmishes, and the kingdom repulses Flos both times even though it got the worst of them. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L425)
## War on Tiqr
- {event} Nerrhavia's Fallen, the Illivere League, Savere and six other nations declare war on Tiqr. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L618)
- On the twenty-seventh day of the war, Pisces calls it a Nerrhavian-led Coalition, with Nerrhavia the largest power by far. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L117,L120)
- A report lists standing forces of at least twelve thousand on Nerrhavia's border, and Nerrhavia reinforces its lines twice until it has over ten times Mars's number of about a thousand. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L292,L298)
- Nerrhavia and Savere lead a joint army that meets Tiqr at the Reen Plains. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L320)
- Thelican says Nerrhavia was a slave-state, in a conversation about turning Tiqr into one. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L165-170)
## Wars of Reim and Jecrass
- Queen Yisame sends a gift of around three hundred thousand gold and mercenaries, perhaps forty thousand strong, to Raelt of Jecrass. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1170-1173)
- Yisame declares war again on Belchan, and she and Nerrhavia are horrified by Belchan's atrocities. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L3,L5)
- Nerrhavia is described as having armies beyond compare, and as marching on Belchan from the south for a piece of the nation. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L456,L458)
- {event} Nerrhavia's Fallen declares war on Reim, as do the Claiven Earth, Savere and a dozen other nations. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L1206)
