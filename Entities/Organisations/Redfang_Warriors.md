# Redfang Warriors
> Type: org | Also called: Redfang band; Redfang Goblins | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.17T
> See also: Redfang Tribe, Redfang Hobs, Garen Redfang, Rags, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- The Redfang band that Garen Redfang sends after Erin is thirteen elite Goblin warriors, each over Level 10 in a combat class and led by a Hobgoblin. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L115-117)
- The Hobgoblin leader is called Grunter. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L143)
- The band includes Badarrow, Headscratcher, Bugear and Rocksoup. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L146,L150-151)
- Garen Redfang himself gave the band its task: to kill a Human, whom they come to suspect is the [Innkeeper] who is friends with Rags. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L121,L148)

## Book 6: the survivors
- {state} Six Redfang warriors survive: Rabbiteater, Bugear, Numbtongue, Shorthilt, Badarrow and Headscratcher, who are Hobgoblins. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L195,L214-216)
- {state} Each survivor has grown abnormally over a week, nearly doubling in height, and they think losing Grunter and the ferocity of the battle triggered the change. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L217,L233-234)
- {state} Their dead companions are Grunter, Rocksoup, Leftstep, Bitefly, Orangepoo, Patchhelm and Justrust. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L223)
- {belief} The warriors think Garen Redfang is wrong and an idiot, and that Rags is their rightful Chieftain. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L229)
- Redfang warriors do not weep for the fallen because Garen Redfang does not believe in it. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L114)
## Book 7: at the inn
- The Redfang Warriors live in the inn's basement, and Headscratcher and Shorthilt take trays down to them, staying out of sight of guests. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L47-48)
- Erin says the Goblins are innocent, harm no one and saved her from Eater Goats. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L21-24)
- Erin moves the Redfang Goblins up to rooms on the third floor. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L167)
- The Hobs wear white armbands marked "Security" as the inn's guards. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L216)
