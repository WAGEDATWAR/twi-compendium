# Liscor Watch
> Type: org | Also called: City Watch; Watch; Liscor City Watch | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.10
> See also: Liscor's army, Adventurer's Guild
## Overview
- The Liscor Watch is the city watch of Liscor, whose guardsmen are not mercenaries or bodyguards but are voted in by the citizens. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L240)
- A Liscor guardsman needs at least fifty ordinary citizens to vouch for them before being sworn in, and a Senior Guardsman needs at least four hundred. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L242)
- The Watch badge is crimson edged in gold and striped twice with purple. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L243)
- Guardsmen must carry their badge at all times and pay a fine if they lose it; Relc keeps his on his belt because it does not stick to his scales. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L245-247)
## Strength and organisation
- {belief} From Rags's point of view, Liscor's Watch numbers nearly a hundred guardsmen, though some are always on the walls and many stay inside the city. (Book 2, Ch2.01G L282)
- {belief} Rags judges that the Watch's patrols are stronger than Goblins, and that the Watch fights together with discipline and can defend easily and sally out in force. (Book 2, Ch2.01G L282-283)
- Olesm reflects that a thousand members of Liscor's Watch and part of Liscor's army had shown in the dungeon against the Raskghar that low-level recruits could take on threats far above their level. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L107)
## Duties and incidents
- Watch Captain Zevara leads the Watch. (Book 2, Ch2.19 L233)
- {belief} A speaker said that Relc, a former soldier turned guardsman, was the reason Liscor had had no monster attacks for the last four years. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L157)
- {event} Relc told Erin that no magic had been detected at the places the thief stole from, so the Watch thought the thief was probably just a human. (Book 1, Ch1.31 L390)
- {belief} A speaker told Erin and Pisces that the Watch would no longer patrol around the inn, because the Captain refused to patrol that area after what happened with Klbkch. (Book 1, Ch1.28 L37)
- {event} When a crowd of Gnolls and Drakes gathered in front of the Watch barracks, Captain Zevara, Relc and Klbkch stood at the entrance and refused entry, saying they were holding a Human until a trial could be set up the next day. (Book 2, Ch2.19 L231-236)
- {event} Culling Shield Spider nests on the Floodplains is a yearly tradition of Liscor's City Watch, and 4th Company took part in it with nearly a thousand Drakes and Gnolls in total. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L26-27)
- {event} Guardsman Beilmark and Senior Guardsman Jeiss spotted a huge Wyvern flight over Liscor, and Beilmark ordered a runner sent to Watch Captain Zevara rather than sound the alarm. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L26-34)
- {event} Zevara came at a run with Wing Commander Embria and watched the Wyverns pass, then ordered priority messages sent to every city south of Liscor and to [Strategist] Olesm. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L35,L57)
