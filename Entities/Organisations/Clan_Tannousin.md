# Clan Tannousin
> Type: org | Also called: Tannousin clan | First appears: Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos
> See also: Nawalishifra Tannousin, Orthenon, Reim, Naq-Alrama steel
## Overview
- Nawalishifra Tannousin sells ingots of Naq-Alrama steel forged in her clan's forges, which she insists are magic and enchantable. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L309-310)
- She says the metal must be smithed under the moonlight until quenching, and must be struck precisely while hot. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L321-323)
- She says the steel is kept from any sunlight, and quenching it in sun-filled waters lets it endure any pressure. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L354)
## The caravan to Reim
- {event} Silmak of Clan Tannousin presents himself to Lord Orthenon of Reim, travelling with thirty-two kin, to honor the oath that Nawalishifra made to the King of Destruction. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L7)
- Silmak names Hesseif, who is tasked with the caravan's defence, his aunt second-removed Bezhavil, who oversees the womenfolk and the caravan, and Nawalishifra, who will forge the Naq-Alrama steel as [Blacksmith]. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L7)
- The men guarding the huge, covered wagon that holds the clan's treasure of Naq-Alrama metal stay standing while the rest kneel. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L8)
- Hesseif vows to guard the metal day and night and let in no one of other blood and no ray of sunlight. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L109-110)
- Silmak's tattooed hands sparkle and light up, and he later speaks as a [Shaman] to Orthenon about the sword that must be sharpened three more times. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L110,L937-938)
