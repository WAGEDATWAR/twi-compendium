# Zel's relatives
> Type: org | Also called: the Shivertail family | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.16S
> See also: Zel Shivertail, Selys Shivertail, Tekshia Shivertail, Zevara
## Overview
- {event} Zel Shivertail's family speaks to Liscor over a scrying orb: an elderly male Drake at the head of a crowd of green-scaled Drakes, a younger woman and others, some richly dressed, one armored and one a [Soldier] off duty. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L475-478)
- A Drake woman says they came to read her brother's will and to see his body. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L481-482)
- {event} Zel's father demands to be shown the body in the open casket; some relatives gasp, stumble or scream, and the children go pale. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L486-487)
- He orders the relatives to stand up straight and not turn away, then tells Zevara to close the casket, saying his son is dead. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L491-494)
- Only the military Drakes among the family can look, and some have never seen a dead body. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L492-493)
- Relatives are outraged at Zel's final request in the will, calling it a scandal and in poor taste. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L545-549)
- {belief} A relative begins to say that they have "a, a Turnscale" in the family before being silenced. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L551-552)
- {event} Xalia, Zel's sister-in-law, claims the will cannot apply to the armor, and she fights her father-in-law for the scrying orb when he orders Zevara to hold the armor. (Book 7, Ch5.17S L26-33)
- {event} Zevara reports a compromise: the funeral will be held in Liscor, with a delegation from Pallass attending, and Zel's ashes will be sent home for burial. (Book 7, Ch5.17S L120-121)
