# Horns of Hammerad
> Type: org | Also called: Horns; Horns of Hammerad (old) | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.08H
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Pisces, Yvlon Byres, Ksmvr, Halfseekers, Griffon Hunt
## Overview
- Ceria is Captain of the Horns of Hammerad, elected in an instant, after the team's earlier leader Calruz. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L239)
- Ceria Springwalker introduces herself in Invrisil as Captain of the Horns of Hammerad, a Gold-rank team. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L690,L809)
## Former members
- {state} Ceria pictures the earlier Horns of Hammerad, Gerial, Calruz, Sostrom, Hunt and Marian, marching beside the present team in a parade. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L386-388)
## Treasure and the Liscor dead
- The Horns hold a magically sealed letter of accreditation that lets them draw coin from the Merchant's Guild. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L274)
- {event} After clearing the dungeon, the Horns plan to give shares of their treasure to the families of those who died in Liscor, as far as the relationships can be confirmed. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L349-352)
- The Horns pulled in nearly a hundred pounds of gold and gems, and forty gold coins are waiting for Erin. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L340,L347)
## Later events
- Erin trusts the Halfseekers, the Horns of Hammerad and Griffon Hunt with news of the enchanted axe, and calls them up to a third-floor room to see it. (Book 9, Ch5.49 L442)
- {event} After a quarrel, Pisces and the other Horns apologise and pledge to stay together; Ceria, Yvlon and Pisces stack their hands and Ksmvr joins. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L1041-1055)
- On the road crew, the Horns travel in a chariot of bone and ice pulled by two undead horses. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L734-735)
- Ceria says Hissle can ride along with the Horns in the chariot, and Reikhle accepts that they cover a mounted team's share of the work. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L759,L774-775)
- {event} In the Gold-rank bar in Invrisil, Captain Todi of Todi's Elites tells Ceria and Pisces to fetch their teammates and buy everyone a round. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L687-702)
- {event} Pisces mocks Todi's seniority, and the room goes quiet. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L742-748)
- {event} Elia Arcsinger, a half-Elf Captain with a silver bow and a Named Adventurer, invites Ceria and Pisces to her table and introduces herself as Captain of Arcsinger's Bows. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L808-811)
