# Council of Liscor
> Type: organisation | Also called: Liscor's Council; the Council | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.44
> See also: Liscor, Olesm, Krshia Silverfang, Zevara
## Overview
- The Council of Liscor is the governing body of Liscor and is described as a body of the heads of the guilds and so on; it normally met in the quiet City Hall. (Book 10, Ch6.11 L274)
- In the attack of the undead, only two of the Antinium Hive's three entrances were known to the Council of Liscor. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L150)
- The Council of Liscor was said to be putting out word of Erin's plan, with Antinium, adventurers, the Watch and Goblins all helping. (Book 9, Ch5.45 L143)
## The election
- After an election that won four districts for Gnolls, the old Council led by the Merchant's Guild head Ulseil schemed to seat only three Gnolls instead of four. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L78,L92-99)
- {event} The old Council cast its last vote unanimously. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L101)
- Krshia Silverfang won the election handily. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L79)
- The new Council includes Krshia, Elirr and Raekea on the Gnoll side and Lism, Jeiss and Alonna as firmly allied Drakes, alongside Tismel and Zalaiss, who side with the old Council. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L519-520)
- The new Council argued all at once, with Krshia, Lism, Jeiss, Alonna, Raekea and Elirr bringing in differing ideas to a room that had only known Drakes. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L908)
- The former Councilmembers convened at City Hall twenty minutes later with last year's Council and earlier Councils, twenty-two people in all, and tried to storm the new Council's room. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L910-911)
## Later
- The Council of Liscor exploded out of their seats when Erin adjusted the door's dial to Invrisil, wanting the insane Human stopped and regulated. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1280-1281)
- The Council of Liscor hired Hexel Quithail, who asked Erin if he and three apprentices could stay at her inn. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1150)
- The Council of Liscor stood on the other side of the door when Pelt announced he was moving to another city. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L267-270)
- A negotiator says they have come to negotiate on behalf of the Council of Liscor with the Free Antinium. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L1093)
