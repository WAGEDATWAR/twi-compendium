# Raverian Fighters
> Type: org | Also called: Ravarian Fighters; Raverian Fighter's Company | First appears: Book 3, Ch1.00D
> See also: Burning March company, Geneva Scala
## Overview
- The Raverian Fighters are a mercenary company that recruits through enlisters in a lakeside city. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L31-36)
- The Raverian Fighters are one of the hired forces, like the larger Burning March company, that push into the jungle to attack the Centaur tribe's goldmine in Baleros. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L16)
## Recruitment and pay
- The [Sergeant] Thriss of the 4th Battalion of the Raverian Fighter's Company offers recruits eight silver coins for each day they see combat and one for each day they do not. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L34-36)
- Thriss also promises a hot meal each night, trustworthy comrades and all the loot a recruit can carry off. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L36)
## The potion shortage
- {event} A night raid by hoofed attackers, which a soldier guesses are the Centaurs hitting them again, smashes the potion chest in the supply wagon. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L428-433,L440)
- Out of more than a hundred potions, the battalion keeps fewer than twenty bottles' worth after the raid. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L440-441)
- With the potions gone, the veterans and officers look to Geneva, a [Doctor], as the only help left. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L442-446)
- Thriss says half-shares of the remaining twenty potions went to officers and veterans, and that the regular soldiers will have Geneva until a new shipment arrives. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L17-18)
- The 4th Battalion has over six hundred soldiers. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L36; Book 3, Ch1.01D L21)
## Aftermath
- {event} The Magehammer Company retreats and cedes the mine rights to the Burning March company, and the Ravarian Fighters reform their shattered battalions. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L456)
- {belief} Among mercenaries, rumours spread of a red cross and a healer-class woman who saves lives and takes no side, and soldiers cling to them as legends. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L457-458)
