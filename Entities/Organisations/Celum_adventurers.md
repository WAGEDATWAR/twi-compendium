# Celum adventurers
> Type: org | Also called: Celum's adventurers | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.03
> See also: Celum Adventurer's Guild, Horns of Hammerad
## Overview
- {belief} Two adventurers at Celum's guild said four adventuring teams died at the Liscor ruins thanks to incompetent Captains, with only a handful surviving, and mocked the Horns of Hammerad's name. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L276-278)
- {belief} One of them guessed the half-Elf had run and was a coward, saying Calruz was the only real fighter in the Horns. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L288)
- {belief} Celum's adventurers told Yvlon they could not prove her team had fought, and suggested she and Ceria ran or got lucky, questioning where the Albez treasure came from and whether it was kept from survivors and the dead. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L656,L659)
