# Wistram team
> Type: org | Also called: Palt's team; Montressa's team; Montressa du Valeross's team | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.57
> See also: Montressa du Valeross, Pisces, Horns, Wistram, Liscor, Zevara
## Overview
- The Wistram team is a group of five full [Mages] from Wistram Academy led by Montressa du Valeross, made up of Palt, Ulinde, Beza and Isceil; all are top of their years. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L549,L719)
- Montressa is an [Aegiscaster] and an expert in wards and protective spells, which gives her a steady, low income. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L182,L206)
- Isceil is an [Oldblood Magus] born with a frost breath from his Dragon heritage, and he treats Oldblood ailments in Liscor. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L190,L213-214)
- Bezale of Maweil is a Minotauress [Spellscribe] and a graduate of Wistram who sells scrolls. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L237,L266)
- Palt is a Centaur [Illusionist] who is also a [Smoker]. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L336,L354)
- Ulinde is a Selphid in a Gnoll body. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L550; Book 14, Ch6.67 L753)
- {belief} Palt says the Revivalists, a Wistram faction, put Montressa in charge of the team. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L811,L816)
- Montressa and Beatrice form a secret-broker duo, and Montressa is fairly high-placed in the Revivalists. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L107)
## Attack in Liscor
- {event} The five Mages come into a Liscor tavern looking for Ceria Springwalker, and Ulinde points her out to Montressa. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L549-552)
- {event} The Mages beat Pisces in the street until he stops moving, and Montressa draws the Silent Box over him; the box later holds the Horns of Hammerad. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L485-498,L773)
- {event} Isceil tells the others that Ceria and her companion are criminals who impersonated real Wistram Mages, and Montressa captures Ceria into the black box. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L699-701)
- Montressa says they are within their rights to hunt fugitives from Wistram, though Isceil says they should have talked to the Watch Captain. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L729-731)
- Palt left Selys Shivertail under a [Mind Blank] with a veil, and it wears off in an hour. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L720-723)
- {event} Zevara declares all five Mages under arrest, calling what they did an attack. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L856)
- {event} The Liscor Watch brings down Palt with batons and clubs before he can utter a spell. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L543-544)
## Release
- {event} Zevara orders the prisoners released as an order straight from the High Command, with their belongings returned. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L16-19)
- {event} Zevara tells the Council that Liscor's High Command, through Wing Commander Embria, instructed her about the treatment of Wistram's team because Liscor's walls and army depend on Mages. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L75-81)
- Zevara says the Wistram team may stay in Liscor under strict surveillance and will be instantly expelled if they cast an illicit spell or threaten a citizen. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L35,L333)
- Selys says she does not want them in Liscor. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L331)
## In Liscor
- Falene says Wistram gave a blanket ban on information about the team, and her friend Bashea assured her they were acting in Wistram's best interests. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L125)
- Dawil tells Ylawes that Miss Springwalker informed them the trouble with the Wistram Mages is dealt with and that Yvlon is fine, while Ylawes says her arms are still damaged. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L110-115)
- The Revivalists' strict orders to Montressa are to make a good impression and charge low fees, since the team is in trouble at the academy. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L205,L210)
- The team does consulting work in Liscor, with Montressa inspecting and repairing wards and Isceil treating Oldbloods. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L178-186,L213)
- Palt warns Erin that Isceil, Beza and Montressa should not be underestimated, and that Isceil can do more damage than a [Siege Fireball]. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L392,L400)
- Palt says Beza can use six enchantments at once, and Montressa's orb artifact can cast [Chain Lightning] on command. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L400)
## The insect swarm and the Crelers
- {event} The Wistram [Mages] link their magic, with Isceil taking the lead, and blast a swarm of insects with Frostspark Galebreath and then [Valmira's Comets], saving the Horns and two Ensoldier Shields. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1028-1042,L1048-1051)
- Isceil says they saved the adventurers because they were not monsters, and Montressa says they did it because they owe Liscor and not Ceria. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1063-1065)
- {state} Isceil dies fighting an adult Creler. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L115,L181)
- {state} After the Creler fight the team buries Isceil. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L368-369,L706)
- {state} Montressa says she is done and Palt stays in Liscor because of his deal, while Beza says they must deliver Isceil's remains to his home. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L740-751)
- {state} Ulinde says she will quit and join the Halfseekers. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L754)
