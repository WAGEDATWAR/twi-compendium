# Goblin Lord's army
> Type: org | Also called: Goblin Lord's army and Flooded Waters tribe (in Book 9) | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.20T
> See also: Reiss's army, Redfang Tribe, Flooded Waters tribe
## Overview
- {event} The Goblin Lord's army pours into Esthelm to the sound of countless drums, some Goblins riding Shield Spiders and others horses. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L128-129)
- Goblin [Shamans] control the undead at the head of the army, which Ylawes judges to be barely a hundred. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L130-131)
- A Goblin riding a massive Shield Spider hacks through Humans with a halberd that cuts through armor, and Ylawes challenges him. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L185)
## Book 6
- The Goblin Lord's army sweeps across the land like a horde of green locusts, burning and destroying anything without walls. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L310)
- Reynold estimates the advance force, which fills the horizon heading north, at tens of thousands of Goblins. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L316)
- {event} Goblins riding Shield Spiders charge a column of fleeing villagers, with a whooping mass of Goblins behind them. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L317)
- A Goblin with black eyes and white pupils raises a hand as black magic swirls around him. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L338)
- The Goblin Lord rides a massive Shield Spider larger than a warhorse at the head of the army, and many ordinary Goblins ride large Shield Spiders too. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L177)
- The army has taken every horse, pony, mule and donkey it could grab, and excess wagons and carts are pulled by teams of six Hobs. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L178)
- The Goblin Lord has thousands of Hobs in the best armor looted from other soldiers, forming a disciplined core, plus regular Goblins in black armor. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L261)
- Osthia judges that a regular Drake army with a competent [General] would beat the army were it not for the undead, which double its size. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L261-262)
- The undead can dig themselves into the ground to ambush and can send exploding Goblin corpses to tear holes in an enemy line. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L262)
