# Bloodfeast Raiders
> Type: org | Also called: Bloodfeast | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.00
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Celum, Mihaela Godfrey, Roshal
## Overview
- The Bloodfeast Raiders are Izril's most feared [Raider] group; they strike north and south with no discernible pattern, have sacked Drake cities and turned whole Human towns to ash. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L50)
- Nobody knows who the members are and no Bloodfeast Raider has ever been caught alive. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L50)
- The members are all high-level and armed with powerful artifacts, and the bounties on them total hundreds of thousands of gold pieces without any of them being caught. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L51)
- The group is described as new, and the Bloodtear Pirates have a standing objection to it taking their name. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L52)
- The Bloodfeast Raiders have connections to Roshal, so that no one would catch a member, and they have an hour to wreak havoc. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L191)
## Early reputation
- A Runner's Guild notice warns that the Bloodfeast Raiders were reported operating out of the Gilliel Swamplands area two months earlier, though they had moved on. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L101)
- Seeing a burnt village, a traveller asks whether the Bloodfeast Raiders hit another place. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L904-906)
- Local bandits copying notorious groups do not dare call themselves the Bloodfeast Raiders, since the real groups are nasty to imitators. (Book 11, Ch6.19H L406-408)
- {belief} One of the Horns recalls the bounty on the Bloodfeast Raiders as four hundred thousand gold pieces if the whole group or most of it is destroyed, eighty thousand for credible information on members or methods, and twenty thousand per head on top. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L534-535)
## Attack on Celum
- {event} Masked marauders with a bloody mark on their masks burn a carriage with a [Fireball] near Celum and surround Ryoka Griffin, who recognises the Bloodfeast Raiders. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1302-1307)
- The Raiders wear dark clothing and masks with a bloody sigil, and some ride horses, one runs as fast as a Courier, and two ride a carpet. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L32-33)
- {reveal} Ryoka cannot hold on to how many Raiders there are or who they are, because each piece of knowledge about their identities fades from her mind as she looks. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L34-38,L47)
- {reveal} The carpet-rider with a [Fireball] wand is the person Ryoka saw in a scrying orb and Fierre had pointed out. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L46)
- A Raider plucks Ryoka's iPhone from her belt, and the Raiders find it expensive-looking but not magical. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L65-69)
- The Raiders' leader gives Ryoka a head start of five minutes to warn Celum, and a Raider reads a scroll to remove the rain. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L103-116)
- The Raiders use [Fireball] wands, a flying carpet, a bow, daggers and a spear, and a Raider's scroll of [Clear Weather] is said to be hard to get in pairs. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L46,L93,L112,L180)
- {event} The Raiders hold a contest for the most kills, with the iPhone as the prize, and agree that kills count if they all see them or if heads are taken. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L168-178)
- {event} The Raiders destroy Celum for nearly an hour and leave, and reinforcements from other cities arrive only after they are convinced the Raiders have gone. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L282)
- The Raiders ransack the Runner's Guild in Celum, taking packages and strolling out. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L295)
- The Raiders celebrate afterward with captives and loot, and the Raider who killed the most people wins the iPhone. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L399-400)
- {reveal} The Raiders had always planned to meet at Celum and sack it, and the iPhone only attracted their attention to Ryoka. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L411-412)
- {event} Watch Captain Zevara of Liscor says Liscor's Council will support Celum and investigate claims that a Drake was among the Bloodfeast Raiders. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L1051)
## Later plan near Reizmelt
- {event} Eleven Raiders gather twenty miles outside Reizmelt, with two of them the same as last time, and are told to take Ryoka alive because two members are interested in her. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L478-486)
- {event} The oldest Raider receives a [Message] that Mihaela Godfrey is in Reizmelt, cancels the Bloodfeast, and puts a knife to the angry carpet-rider's throat when he objects. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L487-497)
- The Raiders avoid people such as Tyrion Veltras or a Named Rank team or adventurer, and leave Reizmelt without killing anyone. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L501)
