# Serpent Hunters of Clais
> Type: org | Also called: Serpent Hunters | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.06KM
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Parasol Stroll, Reim's army at Jecrass border, Khelt army, Nerrhavia
## Overview
- The Serpent Hunters of Clais are a band of String People mercenaries in Flos's army, noted for envenomed blades. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L249)
- Gazi names the Serpent Hunters and Parasol Stroll as part of the army that is ready when she says Flos will go to war. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L207)
- The Serpent Hunters, a band of Stringfolk warrior-mercenaries, and Parasol Stroll joined Flos's kingdom during the winter and were chosen to follow him wherever he went. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L85)
## Leader and class
- Jelaim leads the Serpent Hunters. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L254)
- Jelaim says he is a [Serpent Hunter], a class that puts poison on weapons, and that the group was built from that class. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L290)
- Jelaim says he is a mercenary captain but has none of the soldier classes that go with the title, so he asks to be called Serpent Hunter Jelaim or Hunter Jelaim. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L286)
- Jelaim says the Serpent Hunters carry enchanted blades that are poisoned in their sheaths. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L255)
## Recruitment of String People
- The Serpent Hunters have been in Reim for months by the time of Flos's banquet. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L251)
- Jelaim says Flos wants them to take any of the String Folk into their ranks and teach them, so dozens of recruits have already joined. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L293)
- Jelaim expects hundreds or thousands of recruits if Flos claims a land filled with String Folk. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L293)
- Jelaim says they train only String People out of practicality and not malice, because String People can remove a limb before poison spreads and cloth cannot be hurt by it. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L294-296)
## Display at the banquet
- {event} At Flos's banquet, eight Serpent Hunters with curved black-venom daggers juggle their blades across the hall in patterns. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L105-107)
- {event} Jelaim walks down the hall while his eight warriors throw daggers past him, then duels two of them on a table with real envenomed blades. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L108-113)
## Wars of Reim
- {event} Flos orders Gazi to take the Serpent Hunters and Parasol Stroll, with as many soldiers as she needs, to join Mars against the Khelt army on the western border. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L501)
- {event} In the fight against Khelt's zombies, the Serpent Hunters slash and whirl with poison-coated blades while Parasol Stroll holds the high ground. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L747)
- Raelt of Jecrass recognises the Serpent Hunters among Reim's army as a company famed for its use of venom, a laughing army of String-folk with serpents and bright poison colours etched on their armour and skin. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L441-442)
- Jelaim is among the vassals who follow Flos in his camp. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L255)
- Flos counts the Serpent Hunters and Parasol Stroll as two of four divisions of elites in his army. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L496)
- Jecrass's army sees the laughing Serpent Hunters, armed with poison blades, on one flank of Reim's army. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L123)
