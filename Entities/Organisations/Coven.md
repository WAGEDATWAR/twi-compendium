# Coven
> Type: org | Also called: the coven; Witches' coven; Marshlands Coven (the older coven) | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.34E
> See also: Belavierr, Wiskeria, Califor, Mavika, Laken Godart, Riverfarm, Circle of Thorns
## Overview
- Wiskeria says a coven is a group of [Witches] that meets every few months, and that it is part of the [Witch] class even though most classes have nothing like it. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L547)
- Wiskeria says a [Witch] generally pursues her own craft and meets her coven a few times a month at most to trade news, warnings and goods. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L541)
- Wiskeria says Humans make up most [Witch] covens. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L770)
- Wiskeria says covens can be very large and that Mavika's coven covers all the [Witches] within about three hundred miles, the Marshlands Coven. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L773)
- Wiskeria says the Marshlands Coven is old, with members coming and going, and has been in Izril since Humans first came or earlier. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L775)
- The [Witches] meet when the two moons are full, which is not every month, and Wiskeria says the next meeting is in six days. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L623-626)
## Pressure on Wiskeria
- Wiskeria tells Durene that Mavika is insisting that she join the coven, though Wiskeria says she does not have to. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L542,L561)
- Wiskeria says Mavika will not force her again, now that Mavika has lost two of her crows. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L629)
- {event} Wiskeria refuses a City Runner's demand for a yes, telling him to tell Witch Oliyaya she will not attend her coven's meeting and that she has told Mavika the same. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L767-780)
## The Marshlands Coven and the new coven
- {event} Califor says the Marshlands Coven was called upon by strangers a fortnight earlier, met them without Mavika, refused an offer, and died. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L655)
- Califor says Mavika took vengeance on those who bloodied their blades but not on those who made the offer. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L655)
- Califor says a ritual was cast on the dead coven's hats, and a hat was sent to each of six [Witches] to form a new coven in Riverfarm. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L657)
- When the coven arrives in Riverfarm, Mavika tells Wiskeria it is not her coven, that there are six, and that the seventh is coming. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L476-479)
- Mavika later says a coven was convened as seven, with Nanette as the apprentice and Wiskeria as the last. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L631)
- {event} The coven calls on Wiskeria to join it, claiming the rights of travellers and sanctuary in Riverfarm until Emperor Laken can meet them over an offer and a bargain. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L659)
- {event} Wiskeria accepts the coven after receiving a hat. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L666-667)
- At the coven's first meeting in Riverfarm, six Witches besides Wiskeria are in the room, with Nanette already in bed; they sit in Califor's home with tea and biscuits. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L306,L310,L312)
- Alevica is Oliyaya's representative and apprentice in the coven. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L452)
- Califor leads the coven for now. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L394-395,L452)
- Prost gives the Witches newer houses without payment, and they take lodgings in Riverfarm before talking. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L307-308)
## Purpose
- Belavierr says the coven is united in purpose, namely sanctuary for [Witches]. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L386)
- {event} Califor says the coven wants sanctuary for all [Witches] on Laken's lands and an alliance, because it represents the interest of all Izril's covens and the decision is not just some [Witches]'. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L401,L411)
- Eloise says that if Laken needs them and a deal is struck, he would be the first noble or city in an age to openly ally with their kind. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L408)
- Wiskeria asks why all of them came when a letter would do, and Califor answers that they represent what Laken stands to gain and must prove their value. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L409-411)
## At Riverfarm
- Fierre's notes list the visiting coven as Mavika, Eloise, Wiskeria, Hedag, Alevica, Nanette and Califor. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L687)
- {event} On Day 76 the six remaining [Witches] meet outside the throne room, with Califor gone and Belavierr absent. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L735-741,L747)
- {event} Eloise, speaking for all Izril's [Witches], asks Laken to let [Witches] practice free of persecution in his lands and to void bounties on [Witches] unless there is ample reason. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L750,L753)
- {event} Laken says [Witches] may roam Riverfarm, practice their craft and come there safe from unwarranted persecution if they keep the basic rules of hospitality, in the name of Califor who saved Riverfarm. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L785,L795)
