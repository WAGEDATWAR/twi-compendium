# Goblin tribe
> Type: org | Also called: Goblin tribe near Liscor | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.15
> See also: Red Fang Tribe, Goblin army
## Overview
- A Goblin tribe of perhaps forty armed Goblins by Erin's guess, smaller than the tribe Erin fled on her first day, stood watching her silently on a hill and did not charge. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L96-98)
- The Goblins followed Erin swiftly without screaming or trying to cut her off, and let her go when she ran for her inn. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L109-110)
- Relc called forty Goblins just the local tribe and said he could take out half of them without breaking a sweat. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L79)
- Klbkch said a Goblin tribe is no threat to any but lone stragglers despite its competence, and that a small raiding party usually has at least three hundred Goblins and larger groups have exceeded a thousand. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L83)
