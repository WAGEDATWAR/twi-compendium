# Players of Celum
> Type: org | Also called: Players of Liscor and Players of Celum; Temile's cast | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.04
> See also: Erin Solstice, Wesle, Jasi, Emme, Temile, Kilkran, Wistram
## Overview
- The Players of Celum are an acting troupe that calls itself by that name for now and owes Erin a debt as its patron and muse. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L121,L126)
- Before performing at the inn they put on plays in Ocre, Remendia and Wales and were thinking of travelling further once they had enough [Actors] for two groups. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L126)
- Kilkran is one of their newer [Actors], a former [Blacksmith] with a booming voice. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L145)
## Book 7 to 9
- Pralcem plays Sir Macduff, and a fan praises a performance of Hamlet. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L99)
- The troupe left Miss Agnes' inn on bad terms. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L196)
- {state} The Players of Celum announce they will perform exclusively at The Wandering Inn, guarded by Hobgoblins and an Antinium. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L262)
- The [Actors] sell autographs, and Emme, a half-Dwarf woman, is among them. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L123-125)
- {event} The troupe performs Romeo and Juliet as Juliet and Romeo with Wesle and Jasi, and Wall Lord Ilvriss' outrage at a Drake marrying a Human sparks a riot between Drakes and Humans. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L531-532,L583-593)
- Wesle's class gives him a Skill, [Method Acting], that makes him become the person he plays, about as good as someone with ten levels. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L396-399)
- Wesle, Emme and Kilkran, the former [Blacksmith], are among the Players at a baseball game with Erin's guests. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L339,L347,L436)
- {event} The Players say they have not performed for a week because other inns are smearing Erin's name. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L386-388)
## Lellisdam and the road
- {state} Lellisdam's Mayor offers gold and an escort, and the troupe, now over sixty, leaves for the city eighty miles north; Temile, Damius and Rima stay with new recruits. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L218,L226-234)
- Jasi leaves a letter for Erin explaining the departure. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L234)
- {event} The Players perform Romeo and Juliet through the door, with Temile as Benvolio, and later Hamlet. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L606-611,L1000)
- The troupe is over forty strong, with two teams of [Actors], and is travelling north performing in every town. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L44,L100)
- {event} The Players discover that another group in Invrisil is performing their plays, including Hamlet, Othello and Macbeth. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L593,L639)
- Relc has seen Macbeth about thirty times and is on a backup list for the troupe. (Book 15, ChStory_1_Mrsha_and_Relc L22-24)
- {state} Hearing the Wandering Inn is open again, Temile pulls the Players out of Blazehound to rejoin their Liscor [Actors]; he is their [Stage Manager]. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L566,L570,L574)
