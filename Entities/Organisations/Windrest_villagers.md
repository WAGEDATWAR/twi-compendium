# Windrest villagers
> Type: org | Also called: people of Windrest | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.20E
> See also: Riverfarm, Laken Godart, Goblin Lord's army
## Overview
- Windrest is a village about fourteen miles east of Riverfarm, near a big prairie. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L377)
- {event} A group of around sixty Goblins hits Windrest, slaughters a dozen people and torches buildings, and a man from the village asks Laken for adventurers to guard them. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L380,L383)
- {event} Laken promises to send an escort to Windrest. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L395)
- {event} The entire village's worth of people arrives at Riverfarm with donkey-drawn wagons and overloaded carts, doubling its population. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L83-84)
- The Windrest folk bring their tools, including good [Carpenters] and [Builders], and one man among them is a [Blacksmith] with his anvil. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L103)
