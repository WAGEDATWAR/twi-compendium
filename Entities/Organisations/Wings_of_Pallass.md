# Wings of Pallass
> Type: org | Also called: Bevussa's team | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.36
> See also: Bevussa Slenderscale, Flamewardens, Pallass
## Overview
- The Wings of Pallass is led by Bevussa, a feathered Garuda and Gold-rank Captain, with Zassil, Issa and Kin. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L129,L137)
- Zassil is a winged Oldblood Drake and a [Battle Flier], and Kin is the team's [Mage]. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L132,L137)
- Bevussa asks that in public Issa be called the Captain, though she is the one actually leading. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L38; Book 8, Ch5.43 L100)
## Book 8 to 9
- {event} After Xess leaves, Zassil blames the Scalers' botched ambush, and Bevussa slaps him on the back of the head, forgetting she is not to act as captain in public. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L98-101)
- {event} Bevussa shouts "Wings of Pallass! On me!" and three others fly through the door above the ground and dive on the Redfang tribe, striking four Goblins from behind. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L26-28)
- {event} Redfang riders on Carn Wolves leap at the diving fliers, and a Goblin seizes onto Zassil until Bevussa hurls him off. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L124-128)
## Book 13 to 15
- {event} Bevussa says the team is sticking with the dungeon rather than taking the Council's new missions, because the real gold is there. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L41,L57)
- Liscor's bounties pay two gold coins for an adult Blankipillar's venom sac. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L58)
- The Wings drink Erin's foul Scale Soup, which toughens skin like leather, and Bevussa notes it costs a fraction of a Stoneskin Scroll like the one they bought from a Minotauress. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L133,L137,L147-148)
- Bevussa refuses to pay for the Heartflame Breastplate, which Keldrass is renting from Miss Shivertail for the Flamewardens. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L139-144)
- {event} Bevussa, Zassil, Issa and Kin rescue a young child from a frost Wyvern in the street in front of the inn: Issa pulls the child clear while Kin raises a section of street with [Pillar of Earth]. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L254-262)
- Bevussa uses [Mach Wings], [Instantaneous Redirection] and [Sundering Slash] in the fight. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L300-306)
