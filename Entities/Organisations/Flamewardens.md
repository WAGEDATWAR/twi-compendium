# Flamewardens
> Type: org | Also called: Flamewardens of Pallass | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.36
> See also: Keldrass, Heartflame Breastplate, Wings of Pallass
## Overview
- The Flamewardens are a Gold-rank team of Oldblood Drakes from Pallass, led by Keldrass, a huge Drake in plate armor who coughs smoke or blue flames when he speaks. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L45; Book 8, Ch5.40 L264)
- Keldrass states they will enter the dungeon at daybreak, because if the Raskghar are strongest at night, adventurers must move by day. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L50)
## Fire breath
- {event} Four Flamewardens, Keldrass and Nautia among them, line up and breathe fire together, melting the front row of charging armor into black slag. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L264-267,L276-277)
- Bevussa says the Flamewardens have firepower but no stamina, and after the breath they are unable to breathe for a while, which is why they are not a Named team. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L272-273,L284)
- Keldrass insists they can rest half an hour and burn another tunnel. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L281)
- {event} At the door, Keldrass and his Flamewardens take aim to burn Garen, but a crossbow bolt strikes Keldrass' helmet and the others have to shield themselves, unable to use their breath. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L68-72)
- {event} During the same fight, Keldrass carries a wounded teammate back to the door as Halrac orders a fall back. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L190)
## Equipment
- Keldrass pays to rent the Heartflame Breastplate from Miss Shivertail, so the Flamewardens do not need Scale Soup. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L138-141,L144)
- Keldrass has an armor classified as Siegespell Armor, close to Named-rank gear. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L156-158)
- {event} Keldrass holds a corridor against the undead, with his second-in-command wearing the Heartflame Breastplate, and the Gold-ranks send a distress call. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L770-772,L1285)
