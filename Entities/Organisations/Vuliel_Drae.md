# Vuliel Drae
> Type: org | Also called: the lucky team | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.08T
> See also: Liscor's dungeon, Face-Eater Moths, Tekshia Shivertail
## Overview
- Vuliel Drae is a Silver-rank adventuring team that has adventured together for two years. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L331-332)
- The members are Anith, a Jackal leader and [Mage]; Dasha, a Human [Warrior] with Dwarven ancestry and a chestnut beard; Insill, a black-scaled Drake [Rogue]; Larr, an [Archer]; and Pekona, a [Blade Dancer] with a curved sword. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L332)
- Larr is a Gnoll. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L120)
## The first treasure
- Insill says the magical food the [Innkeeper] fed them lets them use only one enchantment at a time, and that it set them for the next eight hours. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L186-188)
- {event} The team ties itself to ropes to climb out of the hole in the ceiling, being the first to explore the dungeon by this shortcut. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L469,L472)
- {event} The team comes out with a rough map of a new section of dungeon, a monster's head and an enchanted mace, proof that the dungeon has treasure. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L83-86)
- The Mace of Howling, a weapon worthy of a Gold-rank adventurer, sold for thousands of gold, and the team's success reignited fervour for the dungeon. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L42-43)
- {belief} The team says a mysterious masked swordswoman saved them, and swore to it with truth spells. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L56-58)
- Revi calls them the stupid team that got lucky and says eight other parties have gone in and been slaughtered since. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L314-316)
- {belief} The team supposes the masked swordswoman may be a loner or a [Doomseeker]. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L378-383)
## The moth attack
- {reveal} Vuliel Drae is revealed to have been exploring the dungeon the day the Face-Eater Moths attacked and to have smashed a bunch of eggs. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L391-393)
- Dasha says the masked woman smashed eggs first; Pekona says the team hid in a tunnel with the masked adventurer for a day and a half, killing the small moths that came through. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L393,L397)
- Pekona says they had no idea the moths came out of the dungeon. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L397-399)
- {event} Dasha argues that the Goblins' treasures noticed by Vuliel Drae belong to them, and Ylawes grants that a cut is a fair point. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L130-136)
- Tekshia says the Horns and other teams took responsibility and forfeited their possessions, whereas Vuliel Drae hid from the moths they provoked and lied afterward. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L248)
- Tekshia says running away and even incompetence are acceptable, but failing to take responsibility and lying to fellow adventurers and the Guild is the one sin not tolerated. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L254)
## Later
- {event} On the Silver Swords' orders, Vuliel Drae blocks Pisces' way as the Horns try to leave to rescue their Captain. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L118-125)
- Earlia looks away, Nailren shakes his head, and a Pallass Drake says they sometimes have hearing problems about orders, so no one backs Vuliel Drae. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L129-136)
