# Khelt army
> Type: org | Also called: Khelt's army; armies of Khelt; Kheltian undead | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.14K
> See also: Fetohep, Khelt, Flos Reimarch, Serpent Hunters of Clais, Reim's army at Jecrass border
## Overview
- Flos calls Khelt a terrible nation to fight, because its armies are insanely difficult to beat on or near its own lands, cannot be demoralized and do not tire, and Fetohep can bring back more and more. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L460)
- The Khelt army is made up of undead who sprint instead of shuffling or groaning, carrying ancient rusted weapons. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L750)
- The Quarass says Khelt's armies are endless and undying, made of every one of Khelt's own that has ever died. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L788)
- The Quarass says the Khelt army poses little threat to men in armor, and an army of that kind is only a nuisance before Reim's might. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L765,L781)
- The undead of Khelt lie beneath the soil of the barren land around Khelt, ready to fight and be destroyed again and again. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L99-100)
- Kheltian undead rise from the ground to form a barricade protecting Trey Atwood. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L113)
## The twenty thousand zombies
- {event} A [Rider] reports an army of twenty thousand to the west that has crossed Reim's borders at a sprint, armored and without bows. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L442,L457-461)
- Flos says King Fetohep is sending him a message, with a knack for bad timing, and, on learning it is armored and has no bows, says that is typical. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L456,L462)
- Flos orders a [Messenger] to tell Mars to intercept the army and hold the western border with Khelt, and tells Gazi to join Mars with soldiers, the Serpent Hunters and Parasol Stroll, with no casualties. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L501)
- Flos says the twenty thousand are fully expendable because they are just zombies, and that Gazi and Mars can hack them apart with minimal effort if they stay careful. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L509,L512)
- Flos tells Trey he wants to show him Khelt's armies before the trip into Khelt and says they are undead but well in hand. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L739)
- {event} Reim's army, led by Mars and Gazi, fights Khelt's soldiers in a dried valley on Reim's border while Parasol Stroll holds the high ground. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L747)
- Flos says that Fetohep can make zombies run, and that Khelt's only message is this sprint. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L753-755)
- Mars uses [Grand Slash] repeatedly to cut through Khelt's soldiers and Flos fights on foot with a mace and shield. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L770-776)
- {event} The wave is crushed without a single casualty on Reim's side as far as Trey can tell, and then a second army of twenty thousand sprints out of the ground toward the border. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L784-787)
- {belief} The Quarass says four such armies have already fallen to Flos's vassals. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L788)
