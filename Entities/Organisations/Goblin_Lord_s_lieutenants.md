# Goblin Lord's lieutenants
> Type: org | Also called: Goblin lieutenants; former Chieftains | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.46
> See also: Goblin Lord, Snapjaw, Eater of Spears, Eggsnatcher, Reiss's lieutenants
## Overview
- The Goblin Lord's lieutenants are all former Chieftains who once led thousands of their kin into battle. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L78)
- Osthia notes the lieutenants as they file out of the Goblin Lord's tent: Snapjaw, two Goblin [Necromancers], Eggsnatcher on a huge Shield Spider, and a Goblin with no face. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L77)
## Individuals
- Snapjaw is a short Hobgoblin with an unnaturally large head and steel-armor, whose teeth are a fusion of enamel and steel produced by her class. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L48-49)
- Snapjaw has a [Dangersense] that warns her of imminent death. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L266)
- Eater of Spears is a seven-foot Hob whose body looks like a solid wall of muscle, and Osthia has never heard him speak. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L73-74)
- Eater of Spears' tough skin breaks a group of seasoned [Lancers]' weapons. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L262)
- Eggsnatcher is a [Beastmaster] Goblin who rides a Shield Spider as large as the Goblin Lord's mount and commands a horde of spiders. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L77; Book 6, Ch4.48 L263)
- A Goblin called Noface has no face because of an encounter with a Human [Pyromancer], and he blasts Human ranks with fire. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L263,L275)
- Illbreath leads a unit pursuing fleeing Humans. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L265)
## In battle
- {event} Zel Shivertail holds up Noface's severed head to show the Goblins their dead officer, and they hold their ground. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L274-276)
- {event} A pink carriage with a Human [Butler] charges Eggsnatcher's Shield Spider and the two trade blows. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L331-332)
