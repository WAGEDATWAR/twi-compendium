# Reiss's army
> Type: org | Also called: Reiss' army; the Goblin Lord's army | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.30G
> See also: Goblin Lord's army, Reiss' army, Tremborag
## Overview
- {event} Reiss' army marches on Tremborag's mountain, and Tremborag's tribe rushes to its traps and defences and fights back. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L167,L352)
- Reiss' army fights on sixteen different fronts, with camps set about each entrance to the mountain. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L20)
- Reiss has an army of over a hundred thousand, though a good third are Goblins who will not fight unless cornered, such as children and heavily pregnant Goblins. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L54)
