# Rags's lieutenants
> Type: org | Also called: Rags's officers | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.39G
> See also: Rags, Pyrite, Flooded Waters tribe, Tremborag
## Overview
- Rags's lieutenants are the Goblins who think about the future of the tribe, among them Noears, Poisonbite, Redscar, the Rockfall Chieftain and Hobs of Tremborag's mountain. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L50)
- Pyrite is her second-in-command, and the Goldstone Chieftain is staunchly loyal to her. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L54; Book 7, Ch5.19G L198)
## The fireside debate
- {event} Poisonbite says that Rags does not know what to do, and Noears answers that she is aimless but not lost and is waiting for the right chance because the Goblin Lord is dangerous. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L51,L56-66)
- Noears is very intelligent and dangerous, and he plays with a spark of electricity that crawls over his hands. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L53)
- Redscar, who is not talkative, wants to return to the High Passes. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L60-62)
- Poisonbite wants to build a base and raid harder, since she dislikes only robbing travelers on the road. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L68-69)
- A Hob says the Chieftain needs to fight the Goblin Lord, but Noears says they should talk first. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L71-74)
- {belief} The officers agree that Rags is young and smart but not strong, and some say she needs an advisor, not another Chieftain like Garen Redfang. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L75-86)
- The Rockfall Chieftain, a supporter of Rags, says it would be good for Rags to have a mate, and the others suggest Pyrite, who is capable, solid and calm but not aggressive. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L67,L87-90)
- Noears and Redscar are the other likely choices, but neither is interested in the idea, and Noears closes the talk by saying that the Chieftain must do something. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L98-100)
## Chief officers
- Sir Kerrig sees Rags's chief officers following behind her, and they are Redscar, who rides a Carn Wolf, Noears, a powerful [Mage], Poisonbite, who carries a pair of poisoned daggers, the Rockfall Chieftain, a female Hob who once commanded a fairly strong tribe, and Quietstab, a Hob from Tremborag's tribe and a seasoned raid leader who represents the Mountain City Goblins. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L194-198)
- Redscar leads the former warriors of Garen's tribe and is the strongest of the group aside from Pyrite. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L198)
