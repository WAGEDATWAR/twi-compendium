# Rose Knights
> Type: org | Also called: Knights of the Petal; Order of the Petal; the Petal | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.39G
> See also: House Walchaís, Lady Bethal Walchaís, Thomast
## Overview
- The Order of the Petal is an order of [Knights] that obeys Lady Bethal, and Sir Kerrig reports being a member who was captured while attacking the Goblin tribe; no member has been known to lie. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L319-323)
- Their armor is pink. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L189)
## Book 6
- The Rose Knights wear enchanted armor that glances off pike strikes; the impact shock travels back down the pike instead of denting the armor. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L156-158)
- Hundreds of Goblin crossbows loosing together do not harm the Rose Knights, who stride out unscathed. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L160-162)
- The knights' enchanted weapons split steel shields, emit jets of fire, or leave cutting after-trails of light and freezing cold. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L168,L171)
- One knight's roar makes him seem a towering monster to the Goblins, and another hammers his shield with a Skill that creates a target for them. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L191-192)
- A Knight of the Petal casts [Grand Fireball] with a staff, and a voice states that [Knights] can be [Mage Knights] too. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L383-385)
- {event} At Lady Bethal's call to shield her husband, the Rose Knights retreat from the forest; two of their comrades lie unconscious or dead after toppled trees. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L252-253,L396)
- A Rose Knight with a flaming halberd stands at the front of the battle against the Goblin Lord's army and holds the line with other Rose Knights. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L189-191)
## Later
- Sir Nil Moxae and Dame Truvia Cartiil ride with Lady Bethal through the forest as her Rose Knights. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L97)
- {event} The Order of the Petal locks down House Walchaís's border and sends eighteen to escort Lady Walchaís and Sir Thomast home. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L715)
