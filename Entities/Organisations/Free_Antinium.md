# Free Antinium
> Type: org | Also called: the Free Hive; Anand and the Free Antinium | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.15
> See also: Liscor's army
## Overview
- The Free Antinium's Workers could construct and deconstruct buildings overnight, yet they regularly under-produced when providing services, since taking over Liscor's industry would cause economic disaster and the Hive was meant to work with other species. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L14-16)
- The Free Antinium bought food, crafted items and clothing despite having little need for them. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L16)
- After a decade of suffering in neglect, the other Hives were sitting up and looking at the Free Antinium, some warily and some approvingly, and the Free Queen understood the other Queens were waiting to talk to her. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L107)
## In the Grand Hive
- {event} In the Grand Hive the Free Antinium could not sleep in their cubicles, which reminded them of the Free Hive, and Anand told Klbkch he recalled it. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L649-651)
- Klbkch told the Antinium more about how the Hives had been organised by geniuses so that an Antinium took one less step to reach its destination. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L679)
- The Armored Queen told Anand the statue he resembled was Galuc the Builder, in whose image the Free Antinium were made; Galuc died before coming to Izril, and the Shaper Queen Bessachidia saved his form. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L949-950)
- {event} Workers carried a mirror out of the Grand Hive that Anand had built as a link, and Wistram's Archmages were seriously considering sabotaging the connection because of what it meant. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1371-1372)
- {event} Erin ran up to the mirror at the inn and asked where Anand had gone, then Grand Strategist Chaldion arrived with Grimalkin and Pallass officers while Olesm and Selys looked at the waving Antinium. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1373-1379)
