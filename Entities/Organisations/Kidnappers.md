# Kidnappers
> Type: org | Also called: Selys' kidnappers | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.17S
> See also: Selys Shivertail, Zevara, Heartflame Breastplate
## Overview
- {event} In Pallass, big Drakes follow Selys, and one with dark orange scales and a scarred snout confirms her name and orders his men to take her; she is knocked out and carried away. (Book 7, Ch5.17S L520-529)
- {event} Selys wakes bound to a chair in a dimly lit room with about eight Drake [Thugs], one a dark-orange, knife-wielding Drake who threatens her. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L57-71)
- The kidnappers demand that she sign a contract authorizing Watch Captain Zevara to relinquish the artifact in her possession, and promise to let her go once they have it. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L66-68,L73)
- Selys is relieved, having feared something worse, and tells them to untie her hands so she can sign. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L69-71,L76-81)
