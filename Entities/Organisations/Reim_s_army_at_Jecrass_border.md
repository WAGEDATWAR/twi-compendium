# Reim's army at Jecrass border
> Type: org | Also called: Reim army camp; Reim's army; the King of Destruction's army | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.08K
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Jecrass army, Serpent Hunters of Clais, Khelt army, Nerrhavia, Raelt
## Overview
- The army of Reim that marches with Flos Reimarch to Jecrass's border is about sixty thousand strong, mostly infantry. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L385,L405)
- Jecrass musters nearly a hundred and seventy thousand [Soldiers] at the border, nearly half of them mounted. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L402)
- Flos chose to bring a small army because it consumes fewer supplies on the march and is not threatening enough to provoke a huge response or make other rulers paranoid. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L775-777)
- Reim's army is led by Flos, his Steward Orthenon, and Mars the Illusionist as [Vanguard]. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L386)
## Composition
- Half-Giants, the Nomads of the Sky, march ahead of the infantry carrying long axes and staves and shed the illusion that made them look like a normal army. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L432-433)
- Some half-Giants are only nine or ten feet tall and most are no taller than sixteen or twenty feet, though a few carry the old Giant blood strongly. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L434)
- Behind the half-Giants walk a few dozen Parasol Stroll [Mages] twirling their colored parasols. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L440)
- The Parasol Stroll [Mages] are followed by the Serpent Hunters, a laughing army of String-folk with serpents etched on their armor and skin. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L441-442)
- A few hundred Rustängmarder, soldiers in dark armor with visors that hide their faces, flank the King and are called the army that doesn't die or retreat. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L444-445)
- Reim's army does not shine with magical armor as in legend, and its silent march at first looks small to Jecrass's host. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L415-417)
- {event} Reim's army halts on Jecrass's border a few hundred paces from Jecrass's army, with the Nomads of the Sky leaning on their weapons and the Rustängmarder in front. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L451)
- {event} Flos tells Raelt it is a show of force. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L453)
## The camp
- Sixty thousand [Soldiers] disassemble the camp the day after Flos slays a pack of Manticores, and Teres is struck by the camp's tidiness and efficiency. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L113-115)
- Orthenon and Flos have lived at least half their lives on campaign and could set up a defensible camp in their sleep. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L115)
- A Stitch-Woman cook of the Hemp class, who came from Nerrhavia's Fallen, serves Manticore meat to Teres and Flos with a smile. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L124-125,L141)
- The cook distrusts the Gnolls and Lizardfolk in camp, calling Lizardfolk light-fingered and Gnolls half-wild savages. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L140-146)
- Belchan shadows Flos's camp with a small force of about six thousand, while a much larger part of its army does war exercises about twenty miles away. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L761)
## Later
- {event} Jecrass's King Raelt is cheered by Belchan's citizens and his own army after the army of Reim retreats from a battle outside Belchan's walls, with Raelt believing Flos refused to use his [Army of the King]. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1112-1118,L1125)
- {event} Rain batters Jecrass's flatlands and stops Reim's army in its tracks, and four days later it is still admiring the weather, with helmets collecting rainwater. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L435-440)
- Reim's army moves out at twice the normal speed through [Rapid March]. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L1295)
