# Liscor Council
> Type: org | Also called: the Council of Liscor | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.26M
> See also: Zevara, Olesm Swifttail, Lism, Krshia Silverfang, Ilvriss
## Overview
- Olesm sees the Council as only a nominal ruling body handling bureaucratic measures, not leaders. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L272)
- At that time the Council is all Drakes, with an appointed Head Merchant rather than Krshia. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L273)
- The people who seem in control at a Council meeting about the Goblin Lord are Watch Captain Zevara, Wall Lord Ilvriss, General Zel Shivertail and the Named Adventurer Regrika Blackpaw. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L273)
- {event} Jelaqua and Ulrien, as Gold-rank adventurers, are awarded a place at that meeting due to their experience. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L281)
- Zel says Liscor's army is a mercenary one, far away and preoccupied, that has agreed to send a unit to defend the city. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L282-283)
## Composition
- In this scene the working Council is Elirr, Raekea and Krshia on the Gnoll side and Lism, Jeiss and Alonna on the Drake side, with Tismel and Zalaiss also representing Drakes. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L25)
- Tismel and Zalaiss rarely show up, since they lack the votes to sway all but contentious issues. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L26)
- Tismel and Zalaiss are traditionalists who tend to be overridden in most majority votes. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L366)
- Lism is Olesm's uncle. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L455)
- The Council is eight in total and needs a majority of five votes; official ratification of unofficial promises requires a vote. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L838-839)
- Krshia leads the Gnolls and Lism the Drakes, and the two now work together against incompetence. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L872)
## Decisions
- {event} Zevara, Olesm, Lism and Krshia debate the Bloodfields road; Olesm announces no team may go within four miles of the Bloodfields, and Reikhle agrees to adjust the route, with a wall possible. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1085-1099)
- {event} The Council votes against cancelling the agreement with Architect Hexel for being a Lizardperson, and six hands then rise in a joking vote to throw Tismel out of the window. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L448-455)
- {event} With Tismel and Zalaiss not notified of the meeting, the Council plans football and baseball teams, Krshia proposes ticket sales, and they agree to pay the Human in charge a salary. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L332-334,L344-345,L349,L388-392)
