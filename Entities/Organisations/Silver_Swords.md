# Silver Swords
> Type: org | Also called: Ylawes' team | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.20T
> See also: Ylawes Byres, Falene Skystrall, Dawil Ironbreaker, Yvlon Byres
## Overview
- The Silver Swords are Ylawes' team and include a Gold-rank Dwarf and a half-Elf [Mage]. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L323,L326-328)
- The three Silver Swords are known for championing just causes and protecting those in need, and have grown famous in the north, recognisable in most Adventurer's Guilds across the continent. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L82)
- The three have worked together for six years and are known for arguing, as each hails from a different race. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L82)
- The members are Ylawes Byres, a Human [Knight]; Falene Skystrall, a half-Elf [Battlemage] who graduated from Wistram; and Dawil, a Dwarf [Axe Champion] in full plate. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L83)
- Dawil states his age as thirty eight and Ylawes states his as twenty seven. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L108-109)
