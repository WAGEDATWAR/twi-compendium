# Rags's tribe
> Type: org | Also called: Rags's Goblins | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.03
> See also: Flooded Waters tribe, Red Fang Tribe
## Overview
- When Erin told Rags and her tribe they were going to help, Rags's posse backed away from her and the area emptied as the Goblins ran away. (Book 2, Ch2.03 L265-268)
- Rags's tribe has a group of Goblins with classes [Tinkerer], [Builder], [Hammerer], [Armorer] and [Scavenger], over two thirds of them without weapons, who are unique to her tribe. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L171-172)
- Every Goblin tribe has someone who repairs armour or makes crude spears, but Rags's tribe has learned to make crossbows and built crude armour thanks to her [Scavenger Armor] Skill. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L172)
