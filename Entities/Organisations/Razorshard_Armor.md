# Razorshard Armor
> Type: org | Also called: Razorshard Armor company | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.02D
> See also: Magehammer Company
## Overview
- The Razorshard Armor company is a Dullahan-led mercenary company that fought the Centaur Roving Arrow company, over land or possibly an old feud, in Baleros. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L43,L82; Book 10, Ch6.04D L125)
- Gravetender's Fist, a suppression company, was hired to dispose of undead and corpses from the fighting between the Razorshard Armor company and the Roving Arrow company. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L89)
- Each day both companies lined up their forces and sent them into the valley, keeping other forces hidden in the trees or flanking. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L316)
- A War Walker was described as an advancement of the [Juggernaut] class, and the Razorshard Armor company sending them out meant it was determined to win at any cost. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L170)
- {event} Geneva saved a War Walker of the Razorshard Armor company, preserving part of its strength, and a commander of the company came in person to thank her and ask help with other wounded soldiers. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L223-235)
- Both companies began to ignore the rules of war and target third parties; the Razorshard Armor company was comprised mainly of Dullahans and the Roving Arrow company was a Centaur group. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L83)
- {state} The Razorshard Armor company won and drove the Roving Arrow forces away, though both were reduced to a fraction of their force, and it secured the area for four days before Steliers' Ring forced it to retreat. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L122)
