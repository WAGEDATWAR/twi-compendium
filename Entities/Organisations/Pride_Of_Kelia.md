# Pride Of Kelia
> Type: org | Also called: Pride of Kelia; Nailren's team | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.35H
> See also: Gemhammer, Raskghar
## Overview
- {belief} The Pride of Kelia and Gemhammer are credited with having killed two dozen Raskghar, which one of the Horns doubts. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L33-35)
- Nailren leads the Pride of Kelia, a Silver-rank team whose members include Gnolls. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L718,L756)
- {event} Nailren's team joins Earlia's Gemhammer for the dungeon explorations, and the two teams find a ring, gems and a knife on dead Raskghar. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L715-748)
- {event} Nailren has Earlia's team be quiet and takes point to lead them back. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L756)
- {event} The teams freeze when they see a Goblin's head on a stick, the sign of Facestealer, who paralyzes everything in range. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L761-774)
