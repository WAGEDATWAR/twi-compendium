# Halfseekers
> Type: org | Also called: Halfseeker adventurers | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.27M
> See also: Jelaqua Ivirith, Moore, Seborn, Ulinde, Garen Redfang, Griffon Hunt
## Overview
- The Halfseekers are a Gold-rank adventuring team whose present core is Jelaqua Ivirith, a Selphid, Moore, a half-Giant, and Seborn, a Drowned Man. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L157-169; Book 7, Ch5.00 L35-42)
- Moore has a quarter of a Giant's blood, and Seborn smells like fish; Jelaqua says Selphids are unpopular on Izril. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L228)
- The Halfseekers use a private team vote on big decisions. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L459)
## History
- {belief} Typhenous recalls hearing the Halfseekers came from Baleros as Silver-rank and were once among the best Gold-rank teams on the continent; Jelaqua corrects his count of seven to eight members. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L268,L272)
- Jelaqua says they had hoped to become a Named team. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L272)
- {event} After the Sepicus Delve, one of their own turned on them, slaughtered four of the team and fled with everything they had found; they never found him. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L280-284)
- {belief} The survivors think he killed to hide what was stolen, perhaps an artifact or a secret, and Seborn remembers a key, a magical orb, spell scrolls and a few magical weapons. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L292-295,L313)
- {reveal} Jelaqua names the traitor as a Hobgoblin companion, Garen Redfang, who is still alive and has his own tribe. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L305)
- Griffon Hunt tells them that justice should be done for the fallen, to be discussed after the dungeon. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L315)
- Halassia Evergleam, a Scorchling, was once in their ranks; the Halfseekers buried her themselves because her family did not want her remains. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L442-445)
- Ilvriss says Halassia was born in Salazsar and that the Halfseekers took her in when many shunned Scorchlings. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L453)
- Jelaqua says they lost half their members to a Goblin, and Seborn recalls Ukrina's saying that giving other species no chance leaves the world no hope. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L315,L515-517)
## The earlier roster (Garen's memory)
- {event} In Garen's memory, the earlier Halfseekers ride to a city: Jelaqua in a Gnoll body, Seborn, Moore, Halassia Evergleam, the outcast full Drake Ukrina, Keilam, Thornst and the Hobgoblin Garen. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L270-284)
- Keilam is the group's third [Mage] if Moore is counted, and is half-Gnoll, half Cat-tribe Beastkin; Thornst is a half-Elf from Terandria and the newest member. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L279,L284)
- The group has just been certified as a Gold-rank team. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L288-291)
- {event} In Garen's memory, he kills Keilam, Thornst, Ukrina and Halassia in the private room where they divide their loot, holding the key; Halassia calls him a traitor before he kills her with his blade Redfang. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L620-629)
- {event} Jelaqua, Moore and Seborn debate afterward; Seborn thinks Ukrina and Halassia were right about the Goblin King but that Garen attacked them, while Jelaqua says they should have waited for the rest of the team. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L645-665)
## At the inn and the dungeon
- {event} Jelaqua, Moore and Seborn propose teaming up on the dungeon with Griffon Hunt, saying it is too dangerous alone, and Ulrien proposes splitting the spoils evenly. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L200-205,L221-223)
- They have just been kicked out of their own inn. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L224)
- {rel} Griffon Hunt and the Halfseekers have agreed to join forces on the dungeon. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L49)
- Jelaqua notes the Halfseekers are the only Gold-rank team in the inn until the Horns are certified, and the three test the inn's window for durability. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L98,L105-111)
- Jelaqua is a Selphid with a dead body, and Seborn has a claw-hand. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L35,L40)
- {event} Jelaqua says the Halfseekers want the city to build the official dungeon entrance higher, but the Antinium refuse to work in the downpour. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L185,L191)
- Jelaqua now wears a Drake body, and Selys leases the team the Heartflame Breastplate to test. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L56,L98,L101)
- {event} Jelaqua wears the Heartflame Breastplate as the Halfseekers and Griffon Hunt go into the dungeon, and Zevara notes Selys really leased it. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L123-126,L160-163)
- {event} Halrac nudges the cursed ornaments into his bag of holding once Typhenous and Moore judge they will not explode. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L511)
- Erin trusts the Halfseekers, the Horns and Griffon Hunt with the news of the axe, and notes the Halfseekers and Griffon Hunt sacrificed a lot, including Ulrien. (Book 9, Ch5.49 L442,L582)
## After the dungeon

- Jelaqua's spare bodies are stored in Erin's inn. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L961-964)
- {event} Passing two children in a village, the three raise their fists and shout "Halfseekers!", and Moore leaves a trail of blooming and wilting flowers. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L940-953)
- {event} The Halfseekers stall on the way to Invrisil because Seborn objects to Jelaqua's relationship with Maughin. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L237,L270-275)

- {event} Erin hosts a celebration of the Halfseekers' work in Pallass, and Ulinde is the newest member. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L315-317,L387)
