# House Kallinad
> Type: org | Also called: Kallinad family; the Kallinads | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.21D
> See also: Dawn Concordat
## Overview
- Wil Kallinad, one of two Humans in the Titan's class, is a Lord of House Kallinad and was miserable when asked to present something. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L108-109)
- Niers Astoragon said the Kallinad family possesses a steel and unyielding spine, and that Wil, though he doubts himself, shows it on the field and is his best student in logistics. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L1025)
- Niers identified Wil's sister as Lady Talia Kallinad, a [Summer Knight] of the Order of Seasons. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L1043)
- The Kallinads have a personal army, and Lady Talia arrived with that army, her order and the banners of a dozen other noble families. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L1043)
## The Iron Vanguard battle
- {event} Warships in the harbour and the arrival of the army Wil Kallinad led threw the Iron Vanguard into confusion, with soldiers shouting for Kallinad and the Order of Seasons. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L236-238,L250)
- Sir Kelm, a Knight of Autumn and representative of the Order of Seasons, said he and a hundred and twenty-six peers had joined Lady Knight Talia, equipped for a non-lethal confrontation. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L279)
- Lance-Commander Wylint of House Teoring brought two hundred men to Wil Kallinad for orders. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L283)
- Niers said anyone with the might of a Terandrian house could have pulled off such a tactic, and asked how others could compete if Wil Kallinad could pull an army out of his pocket. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L259)
- Wil challenged Tulm the Mithril to a battle in the streets, a name known around the world in a moment. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L273)
- When the Gnoll Feshi asked Wil for a deal, a present for her tribe's Meeting of Tribes in exchange for leading, his older sister Talia offered two trading caravans from the Kallinad harbours through the Gnoll Plains over six years. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1154-1160)
- At the post-battle rewards, Niers called Wil Kallinad forward. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L201)
