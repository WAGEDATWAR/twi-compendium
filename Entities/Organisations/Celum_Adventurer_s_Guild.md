# Celum Adventurer's Guild
> Type: org | Also called: Celum's Adventurer's Guild | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.03
> See also: Adventurer's Guild, Celum adventurers, Liscor Adventurer's Guild
## Overview
- Celum's Adventurer's Guild is bigger than Liscor's, with room for up to six receptionists at once, against the single desk for two in Liscor's guild. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L258)
- The Guild is a two-story building nearly twice the size of Liscor's, worn, with a faded sign and open shutters instead of glass. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L334-335)
- It could hold close to eighty people before feeling cramped, with only about three dozen present including staff. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L362)
- Its board holds open assignments, and exclusive contracts that only one team can take, such as escorting a [Trader]; harder and riskier ones sit near the top. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L503-506)
