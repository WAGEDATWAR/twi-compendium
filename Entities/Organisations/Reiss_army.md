# Reiss' army
> Type: org | Also called: Reiss's army; the Goblin Lord's army | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.61
> See also: Reiss's army, Goblin Lord's army
## Overview
- Reiss' Goblins march in ranks in silence in armor tarred black with resin, which Pebblesnatch finds as unnatural as monsters. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L88)
- Thousands of undead, fallen Goblins and reanimated Eater Goats, move in a separate column from the living. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L89)
- {event} In battle the dead on both sides rise, and for every warrior killed an undead is spawned. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L129,L144-146)
- Goblins in robes with wands and staffs stand behind Reiss' troops reanimating the dead, as does Reiss himself, with his [Necromancers] and [Shamans]. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L148)
