# United Nations company
> Type: org | Also called: The United Nations | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.06D
> See also: Geneva Scala, Kenjiro Murata, Luan Khumalo, Paige, Daly Sullivan, Bushrangers, Gravetender's Fist
## Overview
- {event} Niers notices a newly declared company in Baleros with a few sub-companies of under a hundred members each, whose striking name is the United Nations. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L491-494)
- The United Nations company formed right after the bloody battle between the Razorshard Armor Company and the Roving Arrow Company. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L51)
- It is far smaller than other companies and devoted only to finding more people from Earth and sheltering them. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L138)
## Origins and split
- Daly and others had been part of Quallet's Gravetender's Fist before. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L83)
- {event} Ken proposes that Quallet's company and the United Nations company split, as they are not working toward the same goals, and Quallet agrees and says most of his people will go with him. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L379-395)
- Ken says the United Nations company must earn a living without fighting, with Luan as a Runner and Geneva as a [Doctor], while the two companies remain allies. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L390-391)
## Housing
- The company rents two apartments that hold at least thirty people, and over forty when the Bushrangers, Luan, Ken and Geneva's team are back. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L18)
- Later, the company has a headquarters of a pair of double-story buildings from the Centauress landlady Miss Hastel. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L256,L265)
- Paige says a third building is unaffordable, since rent and other commitments weigh on the funds. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L312-313)
## Leaders and income
- Notable members are Geneva Scala, a [Doctor] with a legend from the bloodiest battlefields; Kenjiro Murata, a [Diplomat]; Luan Khumalo, an [Expert Rower] known as the Rower of Talenqual; and Daly Sullivan with his Bushrangers, a Silver-rank team. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L139-141)
- {event} Ken proposes The List: a definitive record of everyone from Earth, living and dead, with ages, names, homes and next of kin. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L349-350)
- The company's income comes from Paige's crossbows, Luan's deliveries, the Bushrangers' missions, working jobs, and mainly Geneva's condom manufacture, with her clinic also earning well. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L368-369)
## Book 18
- Geneva, Paige and Idis argue about a pandemic cure while Aiko, a [Nurse] working under Geneva, is quarantined in a dedicated wing of the company building. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L106-109,L127-129)
- Paige, Kirana, Daly, Luan, Ken and Siri are among the company's leaders at its headquarters when Joseph is contacted. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L519,L796)
- In Baleros, the company tracks the progress of the penicillin, which is being grown on pumpkins, and the treatment is working. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L12-14,L21)
