# Pithfire Hounds
> Type: org | Also called: Levil's team | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.00
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Levil, Wailer Frogs
## Overview
- The Pithfire Hounds are a six-member adventuring team led by the [Pyromancer] Levil. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L428,L445)
- The team includes the [Mages] Levil and Ullica, the [Beast Tamer] Bram with his dogs Makki and Dassa, and the [Warrior] Lamont. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L541; Book 14, Ch6.60 L498,L506)
- Tally is the team's [Ranger], and Keima is another member; in a later scene the same third woman is named Keida. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L577,L593; Book 14, Ch6.60 L514)
## Book 10
- {event} The six are fighting in the grasslands when a group of huge shapes attacks, and two carry a downed teammate with crushed armor and bones through his chest. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L428)
- {state} After the fight, Dassa is gone and Bram wipes away tears as Makki follows him. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L538)
## Book 11
- Their Captain, a [Fire Mage], takes gold from a black-clad stranger, and Tally hisses that it crosses a line. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L577)
- {event} Levil refuses to kill an innocent City Runner, saying the Pithfire Hounds are not mercenaries or murderers, even if Lady Bethal Walchaís is behind the message. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L584)
- {event} The team turns on the [Mercenary Captain] and the hooded [Assassin]; Levil blasts them with fire, Tally's arrow hits the Assassin, and Levil says the favor was repaid but mostly profit. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L622-627)
## Book 14
- Lamont, freshly healed from smashed ribs caused by Wailer Frogs, thanks Ryoka for saving his life. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L498-505)
- Makki is a wolfhound, smaller than Ryoka's Mousey. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L511-513)
- Bram says Dassa was the only dog they ever lost, when the team was on the verge of being wiped. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L536)
