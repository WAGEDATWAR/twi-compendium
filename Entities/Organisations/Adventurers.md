# Adventurers
> Type: org | Also called: adventurer teams | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.04R
> See also: Adventurer's Guild, Liscor Adventurer's Guild, Horns of Hammerad, Griffon Hunt
## Overview
- {belief} A Horns of Hammerad member complained that most Runners do not deliver to battlefields, some deliver only after the fighting ends, and those who do charge triple. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L194)
- {belief} Ryoka heard adventurers whisper that a person with no levels might be a demi-human, a monster, a fool or cursed. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L353-357)
- {belief} Erin objected that hiring adventurers to guard her inn would cost eight silver coins a day each, even for the lowest-level ones. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L72)
- When Erin's Goblin guests were seen by three human adventurers, one grabbed his sword and a huge one said the Goblins were monsters and that he had lost good friends to them. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L242-258)
- Selys said the Watch hired adventurers to clear giant rats from the sewers, a job that pays well but that no one liked. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L61-72)
- One of the Horns told Erin that one of the rules all adventurers learn is not to touch magical objects unless you know what they are, since they might be trapped or warded. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L291)
## Customs
- A team member said that, with no truth crystals to prove what adventurers kill, they take proof such as a head, and that Liscor was not yet offering a bounty for monsters killed in the dungeon. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L409-415)
- {event} Older adventurers tried the team Vuliel Drae over a mistake in the dungeon that they had not reported, and Jelaqua and Ylawes offered to try to keep it from Liscor's citizens if the team told them everything. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L443-445,L469-475)
- Jelaqua said Gold-rank adventurers are tested regularly for corruption by the Adventurer's Guild and questioned under truth spells. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L472)
## Goblin mountain raids
- {event} Snapjaw told Reiss that fewer than thirty Humans were in Tremborag's mountain, and Reiss asked if they were Gold-rank. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L58-65)
- {event} Adventurers set fire to a network of rope bridges and shot lava at another, sending hundreds of Goblins falling to their deaths. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L468-469)
- Gold-rank teams hunting in the mountain left Goblins in black armour alone unless attacked, while killing other Goblins. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L31-32)
- {belief} One of the adventurers recognised Garen Redfang and called him the team killer of the Halfseekers. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L50-52)
## After the spell
- {event} After the great spell faded, Jelaqua, Ylawes and Halrac chose to stay at The Wandering Inn a few more days. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L695-699)
- {event} The Horns, Griffon Hunt, the Silver Swords and teams like Bevussa's Wings of Pallass gathered at Erin's inn, and Jelaqua's team went first in a relay carrying a door north. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L452,L498)
