# Five Families
> Type: org | Also called: Five Great Houses; Five Families of Izril | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.41
> See also: House Reinhart, Wellfar family, House Byres
## Overview
- {belief} Selys told Erin that the Reinharts, Veltras, Terland, Wellfar and El families were the first humans to settle on Izril, that they were aristocrats related to the kings of Terandria, and that they are still very rich and influential. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L181-186)
- Fals said all the really powerful nobles and the Five Families live closer to the north. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L171)
- The houses of Veltras, El, Reinhart, Terland and Wellfar settled Izril, taking it from the Drakes and Gnolls thousands of years ago, though they were not the only noble houses to sail from Terandria; those that fell were forgotten. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L68)
## Fortunes of the families
- In Ullim's view, El had fallen far from its past heights, Wellfar and Terland kept much of their strength though the Antinium Wars killed some of their scions, Veltras had military might but not huge stockpiles of magical goods, and Reinhart kept most of its artifacts and grew richer. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L69-70)
- Maviola El told young nobles that each of the Five Families has its own curse, and that all are in decline. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L112-114)
- Maviola said the Wellfar family is rich with coastal holdings and control of trade, led the navies to Izril and still rules the seas, but is infertile and shrinking and never expanded far inland. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L117-119)
- Maviola said the Terlands were victims of their success, keeping to mage-towns and spell-castles and looking outward little. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L141)
- Maviola said the Veltras family never settled the untamed wilderness such as the Forest of Vail, and that its scions learn to fight and make excellent [Generals] and [Warmasters] but poor leaders of men. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L147-150)
- Maviola called the Reinharts the richest and most powerful of the families, and said their only curse was Regis Reinhart, who preserved them but took from each generation. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L153-155)
- Maviola said the House of El are great crafters of magic whose curse is that they are out of money, having lost almost all their old artifacts and investments. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L161-165)
- Maviola said the Five Families are allies but not friends, and that they are shadows of what they were, with the nobility of Izril lacking leaders and fewer in number than anyone remembers. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L140,L167)
