# Gemhammer
> Type: org | Also called: Earlia's team | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.28
> See also: Pride of Kelia, Earlia, Raskghar
## Overview
- Gemhammer is a Silver-rank team led by Earlia, a Human with a warhammer. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L166-167)
- The members were all [Miners] before discovering they could kill monsters with hammers and pickaxes, and have mining-related Skills. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L340)
- Gemhammer has a [Gem Mage]. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L223)
- Timgal, Fea and Blaik are among Earlia's team. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L352)
## Raskghar
- {belief} Ceria, hearing a claim that Gemhammer killed a dozen Raskghar, is shocked at how hard six of them are to fight. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L515-516)
- {belief} The claim attributed to Gemhammer and the Pride of Kelia is two dozen Raskghar, which one of the Horns scoffs at after six Raskghar nearly overcame their team and three Hobs. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L33-35)
## Broadcast and dungeon
- {event} Earlia announces her name and her Silver-rank team to the worldwide broadcast, and Niers calls it a smart move since the name was spread across the world in a moment. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L165-176)
- {event} With Nailren's team, Earlia's team of former [Miners] kills a Raskghar team in the dungeon. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L715-718,L721)
- {event} Earlia cuts a gold-and-sapphire ring from a diseased Raskghar's finger; the team also finds a bag of gemstones and an enchanted-looking knife. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L725-748)
- {event} The teams meet Facestealer, who peeks around a corner with a Goblin's head on a stick, and its notes say it paralyzes everything in range. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L761-774)
