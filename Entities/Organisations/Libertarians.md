# Libertarians
> Type: org | Also called: Libertarian faction | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3
> See also: Centrists, Ullsinoi
## Overview
- {belief} A Wistram student said the Libertarians want Wistram to start taking sides with different nations and throwing its influence around, and that many of them are from Terandria and take a deep interest in its politics. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L407-409)
- Archmage Viltach, a Human, heads the Libertarians, a mostly Human faction, and spends as much time in Terandria as in Wistram. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L585; Book 18, Ch7.29B L214)
- Viltach and his Libertarians are known for their links to Terandria, and Viltach is especially political in that sense. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L13)
- The Libertarians are one of the big three Wistram factions with thousands of members and the most voting power in the Council. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L480)
## Members and actions
- Rievan belongs to the Libertarian group, and Charles was expected to join, since his family's deep pockets and influence would be welcome and the faction might help his family in political battles. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L407; Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L141)
- {belief} Pisces told Ceria that Timor invited him to join Charles and himself in the Libertarians with incentives, and that Pisces refused outright, saying the faction wants every new student it can get. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L177,L180-182)
- {event} After Ceria was struck from behind during a test, Calvaron blamed Charles and said the Libertarians had really done it now, and her friends vowed against Charles and the Libertarian party. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L392,L404)
- Welaiat, a Libertarian, gambled and spent freely, and a Preservationist accused the Libertarians of dragging Wistram into continental affairs and always needing deeper pockets to finance their spending. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L139-143)
- Rievan and the other Libertarians did not agree with Archmage Feor's faction or with non-Humans, but sat down when Teura stared at them. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L176-178)
- Montressa told Isceil that at least he was not a Libertarian, and joked about having one on their team. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L214-216)
- {event} Viltach threw his Libertarians in beside the Revivalists, usually enemies, in exchange for votes on a referendum inviting Chandrarian bazaars to Wistram; Feor lost. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L29)
- After Wistram's team rescued Eun and George from a pirate ship, the factions fought over them: the Libertarians claimed Eun and the Sedoli group claimed George. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L219)
- Charles and a group of Libertarians stomped out of a simulation covered in welts, and Rievan of the Libertarians sneered at Elena and scowled at Beatrice. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L46,L211,L214)
