# Az'kerash's Chosen
> Type: org | Also called: the Chosen | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.49
> See also: Az'kerash, Venitra, Ijvani, Bea, Oom, Kerash
## Overview
- {event} Five undead step out of shadow before Zel Shivertail and give their names: Venitra, Ijvani, Bea with Oom, and Kerash. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L156-164)
- Venitra is a giant woman made of bone, in bone armor with a sword and shield and burning green eyes. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L156)
- Ijvani is a skeleton of black bone in a [Mage]'s robes, with golden flames in her eyes and a staff. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L159)
- Bea is a rotting female corpse, and Oom is a faceless humanoid figure in a trench coat and hat who stands beside her; Bea speaks for both. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L161-162)
- Kerash is an undead Gnoll in armor with glowing blue eyes, taller than Zel, with a magic sword. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L163)
- Zel recognises Ijvani's staff from Imenet, and the Necromancer says Zel encountered Venitra and Ijvani before. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L185-188)
- Ijvani casts a [Blackfire Fireball]. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L213)
## Later
- Az'kerash treats the Chosen as his children and creations, and four of them are learning to fight, all below Level 10. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L519-521)
- {state} The Chosen have been granted the ability to level, which Az'kerash counts as true sapience. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L522-523)
