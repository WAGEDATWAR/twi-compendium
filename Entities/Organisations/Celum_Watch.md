# Celum Watch
> Type: org | Also called: Celum City Watch | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.37
> See also: Celum Adventurer's Guild, Liscor Watch
## Overview
- {event} A squad of Celum guardsmen took in three muggers Erin had subdued, one of them a former Bronze-rank adventurer, and the Watch gave her two silver coins, four copper coins and three healing potions. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L264,L275-276)
- {event} Celum's City Watch was bored on an uneventful day, then watched from the walls as Gold-rank adventurers carried the magic door through the city, and then the Goblins appeared. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L572-574)
- {event} Dozens of guardsmen on duty climbed the walls, and people on the streets crowded up to see the crowd in the distance. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L598)
- {event} Celum's City Watch stormed Celum's Adventurer's Guild, ordering Pisces and the others to put their weapons down and holding half the Watch outside, and arrested the adventurers. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L833-835)
- {event} Dozens of guardsmen converged on Octavia's shop, and the Watch Captain announced by order of the [Mayor] that the door to Liscor was confiscated and Celum would no longer do business with the inn. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L829-833)
- The Watch Captain seized only the portal stone, not the magic door itself, and Numbtongue escaped through a back window. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L835-837)
