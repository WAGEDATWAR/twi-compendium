# Rustless Guard
> Type: org | Also called: the Rustless Vanguard (as named later) | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.04D
> See also: United Nations company, Bushrangers, Dullahan
## Overview
- The Rustless Guard is a Silver-rank team of Dullahans in good iron armor carrying shields, maces and axes, led by Captain Eldima. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L664,L680)
- {event} Eldima complains that her team requested the Stelbore assignment first and that Daly's team took their quarry. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L673)
- {event} Eldima tells Daly about a new dungeon entrance cleared by rainfall three miles south, which her team intends to inspect first. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L285-293)
- {event} Eldima says she would like to join Daly's company, after hearing that the company forms alliances, not enemies. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1119,L1124)
- The Rustless Guard is a group of Dullahans in armor who snare a giant snake with ropes and a net, and Eldima kills one with a [Razor Slash]. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L26,L56-57)
- Eldima's [Razor Cut] Skill makes precise cuts easier. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L114)
- Later the Bushrangers have partnered with the Rustless Vanguard, one of the best non-Gold teams in their area. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L106)
