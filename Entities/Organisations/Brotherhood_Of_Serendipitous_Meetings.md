# Brotherhood Of Serendipitous Meetings
> Type: org | Also called: Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings; the Brotherhood | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.54
> See also: Sisters of Chell, Typhenous, Numbtongue
## Overview
- The Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings and the Sisters of Chell are underworld groups, the two largest local players in the region, and the Brothers have a reputation for being polite. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L915-916)
- {event} Four of the Brothers, dressed smartly and wearing rings that make people forget their faces, come to the inn, all [Thugs], and take part in the theft of the magic door alongside the Sisters of Chell. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L889-891,L922)
- The Brothers' leader says an intermediary of Erill gave them the job, whereas the Sisters say "Lady Spellcraft" gave them theirs. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L928-930)
## Book 12
- A few months before, the Brotherhood's best Brothers in the region were contracted for a very expensive, sudden mission and died, and the Brotherhood wants to know what happened. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L239-241)
- The Brotherhood also has a highly lucrative offer from the Tallman of Baleros, and has Brothers in Remendia. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L188,L242)
- An [Enforcer] says that if Numbtongue's actions were chance, the Brothers will not hold much of a grudge, though debts may remain. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L913,L919)
