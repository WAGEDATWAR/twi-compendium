# Bloodtear Pirates
> Type: org | Also called: the Bloodtears | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.33E
> See also: Undersea Crews, Dawn Concordat
## Overview
- The Bloodtear Pirates are described as one of the most feared and dangerous [Pirate] navies in the world. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L354)
- {event} Five Bloodtear ships pursued Captain Gallheart's Waveblade through a storm for five days because it carried a treasure they sought, and three of their ships were lost to the waves. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L354,L357-359)
- The Bloodtear Pirates are cited among groups feared on the seas, alongside Midnight's Laughter, Wrymvr the Deathless and the Army of Texical. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L86)
- A sailor said some pirate armadas kill every person they raid, like the Bloodtear Pirates, a savage armada, while others such as the Illuminary kill only those who fight back. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1505)
## The Emerald Signet chase
- Seborn described the Bloodtear Pirates as marauders who keep their word but give no quarter, with a number of ships spread out in their armada, three being just an attack force. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L399)
- {event} A briefing held that The Gold of Paxil was probably sunk by the Bloodtear Pirates on an intercept course, and a captain's scroll reported an ambush by them in pursuit of The Emerald Signet. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L328,L395)
- A Screaming Sails sailor called the Bloodtears enemies of their crew. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L461)
- The Bloodtear ships had sails filled with magical wind and were as fast as The Emerald Signet, perhaps faster, and they hailed no one. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L628,L646)
- {event} The Bloodtear Pirates fought the Drowned Crews and each other's prey over the casket, and chased The Emerald Signet as it got away. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L688-690,L714)
