# Redfang Tribe
> Type: org | Also called: Redfangs; Redfang Warriors (as a mounted force) | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.17T
> See also: Redfang Warriors, Redfang Hobs, Rags, Garen Redfang, Redscar
## Overview
- The Redfang tribe, once invincible in the region around the High Passes that it claimed, becomes subservient to Rags, whose tribe had been little more than a group of weaklings. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L126)
- Garen Redfang threw his battle with Rags: he took only part of his forces to fight her, raided with quick attacks, and gave her the position of Chieftain over all the tribes, as all the Goblins knew. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L127)
- Rags is technically a Chieftain directing other smaller Chieftains, not a Goblin Lord, and the other Chieftains follow her in a coalition. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L128,L156)
## Reputation and methods
- {belief} Yvlon reports that the Redfang tribe is a notorious Goblin tribe of the High Passes, active for years, that no one has defeated or tracked to its lair. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L461)
- {belief} By Yvlon's account the Redfang tribe is rated as a threat no single Gold-rank team should handle alone, some of its members ride Carn Wolves, and it has done away with several Gold-rank teams. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L465)
- The Redfang Warriors are mounted on the fierce Carn Wolves of the High Passes, and their name inspires fear among Humans. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L117)
- {event} About eighty regular Goblins, not Hobs, ride Carn Wolves out of the forest shouting "Redfang!", and the Human riders retreat. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L115-119)
- The Redfang wolves are lower to the ground and scare horses, which makes them a good counter to mounted brigades. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L119)
- The Redfang Warriors ride rust-red wolves thrice as large as normal. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L173)
- {event} In revenge for their fallen, the Redfang Warriors ride in small groups to Human villages and farms with torches and crossbows and set buildings alight. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L173-174)
- {event} Of the four hundred Redfang Warriors sent out, fewer than two hundred and forty return. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L277)
## Composition and leadership
- {state} Redscar replaced Garen as the Redfangs' chief, and according to Poisonbite changed nothing about the tribe's habits. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L19-20)
- According to Poisonbite, the Redfang elites accept any Goblin who can fight regardless of gender, but under a quarter of the warriors are female and none lead. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L20)
- The Redfang tribe numbers nearly four thousand riders, who now ride horses, after taking casualties; some of the old guard who left for Rags have returned. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L7)
- The Redfang tribe is unique in being mainly male, and it accepts only the best warriors, Hobs included; Hobs are rarer because they must be able to ride. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L36,L38)
- Since the split, Garen made the tribe a fully mounted force that can fight on foot but prioritises movement. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L39)
- The Redfangs mourn their dead without tears and accept deaths as expected. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L37)
- {belief} From a Redfang viewpoint, the Mountain City tribe, the Goblin Lord's army and the Flooded Waters tribe would probably loot the dead and kill a [Knight], while the Redfangs have honor and respect their opponents. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L31-33)
- {event} Redfang warriors bring down the half-Giant Moore with ropes, saying they have fought Gargoyles in the passes and know how to topple giants. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L65)
- {event} The Redfangs snare a three-headed Bone Horror with ropes and hooks while two Hobs on warhorses smash it with a warhammer and a mace. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L192-195)
- Eater Goats see the Redfangs' red war paint and the Carn Wolves they ride and grudgingly avoid them. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L298)
