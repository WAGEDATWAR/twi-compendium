# Sisters of Chell
> Type: org | Also called: Sister of Chell | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.54
> See also: Brotherhood Of Serendipitous Meetings, Numbtongue, Yellow Splatters
## Overview
- {event} Five masked Sisters of Chell slip into the inn behind the Brothers, armed with bows and daggers, to take the door. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L893-894)
- The leader of the Sisters says "Lady Spellcraft" gave them their job. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L930)
- One Sister mumbles a curse at the mention of a Gold-rank Garuda, and some Sisters furtively search the sleeping victims for valuables until the Brother chides them. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L911-912,L920-921)
## Book 12
- A scarred [Nightstalker], whose first class was [Nightwalker], travels with thin blades in her sleeves and a bandaged [Blackguard] as extra muscle. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L17-18,L28-29)
- She says she was sent from the north to find out what happened with a job that went bad right before the battle at Liscor, where her people all died. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L175)
- She says an entire hand of her sisters died through the door. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L216)
- Numbtongue says he killed Humans he saw stealing the door when Liscor was on alert, and the [Enforcer] judges his story consistent. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L901-903)
- {event} The [Nightstalker] jabs a stiletto at Numbtongue and Yellow Splatters grabs her hand and she stabs him with her other stiletto, while the [Blackguard] swings a shortsword at Numbtongue and the [Enforcer] intercepts; Yellow Splatters says poison is useless against the Antinium. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L905-909)
- {event} She leaves, declaring that Numbtongue is marked. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L921-922)
