# Undersea Crews
> Type: org | Also called: Drowned Crews; Drowned Ships | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.11
> See also: Bloodtear Pirates
## Overview
- The Siren's Depths are part of the Undersea Crews. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L830)
- Only Drowned Folk join the Undersea Crews, becoming [Depth Sailors] rather than [Storm Sailors], and the Crews are loyal only to Drowned Folk. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L390)
- Undersea Crews often came to blows but preferred submission to slaughter of their own kind. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L146)
- Depth Captain Therrium Sailwinds of the Undersea Crews commands The Passing Shadow, a ship feared above and under the sea. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L431)
- Captain Rasea, a half-angler who took the Sea's Bargain, joined the Undersea Crews and later left them to be a [Pirate]; even the Crews thought her mad. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1498,L1593)
## The Emerald Signet
- The Undersea Crews' ships were unaffected by fog because they travel underwater. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1226)
- {event} Therrium demanded the treasure of The Emerald Signet in the name of the Undersea Crews, with The Sinking Light beside him. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L500)
- {event} Dozens of Drowned Ships rose around Therrium, all the Undersea Crews in the area, and later pushed back the Storm Sailors. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L921,L1128)
- Pirates and Undersea Crews ships numbering thirty did not fight each other at one point. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1093)
- Some nations pay Pirates or Undersea Crews to acquire medicine. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L381)
