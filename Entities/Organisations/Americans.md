# Americans
> Type: group | Also called: summoned group; the Americans | First appears: Book 2, Ch1.00C
> See also: Chat, BlackMage, Tom, Gravetender's Fist
## Overview
- This entry covers two separate bodies of people from Earth that the books call Americans: the group summoned to Rhir and the mostly-American Earth people serving in Gravetender's Fist. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L13; Book 5, Ch1.03D L124,L376)
## The summoned group in Rhir
- The narrator says that around fifty people his age, about twenty, appeared in this world through a summoning spell in front of an old man on a throne, and everyone screamed that they were the "prophesied heroes". (Book 2, Ch1.00C L13)
- {state} Just over twenty of them are left, and instead of being the heroes of the Blighted Lands they are disgraces sent to garrison a village on the outskirts, barely acknowledged by the throne or the people. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L14)
- When they first came to Rhir the king and court welcomed them with celebrations, feasts and parades until they realized the newcomers were not actual heroes, and then shipped them out to die. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L156)
- They were brought back because some of them proved they could fight. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L157)
- The narrator says over two thirds of their groups died in one night when they were sent to the front lines. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L226)
- The twenty or so Americans summoned to this world ride together between the narrator and their escort. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L36)
- The group has few electronics: Emily keeps a laptop that belonged to Reyanne, who died, Eddy has an Android phone, and Richard carries an iPhone. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L125-130)
- They did not realize at first that they could [Repair] their electronics, and now they share them, except that Emily keeps the laptop. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L128)
- Richard says he wants to talk to the others again but has not had another call from [BlackMage] and has no idea how to call him back. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L131)
## Americans in Gravetender's Fist
- Most of the people from Ken's world in Gravetender's Fist are American, Australian and European, and the Americans are the largest group. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L124; Book 5, Ch1.02D L283)
- {belief} Luan tells Aiko that the Americans say the job is bad and want to abandon the company, desert, head back to a city and sell their swords and armor. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L376)
- {event} Johanas and seven other Americans are caught sneaking through the forest and are made to kneel before a Dullahan with an axe in front of the two armies. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L261-264)
- {state} The eight kneeling Americans are executed, and Ken sees headless bodies and a red axe being wiped clean by a Dullahan. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L274-275)
- Ken realizes in that moment that he can never go back. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L277)
