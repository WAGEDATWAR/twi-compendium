# Liscor Adventurer's Guild
> Type: org | Also called: Liscor's Guild; Liscor's Adventurer's Guild | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.41
> See also: Adventurer's Guild, Adventurers, Liscor Watch
## Overview
- Selys told Ceria and Gerial that the Guild had already approved their request, so they needed no seals, letters or proof to enter Liscor's ruins. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L158-161,L171)
- Liscor's Adventurer's Guild gets a budget, and its cut goes to Liscor's treasury rather than Guild vaults, which it does not have; Selys expected dungeon money to go to repairing gates and walls and paying hurt soldiers. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L17)
- Selys had worked nine hours since before dawn, since a [Receptionist] must be on call at all hours. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L3,L12)
- Maviss said the Guild had barely enough staff to cover shifts since two new [Receptionists] quit, and Selys said Tekshia was looking but needed more budget. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L13-15)
