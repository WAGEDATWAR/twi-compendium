# Parasol Stroll
> Type: org | Also called: none | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.06KM
> See also: Flos, Ulyse, Mirin, Serpent Hunters
## Overview
- Parasol Stroll is a band of robed [Mages] who each hold an enchanted parasol. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L13,L213,L215)
- Gazi says they are mercenaries now but served her King before, and that each [Mage] is above Level 20 at the very least. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L210,L215)
- Each member's parasol is enchanted to enhance their magic and can cast spells on its own like a staff or wand. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L266)
## In Reim
- Ulyse, a middle-aged man, and Mirin, a younger woman, speak for the group. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L266-267)
- Parasol Stroll is busy performing the work of the Mage's Guild and coordinating Reim's magical needs. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L267)
- Ulyse says they could fight an army many times larger but that being His Majesty's sole [Mages] is a weakness. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L313)
- {event} At a banquet, Ulyse presents a performance of light butterflies and birds for the King of Destruction, and Mirin raises a real wave of water. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L91,L97-104)
- Ulyse names Mirin, Palke and Asimi in the performance; one of the [Mages] then casts sparks that turn out real and set fire to the hall. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L91,L110-111)
