# [Message] spell
> Type: spell | Also called: messaging spell | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.08R
> See also: Runners, Tier Magic
## Overview
- Ryoka Griffin, running toward the High Passes, is followed by animated sparks of light like fireflies carrying flashing symbols and a garbled sound, which she guesses may be a curse; she speeds up and loses them whenever they try to lock on. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L22-24)
- {event} A red and gold firefly of light appears at Erin's inn, and Pisces says it is a messaging spell but he has not seen one like that before. (Book 1, Ch1.28 L143-146)
- The light resolves into flickering runes that are painful to look at for Erin and Rags, while only Pisces can look at them without pain, and he leaves to read it. (Book 1, Ch1.28 L147-152)
- A mage service sends a single [Message] for a couple of gold coins, and Ryoka relays from Ceria that the spell is not easy to cast and needs Level 15 or so. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L284-287)
- The service holds any reply for one week before disposing of it. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L338)
