# [Illumination]
> Type: spell | Also called: Illumination | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.01H
> See also: Tier Magic, Message spell
## Overview
- [Illumination] is an advanced version of the [Light] spell: officially Tier-2 magic, but with complexity closer to Tier-3. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L42)
- [Illumination] lights over a hundred meters around the caster by shedding orbs of light, which makes it perfect for dungeons. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L42)
- Ceria is having trouble mastering the spell she is learning. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L41-42)
- In the crypt, light suddenly fills the long corridor as bright white orbs fly up from the ground, spaced ten feet apart, turning the dark into day. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L59)
