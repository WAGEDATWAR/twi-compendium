# Watch House
> Type: place | Also called: the Watch house | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.16
> See also: Liscor, Pallass, Zevara
## Liscor
- In Liscor, the Watch House is where the City Watch is based and where guards can borrow weapons, and news of Erin's play spreads from it. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L503,L511)
- Embria leaves the Watch House with the 4th Company waiting in lines outside. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L202)
- Palt weaves a calming spell for the entire Watch House. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L50)
- Zevara strides out of the Watch House with Selys and the Watch following. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L558)
- Jerci and Tkrn march a thief to the Watch house. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L672)
## Pallass
- Pallass operates many City Watch stations, with almost every floor having a Watch House so patrols are never far from trouble, except the 9th floor. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L3-4)
- The 8th floor's Watch House in the eastern section is generally quiet, run by a Desk Sergeant when Watch Captains are away. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L5-6)
- Pallass's Watch House is like the one in Liscor, but different enough to feel odd to Erin. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L9)
