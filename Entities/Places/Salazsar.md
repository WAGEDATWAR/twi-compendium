# Salazsar
> Type: place | Also called: City of Gems | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.02
> See also: Walled Cities, Ilvriss, Pallass, Wall Lords
## Overview
- Salazsar is the second-largest of the Walled Cities and is built into the side of a mountain as a mountain-fortress, and out of it. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L3,L5)
- The narration says Salazsar is defined by tradition, the lineage of Wall Lords and Wall Ladies, and by mining and gem cutting. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L7)
- Salazsar is called the City of Gems and rules the gem trade. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L208)
## Description
- Ever-burning flames warm only the highest floors of the city, where only the richest denizens can afford to live. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L3)
- A Drake crosses crisscrossing bridges in the sky between the highest towers, and the narration says each Walled City has its own personality. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L6)
- Wall Lords control holdings such as mining areas or enough wealth to finance sectors, and each employed person owes allegiance to a particular Wall Lord, or house of Salazsar. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L102)
- Salazsar is not ruled by democracy like Pallass but by the Wall Lords and Ladies. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L7)
## Defenses and politics
- A speaker says it is impossible to teleport into Salazsar because it has more ward-stones and counter spells than anyone can count. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L380)
- A speaker says Ilvriss and Salazsar have made it clear that war will follow if Pallass takes Erin's door. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L366)
- {belief} The same speaker suspects that more Drake cities and other Walled Cities might take Salazsar's side. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L366)
- The narration says Salazsar is forming a new army in secret. (Book 16, ChInterlude_The_Hangover_After L62)
