# Goblin stronghold
> Type: place | Also called: Tremborag's city; Mountain City; the mountain | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.26G
> See also: Dwarfhalls Rest, Tremborag, Rags, Reiss
## Overview
- Rags sees a former city of stone inside a mountain that, after centuries of neglect and Goblin habitation, still shows traces of what it had been. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L155)
- Rags thinks Dwarves perhaps carved the place and built its monuments, while Goblins broke down windows and walls and strung bridges and rope across it. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L156)
## Description
- Huge furnaces expel black smoke upward, their embers never die, and Goblin [Blacksmiths], whom Rags has never seen before, hammer and reforge metal there. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L154)
- Guards in the stronghold wear steel armor that makes even some Hobs look on in jealousy. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L151)
- Goblins in the hall wear steel or iron arms, some complete plate armor scavenged from adventurer corpses, and others hold glowing magical swords. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L164)
- Rags counts over thirty Hobs in one room, with far more present. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L165)
- The mountain is hollowed out as a dwelling for tens of thousands of Goblins, and the narration says it was built under Tremborag or perhaps stolen and claimed by him. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L7)
- The Goblins took what was apparently an abandoned Dwarf city and chipped away rock walls and ceilings, and the space inside feels vast. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L29)
- Tremborag's tribe is the only tribe there before Rags arrives, but sub-Chieftains who manage parts of it or lead warbands fight each other for influence. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L20)
- Goblins mark factions with ragged armbands or tattoos, and all the [Blacksmiths] wear a chain bracelet or necklace. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L58)
## Fall
- {event} When Elia Arcsinger appears, Tremborag leaps into a lower tunnel and Tremborag's Goblins and the Goblin Lord's forces throw down weapons and flee in panic. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L482-486)
- {state} The narration says Tremborag's mountain is lost as both the Mountain City tribe and the Goblin Lord's army flee the Human army and the memory of her. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L487)
