# Drath
> Type: place | Also called: Drath Archipelago; Empire of Drath; Drathian (adj.) | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.05
> See also: Izril, House of Minos
## Overview
- The people of the Drath Archipelago speak a different language from the rest of the world, which leaves Pekona's command of the common tongue weak. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L334)
- A Gnoll speaker says that, as far as she knows, only two groups have a completely different language from everyone else: Goblins and the people of Drath. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L30)
- Drath is also called the Empire of Drath, and its tutelage is described as proud and formal. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L228)
## Culture
- Venaz says that the House of Minos trades with the Drathians for useful tools such as charms and anti-scrying wards, which most species have few counters against. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L606-607)
- The people of Drath are said to be experts at refining the body, mind and flow of mana, producing pills, liquids and other methods of cultivation. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L231)
- The people of Drath hunt creatures from the world's end, according to a character who has seen others, and this one was the largest. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L646)
- Porcelain from the Empire of Drath is delicate and treasured. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L579)
- Orthenon was raised in the Empire of Drath. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L1086)
