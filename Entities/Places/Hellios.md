# Hellios
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos
> See also: Flos, Germina, Reim, Orthenon, Chandrar
## Overview
- Hellios is a kingdom in Chandrar to the northeast of Germina, a sometimes friendly neighbor ruled by Queen Calliope. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L30)
- Hellios has no [King]: Treland of Hellios died decades earlier at Flos' hands in his first wars of expansion, and Calliope rules alone, followed by her son Prince Siyal. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L30)
- The narration says the capital of Hellios is a mountain-fortress, like the other cities that use geography to their advantage. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L5)
## Conquest by Flos
- {event} Before winter, Flos' armies marched north at speed with Orthenon, Mars and Gazi, and the conquest of Hellios took less than two weeks. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L93)
- Hellios surrendered unconditionally as Flos' armies crossed its borders and crushed its army. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L93)
- {state} Queen Calliope and her son Prince Siyal became captives in their own castle, because Flos made it clear he would behead both royals if the army resisted. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L115)
- Hellios' army laid down arms almost to a man rather than keep fighting, because the royals' deaths would have sparked an uprising. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L115)
- Both Calliope and Siyal had been spared death, for the moment. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L30)
## Later
- {state} Later, Flos calls Calliope the former Queen because she abdicated, and says she is complying, if unhappy, while Hellios supplies resources. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L430-432)
- Flos says Orthenon is free to leave for other work soon, perhaps leaving the upstart [Prince] to manage his own kingdom, and that he would rather have Hellios supply resources and soldiers than be a thorn in his side. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L434)
- Flos requests more [Builders] from Hellios and Germina to colonize old villages and rich farmland. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L428)
