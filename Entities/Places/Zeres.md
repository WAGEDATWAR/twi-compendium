# Zeres
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1
> See also: Walled Cities, Antinium, Drake cities
## Overview
- Zeres is a Walled City that the Antinium encircled and besieged during the first Antinium war. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L77)
## History
- After a week of day-and-night warfare the Antinium were rebuffed from Zeres with extreme casualties and virtually no losses among the defenders. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L78)
- The Walled Cities' three-hundred-foot walls are held up by magic that hardens the stone and lets it repair itself given time, and no army has ever taken a Walled City by siege. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L79)
- The Antinium's living ladders were too short to reach the battlements, and defending archers and mages rained down fire while Antinium arrows could not reach the defenders. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L80)
- The ground around the Walled Cities was impenetrable to conventional tools because of enchantments radiating from the walls, so Antinium Workers dug until their limbs broke. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L83)
- Zeres stood, and the Antinium could not abandon the siege without the army being attacked from behind, which slowed their advance along with the sieges of other Walled Cities. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L84)
## Later events
- Later, Zeres lodges a formal complaint about the Yellow Rivers illness in the city, where [Healers] are fighting it, and its High Command orders any vessel with Yellow Rivers refused entrance to the harbor. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1322)
