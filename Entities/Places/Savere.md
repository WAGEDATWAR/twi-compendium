# Savere
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.14K
> See also: Flos, Tiqr, Illivere, Nerrhavia
## Overview
- Flos says Savere is prosperous, dangerous and seafaring, and that its ruler hates him. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L493)
- Flos names Savere, with Pomle and Illivere, among the nations that are neither Tiqr nor Nerrhavia and are all dangerous. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L492-493)
- Savere's ruler is the Siren of Savere, Revine. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L427)
- In character Savere falls between Tiqr and Illivere: its people are wild, ride forward without order, laughing, and some look like folk from the waters. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L425)
## Revine
- Revine arrives at a gathering of monarchs walking along a path that the waters form for her in the sands. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L423)
- Revine, the Siren of Savere, is open about her hatred for Flos, saying his armies looted Savere's lands and treasuries. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L480,L502)
- Savere is one of four major nations, with Tiqr, Nerrhavia and Illivere, that send leaders to the meeting. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L459)
