# Ruins of Albez
> Type: place | Also called: Albez; Albez Ruins | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.02R
> See also: Celum, Horns of Hammerad
## Overview
- The Ruins of Albez sit at the heart of what was once a magical kingdom, a community of mages or an ancient citadel, and the whole area is saturated in magic. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L2-3)
- The ruins attract both monsters, who nest among the ruined buildings and endless underground tunnels, and adventurers seeking lost treasure. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L3)
- Adventuring groups get official permission to search the ruins for a set period, which lets the nearby cities profit by charging access and keeps adventurers from conflict. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L6)
## Location
- Garia notes that it is a long way from the Ruins of Albez to Celum for someone who is not a Runner. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L150)
- Ceria says the Horns' last mission before Liscor's ruins was digging through the Ruins of Albez, near Celum. (Book 2, Ch2.00H L332)
- Yvlon says that the Ruins of Albez are up north. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L493)
## History and condition
- The Horns of Hammerad occupy the ruins during Ryoka's first view of them. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L4)
- Ceria gets a few enchanted items there, including spelled cooking equipment, an old recipe book and a few expensive plates, enough to cover healing costs. (Book 2, Ch2.00H L337)
- Yvlon says the ruins are famous for treasure but mostly cleaned out decades ago, and that adventuring teams find something extremely valuable only now and then. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L493)
- Ceria says that Albez was unearthed during an earthquake, and that most of the traps and all the big monsters and guardians of the dungeon are gone. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L498)
- The city has sunk into the earth, leaving a basin of half-buried walls and pitfalls, with shifted passages that do not match the maps. (Book 3, Ch3.02H L127-129)
- The Horns under Calruz went into Albez four times, as did other Silver-rank teams; surviving Albez is practically a rite of passage before harder requests. (Book 3, Ch3.02H L150)
