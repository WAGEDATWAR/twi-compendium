# Ailendamus
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.21KQ
> See also: Medain, Kaliv, Pheislant, Itorin II, Tyrion Veltras, Dawn Concordat
## Overview
- Ailendamus is a Terandrian kingdom with an army equipped with armor and blades, ruled by His Glorious Majesty Itorin II, who has ambitious plans and is laying the groundwork for aggression. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L860-862)
- The narration says Ailendamus has swelled in recent years while facing the Dawn Concordat of Calanfer, Kaliv and Gaiil-Drome, known for breaking nations sent against it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L17)
## Conflict with the south
- {event} Ailendamus warships clash twice with Pheislant vessels and blockade trade to the Dawn Concordat, intercepting ships and confiscating or losing goods in 'routine searches'. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L18)
- Wistram's Council settles only for a letter of condemnation after Viltach trades his Libertarians' votes for a referendum inviting Chandrarian bazaars to a Wistram fair, defeating Feor. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L28-29)
- {event} Mercenaries led by an Ailendamus [Sword Captain] raze a farm on the Tergil River in Kaliv, declaring the land forfeit by order of Itorin II. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L865-871)
- The Kaliv [Farmer] says the land was taken in a war about two hundred years earlier and ratified by the Treaty of Everdel, and the [Sword Captain] says Ailendamus no longer recognizes that treaty. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L865,L869-871)
- Ailendamus suffered a bloody nose in the Lord of the Dance incident, losing an entire fleet and suffering diplomatically, though it screamed it had been a trick. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L65)
- Ailendamus offered Hethon, Lord Veltras' older son, the hand of a [Baroness], or an arranged marriage with a noble of similar rank, for Tyrion's noninterference. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L71)
- Tyrion refused instantly, as insulted, and asked for Princess Ozena, who was a decade older than Hethon, and an emissary replied that King Itorin would never marry his daughter to a distant noble house. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L72-74)
- {state} Ailendamus declares war on the Dawn Concordat, marching its armies over Kaliv's border and blockading nations such as Pheislant. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L135)
- Ailendamus seized a cargo of the cure for Yellow Rivers to deny it to Pheislant and other coastal nations. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L274)
- A report says Ailendamus' blockade continues while the cure appears on black markets. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L380)
