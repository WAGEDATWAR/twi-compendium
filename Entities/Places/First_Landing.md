# First Landing
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.09
> See also: Izril, Invrisil, Magnolia Reinhart, Tyrion Veltras
## Overview
- Fals describes First Landing as the biggest city, found by going north to the ocean, over three thousand miles north of Liscor, perhaps closer to four thousand. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L66,L173,L262)
- Its watchful towers keep vigil over the north sea, built long ago by Human nobles who fled Terandria in fear that others would follow. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L45)
- It is called the first and grandest of the Human cities built on Izril, and the ancient seats of power were established there, including the Reinhart mansion, which is taller than some palaces. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L45-47)
## History
- In the war against the Goblin King, the Humans' oldest and grandest city on the continent was the Goblin armies' target and was regarded as the metaphorical heart of the Human people. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L236-237)
- A history notes that the Walled Cities predate the construction of First Landing by at least six thousand years. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L237)
- {event} Eight Goblin Lords besieged First Landing for three weeks, and the Five Families gathered their forces for a stand at its walls. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L238-239)
- Lord Tyrion Veltras took command of the city's defense while Lady Magnolia Reinhart marshaled the rest of the nobility. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L240)
