# Invrisil
> Type: place | Also called: City of Adventurers; Origin of Seeking Death (Goblin name) | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.00E
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Izril, Goblin Lord
## Overview
- Invrisil is called the city of adventurers, named for having the largest population of adventurers. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L55)
- Durene says Invrisil has Gold-rank teams, sometimes Named Adventurers passing through, and markets full of magical items, monster parts, rare gems and artifacts. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L56)
- The narration calls Invrisil the heart of the northern half of Izril, a place that would appear on any map of the world. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L187)
## Description
- Reynold says the [Guardsmen] at the gates check all visitors, even Runners, because Invrisil sees far more magical artifacts and dangerous items than most places. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L175-181)
- The narration says its streets hold String People, Drakes, Minotaurs, Gnolls and Humans, so it is more varied than a Human city like New York. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L190-191)
- Many buildings are painted or given artistic fronts by [Painters] and [Muralists], and hanging orbs of crystal give light even by day, some enchanted to change color. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L193-194)
- Reynold points out that the city guards are fine warriors, some of them retired adventurers. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L195-196)
- Invrisil has a separate route for vehicles so wagons do not mix with pedestrians. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L197)
- Later narration says Invrisil is guarded by low ten-foot walls that seem to expand each year, and that it is one of the largest cities on the continent and a hub connecting the northern Human lands. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L245)
- The Gold-ranks have their own section of the adventurers' guild, reached through an ordinary door. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L490)
## Ownership and culture
- Invrisil is owned, at least in part, by Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L246)
- A gate [Guard] tells the Players that Lady Reinhart encourages the arts and that every inn and tavern in Invrisil has something special. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L398)
- The narration describes Invrisil as a trading hub that dwarfs Celum and Liscor. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L396)
- A merchant selling balms says Invrisil is the last major city going south and gets trade from all over. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L425)
- A performer says a Level 40 [Troubadour] in the city steals audiences from other musicians. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L481)
## Role in Book 6
- The Goblin Lord tells Osthia that the Origin of Seeking Death is the Goblin name for Invrisil and asks if the City of Adventurers can be taken. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L100-104)
- {event} The Goblin Lord decides to go around Invrisil to a lone mountain northeast of it, saying he needs more Goblins and tribes, and that there are more past the city. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L148-153)
- The narration says Invrisil had not come under attack since the Second Antinium Wars and, with the Goblin Lord's armies near, its adventurers are in hiding. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L13)
## Later events
- {event} Later, the gates are closed at night by the Mayor's orders because a decomposing flesh giant was sent at the walls less than a week earlier. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L11-14)
