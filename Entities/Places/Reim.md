# Reim
> Type: place | Also called: the King of Destruction's city | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.00K
> See also: Flos, Chandrar, Jecrass, Hellios, Orthenon, Gazi
## Overview
- Reim is the home kingdom of the King of Destruction, shown as a small dot in the northeast of Chandrar, closer to the border than to the center. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L226)
- Desert and arid land surround Reim, and the map marks a small green patch for the deep underground streams from which it draws its water. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L227-228)
## Palace and city
- Servants and workers in the palace walk on the right side of corridors, those going the other way on the left, with the center left for people in a hurry or carrying bulky things. (Book 5, Ch4.01K L106)
- Trey sees almost no young people in Reim and fears that, if Flos goes to war, there would be nobody left to come back. (Book 5, Ch4.01K L211-214)
- A speaker says Drevish spent the least time on Reim of all his projects and that its walls are merely thirty feet high without added fortifications. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L401)
- Flos says Reim has never been besieged, since he was always on the offensive and Reim sat at the heart of his empire. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L419-421)
- Reim has sixteen towers, and a rune diagram on a tower wall forms a map of the city. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L575-576,L599,L603)
- Gazi says the enchantment [Storm Keep] was made by Amerys and Drevish to destroy entire armies, and that it was never used. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L609-613)
- When Gazi presses her hand to the diagram and traces a line toward the city's center, lightning from the sixteen towers gathers into a ball above the city. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L578,L607-608)
## Siege preparations and refugees
- {event} Flos orders his storehouses opened for frightened, homeless people fleeing to Reim. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L265-267)
- Teres notes that hundreds of caravans came to Reim in one month, many numbering in the thousands, carrying Flos' subjects who still think well of him and are sheltered by his proclamation of peace. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L154)
- It has rained for days, which a speaker calls unheard of out of season. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L88)
- Flos says he issued an [Edict of Bloom], and Teres's Yellat plant grew in seven days instead of the seventy it usually takes. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L99-105)
- A speaker says refugees were safe in Jecrass, where King Raelt gave free passage and let them barter for supplies, but many have become targets. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L419)
- Flos orders potions split and given to anyone in need, and requests more [Builders] from Hellios and Germina for old villages and rich farmlands. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L427-428)
## War
- {event} The King of Destruction orders a retreat and Orthenon to strike Jecrass' army, and Reim's army withdraws in orderly fashion while Raelt wonders why. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1111-1116)
- {state} Jecrass and Belchan are now at war with Reim, with King Raelt in control of both nations. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1164-1166)
- {event} Nerrhavia's Fallen, the Claiven Earth, Savere and a dozen other nations declare war on Reim. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L1206-1207)
- Hellios and Germina, lands Reim seized, now neighbor nations that have declared war. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L1207)
