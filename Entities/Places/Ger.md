# Ger
> Type: place | Also called: the Seat of Ger (palace) | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.12K
> See also: Germina, Flos, Quarass
## Overview
- Ger is the capital of Germina, a city built around an oasis from which the kingdom was founded. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L19)
- The oasis is a vast reservoir of water that has sustained Germina for thousands of years, and the first Quarass claimed the land there. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L29)
- The palace of the Quarass, known as the Seat of Ger, is the tallest building in the city, standing on a slope atop the city and directly over the reservoir that feeds the wells. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L42)
## History
- {event} The King of Destruction personally leads his armies into Ger, cuts down everyone in his way, climbs to the Palace of the Quarass, and beheads her. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L19)
- Nearly a month after the Quarass's passing, Flos's armies still occupy much of Ger and the country to keep order. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L22)
- Germina is a Shield Kingdom, one of four remaining, ancient and sometimes waning but never erased. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L29)
