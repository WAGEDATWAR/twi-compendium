# Chandrar
> Type: place | Also called: Chandrarian (adj.) | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00K
> See also: Flos
## Overview
- Chandrar is the continent on which Trey and Flos' court stand, a hot, dry place that Trey likens to Egypt or a Middle-Eastern country. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L126)
- {belief} Flos says that his kingdom is wasted and its walls crumble while food is scarce. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L139,L142)
## Description
- Chandrar has two moons, one pale and yellowish like the one back home but tinged slightly blue, and one purple. (Book 5, Ch4.01K L512)
- Outside the city the ground is dry and dusty with shrubs of tough greenery growing from arid earth. (Book 5, Ch4.01K L512)
