# Market Street
> Type: place | Also called: the market street | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.36
> See also: Liscor, Liscor market
## Overview
- Market Street is a street of closely packed wooden stalls in Liscor, meant as temporary structures that can be taken down for the next shopkeeper. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L51)
- A fire sweeps through the street and destroys nearly half of it, even cracking the paving stones from the heat. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L51-52)
- Relc says the fire was started by the mystery thief, caught the night before, who used a fire spell to escape. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L66-68)
## Recovery and trade
- Not long after the fire, the unburnt half of the street has resumed business as usual and Workers have already replaced a large part of the burnt half. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L221)
- A Guardsman now walks the street every few minutes, and Humans who enter get unfriendly looks, though shopkeepers who know Erin do not mutter insults at her. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L222)
- In winter the street has braziers, iron poles and basins of burning coal that warm the air, and some shopkeepers cook food over them for passers-by; Erin buys roasted meat from a Gnoll. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L49-53)
- Erin is told that cooking on the street is free except for a fee of five silver coins if she makes over four gold coins' worth of profit. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L44)
- After Erin's example, every Gnoll and Drake food vendor on the street sells hamburger variants, with cheeses, spice blends or different meats, and one Drake sells a quadruple-patty version. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L264-267)
