# Liscor bathhouses
> Type: place | Also called: the public bathhouses | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.22
> See also: Liscor
## Overview
- Liscor has public bathhouses with hot, scented water that cost five coppers to enter, which Erin pays at the same rate as Drakes. (Book 1, Ch1.22 L47)
- Gnolls and other Beastkin pay twice as much as Drakes because of their fur. (Book 1, Ch1.22 L47)
- The bathhouses are of high quality, like Roman ones in Ryoka's eyes, and rent private bathing spots for a small fee. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L204-205)
- Bathers need not wash before entering, since purification spells are used. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L206)
- Drakes and Gnolls bathe in separate rooms, and the baths are also gender-specific. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L207-210)
- Erin says Gnolls are touchy-feely while Drakes are not, and that Selys told her she once went to the bathhouse with Krshia, so the separation is not a strict rule. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L210)
