# Illivere
> Type: place | Also called: Illivere Federation; Illivere League | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.27M
> See also: Tiqr, Femithain, Savere, Nerrhavia's Fallen
## Overview
- Illivere is a mage-state that produces Golems, and its leader is the Magus-Crafter Femithain. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L197,L199)
- Pisces says Femithain is a cautious and pragmatic ruler who may have been reluctant to declare war on Tiqr. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L199)
- The Illivere Federation is a group of smaller states with several thousand years of unity and roots in Golem crafting even earlier. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L199)
- Illivere borders Nerrhavia's Fallen and is a small fish in a large pond, with its might resting in Golems. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L200)
- Each Illivere state votes for a smaller group of Crafters, the elite who manufacture or manage Golems, who in turn vote in the Magus-Crafter. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L201)
- The nation's capital is Demarsel, for now, because Femithain belongs to the state of Dellva, so the head of Illivere shifts when the next Magus-Crafter is elected. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L279)
## Role in the Tiqr war
- Ressa reports that the Illivere League launched an immediate assault into Tiqr. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L261)
- Illivere sends only eight thousand soldiers and four hundred Golems against Tiqr. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L200-203)
- The narration says Illivere's eight thousand are experienced [Soldiers] but its strength lies in the Golems. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L206)
- {state} Tiqr surrenders to Illivere rather than to the coalition, and Femithain keeps Nsiia as a political prisoner of Illivere. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L42,L264)
