# Archmage's mansion
> Type: place | Also called: the Archmage of Izril's mansion | First appears: Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Fierre
## Overview
- The mansion belongs to the Archmage of Izril, who has been a recluse for a decade. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L80; Book 18, Ch7.41 L114)
- The visible mansion is surrounded by a barren circle of about two hundred feet where no grass grows and no rats forage. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L16)
- A thrown rock sets off nothing, but stepping onto the ground makes it explode, and life-sensing spells in the ground trigger artillery bolts from the mansion. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L19-27)
- The real mansion is invisible and rests on a cliff away from the death-trapped minefield, and the illusion fooled market-grade artifacts. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L41)
## Ryoka's attempt
- Ryoka notes that a bare patch of land surrounds the mansion on the rat-infested, grassy isle. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L117)
- Fierre steps onto the first magical landmine and explodes. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L122)
- The Archmage watches a homing [Spear of Light] enhanced to nearly triple its yield and an artillery spell go off at the intruders. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L124-125)
- Fierre says she thinks the spell was Tier 4, since Tier 5 can vaporize and [Fireball] is Tier 3. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L151-154)
- The invisible mansion rests on the cliff's edge, and Ryoka only knew it was there, until a door appears. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L203)
