# Elvallian
> Type: place | Also called: Ellavian | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.25N
> See also: Baleros, Forgotten Wing Company, Niers Astoragon
## Overview
- Elvallian is one of the foremost trading hubs in Baleros, once an obscure city marooned in the jungle. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L14)
- Elvallian is the headquarters of the Forgotten Wing Company, one of the Four Great Companies of Baleros, when it is not on campaign. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L14)
- Aspiring [Strategists] come to Elvallian to learn from Niers Astoragon, whom many claim is the highest-level [Strategist] in the world. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L14)
- The city is large, built up around the front of the citadel, and one of Baleros' trading hubs, with bustling streets. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Foliana L365)
