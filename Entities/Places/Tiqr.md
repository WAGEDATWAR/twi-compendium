# Tiqr
> Type: place | Also called: Empire of Tiqr; Empire of Beasts | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.27M
> See also: Nsiia, Chandrar, Pomle, Kilalle, Nerrhavia's Fallen, Illivere
## Overview
- Tiqr is a prosperous empire of savannas and oases that is renowned for its [Beastmasters], and its [Empress] is known as the Empress of Beasts, Nsiia. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L133)
- Pisces says Tiqr is among the few nations openly sympathetic to, or at least not opposed to, the King of Destruction. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L120)
- Tiqr is allied with the Garuda-led nation of Kilalle to the west, which is militarily inferior. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L140)
## War
- Ressa reports that after Jecrass, Belchan and Nerrhavia's Fall affirmed the King of Destruction's claim, Tiqr was already on the defensive. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L258)
- Ressa says the Illivere League and Savere launched immediate assaults and Nerrhavia moved in, and that war golems and Savere's [Raiders] forced Tiqr's forces to retreat. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L261)
- Pisces says that on the twenty-seventh day of the conflict, a coalition of about ten or eleven nations led by Nerrhavia is attacking Tiqr, with Nerrhavia's Fallen the largest power. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L114,L117,L120)
- Vasraf is told that Kilalle is not coming, as their flocks send only regrets. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L162-165)
- Savere and Nerrhavia offered Tiqr surrender terms, and Nsiia says surrender would be absolute and give her people to slavery. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L635-636)
- Nsiia leaves the surrender letters from Savere's Siren and Nerrhavia's Queen Yisame unopened. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L692)
- {event} Tiqr's army, nearly eighty thousand strong, breaks the encirclement toward the Kilalle Steppes while Nsiia delays by surrendering. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L29-30,L37,L41)
- {event} Later, General Vasraf swears to liberate Tiqr and flees to the Kilalle Steppes with the rest of the army and nearly a hundred thousand citizens. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L300)
- {state} Tiqr surrenders to Illivere and not to the coalition, and Nsiia says Tiqr is no more and she is no longer an [Empress]. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L42-44)
- {state} Femithain makes Nsiia a political prisoner of Illivere, whose army guards her, while Nerrhavia garrisons Oliphant. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L255-264)
## Aftermath
- Tiqr's lands were seized by other nations, which must garrison them, while its army still attacks from the Kilalle Steppes. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L269)
- Refugees fleeing the war in Tiqr have settled in Pomle. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L176)
