# Medain
> Type: place | Also called: Kingdom of Adventurers | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.10K
> See also: Perric, Jecrass, Ailendamus, Flos
## Overview
- {belief} A Gnoll traveler recalls that Medain's [King] did not want them there long, and that they had a difficult time gathering supplies there, unlike in Jecrass. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L680)
- Flos says he knows Medain's [King] but must ask Orthenon his name. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L683)
- A group of Medain [Lancers] shouts a battle cry naming High King Perric, the Golden Ranks and the Kingdom of Adventurers. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L10)
## War and cure
- A speaker infers from the Silver-rank and Gold-rank adventurers fighting beside Jecrass' army that Medain is supporting Jecrass. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L56)
- Raelt's peace letter reaches King Perric among other monarchs. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L701)
- {event} A Medain Knight-Captain sends about four hundred [Lancers] against a breakaway Jecrass wing, which smashes them. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L8-17)
- Medain's ruler seized a cargo of the cure for Yellow Rivers out of desperation, because Yellow Rivers ran in its ports. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L274)
