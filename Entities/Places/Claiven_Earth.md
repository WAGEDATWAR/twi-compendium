# Claiven Earth
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.14K
> See also: Medain, Chandrar, Flos
## Overview
- Flos says that Claiven Earth is the name its people give themselves, that it hates him, and that it is one of the few nations he could not conquer outright before his slumber. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L502)
- The Claiven Earth is a small, reclusive but incredibly dangerous nation settled long ago by half-Elves who came from Terandria and grew a forest along the coast. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L533)
- During his first rise to power, even the King of Destruction chose to make peace rather than fight the Claiven Earth to the last. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L533)
## Neighbours and position
- Claiven Earth is named, with the Thalassocracy of Medain, as one of two major powers neighbouring a realm that is also adjacent to the Helliosians and Germina. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L200)
- A character says that the Claiven Earth and Medain would declare war before letting Flos cross their borders to the north. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L771)
- Merreid is described as lying far to the north, west of the Claiven Earth, almost halfway across the continent. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L471)
## In the wars of Chandrar
- After A'ctelios declares war, Nerrhavia, the Claiven Earth and Oteslia declare war too. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L1214)
- Armies including the Claiven Earth march on Belchan for a piece of the nation. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L458)
- The Claiven Earth sends troops to support Jecrass as "mercenaries", among them half-Elf [Archers], and its Mages include thirty-plus high-level ones. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L410,L426)
- After Mars kills many of its half-Elven [Archers], the Claiven Earth writes to the King of Duels that the loss is too high a toll and that it will send scrolls of enchanted munitions instead of lives. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L434-435)
- The Claiven Earth is represented in a mirror conference of rulers by a half-Elf, the Speaker of the Trees, a position that is not permanent. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L530)
- A character says the Claiven Earth does not like to die on foreign soil. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L557)
