# Reinhart manor
> Type: place | Also called: Magnolia Reinhart's mansion | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.30
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Ressa, Reynold
## Overview
- Reynold names the building ahead as the manor of Magnolia Reinhart, and Ryoka sees it as a mansion guarded by high walls and a single gate. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L45-46)
- Reynold drives through gates that swing open at once with no gatekeeper in sight, and Ryoka wonders if they are automatic magical gates. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L47)
## Description
- A whale swims across the stone front of the house as if it were a sea. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L52)
- A line of metal giants, suits of armor with empty visors, stands at attention flanking the entrance. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L52)
- The air around the mansion is warmer than the winter outside, so that Ryoka feels as though it is spring again. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L52-53)
- A cloud circles Magnolia's garden and, overhead, opens up to rain on Ryoka alone. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L71-72)
## The attack
- {event} The [Assassins] and Nemor attack Magnolia in her home, striking the servants first. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L139)
- After the battle the courtyard is left with burning ground and fallen bodies, and Nemor is reduced to a crater of black ash. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L128-130)
- {state} One wounded manservant has his leg burnt away, though Ressa and Magnolia see that the wound is healed. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L246)
