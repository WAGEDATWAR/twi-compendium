# Goblinhome
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.12G
> See also: Rags, Badarrow, Wyverns, Mountain City tribe
## Overview
- Goblinhome is the name of the fortress Rags built in the mountains with the remnants of every tribe, including the Flooded Waters Tribe, the Mountain City Tribe, the Redfangs, the Cave Goblins and a few survivors among Reiss' subjects. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L305)
- The Mountain City Tribe dug out the fortress, which is a copy of Tremborag's mountain. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L198)
## Defenses
- Part of the fortress is a huge wall of wood built into the stone on one side of a valley. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L84)
- Arrow slits dug into the rock and camouflaged let Goblins shoot crossbow bolts from the darkness. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L97)
- A dozen hidden passages lead to the roof, each trapped with a fire-trap hatch that explodes in the face of anyone following. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L387-391)
- {event} Goblinhome uses its limited bags of holding, relics of tribes or stolen from the Liscor battlefield, to drop boulders from walkways, including an eighty-pound boulder from a hundred feet up. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L360-364)
- {event} Wolf Riders on Carn Wolves use bolas tied to bags of holding to snag Wyverns, so that boulders of about four hundred pounds suddenly appear and drag the Wyverns down; there are four such bags. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L402-406)
- {event} Goblins fire a ballista of Wyvern hide, sinew and bone at the Wyvern Lord, and the force of the shot tears it free of its mounting. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L429-433,L456)
- The last defense against adventurers is a trap-hill with crossbow Cave Goblins and a single Hobgoblin [Greatbow Sniper] on the cliffs behind a Thunderbow, who has killed exactly three adventurers. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L363)
