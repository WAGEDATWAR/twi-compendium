# Elbe
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems
> See also: Illivere, Dellva
## Overview
- Elbe is the capital of one of the neighbouring city-states of Dellva within the nation of Illivere, whose own capital moves with the elected Magus-Crafter. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L279)
- Elbe is a splendid old capital, flatter than Dellva's, with worn stone buildings, an ancient palace and the coliseum where the Testing is held. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L792)
- Elbe is about six hours' travel from Dellva. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L734)
- Elbe's academy and the Purifier are stops on Nsiia's tour of the state. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L737)
- Elbe holds one of the last relics of the Golem Empires of old, which was not lost so much as willfully discarded. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L750)
- A guide says that Elbe believes it is the true capital of Illivere, while another replies that each state has its own wonders. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L793-794)
