# Khelt
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.54K
> See also: Fetohep, Chandrar, Flos, Reim
## Overview
- The Quarass tells Trey that Khelt is a necrocracy, one of two remaining in the world, and an ancient kingdom whose borders are seldom questioned. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L802)
- The Quarass says Khelt is not a Shield Kingdom, since Germina is older, and that Fetohep is the latest of its rulers and is six hundred years old. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L805)
- Jecrass sells some water to Khelt each month, and Fetohep pays dearly for water Jecrass can spare. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L210-211)
## Land and people
- Khelt is a green forest in the desert, with plants and trees blooming across its land. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L821)
- Its villages are built of carved stone, painted without wear, with fully maintained paved streets and roads even outside the capital. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L822,L826)
- The people are well dressed, look healthy, and some are pudgy. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L822,L824-825)
- Hundreds of skeletons harvest, sow and water the fields, and a skeletal horse pulls a plough. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L827-829)
- The Quarass says that undead crave death, that Khelt's undead are held by their [King], and that other undead might seize control of them. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L838)
- Khelt has a playground with a wooden fortress, rope bridges, a slide and swings, because children there are not apprenticed or put to work. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L841-843)
- The Quarass says Khelt's children are safer than anyone because Fetohep's reach touches all his land and his people are buried everywhere. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L849-850)
- The capital has walls only to block sand and no fortifications like Reim or other cities. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L851)
## Law and society
- Fetohep says slavery is banned within Khelt and outsiders are banned, as they spread diseases and violence. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L135,L139)
- Fetohep says his people work four of seven days and give three of the rest to pleasures, working less than laborers elsewhere. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L355)
- {reveal} Fetohep says his people are sterile without his blessing. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L365)
- Fetohep says his people may leave Khelt at any time with his blessing, coin and provisions, and no child may be taken, but those who leave may not return. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L378)
- Fetohep says his fifteenth predecessor, King Thekheldan, observed his subjects grown apathetic and quarrelsome, and the reforms since have kept Khelt's people working so that few take to pure indulgence. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L353)
- In the same line, Fetohep calls Thekheldan the third of Khelt's rulers, which contradicts calling him the fifteenth predecessor. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L353)
## Water
- Fetohep says Khelt uses artifacts such as Oasis Tears, gems that produce limited water, of which he has enough for a few barrels an hour. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L307)
- Fetohep says [Hydromancers] drawing water from the air take from an already dry land, so it is better to have them remove salt from seawater, and Khelt imports water when necessary. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L311)
- Fetohep says every drop of rain and the oceans imported over centuries are stored beneath Khelt, enough for a year or a decade. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L311-314)
- Khelt pays lavishly for [Mages] to carry water from the sea and bring rains each year. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L314)
