# Ruins of Liscor
> Type: place | Also called: the ruins; the Ruins by Liscor | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.32
> See also: Liscor, Antinium Hive, Ruins of Albez
## Overview
- The ruins near Liscor lie about ten miles out of the city according to one account, with so much traffic to and from them that Erin and Selys hitch a ride on a cart. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L3)
- At the ruins, a tent city of makeshift shelters surrounds an excavation pit three times the size of a football stadium, which has unearthed a dark stone opening that Erin and Selys are told is a doorway into a crypt. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L4-5)
- More than a hundred, mostly human, adventurers are in the city and the tent city. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L4)
- A Drake shepherd found the ruins a few months earlier when he tripped on a rock that proved to be part of a building; the Antinium were called, and the hill turned out to be the whole structure. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L107-109)
- The ruins are half-uncovered against a large hill, with a black stone entrance and carved doorway; the roof and lower half have not been excavated, and an Antinium dug down over a hundred feet without reaching the bottom. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L110-113)
- The doors are big enough for two creatures twice Calruz's height or two wagons at once. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L114)
## Dangers and exploration
- The ruins are still filled with dirt and dried mud from years of burial, and adventurers dig while fending off giant burrowing rats, various undead, spiders and other low to mid-level monsters. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L7)
- Several teams find small caskets of silver and jewels, suggesting an old crypt, but the more adventurous teams that went further in never come back, and some return as shuffling undead. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L8-9)
- Around fifteen armed warriors, mainly Drakes and a few Gnolls, guard the doors; Bronze-rank teams and members of the Watch are on permanent guard duty because the place disgorges monsters, such as a mob of zombies, every few days. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L121,L128)
- {belief} Yvlon proposes a six-team raid, claiming the ruins are practically untouched beyond the first level and that Liscor leaves the investigation to adventurers. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L19)
- Calruz says the ruins are less than an hour from the city. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L280)
- Another account says the ruins are several miles from the city. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L74)
- A scout reports empty rooms, urns of dust and old non-magical scrolls, and a few undead. (Book 1, Ch1.02H L25-29)
- Deeper, the adventurers find an open room of stone altars and slabs long enough for a body, which Ceria realises is a crypt where bodies are placed to be cleansed before burial. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L355-362)
- {event} A member of the Watch reports squiggly white tentacled things in the ruins and the suspicion of a brood monster, with the other teams clearing rooms one at a time. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L136)
