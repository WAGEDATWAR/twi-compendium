# Merreid
> Type: place | Also called: the Labyrinth City; the Bazaar of Fables; the city that thinks | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.09K
> See also: Zeikhal, Claiven Earth, Shield Kingdoms
## Overview
- Merreid is a Shield Kingdom, a single city in the desert of Zeikhal, far north and west of the Claiven Earth, near the top edge of the Great Desert, where it sits on a trading route. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L471,L493)
- Merreid is called the Labyrinth City, the Bazaar of Fables, and the city that thinks. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L581)
- Merreid is a safe haven whose bazaars are truly neutral, and no one may break its laws, not even its rulers. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L546)
## Defense
- Its builders trusted to craft and law to make something larger than any person, and dragons and ancient Drake fortress-cities never took it. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L509)
- Djinn serve as Merreid's defenders, bound by an order given before the first Quarass was born to maintain and defend the city, and no ruler of Merreid can command them to battle. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L515,L546)
- Merreid was founded when Djinni and Jinn were both plentiful, and the Jinn have died while the Djinni are slaves. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L520)
- Merreid's rulers control the people and manage trade, but not the djinn, so they are less influential than other Shield Kingdom rulers. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L551)
