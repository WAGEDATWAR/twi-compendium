# Liscor market
> Type: place | Also called: the market; market street | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.13
> See also: Liscor, Market Street
## Overview
- Liscor's market is a wide, cobblestoned street lined with wooden stalls, and Erin first reaches its food section after what she thinks is an hour or two of wandering. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L103-105)
- Drake shopkeepers there sell goods such as weird blue-leaved plants that look like oversized white carrots, and meat cut to order. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L106)
- During the first game of chess in the street, most customers stop haggling to watch, and a Gnoll sells them food as they watch. (Book 1, Ch1.19 L124)
