# Wishdrinks
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.17S
> See also: Liscor, Tailless Thief
## Overview
- Wishdrinks is a very expensive pub in Liscor, which is to a bar what the Tailless Thief is to an inn, where attractive younger Drakes and Gnolls entertain guests. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L137)
- A troupe plays at Wishdrinks and people dance there. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L134; Book 11, Ch6.29 L86)
- Embria rewards the squad that gathers the most Shield Spider nests with a night at Wishdrinks, which she calls one of the best taverns in the city. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L32)
- Wishdrinks is out of the budget of a Senior Guardsman most of the time. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L92)
- A speaker lists Wishdrinks among the great things to see in Liscor, along with Market Street. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L342)
- Teliv Witherscale works part-time as a [Host] at Wishdrinks. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L1003)
- Yvlon finds Wishdrinks the most popular bar in Liscor, with a waiting list. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L501)
- Wishdrinks has private rooms, plush and comfortable, with friendly staff and food Erin admits is delicate and spiced. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L55)
