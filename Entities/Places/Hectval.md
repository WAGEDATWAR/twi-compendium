# Hectval
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.27
> See also: Liscor, Tailless Thief, Three Cities Alliance
## Overview
- Hectval is a small city closest to Liscor, part of the Hectval-Drisshia-Luldem Alliance, a defensive pact between nearby cities and towns. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L413)
- Hectval is the nearest city past the Bloodfields. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L370)
- The Three Cities Alliance is known for crossbows, strong bows and arrow fletching, with hilly terrain that makes fighting its [Archer] battalions a pain. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L533-535)
## Contact with Liscor
- {event} Hectval sends Liscor a letter, exceptionally formal, asking for an exchange of goods, ideas and visitors and for a meeting, as Liscor's new road nears completion under Master Builder Reikhle. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L405,L421)
- {event} Hectval sends a delegation of some of its city leaders to Liscor, the first guests, and Liscor's Council debates a soccer game to show off. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L369-373)
- {event} A delegation of three dozen well-dressed Drakes with packhorses arrives in Liscor during the chaos, saying it is the delegation from Hectval. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L1339-1342)
- The Hectval guests arrived unannounced and are placed at the Tailless Thief. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L382)
