# Drake cities
> Type: place | Also called: Drake city-states | First appears: Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1
> See also: Liscor, Zeres, Pallass, Antinium
## Overview
- During the first Antinium war the Drake city-states declared a state of emergency, but each city preferred to raise its own army and keep its former alliances rather than join together. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L60)
## History
- The Drake cities' separate armies let the Antinium attack with local superiority and overrun many cities around their Hives. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L61)
- Before the war the cities lived in enduring war and skirmishes with each other, and the time needed to breach walls and magical defenses before reinforcements arrived kept most from being conquered. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L62)
- The Antinium's style of attack overwhelmed walls by sheer numbers and speed, so relief armies found cities already full of dead or the Antinium gone. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L63)
- In less than a week eight cities fell, and although all agreed a coalition army was needed, no city would send its army past its walls for fear of being attacked while undefended. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L64)
## Role in Book 5
- {reveal} A speaker reports that the Goblin Lord crushed both armies sent against him, that Garusa Weatherfur is dead, and that Thrissiam Blackwing ordered both armies to buy time for word to reach the other cities. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L500)
- The news reached Liscor and then the rest of the Drake cities almost instantly through [Message] spells sent by Zel Shivertail and Ilvriss, while Human cities heard only in the morning because [Mages] in the Mage's Guild were monopolized by Drakes. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L530)
