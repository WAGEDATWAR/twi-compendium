# Noelictus
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.33E
> See also: Ailendamus, Belavierr
## Overview
- Noelictus is a Terandrian nation, inland, and inconsequential to Manus's politics. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L102,L109)
- Aldonss authenticates reports from Noelictus about one of Manus's Grade 9 threats. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L103-107)
- News of Noelictus has not spread openly, since the Court of Dusks and Landsreight seem to have tamped rumors down, while the [Witches] of the north are apparently gathering. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L122)
## Role in the Belavierr affair
- A speaker says ghosts returned to walk Noelictus. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L160)
- A Knight asks about the Stitch Witch Belavierr, whom they said was in Noelictus. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L496)
- A knight says that Noelictus's armies, Ailendamus's forces, the entire Hunter's Guild and [Knights] far and wide have a right to her. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L608)
- Hunters of Noelictus travel with Ser Raim. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L613)
