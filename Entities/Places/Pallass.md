# Pallass
> Type: place | Also called: City of Invention; City of Inventions; the Walled City (Pallass) | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.43
> See also: Walled Cities, Liscor, Salazsar, Assembly of Crafts, Chaldion, Venim, Erin Solstice, The Wandering Inn
## Overview
- Ilvriss tells Erin that Pallass is a Walled City and about three hundred and eighty miles from Liscor, roughly the distance to Invrisil. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L164,L415)
- Ilvriss says a veteran [Courier] such as Hawk can travel over a hundred miles in a day, far more than a mounted rider can manage without Skills. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L421-423)
- Nelliam welcomes Erin to Pallass as the north-most of the Walled Cities. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L338)
- The narration calls Pallass one of the six Walled Cities of Izril. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L2)
- Pallass is known as the City of Invention, and one of its guides boasts that it manufactures more arms and potions than any other city on the continent. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L314,L328)
- The narration says each Walled City specializes in a trade, so that Pallass forges weapons and does alchemy while Oteslia is an agricultural giant and Salazsar rules the gem trade. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L207-208)
- The narration says inventors in Pallass can be granted a stipend depending on what they do. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L206)
## Layout and description
- The walls, streets and buildings of Pallass are made of a sandy cream-colored stone. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L18)
- Pallass has four walls, one in each cardinal direction, towering in the sky. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L329)
- The city's urban center sits at the lowest level, and four massive staircases rise toward the walls to reach higher floors of buildings. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L334)
- Each level going down is progressively smaller and circles the interior of the walls, so the city looks like an inverted pyramid or a bowl. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L335)
- Houses sit on ledges that protrude from the walls, water is pumped up to hanging gardens, and the four stairwells descend into the center. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L331)
- The highest residential level is too high to see the walls from. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L297)
- Erin's door is set up in a high residential spot chosen as the first empty space. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L21)
- A guide says any new construction must be approved by an [Architect] and an official who knows the city's layout. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L342)
- The Blacksmith's Quarters were built two years before and occupy the second-highest level, in response to complaints about smoke and noise. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L342)
- A guide says that one of Pallass' [Craftswomen] invented the smock mill the month before, a lighter version of the tower mill that can be built from wood and metal. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L321-323)
- Nelliam shows Erin an elevator worked by a hand crank and gears that descends slowly without magic. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L350-353)
- Nelliam says larger lifts powered by mana stones are expensive, but the city has several to move people from the bottom floors to the top. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L389-390)
- Street Runners and City Runners run or slide down the middle ramp of the stairs because it is faster and does not run people over. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L379-380)
- The city's walls are so wide that they hold emplacements for trebuchets and catapults, room for [Guardsmen] to patrol and walkways for citizens. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L407)
- A guide says visitors on the walls can pay to rent enchanted spyglasses to watch the High Passes and roads. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L410)
- Erin sees the street floors as smooth squares of light beige stone, and four walls filling part of the sky, with other floors built into the walls. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L100-102)
- Pallass fuels its elevators with mana stones, which is partly the reason for its nickname. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L106)
- Erin has heard that Pallass holds over a million souls, and finds a city ten times Liscor believable. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L258)
- A guide describes the city as floor upon floor, each floor the size of a smaller Drake city or larger, in a city of millions. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L206)
- A guide says the library of Pallass has tens of thousands of books. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L252)
- A guide says the main cargo lifts carry goods across the city, forges are on the ninth floor, and an irrigation system of canals and water wheels runs a river up the walls from level to level. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L260)
- A guide says Pallass has five bathhouses, each able to hold thousands at once, fed by the river. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L332)
- On the ninth floor the [Smiths] of Pallass work day and night so that the forges never stop, which gives the city its reputation for quality steel. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L336-337)
- Pallass is described as spontaneity and change, where [Alchemists] devise new potions and [Engineers] built the elevators that carry goods from floor to floor. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L338)
- Erin's magic door is on the eighth floor, below the ninth, and higher floors partly shade lower ones, with magical lights as a second daylight. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L561-562)
- Youths of all species, including Dullahans who control their bodies remotely, draw graffiti on the ceilings under the floors above. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L563-564)
## Government
- Pallass is ruled by the Assembly of Crafts, whose members are elected representatives of the major Guilds, and the speaker compares it to Liscor's Council. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L460)
- Ilvriss says a democracy rules Pallass and mocks the city's shamelessness. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L487)
- A speaker says only Pallass elects its Assembly of Crafts. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L568)
- A speaker says the Assembly must reach consensus, which makes Pallass historically the slowest of the Walled Cities to react to incidents. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L428)
- Senator Errif, a Gnoll of the Scalethrift party, tells Olesm and Embria in public that the Assembly will not act until a vote is called and asks for a day or two to verify Liscor's report. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L474-481,L488)
- The narration says the Assembly of Crafts works alongside an inner group of a few [Senators], the Grand Strategist and [Generals] who do what needs doing. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L510-511)
## Military
- Pallass has multiple armies, each with a specialty, a [General] and a goal, and 1st Army is always stationed in the city. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L512)
- 2nd Army handles foreign affairs and campaigns abroad, and the former 3rd Army, monster slayers, was destroyed under Thrissiam Blackwing. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L513)
- 4th Army under Edellein Blackwing handles resource-acquisition and ambushes and is unofficially called "Pallass' Bandits" by other cities. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L514)
- The Dullahan Duln is 1st Army's [General] and is always in the city, and Edellein is the fourth most trusted and highly-levelled [General] of eight armies. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L515-516)
- The narration says there should be nine armies to match the floors, but the ninth has been hypothetical for decades. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L516)
- General Shirka is the newly-minted [General] of 3rd Army, chosen by High Command because it wanted another Drake. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L523)
- 7th and 8th Army are the only armies led by Gnoll [Generals]. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L539)
## Relations with Liscor and Erin's door
- Pallassians mock Liscor as the home of rain, the Antinium and boredom, and a Pallass citizen boasts that Pallass has three dungeons in the area and is six times larger. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L244-245)
- Liscor's Zevara says Pallass exports weaponry and alchemical goods and that over half the arms of Liscor's City Watch come from Pallass, while Liscor has only one or two low-level [Alchemists]. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L305-307)
- Liscor is told that Pallass will impose a trade embargo until Erin's door is returned, and Zevara fears a blockade of the northern trade route. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L301,L307)
- Pallass has strict laws on bringing monsters into the city, so Erin's Goblins are a complication. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L16)
- {state} Pallass' Assembly of Crafts drops all sanctions against Liscor and prepares to open the door to Erin's inn. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L195)
- Zevara says Ilvriss has been in communication with his Walled City for days, that a political struggle between Pallass and Salazsar has been going on, and that Salazsar appears to have won. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L198)
- Erin's door can only send two people through without recharging, so Liscor requires request forms at the Watch barracks and Pallass confirms by [Message]. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L200-203)
- Pallass' Belldrakes spread word of the door, and Liscor's army has already sent a detachment north. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L225,L249)
- The Assembly of Crafts does not take Liscor's report on dungeon raids too seriously, since monster raids from dungeons are not uncommon. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L249)
- A speaker complains that Pallass thinks the Liscor dungeon is like its three Bronze and Silver-rank dungeons, which have been cleared. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L357)
- Venim's company waits at Pallass' end of the door with no Assembly authorization to send troops through while Liscor is attacked. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L346-353)
- {belief} A speaker says Pallass makes siege weapons and keeps their secrets zealously guarded, refusing to sell to Humans. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L262)
- Venim sends Liscor a daily list of travelers through the door, which is normally empty. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L229-230)
- Pallass conscripts over a hundred adventurers under Drake law, together with the Assembly's motion to aid Liscor, and sends them through the door. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L684-686,L708)
- Teams that march through include the Gold-rank Flamewardens and Wings of Pallass, the Silver-rank Scaleshard Mages, Tempest Singers, The Tail of Xil and Meliem, and the Bronze-rank Rhine of Blades. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L698-707)
- Krshia notes that Pallassian soldiers in yellow armor had marched into Liscor the day before. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L46)
- {event} A Pallass parade honors the 3rd Infantry, called the Linebreakers, who served at the Vellir Fields outside Rheist, and the 4th Cavalry, of whom Erin sees only eleven pass. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L58-61)
- A Pallass guard stops Erin with a spear at the door because her arrival was not cleared against the daily list of allowed visitors. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L53-63)
- Venim says he will have a list of approved names worked out with Liscor by [Message]. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L142)

- Sergeant Kel inspects Erin at the gate and sends a note to the Watch Captain that she is in Pallass. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L214-229)
