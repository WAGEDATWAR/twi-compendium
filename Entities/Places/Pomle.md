# Pomle
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.15K
> See also: Tiqr, Orjin, Salii
## Overview
- Pomle is a small, almost completely circular nation lying at the intersection of four nations, smaller even than Reim had been, and known as neutral territory. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L292)
- Pomle has no government or rulers, and those who come are free to do as they please, including outcasts, refugees and criminals. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L293)
- Most residents train for combat as [Martial Artists] or hermits practicing weapon styles, in Pomle's single oasis or on the plateaus. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L293)
- Troublemakers, murder and crime are not tolerated, and Pomle's warriors deal with them themselves. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L294)
## Rule
- Only two things hold sway over Pomle: the tradition of the Strongest and Salii. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L294)
- Whoever beats the current leader of Pomle becomes the Strongest, and Orjin won the position eight years earlier and has defeated all challengers. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L295)
- Salii appeared about five years earlier, started ordering people about, and was mostly obeyed because she had good ideas, such as settling quarrels over bathing. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L296)
- The warriors gather by the oasis seldom, only when the Strongest calls them for war or for momentous occasions. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L172)
- The [Martial Artist] community shelters people who have committed murder, so long as they do not break the peace in Pomle, and the Strongest enforces that rule. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L175)
- The Strongest makes the rules until someone challenges him, and Orjin currently holds the position. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L176)
## Refugees and growth
- Orjin has allowed many outsiders in, including refugees fleeing the war in Tiqr, many of whom stayed and made a small settlement that the [Martial Artists] dislike but tolerate. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L176)
- {state} Near the oasis, the only body of water in Pomle, refugees and visitors have built houses, agriculture and a vineyard, and keep golden-furred sheep that crackle with static electricity. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L86-87)
- The narration says a herd of Shockwoolies can generate enough electricity to stop the heart of anything that attacks them, and their fur is good insulation. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L113)
- {state} Salii says she charged fees to the refugees who stayed and used the coin, with loans from [Merchants], to buy animals and crops, so that Pomle now has a budget. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L119)
- {state} Pomle expands for the first time since its inception when Salii buys part of Tiqr's former land from the king of Lamullt, cheaply. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L250,L264,L269)
- Salii plans to hire [Geomancers] to alter the ground into natural defenses, and to use caves in the new territory for training. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L272)
- Salii has an underground chamber with a ring set up to make money from the [Martial Artists]. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L178-180)
