# Liscor
> Type: place | Also called: the Drake city | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.10
> See also: The Wandering Inn, Antinium Hive, Liscor market, Market Street, High Passes, Bloodfields, Floodplains, Celum, Esthelm
## Overview
- Liscor is a walled city of Drakes, built with the help of the industrious Antinium, that is also home to Gnolls and the occasional Beastkin and is visited by many races. (Book 1, Ch1.12 L101)
- Ryoka is told, before she reaches it, that Liscor is a city far to the south with no humans, only Drakes and Gnolls. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L17)
## Appearance and location
- From the inn, Liscor looks like a black spot in the distance; Relc and Klbkch say it is about ten minutes' run away, while Relc says it is only an hour's walk with few monsters on the way. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L330,L340,L357)
- Erin cannot make out the city from the window, and Klbkch says he believes humans have more limited eyesight than Drakes or Antinium. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L344-354)
- Relc says the area within a few miles of the city is regularly patrolled. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L357)
- Klbkch says that only those with a past record of crime are unwelcome in Liscor. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L359)
- Liscor's wall is nearly forty feet tall, and its gate is two solid metal doors instead of a portcullis. (Book 1, Ch1.12 L84-85)
- The city's buildings are of stone and wood with a lot of roundness, gently sloping roofs and open rooftops; the streets are hard dirt, in places paved with cobblestones. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L86)
- Most of the people Erin sees in the streets are Drakes, with the occasional Gnoll and only rarely an Antinium. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L87-88)
- Drakes stare at Erin in the street while Gnolls and other furry people keep as far from her as they can. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L101)
- Dress in the city varies: some male Drakes wear only loincloths and perhaps a light cloak, many female Drakes wear little more, and only the Gnolls follow a human-style dress code. (Book 1, Ch1.14 L11)
- Liscor's bathrooms are marked with tails instead of stick figures: a long straight tail for male, a curly tail for female. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L78)
## Geography and defence
- Klbkch says herds are usually confined in villages north of Liscor, while the city itself hosts pigs, sheep and horses. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L119-122)
- {belief} Relc claims there are only two ways into the valley, north and south, that the mountains are practically impassable and that the Blood Fields guard the southern border. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L149)
- Klbkch replies that the north is still relatively unguarded and worries that the human nations might unite. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L150)
- Relc says the human nations lie to the north and that Drakes have an okay relationship with them. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L154-157)
- Selys explains that Liscor sits near the centre of the continent where it is narrowest, and that it is the only way through the mountain range apart from the High Passes and the coastal routes to east and west. (Book 1, Ch1.28 L227-229)
- Selys adds that without Liscor, goods must travel around the continent by land or sea or brave the High Passes. (Book 1, Ch1.28 L229)
## History
- A Necromancer nearly destroyed the city about ten years before Erin's arrival, according to Relc. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L136)
- {belief} Klbkch says the three most dangerous things to Liscor are said to be rain, the undead and war. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L139)
## The Antinium Colony
- Relc says Liscor is home to a Colony of the peaceful Antinium, one of five in the world (he corrects himself from six). (Book 1, Ch1.23 L182)
- Klbkch says the Antinium have a standing contract with the people of Liscor, providing services and goods in exchange for their presence. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L184)
- Relc explains that the Antinium defend the city if it is attacked, help with construction and other jobs, and send some of their people to work jobs like Klbkch's; in return nobody kills them. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L187-189)
## Events in Book 1
- A merchant tells Erin that prices have risen since the humans' caravans arrived, that the Watch has been picking fights with adventurers, and that a human thief is said to have come with a caravan, so Humans get the blame. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L220-223)
- A Drake says there have been a number of petty thefts across the city, and that Shopkeeper Lism was the latest victim. (Book 1, Ch1.31 L43)
## Further details, Books 1-2
- Ryoka thinks that the closest city to the High Passes is Liscor, though a runner has to go around the mountain range to reach it. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L14)
- Someone tells Erin that the road south of the city was destroyed ten years earlier and that no one has lived south of Liscor since, apart from her. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L34-35)
- Erin judges Liscor bigger than Celum or Esthelm, and a footnote says its walls are second only to those of the Walled Cities, as all Drake cities have gigantic walls. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L485-486)
- The Antinium dug the tunnels of Liscor's sewers but are not obliged to clean them or clear out the monsters. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L73-76)
- Liscor has no hospital: people heal at home, hire a [Healer], or rely on organizations, and the Adventurer's Guild and the Watch each keep a few beds. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L305-306)
- A speaker tells Erin that Liscor has just over ten thousand souls. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L264)
- The center of the city holds a large park with a playground of towers, slides and rope bridges, where a Wistram [Mage]'s spells, checked each year, slow children who fall. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L491-502)
- Liscor is Sserys's home city; in the Antinium Wars it was besieged by a huge Antinium army but repelled it countless times thanks to above-average magical wall defenses. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L40-41)
## Books 1-5 events
- The city was attacked a few weeks before Erin's walk through it with Klbkch in the snow. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L485)
- Klbkch tells Erin that over eighty guardsmen and nearly two hundred civilians died before the Hive was mobilized, and that the Antinium lost sixty-five Workers and forty-seven Soldiers. (Book 1, Ch1.45 L32-35)
- {event} Two Gold-rank teams enter Liscor that evening. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L454)
- Residents have gradually grown used to Pawn's daily patrols, and his Soldiers bring back Shield Spider and Corusdeer carcasses that are turned into meat for the city. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L26-29)
- {reveal} Hawk reports to the crowd that the Goblin Lord has crushed both armies sent against him, that Garusa Weatherfur is dead and that Thrissiam Blackwing had ordered the armies to buy time. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L500)
- The news reaches Liscor and the other Drake cities almost instantly through [Message] spells from Zel Shivertail and Ilvriss, while Human cities hear in the morning. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L530)
## Book 6 to 9 events
- A Drake says that Liscor does not let undead into the city for any reason. (Book 6, Ch4.37O L99)
- The Siege of Liscor in the Antinium Wars lasted three months by the time it reached breaking point, with every adult taking to the walls. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L131)
- {event} News of Zel Shivertail's death strikes the city like a wave, and Drakes stand weeping in the streets while horns blow. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L487-489)
- Klbkch carries the news to the Queen of the Free Antinium and then, through a black mirror, to the Grand Queen. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L490-501)
- Three days after the news, black banners fly at half-mast on the battlements, Drakes weep openly, and more than half the usually open shops are shuttered. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L54-60)
- Zevara has the Watch check all four walls for cracks before the rains, because she knows enchantments can fray like stone. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L314-318)
- Selys explains that every year, after winter, hard rain falls for about two months, the city is practically closed off, and Liscor floods. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L383-387)
- Zevara recalls that Carn Wolves prowled the Floodplains three years earlier and that everyone's [Dangersenses] warned them to stay indoors. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L354)
- {event} During the Face-Eater Moth attack, Zevara orders the wall command spells prepared and the Antinium summoned, siege alarms sound, and a glowing field of energy springs up around the city. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L383-389,L439,L450)
- After the battle, Liscor's dead have been recovered and cremated, buried or otherwise laid to rest, and only moth corpses remain on the walls. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L4-5)
- Embria declares that her 4th Company will henceforth dedicate itself to defending the city against Goblins, dungeon monsters and other threats. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L438)
- {event} Floodwaters rise halfway up Liscor's walls; Drakes and Gnolls leave on rope ladders and a ramp to a floating dock, where a small fleet of boats waits. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L226-232)
- {event} When the rains stop, a rainbow appears over Liscor. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L842-850)
- {event} Messages arrive that other Drake cities are sending aid to Liscor: Oteslia's Winged Riders can arrive in six days, Zeres pledges eighty thousand soldiers and five thousand Saltscale Wardens, and Manus pledges an army. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L546-553)
- {event} Zevara confiscates the inn's magic door and orders the Pallass Gold-rank teams to stay in Liscor, and Ilvriss demands they fight until the walls fall, with the door staying in the heart of the city as an escape route. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L327,L361)
## Book 10 onward
- {state} Eight days after the Goblin Lord's death, the city is short of wood for cremations, and Zevara notes that the eastern gate is still destroyed and cracks in the wall must be repaired before the next spring rains. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L277,L317)
- Zevara says the trial of the Minotaur prisoner will begin soon. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L323)
- Liscor mainly imports goods, helped by the army's mercenary work and some natural resources, and its shops with preservation runes serve as its grocery stores. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L51-52)
- In Liscor, artisans do not sell personally as in Celum and the north but go through [Shopkeepers] as middlemen, and some [Shopkeepers] run warehouses for others. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L52)
- The Council is usually made up of Guildmasters and other prominent citizens who rotate every two years, with eight Councilmembers; Ilvriss stepped in to aid it after the disasters. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L450-451,L551)
- {belief} Krshia tells a crowd that rent has doubled in some parts of the city without the Council acting and that Zevara handled most of the work during the Raskghar attack and the siege. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L560)
- Krshia says Liscor can hold less than a hundred thousand souls at full capacity and cannot grow larger than its walls. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L602)
- {state} Numbtongue is barred from Liscor: the gates slam shut on him three days running, the Watch tells him the Council voted that no Goblins may enter, and Zevara's signed paper naming him an adventurer does not change the orders. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L430-465)
- Erin backs Zevara, who warns that the inn's magic door to Liscor would be removed if Numbtongue used it. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L492)
- {state} Ulseil Greenscale announces the results of an election that replaces the Council with a democratically elected group of eight for two years: Raekea Silversmith, Elirr Fultpar, Krshia Silverfang, Lism Swifttail, Alonna Swiftwing, Jeiss Sielmark, Tismel Lischscale and Zalaiss Holmfyre. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L128,L138-140)
- {event} A new residential district is being built west of the western gate, with new apartments, Antinium Workers hauling stone, and plans for the city to grow a third larger. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L502-514)
- The expansion's plans come from an [Architect] travelling from Baleros, and the Antinium raised a ten-foot temporary outer wall of stone and dirt in a single day. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L519-522)
