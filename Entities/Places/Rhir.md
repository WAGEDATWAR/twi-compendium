# Rhir
> Type: place | Also called: the Blighted Kingdom (its realm) | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.35
> See also: Blighted King, Demons, Crelers, 5th Wall, Walled Cities
## Overview
- Rhir is a continent whose Blighted King rules it as sole ruler and is at war with the Demon King, who leads Demons and monsters. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L175)
- Tom is told that Rhir has been at war for thousands of years, and its citizens cry out "The Walls stand!" and "Rhir! The last war! The endless war!" (Book 6, Ch1.02C L49-51,L175)
- The narration calls Rhir the smallest and most cursed continent, and says the blight that corrupts it is endemic only to Rhir. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L183)
## History
- Ryoka reads that Rhir is always at war with some threat, and that huge armies from every corner of the world have come to take it back. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L92,L96)
- Ryoka reads that the Demons emerged five thousand years back, catching a prosperous set of kingdoms off-guard after several hundred years of peace. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L94)
- Ryoka reads that a record claims the last Crelers were purged, yet in the book's account a black slime then appears and mutates everything around it. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L98-99)
- {belief} Ryoka concludes that something in Rhir, a dead God as Klbkch said, keeps creating new things to destroy it. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L99-100)
- Ryoka reads that a huge army from three continents went underground searching for the root of the problem, none came back, and a year later abominations made from parts of people slaughtered everyone. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L101)
- Lord Hayvon says Rhir has been settled, lost and retaken many times, and that the last time it was lost, about seven thousand years ago, the world chose to abandon it to the blight. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L16)
- The narration says the Creler Wars ravaged the world six thousand years ago, after which the first Blighted King established his kingdom as a safeguard, fortified with help from around the world. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L185)
- The narration says people had avoided living on Rhir because it was inhospitable and filled with corruption that twisted the form, until the Demons arrived and fought the Blighted Kingdom to a deadlock. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L184,L186)
## Geography
- {belief} Tom is told that beyond Rhir there is no world, only a few islands and unexplored depths and then an abyss, and that the world is flat. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L46-47)
- Tom's procession passes six successive outer walls of the city and a seventh being built, each taller than the last, with new housing added with each wall. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L42)
- Lord Hayvon says one of Rhir's walls, the second, lies in the distance, that four walls shelter the Blighted Kingdom and that a fifth is being built. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L17)
- The narration says the Blighted Kingdom has built four, soon five, massive walls across Rhir to hold back the Demons and create safe zones, with the fifth still incomplete. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L187)
- A speaker reports to the king that the 5th Wall is being built and that the Deathless have not emerged since their defeat. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L149)
## Foreign aid
- A soldier tells Tom that every nation has to send an army to support Rhir under the old treaties, and that two Drake armies would be there if it were all-out war. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L174)
- A foreign company says it was sent to assist the Blighted King and escorts lesser dignitaries around. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L161)
- The people of Rhir know their steel is made of metal mined on other continents and shipped by other nations' fleets, and that Drakes die on Rhir's soil to fulfill ancient oaths. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L8)
## Court and champions
- King Othius the Fourth holds court with his steward Nereshal, a [Chronomancer], and watches events through a scrying orb. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L7,L10)
- The Blighted King's champions at one banquet include a black-furred Minotaur, a half-Elf with a self-standing staff, a pale Selphid with two greatswords and many [Knights]. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L435-436)
- A court speaker says that Richard has reached Level 30 and so is considered a peer of the realm, and that those above Level 30 are honored by the crown. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L446)
- {event} An old woman with a spear defeats Richard in a spar, and he is applauded and led to the table of champions. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L439-442)
- {event} When news that Zel Shivertail allied with Lady Magnolia reaches the Blighted King's court, Drake [Soldiers] in the banquet hall brawl over whether he is a traitor. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L316-328)
- Cirille covers her face with her claws during the Drake brawl, while Richard and the other summoned Humans watch from a table with Rhir nobles. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L331-332)
