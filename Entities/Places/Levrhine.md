# Levrhine
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.13K
> See also: Belchan, Jecrass, Flos
## Overview
- Levrhine is the capital city of Belchan and the home of Grand Mage Esiela. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L416,L513)
- Levrhine has its own garrisons and defenders, probably tens of thousands, to prevent a surprise raid. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L423)
## The march on Levrhine
- {event} Raelt of Jecrass takes three days to reach Levrhine, slowed by his battles and the prisoner trains he takes. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L460-462)
- Esiela is forced by contract to march with the other [Mages] against Levrhine, her home, to be attacked before nightfall. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L413-416)
- {event} Orthenon reaches the capital with six thousand cavalry, intending to prevent the Prime Minister from fleeing, and finds a massive army already encircling Levrhine with its gates open and horses pouring in. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L513-514)
- A speaker proposes to take Levrhine first, to claim it before it is destroyed and its people slaughtered. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L570)
