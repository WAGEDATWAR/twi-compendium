# Stream
> Type: place | Also called: the stream | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.03
> See also: The Wandering Inn, Acid flies
## Overview
- The stream runs down a hill a few miles from the inn, and Erin judges it a good spot to gather water or wash. (Book 1, Ch1.03 L2)
- Erin, fleeing the Goblins, clears the stream in a single leap. (Book 1, Ch1.03 L3)
## Uses
- Erin bathes in the cold water with soap bought from Krshia and is startled by a fish in the water that shoots at her like a torpedo. (Book 1, Ch1.22 L51-52)
- Erin fills her bucket from the stream and splashes water over acid flies feeding on the dead fish, which makes them fall onto their backs. (Book 1, Ch1.22 L62-63)
