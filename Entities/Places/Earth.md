# Earth
> Type: place | Also called: home; Ryoka and Erin's world | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.01
> See also: Wistram, The Wandering Inn, Flos
## Overview
- Ryoka reflects that there must be more people from Earth than just her in this world, interdimensional travelers like her. (Book 2, Ch2.01 L129)
- Ryoka tells Erin that they both come from Earth and that she has learned other people from there are scattered around this world. (Book 2, Ch2.02 L78)
- Ryoka concludes that the system of leveling in this world was not created by someone from Earth. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L46)
## On Earth
- Erin remembers that on Earth, her home, she needed alarm clocks to get up and had a phone to check the moment she woke. (Book 4, Ch3.30 L3)
- Trey and Teres, twins from Earth, are served dishes from their home by Flos's kitchen staff, who try to create something from Earth each day with mixed results. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L156)
- {reveal} Two Humans from Earth appeared in Flos's throne room after he had slumbered for over a decade. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L18)
