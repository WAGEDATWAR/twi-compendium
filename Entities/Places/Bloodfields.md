# Bloodfields
> Type: place | Also called: Blood Fields | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.23
> See also: Liscor, Floodplains, High Passes
## Overview
- Ryoka concludes from her books that the human nations send armies to fight and die in the Blood Fields each year against the non-humans of the south. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L303)
- Relc describes the Blood Fields as a battlefield where armies still fight, so soaked with blood that the entire area is full of Blood Grass, which drinks blood and eats people if one is careless; he says he fought there twice. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L98)
- {belief} Relc claims that the Blood Fields guard Liscor's southern border, one of only two ways into the valley. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L149)
- Ryoka reads that the Blood Fields are an empty wasteland designated for battle and one of two gateways linking the northern and southern parts of the continent, where Drake and Human armies collide when one invades the other. (Book 1, Ch1.13R L197)
- {belief} Ryoka half-remembers reading that the dead do not rise, or disappear, in the Blood Fields, which would make it safe from risings. (Book 1, Ch1.13R L198)
## Description
- On her walk through, Ryoka finds the soil turning crimson and learns that the color comes from a moss-like, rooted plant that drinks blood, not from blood itself. (Book 1, Ch1.13R L193,L207-209)
- Nearer the center, red grass and stalks grow, and the end-stage plants are tall, twisted, barkless structures like sentinels; Ryoka's skin test on one of the plants gives no reaction. (Book 1, Ch1.13R L209-211)
- The Bloodfields lie south of Liscor in flatland that slopes away from the High Passes, where Humans and Drakes traditionally fought by mutual consent until the grass mutated red and a blood-craving biome emerged. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L546-547)
- Beyond the pastures in the valley leading out of the High Passes, a crimson stain cuts off the easy route south. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L15)
- The red grass is razor sharp, large bulbous plants with spore-like protrusions and thorny vines dominate the landscape, and sacs on those plants are filled with insects. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L18-19)
- Watchertrees, pale trees sixty feet or taller, stand in vast circular dead zones, and Erin sees a rabbit with teeth that seems more like a predator. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L19)
## Dangers
- Bevussa, teaching Silver-rank adventurers, calls the Bloodfields a Gold-rank threat, meaning Gold-rank teams can and have died there, and says the only safe time is after an army has burned everything away, with the plants back within the month. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L563)
- To demonstrate, Bevussa shoots a pillow-like bulb with a crossbow, and it bursts to release a mass of insects that spread across the ground. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L610-614)
- Bevussa explains that Watchertrees sense anything moving on the soil for hundreds of feet, and that their roots kill everything in the bare ground around them and can spear through unenchanted armor. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L619)
- Bevussa adds that blood slimes and vampiric spiders live there, that most animals are dangerous, even the rabbits, and orders the adventurers to stay at least two miles from the visible edge. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L621)
- {event} A Watchertree spears a fox through the abdomen with a root tendril when the fox goes for a bird lying bare of grass, then absorbs the blood. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L731-733)
- After the Horns' encounter, Reikhle tells Erin that pollen and pulling roots seen there are new and that the Bloodfields are evolving. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1085-1086)
- {event} The barren ground at the foot of a Watchertree stump drinks up Yvlon's blood, the Bloodfields go silent, the red grass sways, and something wakes up. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1142-1144)
