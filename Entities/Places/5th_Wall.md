# 5th Wall
> Type: place | Also called: Fifth Wall | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Rhir, Blighted King
## Overview
- A speaker reports to the Blighted King that the 5th Wall is being built. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L149)
- The 5th Wall that stands is thirty feet tall and not nearly as wide as the 4th Wall, a temporary deception put up by the Blighted Kingdom in a month. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1305-1307)
- Commander Uxel tells Cirille that the 5th Wall they march beside is not the real wall, and the narration says the real one is set nearly two miles back and deeply under construction. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1311-1312)
- The narration says that [Miners] tunnel down to create a moat of moats with a thousand feet of drop before the top, and that Wistram has sent master [Enchanters] but work is slow. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1312)
- {state} The 5th Wall disappears from the war-map, turning into fragmented pieces, as Demon units flood past. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1061)
