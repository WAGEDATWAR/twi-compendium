# Kingdom of Pheislant
> Type: place | Also called: Pheislant | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.42E
> See also: Ailendamus, Order of Seasons, House Kallinad
## Overview
- A pass leads out of the Order of Seasons' territory and down into the Kingdom of Pheislant. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L613)
- House Kallinad is under the domain of the Kingdom of Pheislant and owes it loyalty. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L251-254)
- Wil Kallinad says Pheislant is a coastal nation, known for its lighthouses, which is not as big as some of Terandria's larger kingdoms and has a weaker land army. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L471,L474)
- Wil adds that Pheislant would probably act if Ailendamus pushed at Calanfer and the other nations, because they cannot be allowed to keep expanding. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L471)
## Ports and politics
- Pheislant's largest port-city is Phel's Light, with harbors on the southwestern edge of Terandria that give easier access to Baleros, Chandrar and Izril than Lord Bel's harbors further east. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L497-502)
- The Order of Seasons is headquartered in Pheislant on its own lands, allied with the kingdom, and is one of the largest orders in Terandria and part of Pheislant's might. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L504,L507)
- Relations between Pheislant and the vast kingdom of Ailendamus, which threatens war, are strained. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L508)
- The 3rd Prince of Pheislant, Tellsiv, is a devoted fan of a travelling [Singer] who performs her way south to Pheislant. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L544,L550,L558)
- A knight says Pheislant has no reason to support Ailendamus and many to oppose it, and that it will decide where it stands if Ailendamus pushes. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L591,L615)
