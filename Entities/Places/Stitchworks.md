# Stitchworks
> Type: place | Also called: Octavia's shop | First appears: Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1
> See also: Octavia, Celum, Door, Alchemist Quelm
## Overview
- Stitchworks is Octavia's boarded-up store down a side street in Celum, and a sign on it says potions are half-off and that matches are no longer sold there, with inquiries to Alchemist Quelm. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L75-79)
- The access point of the magic door is inside Stitchworks, though the real door is still in Liscor. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L86-87)
- A new sign on Stitchworks says it is under the protection of the Redfang Goblins, a Bronze-rank team. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L681-683)
- The sign's note says that if you attack the Goblin, he may hit back, and that he can talk, listen and see. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L683)
