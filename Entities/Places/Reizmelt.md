# Reizmelt
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Invrisil
## Overview
- Reizmelt is a small city that lies between many cities, so Runners passing through often find deliveries there. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L752)
- Reizmelt is further north than Invrisil. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L108)
- It has paved streets of smooth brickwork and a Runner's Guild, where Ryoka is a regular. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L77,L86)
- A speaker tells Magnolia that no Couriers are based in Reizmelt, though some pass through. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L433,L448)
## Residents
- Mad Madain runs the inn the Huntress' Haven in Reizmelt, and the narration calls him its most infamous [Innkeeper]. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L25,L47)
- Madain is a retired adventurer from a Gold-rank team who wakes his guests at his own hours, so his inn is very empty of regular overnight guests. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L48-50)
- Alber the [Fist Fighter] is one of the inn's three regulars and earns his coin challenging visitors and citizens of Reizmelt to fights in the square. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L51-52)
- Later the inn is empty as usual, and Alber is eating there. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L477,L572)
- Guards at the gates and children call Ryoka the Wind Runner and ask for breezes, because her runs bring cooling winds. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L467-476)
