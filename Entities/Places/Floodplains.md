# Floodplains
> Type: place | Also called: Liscor Floodplains; the Floodplains of Liscor | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.23
> See also: Liscor, Bloodfields, High Passes, The Wandering Inn
## Overview
- The Floodplains of Liscor are a valley formed between the High Passes, one of the two routes between northern and southern Izril. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L2)
- For two months of every twelve, the spring rains fill the floodplains with water that engulfs all but the highest hills until it drains, an annual and well-known process. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L2)
## Seasons and wildlife
- Klbkch says that at the time of Erin's first weeks the Floodplains hold few creatures besides Goblins, aside from grazing herds. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L119)
- Relc says giant spiders hide in tunnels there soon, that the quietest time of year gives way to a ton of weird creatures once the rains start, that travelling animals come to graze when the rains stop, and that winter gets really dangerous. (Book 1, Ch1.23 L129-131)
- Quillfish, octopus-fish with teeth and Rock Crabs creeping underwater are among the dangers in the floodwaters. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L24)
## Floods
- {event} In the spring rains, barely a week after they begin, the waters engulf the Floodplains and only a few of the tallest hills and the city stand above the waterline. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L23)
- {event} From her hill, Erin sees the inn's hilltop surrounded by water a few feet deep, with Liscor in the distance also surrounded by water, and the water still rising. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L201-202)
- {state} Later, the eternal rain around Liscor has ended, but the Floodplains are still mostly brown mud, while the hill the inn stands on is lush. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L94,L100)
