# High Passes
> Type: place | Also called: the Passes | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.01R
> See also: Liscor, Floodplains, Wistram
## Overview
- Runners call the High Passes a very nasty place, where more Runners have died than in most places and where tons of monsters live. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L43)
- Fals says that surviving the High Passes would take fighting skills or insanely high levels, probably above Level 30, to get out unscathed. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L27)
- The High Passes are the tallest mountains in the world, estimated by Archmage Chaiemegia at over seventy thousand feet, perhaps higher. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L42)
- Two known routes lead through the mountains: one by a basin, the Floodplains and Liscor, which is mostly safe, and one straight through the High Passes past a Dragon's cave, known as the route of death. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L45-47)
## Terrain
- Ryoka sees rust-colored rock mountains looming, which together form an impenetrable barricade except for a small gap winding through them. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L32-33)
- The gap is a road as wide as two football fields, strewn with boulders taller than fully grown oak trees. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L34)
## Dangers
- Ryoka, wearing a foul-smelling potion by Octavia, is shunned by a Goblin tribe, Carn Wolves, evil toothed goats and gargoyles, all of which flee her smell. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L200-222)
- A naked, human-shaped creature with twisted eyes and a stretching clawed arm stalks Ryoka in the pass. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L101-105)
- Teriarch scares the creature away with fire and tells Ryoka that darker things lurk in the High Passes that either do not smell or have different opinions about smells. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L106-110)
- Halrac warns other adventurers that the High Passes hold Eater Goats, Gargoyles and worse monsters, and that Eater Goats can sneak up on even Named Rank adventurers at night. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L579)
- Halrac says a Weyr lies in the High Passes, where one wrong move means facing a Greater Wyvern and a few hundred Wyverns. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L814)
