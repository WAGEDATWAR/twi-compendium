# Antinium Hive
> Type: place | Also called: the Hive; the Liscor Hive | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.44
> See also: Liscor, Antinium, Klbkch, Pawn
## Overview
- The Antinium Hive beneath Liscor has three entrances, only two known to Liscor's Council; the third lies buried outside the city under several tons of dirt. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L150)
- The Antinium plan to build a tunnel with multiple branching sections to the main dungeon's entrance and unearth it in three days. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L30)
- Lady Magnolia estimates that the smallest Antinium Hive, the one in Liscor, holds at least three thousand Antinium, with two thousand Workers and a thousand Soldiers ready for war at any moment. (Book 2, Ch2.31 L471)
- The Hive holds thousands, even tens of thousands, of gold coins from over ten years of hard work and little spending, in one of several treasury rooms. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L164)
## Inhabitants and politics
- The Queen of the Free Antinium calls Klbkch her "Slayer" and Revalantor, and fears that the Grand Queen will take him from her. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L289-296)
- Xrn is present at the Hive. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L289)
- A speaker tells Klbkch that the Caverns of Consumption parasites infected over forty percent of the Silent Antinium's Hive before being eradicated, and that Antinium aided the Armored Antinium against Giant Moles. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L59,L64)
- Klbkch says the monsters' attacks on the Hive have doubled or tripled as adventurers entered the dungeon, so that for the first time the Hive is losing more Workers and Soldiers than it produces. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L133)
## Inside the Hive
- Sixteen regular Soldiers step out of hidden alcoves to guard the tunnel when Erin enters, then stand down when one of them nods at her. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L210-217)
- Hundreds of Antinium move through the main corridors without lanes or pause, and when Erin sneezes they all stop and look at her; asked for Pawn, Anand, Belgrade or Klbkch, they point her down a tunnel. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L220-229)
- The Hive has no torches or fire, only a glowing mold cultivated along the walls. (Book 8, Ch5.26L L294)
- A barracks hollowed out in the Hive carries painted symbols on its wall, which Lyonette understands to be a memorial to fallen Painted Soldiers. (Book 8, Ch5.26L L295-300)
