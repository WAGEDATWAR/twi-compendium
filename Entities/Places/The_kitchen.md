# The kitchen
> Type: place | Also called: the inn's kitchen | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.04
> See also: The Wandering Inn
## Overview
- The kitchen of The Wandering Inn holds several knives, from a cleaver down to one the size of a finger, and a frying pan. (Book 1, Ch1.04 L159,L168)
- The kitchen has many cupboards and drawers, which hold basic utensils such as spatulas, tarnished silverware and plates. (Book 1, Ch1.05 L25)
## Preserving runes
- One cupboard still holds a bag of flour and a golden block of butter that are fresh despite the thick dust in the room. (Book 1, Ch1.05 L29-43)
- That cupboard is edged with faintly glowing silver runes, and Erin concludes they are magic. (Book 1, Ch1.05 L46-49)
- The other cupboards carry the same small runes around their edges, but these are not glowing. (Book 1, Ch1.05 L51-52)
- Erin scrapes the runes in the good cupboard and splinters the wood so that the line of runes is cut, and the glow at once fades. (Book 1, Ch1.05 L54-57)
## Cooking equipment
- The kitchen has an old oven, which Erin's instincts tell her she can use for baking bread. (Book 1, Ch1.06 L58)
- Among the kitchen's odds and ends are a hammer, a rock and a fire striker, which Erin identifies as flint and steel. (Book 1, Ch1.06 L66-72)
