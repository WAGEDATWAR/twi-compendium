# Celum
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.04R
> See also: Liscor, The Wandering Inn, Octavia, Esthelm
## Overview
- Celum is a Human city with many inns, one of which Ryoka sits in. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L29)
- Celum's walls are low, only about twenty feet tall, and a steady flow of traffic passes its gates, with everyone Erin sees being Human and fair-skinned. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L89,L172)
- In Human settlements, high-level people become mercenaries or adventurers rather than [Guardsmen], so the gate guards lack the competence and pay of Drake guards. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L83)
## Distance from Liscor
- A guard says Liscor is over forty miles south of Celum. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L101)
- A friend tells Erin soon after that it is over a hundred miles from Celum to Liscor, which Toren covered in one day. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L138-140)
- Later narration places Celum a hundred miles north of Liscor, with the High Passes and darker skies visible from the city but not Liscor's weather. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L355)
## Events
- Guards at the gate are sleep-deprived, one muttering Hamlet, and no one pays notice to the Horns of Hammerad when they arrive. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L107-113)
- Erin's reunion with the Horns in an inn full of guests draws attention, and people recognise them as the Conquerors of Albez. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L174-176)
- {event} When aid is sent to Esthelm, wagons stream into Octavia's shop, someone knocks the wall through, [Actors] rush to the shop, and Miss Agnes offers free drinks at her inn. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L362-366)
- Celum hears about Erin's Hobgoblins and boycotts her inn. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L81)
- Quelm's Bazaar of Alchemies sells new matches, striking on a box, at six silvers for twenty; Quelm is a moderately successful [Alchemist] of Level 20 to 25 who backs a new street gang of about forty. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L93,L123,L129,L192)
- A new Watch Captain does not want to risk blood and is being bribed with potions, since the last one left to become an [Actor]. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L101)
- {state} By order of the [Mayor], the Watch confiscates the door to Liscor and announces that Celum will no longer do business with the inn; the Watch Captain only seizes the portal stone. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L833-837)
- {event} The Watch surrounds Octavia's shop, then learns that the confiscated door is only a receptacle for the real magic. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L606,L759)
- The door at Octavia's shop has been gone for weeks, with a sign saying it is closed permanently and moved to Liscor, and a [Mayor] says Celum got rid of The Wandering Inn. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L217-221)
- {event} Bloodfeast Raiders sack Celum: the northern gates are torn open, the walls collapse in seven places, [Fireballs] wipe out a third of the city and flames almost half. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L263-264)
- The raiders destroy the city for nearly an hour and then leave, and reinforcements from Remendia, Wales and Ocre arrive after being convinced they have gone. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L282)
- Mayor Cetris Duiland, wounded and sooty, blames Erin for taking her door, shouting that the city had bets and was supposed to be safe once she was gone. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L1086-1089)
