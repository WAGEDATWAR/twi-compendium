# Talenqual
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.04D
> See also: Baleros, Featherfolk Brigade, United Nations Company
## Overview
- Talenqual is a port city on Baleros, big enough to employ all the Humans looking for a job and to appear on a continental map though not a capital. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L20)
- It is claimed by the Featherfolk Brigade, which is not engaged in major conflicts, and has no vastly rich resources that would tempt others to fight over it. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L20)
- The streets are dominated by Lizardfolk, with Dullahans and Centaurs seen daily, and buildings rarely over three stories, made of wood or Lizardfolk mud brick. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L132-138)
- Centaurs tend to move in the center lanes of the traffic in Talenqual. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L137)
## The company's home
- A member of the company says they must stop and earn money or it will never get to Talenqual. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L286)
- The company's house is two apartments bought and converted into one, on the outer edges of the city near the rice fields. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L704)
- Talenqual is a port city, small to medium-sized, ruled by the Featherfolk Brigade, a mercenary company that seized the city and area and keeps control to this day. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L137)
- The United Nations Company now has four large, adjoining houses leased for the next four months. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L243-244)
- The landlady, Miss Hastel, allowed the company to install connecting doors between the houses. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L279)
