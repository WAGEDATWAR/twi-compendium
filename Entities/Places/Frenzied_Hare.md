# Frenzied Hare
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.40
> See also: Erin Solstice, Ryoka Griffin
## Overview
- The Frenzied Hare is an inn run by Agnes, who tells Ryoka she stayed there when she began as a City Runner. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L403)
- Agnes was a [Tailor] by trade before she became an [Innkeeper] with her husband, who has been ill in bed with a fever for two weeks. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L426)
## Staff
- The barmaids at the Frenzied Hare are Maran and Safry. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L430)
- A patron says Agnes' husband Jerom is a good cook, though Agnes, who runs the inn in his absence, is not. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L497)
## Role in Book 3
- {event} While Erin stays there, the inn is packed each day with customers drawn by her cooking, and Agnes acts as barmaid while Erin flips crepes in the kitchen. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L133-134,L148)
- Agnes is the actual owner of the Frenzied Hare, and she and Erin split the profits. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L148,L154)
- Agnes hopes Erin will become the real manager of the inn even once Jerom recovers from his sickness. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L153)
- The inn serves a strong local drink called a Firebird. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L174)
- A speaker says Erin usually eats at the Frenzied Hare, more often now that the inn has a new cook. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L212)
- Jerom is still feverish and sleeps too much, but Agnes hopes a new alchemist's potion bought with Erin's help will cure him. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L208)
- Safry serves tables and cooks whatever is needed in the kitchen. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L217)
