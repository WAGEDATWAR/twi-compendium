# Izril
> Type: place | Also called: Issrysil (Drake name); Izrilian (adj.) | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.08
> See also: Liscor, High Passes, Blighted Kingdom, Wistram
## Overview
- Izril is the continent of Drakes and Gnolls and a few humans, which the Drakes call Issrysil; Ryoka will only pronounce Izril. (Book 2, Ch2.08 L19)
- Izril is vast, and Humans have lived on it for millennia without settling or intruding into some parts, where monsters, disease or bad luck erased earlier structures. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L235)
- {belief} A Gnoll says it was said long ago that Izril was known not as the home of Drakes and Gnolls but as the home of Dragons. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L105)
## North and south
- The High Passes mountains separate northern and southern Izril, with one route through the Floodplains of Liscor. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L2)
- Zel Shivertail grew up in the south of Izril, where even the smallest Drake city-states have a clear chain of command, open lines of communication and order. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L30)
- The Antinium have six Hives and a foothold in southern Izril, and the Hives and nearby foothills are forbidden zones that Drake cities patrol. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L11,L16)
- The Assassin's Guild in southern Izril never recovered from its war with the Silent Antinium, unlike the flourishing Human guilds in the north. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L152-155)
- Politics in Izril is a game of connections and intrigue, of strategy and alliances. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L254)
## History
- {event} In the Antinium Wars the Antinium barely slowed as they rampaged across Izril, and later the Humans, Drakes and others of the continent united against the Goblin King's armies. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L75; Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L111)
- {event} News of Zel Shivertail's death spreads across Izril and then to every continent at the speed of magic, with riots in the first day in southern Izril followed by silence and tears. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L3,L6,L13)
