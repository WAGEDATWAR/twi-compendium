# Sadomere
> Type: place | Also called: Drevish's Madness | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.21KQ
> See also: Reim, Flos, Drevish, Raelt, Jecrass
## Overview
- Sadomere is a city of Reim's border war that Flos' army took three days before, with simple short walls and standard battlements. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L89)
- Teres sees that Sadomere is not Reim, is not built as strongly, has no secret weapons and has exposed walls the [Builders] made without an eye for sieges. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L400-402)
- Only one of the King's Seven, Mars, is there to defend it. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L401)
## Drevish's Madness
- {state} Sadomere is rechristened Drevish's Madness and becomes a linchpin of the King of Destruction's defensive line as Raelt's armies fall back. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L495)
- Newly reinforced, the city withstands a full-scale assault by General Lael's forces, and Lael refuses to retreat despite the Skill-enhanced fortifications. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L7)
- Magical statues of Orthenon attract arrow fire, and the inner city is designed to stymie assaults and favor defenders. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L43)
- Later the city is no longer under siege. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L30)
