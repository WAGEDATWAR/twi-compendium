# Blighted Kingdom
> Type: place | Also called: the Blighted Kingdoms; Rhir | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.08
> See also: Antinium Hive, Wistram
## Overview
- Rhir is home to the Blighted Kingdoms, described as dying lands locked in eternal conflict. (Book 2, Ch2.08 L16)
- Ryoka learns that the Demons and monsters from the corrupted part of the continent are so dangerous that four walls were built to hold them back, and that the Blighted Kingdom receives aid from the rest of the world, including soldiers. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L177)
- The Blighted Kingdom is the first and only defense against the Demons of Rhir. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L70)
- Ships carrying supplies, food, magical weapons and soldiers come from around the world to Rhir to help hold back the Demons. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L188)
## History
- The Antinium's first attacks on Drake lands matched those the Blighted Kingdom suffered in Rhir, and the Antinium bypassed four of the Walls built by the Blighted Kingdom before they met the Walled Cities. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L54,L78)
- Unlike the Blighted Kingdom, Drakes and Gnolls had no great enemy such as the Demon King to fight against when the Black Tide arrived. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L3)
- {belief} A speaker claims the Antinium overpowered the Blighted Kingdom, which survived only because the Antinium wanted to leave Rhir. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L370)
## The walls and the Demons
- Another kingdom filled with people twisted by corruption, the Demons, fought the Blighted Kingdom and proved too powerful to destroy, which led the two nations into a deadlock. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L186)
- The Blighted Kingdom has built four massive walls across Rhir to make safe zones and is working on a fifth, hoping one day to wipe out the Demons. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L187)
- The capital of Rhir and the first wall date to the founding of the Blighted Kingdom, and each later wall is larger than the last but not necessarily more powerful. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L766)
- Lord Hayvon says that four walls shelter the Blighted Kingdom from invasion and a fifth is being built, and that few enemies have ever crossed the second wall. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L17)
- Cirille hears that the Blighted Kingdom put up a stretch of wall in a month, and Commander Uxel says it shows the Kingdom has power but is not the real wall. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1307-1311)
## Dealings with other lands
- Rhir pays a bounty on all Crelers the world over, on top of local bounties, and the Blighted Kingdom sends a commendation and reward to the slayer of an Adult Creler. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L325,L392)
- The Blighted King has allowed some of Wistram's [Mages] to tour the walls and cities of the Blighted Kingdom. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L80)
- A grand ritual unearthed from an ancient hiding place is found by the Blighted Kingdom to summon heroes in its darkest hour. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L70)
