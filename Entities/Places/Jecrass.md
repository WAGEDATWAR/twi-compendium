# Jecrass
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.15K
> See also: Raelt, Chandrar, Belchan, Reim, Medain, Flos
## Overview
- Jecrass is a kingdom whose nobility are called River Wardens because they control the rivers that make Jecrass rich in horses. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L117)
- Jecrass is known across the continent, and perhaps the world, as a place to buy good horses. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L117)
- Raelt privately calls the River Wardens River Robbers, because anyone who owns even a stream can build farmlands, raise animals and dole out the precious water. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L50)
- Jecrass is famed for the speed of its riders. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L53)
- Jecrass borders the Republic of Belchan, a democracy known for its mage-schools and gold mines, whose ruler is Prime Minister Lyfelt. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L147,L211)
- Jecrass has more water than the southern nations such as Hellios or Reim thanks to its rivers, and sells some water each month to Khelt. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L210)
## Court
- At Raelt's court, the River Wardens Warden Winta, Dulfe, Cerani and the twenty-seven-year-old Emile discuss a paper read out by Geril. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L31-33)
- Warden Dulfe is frog-like, and Raelt notes that he put a copper tax on every bucket of water drawn from his wells. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L39,L53)
- Raelt orders Dulfe to announce and count the citizens claiming the King of Destruction's protection or intending to leave. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L67-71)
- Dulfe suggests in reply that the Wardens contribute less gold to the royal coffers if Raelt fails to protect the borders against Reim, perhaps through a vote. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L72)
## War with Reim
- {event} Jecrass' fast raiding parties attack held settlements, overrun garrisons and threaten Reim's supply lines, so Flos' armies cannot advance too far. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L53)
- A speaker notes that Silver-rank adventurers, a Gold-rank team Orthenon hacked up and Stitch-Soldiers from Nerrhavia appear on Jecrass' side. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L56)
- Other nations back Jecrass unofficially, sending gold, soldiers and even artifacts to Raelt to push back Reim. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L57)
- {state} A speaker, consulting a [Strategist], says Jecrass just lost twenty percent of the entire nation by retreating to fight closer to its main cities. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L622)
- {event} Jecrass' army falls back, General Lael abandons her front, and Reim's armies advance unimpeded, stopping only for the King of Destruction's wounds and to fortify. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L619-620)
- {event} Raelt writes a two-line letter to every nation that has contacted him, saying he intends to sue for peace at any cost by nightfall. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L699-703)
- {event} A breakaway wing of about four hundred Jecrass heavy riders with long-handled axes and shields on bicorns crashes into an equal number of Medain [Lancers] and downs them. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L6,L8,L16-17)
