# Liscor dungeon
> Type: place | Also called: Dungeon beneath Liscor; Liscor's dungeon | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.36
> See also: Liscor, Griffon Hunt, Antinium
## Overview
- Klbkch confirms that a dungeon lies under Liscor and says his Hive has fought monsters emerging from it for many years. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L174)
- Klbkch says that some monsters are drawn to the location, many seem to have nests inside, and the dungeon has traps and many magical defenses. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L174)
- Klbkch says the Antinium Queen once wanted to secure the dungeon herself but found that unfeasible, so she chose to open an entrance in the hope adventurers would eliminate some traps. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L174)
## Entrances
- {event} A speaker says none of the adventurers want to go in, that Griffon Hunt has not yet gone through the entrance, and that the dungeon is another threat for the city besides the Goblins. (Book 3, Ch3.05L L198)
- The official entrance is guarded by sixteen city guardsmen with bows, spears and one apparent [Mage], watching from behind a small wooden rampart. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L132)
- Adventurers also go toward a second entrance, and Halrac and another speaker discuss a set of ropes stretching over a hundred feet down. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L102,L113)
- Later the second entrance is the rift in the ground, a chasm leading to the surface, and Halrac prefers it though the escape route is a long perilous climb. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L9,L174)
- The official entrance leads through a maze of trapped rooms that has not been cleared. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L174)
## Description
- Reports of the dungeon's monsters include Shield Spiders, Crypt Worms, various undead, enchanted suits of armor, Bog Wraiths, a trap full of Face-Eater Moths, giant infested slugs and the Children. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L72)
- Teams in the dungeon have also met groups of Goblins and strange Gnoll-like monsters who use weapons and fight efficiently. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L74)
- The first group to meet them and survive, Vuliel Drae, brought back several heads that resembled a Gnoll's but were far larger with vicious canines. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L75)
- The narration says a nation long forgotten by the living once built a city far underground, and the dungeon was built out of its ruins. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L3)
- Toren notices that trap-filled corridors are not populated by most monsters, while others lead into nests holding tens of thousands, such as the massive Shield Spider infestation. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L60)
- Toren concludes the monsters are deliberately organized so they do not crowd each other. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L60)
- Rainwater pouring down the chasm does not enter the dungeon but pools a few meters overhead as a wall of water suspended in midair. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L9)
- Halrac notes that the layer of water between sky and dungeon has grown. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L174)
