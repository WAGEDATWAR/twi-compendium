# Esthelm
> Type: place | Also called: none | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.10R
> See also: Liscor, Celum, Goblin Lord, Ylawes Byres
## Overview
- Garia places Esthelm down south, about sixty miles away, near the road that goes to Liscor. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L259)
- Esthelm is the furthest Human city south and supplies itself mainly by mining, with few visitors except for trade. (Book 15, Ch7.00 L89)
## The Goblin attack and its aftermath
- {event} A Goblin army attacks Esthelm in the evening, overruns the gates and slaughters people; another Goblin army arrives and fights the first, and in the confusion many citizens escape, while the city burns. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L45,L109)
- The Goblins loot the city's valuables and stolen food, and over half the remaining survivors begin to tear at each other over scraps. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L189)
- Hundreds of the dead rise, including ghouls, and the refugees cannot defend themselves; a Goblin army camped outside shoots at anyone who tries to leave, and a score of people die that way. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L5-7)
- Survivors form camps and gangs in the ruins and fight and kill over blankets, food and weapons, with the strongest leading the others. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L16-17,L21)
- Goblins watching the ruined city feel uneasy at how much the Humans' warring over territory resembles Goblin tribes. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L49-52)
- Umbral recounts that Ylawes Byres rallied the people after the chaos, and with a timely rescue by the other two members of his Gold-rank team, the Silver Swords, drove off the second Goblin army and began rebuilding the city. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L295,L300-301)
- A plaque in the city honours Ylawes and his party, and a statue is planned. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L303)
- Ylawes left to look for his sister Yvlon after hearing she was wounded. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L318)
- A grave in the city center, covered in flowers and unmarked, honours a young woman who fought the Goblins and is buried with the Goblins who fought for her; Umbral credits those Goblins with killing the skeleton with purple eyes. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L307-314)
## Recovery
- Erin and the Horns find Esthelm's wall patched up and manned by archers, and the city vigilant after two Goblin attacks. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L216-218)
- Umbral, a former [Soldier], says the city is surviving but needs supplies, because Celum, Wales and Remendia claim the roads are unsafe for aid. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L257,L261)
- {event} Erin arrives with a relief convoy of supplies, a relief force and Antinium from Celum and Liscor, and the people of Esthelm cheer that aid has come. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L515-527)
- {event} After a day, most wagons, the Gold-rank adventurers and many Antinium return south, while hired adventurers, extra [Guardsmen], [Healers] and a group of Antinium under Anand stay to finish the walls. (Book 4, Ch3.38 L18-20)
## Later events
- {event} Esthelm repels an attack by part of the Goblin Lord's army as it marches by, with a quarter of its people dead and over half wounded; its citizens now nearly all have combat classes and the Antinium repaired its walls. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L57-63)
- A Redfang tribe approaching Esthelm sees repaired walls with many Human [Archers], who fire arrows that fall short. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L260-262)
- {event} Esthelm goes on high alert after seeing the Redfang tribe, and alarm spreads when the fleeing Flooded Waters tribe, led by Redscar, Poisonbite and Noears, races south past the city in front of the Goblin Lord's army. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L786-789)
- Esthelm was sacked once, and survivors rebuilt it stronger and higher after fending off Goblins and undead; its guards keep longbows at hand. (Book 15, Ch7.00 L88,L105)
