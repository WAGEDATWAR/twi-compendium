# Zeikhal
> Type: place | Also called: the Great Desert; Zeikhal desert | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.00K
> See also: Merreid, Chandrar
## Overview
- Zeikhal is a massive desert on Chandrar, which Teres says is probably twice the size of the Sahara. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L235)
- A speaker in Flos's court says that marching an army across Zeikhal is dangerous and is the one thing that has stopped the Emperor of Sands's expansion. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L234)
- Zeikhal is called the Great Desert of Chandrar, larger than entire continents of Trey's world, and it has apparently engulfed countless kingdoms and empires that turned to dust. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L246-247)
- It is said that rain falls seldom so far inland, and in Zeikhal not at all. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L295)
- Zeikhal has not always been there. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L9)
## Places in Zeikhal
- The city of Merreid lies in the desert of Zeikhal. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L471)
