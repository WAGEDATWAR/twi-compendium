# Daquin
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.21D
> See also: Baleros, Elvallian, Forgotten Wing Company, Niers Astoragon
## Overview
- Daquin is a Balerosian port city, large though not quite a metropolis, whose harbor can hold as many as eight warships and whose buildings are mostly stone from a nearby quarry. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L2)
- Daquin lies over a hundred and fourteen miles from Elvallian, the capital of the Forgotten Wing company and training school. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L3)
- The harbor has a bowl-shaped opening with external watch towers and a sea gate that can be closed. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L22)
## The students' exercise
- Niers tells his students that they will gather in Daquin for a competition with the rule of no deaths, no blades and no dangerous magic. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L571)
- Niers describes Daquin as having sea and surf and some very fine prawns. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L600)
- A student notes from supply reports that provisions at Daquin have greatly increased, to at least enough for a thousand soldiers and maybe two thousand. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L829)
- Ships in Daquin's harbor bear the Forgotten Wing company colors, as if Niers has taken over the harbor for his games. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L22)
