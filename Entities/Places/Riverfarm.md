# Riverfarm
> Type: place | Also called: Demesne of Riverfarm; the Emperor of Riverfarm's demesne | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.00E
> See also: Laken Godart, Durene, Prost, Windrest, Invrisil, Unseen Empire
## Overview
- Riverfarm is a farming village on farming land fed by a river, which Durene says gives the village its name. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L172)
- Durene describes Riverfarm as a village of around sixty souls with a blacksmith and a dedicated trader who goes to a town to buy and sell for the village. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L172)
- Later, the narration says the villagers of Riverfarm numbered little over a hundred. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L268)
- Riverfarm lies south of Walta and Bethal's lands and southwest of Invrisil, in the shadow of a small mountain, on land no noble house claims. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L186)
- The Valerund family, whose [Lady] is apparently in Riverfarm, has a small area of influence but is a day's journey from Riverfarm. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L186)
## Early life
- In Durene's account, children often go to learn jobs in other towns as they grow, and new faces are rare except for the occasional adventurers or Runners. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L173)
- Durene says the nearest major city is Invrisil, the city of adventurers, and that Riverfarm is not far north but decently far from the High Passes. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L55-56)
- Laken's narration says all the villagers, Durene included, are poor, earning a few gold coins a year when weather and crops are good and subsisting at the worst of times. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L58)
- Durene says Goblins are the only real nuisance, that the village sends for adventurers if any are spotted, that everyone pays into a bounty, and that adventurers take weeks to arrive. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L50,L53)
- Durene says every family might have one gold coin in savings, and Gamel says the [Merchant] who visited had three gold coins, the most he had seen at once. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L285,L313)
## The avalanche
- {event} Durene realizes the village lay directly in the path of the avalanche, and Laken fears the buildings could not withstand thousands of tons of snow. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L486-489)
- {event} Within an hour of Laken's guidance, all the villagers are found, and the living outnumber the dead. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L313)
- {state} Laken, taking charge, styles himself Emperor of the Unseen, Protector of Durene's Cottage and Ruler of Riverfarm Village. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L419)
- Laken notes that since he took charge he has heard no bad word about Durene from the villagers. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L405)
- {state} The avalanche killed all the village's animals, so the villagers are eating a meaty broth. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L403)
- The dead chickens, pigs and cows were what Riverfarm depended on for food and coin, but their frozen bodies let the villagers eat them one by one to stretch their food. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L358-359)
- Foxes or similar animals gnaw at a buried cow, and Laken suggests storing the dead animals in the barn to keep animals away. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L367-377,L384)
- Laken says the avalanche hit the village a few days earlier, killing many and wiping out a lot of their provisions. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L127)
- {event} On the night before Christmas, Runner Ryoka reaches Riverfarm and tells sentries and Prost she has a delivery from [Emperor] Laken. (Book 4, Ch3.41 L549-555)
- The villagers shelter in the renovated barn, and Prost finds food in the bag of holding Ryoka delivers. (Book 4, Ch3.41 L558-563)
## Rebuilding and threats
- Laken senses that eight buildings in Riverfarm have been dug out of the snow, three more than when he left, and nearly thirty are still buried. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L118)
- The barn is the main hub of the new village, with beds, recovered goods and cooking fires outside. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L184)
- Odveig tells Laken of a Goblin Lord to the south and a Great Chieftain nearby, and Laken and Prost note that Riverfarm never had walls of any sort. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L44-47,L62)
- Laken proposes a palisade using the back of the barn as a wall, and Prost says it will be slow without a sawmill or more hands. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L60-64)
- {state} The population of Riverfarm doubles in a moment when the people from Windrest arrive. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L82-84)
- After the Windrest arrival, Prost calculates that supplies will last for a month but no longer through to spring. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L203)
- Laken senses at least sixty-four Goblins and two Hobs on his land, while the defenders include twelve Celestial Trackers and seven of Beniar's riders. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L145-146,L152,L158)
- Prost says over a hundred and fifty people are at Riverfarm but many are children, old or injured and most have never fought. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L167-168)
- {event} Odveig flees after Laken's confrontation with Wiskeria, and Laken says he is left with one less warrior to defend the village. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L382-385)
## Role in Book 7-9
- {event} A speaker tallies twenty-three acceptances from nobles, almost all those attending. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L66)
- {event} Wiskeria tells Durene that the army from Riverfarm was routed by the Goblins and that Lord Pellmia's cavalry then broke the Goblins in turn. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L172-175,L178)
- {event} Lord Tyrion Veltras himself rode to rescue Riverfarm. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L175,L178)
## Growth in Book 12-15
- When Durene went to war, Riverfarm was a collection of nearly six hundred people, the remnants of a few villages banding together, and it later holds over two thousand. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L525-526)
- {state} Row after row of identical stone and wood houses without glass go up on the new streets, each able to fit a large family. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L8)
- Prost says Riverfarm's [Builders] can put up a house in less than a day, or two or three houses in a day, by dividing the work among teams. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L10-12)
- Beycalt Newman is [Forewoman] of one of the building crews. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L57)
- Chimmy says Riverfarm has a pillory for anyone who argues with Lady Rie, that Mister Helm makes lots of metal but wants their own mines, and that buying ore is costly. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L352)
- Chimmy says some of the village's [Farmers] have [Fast Growth] and one has [One Day Bloom], so Riverfarm does not need to buy food. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L360)
- Chimmy says her father Prost has Mister Tepple sow the seeds and the other [Farmers] till and water, so those with growth Skills make the seeds come up. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L363)
- Lady Rie says the fields have been harvested once and she wants to double them to feed Riverfarm, sell and stockpile. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L716-719)
- The residential district is already nearly four hundred houses, and massive warehouses hold food in another district. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L265)
- Of the over two thousand people in Riverfarm, six hundred are original people loyal to their [Emperor], and the group from Lancrel outnumbers the villagers two to one. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L52,L205)
- Riverfarm's outhouses are small and open, and its toilet paper is a corn cob, so Ryoka uses paper she bought in Walta. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L110-111,L122-123)
- {event} Ryoka reports to Gralton that hundreds and possibly thousands of refugees are coming to Riverfarm, that Yitton Byres will leave within the week, and that the Goblins are kept separate in their own area. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L411-415)
- As summer begins, Riverfarm is the first settlement to claim allegiance to the Unseen Empire, and it begins to export its goods. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L4-6)
- Riverfarm produces far more crops than ever because [Farmers] pool their Skills over vast fields. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L7)
- Riverfarm has a large waterwheel for making flour, and its [Engineers] offer to build windmills, which outrages [Millers]. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L8-10)
- {belief} Rumors say Riverfarm's lands are protected, that [Bandits] avoid it, that [Witches] are treated with respect there, and that its [Emperor] is a blind ancient-blooded man called the Unseen Emperor. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L11-14)
- The narration says Riverfarm is laid out perfectly, no longer a village but a small town, with an assembly-line trial and [Engineers] designing wells and cranes. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L15,L18)
- {event} Humans of Riverfarm build a wall of up to ten feet around the Goblin territory in their free time, since their [Emperor] forbids killing the Goblins. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1074,L1079-1082)
- {event} Laken has a letter from Ryoka carried by Dustrider Charlay, takes thirty minutes to get through it, and begins to incorporate her plans. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L1089-1097)
## Later changes
- {event} Laken has the first bumpy stone roads torn up and replaced with flat brick roads after about four weeks of work. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L44-46)
