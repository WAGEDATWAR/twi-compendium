# Windrest
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.20E
> See also: Riverfarm, Halrac, Goblin Lord
## Overview
- Windrest is a village that suffered a Goblin raid in which about sixty Goblins killed a dozen people and torched a few buildings. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L380)
- The Goblins took the village's animals and winter stores, making off with two thirds of its goods and two young women. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L380)
## Later
- {state} The narration says Halrac returns to find Windrest ash, having been too late, and that sickness, war and monster attacks had long hurt his home. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L9-12)
- Briganda tells Halrac that Windrest is gone, destroyed by raids from the mountain during the Goblin Lord's attacks, and that fires were part of it. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1283,L1287)
- {belief} Briganda says the rumor is that Drakes started the fires. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1287)
- Briganda says Windrest was Halrac's childhood home, where a small village could not hire enough adventurers to fight raiders off. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1284-1286)
- {rel} Briganda says most of Windrest's survivors are at Riverfarm, having joined the Unseen Empire. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1291-1294,L1297)
- A speaker says most of Windrest had gone to Riverfarm, which is still standing. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L22)
