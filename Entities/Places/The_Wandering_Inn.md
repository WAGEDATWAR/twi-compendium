# The Wandering Inn
> Type: place | Also called: Erin's inn; the inn; the new inn | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.00
> See also: Liscor, Celum, Esthelm, Antinium Hive, Stream
## Overview
- The Wandering Inn is an inn on a long slope of grassland near Liscor, an old wooden building that Erin Solstice finds abandoned and reopens as her own. (Book 1, Ch1.00 L5-6; Book 1, Ch1.12 L44-48; Book 1, Ch1.20 L207-217)
## Building and layout
- The inn is a wooden building on a stone foundation, made of oak boards that have weathered the years well, and it is four times the size of a normal house, built to hold crowds. (Book 1, Ch1.00 L5-6)
- When Erin first arrives its signboard is faded and the paint long worn away. (Book 1, Ch1.00 L10)
- The common room is dusty and long deserted, and water drips down through cracks from somewhere upstairs when it rains. (Book 1, Ch1.00 L12,L19)
- The inn is built almost entirely of wood, with wooden walls, ceiling beams and floor. (Book 1, Ch1.01 L12)
- A long counter stands at the end of the common room, made of high-quality wood and long enough for at least twenty people, or fifteen if they want elbow room. (Book 1, Ch1.01 L14,L73)
- A door behind the bar opens into the kitchen, which holds old rusty pans and pots and a bucket. (Book 1, Ch1.01 L27)
- The second floor is reached by a staircase of which nearly every second stair creaks loudly. (Book 1, Ch1.01 L30-31)
- The first room Erin enters upstairs has a curtain hanging at its window. (Book 1, Ch1.01 L52-59)
- The downstairs windows have no glass or curtains; they are square holes in the wall with shutters. (Book 1, Ch1.02 L31)
- The door has a metal bar that can be slid across to keep it shut, and the common room has many windows with wooden shutters and cheap metal latches. (Book 1, Ch1.03 L19-21)
- Upstairs, some window mantles have rotted but the shutters are mostly intact. (Book 1, Ch1.03 L23)
- A skeleton lies in the last bed, in the room at the end of the upstairs corridor. (Book 1, Ch1.03 L24)
- Goblins break shutters on the way in during their first attack, but the door holds. (Book 1, Ch1.03 L24-25)
- The kitchen has a window facing the mountains, through which cool air comes down from them. (Book 1, Ch1.16 L53)
## Location and surroundings
- Erin complains that the stream she fetches water from lies a long walk across the grasslands from the inn. (Book 1, Ch1.06 L115-116)
- Klbkch says the inn's site was once a small community whose people all died of a plague, and that the inn was abandoned nearly three years earlier, so there is no law against occupying the area. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L141,L149,L306)
- The inn stands on a long, gradual incline; Liscor is visible from it. Relc and Klbkch call the walk to the city about ten minutes, but Erin judges it nearer an hour. (Book 1, Ch1.12 L44-49)
- Ceria, discussing the inn with Gerial, says it is odd to see Erin living so far out from the city; Gerial answers that it was barely an hour's walk even with them getting lost. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L160-161)
