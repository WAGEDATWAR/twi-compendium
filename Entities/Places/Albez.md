# Albez
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.08H
> See also: Horns of Hammerad
## Overview
- Albez is a ruined city with a dungeon beneath it. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L180,L359)
- The Horns of Hammerad find treasure in the dungeon below Albez, and when they climb out into a light snow shower they realize it is the middle of winter. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L180,L191)
- Ceria and the others celebrate that they found treasure in ruined Albez against the odds. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L359)
## Later accounts
- Stan says he has been to Albez many times, probably before Yvlon was born, and never found more than a few trinkets. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L654)
- Yvlon tells Stan the Horns' treasure map came from a Drake they met in Liscor who had been part of the dungeon raid. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L664)
