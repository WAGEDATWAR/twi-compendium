# Manus
> Type: place | Also called: the City of War | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.33E
> See also: Walled Cities, Pallass, Antinium, Rafaema
## Overview
- Manus is a Walled City on the border zone next to the five Antinium Hives, on land claimed by the Antinium during the First Antinium War. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L6)
- Manus is called the City of War because it exports military might, not goods, with the largest army and training schools that produced legends such as General Sserys and Dalthson Forwing. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L17)
- Manus is the bastion of Drake might against the numberless Antinium and bordered their Hives in both wars, which is one reason the Antinium failed to expand. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L128)
- The narration says Manus is fairly poor as Walled Cities go, second-poorest after Oteslia, since its military industries and imports of Pallassian steel, Oteslian food and Fissival magics make it weak to economic pressure. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L18)
## Defenses
- Manus is designed as a many-sided star whose walls overlap and hit any threat. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L7)
- The outer wall slants outward a few degrees, so ladders fall on attackers, and it has machicolations that let defenders pour oil, spells and arrows on those below. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L8-9)
- Beyond the first wall lie a glacis and an inner wall on the incline, so attackers who take the outer wall find themselves exposed. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L11)
- The outer wall protects the lowest layer of the city, mostly residential, which can be sacrificed in an attack, while valuables lie past the second wall. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L12)
- The city is full of slanted walkways, choke points, portcullises and plazas that let defenders block an army's advance in three or four ways. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L13)
- The narration says Manus is unassailable by ground and almost so by air, with towers, narrow passages against flyers and sacrificial heights that can be evacuated in case of aerial spells. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L14)
## Academy and government
- The officer's academy in the inner city is to Izril what Wistram is to [Mages], and Drakes and Gnolls from every city come to learn war there. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L15-16)
- Manus' Lords and Ladies of the Wall add to their ranks every decade by merit rather than heritage, and include [Generals], [Captains], [Commanders] and [Strategists]. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L24-25)
- Manus is an elective monarchy whose leader rules for two years per election, and may be re-elected without limit if they do the job well. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L25)
## Wyvern attack on Pallass
- {event} Manus' war council runs to a room with a scrying orb and learns that Pallass is under attack by Wyverns. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L81-85)
- The people gathered include Dragonspeaker Luciva Skybreath, [Hunt Commander] Makhir, General Milka, Wall Lord Kishield and Wall Lady Rafaema. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L81-82,L87,L92)
- Rafaema says she ordered four full wings to alert and that they are already heading toward Pallass. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L118)
- {reveal} Rafaema looks no older than twenty-three yet commands deference beyond her apparent age, and she is a Dragon. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L170-172)
