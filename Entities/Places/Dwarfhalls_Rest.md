# Dwarfhalls Rest
> Type: place | Also called: the mountain; the Mountain City (see Goblin stronghold) | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.21E
> See also: Goblin stronghold, Tremborag, Reiss, Goblin Lord
## Overview
- {belief} Odveig repeats a rumor that a huge Goblin tribe lives in a nearby mountain called Dwarfhalls Rest, which no one had thought occupied, under a Great Goblin Chieftain, or so women the tribe rescued claim. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L44)
- {belief} Magnolia says a Great Goblin Chieftain appeared in Dwarfhalls Rest and has apparently lived there since the last Goblin King fell. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L211)
- Dwarfhalls Rest is the former home of Dwarves in another age, now a Goblin bastion housing the largest tribe in the north under the self-styled Great Goblin Chieftain Tremborag. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L13)
## Battle
- As mountains go Dwarfhalls Rest is not large, a foothill among giants compared to the High Passes. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L4)
- {event} When the Goblin Lord's troops in black armor entered a tunnel mined out of stone, Tremborag's Goblins ambushed them from the shadows and above. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L5)
- Goblin archers crouch on hidden ledges in the tunnel, and the Goblin Lord's soldiers cannot advance a step because the fight is on the Mountain City tribe's terms. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L6-7)
- {state} Reiss loses Tremborag's fortress to the Gold-rank teams and the Kingslayer, and Tremborag cries that his mountain is lost and his tribe flees. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L25,L61)
## Later
- Rags notes that Dwarfhalls Rest is a lot smaller than the mountains of the High Passes, barely more than a giant hill. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L256)
- The narration calls the Goblins that Tyrion Veltras marched south the Goblins of the Great Chieftain of the Mountain's tribe, from Dwarfhalls Rest. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L298)
