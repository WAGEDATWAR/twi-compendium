# Tailless Thief
> Type: place | Also called: Peslas' inn | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.10
> See also: Liscor, Peslas, The Wandering Inn
## Overview
- The Tailless Thief is an inn in the north side of Liscor that Selys calls the most famous and most expensive inn in the city. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L190)
- The Tailless Thief is run by the Drake [Innkeeper] Peslas. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L2,L51)
## Description
- When Erin first visits, the Tailless Thief is nearly full before midday, with retired and well-off Drake customers and Drake barmaids, and Erin sees no Gnoll there. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L208-209)
- The inn has no broken chairs, no holes in the walls, bright lamps and a bustling staff, and Erin judges it a proper inn without flaws. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L210)
- Its clientele is entirely Drake, with no Gnoll or Human present. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L4)
## Reputation and rivalry
- The narration calls the Tailless Thief the most famous and well-reputed inn in Liscor. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L2)
- Even after Erin's Skills make her inn about three times as attractive as other inns, the Tailless Thief remains a byword for Drakes in Liscor, though it is specialized while Erin's inn accepts everyone. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L5)
- Olesm considers the Tailless Thief the best inn in Liscor, because of its food from a [Cook] with [Advanced Cooking], its high-level [Innkeeper] and its advantageous placement. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L2)
## Role in Book 7-9
- After Ilvriss levels up Swifttail's class, he tells Weille to run to the Tailless Thief and have its [Innkeeper] prepare a celebratory feast and says he will buy out the inn for the day. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L108)
- Later, characters talk about the innkeeper's new dish of hamburgers and say the recipe came from Erin. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L514-515)
- {event} Later, the guests from Hectval, who arrived unannounced, are placed at the Tailless Thief. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L382)
