# Wistram
> Type: place | Also called: Wistram Academy; the Academy; the Isle of Mages | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.06R
> See also: Pisces, Ceria Springwalker, Archmages
## Overview
- Ceria says that Wistram accepts necromancers, and that Pisces was expelled for what he wanted to do with necromancy. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L98)
- Ceria adds that every time the Academy learns where Pisces is staying, it sends a team to capture him. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L124)
- Krshia says Wistram is a place of magic where mages go to learn, nationless and a nation unto itself, which has never bowed to any ruler. (Book 1, Ch1.35 L129)
- Krshia says that, since a past grievance, [Mages] from Wistram are not welcome among the Gnoll tribes and the tribes do not trade with their island. (Book 1, Ch1.35 L137)
- Krshia warns that the seas around the college are calm at times but at others devour ships and sink fleets, especially in winter. (Book 1, Ch1.35 L153)
- A speaker tells Pisces that the academy has existed for thousands of years, founded when many nations were at war and mages wanted a place to learn and teach in peace. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L342)
- The same speaker says Wistram is independent, with an army of its own, and that only a grand fleet could reach its shores with weather spells raised against it. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L342)
- Wistram sells enchantments and analysis to adventurers, doing a booming trade in better gear. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L345)
## The isle and the citadel
- Wistram is an island inside a magical bubble where the weather ceases, reached by sailing through a storm. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L252-253)
- Its castle is a citadel built into the island's cliffs, a few interconnected buildings with impossibly tall spires and domes, glowing runes in the air, and a tower that looks engulfed in fire. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L255-257)
- Inside, Wistram is far larger than it appears from outside; mages have built high into the air and far beneath the isle, and newcomers get lost. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L271-276)
- Over thousands of years so many mages have built onto the academy that one can run for hours without crossing it, and a straight line is rare. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L42-43)
- Magical barriers, locked doors and Golem guards near stairwells stop new students from going too high or too low, where dangerous experiments run wild. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L318)
- The top three floors are off limits to new mages, and above them a door is guarded by Cognita and many Golems; mages here can cast Tier 5 or even Tier 6 spells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L366,L373)
- The catacombs hold many stored bones that sometimes reanimate people who are killed down there. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L386)
## Students
- Wistram accepts new students on only one day of the year, when the isle opens. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L7)
- Twenty gold pieces buys a month of study, after which a student must prove their worth by an exam or be sent home. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L9; Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L178)
- Living in Wistram without a scholarship costs hundreds of gold, so most hope to pass the exam, which rewards a high level or a unique spell or Skill. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L179-180)
- {event} Ghouls burst into the dining hall during Ceria's first meal; she and many mages kill them with spells. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L367-380)
- New students take mandatory basic courses, and the academy has no signs or maps, so they must find classrooms and rules themselves. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L34,L76-82)
- Ceria and Pisces never find the illusion class, and Pisces suspects it is a prank on newcomers. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L338)
- Combat mages train in the early morning on the rocks, and duels over wands or other matters, fought with spells or debate, are common and bet on. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L74-75,L92-99)
- After the yearly exams, [Captains] chartered from around the world come to take away the failed students; of the hundreds who come, less than a tenth pass. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L5)
- Thirty-four students pass in Ceria's year. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L13-14)
## Role in the wider world
- The mages on Wistram's isle link scrying orbs, a service offered beyond Drake cities, such as a Titan's mock battle in Baleros watched by kings and lords. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L241-243)
- Pallass sells expensive playbacks of the Liscor battle made by minor Wistram artifacts that replay it, which they call movies. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L338)
- When Earth humans arrive among the students, the Revivalists shelter them and give them autonomy, to Archmage Feor's displeasure. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L396-397)
- {belief} It is rumored that Wistram watches everything through scrying orbs and the Mage's Guilds' [Messages], though this has never been proven. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L549)
- Archmages Feor, Viltach and Nailihuaile watch Empress Nsiia by hijacking her scrying spell: Feor is a half-Elf and leader of the most powerful Centrist faction, Nailihuaile is a Star Lamia enchanter and Revivalist leader, and Viltach is a Human and head of the Libertarians. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L581-585)
- {event} Wistram sends a highest-priority [Message] through every Mage's Guild that the King of Destruction has allied with Tiqr, then retracts it about ten minutes later as an error made without verification. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L305-310)
- {belief} A speaker calls the false announcement beyond scandalous and says Wistram's credibility is ruined. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L326)
- {event} Wistram puts a bounty of two thousand gold on Pisces Jealnet as a Level 26 [Necromancer], sent to Mage's Guilds across Izril. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L890)
- {belief} The bounty text calls Pisces a common-born Terandrian whose family serves House Dultel, accused of petty thievery and necrophilia. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L894)
- {state} Later, Cognita is said to have granted Pisces and Ceria the authority to call themselves graduates, and Grimalkin notes Wistram has amended the false-claim charge. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L149; Book 14, Ch6.61L L115)
- Wistram's single "channel" of magic-vision can show only one event at a time, and it receives requests from over forty nations, so it declines to choose between them. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L26-29,L66)
- Wistram apologises for broadcasting scenes of dead children and blood, and says it will need policies such as delays. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L4)
- {event} Archmages Feor, Nailihuaile and Viltach expose the Golden Triangle as a fraudulent scheme in a broadcast as the scheme collapses and investors lose hundreds of thousands of gold. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L1158-1167)
- New students arriving later include Flynn Patel, two apparent Earthers from Baleros, Terandrian nobles with a crying child, a Gorgon, Oldblood Drakes, Stitchfolk and a swordsman. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L654-685)
