# Golden Triangle
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.19
> See also: Pallass, Montressa, Wistram
## Overview
- Montressa says Wistram wants her and Beza to investigate the Golden Triangle in Pallass on orders from Naili. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L181-183)
- Beza says some [Lords] and nations have joined the movement, and thousands of gold are being turned into profits. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L185)
- Montressa and Bezale discover how much gold is going to the Golden Triangle and file worried reports. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L921)
- Yerzhen says that everyone knows it is a pyramid scheme. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L211)
- {belief} Yerzhen says he heard a rumor that Viltach and Feor both want the scheme to continue, because large groups going into debt helps Wistram. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L215)
- Yerzhen says the Walled Cities are at the center of the scheme and over a hundred thousand gold pieces are in circulation through it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L220-222)
- The Elusive Lot decide to warn friends and gangs, expecting Wistram to move once word spreads. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L218)
