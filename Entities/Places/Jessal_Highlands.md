# Jessal Highlands
> Type: place | Also called: | First appears: Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3
> See also: Antinium, Klbkch, Xrn, Wrymvr, Drake cities
## Overview
- A history book calls the Clash on the Jessal Highlands the event that began the Second Antinium War in earnest. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L30)
- {event} Five days after the start of summer, a Drake army formed by three of the Walled Cities and a number of lesser city-states marched past the Antinium's border and laid siege to one of their Hives by surprise, meaning to exterminate the Antinium. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L31)
- The Drake army laid waste to the Hive, killing Workers and Soldiers, and used rain-based tactics to flood its tunnels. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L32)
## The battle as Klbkch tells it
- Klbkch says that he and Xrn were the only two Prognugators close enough to respond, and that they found the Drake army dug in with a kill zone of [Mages], [Archers] and a wall of [Spearmen]. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L50)
- Klbkch says the Antinium had only seven Queens left, so the Drakes' strike on the Hive forced them either to attack or to lose a Queen. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L59,L69)
- {event} Klbkch charges with the Soldiers while Xrn fights the Drake [Mages], and the Antinium break the Drake lines only when Wrymvr arrives with the Flying Antinium. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L95-96,L110-117)
- On that day the Antinium freed the besieged Hive and its youngest Queen, whom Klbkch personally rescued from drowning and who later led the Hive in Liscor. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L119)
## Aftermath
- The history book says the battle on the Jessal Highlands ended in disaster for the Drakes, leaving recriminations among city-states that had not known of the surprise attack. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L146)
- The history book says the Antinium launched a counterattack at once, and their armies were marching again within a day of the battle. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L146-147)
