# Oteslia
> Type: place | Also called: City of Growth | First appears: Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3
> See also: Walled Cities, Pallass, Salazsar, Manus, Fissival, Cire
## Overview
- Oteslia is a Walled City, known as the City of Growth, that grows cash crops and exports produce cheap as Izril's agricultural giant. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L147,L149)
- The narration says Oteslia is the poorest of all the Walled Cities by a lot, though poorest of them is still richer than most, because a Level 30 [Farmer] harvests six times more than someone from Erin's world. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L148,L153)
- Oteslia grows coffee beans, actually red berries, which it is trialing as an expensive additive in stamina potions. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L145-146)
- Oteslia has saved more than one city from starvation with free food shipments, so smaller cities consider it a hero, but a quarrel with it would only make enemies buy food elsewhere. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L151-152)
## Description
- Oteslia contends year by year with ensuring harvests and fighting monsters such as locusts, Crelers and hungry birds, employing [Druids] and [Gardeners]. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L153-154)
- Oteslia has the largest Pegasus population in the world and the largest Gnoll-to-Drake population of any Walled City. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L154,L156)
- The city is built upward around a vast central tree, the Heart of Oteslia, and is surrounded by a vast lake that serves as defense, water source and entertainment. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L155-156)
- The First Gardener has a balcony built into the wood of the Heart of Oteslia and looks over terraced rice paddies and arid zones where every plant is grown. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L223-225)
## Design and secret
- The narration says the Walled Cities were designed for roles: Manus to train, Zeres to hold the seas, Pallass to create, Salazsar to mine, Fissival to connect and Oteslia to provide. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L226)
- Fissival's teleportation network would have connected all the Walled Cities so that none would starve under siege, thanks to Oteslia's foodstuffs. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L227)
- {reveal} Oteslia's great secret is known to Manus alone among the other Walled Cities. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L222)
- {reveal} The Drake Cire, ward of the First Gardener Shaerrha, is a Dragon over ninety years old who is in the middle of his adolescence. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L262,L277-278,L280)
- The First Gardener forbids Cire to date Plains Gnolls, fearing he will let something slip. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L252,L255,L260)
## History
- {event} During the Second Antinium War, at a private meeting in Oteslia, the Lords and Ladies of the Wall found six Silent Antinium and four [Assassins] dead under the tablecloths after ten minutes. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L154)
