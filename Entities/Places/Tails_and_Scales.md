# Tails and Scales
> Type: place | Also called: Tails | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.09
> See also: Pallass, Rufelt, Lasica, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Tails and Scales is a bar in Pallass that a character calls the best bar in all of Pallass. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L818)
- Tails and Scales is very bar-focused but has a number of tables. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L261)
- Rufelt and Lasica own the bar, and Rufelt serves drinks as its [Barkeep] with an apprentice named Usshi. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L285-286)
- A speaker says it is one of the most famous bars in Pallass, where you normally have to be invited by a regular. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L308)
## Fittings and staff
- A table in the center of the room is a solid flat black block with a furnace below it, which Lasica cooks on as a hot plate table. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L261,L293-294)
- Lasica cooks appetizers or an early dinner at the table while a [Line Cook] learns from her. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L291-294)
- Rufelt serves flaming drinks and a drink that turns the whole glass invisible. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L300)
- A Dullahan [Cellist] in white and green ceramic armor plays in the bar at night. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L301-304)
- Its customers include Drakes, Gnolls, Dullahans and a Garuda couple, [Smiths] such as Maughin, and many other tradespeople. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L288-289)
## Wyvern attack
- {event} During the Wyvern attack, Rufelt waves people inside, slams the door and bars it, and the Wyvern's impact only rattles the door while the bar itself does not shake. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L885-889)
- Lasica says the owners paid for the door to be practically indestructible, so it should stay safe from the Wyverns. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L921-923)
- The bar shelters Erin, Rufelt, Lasica, the Gnoll Felkhr, a Dullahan family, two Drake [Warriors] and a young jet-black-scaled Drake called Lady Salkis. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L895-896,L901-903,L918)
- Viss, an injured Drake [Bodyguard], is next to Lady Salkis, the highborn Drake. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L1001)
