# Baleros
> Type: place | Also called: Balerosian (adj.) | First appears: Book 3, Ch1.00D
> See also: Geneva Scala
## Overview
- Baleros is a continent where, despite nations and kingdoms, much of the fighting is done by mercenary groups ranging from small troops to huge Companies of multiple regiments. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L172)
- Clara tells Geneva that countless Companies in Baleros have their own cities and towns as bases and earn income from contracts protecting places. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L175)
- Clara says the Companies send out their own armies, and the big ones have influence and representatives in other nations. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L175)
## Species and Goblins
- The narration calls Baleros a continent of war and death, where the warring species are Dullahan, Gazers, Lizardfolk, Centaurs and Humans. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L4)
- The warring species have no time for Goblins in the undergrowth until Goblins cause trouble, when adventurers or hired mercenary companies hunt them. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L4-5)
