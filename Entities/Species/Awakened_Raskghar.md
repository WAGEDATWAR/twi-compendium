# Awakened Raskghar
> Type: species | Also called: the awakened; Nokha and the twenty awakened | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.42
> See also: Raskghar, Calruz, Mrsha
## Overview
- The awakened Raskghar are Raskghar who underwent a ritual and call themselves awakened. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L633-634)
- {event} In the ritual, twenty Gnolls are killed on a stone altar, and the chosen Raskghar tear open their chests, eat their hearts, and change, leaving twenty-one Raskghar standing together. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L148-150)
- Calruz inspects the twenty-one and approves. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L151-152)
## Behaviour
- A large female awakened Raskghar who performed the ritual claims to be stronger, smarter and faster than before, hungry for battle, and able to speak language. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L632-636)
- She brings Calruz the head of a giant Face-Eater Moth mother, killed with sixteen others, and says she has found a treasure site that may be opened. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L637-641)
- She says more rituals will mean more awakened Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L641)
- Nokha, a female awakened Raskghar, says "We are whole" to the captive Gnolls. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L160-161)
- The lesser Raskghar give way to the awakened, and the Raskghar then mate in a frenzy that fills the night. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L162-165)
