# Xol
> Type: person | Also called: Xol of Ingrilt; the Juggernaut | First appears: Ch6.22D
> See also: Tulm, Merrik, Peki, Luan, Zevai
## Overview
- Xol of Ingrilt is a Dullahan [Juggernaut], one of the largest and most famous War Walkers in the Iron Vanguard company, in crimson armour with silver inlays. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L695-699)
- Xol is fourteen feet tall when standing and was crouching when he hid before charging. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L695)
- Xol is often seen with Tulm's personal vanguard. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L699)
- A smaller Dullahan carries Xol's head, which is far larger than normal and wears the same helmet. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L535)
