# Blighted Queen
> Type: person | Also called: Her Majesty; the Queen of Rhir | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.03C
> See also: Blighted King, Nereshal, Erille, Fool, Tom
## Overview
- The Blighted Queen is the wife and consort of the Blighted King of Rhir. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L43-45)
- The Blighted Queen is very tall, younger than the King, and cannot be more than thirty, though her height makes her seem older. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L18)
- The Blighted Queen carries a long mace as black as obsidian. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L36)
## Warrior
- {fact} A courtier says the Blighted Queen is at least as skilled a warrior as the champions dueling in the hall, and was known for her mace before marrying the King. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L456)
- A lord says the Blighted Queen was a famed warrior on the battlefield before becoming Queen, which may be why the King was enamored of her. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L101)
- The Blighted Queen is full of action and ready to move out when the Demons invade the palace. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L39)
- The Blighted Queen tells the King that if the Demons are not repelled, all is lost. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L44-45)
- The Blighted King refuses to send her with Nereshal to ambush the teleport mage, because losing Nereshal would bring the end closer. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L42-43)
- The Blighted Queen volunteers to go and fight the Demons herself, mace in hand. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L187-188)
- The Blighted Queen walks out into the courtyard with her mace drenched in gore, beside the King, ignoring Tom's boasts. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L388)
## The Fool's attack
- The Blighted Queen rushes at the Fool in the courtyard and blocks his thrown daggers with her mace. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L422)
- The Blighted Queen trips on a juggling ball thrown by the Fool. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L422-423)
- The Blighted Queen makes a sound of pain when the Fool's dagger draws blood at the King's throat. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L441)
