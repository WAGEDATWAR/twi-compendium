# Sacra
> Type: person | Also called: Odveig (cover identity) | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.14
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Reynold, Ressa, Laken, Wiskeria, Durene, Ryoka Griffin
## Overview
- Sacra is a [Maid] in Lady Magnolia Reinhart's service whose specialty is undercover work. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L203; Book 7, Ch5.10E L14-16)
- Sacra wears boots that do not match the rest of her maid outfit. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L809)
- Sacra fights with a mace, which she prefers in combat. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L14)
## Early appearance
- Reynold and Sacra caught the last assassin as he tried to flee Magnolia's estate, and he is dead. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L178)
## The Odveig cover (Book 5)
- As Odveig, she is a [Macewoman] and the self-proclaimed leader of the Celestial Trackers, with the [Witch] Wiskeria as her subordinate. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L83-86)
- Laken senses that Odveig is paranoid that Wiskeria might take her place as leader. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L87)
- Odveig has an accent that runs "not a" together as one word, which makes Laken think of Scotland. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L19-21)
- Odveig and Wiskeria told Laken the rumour of a Great Goblin Chieftain living on a mountain called Dwarfhalls Rest. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L40-46)
- {belief} Odveig said women were found outside the gates of Merendia after a Goblin tribe left them there, but she called it rumour and hearsay. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L41-44)
- Odveig says the Goblins still in the mountain are angry after a tribe splintered from it. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L49)
- Odveig found the site where Beniar was attacked, with no sign of Fabiel or his horse, and pulled back feeling watched. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L15-18)
- {reveal} Odveig revealed herself to Laken as a spy, knocked out Durene and Wiskeria with her mace, and gave her real name as Sacra. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L354-375)
- {belief} Sacra told Laken that Lady Magnolia would be very interested to speak with him after her report, "if you survive." (Book 5, Ch4.22E L372)
- {state} Sacra then left the cottage and rode out of the village on a horse. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L376-377)
- {reveal} Laken and Durene discuss that Odveig worked for Magnolia Reinhart and posed as a Silver-rank team leader. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L3-8,L81)
- {reveal} Later, Laken refers to Wiskeria as loyal all along after it was revealed that Odveig was the spy. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L144)
## In Magnolia's service
- Reynold knew Sacra as one of Magnolia's special servants, like him, who had trailed Ryoka in Invrisil. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L203)
- Sacra told Reynold of her undercover mission to learn who Ryoka met, and that the stranger was an [Emperor]. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L241-246)
- Sacra served as a mace-wielding armoured battle analyst beside Magnolia in the field, since Ressa was absent. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L7,L70)
- Magnolia regrets that Odveig was outed as a spy but says such is the cost of Sacra's work. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L16)
- Magnolia asks Sacra for her impression of Laken, whom she met in person. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L15)
- Sacra visited Wiskeria in her maid uniform and told her that Sacra is her true name. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L183-186)
- Sacra says Laken knows she is a spy and has at least three people watching her. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L202)
- Laken keeps two watchers on Sacra at all times. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L218)
- {belief} Sacra says Magnolia is concerned about Laken because he is an [Emperor]. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L236-237)
- Sacra and Reynold beat a fleeing [Thug Boss] in Invrisil and left him to the Watch. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L804-818)
- {belief} The Thug Boss claimed Magnolia is hands-off with Invrisil, and Reynold said that is what people say. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L810-812)
