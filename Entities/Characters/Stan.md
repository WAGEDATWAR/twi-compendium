# Stan
> Type: person | Also called: Crossbow Stan; Captain Stan | First appears: Ch6.18H
> See also: Alais, Ksmvr, Ceria Springwalker, Yvlon
## Overview
- Stan, known as Crossbow Stan, is an older Silver-rank [Crossbowman] in his early forties who wears chainmail and carries a dagger and a small pouch. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L433,L446,L459)
- Stan leads the Silver-rank team the Boltspitters, and his teammate Lailli has a broken foot. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L437)
- Stan stays in the Silver ranks because he lacks the punch to go higher. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L459)
- Stan keeps preloaded crossbows in a bag of holding, six in all, and has spent his coin on better bags. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L458)
- Stan carries two crossbows at his side and four more in his bag of holding, and he can fire six bolts in a blink. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L603)
- Alais says Stan has two kids, avoids huge risks and has a lot saved up, and Alais confirms his wife left him. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L686-688)
