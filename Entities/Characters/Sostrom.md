# Sostrom
> Type: person | Also called: Sostrom Reidez | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.07R
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Gerial, Calruz, Skinner, Horns of Hammerad
## Overview
- Sostrom Reidez is a human mage of the adventuring team the Horns of Hammerad. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L278)
- Sostrom is in his early thirties, or very late twenties, and is bald save for his eyebrows. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L282)
- Sostrom tugs a pointy hat lower on his head and wears his mage's hat whenever possible because he is completely bald. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L282; Book 1, Ch1.40 L174)
- Of the three mages in the Horns of Hammerad, Sostrom is the second-strongest, according to Gerial. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L186)
- Sostrom cannot cast [Fireballs], but Gerial has grown to appreciate his ability. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L186)
## Meeting Ryoka and Erin
- {event} Sostrom repaired Ryoka Griffin's iPhone with the spell [Repair], tapping it with his wand, whose tip flashed green. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L269-270)
- Sostrom introduced himself as at Ryoka's service and flushed lightly when she thanked him for the repair. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L278-282)
- Ryoka notes there was a flash of magic when Sostrom repaired her phone, unlike the finger-glow of another mage's spell. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L42)
- Ryoka reminds herself that Sostrom fixed her iPhone and gave her music. (Book 1, Ch1.13R L138)
- Sostrom is one of the Horns who come out of the inn to watch Ryoka leave for the High Passes. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L296)
- Sostrom nudges Ryoka and mutters to her in the inn's common room. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L238)
- Sostrom chokes on his drink when Erin addresses Calruz. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L182)
## Conversation about Lady Magnolia and Gazi (Book 1, Ch1.40)
- Sostrom says the innkeeper, Miss Solstice, was sick, so the Horns all left the inn without breakfast. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L145)
- Sostrom is interested in Ryoka's repaired phone, her fist-fighting skill and her lack of levels, and thinks there is something else to her. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L175)
- Sostrom says Lady Magnolia's name tells you how important she is. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L170-171)
- Sostrom immediately offers his seat to Gazi when he recognises her name. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L213)
- {event} When Gazi's group fires a webbing artifact, Gerial and Sostrom are pinned to the ground, and Sostrom's wand is caught in the webbing. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L283-294)
- {event} After Ceria frees them, Sostrom points to an alleyway and the three adventurers run from the Watch. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L302)
- Sostrom hands Ceria a potion as they walk back toward the inn. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L306)
## The undead dungeon
- The Horns of Hammerad formed two years earlier and have ten members, including Sostrom and the mage Marian. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L4,L11)
- Sostrom is described as liking Marian as the only other human mage in the group who does not tease him about his bald spot. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L6)
- {event} In the first attack of the undead, Sostrom fights back-to-back with Ceria, throwing bolts of fire from his staff. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L64-68)
- {event} When a Crypt Lord seized Sostrom, he screamed in agony while Gerial stabbed it. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L251-253)
- {event} Calruz axed the Crypt Lord and Sostrom fell to the ground, its severed hand still holding him. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L255)
- {event} Sostrom casts [Sticky Ground] at Calruz's order, a spell whose webbing would slow the dead down. (Book 1, Ch1.02H L196-199)
- {state} Skinner took Sostrom's skin and made it part of his body, killing Sostrom. (Book 1, Ch1.02H L333-334)
- Sostrom screamed for help before he died, but Ceria ignored him. (Book 1, Ch1.02H L334)
