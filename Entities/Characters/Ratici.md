# Ratici
> Type: person | Also called: Gentleman Caller (Drake); the hat duo (with Wilovan) | First appears: Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn
> See also: Wilovan, Niers, Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings, Erin Solstice, Numbtongue, Fierre, The Enforcer
## Overview
- Ratici is a short, slender Drake in a cap who works with Wilovan as one of the Gentlemen Callers. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L260)
- Ratici is a master-class [Gentleman Thief] who notices hidden objects and stashes almost everywhere he walks. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L84-89)
- Ratici tells Erin he is the finest [Thief] she will ever see, and Wilovan agrees. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L269)
- Ratici belongs to the Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings, which Palt calls a smaller but elite gang with big players in northern Izril. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L231,L265)
- Ratici cannot see through the door to the Garden of Sanctuary, which drives him crazy. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L89)
- Ratici can produce items such as a cup of ketchup with a flick of his hands. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L703)
## Contract with the Tall Man (Book 17)
- Ratici tells Wilovan that he and Wilovan are late on a contract, and that the Brothers did not tell the Tall Man they were in the south. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L701-702)
- Ratici says the Tall Man pays well and that after the Lady Reinhart business the inn seems the place to be. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L705)
- {event} Ratici rearranges the pieces of a magical chess puzzle board in Erin's room as instructed by the Tallman on the other end of a communication spell. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L1276-1284)
- {event} Ratici confirms to the Tallman that the room belongs to Erin Solstice, and the Tallman is Niers Astoragon, the Titan of Baleros. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L1286-1290,L1297)
- {event} Niers hires Ratici and his companion for escort duty, and Ratici agrees to keep an eye out after Niers offers to double the fee. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L1291-1296)
- {event} Ratici opens the inn's door for Erin and tells another gang he trusts they have an understanding, disarming them unnoticed. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L920-928)
