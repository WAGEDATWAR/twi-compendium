# Pebblesnatch
> Type: person | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.36
> See also: Erin Solstice, Garry, Ulvama, Raidpear, Rabbiteater, Numbtongue, Griffon Hunt
## Overview
- Pebblesnatch is a small Cave Goblin whose name Numbtongue gives to Erin. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L276-278)
- Pebblesnatch is a scrawny, young Cave Goblin with grey skin who wears a big white chef's hat. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L56; Book 15, Ch7.05P L1244)
- Pebblesnatch has crimson eyes. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L493)
- Pebblesnatch keeps a worn white cloth as her pillow and a glowing crimson mana stone she shows no one. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L151-152)
- Pebblesnatch remembers Erin's inn as the one time in her life she felt safe. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L120)

## Prisoner and [Forager Cook] (Book 15)
- {state} Pebblesnatch is carried north in a cage in a wagon after the Humans dragged her from her hiding place. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L111-114)
- Pebblesnatch has nightmares of the dungeon, the Raskghar and the Facestealer, and dreams of the inn that wake her in the cage. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L116-121)
- Pebblesnatch is a [Grabber], [Cook] and [Hider] with over thirteen levels, high for a Cave Goblin. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L319)
- {event} Pebblesnatch steals a pan and ladle, bites Ulvama and flees to hide in a tree trunk. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L467-482)
- {event} Pebblesnatch puts on her chef's hat and orders two Cave Goblins to fetch eggs, and the other Goblins let her pass. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L501-510)
- Pebblesnatch credits Erin and Garry for her cooking, having learned from Garry the night of the cooking competition. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L547-548)
- {event} Pebblesnatch fries meat scraps and eggs into a Wildegg Pocket and wins over Raidpear with it. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L568-578)
- {state} Pebblesnatch reaches Cook Level 6 and gains [Ingredients Sense (Minor)]. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L624-625)
- {state} Pebblesnatch reaches Cook Level 10 and becomes a [Forager Cook], with Grabber removed, gaining [Natural Oils] and [One More Portion]. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L851-855)
- {state} Pebblesnatch's [Natural Oils] distills cooking oil from the animals the Redfangs bring in. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L859)
- [One More Portion] always leaves Pebblesnatch a bit more food than expected, and even Ulvama praises it. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L860-861)
- {event} Pebblesnatch charges alone at the Human army with a ladle, climbs Durene and is held in the air. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1235-1253)
- {event} Mavika and the [Witches] step between the forces, and Mavika declares the Goblins not at fault, ending the battle. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1264-1270)
