# Iratze
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling
> See also: Salthorn, Orjin, Mendi
## Overview
- Iratze is a young man from Mexico who is a martial artist from Earth, and leads a small group of Earthers in Chandrar. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L410,L413)
- Iratze's group consists of a gym's younger members, two aspiring fighters, a girl visiting her father and eight people from outside, one of whom had been in a car. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L410)
- Nearly twice as many people were teleported from Earth as the group that stuck together and survived. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L411)
- Iratze speaks a different tongue to his companion, which Orjin cannot place and takes for Drathian. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L461,L473)
## Joining Pomle
- Iratze watches the Strongest of Pomle fight the Fury of Winds' cult, and notes how superficial the cultists' wounds on Orjin are. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L413,L419)
- {event} Iratze tells Mendi he studied a different school called MMA, or Mixed Martial Arts, similar to Pomle's fighting, and that he can teach a bit. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L445)
- {event} Iratze beats the Dullahan Mendi on the ground, holding her in an arm bar until she taps out. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L449,L452,L457)
- Iratze is better at grappling than Mendi, who is overconfident. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L444-449)
- Pomle's warriors surround Iratze to ask about the new move, and Mendi bows to him. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L457)
- {event} Orjin tells Iratze that if he can teach more like this, there is always a place for him, and Pomle accepts the group. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L462-463)
- Iratze says not all of his group can fight, but some hope to at least get something to eat. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L460)
## Book 18
- Iratze is a new member of Pomle with a mixed-martial-arts background, learning grappling from Master Salthorn alongside a few other Humans. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L55-60)
- Iratze asks Salthorn how she would stop a Selphid, and she explains how a Human would break the Selphid's body down. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L57-59)
- Iratze has shown Pomle's [Martial Artists] what his world developed, though he has not told Orjin so. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L60-61)
- Salii says Iratze helped her come up with Pomle's spectator sparring matches, which already make money from bettors. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L176,L194-195)
