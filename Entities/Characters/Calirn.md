# Calirn
> Type: person | Also called: Knight-Commander Calirn; Knight-Commander of the Order of Seasons | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.42E
> See also: Ser Raim, Talia Kallinad, Belavierr, Order of Seasons
## Overview
- Calirn is the Knight-Commander of the Order of Seasons, who in the Order's stronghold can command even the Grandmasters as a first among equals. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L465-466)
- Calirn is a [Winter Knight], one of the small and solitary Season of Winter. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L505,L565)
- Calirn can turn the cold air around him into a freezing vortex, give his blade a frostbite edge, and form a shield of ice. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L565)
- Calirn is addressed as a grandmaster-level figure, and the Thronebearer Ser Dalimont bows to him as Knight Commander. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L566)
## The Hunt for Belavierr (Book 12)
- Calirn congratulates Dame Talia on the game and says the Summer's Champion is pleased, while the Fall's Sentinel and Calirn himself have reservations about the outcome. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L510)
- {belief} Calirn says the death of the Fence bears Belavierr's magic, but warns it could be one of her thousand misdirections without confirmation. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L494)
- Calirn refuses to contact Ser Raim on so little evidence at first. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L494)
- Calirn confirms to Talia and Dame Wera that Belavierr is the infamous witch who cursed the Griffin Prince Ostevien and destroyed the city of Halen overnight. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L499-502)
- {belief} The Summer's Champion says he thought Belavierr, once driven from a village, would not be seen for another thousand years. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L538-539)
- Frost forms on the walls of the room when Calirn speaks of Belavierr, and melts when the Summer's Champion bursts in. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L530-531)
- Calirn orders the Fall's Sentinel to prepare his Season for a grand ritual spell, though he does not know when or if it must be cast. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L559)
- Calirn tells the Academy of Wistram it may be needed, and tells Ser Raim to return in haste. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L559)
- Calirn asks the Hunter's Guild to send its highest-level experts with Ser Raim, since the Order cannot muster an army in time. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L559)
- Calirn declares that Ser Raim has exceeded Level 50, is far above Talia, and has the Skill and will to end the Stitch Witch. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L582)
- Calirn says Ser Raim injured Belavierr once this winter and she fled his flame. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L582)
- Calirn orders the grand ritual prepared a second time, and the Fall's Sentinel protests that the cost is too high. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L367-370)
- The ritual circle lies in one of the oldest parts of the Order's stronghold, a relic of a half-Elven empire older than the Order. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L368-369)
- The Fall's Sentinel, Calirn's old comrade, tells him the ritual cannot move an army, because in it magic is the true weight. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L377-383)
- The Fall's Sentinel says only thirty-two Knights can be sent, none with a strong aura or Skills that affect the body, and no Autumn Knights. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L386)
- Calirn orders the Summer's Champion and the Spring's Warden to choose volunteers who are ready for death. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L390)
- Calirn sends eight Knights of Summer and eight of Spring, each over Level 30, taking steel and silver only, to reinforce Ser Raim. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L392)
- Calirn speaks to Raim through a scrying orb after the battle. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L716)
- When Raim abandons his honor and walks to fight Belavierr, Calirn calls to him not to do it. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L1071)
- Later, after Raim's death and Belavierr's survival, Calirn lowers his head and turns away. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L1096)
## Ser Raim's Return (Book 17)
- {event} Calirn stands vigil on the docks of Phel's Light, in Pheislant, awaiting the funeral barge that carries Ser Raim's ashes. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L504,L514)
- Calirn has come personally from the Order's headquarters, which is in Pheislant on the Order's own lands, allied with the Terandrian Kingdom. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L504)
- Calirn is asked, by Raim and by his love, that they be buried together, and he will honor the request. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L513)
- Calirn considers Raim by far the highest-levelled Knight to have died here and resolves not to forget Belavierr's added sin. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L517-518)
- Calirn bows as the barge enters the harbour and calls on the Knights to let the Season of Summer welcome their fallen brethren. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L762-764)
