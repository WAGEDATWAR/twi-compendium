# Ivolethe
> Type: person | Also called: the Frost Faerie; Frost Faerie of the Winter Court | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.03
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Laken, Erin Solstice, Frost Faeries, Winter Court, Magnolia Reinhart
## Overview
- Ivolethe is a Frost Faerie who befriends Ryoka Griffin and travels with her through the winter. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L508; Book 3, Ch3.09 L76-77)
- Ivolethe says she is a member of the Winter Court and demands respect. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L32)
- Ivolethe is small and blue, and rides in Ryoka's belt pouch indoors. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L416; Book 3, Ch3.09 L76-77)
## Meeting Ryoka (Book 3)
- Ivolethe says she followed Ryoka with the other faeries, warned her of the armies, led her through the forest, and accepted the second price of her bargain. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L508)
- Ryoka invites Ivolethe into her room with "Come in", since faeries cannot enter uninvited. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L451-453)
- Cold iron saps a faerie's strength and makes her mortal, so Ivolethe cannot use magic indoors. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L41,L82)
- Ivolethe bites a chunk from a man's finger down to the bone. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L47)
- Ivolethe refuses to be Erin's friend, saying she has made one friend and needs no other. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L63)
- Ivolethe says she did not attend Erin's faerie banquet, though her kin spoke of it. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L72)
- Ivolethe does not know why Ryoka and Erin can see her, and says mortals can see fae under a full moon or after drinking the fae's secret drink. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L86,L94)
- {belief} Ivolethe claims to have witnessed the rise of King Arthur and the deaths of the three kings by Lugaid's spears. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L187)
- Ivolethe tells Ryoka and Erin the whole story of King Arthur, which Ryoka judges true. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L198)
- {event} A Runner seizes Ivolethe in the Runner's Guild, and Persua proposes selling her to a [Merchant] or [Alchemist] for thousands of gold coins. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L362-384)
- Ivolethe calls to her sisters for retribution before the Runner silences her, which frightens the other Runners. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L391-394)
- {event} Ryoka frees Ivolethe by using [Flashbang] and throwing a pepper potion on Persua and her friends. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L413-418)
- {belief} Ivolethe tells Ryoka that if she had been killed, others of the Winter Court who deal in death would have killed her killer. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L462-466)
- {rel} Ivolethe offers to teach Ryoka to run like the wind in exchange for friendship, and they shake on it. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L511-521)
## Travelling with Ryoka (Book 3)
- Ivolethe says the fey are born knowing magic and cannot teach it, comparing it to teaching a fish to swim. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L47)
- Ivolethe's glamor leaves Fals seeing only a blur even when she sits. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L144-147)
- Ivolethe stays at Ryoka's side day and night after the fight with Persua, usually riding in her belt pouch. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L77)
- Ivolethe refuses to speak with Ceria, calling her an offspring of whores, and says the fae hate what she has become, not her. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L413,L425,L430)
- Ivolethe refuses to examine Ryoka's artifacts, saying she can read magic's flow but not mortal systems. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L440,L453)
- Ivolethe tells Ryoka that in matters of life and death she must not interfere, and that the rule has been broken twice already. (Book 3, Ch3.10 L204)
- Ivolethe tells Ryoka that Goblin children are marching north in their hundreds, with the Goblin Lord's army below the pass and a smaller fleeing army ahead. (Book 3, Ch3.10 L384-394)
## Book 4
- The fae amuse themselves with Erin's antics in Esthelm, where snow has stopped falling, and Ivolethe reluctantly decides to leave and act. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L23-26)
- Ivolethe tells Ryoka that Laken bears the mark of command, the old destiny of leaders. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L349)
- Ivolethe says in German that Ryoka's pronunciation is terrible, and says she has travelled to many lands and learned many tongues. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L269-272)
- Ivolethe predicts a blizzard that peaks by nightfall and lasts up to three days. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L20,L27)
- {belief} Ivolethe tells Ryoka that a seed lies sealed inside the wand, a child trapped in a tree's last bloom. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L551-553)
- Ivolethe agrees to guide Ryoka to Riverfarm after Laken swears to put up a statue of her. (Book 4, Ch3.41 L7-8,L18-32)
- Other Frost Faeries join as guides and turn many colours, copying Ivolethe. (Book 4, Ch3.41 L80-89)
- Ivolethe warns Ryoka that the Winter Solstice is a day of power when spirits and magic mix, and that winter is two thirds over. (Book 4, ChInterlude_B04_1 L43,L51)
- Ivolethe warns Ryoka not to refuse gifts, enter uninvited, curse, bargain or lie that day. (Book 4, ChInterlude_B04_1 L38,L58)
- {belief} Ivolethe says other things take on different aspects on the solstice, though she does not know if magic grows stronger. (Book 4, ChInterlude_B04_1 L53)
- Ivolethe says she sees through all glamours and that the strange forest road is not a fae working. (Book 4, ChInterlude_B04_1 L137)
- {event} Ivolethe bites Ryoka to break a trance, then is blown away by a gust from the dark figures. (Book 4, ChInterlude_B04_1 L277,L283)
- {event} A tall woman with a sword leads Ryoka out of the dark, and Ryoka calls her Ivolethe, who tells her to run. (Book 4, ChInterlude_B04_1 L485-491)
## Teaching Ryoka (Book 5)
- Ivolethe cannot enter the Guild building because of its cold iron walls and lack of an invitation, and watches Ryoka and Erin through the window. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L553-555)
- Ivolethe values Ryoka's friendship, and flies south. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L556)
- Ivolethe watches two undead among a hundred dead Goblins as the Goblin Lord's larger force approaches. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L557-560)
- Ivolethe does not interfere, since meddling on that scale would bring punishment. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L561-562)
- Ivolethe tells Ryoka that winter will end soon and that they will part then. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L485)
- Ivolethe says teaching Ryoka will be entertaining for her. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L494)
- Ivolethe can speak to whomever she pleases though she seldom does, and she asks Garia for meat left outside without iron in return for a blessing. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L38,L42)
- A snowball falls on Ryoka from above, followed by distant laughter. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L46-49)
- {event} Ivolethe begins teaching Ryoka faerie magic on a hill near the Strongheart farm. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L239,L250-252)
- Ivolethe tells Ryoka that the only step is to sense the wind, which she cannot explain because there are no words for it. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L247,L288)
- {belief} Ivolethe says faerie magic is learned not by practice or books but in a moment of grace. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L293-295)
- Ivolethe says Ryoka lifted herself on the wind by being natural, not because of any help from her. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L374-381)
- Ivolethe weeps, thanks Ryoka for showing her something new, copies Ryoka's corkscrew, and says "Grace". (Book 5, Ch4.17 L384-391)
- Ivolethe says she lived with a body like Ryoka's for years. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L387)
- Frost Faeries cannot tell the future but predict it better than mortals, seeing crossroads where mortals see one road. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L4-7)
- {belief} Ivolethe believes the gods are dead and had better stay that way. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L11)
- {state} Ivolethe begins to melt as winter ends, and accepts it with resignation. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L519-527)

- Ivolethe tells Ryoka she cannot interfere and that doing so has dire consequences. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L165)
- Ivolethe tells Ryoka that the wind is a thing that thinks, and that faeries fly by stepping as if to walk into the sky. (Book 5, Ch4.29 L133)
- Ivolethe says she bent the rules for Ryoka the day before and that tricks on others are not exactly interference. (Book 5, Ch4.29 L328)

- Ivolethe sees Ryoka's death on the road if she runs toward Teriarch. (Book 5, Ch4.29 L338)
