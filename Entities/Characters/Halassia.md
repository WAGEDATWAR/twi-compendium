# Halassia
> Type: person | Also called: Halassia Evergleam | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.43
> See also: Garen Redfang, Jelaqua, Moore, Seborn, Ukrina, Keilam, Thornst, Halfseekers
## Overview
- Halassia Evergleam is a Scorchling, a half-Drake, who was a member of the Gold-rank adventuring team the Halfseekers. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L439,L442,L448; Book 9, Ch5.57 L269,L288)
- Scorchlings are a kind of Oldblood Drake who are born burnt by the fires within them, so that their scales flake off and cause them pain. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L450)
- Halassia's scales are blackened as if by soot, many are missing, and burnt flesh shows beneath. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L269)
- Halassia is an outcast among Drakes, rumoured to be cursed. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L269)
- Halassia was born in Salazsar, and was shunned by her kin. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L451-453)
- Halassia carries a wand and a staff, and is a spellcaster who blocks arrows with a spell. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L270-271; Book 9, Ch5.58 L429)
- Moore says Halassia was gentle, and Jelaqua says she always believed in the best of them. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L467; Book 9, Ch5.59 L631)
## With the Halfseekers
- Halassia tells Garen to stay behind the team and let her do the talking, since they are a Gold-rank team. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L288)
- Halassia and Keilam burn a path through walls of vines for the Halfseekers in the dungeon cave. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L380)
- Halassia smiles at Garen and tells the team to let him open the chest, since he gave them the lead. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L409-410)
- Halassia slaps Keilam's paws away from the chest and sprays him with water from her staff. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L419,L429)
- Ilvriss recalls Halassia as a Scorchling in the Halfseekers' ranks a few years earlier and thanks them for taking her in when her kin would not. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L442,L453)
- Jelaqua says Halassia's family did not want her remains, and that the Halfseekers buried her themselves. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L444-445)
## Her death
- {belief} Garen says Halassia pointed a wand at his chest and said the world must never have another Goblin King, and demanded the key. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L463-465)
- Garen says Ukrina told him to give her the key or bleed, while Halassia insisted. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L463,L482)
- {state} Garen kills Keilam, Thornst, Ukrina and Halassia in the Halfseekers' private room, and Halassia is the last alive. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L621-628)
- Halassia, with a severed hand, curses Garen for having been taken in and calls him a traitor before his sword is buried in her chest. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L623-628)
- Seborn says the Halfseekers tried to take the key from Garen, and that Garen struck first. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L480)
- After hearing Garen, the Halfseekers discuss whether Halassia and Ukrina, as Drakes, provoked him because the Goblin King mattered to them. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L631-645)
- The Halfseekers paid funeral money to the families of Thornst, Keilam and Halassia. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L386)
- Garen remembers the moment he struck at Halassia in his rage and despair as one he can never take back. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L45)
