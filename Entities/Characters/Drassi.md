# Drassi
> Type: person | Also called: Drassi Tewing; Liscor's number one chatterbox | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.14L
> See also: Ishkr, Lyonette, Erin, Selys, Olesm, Noass, Sir Relz, Wistram News Network
## Overview
- Drassi is a yellow-scaled Drake who chatters constantly. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L18,L28)
- Drassi is a friend of Selys and part of her usual gossip circle. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L511,L519)
- Drassi's full name is Drassi Tewing. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L469)
- Drassi grew up around Gnolls in Liscor, dated two Gnolls, and finds them too serious and hairy. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L210)
- Drassi chatters more than usual whenever she is upset or nervous. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L209)
- Drassi has a friend, Wessa, who has no class, sponges money off everyone and never listens to Drassi. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L262)
- Drassi never lies, and Liscor's Drakes and Gnolls know her for it. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L548-552)
## Levels and classes
- {state} Drassi introduces herself as a Level 8 [Barmaid] who also worked as a [Receptionist]. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L28)
- Drassi prefers waiting tables over being a [Receptionist], since a [Receptionist] must sit in one place. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L28)
- Selys warns Erin that Drassi is a gossip on a different level, so much that she has it as a class. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L192)
- {state} Later, Drassi is a Level 17 [Gossip], a high level for a class most people have not heard of. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L39)
- Drassi has the Skills [Time to Chat] and [Trusted Voice], which let her slip a word in whenever she wants. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L39)
- Drassi's Skill [Social Network] passes a story through her friend circle at the speed of rumor. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L557)
- Drassi says she cannot lie. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L82)
- {state} Drassi gains the Skill [Server's Prescience], which lets her anticipate what a customer wants. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L134-136)
- {state} Later, Drassi is a [Barmaid], [Gossip] and now [Bartender], having evolved her class from [Barmaid]. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L163; Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L66)
- Drassi's lower classes include Liscor's mandatory [Soldier], and a [Receptionist] class she lost after eight levels for chatting too much at the Adventurer's Guild. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L65)
- {state} Later, Drassi's [Gossip] class becomes the [Honest Reporter] class after she challenges the Wistram broadcasters. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1314-1315)
