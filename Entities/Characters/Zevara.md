# Zevara
> Type: person | Also called: Captain of the Liscorian Watch; Watch Captain Zevara; Zevara Sunderscale | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.26
> See also: Klbkch, Relc, Olesm, Erin Solstice, Ceria Springwalker, Pawn
## Overview
- Zevara is the Drake Captain of the Watch of Liscor, a tall Drake. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L64)
## Appearance and abilities
- The Captain of the Watch of Liscor, later named Zevara, is a female Drake with light-blue scales, yellow eyes and a scar on the left side of her face. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L84,L101; Book 1, Ch1.42 L64)
- Zevara breathes fire, blasting a zombie off its feet. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L173-174)
- Later, Erin sees that Zevara's eyes are a light purple, and Selys has told her that Zevara can breathe fire. (Book 2, Ch2.03 L24,L28)
- Zevara has a [Dangersense] that rings in the back of her head before danger. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L166)
- Zevara breathes fire at adventurers until the world darkens and she gasps for air, as it always does after she uses her birthright; a mage's [Barrier of Air] disperses the flames. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L180-183)
- Zevara has a great set of lungs, perhaps the Skill [Loud Voice], and is loud when she shouts. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L424)
- Zevara is a Drake whom Selys privately thinks of as a throwback with old blood, a dragon's child, and a hero for holding back the undead with fire. (Book 1, Ch1.45 L101-103)
## Role as Watch Captain
- {belief} At the start of the story Zevara tells Erin that Klbkch, the fourth-strongest Guardsman in the city, died because he wasted his emergency healing potion on her, and that the Watch will stop patrolling her area. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L85,L94)
- Zevara tells Erin that Klbkch was one of her best Senior Guardsmen and that without him there is no one to control the Antinium. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L88)
- Zevara tells Erin that the Guardsmen should never have interfered in the affairs of outsiders, and orders her out of her city. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L94,L102)
- {event} Zevara leads the Watch shoulder to shoulder against the undead, firing arrows into the crowd and cutting down a zombie herself. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L64)
- Zevara tells Tkrn that the attack cannot be the Necromancer's, since he would have brought giants, wights, ghosts and other horrors. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L93-99)
- The Watch has over four hundred guardsmen in a city that holds over ten thousand civilians, and was never meant to protect the city from siege. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L21)
- Zevara demands that the humans responsible for unleashing the undead be held accountable, her voice raw and coughing. (Book 1, Ch1.45 L100-101)
- {belief} Someone refers to Erin as the one who got Klbkch killed. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L225)
- Zevara has served as Captain for six years and notices that Klbkch looks different after nearly ten years, with two arms and a slimmer body. (Book 3, Ch3.21L L213,L222)
