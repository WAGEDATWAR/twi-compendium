# Insill
> Type: person | Also called: Insill the Drake [Rogue] | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.08T
> See also: Anith, Dasha, Pekona, Toren
## Overview
- Insill is a black-scaled Drake [Rogue] in leather armor with a dagger, thinner and shorter than other male Drakes. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L194,L216,L273)
- Insill belongs to the party Vuliel Drae, which includes Anith, Dasha, Pekona and the Gnoll Larr. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L194,L260,L267; Book 7, Ch5.05 L334)
- Insill hides in the dark like a shadow and checks for traps for the group. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L273)
## Events
- {event} Insill introduces his teammates to Toren and the group to her, with Dasha and Pekona as their [Warriors]. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L259-260)
- A teammate marvels that Toren found a trap in the dungeon that Insill could not. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L460)
- {event} Insill says Vuliel Drae owes Toren for last time, since the mace she gave them was worth a fortune. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L362)
- {event} Insill gives masked Toren a cheap steel longsword with a forked rune and a slight enchantment, because her old sword was very old. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L363-364)
- Insill teases Dasha for waxing her beard to try to meet Ylawes Byres at the Adventurer's Guild. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L337)
