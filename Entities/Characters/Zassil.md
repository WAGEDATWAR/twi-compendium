# Zassil
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Zassil | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.40
> See also: Bevussa, Issa, Kin, Wings of Pallass, Keldrass
## Overview
- Zassil is a male Oldblood Drake with wings, and an adventurer of the Wings of Pallass. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L271; Book 13, Ch6.56 L599; Book 14, Ch6.65 L132)
- Zassil is the youngest of Bevussa's team and sometimes shows his inexperience. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L271)
- Zassil is a [Battle Flier], a class unique to flying species, and is a competent [Flier]. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L132)
- Zassil fights with a spear in the air. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L293,L308)
- Zassil is publicly named the captain of the Wings of Pallass, while Bevussa is the real captain, an open secret among adventurers. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L100; Book 15, Ch7.03 L310; Book 19, Ch7.53 L699-701)
## With the Wings of Pallass
- Zassil lands with Issa on a Stone Starer and stabs it with spear and sword, killing it. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L217)
- Zassil stares at the Flamewardens' firepower in awe and says they must be Named Adventurers, as if forgetting he is Oldblood himself. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L269-271)
- Zassil sits up after a Drake from another team leaves and says something about a botched ambush, and Bevussa slaps him across the back of the head. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L98-101)
- Zassil is seized mid-dive by a Redfang warrior who leaps from a Carn Wolf and stabs at his wings until Bevussa hurls the warrior off. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L124-126)
- Zassil flexes his wings and grins at the other Drakes as the team discusses how fliers are poor dungeon-divers. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L544-546)
- Bevussa orders Zassil to fly back and find the other teams so they can rally at the dungeon entrance. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L745)
- Zassil jokes that wars are bad for the economy and looks hurt when Issa snaps at him. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L47-52)
- Zassil snaps at an adventurer captain who complains about keeping two miles away from the Bloodfields. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L599-600)
- Zassil suggests that the team rent the Heartflame Breastplate after its current wearers, and Bevussa asks if he wants them to become a land-bound team. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L139-143)
- Zassil takes the rear of the Wings of Pallass behind Issa as they enter the dungeon. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L162)
- Zassil joins Issa in cutting the Frost Wyvern's neck and wings during the attack on Pallass. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L269,L307)
- Zassil starts when Bevussa orders him and Issa to leave a downed Wyvern, giving orders to her 'Captain' in front of onlookers. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L308-310)
- Zassil leaps with Issa and Kin through the magic door to Pallass after Bevussa calls the Wings of Pallass to arms. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L1248-1249)
## Reputation
- Onlookers at the Wandering Inn identify Zassil as the team captain of Pallass's Gold-rank Wings of Pallass. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L268)
- Zassil is praised as Captain by a Pallassian Drake for taking on the Wyverns, and he answers that they did their duty as Gold-ranks. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L701-703)
- Zassil had served in Pallass's army, and his smile looks strained as the Drake fawns over Bevussa. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L702,L714)
