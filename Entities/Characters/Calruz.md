# Calruz
> Type: person | Also called: Calruz of the Beriad; Captain of the Horns of Hammerad; the Minotaur; Great Chieftain; Chieftain | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.02R
> See also: Healing potions, Free Queen, Ksmvr
## Overview
- Calruz is a Minotaur adventurer who introduces himself as "Calruz of the Beriad" and leader of the Horns of Hammerad, an adventuring party in the region. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L127)
- Calruz is the captain of the Horns of Hammerad, is impatient, and sits at the Adventurer's Guild table bare-chested. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L3,L5)
- Calruz is only around Level 20 and could have joined a Gold-rank team, but wanted to be in command. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L178)
## Appearance and manner
- Calruz is a huge Minotaur with a massive steel battleaxe strapped to his back, and wears plate armor. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L112,L272)
- Ryoka Griffin estimates him at about six foot seven, perhaps a few inches more not counting the horns, with a body of pure Minotaur muscle. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L272)
- He has goat-like legs with cloven hooves. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L307)
- Erin Solstice sees three hundred pounds of Minotaur, closer to seven feet than six. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L213)
- Calruz can eat beef, eats politely and faster than his companions, and dislikes being asked many questions. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L184,L200-204)
- Calruz asks Erin not to call him "Bull Guy", saying Minotaurs are not bulls or cattle. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L186-193)
