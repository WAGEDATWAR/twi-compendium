# Salvia
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Salvia | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.47
> See also: Gershal, Zel Shivertail, Magnolia Reinhart, Goblin Lord
## Overview
- Salvia is a [Captain] from the city of Nonelmar and an old acquaintance of Gershal of Vaunt. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L14-15,L24)
- Salvia greets Gershal with a clash of gauntleted fists, which is her city's unique greeting. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L13)
- Salvia is cheerful and teases Gershal about his tone, correcting him sharply when he calls her Lieutenant. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L14-20)
## Before the battle at Invrisil
- Salvia specifically requested the Invrisil posting so that she would be among the first to fight the Goblin Lord. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L15)
- Salvia mocks Gershal's soldiers as Level 5 [Farmers] who have barely trained with bows for a month. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L17)
- Salvia says she has orders not to join assaults on the Goblin Lord unless she can pull her cavalry out safely. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L37)
- Salvia expects that half the officers in camp will refuse to commit to an assault either. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L37)
- Salvia warns Gershal not to criticise Magnolia Reinhart where her people can hear. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L37)
- Salvia kicks Gershal under the table for insulting the Knights of the Petal, who punish insults to their [Lady] or order. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L43-45)
- Salvia says the pink-clad Knights of the Petal wear custom Dwarf-made armor enchanted by Wistram mages and carry magical weapons. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L47)
- Salvia would want those knights charging into the Goblin Lord's ranks. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L47)
- Salvia mutters to Gershal that someone has to destroy the Goblin Lord. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L72-73)
## The battle
- Captain Salvia leads [Riders] in hit-and-run charges against the Goblin Lord's army, wheeling away before the Goblins can catch them. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L157-158)
- Salvia pulls a rider whose horse was shot into her own saddle and carries him back to the Human lines. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L158-161)
- Salvia notes that the Goblins' Shield Spider riders are too slow to catch her cavalry. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L162)
- Salvia waits for orders from General Shivertail or a [Strategist] between charges. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L161)
- Salvia hopes that Gershal and his cheese-eating soldiers are unharmed while she charges a gap in the Goblin lines. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L164)
- The Human cavalry in the battle numbers some six thousand horse and strikes Goblin rear lines before pulling away. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L296)
- When the Goblin Lord's death magic and Goblin riders strike the cavalry, Salvia orders [Scatter Gallop] to break formation. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L302-303)
- Salvia's own unit scatters successfully, but the rest of the cavalry, untrained in the technique, are hit by arrows and spells. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L304)
