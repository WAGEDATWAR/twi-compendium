# Periss
> Type: person | Also called: Commander Periss | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.24
> See also: Ilvriss, Ryoka Griffin, Az'kerash
## Overview
- Periss is a Drake [Commander] who captures Ryoka Griffin and serves as second-in-command to Ilvriss. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L297)
- {belief} Ryoka thinks Periss seems to worship the ground Ilvriss stands on. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L297)
## Book 2: The march on the Necromancer's castle
- Periss leads elite Drake and Gnoll soldiers who take Ryoka toward the home of the Necromancer Az'kerash. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L225-228)
- Periss goes nearly white and orders a retreat when she learns the castle belongs to the Necromancer Az'kerash. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L226-227)
- Periss seizes Ryoka and demands how to leave the maze, and Ryoka says she knows only a stone that points toward the Necromancer. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L229-235)
- Periss tells her soldiers that it has been an honor, and invites them to say their last words. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L236-238)
- {reveal} Periss tells Ryoka that she has always loved her Lord and wishes she had had the courage to tell him. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L239-240)
- Periss tells Ryoka that Drakes do not run and orders her to go while the soldiers charge. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L285-286)
- {state} Periss and her soldiers, sixteen against a horde, die fighting the undead, and Ryoka hears Periss scream as she runs into the castle. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L286-288,L309)
## Book 6: Ilvriss's dream
- {event} In a dream after Erin spikes his drink, Ilvriss sees Periss, his second-in-command and secret lover, standing over a map of Izril. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L415-420)
- Periss in the dream has light blue scales near the spines on her head, which she brushes with a claw when she studies the map. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L423)
- Periss in the dream says that the Goblin Lord has been destroying villages while avoiding every city, even those he could take. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L437)
- Periss in the dream thinks the Goblin Lord is conserving his forces to move fast, and wonders whether he fears a Drake army from the rear. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L437-439)
- Periss in the dream says that the key is what General Shivertail knows and why he disappeared. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L444)
- Periss in the dream slaps Ilvriss twice and tells him she has been dead less than a month and he is no longer the leader she fell in love with. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L449-453)
- Periss never liked Ilvriss to say her name, and was strict about rank and about flirting before a battle ended. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L455-456)
- Periss in the dream tells Ilvriss that he must stand up because his people and she need him. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L476)
- Periss in the dream calls sending her after a lone Human girl a sound decision, though sending all the elites with her was a mistake. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L481)
- Periss in the dream says she will always be there in the map of Izril, and that Ilvriss should focus on all of Izril and on the inn. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L493-495)
