# Femithain
> Type: person | Also called: Magus-Crafter Femithain; Magus-Crafter; First Crafter | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.53K
> See also: Thelican, Nsiia, Flos Reimarch, Cognita, Remi Canada
## Overview
- Femithain is the Magus-Crafter of the Illivere League and leads its army of Golems and Artificers at the siege of Oliphant. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L5,L62)
- Femithain's spectacles magnify distant sights, letting him see into Oliphant. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L7)
- Femithain leads a nation comparable to Savere, and technically outranks General Thelican. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L62)
- Femithain is old enough to have lived through the King of Destruction's first campaign. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L817)
- Femithain's Illivere League is made of smaller crafter-states that elect their Magus-Crafter periodically. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L138)
## The Siege of Oliphant (Book 13)
- Femithain is tired of blood and death at Oliphant, Tiqr's capital, and he respects Nsiia's kingdom but thinks she erred by refusing to bow. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L4-5)
- Femithain says he has no objection to Nerrhavia sending Golems and elites to seize the Empress of Beasts, but is surprised Queen Yisame wishes her captured. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L61-63)
- Femithain explains the Dreamers of Reim to his officers, saying they grow faster and deadlier with their conviction. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L805)
- Femithain says the Dreamers might number six hundred thousand if the King of Destruction's Skill works on all of them. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L810)
- Femithain says the Sorcelled Blades are weaker than the Dreamers, but elites whose enchanted blades copy and combine each other's enchantments. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L815-818)
- {belief} Femithain believes Flos Reimarch is not invincible, because he loses when outflanked or worn down on multiple fronts, so Illivere must hold as an anvil. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L822)
- Femithain refuses to retreat, saying Illivere is committed and the Dreamers are not invincible. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L813-815)
- Femithain orders Armsmaster Dellic to hold and retreat Illivere's army when he falls, and nominates Artificer Antielle d'Elin as his replacement. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L890)
- Femithain advances with his metal Golems, while Rémi Canada watches from hiding. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L894-895)
- Femithain orders Nsiia arrested, her headband and spear taken, and guarded by Stone Golems, eight [Artificers] and sixty of Dellic's best. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L60-65)
- Femithain says Illivere claims no land from Tiqr, and he calls Nsiia a political prisoner he will defend before the Head Artificers. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L264)
- Femithain backs Nerrhavia's claim to Oliphant and most of Tiqr, and proposes a subservient occupied state in place of selling slaves. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L152-168)
- Femithain persuades Thelican to give up the Empress of Beasts by pointing out that Oliphant will make him wealthy. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L190-198)
## Illivere (Books 19)
- Femithain has stayed in power a long time, keeping everything running smoothly, and drinks stamina-potion on the terrace of his quarters in Delicrel. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L138-141)
- Femithain falls asleep on his desk after drafting Golem designs overnight, and financed the paper Chandrar International. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L141-142)
- Femithain orders a nude clay Nsiia Golem by Siddin of Veule smashed, since the Watch Captain's comments should treat Nsiia as former Empress. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L205-223)
- Femithain says Nsiia abdicated with Tiqr's surrender and that General Vasraf's Tiqr resistance is an unlawful rebellion. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L214)
- Femithain has Siddin escorted to his family's estates. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L223)
- Femithain knows of fewer than ten Cognizant-class beings in the world, and met Cognita of Wistram once when she visited Illivere. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L472-480)
- Femithain says no one in Illivere can craft Sentient-class Golems, and he intends to visit Wistram once the states vote another Magus-Crafter. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L477,L480)
