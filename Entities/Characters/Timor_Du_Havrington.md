# Timor Du Havrington
> Type: person | Also called: Timor du Havrington | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1
> See also: Charles de Trevalier, Pisces, Ceria Springwalker, Wistram
## Overview
- Timor du Havrington is a young noble student of Wistram who carries a sword and stands with Charles de Trevalier. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L125-127)
- Charles de Trevalier claims Timor is an experienced warrior with many levels in the [Warrior] class who would cut Pisces to bits. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L126)
- Timor himself claims to be a [Warrior] and a [Lord], but his voice trembles and he does not hold himself like a warrior. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L127,L130-131)
- Timor and Charles run a Wistram faction called the Libertarians. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L177)
- Timor approaches Pisces to invite him to join the Libertarians, offering quite a few incentives. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L177)
- Timor keeps pestering Pisces after the refusal, wanting him to abandon his friendship with Ceria and add his abilities to Charles. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L182)
- Pisces refuses the invitation outright. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L180)
