# Minerva
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.32G
> See also: Jackal, Garen Redfang
## Overview
- Minerva is a Human [Rogue] who infiltrates the Goblin mountain and blends into shadows. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L10-15)
- Minerva keeps a small white mouse named Keri that scurries up her sleeve and rides in her belt pouch. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L11-13)
- Minerva's leader is Jackal, and she reminds herself of her grandfather when she looks at an older half-Elf [Mage]. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L24,L28)
## Events
- {event} Minerva uses Keri to lure six Goblin sentries into an alcove and kills them with a black dagger. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L9-11)
- Jackal tells Minerva that using her pet mouse again is too slow, and she replies that it gets the job done. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L18-20)
- {event} Minerva follows Jackal into the shadows with four others. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L52)
- {state} Garen Redfang's [Blur Dodge] defeats Minerva's lunge and he cuts her down the chest. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L35-37)
- {state} Garen later slashes Minerva's belly open, and she drops clutching her sword. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L57)
