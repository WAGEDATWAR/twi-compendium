# Typhenous
> Type: person | Also called: the Plague Mage | First appears: Ch2.22
> See also: Griffon Hunt, Halrac, Revi, Ulrien, Moore, Pisces, Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings
## Overview
- Typhenous is an old [Mage] who carries a wand and a glowing staff. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L235,L241; Book 7, Ch5.05 L107)
- Typhenous is a Gold-rank adventurer and a member of Griffon Hunt, alongside Revi. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L109)
- Typhenous has a white beard and a glowing staff, and Griffon Hunt's [Mage] specialises in defence and wards. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L107,L109)
- Typhenous says he was educated for twelve years at the college of magic in the capital city of Pheron. (Book 4, Ch3.33 L266)
- Halrac and Ulrien contracted Typhenous and Revi for their expertise in Griffin hunting. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L494)
- Halrac says he and Ulrien wanted to hire Typhenous so badly because of his best spell, [Valmira's Comet Storm]. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L244,L257)
## Abilities and spells
- Typhenous tries to cast [Eltraz's Bin—] on Moore, and Moore kicks him before he finishes. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L241-243)
- Typhenous spins sticky enchanted webbing from his staff, which lasts only a few minutes. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L211)
- Typhenous casts [Platform of Air] in the corridor fight. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L369)
- Typhenous casts [Sticky Webs] and [Barrier of Air] against Regrika's attackers. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L137)
- {event} Typhenous calls down comet after comet with [Valmira's Comet Storm], which wipes out groups of Shield Spiders. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L244-254)
- Halrac says [Valmira's Comet Storm] is Typhenous's trump card, a Tier 5 spell that Griffon Hunt used against Griffin flights. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L244,L255-257)
- Typhenous casts [Black Burst] against the Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L394)
- Typhenous throws a [Wind Blast] from his finger that knocks Revi backwards. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L705-706)
- Typhenous uses [Mites of Slumber], a higher-Tier [Sleep] variation with living mites as the vector, which he says works even on Gold-rank adventurers. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L835)
- Typhenous fed Relc the mites in his food instead of getting them into his skin. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L842-844)
- Eltistiman calls Typhenous "the Plague Mage". (Book 9, Ch5.54 L845)
- Typhenous casts [Valmira's Comets] from Liscor's walls onto Reiss's army. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L632-633)
- Typhenous names a yellow sludge Numbtongue found as magicore and his voice shakes. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L383-390)
## The magic door
- Typhenous orders specialised lodestones, saying the door cannot be linked to many places without magical beacons. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L117,L122)
- Typhenous says the lodestones should arrive in Celum within the week and refuses to pay a Courier for them. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L126)
- Typhenous has attuned one stone already and says the hard part is delivering each stone to its destination. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L409-411)
- Typhenous says each extra destination is exponentially harder, so he advises three or four set anchors at most. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L414-415)
- Typhenous suggests hiring a City Runner to carry a stone to its destination. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L411-412)
- Typhenous and Pisces agreed that maintaining the portal effect was better than an involuntary translocation, and that testing showed a sudden mana failure did not harm travellers. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L206)
- Pisces works a long time with Moore and Typhenous so that Erin can switch the door by tapping a mana stone against it. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L234)

## Past and reputation
- Typhenous says his team poisoned the Griffins' nests and killed hundreds of Griffins, but the plague got out of control and killed almost every livestock animal and sickening three cities. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L497)
- Typhenous says Gold-rank status was almost lost over the Griffin plague. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L497)
- Griffon Hunt and the Halfseekers swap groups every few hours in the dungeon, with Typhenous planning to impart tactics to the other teams. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L493)
- In Invrisil, Typhenous grabs a gang leader and holds a dagger to his ear, and says he has run with more gangs than the boy has seen. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L950-953)
- Typhenous tells the gang that people call him the Plague Mage, and the gang's members repeat the name with awe. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L959-971)
- Typhenous tells Halrac the theatrics were an introduction and that the gang respects only a display. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L982-986)
- {reveal} Typhenous says he was in a gang once, and that most people who grow up without a proper job or class are. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L986,L1005-1006)
- Gang bosses treat Typhenous as a celebrity, and one says his father knew him from the days of running tricks. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L999-1003)
- Halrac says Typhenous is "a face" in the city's underworld. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1015)
- Typhenous tells Halrac that the door theft was going to happen with or without his interference, and that he mitigated the damage. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L993)
- {belief} Typhenous tells a Drake and a Human that he cannot lie and that his deed helped save a young woman from a theft. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L752)
