# Cetris Duiland
> Type: person | Also called: Cetris; Mayor Cetris; Mayor Duiland | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.07
> See also: Erin Solstice, Octavia, Lyonette
## Overview
- Cetris Duiland is the [Mayor] of Celum, a name so unremarkable that most people in his own city do not know it. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L807; Book 19, Ch7.54 L407-408)
- Cetris has a Skill as [Mayor], [Disadvantaged Renegotiation], that forces a negotiation to begin again with the odds against its target. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1009,L1014)
## Book 15: the door hearing
- {event} Cetris summons Erin to appear before him in Celum, with Montressa, Octavia and Lyonette present, after she opens the door to confront Numbtongue's disappearance. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L807-812)
- The hearing is attended by the Head of the Merchant's Guild, the Baker's Guild representative, the Runner's Guildmaster and the [Innkeepers] Agnes, Ulia and Timbor. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L810-811)
- Cetris tells Erin that the Innkeeper's Association of Celum has the most complaints against her and he does not want a trade war. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L939)
- {event} Cetris says Erin's door will be banned from Celum, citing animosity with three guilds, two of his best inns, and the Hobgoblin. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L952-954)
- Cetris says gates and monitoring could deal with the Antinium and the Hobgoblin, but the real problem is Erin's reputation for attracting trouble. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L958-960)
- Cetris says there is a betting pool, with rich individuals and monarchs among the bettors, on whether Celum will be attacked and how many casualties there will be. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L967)
- {event} Octavia offers to move to Liscor, which would end another of Celum's headaches. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L923-924)
- {event} Cetris calls [Disadvantaged Renegotiation] when the Players of Celum's departure is raised, and the room pauses. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1008-1012)
- Cetris is startled that Lyonette resists his Skill, since only a [Diplomat] or [Merchant] should be able to. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1015-1017)
- {event} Cetris allows the door to stay open under a special permit, so long as the Hobgoblin stays in Octavia's shop. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1024)
- Cetris proposes import and export taxes and a small usage fee for the door, with the Runner's Guild and Merchant's Guild inspecting. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1032)
## Book 17-19: Celum's ruin and recovery
- {belief} Cetris babbles at Erin that the bets were not supposed to be like this and that it is her fault for taking her door away. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L1088-1090)
- Cetris has seen Celum through windfalls from Octavia Cotton's inventions, the [Alchemist] gangs, a Hobgoblin, an Antinium and a freak avalanche. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L409)
- {state} Cetris thought the Bloodfeast Raiders' attack was the end of Celum, and people fled the city. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L408,L411)
- {event} Gold pours into Celum's coffers after Jasi Redigal mentions the city while raising funds on Wistram's television network. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L412-415)
- The donations come as gold sent by other nations, including Ailendamus, Nerrhavia's Fallen and the Forgotten Wing Company, and from nobles such as Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L415,L427-428)
- Cetris fears that someone will ask for the gold back, but he intends to spend it. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L417-418)
- {event} Cetris asks Erin for advice on the relief funding, and she tells him to build a memorial thanking the donors and not to let the money be wasted. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L442-452)
- Cetris says he came to ask about hiring Liscor's [Architect] and [Builders], even Antinium, who he hears work cheap. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L454)
