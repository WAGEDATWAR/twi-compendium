# Exara
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Exara | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.02D
> See also: Quallet, Xalandrass, Ken, Geneva
## Overview
- Exara is a female Centaur [Captain] who leads the suppression company Rot's Bane in the Baleros war valley. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L242; Book 5, Ch1.06D L365)
## Book 5: the valley war
- Exara taps a map and relays a rumour that a skilled [Doctor] treats wounded soldiers of any side in a neutral zone to the north. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L363-366)
- Exara reports that a cease-fire is in force around the zone where the rumoured [Doctor] works, which has caught the attention of both companies. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L366)
- Exara tells Calectus and the newcomers that the first attack killed Ulvial in the first attack on the neutral camp. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L245-246)
- {state} Exara takes command of the rest of Ulvial's company together with Quallet after his death. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L246)
- Exara reports that Xalandrass' camp was also attacked and that the Naga crushed at least six attacking soldiers with his tail. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L246)
- Exara has joined forces with Xalandrass' group and holds position on the neutral ground with Quallet, hoping to avoid becoming a target. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L242-246)
- {event} Ken names Exara of Rot's Bane, Quallet and Xalandrass as neutral parties while addressing the battlefield through an amplifier ring. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L364-367)
