# Ceria Springwalker
> Type: person | Also called: Ceria; Professor Springwalker; Springwalker; Captain Ceria | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.04R
> See also: Pisces, Yvlon Byres, Ksmvr, Calruz, Illphres, Erin Solstice, Ryoka Griffin, Gazi, Horns of Hammerad, Wistram Academy
## Overview
- Ceria Springwalker is a Half-elf mage who meets Ryoka Griffin and Erin Solstice as a member of the Horns of Hammerad. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L154-159)
- Ceria later becomes the unhappy Captain of the reformed Horns of Hammerad. (Book 3, Ch3.02H L95)
- Ceria's right hand was reduced to bone and blackened flesh by magical backlash in the crypt under Liscor, and she casts with it afterwards. (Book 2, Ch2.04 L490; Book 2, Ch2.13 L344)
- Ceria studied at Wistram Academy with Pisces and was expelled with him, then declared a graduate by the Golem Cognita. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L422-425,L462-463)
- Ceria is an ice mage whose class becomes [Cryomancer] and later [Arctic Cryomancer]. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L506; Book 14, Ch6.67 L504)
## Appearance, nature and background
- Ceria Springwalker is a Half-Elf mage with slightly pointed ears and pale yellow eyes; her skin and features seem more vivid and real than a human's. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L154-159)
- Ceria wears a hat indoors, which Ryoka Griffin first takes for bad manners until Ceria removes it and shows her ears. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L154-155)
- In Ryoka's first-person passage after being healed, Ceria's eyes appear to her as gold, like a glimpse of eternity; the same scene calls Ceria a part-immortal with near-supernatural beauty. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L167,L279)
- Ceria has light hazelnut hair and gives off an unearthly feeling that Ryoka tries not to stare at. (Book 1, Ch1.34 L29)
- Ceria is not a morning person; she has lived too long among Humans and other species to rise at dawn like the stereotype of a Half-elf, and she does not frolic. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L39)
- Ceria has lived for forty years. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L341)
- Ceria says she is a Half-elf, not an Elf, and tells Erin Solstice that no one has seen an Elf for thousands of years because they all died long ago. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L99-106)
- Ceria eats meat and says her people are not averse to it; she enjoys properly cooked pork. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L113-117)
- Ceria says everyone has some internal mana, supplied by the air and food; she says Half-elves generate a great deal by existing and so make good mages, while Humans have less, with exceptions. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L25-33)
- Ceria can drink a lot for her small frame, draining mugs in one go; she says mana potions and alcohol do not mix, so if she gets drunk and drinks one, everything comes back up. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L491-496)
- Ceria grew up for part of her life foraging off the land, loves climbing trees to steal eggs from nests, and has eaten earthworms, beetles, bark, birds raw and cooked, mushrooms and roots, having come close to starvation many times. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L13-15)
- Ceria says that being a Half-elf does not mean she knows where flowers are, and that most Half-elves do not even like nature much, taking on the culture they are raised in. (Book 2, Ch2.19 L210-213)
- Ceria recalls that when she was a child, elders said that long ago faeries were supposed to get along with Elves, though she calls it just a story. (Book 2, Ch2.19 L340)
- {state} After the battle with the Creler, Ceria's eyes are winter-pale with faint blue and grey and snowflakes fall around her; she tells Yvlon she leveled up. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L747-752)
## Magic, spells and abilities
- Ceria's wand is a slender piece of unworked pale white wood tinged with green, with pale blue-white wisps of light flickering at the tip, and she carries it on an armband along her arm inside her sleeve. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L53,L72)
- Ceria's new dark blue robes, embroidered with glowing golden sigils, are enchanted so that spilled liquid simply runs off the cloth. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L133-135)
- Ceria casts the [Light] spell with her wand much faster than Ryoka can, and the orb she makes shows colours deeper and subtler than Ryoka's. (Book 1, Ch1.34 L198-199)
- Ceria floats a heap of gold coins in the air with faint green light from her hand and sorts them into four stacks of ten. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L232-233)
- Ceria knows [Lesser Resistance to Ice], a basic mage spell of her school, and [Repair], though she says she is no good with movement and telekinetic spells. (Book 2, Ch2.11 L83-85)
- Ceria knows one or two flame spells but they are not her proficiency, which is ice; she cannot throw fire far or shoot fireballs without a wand. (Book 2, Ch2.11 L129-131)
- Ceria explains that a spell is like a container with a limit to the mana it can hold; a poor spell can be overcharged in theory, but too much mana injures the caster, as it did her hand. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L359-360)
- Ceria says wands amplify a mage's magic and hold stored energy that may or may not restore itself, and that artifacts are like wands with recharging mana and deeper reserves, which people will kill for. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L162-164)
- Ceria says the highest-tier magic she can cast is Tier 3; [Ice Spike] and [Detect Magic] are Tier 3, a Tier 4 spell would need a spellbook and weeks or months for her, and she is not at the level to learn [Grand Fireball] for years. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L175-183)
- Ceria says she cannot cast [Fireball] without a wand, and neither can Pisces; she specialises in cold, having spent winters in snow and ice and liked snowflakes and icicles, so spells like [Ice Spike] make sense to her. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L173,L219)
- Ceria says Tier 4 spellbooks were beyond her price range even when she was with the Horns. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L223)
- {reveal} Ceria discovers that her skeletal hand moves when she channels magic and that she can cast with it more easily than with her good hand; asked by Ryoka whether Half-Elf bones conduct magic more easily, she says it is a secret and asks them to tell no one. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L233-263)
- Ceria can cast [Fireball] with a wand but not strongly, and has no more ice spells in Warmage Thresk's spellbook; she says she taught herself [Frozen Armor] in the Raskghar den. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L128-133,L290)
- Ceria has the Skills [Weak Frost Resistance], [Ice Magic Affinity] and [Frozen Quickshape]. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L293)
- Ceria's [Glaciersheet Ice] and [Adept Iceform] let her shape her ice, such as spikes growing from an [Ice Wall] or a rough couch, and her wall holds against Tekshia's cane; casting still gives her migraines and Palt has told her no magic. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L510-530)
