# Jelov
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.21E
> See also: Laken, Prost, Riverfarm, Windrest
## Overview
- Jelov is an extremely old [Carver] from Windrest with contorted hands and back. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L115-121)
- Jelov carves wooden toys, hilts and handles, as his hands cannot hold an axe or lift heavy loads. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L121)
- Jelov begs Laken for work because he has a granddaughter to support. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L115; Book 5, Ch4.21E L123)
## Riverfarm
- Jelov carves the wooden markers that Laken uses to claim land around the village, finishing three with one being varnished. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L34-35)
- Laken asks Jelov whether the markers are done, and Jelov says they are finished but not polished. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L51-53)
- Laken has seven thick, heavy wooden markers bearing his symbol, and uses six of them to claim the land. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L60; Book 5, Ch4.23E L109)
- {state} Jelov becomes a famous man in the village, with eight apprentices and more work than he can handle, after being a recluse selling small trinkets. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L315)
- Laken sits on a wooden throne decorated with carved eyes in the meeting hall. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L315)
- Prost catches Jelov selling miniature totems as good-luck charms while fifteen replacement totems burned by Goblins are still owed after fifty days. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L82-86)
- Jelov says his mind wanders on large pieces and that small totems take only minutes. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L84)
- Prost confiscates Jelov's pouch of bronze and copper coins, since Riverfarm pools its money. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L85-86)
