# Isceil
> Type: person | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.57
> See also: Montressa, Beza, Palt, Ulinde, Zevara, Ceria Springwalker
## Overview
- Isceil is a Drake [Oldblood Magus] on the Wistram team led by Montressa. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L472)
- Isceil can exhale combined breath magic, using the team's linked magic to produce a three-element blast. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1032-1035)
## Book 13
- {belief} Isceil tells Liscor's people that Ceria and Pisces are criminals who impersonated real Wistram [Mages], and says the Wistram team may release others later. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L698-699)
- {belief} Montressa quotes Isceil in saying the team has the right to hunt fugitives from Wistram. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L729)
- {belief} Isceil sneers that Pisces got what he deserved because he was a [Necromancer], before Erin slams his face into a plate of curry. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L381-383)
- {belief} Isceil shouts to the Watch that Wistram's team has rights, as they are from Wistram. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L692-693)
## Book 14
- Isceil describes the dangers of out-of-control dragonbreath of Oldblood Drakes, such as frostbite, burned scales, heart attacks and acid that melts the stomach. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L216-221)
- Isceil says he has treated most of the bad cases because anyone who has lived long knows to seek him out, though at his level he cannot fix too much. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L218,L225)
- Isceil gives Montressa a sealed tonic for Captain Zevara, whose lungs cannot take the smoke of her fire breath. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L225-230)
- Isceil says Zevara hates his guts. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L228)
- {event} Isceil takes the focus of the linked Wistram mages and exhales Frostspark Galebreath, destroying a swarm of insects, then falls with a torn throat. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1028-1036)
- {event} Isceil and Beza follow Montressa to hunt Pisces, while Palt and Ulinde stay behind. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L471-480)
- Isceil says he agrees with Montressa and has a score to settle. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L475)
- {event} At Palt's call, Isceil fires lightning, fire and air at the Creler, and the Wistram mages add [Valmira's Comets], but the adult Creler is unscathed. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L942-947)
- {state} Isceil loses his right arm to a projectile spat by the adult Creler. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L953-955)
- {state} Isceil stays to fight, saying Drakes do not run, and he breathes flames at the Crelers. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L46-50)
- {state} Later, the adult Creler tears Isceil to pieces as he breathes flames once in defiance. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L114-115)
- Palt tells Erin that Isceil is dead. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L1172)
- The Wistram team buries Isceil, and Montressa's team grieves, since it is perhaps their first lost teammate. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L368,L706-707)
