# Bastion-General Quiteil
> Type: person | Also called: Quiteil | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Bastion-General, Bastion-General Zavarial, Cirille, Merish
## Overview
- Bastion-General Quiteil commands the 4th Wall of Rhir and is the person in charge of the entire 4th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L686,L702)
- Quiteil has a secretary's build, spectacles, ink-stained fingers and blue eyes, and Cirille found him looking like a [Scribe] rather than a war hero. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L735-736,L754)
- Quiteil has a luminescent white patch of skin that covers his wrist and moves up his arm. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L741-742)
## Command style
- Quiteil asks his officers many questions and sends them repeated priority messages that need authentication; he makes no small talk and does not look up while giving orders. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L702,L740,L751)
- Quiteil told Cirille that he has no time to explain himself to every officer who questions his command. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L758)
- Cirille's contacts said he runs a tight ship, requires subordinates to know how many arrows they hold, and loves reorganizing units. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L785,L1130)
- Quiteil assigned two thousand one hundred and three Gnolls under Chief Warrior Merish to Cirille's command, and kept the arrangement in force when objections were raised. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L749,L752; Book 18, Ch7.35C L362)
- Quiteil defended the use of Rhirian [Necromancers], saying necromancy has never been outlawed in Rhir and that he would gladly take ten thousand more like Mage-Captain Hetarria. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1173,L1178)
- {belief} A [Huntress] said he came closest to panicking when someone shouted "Deathless" for fun, and that he had the Lizardperson arrested and shipped back to Baleros. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1302)
- During the serpent-titan attack, Quiteil opened a Tier 7 scroll box from the capital with the [Mage-General] and teleported the scroll to Zavarial. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L180-188)
## Skills and class
- Quiteil is a [Logistics General], in fact a [Peerless Logistics General], promoted by virtue of his calculating nature rather than as a combat expert. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L19-20)
- Quiteil is called Bastion-General of 4th Wall for the same reason, and the narration says he would never lead a battle even from the rear. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L19-20)
- Quiteil places his bedroom next to his office, which is a secretary's office with more space for maps, communication devices and documents. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L450)
- Quiteil picks at the corruption on his right arm as he keys speaking stones. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L452)
- Quiteil wakes many times a week on instinct and trusts his gut. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L448-449)
- {event} Quiteil uses [Eighteen Minutes of the Secretary] to freeze the world for eighteen minutes while enchantments are dead, and spends the time writing memos to his commanders. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1073-1079)
- {event} Quiteil uses [Unstoppable Command] to authorise the Tier 7 spell [The Thousand Gateways of Orlaey] from 4th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1094-1096)
- Quiteil calculates Silvenia's coordinates exactly and notes that it has always bugged him that the wall is 80.3 feet high. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1089-1090)
## Serpent-titan attack
- {event} Quiteil tells the Blighted King that Deathslayer Arrows are ready and portal-spells are prepared to cover the retreat. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L21)
- {event} Quiteil counts 89 spells cast to bring down one serpent-titan, estimating Tier 3-5, by watching the projection on the battle-map. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L30-31)
- {event} Quiteil mutes the Blighted King's speaking stone while he works, and asks how many ballistae are mounted on 1st Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L34-44)
- Quiteil feels a tremor and drinks cold tea after the Arrow of Winter strikes. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L209-211)
## Synergies and Cirille
- Quiteil tells Cirille that synergies are known to Rhir's forces and that other nations lack experience of them. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L640-643)
- {event} Quiteil congratulates Cirille and says he looks forward to her second tour, which a [Scrier] explains is an offer to stay another four years. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L646-652)
## Deathless attack on 5th Wall
- {event} Quiteil wakes on instinct, checks the codes of each wall, and finds Zavarial's code good but remains uneasy. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L448-462)
- {event} Quiteil speaks to a voice mimicking Cirille on the speaking stone, asks about her arrow stockpile, and realises something is wrong. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L470-498)
- Quiteil asked Cirille about arrows the other day, which makes him suspect the Cirille on the stone is not her. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L489)
- Quiteil confirms through the Blighted King that the enemy is Silvenia, the Traitor, and tells her Lord Hayvon is liberating 5th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1151-1155)
- Quiteil tells Silvenia he funded the book she has read, as did Lord Hayvon, and warns her not to trust everything in books. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1160-1163)
