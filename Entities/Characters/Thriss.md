# Thriss
> Type: person | Also called: Sergeant Thriss | First appears: Book 3, Ch1.00D
> See also: Geneva, Okasha
## Overview
- Thriss is a [Sergeant] in the 4th Battalion of the Raverian Fighters' Company, who recruits soldiers from a crowd in a Baleros city. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L34-36)
- Thriss has an amazingly loud voice, which Geneva wonders may be a Skill. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L37)
- Thriss commands 6th Squad, in which Geneva marches. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L142)
- Thriss carries a huge mace into battle. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L430)
## Recruiting Geneva
- Thriss asked if Geneva was a [Warrior] or [Mage] and then hired her when she said she was a [Doctor]. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L40-45)
- Thriss said the company has no magical items or [Appraise] user able to verify a class, so lying about one would be found out. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L49)
- {belief} Geneva suspected Thriss had the healing potion ready for the dislocation demonstration and expected someone to be hurt. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L106)
- After Geneva removed an arrowhead from Clara, Thriss promised to get her the equipment she needed and spoke of respect for her. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L278-293)
- Thriss gave Geneva the iron key to the healing-potion chest, agreed on by the officers, and warned of hell if any potions went missing. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L339-343)
## The first campaign
- Thriss held a full healing potion in a water flask at his side, but the battalion's regular soldiers would not receive potions. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L27)
- Thriss told Geneva the closest force, the Burning March 6th Battalion, refused to give them potions. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L28)
- Thriss said he believes in souls, even if there are no gods to judge them. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L45)
- Thriss wanted to take a wounded Centaur from Geneva for interrogation and threatened to have her executed, but looked away first when she held to her oath. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L346-369)
- {event} On the last day of the war, Thriss killed a Centaur who had spared Geneva, struck her down with his mace and shouted "Traitor!" (Book 3, Ch1.01D L413-419)
- Thriss's blows broke Geneva's spine. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L417-419)
- {state} Later, Thriss dies when Okasha stabs a blade through his chest as he raises his mace over Geneva. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L420)
