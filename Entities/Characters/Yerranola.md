# Yerranola
> Type: person | Also called: Yerra | First appears: Ch6.23D
> See also: Wil, Cameral, Venaz, Merrik, Feshi, Peki, Tulm, Seagrass
## Overview
- Yerranola is a Selphid student of Niers Astoragon who inhabits a body, first a fresh Human one and later a female Dullahan body with the head sewn on. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Foliana L149-151; Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L187)
- Yerranola says she can feel things in her body and disconnects her nerves if something seems funky. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Foliana L149-151)
## Daquin
- {event} Yerranola drives a reinforced wagon at the plaza as a battering ram, laughing atop it, while Dullahans dive aside. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L781-788)
- Tulm has the Midnight Shields stop the wagon, which splinters and sends Yerranola flying. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L809-817)
- {event} Cameral, in Dullahan armour as a decoy, throws his head toward Niers' circle, and Tulm leaps eight feet to catch it, so Niers rules "Out!" (Book 11, Ch6.23D L837-849)
- {reveal} Yerranola and Cameral say the plan was agreed beforehand and Cameral's wounds were real but planned. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L854-856)
## The expedition
- Yerranola helps hold Merrik back from diving into the sea after Peki. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L185-187)
- {state} Seagrass beheads Yerranola, but her inner body tackles him over the railing, and she high-fives Wil with her headless body. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1284-1290)
- {state} Yerranola finds a new body later. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1341)
- {event} A black arrow strikes Yerranola in the chest and she says she is only scratched, then goes silent on deck. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1408-1420)
- {state} Later, Yerranola's body is gone from poisoning and she is kept in a jar of fluid in agony from blowfish poison. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1342-1349)
- Wil, Venaz, Feshi and Merrik resolve to take her to Oteslia for an antidote, though a bounty is on their heads. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1347-1355)
