# Anand
> Type: person | Also called: Individual Anand; Strategist Anand | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.21L
> See also: Belgrade, Pawn, Klbkch, Bird, Erin Solstice, Relc, General Sserys, Galuc, Antinium
## Overview
- Anand is an Antinium Worker and Individual who levels as a [Tactician], and later as a [Strategist]. (Book 3, Ch3.21L L33; Book 14, Ch6.62L L57)
- Anand, Belgrade, Bird, Garry and Pawn are the five Antinium who protected Erin from the undead. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L231)
- Anand is one of Erin's best chess pupils among the Antinium. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L548)
## Tactician years (Books 3 to 9)
- {state} Asked their levels by Pawn, Anand answers fifteen and Belgrade sixteen; both are [Tacticians] playing lightning chess. (Book 3, Ch3.21L L33,L63-66)
- Belgrade plays defensively and Anand aggressively, pushing his queen on the left. (Book 3, Ch3.21L L39-40)
- Anand and Belgrade coordinate the Hive's defenses in place of Klbkch, with Belgrade favouring static defences. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L35-39)
- In the latest skirmish Anand was wounded, leaving him unable to lead, and Belgrade could not command alone. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L138-141)
- Anand and Belgrade took over managing the Hive's defenses from the Queen, who no longer needed to command. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L67-68)
- Anand commands the day's fighting, using the Skills [Decisive Blows] and [Evasive Tactics]. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L31-33)
- {belief} Anand says the Hive is sadly uniform, which makes it difficult to counter individual threats as other Hives do. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L53)
- {belief} Belgrade doubts why [Strategists] level from chess and suspects an error in how the world levels; Anand frowns. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L58-62)
- Anand and Belgrade share a strategy room with maps of the Hive, a chess board and one of Erin's Go boards. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L233-234)
- Anand reports that Face-Eater Moths have declined in ferocity after the slaying of their eighth mother-type leader. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L447)
- Anand was among those consulted on whether Bird could meet Bevussa. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L56)
- Anand told Pawn that the Painted Soldiers would not hold Liscor, and that Pawn should prepare his unit for combat. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L401-404)
## Strategist (Books 11 to 16)
- Anand told Klbkch that Pawn eats and tends the Painted Soldiers but is dispirited since the siege. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L35-38)
- Anand directed Workers in a Hive war room and allowed a Worker to take the name Bishop after the first Bishop died. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L404-406,L438)
- Pawn was angry about the name, but Anand argued the name belongs to both of them. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L434-444)
- {state} Later, Anand is a [Strategist] who tells Pawn his leveling has stopped this month, which distresses him. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L57,L75)
- Anand spoke to Erin and received advice about Klbkch, which he declines to share with Pawn. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L101-104)
- Anand asked Klbkch whether he could call him "Father" and hug him, and Klbkch answered no. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L335-342)
- Anand now wears a chain vest of altered chainmail that glows faintly purple, and carries a hatchet and a wand. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L535-536)
- Anand says Klbkch is still furious at Bird and has not allowed more Antinium than necessary aboveground. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L545-549)
- {belief} Anand believes he is substandard and that he will be discarded if he fails Klbkch. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L563)
- {belief} Anand says Klbkch is the best of the Antinium. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L579)
- Anand tells Relc he wanted to call Klbkch father, and Relc hugs him and goes to speak to Klbkch "dad to dad". (Book 16, Ch7.16L L883-923)
## Free Antinium arc (Books 18 to 19)
- {state} Later, Anand is a Level 27 [Strike Strategist]. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L810)
- Anand befriends Sword Prognugators Klberein, Krrechzzeim, Kiskel and others by asking their names. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L585-589)
- Anand learned from the Prognugators that the Free Antinium were made in the image of Galuc the Builder. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L950)
- Galuc died before coming to Izril, and the Shaper Queen Bessachidia saved his form. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L950)
- Anand built a stone memorial where General Sserys of Liscor fell, with a plaque reading that Sserys stopped the Black Tide. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1353-1385)
- Liscor's Drakes watched the tribute, and the plaque ends "Drakes do not run." (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1381-1385)
- Anand's memorial for Sserys becomes talk of Liscor, and Maviola El was present. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L197-201)
- Anand tapped an aquifer, dug a makeshift sea and floated a small ship while tied to ropes held by Painted Antinium. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L887-890)
