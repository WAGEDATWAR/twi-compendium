# Grand Queen
> Type: person | Also called: Antinium Grand Queen; Grand Queen of the Antinium | First appears: Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2
> See also: Klbkch, Xrn, Free Queen, Twisted Queen, Antinium
## Overview
- The Antinium Grand Queen is the ruler of the Antinium Hives, a position that was entirely new to Drake and Human leaders when she first acted during the Antinium Wars. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L115)
- Klbkch addresses her as "my Queen", and she addresses him as Klbkchhezeim. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L499; Book 18, Ch7.39A L331)
- She is a massive, bloated Queen who rests on a huge dais in the centre of a stone cavern with many tunnels. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L498)
- She says she is Grand Queen of the Antinium of Izril, and that her role has been unlike any earlier Grand Queen's. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L484)
## The Antinium Wars
- {event} During the First Antinium War the Grand Queen sent white-flag Worker messengers carrying identical letters to the armies, requesting an immediate peace treaty with the rest of the continent. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L115)
- {event} The Drake and Human leaders unanimously refused her peace terms, and Sserys' army was given authority to destroy the Antinium in any way it saw fit. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L116)
- {belief} The Drake and Human armies believed the main Hive held most of the Queens and the Grand Queen. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L117)
- {event} According to Xrn, the Grand Queen saw the Goblins as a threat and gave three separate orders to her Prognugators. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L55)
- {event} Xrn says Klbkch, Wrymvr and Xrn herself were each ordered to break the Goblin King's might at any cost. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L55)
## Contact with the Free Antinium
- {event} When Klbkch tells her through a black-glass mirror that Zel Shivertail is dead, the Grand Queen goes still and then replies "Good". (Book 6, Ch4.49 L500-502)
- She says the Goblins are moving again, the Humans stir for war, the Drakes have lost their shield, and the Antinium will rise. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L504)
- Klbkch answers her with a single word, "Perhaps", while gripping the hilts of his swords. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L505-506)
- {event} She communicates with the Free Queen through a mindless miniature Drone replica of a Queen that carries a hand mirror linked to a twin mirror elsewhere. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L197-199,L204)
- She says the vessel was sent for more secure communication and calls the network a series of magical proxies of her own design. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L216,L222)
- {event} The Grand Queen judges that the Free Antinium have made adequate progress and tells them to rejoin the larger Hive through the relay. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L216)
- She confirms that Prognugator Xrn has returned to the Hive. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L218-219)
- {event} She orders the Free Antinium to keep probing the dungeon and to commit whatever resources are needed to acquire artifacts. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L233)
- She is irritated that Klbkch's form was altered without permission, and Klbkch answers that it was a necessary side effect of the Rite of Anastases. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L237-239)
- {event} Through a vessel and a magic mirror, she questions Klbkch and the Free Queen about an injured Worker and about Drones being present in Liscor's Hive. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L153-158)
- She says her Hive forbade the Liscor Hive to experiment with new forms or make Antinium besides new Workers and Soldiers. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L158)
- {belief} Klbkch tells her the Drones were deemed essential for enacting the Rite of Anastases if he fell, which is a lie she does not detect. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L159-160)
- She calls Klbkch and the Free Queen to speak with the other five Hives and tells them to abandon the injured Worker. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L161)
- {event} Her chamber holds an azure Antinium with a staff by her side and Prognugators carrying silvery blades that replicate Klbkch's weapons. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L153)
- {event} She angrily accuses the Free Queen of disobeying orders, saying no Prognugators were to be made. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L17)
- She has nearly two dozen Prognugators of her own, whom she styles the Custodium. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L27)
- The Free Queen thinks each Custodium Prognugator wears copies of Klbkch's long swords made of enhanced chitin, and regards them as a wasteful creation. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L28)
## Klbkch's visit to the Grand Hive
- {event} The Grand Queen receives Klbkch in a grand antechamber with mirrors showing the other five Queens, including the Free Queen. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L321-322)
- She sits on a high dais that serves as a throne, from which she commands the Queens and consults her Prognugators. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L322)
- {event} She asks Klbkch to act as Xrn's replacement, and the Silent, Armored and Flying Queens each petition for his guidance. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L324-328)
- The Twisted Queen does not speak during the audience. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L329)
- {event} Klbkch respectfully declines her invitation and says he will tour every Hive willing to host him. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L331)
- {reveal} In private, the Grand Queen tells Klbkch that the other Hives are fractured and the Queens do not obey her. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L483-484)
- {belief} She says each Queen pursues her own goals, some more loyal than others, and fears the Antinium are splintering like the primordial Antinium. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L489)
- She says she sanctions the works of the Armored Queen. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L489)
- {belief} She says she does not lead the Antinium but keeps each Hive from warring with the others, and that conflicts over resources have occurred more than once. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L508)
- {belief} She says the Twisted Queen sends no envoys and wages war against the Walled Cities against her will. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L494)
- She tells Klbkch the Walled Cities counter-raid Antinium lands with earthquakes, tunnel collapses and floods, increasing since the Pallass incident. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L497)
- Klbkch's mind confirms she is telling the truth, because the True Antinium cannot lie to each other. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L488,L496)
## Plans
- {event} The Grand Queen decides that creating new Queens is a flawed goal and says the Antinium should return to Rhir, hoping Queens remain there with the knowledge to create more. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L516)
- She says she does not forget the vow to return to Rhir with an army a thousand times larger than what was left. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L523)
- She argues the Antinium lack ships that can be sailed properly and that returning past the Blighted Kingdom and the Demons would be too risky. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L528-534)
- She says she would not risk Klbkch on a sea voyage because he could not be restored by the Rite of Anastases if he died at sea. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L536)
- {event} Instead she proposes to destroy the Walled Cities and conquer Izril, then send a thousand ships so one reaches Rhir. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L540)
- She shows Klbkch silos built underground, full of food and kept free of pests by magic. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L609-613)
- She says Xrn's magic created more storage areas over nearly a decade, to sustain an army twenty-three times larger than the first war's peak for three months. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L620-622)
