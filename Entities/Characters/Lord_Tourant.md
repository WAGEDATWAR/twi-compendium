# Lord Tourant
> Type: person | Also called: Tourant | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.21E
> See also: Oswalt, Bethal Walchaís, Rael, Laken Godart
## Overview
- Lord Tourant is a fiery nobleman and the father of Oswalt. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L25; Book 7, Ch5.11E L508-510)
- Tourant punishes Rael, Oswalt and the others when they cause trouble on his lands. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L25)
## Events
- Tourant draws his sword on a fae woman after Oswalt is injured, saying he would cut her down if she were not a woman. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L508-511)
- The fae woman grips Tourant's steel blade with her bare hands and says to duel the fae he must bring cold iron and star metal. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L512-513)
- Bethal Walchaís steps between Tourant and the fae, saying Emperor Godart had warned him of the perils of following them. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L522-523)
- Tourant claims his son was bewitched, and Bethal replies it was passion and that Oswalt lost only part of an ear. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L525-527)
- Bethal orders Tourant to sit down in Emperor Godart's presence. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L530)
- {belief} Lady Rie says Magnolia Reinhart might work through fools like Lord Tourant, whose touch is tactless, to pressure Laken's economy. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L300-303)
- {belief} Rie says Tourant is an old friend of the [Mayor] of Elkhan, and that the boycott by hostile cities could refuse trade caravans carrying Laken's goods. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L303-306)
- Tourant shouts that Goblins are not the same as Humans and is told to be silent. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L331-332)
