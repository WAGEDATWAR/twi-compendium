# Jelaqua Ivirith
> Type: person | Also called: Jelaqua; Miss Ivirith; leader of the Halfseekers; Captain | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.22
> See also: Moore, Seborn, Halfseekers, Garen Redfang, Maughin, Ulinde, Erin Solstice, Halrac, Selphids
## Overview
- Jelaqua Ivirith is a Selphid, a being who wears dead bodies, and the leader of the Gold-rank adventuring team the Halfseekers. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L222-224,L390-392)
- Jelaqua's skin is deathly white in her first body, and she wears black chainmail and carries a three-headed flail. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L222-224)
- Jelaqua's eyes are perfectly black in that body. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L222)
- Jelaqua's cheeks turn slightly orange when she blushes or is embarrassed, because something inside the dead skin heats up. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L93; Book 5, Ch4.19 L266-267)
- Jelaqua's real self sits inside the body, and her blush shows as a faint lattice of orange beneath pale scales. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L113)
- Jelaqua says she is fifty-one years old and has adventured for about thirty-three years, six more than Typhenous. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L576-577)
- Typhenous recalls hearing that Jelaqua came from Baleros as a Silver-rank adventurer, and that the Halfseekers were once one of the best Gold-rank teams, with seven members. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L268)
## Class and fighting style
- Jelaqua fights with a two-handed flail, and uses it to tear through groups of foes. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L444; Book 5, Ch4.19 L386)
- Jelaqua says she is a Level 33 [Iron Tempest], a class derived from [Flailmaster], with Skills to fight a pack of foes at once. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L448-450)
- Jelaqua Rampages with her arms and body turning into a blur, and shouts for Seborn to get back. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L25-26)
- Jelaqua wears Heartflame Armor, a golden-red breastplate that wreathes her body in fire. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L198; Book 8, Ch5.44 L632)
- The Heartflame Armor deflects Shield Spider bites with its magical flames. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L198)
- {state} Later, Jelaqua says she is a [Steel Tempest] after upgrading her class from [Iron Tempest]. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L238-239)
- Jelaqua tells Moore and Seborn that she went first into the corridor because of their rule to always send a Selphid first. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L249)
