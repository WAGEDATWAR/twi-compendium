# Drevish
> Type: person | Also called: the Architect | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00K
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Trey, Teres, Mars, Takhatres, Orthenon, Gazi, Reim
## Overview
- Drevish is known as the Architect and is one of the King of Destruction's Seven. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L8)
- Drevish is an [Architect] who sketches landscapes with fortresses in place of grass and stone, using charcoal and a kneaded eraser. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L8,L24)
- Drevish will not take part in battle, and Flos suspects he would sleep through it or sketch plans for a new fortress. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L70)
- Drevish scowls at his King in a way Orthenon and Takhatres would never dare, and Flos is used to it. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L69)

## Skills and works
- Flos says Drevish was one of the three of his Seven who knew magic. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L309)
- Drevish could enchant a gate so the mightiest battering ram could not break it. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L309)
- Drevish could build a wall overnight with his teams of [Builders], so an enemy army faced a fortified defense. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L309)
- Drevish did not like war, but Flos says he was essential and asked too much of him. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L309)
- Drevish worked on Reim and on Flos's palace there, but made only one major improvement. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L404)
- Drevish disliked remodeling existing architecture and was more interested in Drake construction practices than the Chandrarian style. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L404)
- Drevish found the sixteen towers of Reim's wall aesthetically disgusting, and the wall resembles a crown. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L406-411)
- Drevish told Flos that Reim was an eyesore that ought to be wiped from the face of the earth. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L401,L411)
- Drevish liked the height of Reim's towers and believed in self-sufficient architecture, buildings capable of protecting themselves. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L411)
- Drevish worked hard on Reim despite disliking it, but six of its towers have worn away over the decades. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L416)
- {belief} The Quarass says Fetohep commissioned Drevish the Architect to improve his palace last, as each ruler of Khelt does. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L870)

## Death
- {event} A box from the Emperor of Sands arrives by Courier and contains Drevish's head in a block of ice, and Gazi names him. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L348,L361-366)
- {state} Drevish had died to a swift chop to the neck, and Flos mourns him as his dear friend and builder of marvels. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L368-371)
- Mars grips her sword in tears and rage, Takhatres bows his head in prayer and Orthenon's clenched hand bleeds at the sight. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L371)
- Flos kept the head in ice by a simple preservation spell, as a reminder of his fallen companion, his failure and a promise. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L405)
- After Teres and Trey tell him the truth, Flos agrees it is selfish to keep his friend waiting and promises to bury him privately that night. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L467-473)
- {state} Later, no head is on Flos's dresser, and Trey wonders if Flos has buried it yet. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L578)
