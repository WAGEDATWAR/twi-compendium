# Emily
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Ch1.00C
> See also: Richard, Tom, Keith, Chole, Cirille
## Overview
- Emily is one of the people from Earth summoned to Rhir, a [Hydromancer] who is popular in the group. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L20)
- Tom calls Emily the idol of the group if anyone can be called that. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L20)
## Level and class
- Emily is a Level 21 [Hydromancer]. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L20)
- Emily has the highest level in the group besides Richard, and Tom says she is something like the second leader. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L270)
- {state} Later, Tom calls Emily the group's highest-level [Mage], and by then both Richard and Emily are over Level 30. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L84,L287)
- {state} Later, Emily is Level 34, which Cirille considers insane for a [Mage] to level so fast. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L227)
- Cirille thinks Emily has eclipsed Richard and even Tom in level. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L227)
## Role and relationships
- {rel} Emily is a leader of the group alongside Richard, and Tom calls her beloved. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L75)
- {rel} Emily flirts with Richard at the table, and Tom thinks they are a couple in some sense but not officially. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L379)
- Tom does not think Emily and Richard have had sex yet, because the news would spread quickly in their tight-knit circle. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L379)
- Emily stares at Nereshal when he is introduced. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L287)
## Battle at the breach
- Emily clears the way for Richard's charge at a gap with [Tidal Wave], a wall of water that knocks back the first wave of enemy soldiers and monsters. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L146-149)
- Emily then casts [Summon Water Elemental], and a huge water figure lashes about at her command. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L150-151)
- Emily stays on the wall, staring, after the fight and says she cannot understand why they need the Earthers. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L223-226)
