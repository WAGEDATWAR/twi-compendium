# Bastion-General Zavarial
> Type: person | Also called: Zavarial; General Zavarial | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Bastion-General, Bastion-General Quiteil, Cirille, Rhir
## Overview
- Bastion-General Zavarial commands 5th Wall in Rhir, and Cirille's division was placed under him. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L749; Book 18, Ch7.35C L177,L737)
- Zavarial is a half-Elf and was a champion of [Archers]. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L167,L176)
- Zavarial reports to the Blighted King and his [Strategists] by speaking stone during battle. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L156-159)
## Role at 5th Wall
- Zavarial was reluctant to send out units that had not drilled together, and he held irregular divisions on patrol duty until he pronounced them fit. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L346,L582)
- When serpent-titan copies breached 5th Wall, Zavarial held the wall and asked for Lord Hayvon to hunt raiding parties. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L25,L156-163)
- Zavarial requested the Arrow of Winter Scroll, opened a Tier 7 Scroll on the wall and told the area to be cleared. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L177,L188-191)
- Zavarial authorised a few support units that use death magic, and a necromancer said he would need Zavarial's permission before taking on a new task. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L19,L117)
- Zavarial asked for volunteers to try a blood transfusion tactic against poison. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L799)

## Background and abilities
- Zavarial has scales running up his forearm to the elbow, a heritage of Lizardfolk blood along with his half-Elf ancestry. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L601)
- Zavarial rose from [Archer] to [Ranger], [Veteran Ranger] and [Blightwarden], and in the ranks from [Soldier] to [Captain], skipping [Lieutenant]. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L382)
- {state} Zavarial became a [Blightranger General], an expert in hit-and-away missions in Demon territory, and then Bastion-General. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L383)
- Zavarial's wide-reaching powers come from 5th Wall's enchantments rather than a [General] class like Quiteil's. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L384)
- Zavarial carries six rings given by the Blighted Kingdom's treasury and a bow made from the bones of a long-dead animal. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L393)
- Quiteil taught Zavarial to always make plans, and Zavarial carries a backup single-use speaking stone. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L433)
## Serpent-titan battle
- {event} Zavarial reports that enemy titans are creating breaches and that Demons are flooding behind 5th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L157-162)
- Zavarial notices the lead titan feeding as smaller snakes devour the dead to regain its mass. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L167-171)
- {event} Zavarial requests the Arrow of Winter scroll, and the Blighted King grants it. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L173-175)
- {event} Zavarial fires [Arrow of Winter: Glacius Pillar] after announcing he is opening a Tier 7 Scroll, and a pillar of ice forms hundreds of feet up and shakes the world when it fires. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L190,L203-206)
- The Tier 7 spell turns about a mile of the field to ice and kills the titan collective and the copies. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L213-218)
- Cirille tells Zavarial that she has consolidated her classes into a [Combined-Arms Commander]. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L600-602)
## Death at 5th Wall
- Zavarial gives the code Shelten-Khelt-[Brigand]-Dirt to Quiteil at midnight. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L456-457)
- {event} Zavarial raises his bow in the darkness and fires [Arrow of Greater Dispel] to break the spell, then dives for the stairs. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L394-397,L427)
- {event} Zavarial is lifted into the air, and reports a hundred thousand attackers at 5th Wall to Quiteil before his backup stone falls. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L428-433)
- {event} Zavarial fires a Deathslayer Arrow at a laughing half-Elf in the clouds, who makes it vanish. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L436-441)
- {state} Zavarial dies in an explosion that consumes him and the tower. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L443-445)
- Silvenia tells Quiteil that she killed his friend. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1091)
- Cirille later notes that Bastion-General Zavarial is dead and the gates at their location are broken. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L745)
