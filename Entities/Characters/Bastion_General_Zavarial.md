# Bastion-General Zavarial
> Type: person | Also called: Zavarial; General Zavarial | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Bastion-General, Bastion-General Quiteil, Cirille, Rhir
## Overview
- Bastion-General Zavarial commands 5th Wall in Rhir, and Cirille's division was placed under him. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L749; Book 18, Ch7.35C L177,L737)
- Zavarial is a half-Elf and was a champion of [Archers]. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L167,L176)
- Zavarial reports to the Blighted King and his [Strategists] by speaking stone during battle. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L156-159)
## Role at 5th Wall
- Zavarial was reluctant to send out units that had not drilled together, and he held irregular divisions on patrol duty until he pronounced them fit. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L346,L582)
- When serpent-titan copies breached 5th Wall, Zavarial held the wall and asked for Lord Hayvon to hunt raiding parties. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L25,L156-163)
- Zavarial requested the Arrow of Winter Scroll, opened a Tier 7 Scroll on the wall and told the area to be cleared. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L177,L188-191)
- Zavarial authorised a few support units that use death magic, and a necromancer said he would need Zavarial's permission before taking on a new task. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L19,L117)
- Zavarial asked for volunteers to try a blood transfusion tactic against poison. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L799)
