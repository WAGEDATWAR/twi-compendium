## Overview
- Magnolia Reinhart is a [Lady] who rules House Reinhart, one of the Five Families, and is called the Deadly Flower Blooming in the North. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L195-197; Book 6, Ch4.44M L5-6)
- Magnolia is a high-level [Lady] who strides gracefully, and is cheerful by design while sharper than she appears. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L305; Book 5, Ch4.06KM L243)
