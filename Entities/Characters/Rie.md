# Rie
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Rie; Rie Valerund; Lady Rie Valerund | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.35E
> See also: Laken, Durene, Geram Redfist, Nesor, Magnolia Reinhart, Wiskeria
## Overview
- Rie Valerund is a [Lady] of Invrisil, the last member of the Valerund family, who lives in a mansion near a small town. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L250,L267; Book 7, Ch5.09E L96)
- Rie is a calm, calculating [Lady], with dark purple hair that Durene suspects may be dyed. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L33; Book 12, Ch6.34E L741)
## Goblin attack and Riverfarm, Books 6-8
- Rie sends Laken goat milk and baby powder for the children he protects, with a verbal message since he is blind. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L242-251)
- Rie and her remaining guard count weapons in her mansion, with few bows but a surplus of arrows. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L267)
- Rie carries a poison she plans to take rather than suffer indignities, telling Geram everyone has plans for the end. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L308-309)
- {state} Rie kneels to Laken in the mud with Geram and Nesor, and the soldiers kneel after her. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L365-366)
- {belief} Rie says she no longer considers her home safe after the Goblin attack, and that she would be dead but for Laken. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L254)
- {state} Rie pledges herself as Laken's vassal after the Goblin attack, arriving in Riverfarm three days before the gathering. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L96-97)
- Rie places her fortune at the Unseen Empire's disposal, calling it a gift for saving her life. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L144-146)
- Rie offers to order supplies from Invrisil and other cities abroad. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L141)
- Rie touches Laken's arm and stands close, and Laken concludes she is flirting with him. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L301-310)
- {belief} Rie tells Laken that Lord Tilwa is testing him with his letter, and advises leaving it unanswered for a day or two. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L348-351)
- {belief} Rie calls Magnolia Reinhart dangerous, ruthless and cunning, and thinks the gathering is without a doubt a trap, possibly to kill him. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L391-395)
- Rie pushes for a banquet hall built from the best lumber and expensive furnishings and silverware from Invrisil. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L85,L91,L101-104)
- Rie agrees that older people should wait the tables rather than younger servants. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L210)
- Rie tells Durene she should stand at attendance rather than sit at the head table, and suggests she wear armor. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L228-237)
- Rie suggests a helmet with a visor for Durene, which Laken understands is meant to cover her face. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L310-312)
- Rie tells Laken that refugees are converging on Riverfarm, and that she has received two dozen [Messages] begging for his protection. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L310,L316)
- Rie is mortified that Nesor was drawn away by Lady Bevia, and is unable to say where Nesor went. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L378,L412)
## Riverfarm after the Goblins, Books 12 and 15
- Durene remembers hating Rie and fearing she would seduce Laken, but now finds her jealousy fades and smiles at Rie. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L438-441)
- Rie's captain of the guard is Geram, a bald former [Fistfighter] with scarred knuckles who escorts her as an assistant. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L704-705)
- Rie says Lancrel's people blame Laken for their destroyed city and that some council members want a voice in running Riverfarm. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L733)
- Rie says the hard core of dissenters is only a hundred or perhaps two hundred. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L745)
- {belief} Rie says the rest of Lancrel's people believe the Goblins were a ruse or manipulated by Laken. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L747)
- Rie wants Wiskeria to return as [General] and wants Durene armed. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L755-757)
- Rie sent to her estates for a truth stone for a trial, due by the end of the day. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L77)
- Rie has sold her dresses and jewelry to finance Riverfarm, and fears Magnolia Reinhart will escalate the trade war. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L261,L340)
- Rie's estates are far richer than Riverfarm and only a day's ride away. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L285)
- Rie says a guild can run out of a root cellar with Runner's Seals, something to record requests and money to pay Runners. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L254)
- Rie attends a meeting flanked by her bodyguard Geram and Nesor, and recalls Belavierr as the Temptress of Izril. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L9,L43)
- Rie tells Laken the armed Goblins are dangerous, but Laken says they outsmarted him by doing nothing. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L68-92)
- Rie lives in Riverfarm in a terribly tiny house, and Ieka picks her up for a meeting. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L901,L913)
## The Circle of Thorns
- {reveal} Rie is a member of the Circle of Thorns, of higher rank than Ieka, though Ieka is the richer [Lady]. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L20-21)
- {reveal} Rie is second only to the Petals of Death in the Circle and has authority to recommend actions to it. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L46)

- {reveal} Rie tells the Circle she believes Emperor Godart is an ally and says she is in his confidences while exposing none of its plans. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L97-99)
