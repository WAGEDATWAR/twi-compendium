# Culyss
> Type: person | First appears: Ch2.13
> See also: Erin Solstice, Selys Shivertail, Rags, Tkrn
## Overview
- Culyss is a suave Drake and a ladies' man who becomes the second suitor to visit Erin's inn. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L397)
## The tail incident
- Culyss gives Erin a necklace with a piece of metal hammered into the shape of a chess pawn. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L398)
- {event} Erin asks to touch his tail out of curiosity, and Culyss agrees readily and lets her stroke and poke it. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L401-409)
- Culyss makes odd sounds, twitches and curls his tail around her hand, and afterwards covers his groin with his tail. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L404-416)
- Culyss thanks Erin for letting him and says he would love to further their relationship. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L411-417)
- {reveal} Erin realises about two hours later what stroking a Drake's tail meant, when Selys practically kicks down her door. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L423)
- Selys warns Erin not to touch Hawk's tail, reminding her of Culyss. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L175)
- Culyss returns for a second date with a gift of soaps, and a small group of Goblins yank him off his feet and beat him with sticks while his soaps are trampled. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L473-482)
- Culyss never comes back to Erin's inn after the Goblins beat him. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L485)
- Selys says that Culyss liked Erin even before the tail incident. (Book 2, ChS01_Mating_Rituals L494)
