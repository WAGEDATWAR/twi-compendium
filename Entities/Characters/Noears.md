# Noears
> Type: person | Also called: the dimwitted Goblin without ears | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.28G
> See also: Rags, Redscar, Poisonbite, Pyrite, Tremborag, Greydath of Blades, Flooded Waters tribe
## Overview
- Noears is a Goblin without ears who acts dimwitted around other Goblins and is shoved forward to cast lightning at attacking Humans. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L229-230)
- Noears tells Rags he is a [Spellcaster] to the other Goblins, weak and stupid, but a [Mage] to her, stronger than he seems. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L283)
- Noears was in Tremborag's tribe and knows Ulvama's ways. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L279)
- Noears explains his missing ears with the adventurer bounty of two copper coins for Goblin ears. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L574)
- Noears cannot be excused by Dawil, who says he cannot excuse the ears. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L582)

## Advisor to Rags (Books 4-7)
- Noears warns Rags not to sit with Tremborag that night and advises her to be humble and small. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L288)
- Noears detoured while fleeing Tremborag's mountain to steal all the maps from the Goblin war room. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L173)
- Noears tells Rags that Human [Knights] and [Riders] run down Goblins with lances, that cities have armies and that riding patrols are common. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L181)
- Noears tells Rags of stronger local tribes: one led by a big Hob with many Hobs, one with a [Witch] who knows fire magic, and one of thieves. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L95)
- Noears keeps a charged field of air to hold off bugs around his group. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L254-255)
- Noears teaches fourteen adults and eight children lightning magic aboard a wagon, saying lightning is built up in the air and hands then shot. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L108-114)
- Noears explains that Goblins do not believe in small spells, so he teaches [Lightning Bolt] rather than [Electric Jolt]. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L110-112)

## Wars on the Floodplains (Books 8-9)
- Noears emerges unhurt from the darkness after the night ambush, with lightning crackling, followed by the wheezing Poisonbite. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L346)
- Noears's lightning bolts convince the Humans' camp to keep out of range of the walls. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L224)
- Noears says he has four potions and can cast many spells, and uses [Lightning Bolt] to crack an incoming boulder on the city wall. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L139,L147-148)
- Rags thinks Noears's [Lightning Bolt] can only crack or redirect a boulder and could protect the city for an hour at best. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L152)
- Noears tells Rags that a bag of holding cannot hold living beings, and later carries the tribe's discarded armor and weapons in it. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L56,L110)
- Noears's lightning nearly strikes Ulvama in the fight against Tremborag's tribe. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L197-198)
- Noears sits at the sit-about with Quietstab and grabs Redscar's shoulder to hold him back when Redscar half-rises. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L26)
- Noears holds the pike line against Eater of Spears, staggering him with a bolt of lightning and telling Rags to go. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L57-64)

## At the inn and death (Book 9)
- Noears tells Erin in English that he is Noears, and blinks up at her unusually shy. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L346,L351-352)
- Erin remarks that Noears's name is very accurate, noting the ragged flesh around both earholes. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L353-354)
- Noears stands on the shattered third floor of the inn where Bird's tower had been and calls down lightning bolts minutes apart. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L278)
- Erin feels that Noears is using all the mana the inn normally spends on the magic door, which is not there. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L280-281)
- {state} Poisonbite later recalls that Noears did not flee, was killed by the Humans after calling lightning from the skies, and let more Goblins escape. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L5-6)
