# Merish
> Type: person | Also called: Chief Warrior Merish | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Cirille, Quiteil, Delezza, Uxel, Gnoll, Rhir
## Overview
- Merish is a Plains Gnoll Chief Warrior with light reddish fur, standing over seven feet tall, brawny, with dyed blue and white fur in an intricate pattern. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L825,L830)
- Merish carries a bone axe, wears only light cloth clothing without armor, and says the Tribes send their own pact-warriors to Rhir. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L826,L830,L869)
- Merish is a [Shamanic Warrior] of the Plain's Eye tribe, and he describes it as a magic warrior suited to heavy fighting whose fur becomes armor and blades cut steel. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L864-868)
- Merish tells Cirille his class is his own business at first and answers only reluctantly when she demands it. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L860-864)
- Merish leads sixty warriors of heavy infantry himself and commands Gnoll archers, including two hundred archers. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L844,L854-855)
## Under Cirille's command (Book 18)
- Merish tells Cirille that the Gnolls obey being placed under her command because a [General] said so, but does not believe they will work together. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L826,L833)
- Merish shakes Cirille's hand after she promises not to take direct command of his forces. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L835,L874)
- Merish is the commanding officer of a unit of two thousand one hundred and three Gnolls from the tribes, which Quiteil orders Cirille to fold into her own command. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L752)
- {event} Merish proposes mixed commands, trading his Gnoll [Hunters] for more Drakes and commanding only the heaviest warriors, so others follow if they see Gnolls running. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L548)
- {event} Merish and Cirille try the mixed formations against Uxel's forces and win handily, and then go on to defeat six groups of Rhir's forces in skirmishes. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L554,L581)
- Merish's Gnolls join the archers and heavy infantry, with enchanted-weapon Gnolls best where they must stay in the fighting. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L578)
## Battle (Book 18)
- Merish fights at the breach with [Enchantment: Flashfire], which blows a Demon's face off. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L612-613)
- Merish uses [Enchantment: Leaden Weight] on his second axe to cave in a metal shield. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L614-615)
- Merish survives the battle, covered by Drake and Gnoll bodies, though arrows did not kill him. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1289)
