# Mihaela Godfrey
> Type: person | Also called: Guildmistress Godfrey; Mihaela | First appears: Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2
> See also: Valceif Godfrey, Ryoka Griffin, Salamani, Wrymvr, Courier
## Overview
- Mihaela Godfrey is a Human [Courier], and later the Guildmistress of First Landing's Runner's Guild. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L14; Book 16, Ch7.18M L536)
- She is regarded as one of the most famous Couriers to run across Izril. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L536)
- She is rarely active as a Courier in her later years. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L536)
- She is the mother of Valceif Godfrey. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L537)
- In her late fifties, she has white or silver hair, many lines and scars, and the build of a marathon runner. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L444)
- Her eyes are violet torn by yellow, and Ryoka thinks she could pass for a Dragon if the irises were mismatched. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L617)
## First Antinium War
- {event} Two days after the Antinium attacked the city of Geir, Mihaela broke through the Antinium lines from within. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L14)
- She delivered to General Sserys a message written by a young Drake [Lieutenant] stationed in the city. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L14)
- {fact} She says she got her lung damage on her last run during the First Antinium War, when Wrymvr the Deathless poisoned her. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L812)
- Wrymvr could not hold her on the Courier's Road, but he still got her. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L812)
## Guildmistress of First Landing
- {event} She tells Tritel, Salamani and Hawk to deliver a package together and doubles the delivery fee to send a message. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L528,L531-534)
- She knew Hawk was coming north and insisted he join the delivery. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L534)
- Tritel says she is in a foul mood and would pull off Hawk's ears and tail if he got within a hundred miles of First Landing. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L538)
## Meeting Ryoka Griffin
- {event} She comes north to Reizmelt specifically to meet Ryoka Griffin and tells Salamani to get lost. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L447)
- {belief} She says she heard Valceif met a City Runner and that this was how he lost his charm. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L524)
- She says Ryoka sent her a [Message] saying Valceif's death was Ryoka's fault. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L524)
- She presses Ryoka to say why Valceif lost the charm that would have kept him alive. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L530)
- {event} Mihaela punches Ryoka in the jaw, side and back of the head before Ryoka can raise her hands, then tells her to heal up because it is a test. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L560-568,L574-575)
- She hits Ryoka a third time in the ribs after the potion, and Ylawes hears something crack. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L578-581)
- She tells Salamani he is starting to annoy her, and warns Ylawes she knows his mother and will remove him. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L571,L599)
- Ylawes thinks that if Couriers had a Named-rank, Mihaela Godfrey would hold it. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L602)
- {event} She wins against the Silver Swords, Salamani and the crowd with a wand spell, saying "[Transfer Momentum]. [Lightning Bolt]." (Book 19, Ch7.44 L616-619)
- The stored lightning hangs almost without momentum, then grounds itself, and Ryoka's [Forcewall] explodes as Ylawes, Dawil, Falene and Salamani are thrown. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L621-623)
- She moves with a passive movement Skill and coughs a deep, wet cough. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L523)
- {belief} She rules that Ryoka lacks anti-[Appraisal] spells and fights only with bare hands, and would fail First Landing's Courier test even if someone else gave it. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L702-705)
- She says the Healer of Tenbault can restore flesh but cannot remove the poison in her chest. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L814)
- She calls herself the greatest Courier of Izril and says she will die of the lung damage. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L814)
