# Hesseif
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos
> See also: Nawal, Silmak, Bezha, Clan Tannousin
## Overview
- Hesseif is one of the [Caravan Guards] of Clan Tannousin, who pokes his head in to check on Nawal and then goes back to watching for thieves. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L358)
- Hesseif is chiefly tasked with the defence of the Tannousin caravan. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L7)
- Hesseif is a large man with a broad, shaved head, and a proper [Warrior]. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L110,L792)
- Hesseif is rather timid outside of battle, although he and Silmak are technically in charge of the caravan. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L86)
- Bezha is Hesseif's aunt. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L79)
- Nawal calls Hesseif the best fighter the clan sent. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L232)
## Before Reim
- Nawal is relieved to feel Hesseif's presence at her back when her clansmen appear to face the [Slaver] who tried to enforce her brother Allaif's sale contract. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L417)
- Hesseif shifts behind Nawal as she readies for a bloody fight with the [Slaver] and his thugs. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L419)
## At Reim
- Hesseif bows with Silmak, Bezha and Nawal to greet Orthenon and Gazi when the caravan reaches Reim. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L7-9)
- Hesseif has a private room in the palace, as do Silmak, Bezha and Nawal. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L76)
- Hesseif confirms that Trey is the same foreign boy whom Nawal met earlier. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L94-96)
- Hesseif promises to guard the Naq-Alrama metal day and night, letting no one not of the clan's blood enter and no sunlight in. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L109)
- Hesseif and Silmak inspect the swords and crucible steel in Master Daiton's forge, after Nawal's challenge. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L544)
- Hesseif and three other men carry a huge stone box into the forge. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L569)
- Hesseif appears behind Trey and a smith's apprentice during the forging, and shakes his head at a twice-quenched blade that is not perfect. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L792-805)
- Hesseif leads a group of men dragging the barrel into the circle where air is not permitted. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L917)
- Nawal hands Hesseif the finished sword and tells him to guard it until the King of Destruction touches it. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L970-971)
- Hesseif nearly tackles Trey when Trey catches Nawal as she faints. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L250)
## The ruse
- Hesseif stands behind Nawal, with Bezhavil and Silmak, while Nawal lies to Lord Venith and Lady Maresar about the Naq-Alrama steel's progress. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L216,L221)
- Hesseif agrees with Nawal that the clan can wait for Trey Atwood no longer and should tell the truth. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L231-232)
- Hesseif cannot meet Nawal's eyes, in shame, when she storms out after the clan refuses to tell Flos. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L240)
