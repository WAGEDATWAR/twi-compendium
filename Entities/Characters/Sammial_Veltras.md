# Sammial Veltras
> Type: person | Also called: Sammial; Sammy; Lord Sammial | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.24
> See also: Tyrion Veltras, Hethon, Jericha, Ullim, Circle of Thorns
## Overview
- Sammial Veltras is the ten-year-old younger son of Tyrion Veltras and already has an aura that influences those around him. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L477-478)
- Sammial is described as a Veltras born, fiery like his mother, with the Veltras rage and command, but he has a lot to learn about emotions. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L476-477)
- Sammial is very good at most athletic things, and his [Fencer] instructor says he is a natural with a sword. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L498)
- Sammial has had coughing fits since he was young, and his lungs were always weak, according to Tyrion. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L473-474)
## Tantrum (Book 17)
- {event} Sammial bites his tongue hard while riding with Jericha, who heals it, and he blames her, saying it was a rock. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L483-490)
- Sammial refuses to apologise to Jericha for striking her, and says he hates her. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L505-511)
- {event} Sammial spits at Jericha and shouts that she is just a servant, not his mother. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L514-515)
- {event} Tyrion spanks Sammial over his knee, and Sammial screams that he hates his father and wishes he had died too. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L522-525)
## Illness (Book 19)
- Sammial has a summer cold, fever and coughing the day after Tyrion rescues Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L461,L471)
- {state} Sammial falls into a coma struggling for breath, as his airways close within a day, and the [Healer] cannot explain it. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L114-116)
- {state} Sammial worsens overnight and by morning is barely responsive, and a second [Assassin] arrives later that day. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L894-895)
- {state} Hethon and Sammial remain gravely ill while Tyrion sits staring at the blood contract left by the [Assassin]. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L1336-1338,L1345-1346)
