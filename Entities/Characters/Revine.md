# Revine
> Type: person | Also called: Siren of Tides; Siren of Savere; Queen Revine; Revine Zecrew | First appears: Ch6.15K
> See also: Nsiia, Femithain, Urele, Orjin
## Overview
- Revine is known as the Siren of Tides or the Siren of Savere, and unofficially as one of the [Bandit Queens] that plague the world. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L760)
- Revine commands a mostly female group of [Bandit Ladies] and keeps slave-[Mages] in magical collars. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L760,L763)
- Revine can blast tonnes of water with a gesture, destroying a dresser full of costly garments. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L765)
- Revine has a sister. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L508)
## Actions
- {belief} Revine tells Nsiia at Pomle that the King of Destruction looted Savere, his armies plundering her lands and treasuries. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L502)
- Revine warns that if Reim's territory expands towards any coast it will not be safe from her wrath or her sister's. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L508)
- Revine nearly comes to blows with Nsiia, and the watering hole's surface shifts ominously. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L501,L507)
- {event} Revine rages at a slave-[Mage] because every other nation was warned before her, and she learned of the plan from one of her own people. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L760-762)
- {event} Revine orders her fleet pulled back, and calls in the Sandwave Fleet, the Bloodtear Pirates and every [Pirate Captain] within a week's sailing. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L768)
- Revine orders Urele to kill the Empress of Beasts instead of capturing her. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L770)
- {event} Revine speaks to Femithain through an enchanted conch held by Urele and demands the Empress be handed over, threatening to challenge Illivere. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L206-210)
- Revine goes quiet and cold when Femithain mentions Queen Yisame's claim on Nsiia. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L211-214)
- Revine backs down when Femithain says Nerrhavia wants Nsiia and Illivere cannot resist its demand, but says she wants Nsiia. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L215-225)
- Revine tells Femithain to keep Nsiia for now and says she will discuss terms when everything is quiet. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L225)
- Revine says Oliphant is Savere's and that she will deal with General Thelican after this. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L225)
