# Joseph
> Type: person | Also called: Joseph Ortega; Mr. Soccer (Kevin's tease) | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.20
> See also: Kevin, Rose, Imani, Galina, Troy, Leon, Ryoka Griffin, Ekirra, Jeva Silverfang, Blackmage, Erin Solstice (uses the name Joseph in messages to Geneva, a different person)
## Overview
- Joseph is a young man from Spain on Earth, one of the seven Earthers who travel to Liscor together. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L77,L81,L96)
- Joseph is not a soldier, having never been in the army, and sees himself as just a kid from Spain. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L1023)
- Joseph is the Earther known as the inventor of soccer in this world, credited with bringing the game here. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L106-107)
