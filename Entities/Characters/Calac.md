# Calac
> Type: person | Also called: Lord Calac | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.02K
> See also: Venith, Maresar, Flos, Trey, Teres
## Overview
- Calac is the only son of Venith and Maresar, and a [Lord] trained since childhood to fight and lead soldiers. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L51; Book 13, Ch6.54K L487)
- Calac is maybe a year younger than Trey and Teres, but taller and muscled like an athlete. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L487)
- Calac wears plate armor and is known to know how to use a sword. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L38; Book 13, Ch6.54K L487)
## Events
- {event} Calac charges Flos with a spear at Manimar, shouting for the King of Destruction to die, but Flos catches the shaft and unhorses him. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L121-124)
- {rel} Flos recognises Calac as Venith's son, remembering that Venith wrote the day Calac was born and Flos drank to his health. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L133)
- {event} Venith slaps Calac across the face with a gauntleted hand, drawing blood, after the attack on Flos. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L141-142)
- {event} Venith accuses Calac of disgracing himself in battle the day before, when Calac tried to end things easily by dishonorable means. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L65-68)
- Calac admits his dishonorable act was wrong and presses Venith to explain why he lets the Hellios men go. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L67-70)
- Calac accuses Venith of hiding behind oaths while Hellios sends an army to kill their people by dishonorable means. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L48,L52)
- {belief} Calac says Venith always told him Flos was a fool, an oathbreaker and a coward, and that he came to see Flos as more honorable than the other rulers. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L58-60)
- {state} Calac abandons his father's post and rides with some soldiers to join Flos, saying he never learned what loyalty meant. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L72,L81)
- {event} Calac rides with Venith to the army four miles from Reim and sees the enemy soldiers, more people than he has ever seen. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L512-514)
- Venith orders Calac to stay back and take the foot and archers to his mother, Maresar. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L518)
## Later
- Calac stays in Reim with Orthenon, as Venith did not let him join the Ger posting after his previous failure. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L60-63)
- {state} Later, Calac is among the Germinan guests introduced by Teres alongside Nawalishifra, the veiled [Blacksmith] Nawal. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L480-481)
- Calac is in disgrace with his father and resents being lumped with the guests in the room. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L487)
- Trey remembers Calac as headstrong and eager to prove himself, and not at fault for his trouble with Venith. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L488)
