# Mars
> Type: person | Also called: Mars the Illusionist; the Illusionist; Lady Mars; the King's Champion | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00K
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Trey, Teres, Orthenon, Gazi, Takhatres, Drevish, Ytol, Venith
## Overview
- Mars is a [Vanguard], a variation of the [Warrior] class, and one of the King of Destruction's Seven. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L239; Book 13, Ch6.52K L297)
- {state} Orthenon tells Trey that Mars is Level 66, the highest-leveled of Flos's group and the second-highest-level warrior class on the continent. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L211)
- Mars says she is no [Mage] and can do no magic, although she has several magical items, which is what her name refers to. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L239)
- Mars is called the Illusionist not because she knows illusion magic, but because she possesses many trinkets. (Book 5, Ch4.01K L98)
- A [Vanguard] is an advanced type of the [Soldier] class, a powerful warrior class. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L144; Book 5, Ch4.00K L197)
- Mars is the King's Champion, a [Vanguard] who stands at the head of every battle. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L184)

## Appearance and illusion
- Mars appears as a tall woman with flawless skin pale as a moonbeam, red curling locks, huge breasts and a dancer's grace. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L177-179)
- Trey suspects part of Mars's attraction is magical, since she is not called the Illusionist for no reason. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L20)
- Mars has no battle scars, unlike Flos and Orthenon, as Teres snidely points out. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L21)
- {belief} Teres claims that Mars's breasts are fake. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L321-324)
- {reveal} Mars twists a ring and her illusion vanishes, revealing a shorter figure in plain grey armor who is spattered with blood and grime. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L502-503)
- {reveal} Mars's true appearance is a bit plain, with pale skin from lack of sunlight, faint freckles, brown hair and a snubbed nose. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L504)
- Mars shows the truth only briefly, and by the time the crowd cheers she stands beside Flos with her illusion restored, waving. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L507)
- Trey realizes the ring and the brooch Mars gave Flos explain it: Mars is no good at magic, but she has a lot of magic on her. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L508)
- Flos kisses Mars on the forehead and calls her his beautiful vassal, with pride that has nothing to do with looks. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L505-509)
- Gazi flicks sand at Mars and her seductive illusion flickers, revealing a shorter, less enthralling woman. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L26-27)
- Gazi tells Mars she needs a new artifact for a bad illusion. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L28)
- Mars's illusions change: her hair is red, then purple, then short-cropped green, with the green guise looking younger and more like a soldier. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L521,L660)
- Mars asks Teres if she likes the spell as a new look, and Teres asks if all her illusions have huge breasts. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L493-494)
- Mars never leaves her armor, and her illusions let her appear as a beautiful woman, changing appearances as Teres changes clothes. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L175)
- Mars has a hangover in Reim's camp, and the illusion captures even her haggard look, including red eyes. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L173-174)

## Abilities and equipment
- Mars carries a round shield on her back and a sword at her hip. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L52)
- Mars says swords are rarely over five pounds unless used against Minotaurs or specialists, and teaches the twins to grip them. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L284-285)
- Mars's armor stops lances, arrows, stone shards and boulders, and Mars swings her shield like a mace. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L440-451)
- Mars killed the Quinfer, Germina's champion, in a duel that lasted only a minute, then charged into the army. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L100)
- Trey thinks Mars could have destroyed Germina's army alone if she had enough time. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L100)
- Mars wades through arrows and a [Fireball] unscathed at Blalevault, and the defenders give up shooting within forty feet. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L523-525)
- Mars uses [Grand Slash] repeatedly in battle with Khelt's undead, destroying hundreds of zombies at a time. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L772-777)
- The Quarass says Mars uses [Six-fold Grand Slash], able to use it six times for every time a lesser [Warrior] would. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L781)
- Mars uses [Call of the Champion] against Belchan's army; if no champion answers, it demoralizes the army and any champion classes lose their class. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L183-186)
- At Belchan, Mars wields a sword of living fire twelve feet long, a black shield with a snarling face that bites, and a second moonlight-pale blade from her bag of holding. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L273-278)
- Mars has used [Call of the Champion] on Jecrass's armies without being answered. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L516)

## Book 2: Arrival at Flos's court
- Mars presents Flos with a black bag with glowing symbols, containing a small armory of weapons, armor and magical items for his army. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L288-290,L295-296)
- The bag includes a curved, double-sided scimitar that glows purple. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L291)
- Mars, Gazi, Takhatres and Orthenon each want to please Flos best, and Mars's gift is a challenge to the others. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L293-294)
- Mars bowed her head blushing when Flos smiled at her, and later reaches for her sword at Gazi's insults. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L184,L298)
- Mars grips her sword with tears falling and rage in her eyes when Drevish's head arrives. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L371)

## Book 5: The war against the Emperor of Sands
- Orthenon says Mars is on the way and should arrive by midday, sooner if she is marching as fast as he thinks. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L59)
- Trey knows only that Mars is a [Vanguard] nicknamed the Illusionist; she is angry that the world does not yet know Flos has awoken, but Orthenon is unruffled by her ire. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L197)
- {belief} Mars thinks Flos is waiting until he is ready, since she has not seen him on the training grounds in twenty years. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L295-298)
- Mars slings an arm around Trey's shoulder and says she once took a blow from Flos and could not see straight for two days. (Book 5, Ch4.01K L420-421)
- Mars is not suited to leadership, being a [Soldier] who fights rather than leads, and Flos admits she is lacking at other matters. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L145; Book 5, Ch4.04K L396)
- Orthenon orders Mars to stay with Flos to guard the city while the others take the field, and she argues, rages and overturns chairs. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L381-388)
- Mars sulks, doing nothing, while the others work, and Orthenon, Gazi and Flos struggle to explain her use outside battle. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L389-396)
- Mars rides past Lord Venith without a word and kicks him in the back as he gets to his feet. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L542)
- Mars arrives alone on a hilltop in silver armor with red hair, bare-headed, and Flos calls her his champion and [Vanguard]. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L418-435)
- Mars charges the enemy alone; lancers and arrows break on her, boulders break instead of her, and she runs through the enemy ranks. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L439-452)
- A stone larger than a car crushes the mud where Mars stood, and then she walks out of the air itself and runs the [Geomancer] through. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L448,L458-462)
- Mars says she disobeyed Flos's orders to come because Venith's son Calac sent word that Flos was in danger. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L489-491)

## Later campaigns
- Mars camps with her soldiers outside Blalevault in Hellios, and tells Flos the defenders dare not sally out so long as she is there. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L487-491)
- Mars sits with Flos in her tent after Blalevault surrenders, speaking softly about the past. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L659-661)
- Mars leads about a thousand of Reim's [Soldiers] across no-man's land from Nerrhavia's border force of at least twelve thousand, with no movement on either side. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L292-297)
- Nerrhavia reinforces its lines twice until it has over ten times her number. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L298)
- Flos rides to the border, tells Mars to ride with him, then turns back without charging, and Mars remains and spits in the dust. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L603-611)
- Mars charges left in the battle at Khelt, covering Flos's flank. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L770)
- Mars is normally spoiling for a fight and laughing, even drinking before battle, but looks intense and checks her armor at the sight of the Minotaurs. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L52)
- Ytol suggests retreat from the Minotaurs, and Flos orders an advance instead, with Mars glancing at Ytol and nodding. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L50-60)
- Mars challenges the House of Minos as Champion of the King of Destruction, using [Call of the Champion] to demand they send a fighter or be forsworn. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L512-516)
- Sixteen Minotaurs step forward, and Mars beheads the first, Lewril of Maweil, in one stroke with an elongating sword. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L536-547)
- All sixteen Minotaurs die to Mars, and the Minotaurs cheer their names as General Ozem declares the lawbreakers' shame ended and honor upheld. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L583-587)
