# Kissilt
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.21D
> See also: Niers Astoragon, Foliana, Venaz, Marian, Jekilt
## Overview
- Kissilt is a Drake student in Niers' strategy class at Wistram, and is described as surly and grumpy. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L186; Book 13, Ch6.54K L369; Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L197)
- Kissilt has mild ice breath and pale white-purple scales. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L197)
- Kissilt protests mildly when the Centaur Jekilt complains about the arrogance of young Humans and Drakes. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L184-186)
- Niers notices Kissilt about to speak and warns him to think before reacting to Olesm Swifttail's idea. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L557)
- Kissilt mentions that Garen Redfang was slain by the Gecko of Liscor, and Niers asks him to elaborate on Liscor's champion. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L561-564)
## Among his classmates
- Feshi calls Kissilt an example of Drakes who hold a grudge forever. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L336)
- Kissilt normally does not keep company with Marian and her group, given Umina's presence, and demands that Marian give him his money. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L369-371)
- Kissilt bursts out in fury when Jekilt offers a trade of questions, saying he meant to ask a question about classes. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L297-303)
- Foliana refuses to lend Kissilt her inkpot, saying he has been rude to her and must amend his ways. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Foliana L159-165)
- Foliana puts Kissilt's own inkpot back in his bag, and later his inkpot goes missing again at his desk. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Foliana L160,L252)
- Niers places Kissilt on a team as the resident Drake, to partner with Illial and Leondir. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Foliana L200)
- Kissilt enjoys a moment and puts an arm around Wil and Cameral, who edge away. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Foliana L503)
