# Jelaim
> Type: person | Also called: Hunter Jelaim; Serpent Hunter Jelaim | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.13K
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Orthenon, Ulyse, Serpent Hunters
## Overview
- Jelaim is the leader of the Serpent Hunters, a group of Stitch-folk warriors among the vassals of Flos Reimarch, the King of Destruction. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L254; Book 10, Ch6.14K L108; Book 15, Ch7.10K L255)
- Jelaim is a Stitch-Man, one of the String People, with golden thread showing at his bare shoulder. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L296-297; Book 15, Ch7.10K L672)
- Jelaim says he is a [Serpent Hunter], a class that uses poison in weapons, and that the group was built from it. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L290)
- Jelaim tells Trey Atwood not to call him Captain, since that is a class term and he has no soldier classes, though he may be a mercenary captain. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L286)
- Jelaim prefers to be called by his name, or Hunter Jelaim if honorifics are needed. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L286)
## Weapons and training
- Jelaim and his warriors carry curved, enchanted blades that are coated in poison in their sheaths. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L255-256)
- Jelaim asks Orthenon for more enchanted weapons from the treasury, since steel blades would corrode from the Serpent Hunters' venoms. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L259-262)
- Jelaim says the Serpent Hunters have dozens of new recruits learning their tricks, and the king wants them to take in any of the String Folk. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L259,L293)
- Jelaim teaches only String People, because they can remove a poisoned limb and have a new one woven. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L294-296)
- Jelaim loves magical swords and wears scimitars on either hip. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L293)
- Jelaim tries to swing Flos' sword and cannot manage a proper slash, though his muscles bulge. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L344-346)
- Jelaim hurries to Chieftain Nelrra, excited at the idea of an easy new coating for his blades. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L672)
## Role in the war
- Jelaim performs a dueling display on a banquet table with two of his warriors, using real envenomed blades, to honor the King of Destruction. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L108-113)
- Jelaim is counted among Flos' vassals, alongside Ulyse, Mars and Orthenon, at the meeting with the Gnolls. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L521)
- Jelaim throws a knife into a charging Minotauress' unguarded arm, helping the Death Warriors cut her down. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L794-795)
- Jelaim's Stitchwarriors use poisoned weapons to support Flos' advance. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L795)
- Flos orders Jelaim to find the enemy catapults and smash or capture them. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L797)
