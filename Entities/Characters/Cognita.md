# Cognita
> Type: person | Also called: Carved Golem; Truestone Golem; Truestone Construct | First appears: Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1
> See also: Archmage Zelkyr, Pisces, Ceria Springwalker, Amerys, Feor, Naili, Aaron Vanwell, Wistram, Golems
## Overview
- Cognita is a Truestone Construct who introduces herself as a Carved Golem designed to oversee Wistram and its mages. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L297)
- She says she and her kindred maintain and preserve the building and will assist students during their stay. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L297)
- She has emerald eyes and is first seen as a seven-foot Golem with a carved face. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L291,L298)
- Later she is described as eight feet tall, carved of pure white stone finer than marble, with a dress and body that move like cloth and flesh. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L72; Book 14, Ch6.60 L191)
- Archmage Zelkyr shaped and gave her life, and she alone among Golems has personality, thought, feeling and perhaps a soul. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L3-5)
- She has held Wistram for over a century, ordered by Zelkyr to protect it and test every [Mage] who might ascend to the higher floors. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L192)
- She is the leader of the Golems of Wistram, a Truestone Construct and an adjudicator of Wistram's laws beholden to no one, not even the Archmages. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L74)
- Many mages are afraid of her, and the Archmages are described as looking at her and seeing something they cannot surpass. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L415)
## Rules and role
- {fact} Cognita gives new students three rules: do not venture unaccompanied into the high or low parts of the castle, do not attack or obstruct Golems, and consult before casting area-wide magic. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L299)
- She guards the upper floors, where one must defeat her and the four other Golem guardians Zelkyr placed as a test. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L75)
- Aaron knows she is the fifth and most deadly of the guardian Golems. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L177)
- She has killed hundreds, perhaps thousands, of mages, yet is subservient, helpful and polite. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L7)
- To Blackmage's knowledge no [Mage] has ever attempted her test and survived. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L75)
- {belief} Pisces says that even Cognita admits her existence was an accident, though she was Zelkyr's greatest creation. (Book 3, Ch3.25 L42)
- Pisces says the Wistram Golems are far more intelligent than a fire guardian, which was merely a summoned killer. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L81)
- Cognita says she is bound to be truthful, and will relate what she has seen only if she is asked about specific events. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L163-165)
