# Lady Salkis
> Type: person | Also called: Salkis | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.38
> See also: Kevin, Jasi, Numbtongue, Mrsha, Pallass, Wesle Salkis
## Overview
- Lady Salkis is a rich young Drake [Lady] of Pallass, with an onyx-scaled hide and a tail that wags when she is excited. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L938,L970; Book 19, Ch7.50 L1409)
- Salkis travels with [Bodyguards] who try to restrain her, and her father is said to be worried by her visits. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L941,L970; Book 19, Ch7.50 L1407)
## Skateboard (Book 18)
- Lady Salkis pauses demurely with her bodyguards among the young people watching Kevin, hiding a strange artifact she cannot puzzle out. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L938)
- {event} Salkis insists on watching Kevin's skateboard ride, saying she would like to see what the Human is doing. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L939-941)
- Salkis would have joined the mob around Kevin but for her bodyguards, and orders them to find out what the skateboard is. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L970-971)
## The inn (Book 19)
- {event} Salkis follows Jasi's song to the inn despite a bodyguard warning that it is a hotspot of dangerous activities. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1407-1408)
- Salkis sees Numbtongue walk out of the Garden of Sanctuary and stares at a Hobgoblin, while her bodyguards reach for weapons until Mrsha kicks them. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1409-1410)
- {state} Salkis decides she is going to like it at the inn. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1411)
