# Ressa
> Type: person | Also called: Miss Ressa; Magnolia's head maid | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.20
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Reynold, Erin Solstice, Ryoka Griffin, Zel Shivertail
## Overview
- Ressa is the [Head Maid] in service to Lady Magnolia Reinhart, and the head of her staff. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L85,L116)
- Ressa looks almost inhumanly good, which is a feature of her class, and is also stern and imposing like a head matron or queen. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L95-97)
- Ressa is cold and austere, and unconcerned by weapons pointed at her. (Book 3, Ch3.10 L60)
- Ressa is a silent [Head Maid] who accompanies her mistress everywhere and can be as quiet as a falling leaf. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L109)
- Ressa is Magnolia's childhood friend, and Magnolia's right hand and best friend. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L9; Book 11, Ch6.27M L97)
- Ressa is Magnolia's long-time friend, whose mouth tightens in a way only Magnolia notices. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L265)

## Class and abilities
- Ressa is a [Maid] who was trained like an [Assassin] and inherited the best Skills of both classes. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L12)
- Ressa uses an intimidation effect with her glare that makes servants flinch, but it does not work on Erin Solstice. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L102-113)
- Ressa moves at near-invisible speed, hearing through a closed hatch in a speeding carriage. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L36,L251)
- Ressa casts [Invisibility Field] from the carriage and throws throwing stars at a Hobgoblin on a Carn Wolf. (Book 3, Ch3.10 L418-419,L432)
- Ressa wears a magical shield that vaporizes weapons and flesh that touch her. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L24-26)
- Ressa walks on ceilings and drops twenty feet to the floor as part of testing her staff. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L30)
- Ressa wields a magical dagger called the Vision of Grasses, given to her by Regis Reinhart, coated with many poisons. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L249-250)
- Ressa uses summoning talismans from Drath, including one that calls a Hydra. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L210-212)

## Book 2: Erin Solstice and the Reinhart mansion
- Ressa is a head maid whom Valceif has worked to get on the good side of, and she reacts dramatically at Ryoka Griffin's name. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L399,L405)
- Ressa leads Erin through the Reinhart mansion with two maids and servants bobbing their heads, introducing herself as the [Head Maid]. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L84-85,L93-97)
- Ressa agrees to let Erin look at the mansion's displays after Erin asks politely and appears unaffected. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L115-117)
- Ressa closes a door and locks it from the outside with a key from her pocket. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L390)
- When Erin throws a teapot at Magnolia, Ressa catches it, shields Magnolia from the hot liquid and reaches for Erin's throat before Magnolia says stop. (Book 2, Ch2.31 L203-205)
- On Magnolia's word, Ressa smacks Erin lightly on the back of the head, and does so again when Erin says guns would be bad. (Book 2, Ch2.31 L310,L314)
- Magnolia warns Ressa that Erin would hit her back, since Erin has [Bar Fighting] and [Minotaur Punch]. (Book 2, Ch2.31 L315)
- Ressa disapproves of the heavily sweetened raspberry tea Magnolia drinks and has said so many times before. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L9-10)

## Book 3: Ryoka Griffin
- Reynold and Ressa arrive in Ocre in a horseless noble carriage to summon Ryoka to Magnolia's estate. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L490-502)
- Ressa tells Ryoka that Lady Reinhart is expecting her. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L502)
- Asked what happens if Ryoka refuses, Ressa answers "Feel free to try", and refuses to give any promises. (Book 3, Ch3.10 L68-69,L88-90)
- Ressa tells Ryoka she will go with her, or be brought against her will. (Book 3, Ch3.10 L90)
- Ressa asks Ryoka in the carriage whether she would like to be hit, and Ryoka falls silent. (Book 3, Ch3.10 L310-313)
- Ressa orders the carriage inspected and the spell lattice tested because [Invisibility Field] should not have failed to a mere unenchanted bolt. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L60-62)
- Ryoka guesses Ressa is a [Ninja], and Magnolia laughs, saying Ressa is a [Maid] and [Assassins] are the class of choice here. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L126-136)
- Ressa stops Ryoka's punch at Magnolia, and Ryoka never sees her move. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L249-251)
- Magnolia asks if Ressa took a teapot to the face from Erin, and Ressa corrects that it was to the chest and was lukewarm tea. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L265-268)
- Ressa blocks an [Assassin] with her chest, kills him with her fingers and flicks projectiles back at the others. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L22-27)
- Ressa swallows a mage's fireball with a mirror from her skirt and throws a tripvine bag that engulfs his Golems. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L108-110)

## Books 5-6: At Magnolia's side
- Ressa holds the carriage door for Magnolia at a social event, with servants backing off like prey from a hunter. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L234-235)
- Ressa travels with Magnolia, and the staff includes Janica, Bekia, a Gnoll [Maid], Jefry and the driver Alkran, whose arm was broken by assassins. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L145-148,L229,L306)
- Ressa tells Reynold that the matter is important enough that he must drive back at once. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L305-307)
- Magnolia says Ressa glares out of protectiveness and nagging, and Zel adds that she is armed. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L125-126)
- Magnolia tells Zel Shivertail that Ressa is her protector. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L128)
- Ressa wrinkles her nose at the mud on stepping out at the officers' camp. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L80)
- Ressa rates Magnolia's speech four out of five, not the sort given on the precipice of a battle but stirring enough. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L186)
- Ressa stabs a Ghoul in the back with the Vision of Grasses, as some of its poisons still paralyze the undead. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L249-250)
- Ressa fights Ghouls beside the [Assassin] Theofore, telling him to shut up and stab. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L251-255)

## Book 11 and later
- Ressa rises before dawn and flits from shadow to shadow inspecting hallways for dust and infiltration, and the staff fears her. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L10-12)
- Ressa tests combat-capable staff on her patrol, passing the Gnoll Bekia. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L29-30)
- Ressa eats porridge and fruit in under a minute, and is at Magnolia's bedside as she wakes. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L96-97)
- Ressa avoids Magnolia's punch, since being both [Assassin] and [Maid] means Magnolia cannot hit her. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L449)
- Ressa leaps out of the carriage at the Circle of Thorns' ambush, slashing with the Vision of Grasses. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L193)
- Ressa takes three cuts from a [Blademaster Assassin] without damage to her uniform, and throws summoning talismans while other maids call for help. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L208-210)
- Ressa recognizes the old [Trapmaster] Sithorn among the assassins and is stunned. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L293)
