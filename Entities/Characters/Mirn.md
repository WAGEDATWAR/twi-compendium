# Mirn
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.17S
> See also: Onieva, Selys Shivertail, Saliss
## Overview
- Mirn is a Drake with dark, nearly midnight-blue scales, an infectious smile and a scar across both his lips. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L747)
- Mirn is an old acquaintance of the Named Adventurer Onieva, and owns the pub where they meet at that moment. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L747,L943-946)
- Mirn runs a bar, though he is a rude Drake. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L263,L269-271)
## Book 16: the secret pub
- Mirn cannot cook and serves Onieva stale sandwiches bought that morning, because his bag of holding is poor at preserving food. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L744-753)
- Mirn's pub has clients who mostly bring their own food or drink, and someone plays a music crystal of the Queen of Pop. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L760-763)
- Mirn's pub has a secret exit, a hole in the basement leading to the 1st floor, and three reinforced and magically locked doors that he installed. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L929-931,L934)
- {event} Mirn herds his clients out of the secret exit when the Watch arrives, since they have done this before. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L929-934)
- Mirn holds a blade with two razor-sharp edges in a side pocket, and hesitates before leaving Onieva behind. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L940)
- {event} Onieva tells Mirn to go and buys him a minute, so Mirn vanishes through the trap door. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L939-942)
- The Watch finds no Turnscales and no illegal activities in the pub, only Onieva, whom they fight and she knocks out. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L943-947)
## Book 18: looking for a safe place
- Mirn is stalking through the crowd in Pallass with Onieva, who says the Watch is cracking down after the riots. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L227-228)
- Onieva says that Mirn gravitates towards people he needs to protect, and that he did not think he had anything in common with Selys. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L264)
- Onieva and Mirn have been looking for a safe place to establish his bar, where no [Guard] will kick down the door and arrest everyone. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L274-275)
- The riots put the Watch on full alert, and they go after criminals and deviants when angry, which makes finding a spot harder. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L276)
- Mirn and Onieva cannot use Saliss's laboratory for safety reasons, and cannot go outside the city. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L277)
