# Okasha
> Type: person | Also called: the Selphid roommate; the Selphid sharing Geneva's body | First appears: Book 3, Ch1.01D
> See also: Geneva, Thriss, Calectus, Idis, Selphids
## Overview
- Okasha is a Selphid who first appears as a [Rogue], Level 23, of the Burning March 6th Division, living in a dead woman's body with a useless right side. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L229-234)
- Okasha offers to help the [Doctor] Geneva, who had put her into that body, and becomes her assistant. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L234-250)
- Okasha has lived in Dullahan and Lizardfolk bodies before. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L254)
- In a dead body, Okasha breathes and blinks much less than normal people. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L253)
## Sharing Geneva's body
- {state} After Thriss broke Geneva's spine, Okasha entered Geneva's living body to bridge the gap, an act forbidden to Selphids. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L420-443)
- Okasha stabbed Thriss through the chest as he raised his mace over Geneva. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L420)
- Okasha controls Geneva's right arm and speaks inaudibly inside her head. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L30-34)
- Okasha hums to Geneva by projecting sound directly into her inner ear. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L49)
- Okasha is a Level 23 [Rogue] and a Level 15 [Medical Assistant]. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L147)
- Okasha puts Geneva to sleep by triggering her brain to secrete drowsiness chemicals, which Geneva calls melatonin. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L139)
- {belief} Okasha wonders whether Selphids' predecessors worked with their hosts rather than just controlling them. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L154)
- Okasha takes full control of Geneva only when she is asleep or in dire need, and is uncomfortable with that. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L143)
- Okasha urged Geneva to leave the battlefield camp for four days, but Geneva refused to take sides. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L469-482)
- {belief} Okasha says the Centaurs paid Zalthia to attack Geneva's camp. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L500)
- After Geneva was stabbed, Okasha spoke with her voice, pumped her stopped heart manually and held her internal wounds closed until Calectus's potion arrived. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L95,L104,L112)
- Geneva's spine is broken, with a neck bone crushed by a soldier's hit, so she cannot move without Okasha bridging the connection. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L122)
- Okasha leapt from tree to tree in Geneva's body to scout, and warned the leaps would tear Geneva's muscles. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L184-196)
- Selphids can exceed a body's limits for a brief period at the cost of damage. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L188)
- Okasha secretes a pain-relieving agent and told Geneva to use a healing potion for torn muscle. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L216)
## Book 10 to 16
- Geneva describes Okasha in Book 10 as the Selphid who lives in her body since Thriss paralysed her, bridging her nerves and helping her move. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L306-308)
- Okasha can release hormones into Geneva's brain to change her mood, which Geneva objects to. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L317-327)
- {belief} Okasha is gullible about biology and believes Geneva when she claims to know everything about her body. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L328-330)
- Okasha can force Geneva's muscles to exercise, and gave her more muscular strength and fine control in her hands. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L434-439)
- Okasha speaks through Geneva's mouth in a higher, more cheerful voice. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L380-383)
- Okasha speaks for Geneva in the meeting with Fezimet, acting enough like her to nearly fool Daly. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L678-688)
- Okasha complains of feeling like a prisoner and demands sex, saying she cannot control the body unless Geneva sleeps. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L297-302)
- Okasha calms Geneva's adrenaline spike with endorphins and makes her pinky-swear to find someone that night. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L336-353)
## Inner Friend
- {state} Later, Okasha is a Level 11 [Inner Friend], and Geneva says Okasha was Level 4 earlier. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L441-445,L778-780)
- Okasha has the Skill [Host: The Thinking Room], gained at Level 10, which separates Geneva's mind from her body. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L443-444,L782)
- Okasha is the first Selphid in current memory to gain a class like this. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L445)
- Okasha's Skill [Physical Enhancement: Flexibility] lets her bend Geneva's arm nearly a hundred and eighty degrees. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L766-767)
- Another Skill lets Geneva move even when Okasha is not awake. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L773)
- After the Xeppal incident, Okasha got three days a week of six hours each to pilot Geneva's body, with sex with others off limits. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L437-439)
- {belief} Okasha claims "our body" and says Geneva is sick from overwork and her image. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1118-1123)
- Okasha seizes control of Geneva's body and puts her in the box. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1137-1146)
- {state} Later, six Selphids led by Calectus take Okasha out of Geneva and seal her in a holding-jar. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1178-1183)
- Calectus says Okasha committed the sin for which the Selphids were destroyed, and the Minds will sentence her. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1183)
- Idis is introduced as Okasha's replacement and takes Geneva's body. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1188-1195)
- In Book 18 Geneva misses Okasha, and Calectus says she is being held and cannot be spoken with. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L23,L329-333)
