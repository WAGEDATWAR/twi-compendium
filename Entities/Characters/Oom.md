# Oom
> Type: person | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.49
> See also: Az'kerash, Bea, Ijvani, Zel Shivertail
## Overview
- Oom is an Acid Slime created by Az'kerash, with a mana stone as its heart. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L305; Book 13, Ch6.51A L142)
- Oom is one of the Chosen, undead who can think and grow, though not by levels; Az'kerash had five of them. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L94)
## Death
- {state} Zel Shivertail finds the core of Oom's slime body, a mana stone as large as his fist, and crushes it while Bea begs him not to. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L304-308)
- Oom's slime body burned Zel and tried to infiltrate his body before the stone broke. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L304-305)
- Ijvani considers Oom destroyed beyond repair, gone rather than merely broken, and fears she could be gone too. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L439,L458)
- Az'kerash thinks of Oom, an Acid Slime that perished fighting Zel Shivertail, for the first time while speaking with Bea. (Book 13, Ch6.51A L142)
- Az'kerash names Oom's destruction by Zel Shivertail as the reason the gathering of his remaining Chosen is reduced. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L113)
