# Warden Winta
> Type: person | Also called: River Warden Winta | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.15K
> See also: Raelt, Jecaina, General Lael, Flos
## Overview
- Winta is a River Warden of Jecrass, one of the nobles who control the rivers that make the kingdom rich in horses. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L117-118)
- Winta is a woman who fights with a wand and leads elite troops for King Raelt. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L498,L559)
## At Raelt's court
- Winta dabs at her forehead in the court as Geril reads Flos's letter demanding that his people be released. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L29-31)
- Winta argues that the King of Destruction's oath means nothing needs to be feared, since all of Chandrar would fall on him if he invaded. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L116-119)
- Winta suggests that Jecrass's fast cavalry could harass Reim's armies by raiding their supply lines. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L159-160)
- Winta squeaks and turns dead white when Raelt receives a missive that Flos is flying his banner at the border. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L315-317)
- Winta starts as the army of Reim approaches and the rumble of its footsteps carries a layer of sound. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L426)
- Winta tells Raelt that no one would begrudge him bringing poison or any weapon to his duel with the King of Destruction, and Raelt answers that he would begrudge it. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L732-733)
## The battle against Flos
- Winta bows to Raelt as he leaves his tent for the battle, with worry in her eyes, and tells him Jecaina is in the capital tending to the people. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L448-454)
- Raelt tells Winta she does not have to ride into battle, and she answers that she knows. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L456-457)
- Winta leads elite troops on the right with Sekirst, while Emile leads on the left. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L498)
- Winta uses [Step of Light] with her wand to clear the way for the Trick Riders. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L515-517)
- Winta blasts a Death Soldier off his horse and knocks back Flos's bodyguards with another spell from her wand. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L559-560)
- Winta shouts that the King of Destruction is dead after Raelt's rapier pierces Flos's heart, and orders a Trick Rider to pull Raelt back. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L565-570,L581)
- Winta rides at Flos's body and is run through with Raelt's rapier when Flos pulls it from his heart. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L584-586)
- Raelt later counts Winta among the dead, and thinks of her death when he decides to sue for peace. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L615; Book 19, Ch7.46K L498)
