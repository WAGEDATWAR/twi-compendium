# Issa
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.36
> See also: Bevussa Slenderscale, Zassil, Kin, Wings of Pallass
## Overview
- Issa is a Drake adventurer and a member of the Gold-rank team Wings of Pallass, led by the Garuda Bevussa Slenderscale. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L390,L469)
- Issa is publicly presented as the team's captain, while Bevussa is the real leader, because a Garuda would not be accepted leading Oldblood Drakes. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L38; Book 8, Ch5.40 L469)
- Issa admires Bevussa greatly and is the one tasked with pretending to be captain of the Wings of Pallass in public. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L469)
- {rel} Before Drake investors and influential Drakes in a walled city, Issa is treated as the captain and Bevussa as the pretty Garuda. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L2)
## In the Liscor dungeon, Book 8
- Issa and Zassil land on a Stone Starer together and kill it with a spear and a sword. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L217)
- Issa is hit by one of a Stone Starer's beams, and her armor partly blocks it, so Bevussa has her pull back to be checked. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L220-227)
- Issa pulls out a healing potion after the beam strike and protests that she can still fight. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L224-227)
- Issa turns bright red and her wings quiver in embarrassment when the mage Typhenous catches her and Bevussa gossiping about his team's past. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L489-491)
- Issa gasps at the sight of a bone spider-like monster, and imagines it charging into her team. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L511-517)
## Later appearances
- Issa and Zassil throw bread pieces at each other while the team sits in Erin Solstice's inn. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L58)
- Bevussa orders Issa to use a Wand of Lightning when greater undead appear in the dungeon. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L742)
- Issa uses wands and flashes of [Light] as one of the fliers, alongside Kin's fire spells, against a Wyvern attacking Liscor. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L293)
- Bevussa calls on Issa to use [Pillar of Earth] during the Wyvern fight, and Issa dives to pull a young child out from under the Wyvern's claw. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L257-258)
- Issa shoves the rescued Drake child to Noass and Sir Relz and tells them to get to safety. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L261-262)
