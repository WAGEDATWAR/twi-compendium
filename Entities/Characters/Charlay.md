# Charlay
> Type: person | Also called: Dustrider Charlay; Dusty; Charlay the Dustrider | First appears: Ch6.35
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Alevica, Durene, Rie
## Overview
- Charlay is a Centauress City Runner, over seven feet tall and weighing at least a ton, with a tan coat with large brown spots and short blonde-grey hair. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L43-44)
- Charlay has the nickname Dustrider Charlay, earned after two years running in the area. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L115)
- Charlay comes from Baleros and says Centaurs are common in central Baleros but rare in Izril. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L106,L148-150)
- Charlay says Izril has lovely flat places and there would be tens of thousands of Centaurs there if not for the ocean crossing. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L150)
- Charlay has a horse's palate for bland foods, cannot handle spice, loves salty savoury food, fears snakes and holds grudges. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L151-152)
- Charlay has the Skill [Spray Hoof], which kicks backward to throw up dust, or mud and water when the ground is wet. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L124-126)
- Charlay carries two wands and a dagger, outruns people, and can knock someone off a horse with her wands and make a dust cloud. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L439-441)
## Rivalry and friendship with Ryoka
- Charlay trails Ryoka for three days, saying she is scoping out her competition. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L113,L151)
- Charlay calls Ryoka "Pukey" because Ryoka vomited on her. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L129)
- Charlay throws Ryoka a drying stone to clean off the mud she splattered on her. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L138-140)
- {belief} Charlay boasts she is twice as fast as Ryoka, whom Ryoka thinks she is faster than, though not by that much. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L219-222)
- Charlay is terrified of Alevica and says she thinks Alevica cast a spell that set her tail on fire after Charlay objected to being cut in line. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L233,L401-403)
- Charlay's tail bears a faint burn scar, and she needed four healing potions to stop the fire. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L403-404)
- Charlay agrees to run with Ryoka to Riverfarm, saying she would like to come, and Ryoka calls her a friend. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L408-420)
- Charlay says insulting each other is what friends do among her Centaur friends in Baleros. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L415-418)
- Ryoka and Charlay pass eight burned ruins on the road south. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L389)
## Riverfarm Runner's Guild
- Ryoka announces she will start a Runner's Guild in Riverfarm, and Charlay immediately asks for dibs on Guildmistress. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L241-243)
- Charlay helps lay out the plan to build a network of Runners and asks to be listed as Riverfarm's top Runner. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L248-249,L258)
- Rie agrees to convert a warehouse into a Guild, and Charlay passes on being Guildmistress when she hears it pays nothing. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L260-266)
- Charlay runs thirty-two miles to Filk and announces she is Riverfarm's top Runner from its new Runner's Guild. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L276,L631)
- Charlay demands guild seals and supplies on Lady Rie's account from a Filk [Receptionist]. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L633-636)
- Charlay pays a Filk [Fence] and [Broker] three gold in advance for information on [Witches], after haggling him down from five. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L640-660)
- The [Broker] tells Charlay to return in four days, with more gold. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L661)
- Charlay tells Durene to stop thinking like a Human and be proud of what she is. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L1033-1037)
## The fires and after
- Charlay tells Ryoka she is going to run south through the fire, and Ryoka protests that the smoke will kill her. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L727-741)
- Charlay collapses from the smoke, and Durene carries her limp form back to Wiskeria. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L762-764)
- Charlay carries Eloise to Gralton's lands in two days, for a fee tripled from twenty-five to seventy-five gold. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L251-261)
- Later, Charlay says goodbye to Ryoka days earlier, because her city misses her and she is restless after staying through fire and fury. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L327-331)
- Charlay hugs Ryoka and tells her friends cry, and they call each other Pukey and Dusty. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L331-337)
