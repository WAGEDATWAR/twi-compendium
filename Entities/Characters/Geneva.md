# Geneva Scala
> Type: person | Also called: Doctor Geneva; Geneva Scala; the Last Light of Baleros; Last Light; Miss Scala | First appears: Book 3, Ch1.00D
> See also: Okasha, Thriss, Luan Khumalo, Ken, Aiko, Calectus, Idis, Fezimet, Octavia, Ylawes Byres
## Overview
- Geneva Scala is a young woman from Earth who becomes a [Doctor] in Baleros, the only true doctor on that continent. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L2-6; Book 5, Ch1.03D L46)
- Soldiers and later the wider public call her the Last Light of Baleros, a title she dislikes. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L391; Book 5, Ch1.05D L15-17)
- She refuses to take sides in wars and treats any patient regardless of species or army. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L61; Book 5, Ch1.06D L305)
## Background on Earth
- Geneva was born in Wisconsin and is Italian by heritage, and she had often visited Italy. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L194)
- Geneva is twenty-four, going on twenty-five, when she joins the Raverian Fighters. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L163)
- She wanted to be a doctor after trying to hold a dying friend together in the garden of her house. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L2)
- Her aunt died when Geneva was twelve, and she had dreamed of saving even people who had to be buried in pieces. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L3)
- Geneva was a third-year medical student and had not graduated when she came to this world. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L83)
- She left a lecture to go to the bathroom and never came back. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L5)
- Geneva walked down a school hallway, turned, and found green foliage and dirt where sterile tiles had been. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L77)
- On Earth she studied as a general surgeon, cut up corpses and was a good student, but her experience was almost all theoretical. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L254)
- Geneva carries a personal oath modelled on the Declaration of Geneva, which she adopted because it is her namesake. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L129-131)
- She is a pacifist who has sworn an oath not to harm, though she does not count animals or stinging insects. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L39)
## Joining the Raverian Fighters (Book 3)
- Geneva was recruited in a Baleros city by Sergeant Thriss of the 4th Battalion of the Raverian Fighters, who hired her when she called herself a [Doctor]. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L34-48)
- Thriss accepted her claim because no one in the company can verify classes, and he judged it an odd sort who would lie about it. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L44,L49)
- Geneva marched in Thriss's 6th Squad. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L142)
- She gained [Doctor] Level 1 and [Skill - Lesser Resistance: Disease] on the night she made her oath. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L135-137)
- {belief} Geneva suspected that Thriss staged the dislocated-shoulder training injury, with the healing potion ready, to build a recruit's confidence and test her. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L106-107)
- Thriss trusted Geneva with the iron key to the healing potion chest after her first operation, warning that missing potions would bring hell. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L339-343)
- The scene that opens the chapter shows a soldier dying under her hands, the fifth person she had seen die. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L22,L29)

## Methods and innovations
- Geneva wanted penicillin and antibiotics from her first days, but she did not remember how to make penicillin from scratch. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L388-390,L418)
- With no antibiotics, she treated a Dullahan's infected wound with boiled water, salt saline and a sparing dose of healing potion. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L388-400)
- She asked for tweezers, a digging instrument, and a strong spirit to clean her tools. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L285-288)
- Geneva invented triage in the camp, tying white armbands on priority patients and marking lower-priority ones with charcoal. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L277-281)
- Her triage meant she left patients she could not save alone, and she told them she prioritises the people she can save. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L281,L304)
- Geneva flew a red cross flag on white cloth over her tent, and soldiers began waving crosses of their own when bringing wounded. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L372-375)
- {event} An enemy [Mage] who was blasting the camp turned away and left after seeing Geneva hold up the flag before the wounded. (Book 3, Ch1.01D L379-384)
- Later, she triages with coloured stones: green for the least wounded, yellow for moderate wounds, red for those about to die, and black for the dead or untreated. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L62)
- She uses healing potion sparingly, working with a scalpel, curved needle and forceps. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L15-25)
- Geneva cannot make blood transfusions because she has not been able to build a needle and pump. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L32)
- She removed an Evercut arrowhead from Luan's stomach lining with forceps and then closed the organs with healing potion. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L90,L103-111)
- She saved a War Walker with nineteen Evercut arrows in him and a nearly severed arm, and reconstructed the arm over an hour. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L192-204)
- {belief} Geneva says she is just a [Doctor] and not fully trained, and wishes for a true heart surgeon, an anesthesiologist, or someone who knows how to make penicillin. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L17)
- She has no treatment for poison-gas victims, whose effects resemble mustard gas, and told Ken and Luan to stop bringing them. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L385-397)
- Geneva carries a primitive wooden stethoscope, a measuring tape, tongue depressors, clean gauze and a surgical kit. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L251-252)
- She sketched a condom of waterproofed linen treated with something to neutralise infection, to stop the spread of bacterial infection. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L844-850)
- Geneva asked Paige to supply the linen and designed the condoms to be tailored to different species' sizes. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L844-849)
- She tried mixing the [Healers]' poultices into new batches to find an antibiotic to replace penicillin. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L710)
- Geneva was determined to give the United Nations company an antibiotic, and says she would trade her left leg for penicillin. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L713)
- She performed her first Caesarian section on a Centauress, saying only a [Doctor] can do it. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1073)
- Delivering the colt made her feel her purpose as a doctor, and she joked she should have trained as an obstetrician. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1098-1102)
- By the time of Book 11 she has done eight Caesareans, and a mother travelled fifty miles because a [Doctor] was in town. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L615)
- Aiko says Geneva is reluctant to give many Caesareans on principle. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L333-334)
- Geneva designed a protective mask, protective gear and a sealed laboratory for working with Yellow Rivers samples. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L599-601)
- Her laboratory has a metal door and is as airtight as she can make it. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L599)
- Geneva grew cultures of the Yellow Rivers bacteria in glass petri dishes and tested samples Paige brought. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L610,L619-622)
- Her protective armour is brown resin-covered leather, with a red cross painted on the back. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L728)
- Geneva worked up an electrolyte solution to replace fluids lost to Yellow Rivers fever. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L17)
- Geneva requires patient details such as gender, weight and symptoms written on a clipboard for each case. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L288-289)
- A Zeres [Healer] reported that the mould-based penicillin substitute worked on nearly two dozen trial patients. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L3-4)
- {belief} Geneva noted that Occillium was not a good name but she was already using it, and she wanted to name it after the mould. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L7,L14)

## Arguments about healing potions
- {belief} Geneva says that a drop of healing potion makes the Yellow Rivers bacteria multiply wildly and turns a small infection into an open sore. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L831-838)
- She insisted that healers use a drop test: if a potion has no visible effect on a wound, the patient is infected and must be quarantined. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L869)
- She told the Talenqual [Healers] that nothing works against the disease except keeping patients hydrated, clean and rested. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L876)
- Geneva examined and packed a month-old abscess on the [Guard] Ceriche that she says healing potions caused. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L10,L20-29)
- She says the abscess needs surgery that cannot use healing potions, because potions accelerate all parts of the body. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L26,L32)
- {belief} Geneva says potions increase the chance that small preventable ailments become medical emergencies, and are not a cure-all. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L32-34)
