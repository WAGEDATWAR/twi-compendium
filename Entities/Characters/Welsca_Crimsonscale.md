# Welsca Crimsonscale
> Type: person | Also called: Welsca | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.24
> See also: Ilvriss, Erin Solstice, Ryoka Griffin, Olesm
## Overview
- Welsca Crimsonscale is an older red-scaled female Drake with a scar down one arm who serves Wall Lord Ilvriss. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L239-240)
- Welsca is one of Ilvriss's personal adjutants and his best staff member for coordination. (Book 10, Ch6.11 L279; Book 10, Ch6.11 L308)
## Events
- {event} Welsca enters Erin and Ryoka's private bath on orders from Ilvriss, despite their hostile faces. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L237-240; Book 5, Ch4.24 L255)
- {event} Ryoka kicks Welsca, who slips on a wet tile, into a pool filled with Gnolls. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L261)
- {state} Welsca is a former field officer in Eartiv's army, recruited by Ilvriss twelve years earlier, and now a [Veteran Adjutant] above her old [Strategist] class. (Book 10, Ch6.11 L308)
- Ilvriss gives Welsca work on Olesm's chess magazine copies, and she corrects Olesm's mistakes in a game. (Book 10, Ch6.11 L278; Book 10, Ch6.11 L304)
- Ilvriss says Welsca is a fan of the magazine though she would hardly admit it. (Book 10, Ch6.11 L305)
- Welsca remarks on the commentary about the new game of Go that first appeared in Olesm's last issue. (Book 10, Ch6.11 L292)
