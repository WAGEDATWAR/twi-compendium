# Amerys
> Type: person | Also called: Archmage Amerys; Calm Flower of the Battlefield; the mage of the King of Destruction's Seven; Archmage of Chandrar | First appears: Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Illphres, Cognita, Feor, Naili, Aaron Vanwell, Wistram
## Overview
- Amerys is an Archmage of Wistram and one of the King of Destruction's Seven. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L265)
- The students call her the mage of the King of Destruction's Seven, and she belongs to no faction and has never approached one. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L448)
- She is known as the Calm Flower of the Battlefield, one of the King's generals who conquered a continent and threatened the world. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L49)
- Ceria thinks of her as a living legend, the equivalent of any Named Adventurer. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L366)
- Her skin is dark and she is a native Chandrarian. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L895)
- Her levels came from war, unlike the academic Archmages Viltach and Feor. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L890)
## Abilities
- Amerys flies by a flight spell, and she can descend from the sky next to Flos. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L21)
- She stands in the air above the sea and kills a sea serpent with repeated bolts of lightning from her palm. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L240-243)
- Ceria hears her laughing in the middle of the fight. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L241)
- She tells her fellow generals to try not to get in the way of her lightning. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L46)
- Illphres says Amerys uses lightning and air, prefers rapid all-out assaults and does not think, while Illphres plans every move. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L415)
- Illphres says Amerys has about six or eight more levels than she does. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L400)
- Amerys says lightning runs in her body, and that she would have killed Viltach had her cell not been shockproof. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L889)
- She calls herself the most destructive of the Seven in combat and the most fragile. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L924)
## With the King of Destruction
- {fact} Amerys sits among the generals who drink with Flos and Orthenon the night before a battle. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L40)
- Orthenon asks her to attack the left flank and tie it up so the others can focus on the center and right. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L41)
- {fact} She tells Ceria she will not die until her king returns and says Flos is a king worth being a coward for. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L367-370)
- She says she will do all she can to live until the day he calls for her. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L370)
- In captivity she says she often clashed with Orthenon, while she got on well with Mars and liked Drevish. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L920)
- She hears that Drevish was killed. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L920)
- She calls Flos her liege and her king of war. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L367; Book 18, Ch7.29B L922)
- She says Flos said she often reminded him of a cactus that spat needles, and she liked the compliment. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L916-918)

## Captivity
- {state} Amerys is shackled and bound in a Wistram cell, protected by wards and an antimagic field. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L291,L301)
- The Archmages Feor, Naili and Viltach tell her she has refused to eat for a week, and Feor says she bit off the fingers of the mage Teligrain. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L294-298)
- {event} Amerys offers Naili to ally Reim with the Revivalists and give her Wistram if Naili sets her free. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L320)
- {reveal} Her captivity is a secret in Wistram, and only Archmages may see her. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L875)
- {state} Later, she is bound in a magical straightjacket, chained and gagged, because she bit the fingers off a mage serving her food. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L872,L876-878)
- Cognita conveys Amerys's summons of Aaron because the Council has not formally stripped her of her position as Archmage. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L760)
- Amerys tells Aaron she could leave at any time if she renounced her lord, turned her back on Reim and swore in blood to serve only the academy. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L900)
- {belief} She says Flos will free her, even if it takes him razing Wistram stone by stone. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L952)
- She says she clings to sanity because her king is not a patient man. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L957)
- She says she is the only Archmage in Wistram who tells the new arrivals the truth. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L912)
- She asks Aaron to set her free, and he cannot because the magical bindings are beyond powerful. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L926-928)
- Amerys helped Aaron make magicore batteries, and she refuses to remember his name, calling him boy or bratling. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L885,L900,L903-904,L929-930)
- She says High Mage Telim has a kind heart and is a lazy generalist whose genius lies in small spell details such as speech-quills. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L943-946)
- She says Cognita lets her summon only Aaron, and asks what Cognita wants. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L893,L896)
