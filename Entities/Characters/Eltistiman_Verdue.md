# Eltistiman Verdue
> Type: person | Also called: Eltistiman | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.52
> See also: Typhenous, Erin Solstice, Octavia, Jasi, Wesle, Olesm, Players of Celum
## Overview
- Eltistiman Verdue is a [Magician] with bright green hair and blue robes decorated with magical symbols, who does illusions. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L20,L26,L35; Book 14, Ch6.63P L772)
- Eltistiman can cast illusions, is proficient in other spells, and fights fairly well. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L793)
## Book 9: The Door
- {event} Eltistiman appears to Erin as a Dragon, and she punches through the illusion and breaks his nose. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L19-20,L26-27)
- Eltistiman says he hoped to audition for the Players of Celum, and wants to reach Pallass through the door. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L35,L581)
- Olesm tells Eltistiman the door sends only two people at a time and that Pallass requires a request form. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L583-585)
- {reveal} Eltistiman says a [Lady] with fine taste in horticulture hired him to keep the door at the inn. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L849-852)
- {event} Eltistiman takes Typhenous captive at wand-point and has him cuff himself, until a man bursts through the door and beats Eltistiman. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L856-884)
- Typhenous says the Smiling Man hired him and that he did it for the favors owed and his team. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L854,L869)
- {belief} Lyonette says Eltistiman vanished the night the door was stolen, and Olesm thinks he is guilty, with a bounty on his head. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L474-479)
## Players of Celum (Books 14-16)
