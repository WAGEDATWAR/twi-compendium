# Liska
> Type: person | Also called: Ishkr's sister | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.31
> See also: Ishkr, Drassi, Lyonette, Erin Solstice, Turnscales
## Overview
- Liska is Ishkr's younger sister, two years younger than him, whom Drassi describes as difficult. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L1288)
- Liska is a Gnoll with red-brown fur and a sullen manner, who growls something like hello when Ishkr nudges her. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L186-189)
- Ishkr asks Erin about his sister Liska, and Ishkr does not want anyone but himself, or perhaps Erin, to find her. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L1268,L1291)
- {belief} Drassi says Liska gets in trouble because she is often with female friends, which Drassi explains as Turnscales, and she says Ishkr asked her to keep it secret. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L1291-1297)
## Working at the inn (Book 19)
- Liska works the morning shift at the inn behind Ishkr, who insists she say hello. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L186-193)
- Lyonette says Liska has a bad attitude but follows orders, so she is not unmanageable like Maran and Safry. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L195)
- Erin suggests work for Liska where she does not have to smile at people, like loading jobs with the Antinium. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L199)
- Lyonette says Liska might suit writing receipts or secretarial jobs, and that she is rebellious rather than untrustworthy. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L202)
- Lyonette says Liska works better when Lyonette talks to her than when Ishkr does. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L202)
