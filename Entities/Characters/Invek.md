# Invek
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 19, Ch7.43G
> See also: Rags, Redscar, Dolom, Clan Tormek Al, Ogres
## Overview
- Invek is an Ogress and the leader of the Ogre Clan of Tormek Al, in the hills northeast of Invrisil. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L380,L392,L398)
- Invek is a female Ogre who is not as tall as some, equipped with a large double-sided axe enchanted to return to her hand. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L392)
- Invek's mate is dead, killed by the [Lady] of House Ulta. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L408)
- Invek has the best mastery of the common tongue among the Ogres of her clan, and speaks it simply and bluntly. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L413)
- Rags finds Invek reasonable, unlike some Ogres who look down on the much smaller Goblins. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L432)
## Negotiating with Rags
- Invek watches Redscar defeat her clan's champion without Skills, and says that Redscar has an enchanted sword. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L385-395)
- Invek declares that Tormek Al makes peace with the Flood Water Tribe, since Redscar proves himself. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L399)
- Negotiations take six days after an Ogre patrol chances upon Goblinhome and nearly fights the Goblins. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L401)
- Invek agrees that Tormek Al will come to the High Passes, because Humans keep killing Ogres and her clan wants to leave. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L402-406)
- Nearly two hundred Ogres sit in the circle around Invek, and Rags knows her tribe could slaughter ten times its number of Goblins without elites or Hobs. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L400,L409)
- Invek tells Rags that Tourek was a clan leader of Tormek Al who died, a best warrior who killed Gold-ranked Humans. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L425-431)
- Invek shouts that Goblins and Ogres will not fight each other, that they will eat that night, and that gifts will follow the next day. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L433-434)
- Rags gives Invek large nuggets of gold and cut gems from Goblinhome's stash, which delights Invek. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L469-470)
- Invek gives Rags Goblin-sized steel daggers, armour too small for Ogres, and three full sets of plate armour in return. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L471)
- Invek stops smiling when the biggest female Ogre announces that she and fifteen others want to join Rags' tribe. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L476-477)
- Invek is displeased by the Ogres wanting to join Rags' tribe, and consents to talk about it despite her annoyance. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L482,L502)
- Invek smirks that Ogre shamans make Ogres superior, and dismisses Snapjaw's [Burning Blade] as not a big fire. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L528-533)
- Invek is happy to receive eight Ogres' weight of meat from Rags. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L542)
- Invek's eyes lock onto Rags' mana potions, and Rags gives her one in a trade for friendship. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L544,L551)
- Invek smacks a badly bleeding young Ogre who was cut by a Redfang, telling him the Goblins are friends. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L559-560)
- Invek touches her fist to Rags' hand when the Goblins leave, agreeing to meet again in the Highest Mountains. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L574-576)
