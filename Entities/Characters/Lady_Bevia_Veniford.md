# Lady Bevia Veniford
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Bevia; Bevia | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.11E
> See also: Rael, Laken Godart, Tyrion Veltras, Lady Rie, Riverfarm
## Overview
- Lady Bevia Veniford is the matriarch of the Venifords and the aunt of Rael. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L6)
- Bevia is an old, high-level [Lady] who has survived two husbands and both Antinium Wars. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L6)
- Bevia is adept at social graces and brings the Venifords income through trade ties, to Rael's anger at her dealing with [Merchants]. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L6)
- Laken thinks Bevia is much more intelligent than her nephew, and she sounds two decades younger than her age. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L326; Book 8, Ch5.31G L370)
