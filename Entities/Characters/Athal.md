# Athal
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.09K
> See also: Baosar, Trey Atwood, Gazi, A'ctelios Salash
## Overview
- Athal is a young man of A'ctelios who serves as one of the escorts guiding Trey Atwood through the Carven City. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L733)
- Athal is tall, pale-eyed and sharp-toothed like the rest of A'ctelios' folk, and is lively and fascinated by outside visitors. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L733)
- Athal calls Baosar, the ruler of A'ctelios, his father. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L769-771)
## Guide to A'ctelios
- Athal shows Trey the Trade Quarter in the head of A'ctelios, where outsiders live and trade temporarily. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L734,L745-747)
- Athal tells Trey that A'ctelios sells meat, though few outsiders buy it, and mostly leather and bone. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L751)
- Athal proudly shows Trey a [Butcher]'s counter where meat of A'ctelios costs four coppers for a family-sized steak dinner. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L760-767)
- Baosar scolds Athal for tempting a guest of the Quarass with meat, and Athal bows his head and apologises. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L769-774)
- Athal says A'ctelios has crime and quarrels, and uses appointed peacekeepers instead of a City Watch. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L776-778)
- Athal has lived in A'ctelios all his life, has no wish to leave, and expects to die there. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L783-787)
- Athal is fascinated by Trey's iPhone and compares it to the scrying orbs that merchants have been selling in the city. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L790-795)
- Athal keeps children from swarming Trey's iPhone, and warns him that children would pry it away in an instant. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L798-799)
- Athal hurries Trey away from a window where a baby is crying, and begs Trey not to tell Baosar he took him there. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L803-807)
- Athal explains the baby's strange cry by saying it was born with illness and the shapers will fix it. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L810)
- Athal tells Trey that outsiders do not travel below the first layer of the city, and that only natives pass the checkpoint of bone-armoured peacekeepers. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L813-814)
## The banquet and the pakeil
- Athal eats steak in front of the others at the meeting of the Shield Kingdoms, taunting some of the younger Garuda and Humans. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L859)
- Athal and other young people from A'ctelios try to press food, wine and water on the guests, which seems to be a game among them. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L856,L876)
- Athal leads Trey, Gazi and the Quarass to a private room when Baosar asks for them. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L955-956)
- After Trey is covered in blood, Athal's cheerfulness drains, and the Quarass sends him off so that he practically runs away. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L1155-1158)
- When a pakeil charges Trey, Athal calls out for Gazi to stop, and Trey recognises that Athal had named the creature a pakeil. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L1182-1183,L1201)
- Athal backs away when Gazi points her sword at his face after Trey finds a human backpack in the pakeil's flesh. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L1195-1197)
- Athal tells Baosar the pakeil attacked Trey first, and then says the pakeil are not people but outsiders. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L1214-1220)
- {event} Athal screams that Trey has attacked Baosar, and the peacekeepers draw their weapons until Baosar raises a hand. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L1237-1239)
- During the escape from the Carven City, Athal leaps snarling at Trey and Gazi from above, and Trey blasts him away with three [Light Arrows]. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L1285,L1291)
