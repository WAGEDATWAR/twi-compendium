# Lyfelt
> Type: person | Also called: Minister Lyfelt; Prime Minister of Belchan | First appears: Ch6.15K
> See also: Raelt, Flos, Perric, Geril
## Overview
- Lyfelt is the elected [Prime Minister] of Belchan, a career [Politician] who consolidated his class into [Minister]. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L192-193)
- Lyfelt has a pleasant, soothing voice and a charisma that is partly inborn and partly from his class. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L192)
- Lyfelt has served for six years as [Prime Minister] and is new enough to find power intoxicating. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L208)
- Raelt and Lyfelt speak through a marble bust and mirror that cost Jecrass 28,413 gold. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L183)
- The bust lets Raelt and Lyfelt talk instantly without trusting a [Mage] with [Message] spells. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L184)
- Raelt warns Lyfelt that Flos is no one to play games with, since he knew Flos when he was becoming the King of Destruction. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L207)
- {belief} Lyfelt says Claiven and Medain hate the King of Destruction too much to worry about Belchan except as bulwarks. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L209)
- Lyfelt says Claiven and Medain keep sending him [Messages] about a defensive pact. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L209)
- Lyfelt says Perric of Medain knows Lyfelt has a [Court Mage] and that Claiven has also reached out to him. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L213)
- Raelt says Lyfelt told him Reim's worries lie south, where rulers are gathering. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L247-251)
- {belief} Lyfelt is content to let the gathering do whatever it is doing, Raelt says. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L251)
- {belief} Lyfelt tells Raelt that the Manticore hunt serves Belchan, since he had time to consider how to use Flos best. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L735-737)
- Lyfelt says he is a [Politician] and that events like this help him and Belchan. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L739)
- {belief} Lyfelt says Flos is clever about goals but is a novice at politics. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L744)
- Lyfelt says he has allowed refugees through the border but his aid to Flos ends there. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L762)
- Lyfelt says the refugees are much hated in Belchan and he thinks he can easily win re-election this year. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L762)
- Lyfelt uses a Skill to calm Raelt in conversation, though it does not work. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L734)
- {belief} Lyfelt claims he did not order the attack on the refugees when Raelt tells him they were slaughtered. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L1207)
- Raelt tells Lyfelt that children were hacked apart and the rest were being sold as slaves. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L1208)
