# Yisht
> Type: person | Also called: Scalespeaker Yisht | First appears: Book 19, Ch7.52
> See also: Lism, Krshia Silverfang, Hectval, Liscor Council
## Overview
- Yisht is the Scalespeaker of Hectval, a Drake who leads the Hectval delegation to Liscor. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L1048,L1052)
## Visit to Liscor (Book 19)
- {event} Yisht rushes to Lism at the reception, gushing with gratitude and apologising, and says that morning he thought Liscor a cesspool. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L1048-1049)
- Yisht says the fault was his, due to misconceptions, and promises proper gifts to match Liscor's hospitality. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L1054)
- Yisht says Liscor's election was a damned shame and that Lism did his best to keep the Council from being stolen. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L787)
- {belief} Yisht says three Gnolls on Liscor's Council is as bad as the Council being stolen, though it is just as well they are not a majority. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L789)
- {belief} Yisht tells Lism it is a disgrace that Gnolls took over the city, and says Gnolls bring more theft and troublemaking and smell bad. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1010)
- Yisht says Hectval keeps its Gnoll population regulated with separate districts, and that if Liscor's election was redrawn the old Council did it to save seats. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1010)
- Yisht says Hectval's laws make citizenship hard for Gnolls, which Lism reacts to with thirteen years. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1011-1013)
- Yisht says that with the King of Destruction and the tribes restless, Hectval no longer kicks Gnolls out but institutes the right laws. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1013)
- Yisht says Hectval's Drake households above a certain wealth have Paworker Gnolls as [Carers], [Cleaners] and [Porters], put in uniform and given a collar. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1013-1015)
- Yisht calls the collar a sign of affection and says Liscor was thought a lost cause with the Antinium. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1018)
- {belief} Yisht calls Humans the enemy and the last straw, and disgusting, loudmouthed and arrogant. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1038,L1115)
