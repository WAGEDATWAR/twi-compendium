# Leafarmor
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.05P
> See also: Raidpear, Ulvama, Pebblesnatch, Garen Redfang, Redfang Warriors
## Overview
- Leafarmor is a female Hobgoblin and a loyal Redfang warrior, whose name Pebblesnatch learns from another Goblin. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L296,L641)
- Leafarmor once made armor out of leaves after being separated from her tribe in a bad attack and wounded. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L642)
- Leafarmor killed her way back to the Redfangs through a dozen Eater Goats, and Garen Redfang gave her the nickname. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L642)
- Leafarmor had once been an enemy of Raidpear, who defected to the Flooded Waters tribe, before both ended up in the same tribe. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L386)
## Captive with Pebblesnatch
- Leafarmor sharpens her spear after Raidpear says a dead Goldstone Chieftain was strong. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L386-393)
- Leafarmor throws axes, and sews leaf scale mail from stiff, thick leaves, a skill she calls [Tough Leaves]. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L643-649)
- Leafarmor shows Pebblesnatch a wrapleaf, which curls around her hand and leaves burrs in her skin, then helps remove them. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L754-759)
- Leafarmor explains to Pebblesnatch what Ulvama meant, and grins in a way that is not a smile. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1007)
- Ulvama paints sigils on Leafarmor's face, and Leafarmor is very impressed. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1036-1049)
- Leafarmor swings up her axe as the Goblins wait while Humans argue with the Emperor's soldiers. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1206)
