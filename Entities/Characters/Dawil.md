# Dawil
> Type: person | Also called: Dawil Ironbreaker; the Dwarf of the Silver Swords | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.45
> See also: Falene, Ylawes Byres, Yvlon, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Dawil Ironbreaker is a Dwarf of the Ironbreaker family and a member of the Gold-rank adventuring team the Silver Swords, with Ylawes and Falene. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L93,L163)
- Dawil is a few inches over five feet tall, taller than Erin expected of a Dwarf. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L126)
- Dawil says he has a bit of Human blood from a great-great-great grandmother, but insists he is a pure Dwarf in spirit. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L127-129)
- Dawil is thirty-eight years old and feels Humans are very young. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L126)
- Dawil has the [Axe Champion] class, which he won while dead drunk in a competition, though he fights with a hammer. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L558; Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1379)
- Dawil calls the class the wrong specialization, though the Skill is nice. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L558)
## Role on the team
- Dawil says he hits things with his hammer, Ylawes with his sword, and Falene shoots spells, so the team charges in and sorts things out afterwards. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L163)
- Dawil lectures Erin on blacksmithing, calling adamantium, orichalcum and star metal mostly hype. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L223-225)
- Dawil says that anyone offering to work adamantium into armor is either lying or Level 50. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L225)
- {belief} Dawil tells Ylawes that women are like metamorphological magic: neither of them understands it, and blundering about only makes things worse with his sister. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L112-116)
- Dawil uses [Thunder Blow] with his hammer to snap a giant moth's leg. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L513-515)
- Dawil describes himself as the voice of reason on the team, which has no tactical mind. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L141-144)
- Dawil knocks Yvlon out with the Silver Swords to keep her from entering the dungeon, saying she will need tying down. (Book 8, Ch5.39 L276-284)
- Dawil calls Falene a half-wit when she tries to buy Numbtongue's mana stones, and apologises that she is slow. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L465-468)
- Dawil nicknames Falene "Pointy" while saying goodbye to Erin. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L433)
## The siege of Liscor
- Dawil tells Erin that the Silver Swords, Halfseekers, Griffon Hunt and probably the Horns plan to leave Liscor, and not to tell the Watch Captain. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L109,L122)
- Dawil says the orange stone is already in the door's bowl and the Flamewardens are carrying the door south. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L119)
- Dawil says adventurers are big targets in wars, being too fragile to defend from all sides, and the Gold-rank teams will leave when the army arrives. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L129,L146-151)
- Dawil rebukes Falene for calling bounties a historical way of culling populations. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L578-579)
- Dawil kicks Ylawes in the shins, telling him not to carry his sister off like a damsel. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L272-273)
- {state} Dawil leaves Liscor going north with Ylawes and Falene, saying "Farewell. Let's all meet again someday." (Book 9, Ch5.60 L291,L298)
## Later

- Dawil tells Ylawes that Terandria has a hundred knight orders, far more than Izril, when discussing joining an order. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L49-54)
