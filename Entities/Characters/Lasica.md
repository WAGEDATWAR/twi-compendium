# Lasica
> Type: person | Also called: Lasica Feltail | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.09
> See also: Rufelt, Erin Solstice, Grimalkin, Lism
## Overview
- Lasica Feltail is a middle-aged female Drake with bright purple scales, and she introduces Rufelt as her husband. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L467-468)
- Lasica is a [Chef] who owns the kitchen of Tails and Scales, the Pallass bar that Rufelt runs, in a partnership with him. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L761; Book 10, Ch6.10 L92)
- Lasica says she and Rufelt both hold rare versions of their classes, the kind a person only gets past Level 30. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L144)
- Lasica is known for making food that makes people shed weight, or gives them energy all day. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L124)
