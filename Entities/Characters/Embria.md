# Embria
> Type: person | Also called: Embria Grasstongue; Wing Commander Embria | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.13
> See also: Relc, Zevara, Olesm, Lism, Krshia, Vell, Wikir, Ilvriss, Selys, Yellow Splatters
## Overview
- Embria Grasstongue is a young Drake [Wing Commander] and the 4th Company Commander of Liscor's army. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L93,L165)
- Embria has light red scales, later described as crimson, and carries a spear enchanted with lightning. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L93; Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L2)
- Embria is younger than Zevara and had last visited Liscor six years before her arrival. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L93,L207)
- Embria's [Commander] rank gives her autonomy and authority, and [Wing Commander] is roughly equivalent to [Major]. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L22-23)
- Embria's 4th Company has ninety-seven soldiers in total, all of whom are Drakes and Gnolls. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L14)
- {belief} A Pallass observer states that Embria's main combat class is [Spear Hunter]. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L733)
- Embria is a [Spearmaster] who learned her spear work from Relc, according to Captain Pielt. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L328)
- Embria uses an Evercut spear that halts regeneration and slows clotting, which she says is not the best but served her for years. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L673-676)
- Embria killed her first Wyvern at eighteen, while Relc killed his at twenty-six. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L924)
- Embria copied Relc's vault trick, which took her a decade to learn, and unlike him she has used it in battle. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L823-825)
## Family
- Embria is Relc's daughter by a [Major] to whom he was not married, and her mother had pink scales. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L445-446,L721; Book 9, Ch5.60 L682)
- Embria grew up travelling with Liscor's army, which Pallass observers assume meant she was raised by both parents. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L721)
- {reveal} Relc introduces Embria publicly to Erin as his daughter. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L486)
- Embria's mother died in service, and Embria says she died a hero. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L654-656)
- {belief} Embria says Goblins killed her mother, and refuses to make peace with them. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L729)
- Embria demands that Relc call her by rank while they are on duty. (Book 8, Ch5.44 L372-376)
- Embria asks her father why it is so hard to do her job, calling him Dad. (Book 8, Ch5.44 L436)
- Embria thinks her father is a disappointment and wants him to reenlist, which Relc refuses. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L684,L705-709)
- Embria gives Relc her will, which leaves her possessions to him, as is standard practice before a big battle. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L728-735)
- Embria tells Relc that his name and a weapon worthy of a commander were awarded to him because he hunted down enemy commanders. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L709)
- Embria and Relc reconcile while she practises spear forms, and Relc copies her movements in step with her. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L763-772)
- Embria holds Relc's hand as they walk to the inn and recalls running away when she was thirteen. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L870-879)
- Relc tells Embria he left the army out of fear and unhappiness, and refuses once more to return. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L890-894)
- Embria says it is the last time she will ask Relc to return. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L897)
- Relc agrees to attend morning practice, and Embria says the time has not changed. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L999-1002)
- Embria ends the day thinking she had always had a father and now has a friend. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L1009)
- Embria and Relc have weekly father-daughter get-togethers at the inn. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L128)
- Embria brings back her father's spear, saying she found Soot but not Bearclaw. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L73)
- Embria drinks in the inn in a depressed state after her father is reassigned. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L129)
## Arrival at Liscor
- Embria's army was besieging Zeres under contract to Oteslia and was delayed in getting to Liscor. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L155)
- Embria's orders were to return to Liscor and defend it. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L155-156)
- Embria tells the Redfang Hobs that all Walled Cities raised Goblin bounties fivefold after Zel Shivertail's death. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L786)
- Embria says her army eradicated two small Goblin tribes on the way. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L788)
- {belief} Embria says the Human nobility are mobilising against smaller tribes and that no Goblin will survive near Liscor once the Human with the large army moves. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L790)
- Embria tells the Redfangs to run while they can, since everyone in the world is hunting them. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L792)
- Embria asks Selys to put the breastplate in the city's custody, and Selys refuses. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L546-550)
