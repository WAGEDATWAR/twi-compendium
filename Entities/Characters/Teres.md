# Teres
> Type: person | Also called: Teresa Atwood; Teresa; Lady Atwood | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00K
> See also: Trey, Flos Reimarch, Orthenon, Mars, Gazi, Fetohep, Reim
## Overview
- Teresa Atwood prefers to be called Teres and is the twin sister of Trey Atwood. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L95)
- Teres and Trey are sixteen, nearly seventeen, and come from England, with moderately dark blonde hair and fair skin. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L92-93)
- Teres and Trey are both 5'10 tall, and are fraternal twins rather than identical. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L93-94)
- Teres and Trey are close enough that they can usually understand what the other is thinking, and mirror each other's emotions. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L91; Book 5, Ch4.00K L110)
- Teres is summoned to this world with Trey and is one of Flos's two wards and personal attendants. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L78,L88,L109)
- Teres and Trey come from another world, which Flos says tells of armies and weapons that would humble the greatest monsters, and it is they who reignited his flame. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L109)

## Arrival and first months (Book 2-5)
- Teres and Trey fell into Flos's throne room after stepping off a Tube subway train's ledge expecting to reach the platform. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L104)
- Flos, sitting alone in a small chair in the empty throne room, was woken by their arrival after twenty years of slumber. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L103-105)
- Teres and Trey had been in this world little more than a month by the time of the war with the Emperor of Sands. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L102)
- Orthenon nearly ran Trey through before Flos stopped his men, and then the twins told the court everything. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L106)
- Teres and Trey do not hold a servant class; Orthenon tells them to think of themselves as aides, expected to become bodyguards and assistants. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L121)
- Teres and Trey are required to follow Flos around, mostly watching him attend to the kingdom. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L121)
- {belief} Teres claims that Mars's breasts are fake. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L321-324)
- Teres usually starts the twins' fights and is the first to pop her lid when they get angry. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L458)
- Teres and Trey openly refuse Flos's order, shouting that they are not his subjects and that he is wrong. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L452-457)
- Flos laughs ruefully and thanks Teres and Trey for speaking the truth, apologizing for demanding the opposite. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L475)
- Teres and Trey wake at dawn for training; Teres takes Trey's pillow away to wake him, and stands swaying with her eyes closed. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L2-4,L12)
- Teres trains every day away from the courtyard with Orthenon and comes back looking like she ran a marathon. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L47)

## Book 7-10: Rift with Trey
- Teres and Trey fought with Flos and each other over Flos selling the first army he crushed to slavers, and the rift lasted months. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L64-65)
- {state} Teres is a Level 18 [Warrior] taught by Orthenon, soon to become a [Swordswoman], and spars with Flos and Mars. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L66)
- Teres explains television to Flos, who listens with growing interest, and she curses Trey for leaving her behind. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L304-307)
- Teres and Trey feel like friends or siblings but not like twins, and Trey cannot tell what Teres is thinking. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L258-259)
- Teres gave Trey a full account of what had happened with Flos, the Quarass and Hellios. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L258)
- Teres tells Trey that Gazi was once a slave whom Flos found and raised, and who became one of his Seven by killing many people. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L273-275)
- Teres is with Flos at Blalevault, where she asks Mars whether all her illusions have huge breasts. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L494)

## Book 13-15: Reim's march
- Teres rides with Flos while Trey goes with the Quarass to Khelt, since Flos thinks Trey the better [Diplomat]. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L614-621)
- Teres's iPhone is recharged by a [Repair] spell cast by Ulyse, leader of the [Mage]-group Parasol Stroll, after both twins' phones had died within two days of arrival. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L166-168)
- Teres photographs Flos on the balcony as returning refugees approach, and he stiffens into an old-photograph pose. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L181-198)
- Teres urges Flos to smile or point, and he gives his fakest smile, and the flash makes the picture a work of art in the hall of failure. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L190-198)
- Teres is one of the King of Destruction's personal servants and befriends a Garuda [Soldier] named Mefaa, who rose from Level 2 to Level 13 in under two weeks. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L780-781)
- Flos forbade Teres to fight Manticores, since they are above her level. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L782)
- Teres likes Orthenon, and Orthenon tells her she is to be Flos's bodyguard rather than a [Commander]. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L115-117)
- {state} Teres wears a slightly curved sword at her hip and is addressed as Lady Atwood. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L118,L164)
- Teres was haunted by the people she killed with a sword after the battle at Reim, but she no longer dreams of her kills. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L16-17)
- Teres rides a mare she named Frecklehooves, and this was her sixth major engagement, at the border of Jecrass. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L17-18,L28)
- Flos was offended at the name Frecklehooves. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L28)

## Book 19: Khelt
- Teres arrives freshly bathed in Fetohep's court as the only outsider present, and denies she saw Vampires. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L14-16,L46)
- Teres tells Fetohep that Trey is on special business she cannot name, and that Flos told her to take a break and placate him. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L73)
- Teres says that Flos did not have Trey fight in the war, and that peace talks are under way. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L73)
- Teres tells Fetohep that Gazi is gone, and that Trey and two of Parasol Stroll's [Mages] are heading to a harbor in Nerrhavia's Fallen. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L184)
