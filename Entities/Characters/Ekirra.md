# Ekirra
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.06
> See also: Visma, Mrsha, Jeva, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Ekirra is a Gnoll cub with red fur and a black splotch across one eye, around Mrsha's age, who can talk. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L306-309)
- Ekirra is the son of the Gnoll Jeva, and is excitable and a great deal less shy than his friends. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L305,L308,L343)
- Ekirra has an older sister who brings him places and makes him use utensils at the table. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L257,L260)
## Friendship with Mrsha and Visma
- Ekirra races excitedly into Krshia's room calling Mrsha's name and asking to go to the park to play with her and show his new ball. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L306-310)
- {event} Ekirra's new ball is stolen by Visma, who calls it hers; when he demands it back she punches him in the nose and runs off. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L348-363)
- Ekirra is stunned and left sitting on the ground, though his nose is not bleeding. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L364)
- Ekirra has a special ball with stars on it that glow in the dark, a birthday present he waited a long time for. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Gold L245,L254-255)
- Ekirra's new ball replaces an old, ratty leather one that his sister had owned. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Gold L255)
- {event} Ekirra is dismayed when he sees that Mrsha's magical red ball also glows in the dark and can come back on its own. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Gold L254-255)
- {event} Ekirra, Mrsha and Visma are about to surprise Erin when they overhear her cursing mildly, and Ekirra repeats "Damn!" (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L57-58)
- Visma tells Erin that Ekirra learned a bad word and that his mother got mad when he said it. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L73)
- Ekirra and Visma are Mrsha's best friends, and Erin is trying to make playdates a regular thing. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L74)
- {event} Ekirra acts as the neutral party and explains to Erin that Mrsha hit Visma for pulling Apista's wings. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L231-232)
- Ekirra paces anxiously while Mrsha tries to apologize to Visma, then cries along with his two friends, and the three share chocolate. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L241-248)
- {event} Ekirra and Visma come to breakfast at the inn, brought by their older sister and brother, and Ryoka meets them for the first time. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L255-257)
- Ekirra puts a crepe on his head, licks syrup off his arm fur and stares at Ryoka as she eats. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L257,L265)
