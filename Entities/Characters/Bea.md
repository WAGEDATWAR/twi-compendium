# Bea
> Type: person | Also called: Bea (Chosen); Plague Zombie Woman, One Of Az'Kerash Chosen | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.25
> See also: Az'kerash, Venitra, Kerash, Ijvani, Oom, Zel Shivertail
## Overview
- Bea is one of Az'kerash's undead servants, a rotting woman who stands with Venitra and Kerash in his castle. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L402)
- Bea is one of Az'kerash's chosen and a spellcaster, the other besides Ijvani. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L350)
- Bea's body is rotting and putrefying, though she looks much like a normal Human woman. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L359; Book 6, Ch4.49 L306)
- Bea was not designed personally by Az'kerash as Venitra was, but was either reanimated from the dead or self-forming. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L124)
## Appearances
- {belief} After Az'kerash lets Ryoka go, voices identifiable as Venitra, Kerash and Bea say his decision may be in error, and agree to hold her. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L402-407)
- {event} Bea holds an image of Ryoka running through the snow and tells Az'kerash where she is. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L349-350)
- {event} Bea touches Zel Shivertail's arm, giving him a magical plague that a healing potion then spreads. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L322-329)
- Zel's claw takes off Bea's arm as she retreats, and she picks it up. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L324,L328)
- Az'kerash praises Bea for the touch, and Bea mockingly names the plague. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L327,L330-331)
- Zel cuts Bea in two and stomps on her, after Venitra lands on her and breaks her body. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L344,L361-365)
- {event} Bea asks Az'kerash about the particulars of intercourse, which he tries to forget, and Venitra later asks him about it. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L143-150)
