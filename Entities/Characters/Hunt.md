# Hunt
> Type: person | Also called: a Horn of Hammerad | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.00H
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Gerial, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Hunt is one of the most senior members of the Horns of Hammerad, with a mace and a beard. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L5,L45,L122)
- Hunt has egg in his beard at supper, which Gerial does not mention. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L45)
- Hunt growls and grips his mace when he sees another group of armed adventurers sitting by a fire. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L121-122)
- {state} Hunt is left out of commission but alive after the first ambush. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L118)
- {state} Later, Erin finds Hunt lying still and dead among the bodies on the Ruins' second floor. (Book 2, Ch2.04 L354)
