# Hekra
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.28
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Stone Spears Tribe
## Overview
- Hekra is a Gnoll of the Stone Spears Tribe who carries a bow and hunts in the winter forest. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L154-156)
- Hekra moves so unseen that Ryoka's heart skips when she steps out from behind a slim tree. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L154-155)
- Hekra greets Ryoka by name and says she is hunting, though not for game, as there is none to be found. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L156-159)
- Hekra asks Ryoka whether she was talking with the Winter Sprites. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L161)
- Hekra fights the Goblins as an archer with three other archers. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L324)
- Hekra's hand blurs as she shoots, and two Goblins fall with arrows in their chests. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L324-325)
