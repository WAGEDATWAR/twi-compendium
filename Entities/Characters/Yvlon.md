# Yvlon Byres
> Type: person | Also called: Yvlon; Miss Byres; the Wounded Warrior; the Silversteel Armsmistress | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.11R
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Pisces, Ksmvr, Ylawes Byres, Magnolia Reinhart, Skinner, Toren, Horns of Hammerad
## Overview
- Yvlon Byres is a Human adventurer, once Captain of the Silver Spears and later a member of the Horns of Hammerad. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L184)
- Yvlon is the Third Daughter of House Byres, a small noble house in Izril that mines silver. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L191; Book 18, Ch7.37 L999)
- Yvlon was a [Warrior], later a [Wounded Warrior], and by Book 14 is a [Silversteel Armsmistress]. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L736)
