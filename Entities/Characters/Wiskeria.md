# Wiskeria
> Type: person | Also called: Witch Wiskeria; General Wiskeria; Miss Wiskeria; Wis | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.20E
> See also: Laken, Durene, Beniar, Belavierr, Califor, Mavika, Odveig, Celestial Trackers, Coven, Rehanna, Ryoka
## Overview
- Wiskeria is a young [Witch] with spectacles and a pointed hat, which she wears even indoors. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L39; Book 5, Ch4.21E L296)
- Wiskeria is a few years older than Durene and wears a long dark-blue robe, a hat and spectacles. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L147)
- Wiskeria tugs her pointed hat lower when she is thinking. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L260)
- Wiskeria brews very strong, minty tea and carries it in canteens at her saddlebags. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L139)
- Wiskeria's dark-blue hat has grown with her since she was six, and her mother sewed a charm into it. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L599-601)
## Celestial Trackers
- Wiskeria is a [Witch], a specialised type of [Mage] who hinders opponents with spells, brews potions and assists friends. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L86)
- Wiskeria is subordinate to Odveig in the Celestial Trackers but seems to be the brains behind the group. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L86-87)
- Wiskeria is the only truly specialised spellcaster in either hired adventuring group, and she cannot throw fireballs. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L31)
- Wiskeria and Odveig are old friends, and Wiskeria joined the Celestial Trackers only about two months earlier. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L264; Book 5, Ch4.22E L267)
- Wiskeria can manipulate animals, once pulling a Corusdeer's fur and later summoning a herd to fight Snow Golems. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L268-269)
- Wiskeria hesitates when Laken asks whether she has already sent word to Invrisil that he is an [Emperor], and she apologises. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L314-318)
- {belief} Wiskeria explains, as hearsay, that a Goblin tribe left rescued women outside Merendia's gates and that a Great Goblin Chieftain lives on Dwarfhalls Rest. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L40-49)
- Wiskeria says the Goblins left in the mountain raid with Hobgoblins and real weapons after another tribe splintered away. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L49)
- Wiskeria pulls the arrow from the poisoned Beniar and applies a mash. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L449-450; Book 5, Ch4.21E L462)
- Wiskeria pounds roots, dried bugs and an egg into a paste that neutralises the poison in Beniar. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L13)
- Wiskeria admits she took Mossbear fur while the creature slept, claiming it was a precaution, and says Odveig insisted on the escort contract. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L286; Book 5, Ch4.22E L340-342)
- Wiskeria says the Celestial Trackers normally take monster-extermination and scouting contracts, plus occasional requests from the Reinhart family. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L340)
- {event} Wiskeria uses [Paralyzing Touch] on Laken in order to escape. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L326-327)
- Wiskeria gives four-in-ten odds that a party planting Laken's markers is attacked, if it moves fast and keeps to the open. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L74-76)
- Laken orders Wiskeria to take Durene and plant seven markers four miles from Riverfarm. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L77-79)
- Wiskeria apologises to Laken for casting a spell on him, hiding the Mossbear fur and Odveig's actions, and says the fur was meant to drive the Mossbear off. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L189-192)
- {belief} Wiskeria believes the Celestial Trackers are finished and that she cannot return to Invrisil. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L369-371)
