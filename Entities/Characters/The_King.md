# The King
> Type: person | Also called: Flos; the King of Destruction | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.07R
> See also: Free Queen
## Overview
- A narrator describes a warlord who conquered a whole continent and was sweeping through the next when his empire suddenly collapsed; the books never say why, and though he was not killed or harmed, he abandoned world conquest. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L307)
- That king now sits in a crumbling kingdom while other countries pick apart his empire, and he is called the King of Destruction. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L307)
## Description
- In the Interlude, the King is an older man in his forties, with red-gold hair beginning to streak with grey, a muscled physique and emerald eyes. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L6,L10)
- The King sits in a smaller chair across from the empty golden throne in a cracked, decaying throne room, wearing worn, finely woven gold-embroidered robes over chainmail. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L5,L8-9,L11)
- The King tells two young strangers from another world that he knows they are telling the truth, not just because of a Skill. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L17)
## History and return
- The King describes his empire as once packed with people of every nation, with goods from afar and armies marching, until he abandoned his dreams of conquest and let the nation he had built collapse. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L33-35)
- The King's city below his balcony is crumbling, its people malnourished and its food rotten. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L34)
- {event} Moved by the twins' news of a greater world, the King takes up his throne, tells his steward Orthenon that he has returned, and calls his kingdom to wake. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L65,L83-87)
- Orthenon reports that the kingdom is failing: the treasury is empty, crops have failed, armories are rusted, nearly every decent soldier has fled and foreign armies hold the vassals' lands. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L75,L81)
- {event} The King orders his vassals to dispose of the foreign forces within two days and rejoin him with warriors and youths of worth. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L83)
- {event} The King names the twins his personal attendants and bodyguards. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L101)
- {reveal} The Interlude ends with the word spreading across the countryside and the world that the King of Destruction, Flos, has returned. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L110-111)
