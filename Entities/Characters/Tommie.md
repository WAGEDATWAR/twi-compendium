# Tommie
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.64
> See also: Walt, Belt, Ceria Springwalker, Yvlon, Ksmvr, Creler
## Overview
- Tommie is a human [Warrior] on Walt's all-male [Warrior] team, the Ensoldier Shields. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L792,L796,L870; Book 14, Ch6.66H L268)
- Tommie's closest teammate is Belt, another [Warrior] who fights beside him. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L870-873; Book 14, Ch6.66H L269)
## The Bloodfields
- Tommie grumbles to Walt that the Horns are eating snacks while riding their chariot, while the Ensoldier Shields march on foot. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L795-797)
- Tommie and Belt are turned by pollen and walk into the Bloodfields, and Tommie is dragged towards the spore sacs by a root. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L870-876)
- A Watchertree spears Tommie's right leg and shoulder with roots as he tries to heal himself with a potion. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L887-889)
- Ksmvr leaps in, and Yvlon cuts the roots with her Sword of Weight so that Ksmvr drags Tommie free. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L981,L998)
- Yvlon lifts Tommie into the Horns' chariot as they flee. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1003)
- Tommie and Belt look half-dead even after potions, but are later on their feet with holes in their armour. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L1047; Book 14, Ch6.65 L202-203)
## The Creler fight
- Tommie says he owes the Horns one and proposes killing the adult Creler by attacking it from the inside. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L265,L269)
- Tommie uses [Armored Leap] to jump into the adult Creler's mouth, with Belt covering him with [Target Shield]. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L273-278)
- Tommie is covered by baby Crelers pouring out of the adult's stomach, and his corpse is shaken out of the Creler's mouth. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L281-282)
- Later, Terrim of Walt's team remarks that Tommie would have been proud of the jump that took down the adult Creler. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L408-409)
