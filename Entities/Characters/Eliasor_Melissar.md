# Eliasor Melissar
> Type: person | Also called: Eliasor; Lady Eliasor | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.06KM
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Lady Zanthia, Thomast, Bethal
## Overview
- Eliasor Melissar is the daughter of Lady Patricia Melissar and is the last of her line, becoming a [Lady] of Izril. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L515; Book 11, Ch6.27M L501,L519)
- Eliasor's father died in the Sacrifice of Roses before she was born, and her mother Patricia had walked the same trial and survived. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L496-498)
## Arc
- {event} Magnolia Reinhart announces after Lady Patricia's murder that her daughter Eliasor is the new head of the Melissar family and asks the room to welcome her as a peer. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L515)
- Bethal curtsies to Eliasor first, and Magnolia and the other [Lords] and [Ladies] follow. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L516)
- {event} Eliasor sees her mother's head and the [Assassin] fall to the [Chevalier] Thomast's blade at the ball. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L499)
- {state} Magnolia tells Eliasor that she is now a [Lady] of Izril whose line begins and ends with her, and that she must rise. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L501)
- Eliasor gains her class after her mother's death, though her mother had expected it when she turned ten or later. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L502)
- Eliasor stops eating after her mother's death, so her servants call for Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L504)
- {state} Later, Eliasor becomes a ward of Lady Zanthia, who is sent by Magnolia, and studies with six other young [Ladies]. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L505-506)
- Eliasor resents Zanthia's strict discipline and has studied under her for two months. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L508-510)
- Eliasor was ten years old at the time of the attack on the street. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L551)
- {event} Eliasor tells Zanthia she does not want to be a [Lady], but Zanthia says she has the class and no right to refuse. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L517-519)
- {event} Eliasor stands still when an [Assassin] swings at her in the street, saying a [Lady] faces death without fear. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L550-551)
- {event} Zanthia blocks the blade an inch from Eliasor's neck, embraces her, and says she will be as great a [Lady] as her mother. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L552-554)
- Zanthia promises Eliasor vengeance for her mother's death, whether by Magnolia's hand, Zanthia's or her own. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L615)
