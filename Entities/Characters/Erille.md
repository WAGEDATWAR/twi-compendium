# Erille
> Type: person | Also called: Princess Erille | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.03C
> See also: Fool, Tom, Blighted King, Nereshal, Lady Zekyria
## Overview
- Erille is the youngest [Princess] of the Blighted Kingdom, a girl in a violet dress who wants to learn backflips from the Fool. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L337-340,L400)
- Erille is the half-sister of Princess Isodore. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L398)
- Erille speaks directly to Tom and says her father claims the Fool has hidden depths. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L336-340)
- Erille hides behind the Fool and Tom like any kid her age, and is fond of the Fool. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L401,L407-409)
## Evading her guards
- Erille wears two rings, one of which hides her from Nereshal's scrying, and she evades her guards. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L396-398)
- Isodore warns her that her guards are searching in a panic and that Nereshal was about to scry for her. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L396,L399)
- Erille reluctantly comes out from behind the Fool and returns to the palace with Isodore, who refuses to let the Fool accompany her. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L405-409)
## After the Demon attack
- {event} Erille is alive after the Demon attack and kept in a safe place under the highest guard. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L7)
- {event} Erille thanks Tom in person for saving her life, mumbling her gratitude and then fleeing. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L130-135)
- Erille's tears have to be hidden, because as a [Princess] she cannot weep in public. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L507-508)
- The Fool cannot look at Erille while he holds a knife to her father's throat. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L438)
- {event} The dying Fool asks Tom to protect Erille and take her away. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L502-503)
- Erille watches as Tom kills her beloved Fool and stares at him afterwards. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L503)
