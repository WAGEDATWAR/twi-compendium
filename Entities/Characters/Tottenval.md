# Tottenval
> Type: person | Also called: the Blooming Plague; Flos's Gardener | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.02K
> See also: Flos, Queravia, Drevish, Mars, Orthenon
## Overview
- Tottenval is one of the Seven, the highest-levelled and most trusted vassals of King Flos Reimarch of Reim. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L294-296)
- Tottenval is a [Gardener], not a warrior, and not even a magical one. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L299; Book 10, Ch6.14K L315)
- Tottenval is a Beastkin from Baleros, according to Flos, and nicknamed the Blooming Plague by Mars. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L315; Book 15, Ch7.10K L649)
- Flos describes Tottenval as a Beastkin of the Fox Tribe with some Gnollish blood, a gentle, merry soul and a prankster. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L622)
## Life with Flos
- Tottenval makes Reim bloom and feeds Flos's entire kingdom, which Flos says would bloom eternally had he been there. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L315; Book 13, Ch6.53K L97)
- Flos found Tottenval good company for talks because he was incurious, answering Flos's grand schemes and enemies with a shrugging "so?". (Book 10, Ch6.14K L309,L317)
- Flos showered gold on Tottenval for flowers, according to Mars. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L649)
- Flos says Drevish and Tottenval each had an army of [Builders] and [Farmers], in their way, unlike the other Seven's fighting armies. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L496)
## Death
- Flos tells Venith that Tottenval and Queravia are dead. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L445)
- Tottenval and Queravia died in the war, before Flos entered his slumber. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L318; Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L20)
- Tottenval died at sea saving Flos's life where no one else could. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L314-315)
- Flos says he still owes Tottenval's people a debt. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L622)
- Teres knows that Minotaur navies ambushed Flos's fleet at sea, and that but for Tottenval's sacrifice they might have killed Flos himself. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L44-45)
- Flos murmurs during the fighting against Minotaurs that Tottenval was his Gardener and took no lives. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L811)
