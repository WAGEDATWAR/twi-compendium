# Selys Shivertail
> Type: person | Also called: Miss Shivertail; Selys; niece of Zel Shivertail | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.13
> See also: Tekshia Shivertail, Zel Shivertail, Erin Solstice, Pisces, Krshia Silverfang, Heartflame Breastplate, Halfseekers, Olesm Swifttail, Drassi, Hawk
## Overview
- Selys is a light green female Drake who works as a receptionist at the Adventurer's Guild in Liscor, smaller than Relc and with thinner limbs. (Book 1, Ch1.13 L7,L29)
- Selys is a close friend of Erin Solstice and one of Erin's earliest Drake contacts in Liscor. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L356,L366-367)
- Selys is the niece of Zel Shivertail, whom she calls Uncle Zel, and the granddaughter of Tekshia Shivertail, who is her boss. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L394; Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L135-136)
- Selys is a young Drake just past twenty years old, stubborn and opinionated, who dresses stylishly. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L21-22)
