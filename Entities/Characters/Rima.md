# Rima
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.08
> See also: Jasi, Wesle, Pralcem, The Players of Celum
## Overview
- Rima is one of the Players of Celum, a troupe of [Actors], and is described as dark-skinned and deep-voiced. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L32)
- Rima left her job to join the Players, as acting stole Wesle, Kilkran, Pralcem, Rima and others from theirs. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L44)
- Rima is an [Actress] and one of the first [Actors], part of the Players' leadership with Jasi. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L90,L413)
- Rima was arrested as a [Thief] once. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L583)
## With the Players
- Temile says he and two of the old crew, Damius and Rima, stayed behind with some new recruits when the Players left for the north. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L234)
- Rima gives Jasi her view of the new recruits and says they need a week, as they are still a bit raw. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L89-91)
- Rima and Jasi walk through a city together, and Rima gawks at buildings that are a precursor to skyscrapers. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L413-417)
- Rima envies Jasi's Skill that summons five gold pieces' worth of performance supplies. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L439)
- Rima frets about competing entertainments, and Kilkran tells her to relax because the Players have done well so far. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L222,L404)
- Rima appears in the play alongside Wesle, Pralcem, Jasi, Chellise and Medel, the troupe's best. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L727)
