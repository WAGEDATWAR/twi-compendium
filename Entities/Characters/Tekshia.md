# Tekshia
> Type: person | Also called: Tekshia Shivertail; Guildmistress Tekshia; Tekshia the Spearmaster; Selys's grandmother | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.26M
> See also: Selys, Zel Shivertail, Relc, Olesm, Zevara, Ilvriss
## Overview
- Tekshia Shivertail is a very old Drake lady who leads the Adventurer's Guild of Liscor and sits on Liscor's Council. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L306)
- Tekshia is the grandmother of Selys, the guild's [Receptionist], and holds the title Guildmistress. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L275)
- Tekshia is the aunt of General Zel Shivertail, which makes Zel Selys's uncle. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L34,L443)
- Tekshia is a former adventurer who retired and has run Liscor's Adventurer's Guild for over three decades. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L391)
## Description
- Tekshia has dusky purple scales that are greyer and less lustrous than Selys's, yet she is spry and still attractive. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L139)
- Tekshia lashes her tail irritably when she glares at Selys, and her temper is short. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L139-140)
- Tekshia has shrunk with age and is shorter than Selys, but still carries far too much muscle for her years. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L244)
- Tekshia's scales are faded and patchy, and she likes to say she was a beauty in her youth and still matches Selys. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L244)
- Tekshia holds old-fashioned views, saying that in her day everything was tougher and Drakes took matters into their own claws. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L245)
- Tekshia complains of aching bones and a bad back, yet she can still do backflips and outrun Street Runners. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L325)
- Tekshia's tail gives away nothing when she does not want it to. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L325)
- Tekshia carries a little wooden cane that occasionally turns out to be a spear. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L494; Book 14, Ch6.67 L178)
- Tekshia smokes dreamleaf puffers when someone is in the area, and offers one to Ceria. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L634-638)
