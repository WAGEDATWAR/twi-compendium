# Tessia
> Type: person | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.21E
> See also: Gamel, Helm, Laken, Wiskeria
## Overview
- Tessia is one of Laken's two [Engineers] and was the first to acquire the class. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L97)
- Tessia is the higher-leveled of Laken's two [Engineers]. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L97)
- Tessia is a young woman who lived all her life on a farm before finding a vocation making weapons of war. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L97)
- Tessia pulls the rope on Laken's trebuchets and took to them remarkably well. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L97)
- Tessia is Gamel's girlfriend. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L253)
- Tessia, an [Engineer], is in Wiskeria's war tent and looks beside herself as the general speaks to her about the enemy's trebuchets. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L253-254)
