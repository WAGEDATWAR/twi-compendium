# Jeiss
> Type: person | Also called: Senior Guardsman Jeiss; Councilman Jeiss | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.08
> See also: Zevara, Beilmark, Lism, Tekshia
## Overview
- Jeiss is a Senior Guardsman of Liscor's Watch, and a Drake who heads the group of guards waiting for Zevara in the barracks. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L473-475)
- Jeiss and Senior Guardswoman Beilmark are the Gnoll-Drake pair in charge of oversight of the walls, known throughout the city. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L684)
- Jeiss is Beilmark's partner and one of the most respected Senior Guardsman pairs. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L475)
## Career
- Jeiss and Beilmark bet silver on whether a small bee escapes a fireball, and Jeiss welcomes the junior guard to the Watch. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L733-738)
- Jeiss speaks for the Watch when it asks Zevara about the election and whether she is for Krshia Silverfang. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L475-476)
- Jeiss asks Zevara whether she would take a better option if there were one. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L497-498)
- {state} Later, Lism advertises Jeiss as a candidate for a seat on Liscor's Council, and Jeiss salutes Zevara awkwardly. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L860-861)
- Beilmark says nothing, and Jeiss avoids her gaze, when Lism proclaims him a candidate. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L861)
- Jeiss and Lism try to persuade Tekshia to congratulate the Horns, but she refuses to give a medal. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L215-219)
- Tekshia calls the party a wake after the battle, and says she told the Council not to throw one. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L647)
- {state} Later, Jeiss is a Councilman of Liscor, sitting on a Council of working-class Gnolls and Drakes. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L38)
