# Shailt of Tergim
> Type: person | Also called: Armor Captain Shailt; Sixth Armor Captain | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.23D
> See also: Venaz, Iron Vanguard, Tulm the Mithril
## Overview
- Shailt of Tergim is a Minotaur and the Iron Vanguard's Sixth Armor Captain, as large as Venaz, who challenges him to a fist duel and threatens that running means forfeit of his honor. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1058-1059,L1068)
- {event} Venaz wins the weaponless duel, and her jaw is broken; her Dullahans carry her away and retreat. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1069-1074)
- Venaz says she is at least a Level 30 [Warrior] and that she would have beaten him if she had kept her weapon. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L407,L428)
- Venaz says his gauntleted punch shattered her bones because he misjudged his strength. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L430)
