# Badarrow
> Type: person | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.19T
> See also: Headscratcher, Rabbiteater, Shorthilt, Numbtongue, Snapjaw, Calescent, Rags, Greydath, Redfang Tribe
## Overview
- Badarrow is a Redfang Hobgoblin archer who never wastes an arrow. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L172-173)
- Badarrow leads a faction of Redfang archers who hunt, the smallest of the Redfangs' groups. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L19-20)
- Badarrow spends his time on the quest for the perfect arrow, fletching and shooting at targets night and day. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L20)
## Esthelm and the first levels
- Badarrow shot zombies from a watch ledge, one arrow each, grinning at the Horror. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L162-165)
- He killed two Goblin [Shamans] with his two best arrows at Esthelm. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L287-289)
- Three arrows hit Badarrow, but the one aimed at his chest was badly made, with its glue broken. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L290,L373)
- Badarrow learned [Farshot Mastery] after sniping the Goblin [Shaman] at Esthelm. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L326)
- He shot Bird with arrows in three-at-a-time volleys from range. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L329-330)
## Book 7 to 9
- Badarrow stood by the door of Erin's inn, grumpy but interested in the Humans. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L218)
- Badarrow hung upside down held by Shorthilt and Headscratcher to shoot Raskghar from the cliff. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L82-83)
- He used the Skill [Shattershot], but the arrow was stopped by a wicker shield. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L116)
- Raskghar arrows struck Badarrow in the back and shoulder as he climbed out. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L267-269)
- Headscratcher carried the wounded Badarrow through deep water toward the surface, both nearly drowning. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L268-273)
- {event} Badarrow rang a bronze and blue metal bell once, and it made a vast hollow sound that bled his nose, ears and eyes. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L267-272)
- {state} Badarrow became a Level 25 [Archer], changed to a [Sniper], and gained [Doubled Range]. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L593-595)
- His [Keen Eyes] Skill changed to [Eagle Eyes]. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L596-597)
- Five Redfang Hobs including Badarrow held off the Raskghar while leading Cave Goblins and Gnolls. (Book 8, Ch5.44 L28,L34,L49)
- Badarrow throws a pitch that drops under Wesle's bat, with Shorthilt catching, in Erin's baseball game. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L413-417)
- He stabbed Greydath's shoulder with an arrow held in his hand, and Greydath struck him unconscious. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L440-443)
- Headscratcher concluded Greydath could be hurt because Badarrow had hurt him. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L780-781)
- Badarrow and the other four Hobs challenged Garen and were beaten bloody. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L519-570)
- Badarrow sat with the Hobs who spoke of being Redfangs while Garen was gone. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L781-782)
- Rabbiteater and Badarrow report to Rags that Headscratcher, Shorthilt and Numbtongue are dead. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L22-27)
