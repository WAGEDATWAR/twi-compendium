# Ser Raim
> Type: person | Also called: Raim; Ser Raim of the Season of Summer | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.42E
> See also: Calirn, Talia Kallinad, Belavierr, Order of Seasons
## Overview
- Ser Raim is a [Summer Knight] of the Order of Seasons who has exceeded Level 50 and is one of the finest members of the Order. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L582; Book 12, Ch6.43E L1529)
- Ser Raim is at the edge of his forties or the end of his thirties, in yellow, orange and gold armor. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1511)
- Ser Raim's gaze burns as though fire flickers in his pupils. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1511)
- Ser Raim carries the last torch of Paladin Arteis, a flame born of a long-dead Paladin's wrath. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1613; Book 12, Ch6.44E L42-43)
- Raim's flame burns magic and steel alike, and its source consumes his life while he lives. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L43,L85)
## Motive
- Ser Raim and the Hunter's Guild have been hunting Belavierr, and the Summer's Champion calls it his battle. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L553)
- Calirn says Raim injured Belavierr once this winter and she fled his flame. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L582)
- {reveal} Belavierr killed Raim's fiancee, the Autumn Knight Dame Fale, almost a decade ago, according to the Spring's Warden. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L593)
## The Battle at Riverfarm
- Ser Raim leads six [Witch Hunters] from Terandria on foot toward Belavierr, and declares her death ordained by the Hunter's Guild of Noelictus and the Order of Seasons. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1510-1511,L1530)
- Ser Raim names himself [Summer Knight] of the Order of Seasons and promises to bring Belavierr to justice for the dead. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1530)
- Ser Raim uses [Aura of Righteous Fire], which melts Belavierr's needles before they touch him and sets a path of fire around him for twenty feet. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1553-1555)
- Ser Raim offers his own time and life through [Lifeburning Flames], invoking the last torch of Paladin Arteis. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1613)
- Ser Raim then offers everything with [My Life, be Thou My Fire], and the flames burn the shadows themselves. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1619-1622)
- Ser Raim's sword sets Belavierr's tapestry alight, and his flames burn her despite her wards of ancient Dragonscale. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L44-47)
- Belavierr sees her death coming in Raim for the first time in an age. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L48)
- Ser Raim cleaves an artifact, stabs Belavierr in the chest, and later stabs her stomach a third time. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L82-85)
- The [Hunters] use Raim's aura as a shield, letting him burn weaker puppets by his presence. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L84)
- {state} After the first clash Raim has aged, with grey hair and a lined face, as though he burned thirty years away in under thirty minutes. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L715-716)
- Raim insists he has at least twenty more years to burn and that Belavierr will not escape a second time. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L714)
- {belief} Raim tells Ryoka that Belavierr's Noelictus crimes include renting out Helpful Servants who were prisoners in their own minds, bound by a contract. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L833-839)
- {belief} Raim tells Ryoka that Belavierr killed scores of people before the Order drove her from Noelictus. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L842)
- Raim says he was a rather broken man until someone gave him a spark. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L839)
## Death
- {state} Raim abandons his honor and says he is a [Summer Knight] no longer, threatening to slay Belavierr's daughter unless she stands. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L1059-1063)
- Raim tells Talia that if he falls none are to pursue Belavierr, and orders her to return to the Order. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L1067)
- Ser Raim walks to Belavierr as the Order of Seasons salute him, and Riverfarm's people cheer him on. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L1072-1074)
- {state} Ser Raim burns away his life, whispers his love's name, and dies, leaving Belavierr alive but mortal. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L1085-1095)
- Belavierr's eyes no longer glow after Raim's death, and she does not remember her death's name. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L1095,L1103)
## Afterwards
- {event} Raim's ashes return aboard a black barge to Phel's Light, where Calirn and the Season of Summer receive them. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L514,L762-766)
- Raim is buried alongside his love, and is the highest-levelled Knight of those who had died there, by far. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L513,L517)
