# Cirille
> Type: person | Also called: Cirille Bitterclaw; Commander Cirille | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.02C
> See also: Ilvriss, Tom, Richard, Blighted King
## Overview
- Cirille Bitterclaw is a black-scaled female Drake [Commander] from Manus who serves in the armies defending Rhir. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L136,L146; Book 18, Ch7.34C L704)
- Cirille is an Oldblood with two small vestigial wings on her back that cannot lift her and only hurt in armor. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L178-179; Book 18, Ch7.34C L784)
## In the Blighted Kingdom, Book 6
- Cirille shouts at her company to stop running over civilians, then introduces herself to Richard Davenport and Tom as the "sorry" [Commander] of the lot. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L135,L146)
- Cirille wears a plumed helmet, and unlike her soldiers her armor is open at the back to let the wings out. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L136,L178)
- Cirille says her wings are only for show, and that being Oldblood is why she was chosen for a year's service in Rhir. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L179)
## Service on Rhir, Book 18
- Cirille commands about ten thousand Drakes, who turn out in minutes when she thinks a message from High Command means an attack. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L683-684,L704)
- Cirille is woken repeatedly by Bastion-General Quiteil of 4th Wall, who tests her readiness and identity with codes and questions. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L686-702,L705)
- Cirille is tested eight times in a single day by Quiteil, which leaves her angry, and she names Spearmaster Lulv as her superior in Manus. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L722-723,L732)
- Cirille has been stationed in the capital of Rhir for nearly three years of a four-year rotation. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L767)
- Cirille was sent because the Walled Cities send their second or third-best to Rhir as a matter of pride. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L767)
- Cirille owes her military discipline to Manus, her home city. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L704)
- Cirille's closest officers are Captain Shellc and Lieutenant Ossky. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L867)
- {state} Quiteil orders Cirille's division to 4th Wall and then to the 5th Wall, under Bastion-General Zavarial. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L709,L749)
- {state} Quiteil assigns Cirille two thousand one hundred and three Gnolls under Chief Warrior Merish to fold into her command. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L752)
- The secretary-general keeps Cirille in command rather than replacing her with Merish, and tells her to settle her grievances with Captain Hetarria. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1186)
- Cirille explains Manus doctrine that artifacts can be lost, so it is better to commit only to a battle that can be won. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L272-274)
- {belief} Delezza argues that the Blighted Kingdom gets scrolls from around the world and has had thousands of years to build up a stockpile of them. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L263-266,L271)
## Class and Skills
- {state} Cirille reaches [Foreign Commander Level 33!] and changes class to [Combined-Arms Commander]. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L585-586)
- {state} Cirille gains the Skill [Copy Weapon Art] and the Synergy [Gnoll-Drake Command] with the class change. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L587-588)
- Under the Gnoll-Drake Command Synergy, about a third of Cirille's Drakes develop traits such as a stronger bite, keener smell or hearing. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L630)
- Under the same Synergy, some of her Gnolls develop Oldblood-style frost breath, which Merish demonstrates. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L632-635)
- Quiteil says Synergies resemble Combination Skills and are known to Rhir's forces because other nations lack mixed-species armies. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L640,L643)
- Quiteil notes the matchup in Cirille's file and congratulates her, and mentions a second tour. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L640,L646)
- A [Scrier] explains that a second tour is an offer to stay another four years, a sign that Rhir finds her valuable. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L650-652)
- Cirille recalls the Walled Cities picked her because she was decent but not good enough to be needed. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L654)
## Recruiting and the Salazsar offer
- Cirille receives a separate offer from Wall Lord Ilvriss of Salazsar to become [General] of a private army, with high pay. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L814-818)
- Cirille decides to accept the offer, noting that a [General] class would otherwise take her another decade to reach. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L860)
- Cirille proposes that Ilvriss also hire Uxel's company, Merish's Gnolls and Ser Vorn's Order as mixed-species troops. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L882-892)
- Uxel, Merish and Ser Vorn each say they will consider her offer. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L902-905)
- Cirille recommends Captain Shellc to replace her as [Commander]. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L867-868)
- Cirille hopes to prove the value of a mixed-species army and to change the insular Walled Cities from within. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L882,L898; Book 18, Ch7.36C L96)
- Cirille invites Mage-Captain Hetarria to join her in Salazsar, admitting she still dislikes [Necromancers] but believing Izril might need her. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L86-113)
- Cirille says she has fought the Antinium six times, twice on Izril's side of the border. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L79)
- {belief} Cirille says the peace with the Antinium is only writing on paper and that a tunnel links the Hive in Liscor to the others. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L77)
## Death
- {state} Silvenia descends from the sky carrying fire and burns Cirille away as dawn appears, killing the last commander. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1010-1014)
- {event} Cirille's ghost returns to Manus with Ossky and about a thousand dead soldiers and reports the annihilation of her division. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1347,L1354)
- Cirille's ghost reports that Demons assailed the 5th Wall and the Death of Magic overran it before being stopped at the 4th. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1363-1365)
- Cirille's ghost asks Dragonspeaker Luciva for reinforcements for the Blighted Kingdom, with Lulv and Lady Rafaema present, and High Command answers "Granted!" (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1346,L1371-1373)
- Luciva relieves the flickering ghost of Cirille from her post. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1374-1375)
