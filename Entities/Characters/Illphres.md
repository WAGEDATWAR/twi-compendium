# Illphres
> Type: person | Also called: ice demon; the ice mage | First appears: Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Amerys, Cognita, Jurix, Bastam, Wistram, Isolationists
## Overview
- Illphres is an older Human [Cryomancer] at Wistram, described as one of the best [Cryomancers] in the academy and a temperamental teacher. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L410-411)
- Calvaron places her with the Isolationists, and he warns Ceria she is angry. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L407,L410)
- She appears to be in her late thirties, with slightly greying hair and a smooth, wrinkle-free face. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L347)
- She teaches elemental magic, which she says she was forced to do by the Council even though she has better things to do. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L349,L352)
- She speaks abruptly with no welcome, and her face seems frozen, neither smiling nor frowning nor blinking. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L350,L382)
## Magic
- {fact} In the dining hall, Illphres casts the Tier 4 spell [Ice Lance] within seconds, without a wand. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L415)
- Ceria is impressed by the speed of her casting, though ice is not Ceria's specialty. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L415-416)
- She sets her entry test with an [Ice Wall] sealing the classroom door and orders students to melt it without an artifact or another mage. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L354-357)
- She says she made the wall impervious to spells below Tier 4, and did not expect anyone to use salt. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L456-457)
- She freezes a seagull's wings with a shot of ice to demonstrate aim, and its wings melt as it nears the water. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L64-66)
- She says everything is a moving target and that Ceria will never improve by aiming at stationary points. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L68)
- She explains that a silver bell is given for reaching a certain [Fencer] level or for proving oneself against a master duelist. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L385)
- Her spellbook has a light blue cover, and she says a spell can only be scribed by someone of equal or higher level than the spell. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L78,L123)
- She says it would take a Level 40 [Magical Scribe] to write down a spell such as the one in her book. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L120,L123)
- She says Level 50, let alone Level 60, is the utmost limit mages at Wistram can strive for. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L291)
- Illphres says Amerys has only six or eight levels on her. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L406)
- Illphres plans to use [Fortress of the Ice Queen], a famous spell, right away in the battle to shield the others. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L400)
## The ice mask
- {reveal} Illphres' lower face is blackened with dead, frostbitten skin, hidden beneath a mask of ice. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L465-467,L541)
- She reshapes the ice to smile and says she has to alter her face to do so. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L542-543)
- She often covers her mouth when she changes her features to a grin. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L344)
- Jurix knows her secret. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L344)
- {reveal} Ceria later realizes Illphres is actually a bit younger than herself, though she feels much older. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L500)
## Teaching Ceria
- {event} After Ceria demands to be taught, Illphres tells her to melt the ice first. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L70-71)
- Ceria melts the wall using salt rubbed on it and heat from her own spells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L455-456)
- {event} Pirates attack Wistram's ship, and Illphres skates across the sea on ice, breaking hulls with pillars of ice and killing a mage with an ice spike. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L424-427)
- Her mask falls to pieces as she fights the [Storm Captain], and her freezing spell freezes the deck and the captain. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L457-461)
- She tells Ceria that this is what the face of a true mage looks like. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L458)
- {rel} She agrees to teach Ceria magic if she truly wants it, and Ceria takes her hand. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L566-573)
- {rel} Illphres says she and Amerys compete and sometimes train together because they are a good match. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L415)
- She says Amerys prefers rapid all-out assaults while she uses traps and drawn-out battles, and Amerys will not join her because she is waiting for her King. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L415-419)
- Amerys later says she refused Illphres' request to help despite the risk. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L361-364)
## The challenge to the Golems
- Illphres tells Ceria she has prepared for the challenge for over a decade. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L377)
- {belief} She claims that winning will end the Council's rule, so mages will guard the entrance and it will be every mage for themselves. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L494)
- {belief} She reasons that the missing original Golem shows a past group broke the Golems' tandem formation and nearly won. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L97-99)
- She tells Ceria she grew up in Wistram and never adventured, and might not be so scared if she had. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L227,L230)
- Before the doors close she says "This is magic". (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L235)
- {state} Illphres enters the Golems' room with Jurix, Qum, Bastam and Ophelia, and dies in the challenge. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L236-238,L299,L331-334)
- Her body is brought out and raised as a Lich by Pisces, and she hits a Golem with a stone-cracking blow before they flee. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L299-307)
- Amerys later blasts open a frost-covered door, comes out with an ice-blue spellbook and orders a student to send it to Ceria. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L428-433)
- Charles de Trevalier drops it into the sea, where it freezes the water and sinks. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L436-437)
- {event} Later, Ceria recalls Illphres telling her that she could not yet shape her ice, comparing the structure of her [Ice Walls] to an [Architect]'s. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L318-320)
- Ceria also recalls Illphres calling the mask an example of ice shaping, and telling her to put an edge on a dagger. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L322)
