# Wilovan
> Type: person | Also called: Gentleman Caller (Gnoll); the hat duo (with Ratici) | First appears: Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn
> See also: Ratici, Niers, Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings, Erin Solstice, Numbtongue, Fierre, Mrsha
## Overview
- Wilovan is a burly Gnoll with a nearly-top hat and a polished club at his side, who works with Ratici as one of the Gentlemen Callers. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L260; Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L905)
- Wilovan is a [Thug] and describes himself as a humble fellow with a club who taps folks and absconds with coin. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L269)
- Wilovan is a member of the Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings, which Palt calls a smaller but elite gang with big players in northern Izril. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L101; Book 19, Ch7.52 L231,L265)
- Wilovan speaks with a polite, posh manner and plays cards by memorising his hand at a glance and laying it face down. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L902; Book 19, Ch7.43G L151)
- Wilovan keeps a pipe, and smokes at the inn beside an open window. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L176-177)
## Contract with the Tall Man (Book 17)
- Wilovan agrees with Ratici about taking the contract, citing the proximity to home, the pay and the distinction. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L704-706)
- Wilovan says he would like to visit home even if they accept the contract. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L706)
- {event} Wilovan confronts a gang of eleven planning to kidnap little Gnolls, introducing himself as a representative of the Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L901,L906-910)
- {event} Wilovan leads the gang into an alley and walks out alone ten minutes later, humming a children's song about wet bodies. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L913-919)
- Wilovan says Mrsha is not his priority but is a child, and calls kidnapping a child bad business. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L906)
