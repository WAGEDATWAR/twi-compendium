# Queravia
> Type: person | Also called: Gambler of Fates; Gambler Strategist | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.02K
> See also: Flos, Niers, Tottenval
## Overview
- Queravia was one of the King of Destruction's Seven and the strategist who led his armies. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L318)
- Queravia won the fiercest battles in Flos's name by directing soldiers while his other vassals fought on the front lines. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L318)
- Queravia was a [Gambler Strategist] who bet against luck itself. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L326)
- {state} Before she died, Queravia advanced to [Gambler of Fates]. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L326)
## Death
- {event} Queravia and Tottenval died in the war, and Flos did not see her end but heard of it. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L318,L326)
- Queravia lost her last bet in Baleros. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L326)
- Flos had thought it impossible that Queravia would ever die. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L326)
- {event} In a flashback, Gazi and Flos tell Venith that Queravia and Tottenval are dead, whereupon Flos declares himself finished as King. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L445-452)
## Niers
- Niers fought a famous battle against Queravia, the Gambler of Fates, the King of Destruction's [Strategist]. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L591)
- {belief} Niers thinks Queravia had a sixty percent chance in any scenario left to the odds, so she won six of ten even matchups. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L646-651)
- {belief} Niers says her tactics sometimes worked against impossible odds, creating a huge difference over time. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L651)
