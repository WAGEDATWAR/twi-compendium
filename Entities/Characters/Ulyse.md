# Ulyse
> Type: person | Also called: Mage Ulyse | First appears: Ch6.13K
> See also: Mirin, Daiton, Orthenon, Trey Atwood, Rasea
## Overview
- Ulyse is the middle-aged leader of the [Mage] group Parasol Stroll, who has distant eyes, greying hair and a vacant gaze. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L266,L269)
- Ulyse carries a bright yellow silk parasol embroidered with silver moons, and each member of Parasol Stroll carries an enchanted parasol. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L266,L269)
- Parasol Stroll is busy with the work of the Mage's Guild and coordinating the rest of Reim's magical needs. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L267)
- Orthenon asks Ulyse whether Parasol Stroll has an [Enchanter] who can enchant blades for Jelaim's warriors. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L267)
- {belief} Ulyse says the quality of metal is not good, and Daiton's blades are beautiful but flawed and cannot hold enchantments. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L272)
- Ulyse is one of the [Mages] who recently came to Reim, and he fixes Trey's iPhone with a [Repair] spell. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L168)
- Ulyse takes Flos' iPhone, levitates it and taps it with his parasol while casting [Repair]. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L327-329)
- {state} Ulyse dies in the fight with Medain's army, stabbed through the throat by Rasea as he raises his staff. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L1156-1157)
