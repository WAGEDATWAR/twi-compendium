# Seve-Alrelious
> Type: person | Also called: Seve; the Hundredfriends Courier; Courier Seve | First appears: Book 19, Ch7.49
> See also: Geneva, Idis, Tritel and Ci, Umina, Seql, Nelgaunt Arveil, Erek, Tombhome
## Overview
- Seve-Alrelious is the Hundredfriends Courier, a dark-skinned Sea Courier from Chandrar who left the sands long ago, and a [Worldtraveller Inkfriend], a class of his own. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L574,L578,L582)
- Seve comes from A'ctelios Salash, called Tombhome, a Shield Kingdom of Chandrar, which he calls peculiar and dangerous but home. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L57-63)
- Seve carries living tattoos on his skin, including six dolphins, an ape and a Nelgaunt, each a friend met at sea and made into ink. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L577-581)
- Seve's living tattoos are both Skill and ink, and he says the practice is Drathian, since Drathians turn pictures into magic. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L78)
- Seve says his tattoos stay with him because feeding so many friends is hard work. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L78)
- Seve's tiny craft is the World's Pact. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L651)
## Tattoo companions
- The Nelgaunt Arveil, a friend of ships, comes from Seve's arm, and Seve hastes it with a scroll so it can pull the ship through the waves. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L575-577)
- Six large armored dolphins drag Seve's ship ahead in the typhoon. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L645,L651)
- Seve uses his Steelfriend tattoo, a little waving suit of armor, with [Steelfriend's Pact] to guide him through the storm, and the pact with Seql still shines. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L647-651)
- Erek is Seve's Orangutan tattoo, an intelligent ape from the jungles who hid on his boat. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L96-100)
- Seve says Erek cannot properly speak but can play chess, sail the boat and use magical artifacts, and is as strong as a Gemlion. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L103)
## Tombhome (Book 19)
- {reveal} Seve says he is one of the [Fleshchosen], who grew up eating Tombhome's meat and so heals faster, is stronger and lives longer. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L118)
- Seve says he grows hungrier over time, that food he brings from home never rots, and that he must return at least once every few years. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L131)
- Seve says that someday the Carven City will call him home and he will say goodbye to his friends and not leave again. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L131)
- {belief} Seve says Tombhome welcomes anyone who is lost or alone, that it is always a choice, and that its [Flesh Shapers] do healing work like Geneva's. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L127)
- Seve was born in the Carven City, and says its rumors of those who enter and do not leave have some truth. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L123-124)
## Delivering the cure (Book 19)
- Seve carries three crates of the cure and has not slept for a day as he heads into the magical typhoon. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L583,L645)
- Seve says Seql's family and the Great Companies paid him for his delivery, though he would have done it for free. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L138)
- Seve says he charges no fee when the cause is enough. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L142)
- {event} Seve asks Geneva for the names of those who made the Occillium, and after Idis interrupts, Geneva gives only the name Joseph from Liscor. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L163-173)
