# Ulvama
> Type: person | Also called: Chief Shaman of Tremborag's tribe | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.28G
> See also: Tremborag, Rags, Pyrite, Pebblesnatch, Redfang tribe, Raidpear, Halrac, Typhenous
## Overview
- Ulvama is a female Hob, the chief [Shaman] of Tremborag's tribe, nearly naked with chalky body paint in exotic patterns. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L100-101)
- Ulvama tells Rags that five other [Shamans] are in the tribe but she is strongest by far. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L127)
- Ulvama's magic is basic but strong with numbers; she casts lightning, fire and shields, and heals by closing flesh and restoring blood but cannot fix a missing arm. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L119,L123)
- Ulvama is a Goblin whose mother was a Goblin, and who says she is both Human and Goblin. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L603-606)
- Ulvama has crimson eyes and wears the markings of her class, colorful paint worn from long days. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L127)

## In Tremborag's tribe (Books 4-9)
- Ulvama casts a [Bloodfury] spell on young Goblins that makes the red lights in their eyes brighten and their bodies grow before the attack. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L49-51)
- Ulvama shoots masses of fire from her staff onto the Goblin Lord's grouped army. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L49)
- Ulvama marches in the center of Tremborag's tribe beside his Hob lieutenants, her skin painted with symbols. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L41)
- Ulvama casts a hazy illusion that makes Tremborag and nearby Goblins flicker, snapping an arrow aimed at Tremborag's face. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L241-242)
- Ulvama screeches and throws herself down as Noears's lightning bolt earths just past her. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L198)
- Ulvama tells Rags that her people were happy in Tremborag's mountain with sex, food and what they wanted, and that Tremborag was a great Chieftain. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L603)

## Joining Rags (Book 9)
- Ulvama tries to seduce Pyrite as a second for Chieftain Rags, kicking Hammersteel aside. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L269-271)
- Pyrite tells Ulvama he knew her as a small Goblin, an apprentice to the Chief Shaman, remembered for getting in trouble over paints. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L272-275)
- Pyrite turns Ulvama down because he is busy and does not like her. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L282-285)
- Ulvama leads Hobs and Mountain City Goblins who bow low to Rags and ask what now. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L428-430)
- {state} Ulvama is among the Goblin prisoners after the battle, injured and staring hatred at Laken and every Human. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L334-335)

## Prisoner and camp leader (Book 15)
- {state} Ulvama is a captive in Pebblesnatch's wagon, one of three Hobs captured by the Humans, and the chief [Shaman] of the Mountain City tribe. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L130-131)
- Ulvama pats Pebblesnatch on the head, then flicks her and tells her to shush. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L126-129)
- Ulvama strides into a fight with a crude wooden staff as a symbol of her authority and breaks it up with blows and shouts. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L284-290)
- Ulvama gives herself and those who obey a large portion of meat and the rest scraps, the way the Mountain City tribe does. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L290)
- Ulvama strikes a deal under which the Redfangs hunt for the camp in return for three huts, ejecting the Mountain City Goblins. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L294)
- Ulvama raises a pillar of fire from a campfire to show that [Shamans] have magic. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L408-409)
- Ulvama smears ash and dirt on a Redfang [Scout] and says she will hide the Goblins sent to search. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L412-418)
- Ulvama grinds marrowless bone with rust to make a dye, and makes Pebblesnatch's dough rise out of its bowl. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L662,L671)
- Ulvama smacks Pebblesnatch on the head, then uses [Silenced Agony] to take away the pain. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L699-701)
- Ulvama says she will give Pebblesnatch and two other Goblins proper names so they are remembered through memory when they die. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1065-1069)
- {belief} Ulvama tells Pebblesnatch that power is strength, sex and food, and that she can be second-strongest by taking power any way. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L717-724)
- {belief} Ulvama says Tremborag was Great Chieftain, bigger than Rags and older than Garen Redfang, and that Humans always kill Goblins. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L729-731)
- Ulvama finds Pebblesnatch's hollow tree empty except for a glowing red stone and a crude bark door, and fits the stone into the door. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1322)
- Ulvama inspects the stone, stares south and says it is too far, very far and too weak. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1324-1326)

## Goblinhome and the Goblinlands (Books 16-19)
- Ulvama uses sex as influence and currency, an essential part of her class and position as [Shaman]. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L272-273)
- Ulvama takes Raidpear, the strongest Hobgoblin of the Solstice Goblins, as her partner, judging males easier than the female Hob Leafarmor. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L273)
- Ulvama blows soil at about ten young Goblins from the Mountain City and Flooded Plains tribes who spy on her and Raidpear. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L285-289)
- Ulvama counts little magic-charged gems that the Goblins dug up beside a crude door in her hut. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L497-499)
- Ulvama watches a glowing crimson stone set in the door, which has grown a stronger connection, and sees the door is open. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L500-502)
- Ulvama tells Pebblesnatch to say that other Goblins come because they hear the Goblinlands are safe. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L516-521)
- Ulvama casts a flea hex at Halrac during a parley, and Typhenous's [Hex Eater] and Revi's [Wind Orb] react, and Typhenous identifies it as a minor flea hex. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L579-589)
- Ulvama calls the hex a little prank, and the Goblins relax once Halrac lowers his bow. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L595-596)
