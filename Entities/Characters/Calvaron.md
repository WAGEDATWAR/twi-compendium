# Calvaron
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1
> See also: Beatrice, Ceria Springwalker, Pisces, Montressa du Valeros, Wistram, Centaur
## Overview
- Calvaron is a Centaur and a full student of Wistram, tall and cheerful, who wears only a shirt on his upper half. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L277,L281)
- He greets Ceria and Pisces after their sea crossing and offers to show them around the academy. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L278,L281)
- He says the whole academy watched the sea serpent and placed bets on whether Amerys would reach the ship in time. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L281)
- He is undeclared in Wistram's factions for now and not yet important enough for them to pressure him. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L361)
- He is a generalist [Mage], and Beatrice is a [Runeshaper]. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L419)
## Broker
- {fact} Calvaron tells new students that rooms in Wistram vary because many people with different styles worked on the castle. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L316)
- He buys and sells secrets and calls himself just a broker or middle-Centaur. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L313)
- Ceria notes that his independence lets any faction treat with him without fearing he will trade their secrets. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L316)
- He tells new students that most magic classes require at least ten levels in [Mage], and many require twenty. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L233)
- He says the academy gives new students old spellbooks, and older mages sort them so nothing above a Tier 2 spell remains. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L259)
- Calvaron says the locations of secret libraries and tomes are among the biggest secrets to trade, and he cannot trade for one. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L265)
- He tells Ceria that all knowledge is useful to someone, and that Pisces' rapier and silver bell are important knowledge. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L271)
- Calvaron hints that he knows Ceria's sealed origins and that an old Terandrian city warrant lists a small bounty on a half-Elf kid for theft and property damage. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L276)
- Other students joke that he charges an arm and a horse's leg for the location of the illusion class. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L421)
- Calvaron owns a truth crystal enchanted to glow red when someone tells the truth, the opposite of most truth spells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L335-337)
- Calvaron carries a very limited bag of holding. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L146)
- He buys a Sand Tumbler, a drink from Chandrar made with limes and lemons, from a ship captain with a cargo of drinks. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L151,L159)
- Calvaron tells Ceria and Pisces that Charles de Trevalier got into Illphres' class by having someone else melt her ice wall. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L134-138)
- Calvaron says Charles is heavily in Rievan's camp and part of the Libertarian faction. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L141)
## Final year
- A student reports that Calvaron and Beatrice have put money on Ceria and Pisces passing the exams. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L467)
- {fact} Calvaron is in his sixth year, with one more to go before he can call himself a mage, and he is terrified of the exam. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L72-73)
- He says becoming a mage would embroil him in faction politics, so he wants to stay classless and become an influential broker between all sides. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L75)
- Beatrice wants him to join the Revivalists, and the two have been fighting about his future. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L77)
## Death
- {state} Calvaron is killed in the banquet hall by the shades called from Archmage Nekhret's spell. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L382-385)
- He lies fallen mid-gallop with a wand still gripped in his hand and a desperate, fierce expression. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L384-385)
- Beatrice holds his body, and Montressa sits on the ground beside him. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L385-387)
- Beatrice later inherits his network of connections. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L204)
