# Garen Redfang
> Type: person | Also called: Garen; Garen Red Fang; the Redfang Chieftain; the Hobgoblin traitor | First appears: Ch2.02G
> See also: Garen Red Fang (earlier Book 2 file), Rags, Tremborag, Reiss, Halfseekers, Jelaqua Ivirith, Redfang Tribe, Velan the Kind
## Overview
- Garen Redfang is a Hobgoblin and Chieftain of the Redfang tribe who rides a rust-red Carn Wolf. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L62)
- Garen's second name Redfang comes from the enchanted red blade he carries, and Garen is the name he claimed himself. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L36)
- Garen says he is fourteen years old, which Tremborag calls old for a Goblin and young for anything else. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L60-63)
- Garen speaks Human fluently, and he is called a famous legend whose name Goblins know. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L71; Book 7, Ch5.19G L15)
## First meeting and the duel with Rags (Book 2)
- The Red Fang Tribe's Chieftain, a Hob Goblin, leads sixty Goblins riding rust-red giant Carn Wolves and carries a red sword. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L67)

## Origins and adventuring past
- Garen was named by the Shaman of the Old Fire Tribe, and he wandered away from his tribe as soon as he grew older. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L106)
- Garen levelled quickly as a [Warrior] and became a Hob, terrorising a stretch of land far to the north alone. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L107)
- A team of adventurers hunted Garen down and nearly killed him, but its leader spared his life and chased him away. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L108)
- Garen disguised himself in robes and a crude mask and listened to Humans in villages and towns, and learned to speak the Human language. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L109-110)
- Garen challenged the adventurers a second time, lost again, and then volunteered to join them, becoming the first Goblin to journey with adventurers. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L111-112)
- Garen's team explored dungeons and progressed to Gold-rank status, and Garen got his sword during this time. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L112-113)
- Garen met Jelaqua when he killed an Eater Goat that adventurers wanted, while posing as a Bronze-rank adventurer. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L143-145)
- Jelaqua invited Garen into the Halfseekers, introducing a team that was a band of misfits, and Garen gave only his name. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L162-171)
- Garen had been a Halfseeker for about a year when his memory of Jelaqua asking which way to go takes place. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L113)
- Garen was the newest member of the Halfseekers and felt out of place, though he often argued over equipment with Seborn. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L113; Book 9, Ch5.58 L97-106)
- Jelaqua and Moore recall that Garen was Silver-rank when he hid in a forest with Halassia and attacked a prison with illusion spells. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L698-699)
- In Redfang legend, their Chieftain once walked through Human cities masked and hooded, and spoke with Humans. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L155)
- Garen was Gold-rank for a time and the only Goblin to hold that rank, but he has since lost most of his artifacts in fights. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L15; Book 8, Ch5.37G L33)
