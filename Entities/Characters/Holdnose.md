# Holdnose
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.05P
> See also: Pebblesnatch, Rags, Ushel
## Overview
- Holdnose is an elderly Goblin of nearly seven years, from the Flooded Waters tribe, an original Goblin of Liscor. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L806)
- Holdnose knew Chieftain Rags when she was born, followed her north, and returned to Liscor, only to be captured at the end of the terrible battle. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L807)
- Holdnose has the class [Rotter], which Pebblesnatch has never encountered before. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L807-808)
- Holdnose is a fermentation expert who knows how to turn perfectly good food into a rotten, fermented substance. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L814)
## Book 15: fish sauce
- Holdnose sets up a stinking camp in a stone cave by the river, with pots, pans and stone vessels full of fish sauce. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L809-812)
- Holdnose explains that fish sauce takes a month or two of sealed fermentation, but his Skills let him finish a batch in only one week. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L816-821,L825)
- Holdnose trades Pebblesnatch a precious jar of fish sauce for sixteen wrapleaf rolls, and promises more as he experiments with everything. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L829-831)
- Holdnose plans to ferment cucumbers in another pot. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L832)
## Book 16
- The Gnoll [Fermenter] Ushel runs a substantially cleaner and more upscale operation than Holdnose's, though the two would respect each other as fellow craftsmen. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Gold L201)
