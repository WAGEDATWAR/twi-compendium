# Gaile
> Type: person | Also called: Hunter Gaile; Gaile the [Beastslayer] | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.43E
> See also: Tagil, Ser Raim, Belavierr, Wiskeria
## Overview
- Gaile is a [Witch Hunter] from Terandria who carries an axe and fights in Ser Raim's band against Belavierr. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1567-1572)
- Gaile was formerly a [Beastslayer] who became a [Witch Hunter] for vengeance, according to Ser Raim. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L702)
- Gaile says Belavierr is the reason she buried her own daughter. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1581)
- Ser Raim says he and Gaile sought Belavierr for six years. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L702)
## Riverfarm
- {event} Gaile runs at Ryoka and Wiskeria and demands that Belavierr hold or her daughter dies, with a needle buried in her cheek. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1567-1569)
- Gaile presses her axe into Wiskeria's neck, drawing blood, and tells Ser Raim that nobody there can stop her blade. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1572-1573)
- Gaile says that she is tired of the chase and that this is the chance to take Belavierr's daughter from her. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1579)
- Gaile apologises to Wiskeria, who did not choose her mother. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1581)
- Gaile shoves Wiskeria aside and strikes at Belavierr with a Skill, only lightly parting Belavierr's robes with the axe. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L131-133)
- {state} Belavierr crushes Gaile's neck and ribs with her hand, driving her body down, and Gaile dies. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L134,L156)
- Gaile admits that her threat goes against Ser Raim's pride and honor, and she accepts she will die if she swings. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1579)
- Ser Raim says afterward that without Gaile's actions Belavierr might have been lost again. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L702)
- The Order of Seasons carries Gaile's ashes away with those of the other fallen Hunters. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L110)
