# Redscar
> Type: person | Also called: Sir Redscar; leader of the Redfangs | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.31G
> See also: Garen Redfang, Rags, Pyrite, Poisonbite, Noears, Thunderfur, Spiderslicer, Redfang tribe, Tyrion Veltras
## Overview
- Redscar is a scarred Goblin who leads the Redfang warriors, Garen Redfang's former second-in-command, and obeys Rags instantly. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L126)
- Redscar leads the Redfangs as Rags's mobile attack force and vanguard. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L76)
- Redscar rides a Carn Wolf larger than all but Garen's, which he raised from a cub. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L409)
- Redscar's Carn Wolf is named Thunderfur, and he cares more for it than for himself. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L54-55)
- Redscar carries an enchanted frost sword, which casts a frost spell. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L353,L383)
- Redscar has two classes, [Beast Tamer] and [Raid Leader], both useful to what he wants to do. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L31)
- Pyrite thinks Redscar is the best fighter among the marching Goblins except Garen, Reiss and Eater of Spears. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L31)
- Pyrite believes Redscar's two classes will merge in time if he lives long enough. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L33)
- Redscar is a poor reader, taking a while to make out the sign on the inn's door. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L275)
- Later, Redscar has become a full Hob, a little taller than Rags but the shortest Hob of all, with strength that shot up. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L480)
- Redscar likes male Goblins, and is the leader of the remaining Redfangs. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L215)
- {state} Later, Redscar is called a [War Leader] and [Beast Master], Rags's second and the strongest Goblin beside her. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L482)

## Under Rags (Books 4-8)
- Redscar trains Rags in push-ups, stretches and sparring, noting that training means winning too. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L233-253)
- Redscar wants to go home to the High Passes and fight a big herd of Eater Goats, which eat Carn Wolves, to make the tribe strong. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L132,L176-179)
- Redscar rides out of the darkness with his frost blade covered in blood, unharmed and furious, wanting to hunt the Humans. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L353-354,L383-384)
- Redscar stabs Pyrite in the arm with his frost sword when Pyrite stops him from riding after the Humans, then obeys and sends sixty riders. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L241-247)
- Redscar leads Redfang Warriors out of the darkness with a glowing longsword and cuts down two Humans. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L185)
- Redscar charges the Humans at the village, and Tyrion Veltras's lance pierces his right shoulder. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L409,L421)

## Flight south (Book 9)
- Redscar scouts ahead on Thunderfur despite his shoulder wound from Tyrion's lance three days earlier. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L44,L55)
- Redscar tells Rags "We chose you" when the Redfang Warriors confront her. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L499)
- Redscar angrily asks what the former Chieftain did, hiding in a mountain, and says Rags was a better Chieftain than Garen could be. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L537)
- Redscar half-rises with flashing eyes when Tremborag calls Rags a nameless child, and Noears and Quietstab hold him down. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L25-26)
- Redscar tries to hold Reiss's army off from the flanks and shouts "Betrayer! Cowards!" while rallying the tribe. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L49,L197-198)
- Garen calls the Redfangs to follow him, but the ones around Redscar do not, and Redscar shouts "Run!" and flees. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L214-217)
- Redscar arrives at Liscor with the Flooded Waters remnant on the eleventh day. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L791)

## At the inn (Book 9)
- Redscar leads the Flooded Waters tribe toward the inn, sensing a friend ahead, and leaves Poisonbite and Noears behind to climb the hill. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L255-269)
- Redscar reads the sign on the inn's door and laughs when he understands it, "No killing Goblins". (Book 9, Ch5.59 L275-281)
- Redscar speaks English with Erin and introduces himself, holding her hand gingerly. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L340-349)
- Redscar reunites with the five Hobs, hearing Headscratcher say his name. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L460-464)
- Redscar duels Spiderslicer with identical unenchanted steel shortswords, and both heal their wounds afterward. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L488,L507)
- Halrac bets two hundred gold on the one with the scar, Redscar, and Pisces adjusts the odds. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L504-505)
- Redscar asks the Halfseekers whether Garen was a good teammate, and Seborn and Jelaqua tell him Garen was terrible but strong and trouble-prone. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L687-692,L696)
- Redscar concludes that Garen was a bad friend, teammate and Chieftain, but good one sometimes. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L706)

## Battle of the Floodplains (Book 9)
- Redscar directs the Flooded Waters tribe to spread out and leads them forward in formation. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L79)
- Redscar points out undead to Poisonbite's female warriors in the battle. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L132)
- Redscar fights on with the last Redfangs against encircling Humans, holding a frost-enchanted sword and the rust-red Redfang. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1148-1154)
- Redscar kills Sir Vumat in single combat after Vumat judges he would become as great a threat as Garen Redfang. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1165-1171)
- Redscar senses Rags running, sees shapes fleeing toward the mountain, whispers "Chieftain", and rides away with the last Redfangs. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1174-1176)
- Redscar finds Badarrow fighting with a dagger and drags him away. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1176)
- Redscar brings Garen's rust-red sword Redfang to Rags on the back of the badly injured Thunderfur. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L31-33,L40)
- Redscar is one of five survivors who reach Rags with Poisonbite, Snapjaw, Rabbiteater and Badarrow. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L41)

## Goblinhome (Book 16)
- Redscar rides one of the largest Carn Wolves and slashes Wyverns with his frost sword and Redfang. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L410-416)
- Redscar's [Flurry Strikes] send fire from the crimson blade and frost from the other. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L413-414)
- Redscar suggests sending someone to the inn to find survivors, and proposes Badarrow with Snapjaw. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L565,L572)
- Rags refuses to send thirty Redfangs, ten Hobs and seventy Goblins, and says two is enough. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L575-580)
- Rags fears exposing Erin, so she will not go herself but agrees to send Badarrow and Snapjaw. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L583-588,L659)
