# Merrik
> Type: person | Also called: Merrik of Deríthal-Vel | First appears: Ch6.22D
> See also: Peki, Wil, Sillk, Xol, Venaz, Yerranola, Feshi, Perorn
## Overview
- Merrik is a Dwarf student of Niers Astoragon, whom Niers calls a fine [War Leader] rather than a formal military class. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L890)
- Niers says Merrik fought several brisk battles against Trolls in the mountains before coming to the academy. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L890)
- Merrik introduces himself to Perorn as a fighting maniac from Pomle. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L184)
- Merrik is from Deríthal-Vel, the Mountain Kingdom of the Dwarves, which most people call Dwarfhome. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L314)
- {belief} Merrik claims Dwarfhome has never been taken by force of arms, saying it has surrendered but never been breached or starved. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L336-342)
- Merrik can swim in armour, unlike Peki, and he is a Dwarf [War Leader]. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L183,L189)
## Skills
- Merrik uses [Body of Stone], and his armour hides steel under his tunic. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L814-815)
- Merrik says his real Skill is [Giant's Hammer] and he shouts fake Skill names for fun. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L810-814)
- Merrik calls [Battalion of Glory] his big one, making his club shine and his allies glow as they charge the Iron Vanguard. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L860-861)
