# Lacel the Leaper
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.15R
> See also: Lord Bein Terland, Hawk, Griffon Hunt
## Overview
- Lacel the Leaper is a lanky Courier who runs nearly as fast as a horse but has more endurance Skills than mobility. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L69-70,L75,L83)
- Lacel carries Lady Hetessana's pendant, which has a ten-thousand-gold bounty set by Lord Bein through unofficial channels. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L70-71)
## Book 16
- {event} Lacel is attacked on the trade road by eighteen [Raiders], and escapes by turning into six illusionary figures that race in different directions. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L77-79)
- Lacel jumps straight up into the air thirty feet to the top of a hill and leaps again, fleeing the [Raid Leader]. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L84-86)
- Lacel has two [Thieves] try for his bag of holding the day he reaches an inn where Griffon Hunt and the Players of Celum are celebrating. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1211-1212)
- {event} A disguised [Assassin] of the Assassin's Guild drugs Lacel and tells him it is a message that Magnolia Reinhart holds no sway over the Guild. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1215-1221)
- {state} The [Assassin] breaks Lacel's legs with a hammer, and Typhenous says they are well and truly broken, a message with his bag of holding still locked. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1223-1225,L1265)
- The [Assassin] tells Lacel to send Lord Bein the pendant of his own will the next day, and says the injury is not meant to be permanent. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1224)
- Hawk hears that Lacel was intercepted while running a delivery to Lady Dealia in the Players of Celum's inn. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L450)
- Lacel leaves the city with broken legs, but the package for Lady Dealia is still there. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L452-453)
- The Assassin's Guild is back, now accepting contracts on Runners, as the attack on Lacel was meant to send a message. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L455)
