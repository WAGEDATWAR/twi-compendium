# Prost
> Type: person | Also called: Prost Surehand; Mister Prost; Steward Prost | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.00E
> See also: Laken, Durene, Yesel, Riverfarm, Rie Valerund
## Overview
- Prost is a [Farmer] of Riverfarm whom Durene helped with his broken wagon before Laken arrived. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L339,L393)
- Prost is married to Yesel, who gives Laken a basket of goods for Durene's home. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L403)
- Prost's son Finnon fetches Durene when the family wagon loses a wheel, and the family's old plow horse is Evera. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L330,L339,L348)
- Prost shakes Laken's hand first when Durene brings him into the village. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L393)
## Opposing Laken and Durene (Book 3)
- Prost tells Durene she does not belong with the villagers and says they took her in and fed her at risk to themselves. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L247-250)
- Durene answers that the village only took her in because her mother asked, gave her scraps, and made her sleep in the barn. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L251)
- {belief} Laken says Prost called Durene a monster, a freak and inhuman. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L246)
- Laken greets Prost coldly because he remembers their last meeting, and asks after Yesel and the children. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L225-229)
- Laken offers Prost one gold coin for dried meat and eggs, then two if the village adds to it. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L290)
- Laken infers that Prost later apologised to Durene, though more briefly than he would have liked. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L323)
## The avalanche and the claim (Book 3)
- After the avalanche, Prost stands humble before Laken and calls him "Emperor". (Book 3, Ch3.12E L350-352)
- Laken swears to Prost, as an [Emperor], that he will return to Riverfarm. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L447)
## Becoming Steward (Book 5)
- Laken says Prost lost his youngest boy in the avalanche, though his attitude toward Laken and Durene has since improved. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L166)
- Prost reports that Ryoka brought food a week before Laken's return, and that the village is rationing it. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L167-169)
- Prost helps Laken sketch plans for a rebuilt Riverfarm, doubting at first that it can be done. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L211-216)
- {state} Laken asks Prost to be his [Steward], though Prost says he is not worthy of the class. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L252-273)
- Laken says he saw Prost change from refusing a half-Troll to calling him [Emperor]. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L266)
- {state} Later, Prost gains the class [Steward] overnight and loses all his earlier levels. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L286-289)
- Prost had been a Level 20 [Farmer], plus a few levels in other classes, before becoming a Level 8 [Steward]. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L288-289)
- Prost has the Skills [Talent Finder] and [Eye of Need], which help him find the people best suited for tasks. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L300)
- Prost's wife and two surviving daughters regard his loss of levels as terrible but made up for by a superior class. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L299)
- Prost shouts "Kneel before Emperor Laken, the Protector of Riverfarm!" to the Windrest refugees until Laken elbows him quiet. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L92-93)
- Prost organises the Windrest refugees by class, with Carpenters and Woodcutters going straight to work. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L110-111)
- Prost leads Laken to ground covered in sawdust and plans stone work from the quarry. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L40-41)
- Laken sees that Prost's wife is the one who lost her leg in the Goblin attack, though his children are alive. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L359)
- Prost tells Laken that his people are sworn to him and bound to him by more than class or Skill. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L360-367)
- Prost tells Laken that Rehanna had likely been prompted, and that only the adventurers brought alcohol. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L242-248)
## Prost as right-hand man (Book 6-7)
- Prost coughs gently to stop Laken when he is being foolish, a system they have developed. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L44)
- Prost reports that three villagers became [Tinkerers], and Tessia learned [Detect Flaw]. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L99-106)
- Laken says Prost is now his right-hand man who oversees construction, settles disputes and coordinates food and housing. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L74)
- Prost tells Laken that families fear nobles attending the banquet will seduce or carry off their daughters. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L175-183)
- Laken suspects Prost nudged him to intervene in the builders' feud because the two men would not heed a new [Steward]. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L443-447)
- {belief} Prost tells Laken that [Emperors] do not make mistakes, because they know what they are doing. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L457-461)
## Prost in the Goblin war and after (Book 12)
- Prost pools villagers' coins, charges no rent, and makes everyone work for their food. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L85-88,L106)
- Prost says he would obey Laken without question, because Laken found his daughter in snow ten feet deep. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L134)
- Prost admits to Durene that he never apologised after the avalanche. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L114)
- Durene tells Prost she was terrified when she beat a thief four years ago, and cried for three days afterward. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L466-469)
- Prost tells the village that Laken is about two weeks away with the [Engineer] teams, escorted by two [Lords]. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L54)
- Prost uses [Crowd Control] to silence the crowd. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L168-169)
- Prost declares an execution just, makes Hedag swear on a truth stone, and says his authority rules until Laken returns. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L56-60,L83)
- Prost agrees to send wagons, Beniar and bows to help, and strides out before Rie can object. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L977-978)
- Prost orders Belavierr in Laken's name not to bargain with anyone, and she walks past him without a word. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L393-396)
- Eloise promises Prost that the coven will remind Belavierr of Laken's request. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L402-405)
- Prost lost children in the Riverfarm avalanche, and Belavierr shows him a picture of a boy waving at him. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1321,L1329)
- Prost sees his living children Finnon and Margery run toward him in the street, led by Califor. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1355-1358)
