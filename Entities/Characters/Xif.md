# Xif
> Type: person | Also called: Master Xif | First appears: Ch6.09
> See also: Saliss, Erin Solstice, Octavia, Numbtongue, Mrsha
## Overview
- Xif is a Gnoll [Alchemist] who runs the shop Cunning Crafts in Pallass, on the ninth floor among the alchemists' houses. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L707-713)
- Xif calls himself a City Gnoll and has shaggy tan fur stained every colour and singed in two places. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L709,L713)
- Xif smells overwhelmingly of chemicals and says his sense of smell has mostly gone as a byproduct of his work. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L710-711)
- Xif claims to be only the second-best [Alchemist] in Pallass and says someone far more specialised is better than him. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L529-531)
- Xif is the most famous regular [Alchemist] in Pallass, specialised in selling his creations, and is Saliss' friend or rival. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L150; Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L209)
## Skills
- Xif says he specialises in plant work, with [Poison Immunity] and [Perfect Reduction] among his focused Skills. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L377)
- Xif invokes [The Avid Collector] to force a sale: Erin must name a price for one Faerie Flower, and she says 220 gold. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L571-575)
## Role in Book 12

- {event} Xif buys one Faerie Flower for 220 gold from Erin, pulling out a bag of holding and stacking the coins on a table. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L575-579)
