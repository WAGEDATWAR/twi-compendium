# Takhatres
> Type: person | Also called: Takhatres, Lord of the Skies; the Lord of the Skies | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00K
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Garuda, Mars, Gazi, Drevish, Orthenon
## Overview
- Takhatres is a Garuda, a bird-man native to Chandrar, with blue and green feathers, a bird's face with a curved beak and clothing mixed with light fabric. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L52; Book 5, Ch4.00K L35)
- Takhatres has wings for arms, with long feathers and nimble talons at the ends. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L184)
- Takhatres is called the Lord of the Skies and is one of the King of Destruction's Seven. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L34,L80)
- Takhatres is a vassal of Flos Reimarch and a sworn enemy of the Empire of Sands. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L34)
- Takhatres cannot fly, but runs like a man and is called the fastest warrior on the continent. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L91-92)

## Service to Flos
- Takhatres tells Flos he has brought his tribe, which has quadrupled in number since he last led it to war. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L299)
- Takhatres says his warriors are trained to fight fiercer than before and will blot out the sun on Flos's enemies. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L299)
- Flos says he would not trade five armies for Takhatres' tribe and expects it to spearhead the war. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L300)
- Takhatres bows his head, crossing his wings together, and murmurs what sounds like a prayer at the sight of Drevish's head. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L371)
- Takhatres drinks two cups of wine at most and sips slowly, making his drink last. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L44)
- Takhatres tells Flos he will lead his kin into the left as Flos attacks, to take out the enemy mages. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L45)
- Takhatres leaves the very night he promised, to strike the first blow against the Emperor of Sands with his tribe. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L134)

## Fighting style and later
- Takhatres' army of Garuda drops stones from hundreds of feet overhead, and a ten-pound stone crushes a camel's back. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L63-65)
- Takhatres has small blades in his feathered wings and daggers on his claws that cut through steel armor. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L96-99)
- Takhatres leads a tribe against an army of eight thousand led by a young Sand commander, whose subordinate Khal fears him. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L33-35)
- Takhatres fought two battles against armies sent by the Empress of Sands specialised against Garuda, dealing as much damage as his tribe received, then retreated. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L37)
- {event} Takhatres becomes a moving force, striking weak targets and rallying enemies of the Empire of Sands against her. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L37)
