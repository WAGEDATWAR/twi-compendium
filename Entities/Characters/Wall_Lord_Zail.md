# Wall Lord Zail
> Type: person | Also called: Zail Gemscale | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.50I
> See also: Ilvriss, Alrric, Salazsar
## Overview
- Wall Lord Zail is Ilvriss' father; he is a very old Drake, eighty-three years of age. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L364)
- Zail Gemscale is the father of Ilvriss and the husband of Helessia Gemscale, and the family of four is split between father and son on one side and mother and daughter Navine on the other. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L615; Book 15, Ch7.04 L760-761)
## Description and health
- Zail is retired and bored; he complains that a Gnoll is managing everything now and that he never should have quit managing the mines. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L366,L378)
- Zail drifts between sharp attention and confusion, though an old Drake still keeps all his levels and Skills. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L364,L367,L380)
- Zail has a scar on his chest where lightning struck him and a slight indentation along his skull where the scales are almost gone, and the text says he sacrificed much for the city he loved. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L383)
- Zail keeps his quarters at the peak of the tower, right above Ilvriss' quarters, and has slipped his attendants to visit his son. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L367-368)
- Zail dislikes the diet his [Healers] prescribe and says they want him to live forever. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L417-420)
- Zail's wife Helessia lives in a separate tower, which spares her from constantly running into him. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L615)
## Book 13: the ring and the oath
- Zail asks whether his new tower is finished, and Ilvriss walks him to a window to see it, a tower that Gemscale funds helped build. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L384-389)
- Zail says that when he dies his ashes should be scattered from the tower, and Ilvriss answers that he knows. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L390-391)
- Zail notices that Ilvriss no longer wears the ring Zail gave him, and demands to know whom he is marrying. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L393-395)
- Zail says he knows exactly what the ring means, since he gave it to Ilvriss, and that it stands for trust. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L399)
- Zail repeats rumours that Ilvriss left his duty to play in a border city, lost his head, or fled the Goblin Lord out of cowardice, and asks where Ilvriss was when General Shivertail died. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L405-408)
- Zail twists a ring that casts a truth spell and makes Ilvriss swear his loyalty to Salazsar. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L411-413)
- The truth stone glows blue at Ilvriss' oath, and Zail relaxes and repeats the oath's final line. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L414-415)
- Zail later asks when Ilvriss will bring home a wife worthy of their line, and while the truth stone still shines on Zail's claw Ilvriss answers that there was one but he could not even bury her. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L423-425)
## Book 15: the heirlooms
- Zail wears his sword in its scabbard and watches a scrying orb replay the Wyvern attack on Pallass; he is paler than usual and his claws shake. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L715-719)
- Zail had fought in wars before and commanded during the two Antinium Wars, though his health is gone. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L719)
- Zail recoils from the sight of Bird on the scrying orb and asks what madness lets an Antinium stand on a wall, even in Pallass. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L723-728)
- Zail says that Drakes killed an Antinium called the Slayer and others, and Ilvriss supplies the name Devrkr the Glowing. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L731-732)
- Zail says he saw Devrkr die and that they had to kill it again and again because it kept coming back. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L733)
- Zail counts three of the Antinium already and now four, and warns Ilvriss not to underestimate Bird. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L735-738)
- Zail wears a ring that casts a more powerful [Truth] spell, and Ilvriss speaks carefully near him. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L730)
- Ilvriss asks Zail for permission to withdraw heirlooms from Salazsar's treasuries; Zail hesitates over the artifacts, then agrees and says he will get the key. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L744-752)
## Book 18: at Alrric's company
- Zail's family holds the mining company run by Alrric, and Alrric tells him it makes excellent profits, better since Ilvriss' return. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L483,L488)
- Zail answers that the company had better be profitable since his son has placed trust in Alrric, and by 'you' he means Gnolls, glaring as if Ilvriss had been overly generous. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L484-485)
- Zail comes to inspect the company leaning on a cane after sneaking out without his minders, whom Alrric had already alerted. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L487-489)
- Zail is escorted back by two Drakes sent by Ilvriss, and immediately demands to see the new task force. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L490-492)
