# Ilvriss
> Type: person | Also called: Ilvriss Gemscale; Wall Lord Ilvriss; Lord of the Wall Ilvriss | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.24

## Overview
- Ilvriss Gemscale is a Drake [Lord] and Wall Lord of the Walled City of Salazsar. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L291; Book 7, Ch5.01 L206; Book 5, Ch4.09 L8)
- Ilvriss has a sworn duty as a Lord of the Wall to defend his city and Drakes from enemies. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L363)
- Ilvriss tends to be proud and quick to anger toward Humans and Goblins, but later changes in his dealings with Erin and Liscor. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L19-21; Book 18, Ch7.33I L583-584,L629)
