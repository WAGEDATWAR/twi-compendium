# Siri
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.04D
> See also: Daly, Dawson, Kirana, Luan, Paige
## Overview
- Siri is a Swedish woman from Earth who joined the Bushrangers in Baleros, and who has no relation to the voice assistant of the same name. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L24-25)
- Siri and Tofte are the two Swedes among the Bushrangers. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L24)
- Siri was picked up by the Bushrangers three weeks before a later scene. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L655)
- Siri came up with the idea of camouflage, which is how she and Tofte survived after being transported to Baleros. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L655)
- Siri likes all things Lord of the Rings. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L738)
## Roles
- {event} Daly names Siri his backup leader, calling her the best of the Bushrangers and a solid head. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L485)
- Paige names no leader of her own, though she says Kirana is good but bosses around the girls from her country. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L489-491)
- Siri visits the Centaur landlady Miss Hastel with Daly, and asks when her child is due. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L163-167,L213)
- Siri sits white-faced on the ground after the birth, having once faced down a serpent with a crossbow and a dagger. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1059)
## Years later
- Siri has the names of her family tattooed in silver on the inside of her arm, including her brother. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L352)
- Siri sometimes forgets she has a brother, and Luan has a matching tattoo in gold. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L353)
- {belief} Tattooing names so as not to forget is a growing trend among some of the company. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L353)
