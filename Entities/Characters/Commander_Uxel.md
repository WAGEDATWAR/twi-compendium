# Commander Uxel
> Type: person | Also called: Uxel | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Cirille, Delezza, Merish, Viri, Ser Vorn, Lizardfolk
## Overview
- Commander Uxel is the commander of a Lizardfolk company at the Blighted Kingdom's 4th and 5th Walls. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1095,L1198; Book 18, Ch7.35C L345)
- Uxel is a Gorgon, a tall muscular serpent-man with a fast-flicking tongue and serpentine eyes, who fights with scimitars. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1098,L1209,L1240,L1422)
- Uxel is a Gorgon whose stare can paralyse enemies. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L572-574)
- Uxel seems to hate Drakes more than Cirille objects to Lizardfolk, or perhaps takes Shellc's threats very personally. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1240)
## Meeting Cirille
- Commander Cirille apologises to Uxel for a subordinate's rude threats after the subordinate calls Uxel's company friends. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1094-1098)
- Uxel marches with Cirille's Drakes, Gnolls and his own Lizardfolk out of 4th Wall towards 5th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1100,L1308)
- Uxel tells Cirille that the records say the real wall lies about two miles behind the visible one, under construction. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1310-1312)
- Uxel hisses and draws his scimitars when Cirille's group sees serpents that Uxel says are not snakes. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1420-1424)
## The card nights
- Uxel plays the nightly card game with Delezza, Ser Vorn, Merish and Cirille, and is hard to read only because he is a serpent. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1198-1209)
- Uxel and Ser Vorn watch Delezza suspiciously before she and a Garuda commander vanish when the glowing card is played. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1212)
- Uxel throws down his cards with a hiss after a loss. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1284)
- Cirille wonders whether Uxel is reading body temperatures. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1265)
## The Demon war
- Uxel moves with Cirille to intercept serpent-titans on the wall in the battle for the 5th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L83)
- Uxel grins as he runs through a lumbering Demon nearly as big as he is. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L221)
- Uxel glares about, gesturing at the sky, and Delezza tells him the flying creatures are Harpies, saying they are Harpies who are scared by Gorgons. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L307-312)
- Viri leads the other wing of Uxel's Lizardfolk [Skirmishers] in the practice battle. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L331)
- Uxel has learned to respect that attacking the Drakes from the front means losing. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L353)
- Uxel grumbles during the joint practice battles, while Lizardfolk and Drakes work together better than Gnolls and Drakes. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L360)
- Uxel curses when Viri's card fails to produce the black fog. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L350)
- Uxel is under a mind-spell during the attack until Cirille punches him in the face, and he then hisses and draws himself up. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L369-372)
- Uxel holds a section of the 5th Wall curtain with his Lizardfolk, some Drakes and Merish's Gnolls, and links with Cirille's forces. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L569-576)
- Cirille says she will ask a Wall Lord to hire Uxel's company as well as some Gnolls and Terandrian [Knights]. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L88,L113)
