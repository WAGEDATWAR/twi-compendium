# Lord Bein Terland
> Type: person | Also called: Lord Bein | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.15R
> See also: Lacel the Leaper, Hawk, Lady Dealia, Hetessana Wellfar
## Overview
- Lord Bein Terland is a [Lord] of the Terland family, one of the Five Families of Izril, though not from the main branch. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L7)
- The Terland family is known mainly for its trade in magical artifacts, and has fallen on hard times since its days of glory. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L18)
- Lord Bein's father is Lord Sheldae Terland, whom he persuaded to support his engagement to Lady Dealia, though his mother disliked the concept. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L10)
- Lord Bein is a [Lordling] who travels with nearly a hundred private Terland guards. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L621)
## The engagement scandal
- Lord Bein is engaged to Lady Dealia of the Zolde nobility, who count as Chandrarian to many nobles, and the wedding is scheduled for the next month. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L7-8,L13-14)
- Lady Dealia is one of the most beautiful [Ladies] in Izril, ranked fourth on lists of top [Ladies] to marry. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L12)
- {reveal} Lord Bein had been secretly seeing the widowed Lady Hetessana Wellfar for about two years, before falling for Dealia. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L17,L23)
- Lord Bein flees Hetessana's bedroom half-naked as she pursues him, calling him a faithless dog. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L16-17,L22)
- Lord Bein tells Hetessana he cannot call off the engagement and that his hand was forced, claiming he tried to talk his father out of it. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L30)
- Lord Bein puts a ten-thousand-gold bounty on Lady Hetessana's pendant, spread through unofficial channels. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L70-71)
## The Couriers' run
- {event} Lord Bein orders his guards to spread out and block the road so no one goes around the Couriers. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L621-622)
- Salamani uses [Invisibility] and [Flash Step], Tritel and Ci clear the guards with [Gazelle's Leap], and Hawk runs around them, so the blockade fails. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L649-659)
- Clods of dirt thrown up by Ci's landing hit Lord Bein in the head as the Couriers pass him. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L656-657)
- {reveal} Lady Dealia confirms the locket is Bein's, and the Zolde brothers announce the engagement is off, and Qiam calls Bein a filthy Terland rat owing a blood debt. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L822-825,L832)
- {reveal} Noass tells the crowd that Lord Bein is unfaithful and that the Zolde and Terland Houses are at war, though the older Zolde brother says they did not say war. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L834-836)
- {state} Lord Bein is outed as a treacherous philanderer, and the Zolde family and the Terland House are at war. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L843)
