# Safry
> Type: person | Also called: Safry and Maran (with co-worker Maran) | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.12
> See also: Maran, Lyonette, Erin, Agnes, Mrsha, Ishkr
## Overview
- Safry is a Human [Barmaid] who works at Agnes' inn, the Frenzied Hare, alongside Maran, an older [Barmaid] and her friend. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L383,L400-410)
- Safry is a Level 21 [Barmaid], according to Maran, while Maran is a Level 22 [Barmaid]. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L35)
- Safry and Maran claim to be the highest-leveled workers at Erin's inn. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L33-35)
- Safry says she worked at Agnes' inn for over eight years. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L201)
## At Agnes' inn
- {event} Safry asks Erin to hire her, then calls Maran over, and the two say they do not like working at Agnes' inn. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L400-413)
- Safry and Maran say Agnes screams at staff over wrong orders and docks pay for breaks, and that she is tight with her coin. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L416-419)
- Safry says Agnes told her she would be fired if she did not work with a terrible cold, though Maran was down with the same illness. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L418)
- {belief} Safry says Agnes is only Level 18 despite nearly a decade at the inn, and that Agnes' husband holds the cooking Skills. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L437)
- {belief} Safry says Agnes is afraid of Erin, a Level 20 [Innkeeper] at least, because her inn earns ten times more since Erin came. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L431-437)
## At Erin's inn
- {event} Erin hires Safry and Maran along with Drassi and Ishkr to help Lyonette run the inn. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L17)
- {event} Safry and Maran treat Lyonette's advice as suggestions and think of themselves as in charge. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L63)
- Safry serves a Drake the wrong food and cannot tell Drakes apart, saying they all look the same. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L64-73)
- Safry serves Gold-rank adventurers first, leaving Zel for last, hoping to be tipped. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L136-142)
- {belief} Safry says Gold-rank adventurers throw gold around like water and tip a gold coin just for serving their table. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L145)
- Safry and Maran flirt with Halrac, Ulrien and Typhenous of Griffon Hunt. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L146)
- Safry and Maran are visibly uncomfortable around Bird and walk wide of him. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L110-111)
- {event} Safry pushes Mrsha off a table onto the floor, and Lyonette threatens her until Ishkr drags Lyonette back. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L37-47)
- {event} Safry and Maran bring about two dozen Human friends and take a thirty minute lunch break at the height of the rush. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L107-118)
- Safry and Maran say the Innkeeper's Guild policy gives them a break, and Erin agreed to it. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L117)
- {state} Safry quits, saying she deserves better treatment than Lyonette gives, and Maran says she will quit too if Safry does. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L198-202)
- {state} Erin fires Safry and Maran for their conduct, saying she believes Ryoka and Lyonette. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L233)
- {belief} Safry and Maran claim Agnes will not take them back and demand pay from Erin. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L240)
- {event} The inn's [Inn's Aura] Skill terrifies Safry and Maran into fleeing the inn into Octavia's shop. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L249)
