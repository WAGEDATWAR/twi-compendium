# Tallis the Stormbreaker
> Type: person | Also called: Tallis Stormbreaker; the Stormbreaker | First appears: Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4
> See also: Xrn, Wrymvr the Deathless, Velan the Kind, Krsysl Wordsmith
## Overview
- Tallis the Stormbreaker is a Goblin Lord and the great [Shaman] of the Goblin King's armies. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L18-19)
- Velan the Kind left the southern strategy to Tallis, the mightiest of his Goblin Lords. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L17-18)
## Role in the Second Antinium War
- Tallis joined the war between the Antinium and Drakes late, beginning by sacking port cities on the western coast. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L19)
- Tallis then moved slowly inland and routed Drake armies with contemptuous ease through his magical might. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L19)
- The Antinium were already besieging Pallass and Zeres when Tallis advanced, and Drakes despaired at news of him. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L19)
- Tallis's advance brought him close to an Antinium Hive, which sent Xrn the Small Queen against him. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L20)
- Tallis annihilated that Antinium army with a single spell within ten minutes of its charge, the Antinium's first great defeat of the war. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L20)
- Tallis defeated the first, second and third armies the Antinium sent against him, according to Xrn. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L31)
## The hole in the sky
- {event} Tallis opened a hole in the sky above the Antinium charge, which widened into a vortex that tore up trees, rocks and earth. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L40-42)
- Tallis's spell sucked tens of thousands of Antinium Soldiers and Workers upward, and Xrn could not dispel it. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L44-46)
- Even the Flying Antinium were dragged hundreds of miles up and hurled down by the winds. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L46)
- Tallis laughed with a voice like thunder while other Goblin [Shamans] and [Mages] broke Xrn's spells, and she fled. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L47)
- Tallis could not cast the grand spell on the second or third army sent against him, but still broke them with magic. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L55)
- Tallis grew more exhausted with each spell he cast, according to Xrn. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L55)
- After the three defeats, the Grand Queen ordered Klbkch, Wrymvr and Xrn to break the Goblin King's might at any cost. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L55)
## Death
- {state} Three Archmages of Wistram cornered Tallis's army and fought him in a magical battle lasting two days and two nights. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L109)
- On the second day Xrn and an Antinium force attacked from his side, and the history thinks her unpredictable magic helped overwhelm him. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L109)
- Tallis unleashed a storm as he fell that battered Izril for a week with drenching rain and hurricane winds. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L109)
- Tallis's fall told the world that the Goblin King was on the verge of defeat. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L109)
