# Welca Caveis
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Caveis; Lady Welca Caveis; Welca | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.19G
> See also: Kerrig, Bethal, Pyrite, Pellmia, Tyrion Veltras
## Overview
- Welca Caveis is a young Knight of the Petal with black hair, and a junior [Knight]. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L169,L186; Book 7, Ch5.20G L677)
- Welca comes from a larger noble house than Sir Kerrig, though he is her senior in the order. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L5-6)
- The Knights of the Petal admit no one below Level 20, the prerequisite for the [Knight] class. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L677)
## Captivity (Book 7)
- Welca had been dueled by Pyrite and nearly drowned in battle before her capture. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L186)
- {event} Welca and Sir Kerrig are captured by the Flooded Waters tribe after trees are dropped on them. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L169,L184)
- Welca rages at her Goblin guards, fears for her chastity and honor, and demands to be killed. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L170,L186)
- Rags forbids any abuse of her prisoners, and the Goblins only want to give Welca food and clothing. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L187)
- {event} Welca tries to flee after Pyrite nods her back to her wagon, and he knocks her out gently. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L279-280)
- {belief} Welca insists the Goblins are monsters who will always be Humanity's enemy, even if they can speak. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L620)
- {event} Welca escapes the Goblin camp on a horse at Sir Kerrig's order, promising to return. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L636,L644-646)
## Search for Kerrig (Books 8-9)
- {belief} Lady Bethal believes Welca and Sir Kerrig perished until Rags says both are prisoners. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L159,L173-177)
- {event} Welca rides with Lord Pellmia's cavalry and tells him she cannot see Sir Kerrig among the Goblin army. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L441-443)
- {event} Lord Pellmia leads a charge using [Second Wind] alongside Welca. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L445)
- Welca had informed Lord Veltras of the Goblin tribe. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L441)
- Welca has been searching for her missing companion Sir Kerrig, and Tyrion tells her he was safe in Riverfarm. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L248-253)
- Welca is assigned to Lord Pellmia's command to help locate the Goblins. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L162)
## Later
- Welca reports to Lady Bethal that the Runner's Guild confirmed the roses were all genuine. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L410-412)
