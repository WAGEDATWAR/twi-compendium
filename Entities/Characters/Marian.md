# Marian
> Type: person | Also called: Marian Felthof; Fast Hooves (Umina's code name) | First appears: Ch1.00H
> See also: Umina, Niers Astoragon, Perorn, Feshi, Tulm the Mithril
## Overview
- Marian is a Centauress [Strategist], one of Niers Astoragon's students, and the friend of the Lizardgirl Umina. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L146-151)
## A different Marian: the human mage of the Horns of Hammerad (Book 1)
- Marian is a human mage in the Horns of Hammerad, and the only other human mage in the group besides Sostrom. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L6)
- Marian is a solid Level 16 [Elementalist], ordinary in both class choice and spell selection, who pulls her weight in the party. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L7)
- Ceria does not consider Marian a true adventurer, because Marian only wants to earn enough money to start a store or an inn. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L9)
- Ceria has not worked out whether Sostrom and Marian are sleeping together. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L6)
## Marian the Centauress, student of the Titan (Book 2 onward)
### Introduction and background
- A Centauress named Marian is one of Niers Astoragon's students; Centaurs dislike small places such as his command tent, as horses do. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L251-254)
- {belief} Marian guesses that Niers' unbeatable chess opponent must be a Dragon, since only a brilliant mind could repeatedly defeat him. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L252-255)
- Niers rejects the guess because his opponent switched to a risky style after losing, which he reads as curiosity, whereas Dragons are touchy and hate losing. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L258-261)
- Niers calls Marian's guess a good one, and tells her kindly that she could not have known the opponent's behaviour. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L257-263)
- Her full name is Marian Felthof, and a young Centaur laborer in the crowd shouts it to cheer her on. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L97-98)
- Marian received a great deal of support from her Centaur clan, whose members all contributed money to fund her studies, and she feels she must repay them. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L701-702)

- Marian brings back a huge yellowed fang from a freshly hunted creature as her homework for the Professor. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L15)

### Aftermath of the game
- {state} Marian's leg is badly burned by a Dullahan [Mage]'s fire spell, and she can smell her cooked flesh. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L15,L20)
- An old Lizardwoman [Healer] identifies it as a Tier 2 magic burn, and first applies a caustic cream that hurts badly so the potion can reach the skin. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L22-28)
- {state} The [Healer] then treats the leg with a healing potion, the flesh regrows, and she tells Marian not to gallop for a day. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L36,L73)
- {belief} Perorn angrily tells Tulm the Mithril that his fire spell could have killed Marian or crippled her for life, as it seared her to the bone. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L39-40)
- Tulm explains that the fire spell was an error by his panicking [Mage]. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L41)
- Perorn rushes to Marian and offers a higher quality potion, which the [Healer] says she does not need. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L31-35)
- Niers tells Marian that the flaw of every [Strategist] is weakness in combat compared to [Warriors], after she admits she fared poorly at close quarters. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L170-171)
- {event} Niers' reward for Marian is personal instruction from his weapons masters until she can fend off even a Level 30 [Warrior] with a bow. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L171)
- {event} Marian hugs Umina but says she is not forgiving her yet, because she lost money and must now duel a [Weapon Master] every morning. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L360-361)
- Marian demands details of what Niers' room is like and what he says to Umina as payment. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L361)
- Marian drinks mug after mug of alcohol and becomes cheerful, in line with the legendary Centaur tolerance for it. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L362)
