## Overview
- A man describes the Necromancer as a powerful [Necromancer] with pale white hair, white skin and violet eyes, who may use illusions to hide them. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L135)
- The same man says the Necromancer has an army and a kingdom of the dead. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L138)

- {reveal} Az'kerash appears to Ryoka as an old man with lined face, white hair and albino skin, resembling Teriarch's human form without the physique. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L337-339)
