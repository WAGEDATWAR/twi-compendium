# Flying Queen
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons
> See also: Free Queen, Grand Queen, Klbkch, Pivr
## Overview
- The Flying Queen is an Antinium Queen whose Flying Antinium are designed for aerial superiority. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L89)
- The Flying Queen's Hive is normally a chaos of Antinium moving around her, with winged attendants buzzing about. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L90)
- The Flying Queen's creations show originality but lack of focus. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1068)
## Book 15: contact with the Free Queen
- {event} The Flying Queen contacts the Free Queen by mirror while her Hive is in uproar, and asks whether the Free Queen's Bird is a challenge. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L89-90)
- The Flying Queen worries that ranged Antinium might be an overture to the Armored Antinium, and that the Grand Queen is punishing the Free Queen. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L91)
- The Flying Queen asks why the Free Queen's food is so colorful and whether it is a new nutritional mix. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L91)
- The Flying Queen calms as the Free Queen explains that Bird is a single Individual and not an attempt to jeopardize her Hive. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L94-100)
- {event} The Flying Queen says the tunnel is completed, as Pivr tells her, and agrees to talk. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L102)
- The Flying Queen asks why the Armored Queen sent Prognugators before the Flying Antinium did. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L102)
## Book 18: the Queens' plans
- The Flying Queen has yet to get any of her Antinium to fly, but assures Klbkch that she will soon. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1068)
- The Flying Queen plans to give her Soldiers thorn-projectiles in place of their acidic bile. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1069)
- {belief} The Flying Queen says the Walled Cities will fall if her Hive bypasses their walls with flight. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1070-1071)
- The Flying Queen says flight will allow the Antinium to return to Rhir, and plans for them to fly across the sea with wings. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1071-1077)
- Klbkch finds the Flying Queen innovative but disinclined to do more than encourage her to work with the Free Queen. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1078)
- {event} The Flying Queen shows Klbkch a stuttering Worker from her own Individual experiments, which can say "I" only after several attempts. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1080-1088)
- The Flying Queen says the stuttering Worker has no name yet, but she believes she will create Individuals in time. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1083-1085)
