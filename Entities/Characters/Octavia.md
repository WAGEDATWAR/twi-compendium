# Octavia
> Type: person | Also called: Octavia Cotton; Miss Cotton; Stitch-Girl; Stitchgirl | First appears: Ch2.09
> See also: Erin Solstice, Ryoka Griffin, Saliss of Lights, Krshia Silverfang, Xif, Quelm, Numbtongue
## Overview
- Octavia Cotton is a young dark-skinned [Alchemist] who owns the shop Stitchworks in Celum. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L306,L316; Book 10, Ch6.02 L2)
- Octavia is a String Person, a Stitch-Girl whose body is cloth held together by string, and she can remove and reattach limbs. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L364-366; Book 10, Ch6.02 L44-46)
- Octavia has a braided ponytail or dreadlocks, stitch marks around her shoulders and neck, and a muscular build. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L316,L322; Book 2, Ch2.37 L336,L345)
- Octavia is a String-Girl of the Cotton folk, which is where her surname comes from. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L45)
- Octavia says she is from Chandrar, and specifically from Nerrhavia Fallen. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L548,L557)
- Octavia has not returned to Nerrhavia in nearly five years, but she still receives [Messages] with gossip from there. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L273)
## Body and abilities
- Octavia feels sensations, burns easily, and would die if cut apart, and she cannot throw her arm like a Dullahan. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L109)
- Octavia can take her arm off, and the cloth is unharmed by the effect of a potion, which still works after she reattaches it. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L217-218)
- Octavia has Skills that protect her against acid, burns and poison. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L38)
- Octavia is a Level 21 [Alchemist], just at the point where she could take on an apprentice. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L29)
- Octavia has the Skills [Magic-Water Solvent] and [Mineral Distillation]. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L379)
- Octavia removes her stitched hand to doodle on a table, and her cotton eyes can pop out of their sockets. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L367; Book 2, Ch2.14 L199-203)
