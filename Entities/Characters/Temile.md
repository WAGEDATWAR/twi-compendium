# Temile
> Type: person | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.56
> See also: Jasi, Erin Solstice, Players of Celum
## Overview
- Temile is an [Actor] who has become a low-level [Producer] in charge of the second-string Players of Celum. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L174)
- Temile whips a new cast of Drakes, Gnolls and Humans into shape during rehearsal. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L174)
- Temile says the Players who went north are earning more money than they can spend in sold-out shows near Invrisil. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L183)
- Temile says money from the northern team has let the troupe hire [Builders] and [Carpenters] and lease apartments. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L185)
- Temile's [Producer] class gave him a few Skills, among them [Eye for Talent], [Pinpoint Discord] and [Revitalizing Touch]. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L546)
- Temile has a stump on his hand, and Jasi observes it, since he fought Crelers. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L615-616)
