# Timbor Parithad
> Type: person | Also called: Timbor; Mister Timbor | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.18
> See also: Erin Solstice, Garia Strongheart, Ksmvr
## Overview
- Timbor Parithad is a big, bearded, red-haired man who owns the Drunken Swordsman, which he calls one of the finest inns in Celum. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L16; Book 5, Ch4.18 L27)
- Timbor is a Level 24 [Innkeeper]. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L78)
## Feud with Erin
- Timbor threatens Erin and tells her to make things right, as an experienced [Innkeeper] lecturing an upstart. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L77-78)
- {belief} Pisces says Timbor holds a particular grudge against Erin Solstice, having been one of the [Innkeepers] who upset her. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L50-51)
- Garia protests that Timbor is a great innkeeper who has had her over for years, and wants to speak to Erin. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L52)
- Timbor worries about the Antinium Ksmvr in the room and asks the Horns of Hammerad for a promise that it will not act up. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L183)
- Timbor sends a free round of drinks around the room. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L198)
## The loss of the Drunken Swordsman
- {state} Timbor sits on the ground in tears before the ruins of his inn, which is gone. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L1154)
- Erin sits with Timbor and hugs him as he breaks down, though they were enemies a day earlier. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L1156-1157)
- Erin takes Timbor to the magic door and helps him through the line, and Lyonette leads him to a bed. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L1158-1160)
- {state} Later, Timbor leaves Celum and opens a second Drunken Swordsman in Liscor under the new name The Drunken Gnoll. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1018-1020)
