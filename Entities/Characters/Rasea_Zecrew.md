# Rasea Zecrew
> Type: person | Also called: Rasea; Rasea the Shining; Captain Rasea; Hero-death (Goblin name) | First appears: Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1
> See also: Revine, Wil, Lasc, Flos, Orthenon, Ulyse, Illuminary
## Overview
- Rasea Zecrew is a half-Drowned [Pirate Captain] of the Illuminary, glowing yellow in her right eye and shoulder like her ship. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1387,L1453)
- Rasea is half-Anglerfish, grins with sharp teeth on one side of her face, and keeps the other half of her face Human. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1387-1388)
- Venaz names Rasea as Captain Rasea Zecrew of the Illuminary, called Rasea the Shining, one of the most feared [Pirates] on the seas. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1453)
- Rasea is the younger sister of Revine Zecrew, the Siren of Savere, and calls Revine her big sister. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1455-1456)
- Captain Lasc says Rasea joined the Undersea Crews, the drowned Folk's brand of piracy, and took the Sea's Bargain. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1498)
- Lasc says Rasea later left the Undersea Crews and is a [Pirate] who preys on everyone, including Drowned vessels. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1498-1499)
