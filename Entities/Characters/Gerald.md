# Gerald
> Type: person | Also called: captain of Kyrial's Pride; the axe-captain | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.00H
> See also: Cervial, Lir, Olesm, Skinner
## Overview
- Gerald is the captain of Kyrial's Pride, a Silver-rank adventuring team. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L242)
- Gerald is a big man in armor who carries a battleaxe almost as large as Calruz's. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L242; Book 1, Ch1.00H L162)
## In the Ruins
- Gerald's team declares itself armed and ready to fight, and he scowls at Olesm for tagging along. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L149,L162-163)
- Gerald says his team will not quit until they reach the bottom floor, a change from what he said earlier. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L48)
- {event} Gerald calls Olesm a coward for running, before another mage explains that Olesm saved them with a skill. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L140-145)
- {event} Gerald uses [Whirlwind Cleave] to scythe through three zombies and a ghoul. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L243-244)
- {state} Gerald's face is seen in Skinner's mass of skinned bodies, along with Cervial and Lir, which shows he has been killed and absorbed. (Book 1, Ch1.02H L271-282)
