# Visma
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.06
> See also: Ekirra, Mrsha, Selena, Feiss
## Overview
- Visma is a young Drake girl with pearly-white scales speckled with greens, the daughter of the [Painter] Selena. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L332-333)
- Visma is shy and timid, and likes copying her mother by making sculptures out of mud or things she finds. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L343)
- Visma became friends with Mrsha when she asked to paint Mrsha, whose lovely fur she likened to a canvas. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L344)
- Visma is in her hoarding year, according to Selena. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L368)
- Visma has an older brother, Feiss, a young Drake of around fourteen who is a [Fletcher]'s apprentice. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Gold L233,L239)
