# Lady Dealia
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Dealia Zolde; Dealia | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.15R
> See also: Lord Bein, Hawk, Tritel, Salamani
## Overview
- Lady Dealia is a [Lady] of the minor house of the Zolde nobility, whose family fled unrest in Chandrar two hundred years earlier. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L2,L7)
- Lady Dealia is regarded by many nobles as still Chandrarian, and counts among the most beautiful [Ladies] in all of Izril. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L8,L12)
- Lady Dealia is ranked fourth on the list of the top ten [Ladies] to marry, just below Lady Wuvren. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L12)
- Lady Dealia has two brothers, the younger of whom is Qiam and might be seventeen. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L821,L825,L831)
## Engagement to Lord Bein
- Lady Dealia is engaged to Lord Bein of the Terland family, one of the Five Families of Izril, in a match thought scandalous. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L2,L7,L10)
- Lady Dealia's family was delighted by the match, and Lord Bein won over his father, Lord Sheldae Terland, to support it. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L10)
- {belief} Lord Bein was said to have been seeing Lady Hetessana of the Wellfar family for two years before falling for Lady Dealia. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L23)
- Lady Dealia is troubled by the claims against Lord Bein but stands by him at first. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L65)
- {belief} Rumor says Lady Dealia has promised to call off the engagement if the claim is true, and that her brothers swore to stab Lord Bein. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L237)
- Lord Bein tries to reassure Lady Dealia that the matter is a misunderstanding before heading south to recover the item. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L608)
## The delivery
- Couriers Tritel and Hawk race to Lady Dealia's convoy of minor nobles, and her brothers and bodyguards reach for weapons. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L803-804)
- Lady Dealia confirms her name to the Couriers and receives the Terland amulet. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L805-806,L815-819)
- Lady Dealia says she knows the amulet is real because it is Bein's, and concludes that Lord Bein was unfaithful. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L822-824)
- Lady Dealia is truly heartbroken, and her brother Qiam declares the engagement off before a scrying mirror. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L823,L831-833)
