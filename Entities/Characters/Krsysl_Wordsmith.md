# Krsysl Wordsmith
> Type: person | Also called: Wordsmith | First appears: Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3
> See also: Niers Astoragon, Zel Shivertail, Magnolia Reinhart, Tallis the Stormbreaker, Elia Arcsinger
## Overview
- Krsysl Wordsmith is the [Writer] who authored "The Second Antinium Wars, a History". (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L2,L5)
- Krsysl began writing the book six months after the Second Antinium Wars ended. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L5)
- Krsysl wrote the book because no other history of the war existed and he wanted a record before living memory faded. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L5)
- Krsysl openly admits the book may contain inaccuracies and bias, and he wants to be remembered as the first [Writer] to collate the war's accounts. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L6)
- Krsysl dismisses his critics as lesser writers and claims his account is colored only by love of people and continent. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L7)
- The book was widely sold across Izril and widely criticized, and later editions added the Introduction and substantive revisions. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L2)
## Views and style in the book
- {belief} Krsysl writes that the Walled Cities predate the construction of First Landing by at least six thousand years. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L237)
- {belief} Krsysl speculates that the Necromancer's siege of Liscor, which cut the continent in two, may have aided the Goblin King. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L329)
- {belief} Krsysl praises Drake courage and claims a single Drake [Spearman] stopped a charging Draugr that Gold-rank adventurers might struggle with. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L132)
- Tekshia scoffs at the Draugr claim, saying Draugr run down a spear and gut the wielder, and she mocks the claim that Drakes fought off wraiths with swords. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L152)
- {belief} Krsysl calls Niers Astoragon a coward for refusing to fight the Goblin King, claiming his spine was weaker than a low-level Drake [Soldier]'s. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L73-74)
- Krsysl writes that all non-Drake heroes appear to have a fatal flaw, which shapes how he judges Niers. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L73)
- Niers says he knew any battle with Velan would be disastrous because his army was dispirited and lacked unity. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L102)
- Niers Astoragon never answered Krsysl's letters, and the book leaves that part of history blank. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L77)
- Niers Astoragon had told Krsysl not to print the passage and curses him as a clod-footed village-smasher after reading it. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L78)
- Xrn says Krsysl exaggerates and twists facts to suit his narrative but does not invent false events. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L31)
- Krsysl credits Elia Arcsinger, a Silver-rank half-Elf adventurer, with killing the Goblin King Velan with a single arrow and thanks her for her steady aim. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L148,L154)
## Relationship with Magnolia Reinhart
- Magnolia Reinhart paid Krsysl to write the history of the First Antinium Wars shortly after it ended, and it was widely praised. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L198)
- Magnolia Reinhart refuses to put her name on the second history and says she did not fund it. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L193,L196)
- Krsysl invested the money from his first book into the second, which he wrote as a patriot hoping to be beloved. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L200)
- Magnolia says fame went to Krsysl's head and that the second book earned him criticism from his own people. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L200)
- Many Drakes still consider Krsysl a good writer, according to Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L200)
- Magnolia says she tried to persuade Krsysl to write a more unbiased history, but he detests her. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L200)
