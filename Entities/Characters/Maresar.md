# Maresar
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Maresar; Mares; Bandit Lord Maresar | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.02K
> See also: Venith, Calac, Flos, Gazi, Orthenon
## Overview
- Maresar is Venith's wife, a tall, dark-skinned woman with short-cropped black hair and a longbow. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L361,L503; Book 10, Ch6.12K L46)
- Maresar speaks with a slight lisp, or in a deep, slightly slurred voice. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L379; Book 5, Ch4.03K L96)
- Maresar says little but has a sense of humor, and Trey finds her hard to read. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L96)
- Maresar is a former [Bandit Lord] of renown who pledged her service to Flos after being defeated. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L115)
- Maresar says there is no [Bandit Lady] class, as all leaders are called [Bandit Lords] regardless of gender. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L120)
- Maresar's Skill [Eagle Eyes] enhances her eyesight. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L48)
## Arc
- {event} Maresar looses two arrows at Flos from a hilltop during his duel with Venith, and Flos cuts the second from the air. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L361-363)
- Maresar says she follows her King, calls Venith a fool, and says he will not die while she lives. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L378-380)
- {state} Maresar tells Venith that Flos is her King too, and rides away with half their retainers to serve him. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L494-497)
- {event} Maresar slaps Flos for leaving them, kisses his cheek, and says that was for coming back. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L504-507)
- Maresar leads her soldiers to Flos, and they kneel, having come to fight for him rather than march against him. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L512-513)
- {belief} Maresar says Venith will return in time, once he sees Flos is back in spirit as well as body. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L102)
- {event} Flos asks Maresar to lead the archers in his army, though it was not her role before, and she accepts. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L134-135)
- {event} Maresar mounts and fires into the fog, hitting enemy mages at the targets Gazi calls out for her. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L280-298)
- Maresar kills one mage and distracts two, forcing the enemy [Mages] to scramble for cover instead of dispelling the fog. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L298)
- {event} Maresar orders her archers to volley the soldiers on both flanks as the fog lifts. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L313-315)
- {state} Maresar returns to Venith's side after he joins Flos, and the two stand together. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L563)
- Maresar and Venith hold Ger, where her bow can reach half the city. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L52)
- Maresar declines Venith's monocle and judges a group of armed youths no threat using [Eagle Eyes]. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L48-49)
- Maresar notes that Venith told Calac no, so she refused him too, when Calac wanted to come to Ger. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L64)
- Maresar and Orthenon dislike each other, since she was a [Bandit Lord] and he hates criminals of all kinds. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L35-36)
