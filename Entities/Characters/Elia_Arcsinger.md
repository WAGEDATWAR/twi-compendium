# Elia Arcsinger
> Type: person | Also called: Kingslayer; the Goblin King Slayer | First appears: Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5
> See also: Velan the Kind, Tremborag, Tyrion Veltras, Reiss, Krsysl Wordsmith, Invrisil
## Overview
- Elia Arcsinger is a blonde half-Elf archer and a Named Adventurer. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L153; Book 8, Ch5.32G L472)
- Elia's team includes a younger half-Elf archer, who is her daughter, and other archers. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L474; Book 19, Ch7.43G L820)
## Killing the Goblin King
- Elia was a Silver-rank half-Elf adventurer when she loosed an arrow into the Goblin King's eye, killing him instantly. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L148)
- Elia used the Skill [Piercing Shot] at nearly point-blank range, as the Goblin King's defences had been stripped by other attacks. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L148)
- Elia owned an enchanted Arrow of Slaying known for piercing, and her longbow was certified by Gnoll [Bowmakers] to reach four hundred meters. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L150)
- {belief} Many [Strategists] argue that the Goblin King should have dodged the shot, since he had caught and dodged arrows in other battles. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L149,L152)
- {belief} A vocal minority claims Elia used a special Skill or an enchantment she was unaware of. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L152)
- Killing Velan the Kind earned Elia the title of Kingslayer and the Skill [Line-Ender Shot]. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L153)
- Elia became known as one of the greatest Named Adventurers of her age because of the kill. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L153)
## Dwarfhalls Rest
- Elia arrives above Tremborag's army in Dwarfhalls Rest by [Lesser Teleport], with four archer companions and a silver-glowing bow. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L472-476)
- Goblins freeze in dread at the sight of Elia, and Tremborag recognises her face with horror. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L473-477)
- {state} The Goblins of Dwarfhalls Rest flee in panic from the adventurer they call Kingslayer. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L2-4)
- Elia's Skill affects the whole mountain, terrifying the Goblins there, and Reiss suspects it is more than mere memory. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L10)
## The Goblin Lord's army
- Elia marches with the great Human army against the Goblin Lord, and Rags's Goblins fear her as Kingslayer. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L337-338)
- Elia stands at the head of the Human ranks beside Tyrion Veltras, and the Goblins remember golden hair, a bow and a fallen King. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L404-405)
- Elia shoots Tremborag in the back with an arrow, which only wounds him. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L407-410)
- Tyrion Veltras's [Mages] then cast a [Siege Fireball] at Tremborag, who dives clear. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L411-415)
## Later
- Elia is staying in Invrisil as a Named Adventurer, though her contract expired and her team is not Named. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L615-621)
- Elia is reclusive, and Typhenous says she is unlikely to drink with Todi. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L621)
- Halrac and Revi recall that Elia helped rout the Goblins in the Dwarfhalls Rest mountain. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L618)
- Elia's name makes Goblin tribes flee, and the Greystone Chieftain shudders at it. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L493-497)
- The Greystone Chieftain says retaliation from adventuring teams wiped out the Goblin tribes that traded with the Greystones. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L497)
- Elia leads her team against Goblins in a city street, and the crowd cheers "Kingslayer!". (Book 19, Ch7.43G L810-812)
- The Goblins in that fight chant "Arcsinger!" and a Hob raises his shield, remembering her legend. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L813-816)
- Elia fires a [Greenbane Arrow] with [Great Enemy: Goblin], which detonates against a Hob's shield and takes his arm. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L821-822)
- Elia then readies a [Line-Ender Shot] at a charging Hob. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L823-826)
- Elia and her team kill the Hobs in that street fight, and seven smaller Goblins flee because the Named Adventurer is too hard to kill. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L828)
