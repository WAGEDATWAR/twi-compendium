# Orthenon
> Type: person | Also called: Steward Orthenon; Lord Orthenon of Reim; the King's Steward; Left Hand of the King of Destruction; [Ruinbringer Steward] | First appears: ChInterlude_King_Edition
> See also: Flos, Gazi, Mars, Venith, Maresar, Raelt, Reim
## Overview
- Orthenon is the steward of King Flos, a tall, gaunt man who walks with unnatural grace and bows to his king perfunctorily when summoned. (Book 1, ChInterlude_King_Edition L51-56)
- Gazi sees Orthenon as tall, serious and once gaunt but now filled out, with a long mustache, dark somber clothing with an edge of faded gold, and a stern, unwavering, formal manner. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L27-28)
- Orthenon and those who stayed kept the palace better maintained than the rest of the ruined city. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L21)
- Orthenon says his role differs from the others' because he is the King's steward and can hardly leave his side. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L208)
- Orthenon warns the twins to stay away from Gazi, who is the most feared and perhaps the most possessive of their King. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L196)
- Orthenon is not one of the Seven but is second only to Flos, and is called the Left Hand of the King of Destruction. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L327-329)
- Orthenon was raised in the Drath Archipelago, believes in formality, and will not call Flos by name alone though Flos sees him as friend as much as vassal. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L195-197)
## Strategist and Steward (Books 5-10)
- Orthenon dresses for war in form-fitting enchanted leather with a single-edged sword at his belt, unlike Flos's plate or Gazi's scale armor. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L48-49)
- Orthenon nearly ran Trey through with a sword when the twins first met Flos, before Flos stopped him. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L106)
- Orthenon reports that he has found two more vassals in mercenary companies, one in Baleros, and sends encoded [Message] spells from other cities, which may be intercepted. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L189-192)
- {belief} Orthenon says word of Flos waking is probably known to the powerful leaders of nations and perhaps Wistram's mages or Roshal's Traders, but not the world, since he has kept a lock on it. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L199)
- Orthenon advises Flos to strike nearby nations first to recover strength before fighting the Empire of Sands. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L248)
- {belief} Orthenon tells the twins that caution may be what holds Flos back from marching. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L259)
- Flos says Orthenon learned his swordsmanship from a foreigner and studied it, so he cannot be copied by Mars or Gazi; Flos says he is not Mars's match, but expects him to be a good teacher for Teres because their tempers are similar. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L63-67)
- Flos says he has never known Orthenon to unsheathe his sword without wrath, and Mars says Orthenon once knocked Venith off his horse in one blow. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L72-75)
- {event} Orthenon arrives at Manimar with about four hundred mounted warriors and knocks Venith from his saddle with a winged spear. (Book 5, Ch4.02K L533,L540)
- {event} Orthenon scolds Flos for the reckless trip and then receives the order 'Take care of it. Please.' (Book 5, Ch4.02K L546-552)
- Orthenon reports that three more villages of Venith's lands have declared for Flos, and that messengers to Rast, Belevadim and Chalm have had no reply. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L106,L126)
- {belief} Orthenon says Mars is competent in battle but best used at present sitting still, though her presence may inspire others. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L391-393)
- Orthenon organises the realm for war, sending riders with news, readying walls, gear and recruits; Flos notes a formal declaration gives time because an army will not arrive for days. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L99-106)
- Orthenon estimates the coalition army at probably 50,000 to 80,000 although one claim says seventy thousand, and thinks it led by Duke Balimar Wulten, who earned his [Duke] title in battle and is a [General]. (Book 5, Ch4.04K L225-227,L242-246)
- {rel} Orthenon led Flos's armies to victory with Queravia. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L203)
- Orthenon, using the Skills [Sword Art: The Tide Breaks] and [Sword Art: Scattering Petals on the Wind], cuts down pursuing lancers in battle. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L401-404)
- Orthenon, as the King's [Steward], rides with a spear in the rescue of refugees. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L146)
- Orthenon manages the conquered Hellios and Germina, quelling unrest, while Venith and Maresar guard Reim. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L59,L91-93)
- {event} Orthenon greets Silmak and Clan Tannousin at Reim, offering sanctuary, and tells Nawalishifra that Flos accepted her pledge to forge a blade without equal. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L7-16)
- Orthenon says he prepares to ride north that day despite Reim's affairs, and that he will relieve Venith and Maresar. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L21,L224)
- {event} Orthenon tells Flos he will leave as soon as they finish speaking for Germina, send back Venith and Maresar, then Mars on reaching Hellios. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L199)
## Vanguard of the King (Books 14-19)
- Orthenon reports that Takhatres says the Empire of Sands has pulled back armies and [Mage] battalions, and that it may be half as large as Nerrhavia's Fallen by summer's end. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L261-264)
- {fact} Flos calls Orthenon his glorious left hand, his [Ruinbringer Steward]; Orthenon uses the Skill [Vanguard of the King, the Phantom Storm]. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L824-826)
- {event} Orthenon leads about a hundred elite riders and knocks out the third Wistram [Mage] covering the battle in Belchan with the butt of his spear. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L12-20)
- {event} Orthenon circles with nearly ten thousand [Riders] to Belchan's rear and breaks its cavalry with [Sword Art: The Tide Breaks]. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L325-338)
- {event} During Flos's duel with Raelt, Orthenon holds his sword to Geril's throat and tells General Lael the duel will not be interrupted. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L974-975)
- {event} Orthenon sets out Flos's terms to Raelt: Jecrass cedes nearly a quarter of its lands, becomes a vassal by magical contract, cannot declare war, and sends tribute and troops to Reim for fifteen years. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L503)
- {event} Orthenon says Lyfelt and the officials responsible for the refugees must die; Raelt wins that their families be spared and held under his charge. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L489-491,L509-518)
- {event} Orthenon charges the Minotaur lines with [Vanguard of the King, the Phantom Storm] and [Steelflesh Charge], and falls back after veteran [Lineholders] using [The Line Holds] stop him. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L668-695)
- {state} Rasea severs Orthenon's forefinger and middle finger of his right hand. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L1182,L1196)
- {state} Later, a Rustängmarder [Death Keeper] reattaches the two severed fingers, and tells Orthenon to watch for pain or stiffness. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L37-43)
