# Quietstab
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.47G
> See also: Rags, Tremborag, Pellmia, Pyrite
## Overview
- Quietstab is a Hob of Rags' tribe who wields a huge stave or cudgel. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L417)
- Quietstab followed Rags from Tremborag's mountain and misses some of the luxuries Humans enjoy, such as alcohol. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L513)
## Events
- {event} Quietstab knocks Lord Pellmia's son Gilam from his saddle with the stave during the forest ambush. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L417)
- {event} Quietstab ignores Gilam, yanks down his white mare and mounts it, riding it off. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L418)
- {event} Drunk and happy after the celebration, Quietstab goes to the latrines outside Rags' camp. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L512-514)
- {event} Tremborag greets Quietstab as "traitor", twists his head and tosses him into the latrine. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L515-518)
- {state} Quietstab dies, his neck snapped by Tremborag. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L517-518)
- {state} Goblins find Quietstab's body in the latrine ditch, and Rags recognises his terrified face. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L30-32)
