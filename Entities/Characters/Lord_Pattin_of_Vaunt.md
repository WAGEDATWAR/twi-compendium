# Lord Pattin of Vaunt
> Type: person | Also called: Pattin; Lord Pattin | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.11E
> See also: Laken Godart, Lord Tourant, Rael, Riverfarm
## Overview
- Lord Pattin of Vaunt is a young [Lord], the heir to Vaunt, a small city with a few villages that produces cheese and little else. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L28,L353)
- Lord Pattin ranks lowest among the visiting nobles in wealth and land, and wears worn riding clothes and rides a sad mare. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L28,L35)
- Vaunt's herds suffered sickness among the cattle six years earlier and have not been replenished, leaving the house in hard times. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L353)
- {belief} Laken's [Lady] Rie reports that Lord Pattin is considered a very intelligent [Lord] and that Vaunt will prosper when he replaces his father. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L353)
## Riverfarm visit
- Lord Pattin corrects Lord Andres that the Valerund family was lost in the First Antinium War, not the Second. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L31-33)
- Lord Rael, annoyed at Pattin's lecturing tone, tells him that no one wants his opinion and insults his poor house. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L38,L346)
- Lord Pattin warns Rael that the fae outrank him and that pursuing them is not seemly, and flushes under the insult. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L347-348)
- Laken notes that Pattin interests him more than Rael, who does not strike him as hugely bright. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L350)
- Lord Pattin tells Lord Andres that a nearby stone is not magic, since he detected nothing from it when it was inspected. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L13-14)
- Lord Pattin tells Lord Tourant that the trebuchet resembles Drake siege weapons, though he has only seen sketches. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L16-22)
- Lord Pattin answers politely, unworried, when put on the spot, saying the weapons resemble those defending the Walled Cities and the design seems easy to replicate. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L47-49)
- Laken's narration singles out Lord Pattin as one of the interesting young nobles. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L78)
