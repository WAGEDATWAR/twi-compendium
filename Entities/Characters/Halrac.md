## Overview
- Halrac is the lead [Scout] and [Marksman] of the adventuring team Griffon Hunt. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L177)
- Halrac is a Gold-rank adventurer. (Book 2, Ch2.00H L372)
- Halrac fought Drakes and Gnolls off and on as a soldier over the years, and he distrusts both. (Book 2, Ch2.26 L129)
- Halrac distrusts the Antinium and calls them heartless monsters, though he agrees to teach Bird archery later. (Book 2, Ch2.26 L130; Book 5, Ch4.18 L323-324)
- {state} Halrac is a former [Soldier]. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L38)
- {state} Later, Halrac is described as a [Veteran Scout], a former soldier and a Gold-rank team captain. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L4)
- Halrac's unofficial nickname is Halrac the Grim, which Todi started more in jest than as a Named title. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L104; Book 18, Ch7.40ER L5)
- Halrac hates the name Halrac the Grim. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L82-83)
