# Rabbiteater
> Type: person | Also called: the Goblin Slayer; Ser Solstice | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.04
> See also: Numbtongue, Headscratcher, Badarrow, Shorthilt, Garen Redfang, Erin Solstice, Talia Kallinad, Rags, Snapjaw
## Overview
- Rabbiteater is a Redfang Hobgoblin, one of the five Hobs who stay at Erin Solstice's inn. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L76; Book 7, Ch5.14 L150)
- Rabbiteater earned his name by catching rabbits and is an expert at running fast. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L128)
- Rabbiteater was as close to a [Scout] as the Redfang Warriors had, but Garen promoted him because he was a better warrior. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L128)
- Rabbiteater learned to hunt rabbits in the grass and became the stealthiest of the five Hobs by honing his skills. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L203)
- Rabbiteater is the only Goblin of the five with [Dangersense]. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L96)
- Rabbiteater is the jack-of-all-trades among the five Hobs, and later teaches his followers about traps, foraging, nets and beds. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L23)
