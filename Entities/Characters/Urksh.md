# Urksh
> Type: person | Also called: Chieftain Urksh; Urksh of the Stone Spears Tribe | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.23
> See also: Mrsha, Ryoka Griffin, Stone Spears Tribe
## Overview
- Urksh is the Chieftain of the Stone Spears tribe of Gnolls. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L21; Book 2, Ch2.28 L213)
## Book 2: Host of Ryoka Griffin
- Urksh helps dig Ryoka out of the snow and offers her food and shelter in the Stone Spears camp. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L21)
- Urksh treats Ryoka's arm with healing ointment, which he says is not a potion but works well and avoids wasting one. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L26-28)
- Urksh grips Ryoka's arm as lightly as he can while he applies the ointment, and the swelling begins to recede. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L30-34)
- Urksh apologises to Ryoka that Mrsha is young and does not yet know boundaries. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L124)
- Urksh tells Ryoka that Mrsha found her by smell, since young Gnolls have the best noses. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L131)
- Urksh scowls at the laughing adults and chases after Mrsha when she flees from Ryoka's tent. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L160)
- Urksh tells Ryoka that there is no debt for the joy she has given the tribe, and that she is welcome to stay. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L345)
- {belief} Urksh says the tribe knows of one Necromancer but he is dead, and that following only a stone toward a necromancer is dangerous. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L353)
## Book 2: Mrsha's disappearance
- Urksh tells Ryoka that Mrsha has gone missing after wandering away from a gatherer in the morning. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L223-227)
- Urksh says that Mrsha does not speak although her throat is undamaged, so her silence worries the tribe. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L234-239)
- {belief} Urksh and the Gnolls accept that Mrsha will probably be dead if she is not found by the next day. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L310-311)
- Urksh gives Ryoka healing potions, a magical rope that stretches a long way, and a child-sized fur coat. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L413-419)
- Urksh's hunter Hekra reports that thirteen adults are missing across three patrols sent to look for Mrsha, and that none came back. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L66-71)
- Urksh worries that those who vanished left no trace and no howls, meaning that whatever took them acted swiftly and by surprise. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L72)
- Urksh orders every warrior ready and packed in case the tribe has to flee. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L73-74)
- Urksh catches a faint, twisted scent of his enemy on the air, with death in it. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L75)
## Death and legacy
- {state} Urksh clutches his stomach as he holds in his wounds, joins Ryoka in offering everything to the faeries, and falls when a Goblin stabs him from behind. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L400-402)
- Urksh had been teaching Mrsha in the tribe to walk on two legs. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L110)
- Mrsha remembers Urksh among the dead Stone Spears and thinks of herself as the lone survivor. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L319-321)
- {reveal} Urksh exiled Mrsha's hunter parents after they tried to abandon her for being mute, saying that being unable to speak was not a crime worthy of death. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L86)
- Urksh raised Mrsha like a father until Goblins slew him. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L86)
- Urksh taught Mrsha to read basic things and do numbers only sparingly. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L276)
- Urksh was older than the old Liscor Gnoll who shares Mrsha's captivity, who is described as not as old as Urksh had been. (Book 8, Ch5.39 L185)
