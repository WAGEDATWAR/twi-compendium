# Briganda
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.15R
> See also: Cade, Halrac, Revi, Typhenous, Griffon Hunt
## Overview
- Briganda is a scarred Human [Shield Maiden] in her late thirties, an original member of the Griffon Hunt, with a stocky, muscular build. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L635-636)
- Briganda carries an enchanted hatchet and has split more monsters' heads with it than Revi could count. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L636)
- Briganda has violet mixed into her brown hair, a hereditary trait from magic in her ancestry. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L639)
- Briganda once would unapologetically kick her teammates out of the way to use the bathroom, and never took more than a minute to decide anything. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L645,L715)
- Briganda cannot swim. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L233)
## Son Cade
- Briganda has a young son, Cade, about four years old, with dark violet hair deeper than hers and fair skin. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L641)
- Briganda keeps Cade occupied with a Box of Simulacra that releases a tiny illusionary Dragon with substance, which cost her an arm and a leg. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L656-662)
- Briganda cautions Cade that if he breaks the things that come out of the box, he has to wait a long time for them to come back. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L658)
- Cade expected Laken to wear a crown because Briganda had said he would, and Laken says he will have a circlet made when he has time. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L234-236)
## Rejoining Griffon Hunt
- Briganda finds Griffon Hunt after the team disbanded on a bad night, nearly two years since they last saw each other. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L638,L644)
- {belief} Briganda tells Halrac she has heard the survivors of the burned village all went to Riverfarm and joined the Unseen Empire, with some powerful noble, an actual [Emperor]. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1291-1294)
- Griffon Hunt offers Briganda two thousand gold pieces for personal funds, with four thousand of the six thousand going back into the team's pool, as she would be part of the team. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L700,L709)
- {state} Briganda rejoins Griffon Hunt, answering absolutely when Halrac asks her, and Revi hugs her. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L784-786)
## Book 17: Numbtongue
- {event} Briganda charges Numbtongue with her axe and buckler when Cade screams about a Goblin, and she slams into the invisible barrier of the Garden of Sanctuary. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L501,L509-519)
- Briganda is the only one of the adventurers not used to Numbtongue, and the others try to grab her too late. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L511)
- Briganda hacks at the invisible door with her enchanted axe, leaving marks, until the others pull her off. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L521)
- {event} Briganda apologizes to Numbtongue after Halrac tells her to, saying she reacted on hearing Cade scream. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L524-527)
