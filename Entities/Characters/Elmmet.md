# Elmmet
> Type: person | Also called: Elmmet Patterson; Master Elmmet | First appears: Ch6.34E
> See also: Hedag, Beatica, Prost, Beniar, Rie
## Overview
- Elmmet is a Lancrel [Councilman] and one of Lancrel's big leaders, with white hair, who is hardly spry. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L427; Book 12, Ch6.37E L140)
- Elmmet is a Level 30 [Thief] who probably has [Master of Faces] as a Skill, in the view of a speaker who reports it to Laken's circle. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L739)
- Elmmet has a face-changing Skill, so that witnesses saw a young man or a woman rather than him. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L412; Book 12, Ch6.37E L139)
## The thefts
- Beniar chases a hooded [Thief] with his horse, and Durene reaches the pair as the armoured Beniar knocks the man out. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L388-389)
- Beniar pulls off the hood and swears when he sees the [Thief] is Master Elmmet. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L391-392)
- Lancrel's folk say Elmmet has been set up, and a speaker is disgusted that a [Councilman] was the thief. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L444)
- Elmmet is held under guard, and a public trial with a truth stone is planned for the next day. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L66,L77)
## Trial
- Elmmet is pilloried with head and hands through it while Prost holds a cheap two-setting truth stone, red for false and blue for truth. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L132,L137)
- {belief} Elmmet claims he is innocent and was only caught by Beniar while trying to catch the [Thief] himself. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L133)
- {belief} Elmmet claims the truth stone can be faked and that Prost and his hired adventurers framed him. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L139)
- Elmmet answers Prost's question about [Master of Faces] by saying the stone would lie if he answered. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L141-143)
- Elmmet demands an impartial judge and a proper trial, and Prost replies that this is his one trial. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L147-148)
- Elmmet asks that his wife and daughter be asked to vouch for him, and they are pushed forward. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L172-173)
- Elmmet's daughter stares at the crowd, and at her father, in terror. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L173)
## Death
- {state} Hedag's axe swings down at the trial, and the crowd shouts that Master Elmmet is dead. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L258,L405)
- Prost says afterward that he would swing an axe twice if Elmmet stood before him again, and calls the verdict just. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L56)
- Prost asks Hedag to swear before all present that her Skill revealed Elmmet Patterson's sins and crimes. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L58)
