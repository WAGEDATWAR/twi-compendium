# Bekia
> Type: person | Also called: Gnoll [Maid] | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.44M
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Theofore, Ryoka Griffin, Urksh
## Overview
- Bekia is a Gnoll [Maid] who has been in Magnolia Reinhart's service for years. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L311-312)
- Bekia serves a plate of food to the young [Assassin] Theofore, whom Magnolia shelters because his own guild has marked him for death. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L311-315)
## Former [Chieftain]
- {reveal} Bekia is a former [Chieftain] of a Gnoll tribe, informal unlike the other servants in the household. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L656)
- Bekia says she got in trouble and was kicked out, and now finds being a [Maid] fun and relaxing, though she misses fighting. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L658)
- Bekia carries a battleaxe and her own bag of holding, and swings the axe close to Ryoka. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L792)
