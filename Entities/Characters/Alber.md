# Alber
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.00
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Madain, Fierre
## Overview
- Alber is a young, stocky, bare-chested fighter in Reizmelt, shorter than Ryoka but well muscled. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L13)
- Alber takes fights for money, charging silver from normal folk and gold from adventurers, and moves between cities. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L40-42; Book 17, Ch7.24 L969)
- Alber is a [Fist Fighter] who is self-taught and not from Ryoka's world, as far as she can tell. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L67-68,L342-343)
- Alber is the only boxer-style fighter Ryoka has seen in this world, which has no boxing tradition in Izril. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L67-68)
## Events
- {event} Alber calls Ryoka "the Braggart" and guards the stairs against the drunk Madain, offering to shout if he comes up. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L26-31,L46-49)
