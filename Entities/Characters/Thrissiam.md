# Thrissiam
> Type: person | Also called: Thrissiam Blackwing; General Thrissiam | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.16
> See also: Garusa, Osthia Blackwing, Az'kerash, Zel Shivertail, Pallass
## Overview
- Thrissiam Blackwing is a Drake [General] from Pallass, the northernmost of the six Walled Cities. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L7)
- Thrissiam is a [Lineholder General] whose formations suit fighting Goblins. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L19)
- Thrissiam is chosen to lead the suppression effort against the Goblins and the Goblin Lord, and he rides a mare. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L7,L57)
- Thrissiam fights with a spear and has an enchanted spear. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L221,L339)
- Thrissiam is a relation of Osthia Blackwing, technically her uncle but only about ten years older and a distant cousin. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L72-74)
- Thrissiam taught Osthia to use a spear, accepted her into his army and gave her her first promotion. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L332)
## The campaign
- Thrissiam expects to take seniority against the Goblin Lord, but meets Garusa Weatherfur, a higher-ranking and higher-level Gnoll [General]. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L6-8)
- {event} Thrissiam bows his head first and accepts overall command when Garusa declines to lead. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L9,L19)
- Thrissiam sends Osthia's Oldblood company on scouting flights. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L76)
- {rel} Thrissiam sleeps with Garusa during the siege, and Osthia later recalls the Gnoll as someone he loved for a short few days. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L184,L199; Book 6, Ch4.46 L22)
- Thrissiam tells Garusa he will not risk her, calling her an asset that cannot be replaced. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L200-204)
- {event} Thrissiam slaps Osthia after she dives at the Goblin Lord, then forgives her and tells her she is a treasure of their family. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L225-236)
- {event} Thrissiam and Garusa plan an ambush of the Goblin Lord's forward camps, having observed his predictable movements. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L252-255)
- Thrissiam realizes the Goblin Lord's master is the Necromancer, whom he says is not dead. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L305-310)
## Death
- {event} Thrissiam orders his Scouts and Runners to flee south to warn the cities, and orders Osthia to fly away. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L312-321)
- Thrissiam tells Osthia to tell them the Goblin Lord has a master, the Necromancer, and to bring back Shivertail and the Tidebreaker. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L338)
- {state} Thrissiam kills the undead Garusa, fights on against the undead and dies holding a Gnoll in his arms. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L343-347)
- {state} An hour after his death, Thrissiam's corpse rises as undead and lurches north after the Goblins, not to Liscor. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L348)
- Osthia fears she will someday see her uncle among the Goblin Lord's undead, and has so far not found him. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L22-24)
- A dying Drake soldier reports that Thrissiam ordered both armies to buy time so word could reach the other cities. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L500)
- {belief} A red-scaled Drake at a table says Thrissiam and Garusa died with over twenty thousand Gnolls and Drakes and blames bickering over the size of the force. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L385)
