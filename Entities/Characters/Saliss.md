# Saliss
> Type: person | Also called: Saliss of Lights; Saliss Oliwing; Master Saliss; Onieva; Onieva Oliwing | First appears: Ch6.10
> See also: Xif, Octavia, Erin Solstice, Chaldion, Mirn, Selys, Maviola El, Teriarch, Zel Shivertail
## Overview
- Saliss of Lights is Pallass' resident Named Adventurer, and Lasica says he was arrested in front of Erin on her first visit. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L216)
- Chaldion introduces Saliss Oliwing as the highest-leveled [Alchemist] in Pallass, perhaps the world, and its only permanent resident Named-rank adventurer. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L994)
- Chaldion says the Assembly of Crafts has given Saliss immunities, so he does what he pleases even in prison. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L1001)
- Lasica says Saliss is arrested every other week for nudity but is above the law because the city needs him too badly. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L999)
- Saliss is a Drake with dusky yellow scales and a slim, tall frame, who is usually completely naked. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L172-173)
- Saliss says he is arrested at least once a week for stripping whenever clothes go on him. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L464)
- Saliss says his Skills help him resist effects on his scales, so spilled substances usually ruin only his clothes. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L484)
- Saliss also calls nakedness an act of expression, a protest against Drake society and its shackles. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L493)
- Saliss says he wanted to be a [Performer], and he dances very well. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L850,L865)
- Saliss says he makes poisons. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L638)
- Pallass' citizens call him a naked bastard, their number one nuisance and an insane [Alchemist], but also treat him as their own. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L671)
