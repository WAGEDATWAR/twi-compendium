# Bishop
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.42
> See also: Pawn, Anand, Knight, Garry, Antinium
## Overview
- Bishop is the name chosen by an Antinium Worker who joins the Workers guarding Erin's inn when the dead are coming. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L307-316)
- Bishop declares that he has chosen a name and is individual, the same moment the other Workers announce names such as Vladimir, Calabrian, Emanuel, Garry and Milner-Barry. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L313-322)
- Knight tells Erin the Workers have chosen to be individual, and that Pawn was kept in the Hive by the Prognugator. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L312-315)
## The first Bishop
- {state} Pawn says that Bishop named himself in the battle for Liscor and died there. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L434)
## The second Bishop
- Anand introduces a Worker named Bishop to Pawn, a Worker who holds a clipboard that is a nail board. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L427-429)
- Pawn rejects the new Worker's name and insists that the name Bishop belonged to the Antinium who died in the battle for Liscor. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L430-434)
- Anand tells Pawn that he allowed this Worker to take the name Bishop, and that two Antinium may share a name. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L438-441)
- Anand tells Pawn that the name belongs to both Bishops and that the earlier Bishop would not have begrudged it. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L441-444)
- The new Bishop shudders when Pawn rejects his name, and Pawn's anger subsides when he sees him. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L445)
- Pawn apologises, and Bishop says he bears no ill will. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L446-451)
- Anand says Bishop is the closest of the Autonomous Workers to being truly Individual, because Anand plays chess with him and Bishop helps him. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L454)
- Bishop tells Pawn he will try his best to become worthy of the name Bishop, and Pawn thanks him. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L493-495)
