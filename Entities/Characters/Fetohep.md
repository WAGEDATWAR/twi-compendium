# Fetohep
> Type: person | Also called: Fetohep of Khelt; Eternal King of Khelt; Eternal Majesty | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.54K
> See also: Quarass of Germina, Trey Atwood, Teres Atwood, Flos Reimarch, Wil Kallinad, Khelt
## Overview
- Fetohep is the mummified undead [King] of Khelt, with sunken sockets in which golden flames burn with intelligence and personality. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L895,L899; Book 13, Ch6.55K L2-3)
- Fetohep has lost his lips and nose, and his voice comes out of his mouth without a tongue or vocal cords, with a subtle echo. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L2,L12)
- Fetohep calls himself a Revenant, one of the few undead who kept the will of their life. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L122)
- Fetohep says he does not level, but keeps all his Skills of old. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L122)
- Fetohep says Revenants are consumed by grudges and hatred of the living, but he is not, since he swore in life to protect Khelt. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L122)
- Fetohep was a [Warrior] in life and learned magic only after his death. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L25)
- Fetohep says he was chosen as ruler because he loved his people, more than being the best warrior. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L273)
- Fetohep has ruled Khelt for six hundred and fifty-three years and is the nineteenth ruler, after eighteen predecessors. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L321,L441)
- Fetohep has a chair placed so that in every room of his palace he is never without a throne. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L11)
- Fetohep drinks a goblet of mana and death essence, and calls a similar draught Ghost's Tears. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L24; Book 13, Ch6.55K L336-340)
- Fetohep walked each continent in turn before his death. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L220)
- {state} Fetohep says his flesh is rotting, and within a century or two the magics that keep him will fail and he will turn to dust. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L450)
## Rule of Khelt
- Fetohep declares that every child, man and woman in Khelt is under his protection, and anyone who harms them will face Khelt. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L81)
- {belief} Fetohep holds that citizens of other nations are free only in illusion, constrained by needs of body and money, whereas Khelt's citizens are fed and sheltered. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L347)
- Fetohep has lived six hundred years maintaining Khelt's peace, a passionless [King] who is a slave first to his people. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L139)
- Fetohep says Khelt is neutral about Jecrass and Belchan, though it trades with Reim. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L461)
- Fetohep says any nation that objects to his will need only cross his borders with an army. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L461)
- Fetohep's paradise is built on the will of Khelt's rulers and is not a democracy. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L138)
- Fetohep says his people may travel the breadth of his kingdom at will in undead carriages without cost. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L692)
- Fetohep says Khelt's rulers have weathered millennia, each ruling for centuries or even a thousand years. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L321)
