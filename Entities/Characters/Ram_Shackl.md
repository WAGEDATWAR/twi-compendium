# Ram Shackl
> Type: person | Also called: Headman Ram; Mister Ram | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.41E
> See also: Riverfarm, Ryoka Griffin, Laken Godart
## Overview
- Ram Shackl introduces himself to Ryoka at Riverfarm as a [Rancher] by class who is working as a [Farmer] for now. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L127)
- Ram Shackl says he heard from Prost and other Riverfarm folk that Ryoka delivered food to them as a City Runner during the winter. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L127)
## Opinions and Role
- Ram Shackl says delivering letters to his farm used to be hellish because he cannot pay silver or gold for a delivery. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L130)
- Ram Shackl says there are no Runners about Riverfarm, only former Street Runners such as Beycalt from years ago. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L130)
- Ram Shackl says all of Lancrel's Runners just found work elsewhere because Runners are in demand. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L130)
- Ram Shackl tells King Laken that he and the [Farmers] will soon have the storehouses bursting. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L397)
- {belief} Ram Shackl assumes Laken will use the Goblins as monster bait or as workers, and he fears they will slit the farmers' throats. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L397)
- Laken answers that his plan is nothing like Ram's guess and tells him not to worry about the Goblins. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L398)
