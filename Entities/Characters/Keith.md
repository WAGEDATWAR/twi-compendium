# Keith
> Type: person | Also called: Inferno Smith | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.02C
> See also: Richard, Emily, Cynthia, Eddy
## Overview
- Keith is one of the Americans summoned to Rhir, and the narrator describes him as a [Blacksmith] who wants to find out if an [Engineer] class exists. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L75)
- Keith is into architecture and would have been a freshman at Lehigh University in Pennsylvania, an engineering major. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L21-23)
- Keith has lost the [Hero] class, as he did not want to kill. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L317)
- Keith is an [Inferno Smith], though despite his [Blacksmith] class he has no strength-enhancing Skills. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L306)
## With the other summoned Earthers
- Keith nervously sits with Cirille between himself and the narrator, and stares at her in worry. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L80,L371)
- Keith flinches along with Emily and Richard when the group is struck by fear. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L6)
- Keith charges at Eddy over a dispute and freezes when Eddy puts up his fists, and Richard intervenes. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L301-307)
- Keith flips Eddy off from behind after Eddy walks away. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L312)
## Smelting titanium
- Keith finally figures out how to smelt titanium from their world, which was only powder when given to him. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L419-421)
- Keith is confident he can make more of the titanium. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L422)
- Lord Hayvon promises to prepare a gift for Keith after cutting a chunk off the metal and finding it not magical. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L424-428)
- Keith is among the Americans who help in their own ways rather than fight, and is offered sanctuary in the kingdom to work. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L652,L669)
