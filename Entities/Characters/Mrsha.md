# Mrsha
> Type: person | Also called: Mrsha the Great and Terrible; Mrsha of the Stone Spears; Doombringer; Mrsha du Marquin; the White Druid of the Inn | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.23
> See also: Erin Solstice, Lyonette du Marquin, Ryoka Griffin, Urksh, Krshia Silverfang, Elirr, Apista, Calruz
## Overview
- Mrsha is a young Gnoll child of the Stone Spear tribe, mute from birth, who is later raised at Erin Solstice's inn. (Book 2, Ch2.23 L124-131; Book 7, Ch5.06M L85-86)
- Mrsha's fur turns from light brown to pure white during the events at the tribe's end, and Gnolls and others treat white fur as an omen. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L278; Book 2, Ch2.29 L449-450; Book 8, Ch5.42 L695)
- Mrsha is the only survivor of the Stone Spears, who were killed by Goblins. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L16,L85; Book 8, Ch5.27 L279)
- Mrsha later becomes a [Druid] with a wand, a growth spell, and a bond with Apista and the Fortress Beavers. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L337-342; Book 17, Ch7.28 L966-968)
- Mrsha speaks through her own sign language, written notes and pre-written cards. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L31; Book 18, Ch7.37 L275; Book 19, Ch7.50 L128)
