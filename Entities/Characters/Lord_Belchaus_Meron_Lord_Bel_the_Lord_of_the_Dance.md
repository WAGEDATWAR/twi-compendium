# Lord Belchaus Meron (Lord Bel, the Lord of the Dance)
> Type: person | Also called: Lord Belchaus; Lord Bel; the Lord of the Dance; Belchaus Meron | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.68
> See also: Nadel, Order of Savellia, Krsysl Wordsmith, Terandria
## Overview
- Lord Belchaus Meron is the [Lord] of Terandria known as the Lord of the Dance, who tops the lists for both eligibility and strategic genius. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L3)
- Lord Belchaus held Terandria against foreign fleets for over a decade, and his presence alone kept the coast where his fleets patrol a safe harbor even in wartime. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L3)
- Lord Belchaus is unmarried and is called the most eligible bachelor in the world. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L39,L107)
## Rankings
- The Drake [Writer] Krsysl Wordsmith ranks Lord Belchaus first among the five most important [Lords] in the world, placing Tyrion Veltras second and Hayvon Operland fifth. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L215-217)
- Lord Belchaus is announced on a Pallass broadcast as the best [Lord] in the world according to Sir Krsysl Wordsmith, though the broadcaster notes that the list has been disputed. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L39)
- {belief} Fierre says the title of best [Lord] in the world is only what some Drake wrote, but that Lord Bel is one of the best [Lords] in combat and other aspects. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L44-45)
## Dancing and the Order of Savellia
- Lord Bel studied dancing from every species and pays to see new dances or to meet people who are really good at it, according to what Fierre has heard. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L107)
- When he is not defending his lands, Lord Bel entertains challenges like the Order of Savellia's all the time. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L107)
- {event} The Order of Savellia enters Lord Bel's ballroom, where hundreds of his [Dancers] wait, and Lord Bel stands at the far end posed on the balls of his feet, wearing an old-fashioned Terandrian noble suit in pale blue and rose-red. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L51-52,L58-60)
- Lord Bel refuses to bow to the Order of Savellia and dances against its grandmaster, his style being freer and more energetic than the Order's slower dances. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L93,L97,L100)
- Lord Bel and the Savellian [Knights] are both famous for their dancing, and Fierre says they fight in perfect formations in battle and that Lord Bel is said to be better than most [Strategists]. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L89)
- Lord Bel catches a young Savellian [Knight] who challenges him in a dance, lifts him as if he were a [Lady], and the two then waltz together. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L111-121)
- {belief} Ryoka tells Fierre that the Lord of the Dance could be homosexual or else straight and simply open to dancing with men. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L393-394)
## Nadel and the sea
- Lord Belchaus rules Nadel, a safe-zone near the middle of Terandria's southern coastline, where no fleet except the Lord of the Dance's is welcome. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L69,L106)
- Lord Belchaus is infamous for keeping his waters free of marauders even in wartime, and his harbors are poor for illicit cargo and [Slaves], so anyone at odds with him steers clear of his waters. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L500-501)
- Lord Belchaus has always been neutral, and his harbors were thought practically unassailable. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L70,L83-84)
- {belief} General Yerzhen tells Admiral Dakelos that Lord Belchaus has agreed, unhappily, to let Ailendamus' ships land at Nadel to flank the Dawn Concordat, and that the fight with the Lord of the Dance is a staged attack. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L81,L113)
- {event} Lord Belchaus answers the Ailendamus demand for passage by stating that Nadel bows to no other nation and threatening to destroy their fleet. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L120-123)
- {event} Lord Bel's flagship rams The Wrath of Sel, and he leaps aboard with a sword and beheads its [Captain], then kills the [First Mate] while his retinue follows. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L130-132)
- Admiral Dakelos realises that the Lord of the Dance is not acting. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L134)
