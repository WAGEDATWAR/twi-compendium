# Wil
> Type: person | Also called: Wil Kallinad; Lord Kallinad | First appears: Ch6.21D
> See also: Niers, Feshi, Venaz, Yerranola, Merrik, Peki, Tulm, Fetohep, Umina, Marian, Foliana
## Overview
- Wil Kallinad is a Terandrian [Lord] and student [Strategist] at Niers Astoragon's academy, one of two Humans in his group of friends. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L189-190; Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L77)
- Wil is a second son of the high nobility in Terandria, not as important as his older brother, and his family paid a fortune for him to study. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L753-757)
- Wil admits that Terandrian nobility, himself included, are the kind of Humans others blame for things. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L190)
- Wil is a coastal swimmer who has experience on ships and dives into the sea to rescue Peki. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L199-202)
- House Kallinad is a coastal duchy, and Pheislant is a coastal nation known for its lighthouses and a weaker land army. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L228; Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L471-474)
- Wil's sister Talia Kallinad is a Knight of the Summer in the Order of Seasons, and Wil has watched her spar her peers. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L1043; Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L611)
- Wil has an Aunt Delicha, his father's sister, who is married to a [Baron] of Ailendamus. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L241)
- Wil's cousin Girent is engaged to Lady Cassicel Havrington, aged thirteen, as his father wants peace with the Havringtons. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L733-741)
- Wil explains that Terandrian nobles use inheritance Skills like [Adroit Progeny] that pass on a parent's talents, which disturbs the other species. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L268-273)
