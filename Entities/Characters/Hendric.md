# Hendric
> Type: person | Also called: captain of the Swordlock Dogs | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.00H
> See also: Calruz, Gerial, Ryoka Griffin
## Overview
- Hendric is a Silver-rank adventurer and the captain of the Swordlock Dogs. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L160,L169)
- The Swordlock Dogs are a Silver-rank team more known up north. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L169)
- Hendric has a scarred face and talks about how to behead the goats properly. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L161,L168)
## Role in the Ruins expedition
- {event} Hendric pulls out of the expedition into the Ruins of Liscor at the last moment, and Calruz calls him a coward. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L33-36)
- Hendric was supposed to be talking to Calruz and the others in Liscor about the expedition. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L169)
