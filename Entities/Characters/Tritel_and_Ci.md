# Tritel and Ci
> Type: person | Also called: the Moonlight Rider; Tritel (the Courier); Ci (his silver mare) | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.24
> See also: Erin Solstice, Geneva, Seve-Alrelious, Salamani, Yellow Rivers cure, Solstice Booth
## Overview
- Tritel is a Courier known as the Moonlight Rider, a short man who looks more like a horse jockey and is confident and grinning. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L950-951)
- Ci is Tritel's beautiful silver mare, whom he insists goes everywhere with him, and who is perfectly behaved in the inn. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L950,L955)
- Tyrion Veltras tells Ullim to hire the Moonlight Rider, Tritel, as a Courier if needed to fetch gelato ingredients. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L447-454)
- Ryoka vaguely recognises Tritel as the Moonlight Rider when she sees him, and he rides with his horse. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L293,L352)
- Ci blows tobacco smoke back at Tritel and is called a tyrant by him. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L958-959)
## Carrying the cure (Book 18)
- Tritel asks Erin what she wants and says the Yellow Rivers cure must be kept out of bags of holding because the mold is alive. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L951-960)
- Tritel says a sea-Courier could get the cure to the port city of Talenqual fast but that it is not cheap. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L962)
- Tritel says he has been begged thousands of times for free deliveries and now needs compensation. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L974-978)
- {event} Erin offers Tritel and the sea-Courier exclusive seats in the Solstice Booth, with a dedicated performance by the Players of Celum for a month. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L995-998)
- {event} Tritel agrees to carry the cure for free in return for the booth seats, after Erin adds a place for Ci. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1003-1005)
## Messages about the cure (Book 19)
- Tritel and Ci send a message that no Couriers at the port city of Altvil will take the delivery, and that three samples were entrusted to three [Captains]. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L139-142)
- Tritel's message says two vessels were impounded and that the Waverider's Wake was heading for the nearest Balerosian harbour after passing Wistram. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L143-144)
- Tritel's message says worsening weather threatens delivery and the [Harbormaster] fears a magical storm. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L145)
- {event} Tritel reports that the Waverider's Wake went down with all hands, and that Wistram saw the ship's demise on a scrying. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L371-372)
