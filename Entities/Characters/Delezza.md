# Delezza
> Type: person | Also called: Huntress Delezza; Hell's Warden | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Cirille, Merish, Ser Vorn, Tom, Emily, Zavarial, Rhir, Demon Hunter (class)
## Overview
- Delezza introduces herself as Huntress Delezza of Noelictus, a veteran [Demon Hunter] of the Hunter's Guild of Terandria, on assignment in Rhir. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L918)
- Delezza wears a large hat and dark clothing over leather armor, and carries an oversized crossbow with Featherweight Runes. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L917)
- Delezza's crossbow is dwarfsteel, enchanted by Dwarf [Runemasters], with an effective range of about 200 yards unaided. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L944)
- Delezza says the Hunter's Guild can field only five of her kind among thousands of [Soldiers], and that [Demon Hunters] go to Rhir. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L957-959)
- Delezza says most [Hunters] get [Automatic Reload] at Level 30 if they use crossbows, or have their crossbow enchanted to do it. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L951)
## Time in Rhir (Book 18)
- Delezza tells Cirille she has been in Rhir for eight years since she last left, and that the Hunter's Guild will not replace her until she goes. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L961-964)
- Delezza explains that Monarch's Pass lies nearly opposite 5th Wall and is where Rhir's monsters are funnelled down. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1268-1271)
- Delezza says she left her family at Monarch's Pass, a husband and two children who were all [Demon Hunters]. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1276-1280)
- Delezza says it has been eight years since she went home, that she will die in Rhir, and that she is more like Rhir's citizens that way. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1280)
- {belief} Delezza says the Antinium of Rhir broke past every wall and stole every ship, and are worse than the Antinium of Izril. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1294)
- Delezza says that if Rhir's Antinium had reached Izril in one piece, Izril would not have lasted one month. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1294)
## Death (Book 18)
- {event} Delezza is wounded by dozens of cuts and arrows in an ambush on a patrol and bleeds while the healing potions do not work. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L749-753)
- Sisth says the poison used is new, and the [Healer] says the Demons must have created a new toxin that stops healing with potions. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L753-756)
- {event} Tom demands a blood transfusion, and Emily relays Chole's advice on checking blood types by mixing blood and watching for platelets. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L766-782)
- {state} Delezza dies before the transfusion can be carried out, as the lead [Healer] announces. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L784-793)
- Ser Vorn says Delezza fought the Demons for years and earned the title of Hell's Warden. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L796)
- Ser Vorn says the [Demon Hunters] under Delezza's tutelage were the finest in the world, and that her entire family fought in hell and died there. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L796)
- Ser Vorn says he will speak her name in Noelictus and lay her among the honored dead. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L796)
- Zavarial says a few words as Delezza is put into a casket for honored burial at Terandria, and Noelictus entombs its fallen. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L856)
