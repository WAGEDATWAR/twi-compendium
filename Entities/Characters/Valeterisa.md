# Valeterisa
> Type: person | Also called: Archmage Valeterisa; Archmage of Izril | First appears: Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Fierre, Salamani, Ieka, Feor, Archmage Zelkyr, Az'kerash, Karsaeu-Dequoa
## Overview
- Valeterisa is the Archmage of Izril, an old-looking Human woman in her sixties who seems surprisingly normal and distracted. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L720)
- Valeterisa has no grand otherworldly features like Az'kerash, though a faint tinge of magic hangs about her. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L720-721)
- As a girl, Valeterisa wanted to be an [Archmage], and she kept that dream as she aged. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L6)
- Valeterisa reached Feor's level of spellcraft within her short Human lifespan, though the half-Elf Feor is nearly four times her age. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L6)
- Valeterisa stole away from the politicking of Wistram Academy to pursue her goal, though she survived Wistram to become Archmage. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L6; Book 19, Ch7.44 L321)
- Valeterisa achieved one great technique of old but dived too far into the heart of magic without a guide to pull her back. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L7)
- Valeterisa claims she is bad with money and takes jobs, and says people lie to her. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L278)
## Fractured mind (Book 18)
- {state} Valeterisa no longer dreams or sleeps, and uses the Skill [Parallel Thoughts] to split her mind into over forty selves. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L2,L9-10,L13)
- Valeterisa has forgotten her own name, and a singular part of her remembers it for her. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L13)
- One of Valeterisa's selves keeps her body alive, another looks for danger, and most pursue magic. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L8,L14)
- Another of Valeterisa's selves screams, is angry and breaks things when the stress gets too much. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L15)
- The narration says Archmage Chandler used the same Skill but would never have gone this far, and Az'kerash can sustain about fourteen thoughts. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L10-12)
- Valeterisa has pursued one grand theory for over ten years, and few of her selves have time for anything else. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L14)
- Valeterisa's fortress of a home holds a captive Courier, whom she knows of only dimly. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L17)
- Valeterisa has spent ten years in the fortress, and countless Couriers, [Knights] and adventurers have failed to rouse her. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L18-19)
- One of Valeterisa's fractured mental processes tracks intruders, and her shadow familiars carry them into holding cells that she has not investigated for six years. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L472-473)
## Ryoka's delivery (Book 18)
- {belief} Valeterisa believed no one would reach her and filed Ryoka's team as statistically high but not at the top. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L661-662)
- Valeterisa calls the team's message that they are Runners ridiculous. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L717-718)
- {event} Valeterisa appears over Ryoka, Fierre and Salamani with nearly a hundred shadow familiars, a Golem army and a giant maw of shadows. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L707-711)
- Valeterisa counts 4,388 [Messages] triggered by the fire on her isle and demands to know who the intruders are. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L713)
- {event} Valeterisa's fragmented selves reunite when Ryoka says they are Runners with a message for her, and she realises how much she has lost. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L715-717)
- Valeterisa looks at her burning isle, with summoned monsters still dueling her defensive spells. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L719)
- Valeterisa panics on learning prisoners starved in her cells, calling it an error that she forgot to monitor them. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L4-7)
