# Peslas
> Type: person | First appears: Ch2.10
> See also: Erin Solstice, Selys Shivertail, Isshia
## Overview
- Peslas is the biggest Drake Erin has ever met, apart from possibly Relc, and he is huge rather than muscular. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L196)
- Peslas is the innkeeper of the Tailless Thief, the most famous and expensive inn in Liscor, on the north side of the city. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L190)
- Peslas is generous, and sometimes insists a meal is on the house. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L195,L220)
## Levels and classes
- {belief} Peslas says that he is Level 30, as are some of the innkeepers around his age, and most are Level 20 or higher. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L229)
- Peslas has the Skill [Intuition], and says it has never been wrong. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L282)
- {belief} Peslas says he has heard that an innkeeper in First Landing is nearing Level 40, and that she is the highest-level innkeeper on the continent. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L227-229)
- Selys says Peslas is above Level 30 as an [Innkeeper], and that he levels only about once every couple of years because he is old and takes it easy. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L533,L536)
- Peslas last levelled up six years before this conversation and threw a huge party, to which Selys and her grandmother were invited. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L536)
- According to Selys, Peslas was a [Warrior] who reached Level 16 serving in the army, and has a few levels each in [Cook], [Bartender], [Singer] and [Dancer]. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L542-544)
- Later, Pisces refers to Peslas as the Level 30 Drake [Innkeeper] in Liscor, saying he would keep a large sum on hand. (Book 9, Ch5.49 L448)
## Relationships
- Peslas welcomes Selys because she is the grandchild of Isshia. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L204)
- Selys says Peslas is only an acquaintance of her grandmother, whom she talks to every other month, and that he married a girl nearly as young as she is. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L290)
## Advice to Erin
- {belief} Peslas says that a Human once ran Erin's inn, and that he had told the man its location was bad. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L249-252)
- {belief} Peslas says that before the Necromancer, people travelled north across the Blood Fields, and a small village grew around the Human's inn but was destroyed. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L252)
- {belief} Peslas says that the undead forced everyone to flee the area, after which the Human never got the inn running again. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L255)
- Peslas tells Erin she needs something special, a hook, and not just her best. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L261)
- Peslas sells hamburgers in his inn after Erin's success with them. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L317)
