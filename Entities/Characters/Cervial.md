# Cervial
> Type: person | Also called: the archer-captain | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.11R
> See also: Yvlon Byres, Gerald, Lir, Ceria Springwalker, Skinner
## Overview
- Cervial is a Silver-rank adventurer who captains a team and carries a large longbow. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L9)
- Cervial leads a team called the Flawless Flights, which is one of three willing to enter the Ruins of Liscor first. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L165)
- Cervial is a Silver-rank archer-captain who also uses a Dwarven crossbow. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L21; Book 1, Ch1.00H L269)
## Before the Ruins
- Cervial says he will join the plan to enter the Ruins only if Ceria Springwalker thinks it worth the risk, since she has more experience than the whole room. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L53,L56)
- Cervial sits next to Ryoka at the table and explains that Calruz wants the challenge and experience from any enemy, as an old-fashioned Levelist. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L158-159,L172)
- {event} Cervial offers Ryoka personalized Runner's Guild contracts for exclusive deliveries, and she declines, saying she is not a team player. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L207-208,L231)
- {event} Cervial says his [Appraisal] Skill, which [Marksmen] sometimes get, shows him no Level or Class for Ryoka. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L351)
- Cervial whistles at the inn and complains that his group sleeps three people to a room in their own inn. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L256)
- {event} Cervial and Yvlon run to Liscor and back in under thirty minutes looking for the missing girl, and report that the gate guard saw no one. (Book 1, Ch1.38 L224-226)
- Cervial found someone willing to explore and map the first level of the Ruins, though nobody will go further. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L83)
- Cervial owns a massive Dwarf-made crossbow of sleek black metal, which cost nearly every gold piece his team had. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L366-369,L372)
- Cervial says the crossbow cracked the armor of a Stone Golem with one shot and has brought down many shielded enemies. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L372)
## In the Ruins
- Cervial kneels with the Dwarven crossbow among eight archers in the front line, with two slings and several longbows and shortbows. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L268-269)
- {event} Cervial's Flawless Flights lose two members in the ambush and two more in the scouting group. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L123)
- Cervial weeps while reloading, saying his bolt hit a monster that still took Elise and ate her. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L123-124)
- {state} Cervial's face is seen in the white mass of skinned bodies, empty and vacant, along with Gerald and Lir, and the adventurers realise the creature has absorbed them. (Book 1, Ch1.02H L271-282)
- Cervial is later named by Ceria as one of the Silver-rank Captains who did not sense anything amiss about the sealed door. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L364)
