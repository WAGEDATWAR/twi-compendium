# Maughin
> Type: person | Also called: Maughin the Armorer | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.09
> See also: Jelaqua, Lorent, Pelt, Erin Solstice, Grimalkin
## Overview
- Maughin is a huge Dullahan smith in Pallass, nearly as broad-shouldered as Moore, who wears light engraved steel plate. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L596)
- Maughin works the forges of Pallass, and says Dullahans there supply much of the southern continent's armor and weapons. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L673)
- Maughin is Baleros-born, from Invictel in the north, and spent nearly thirty years there before coming to Pallass. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L1035-1038,L1051)
- Maughin has lived in Pallass for over six years at the time he gives his citizenship figure, enough for voting rights. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L388)
- Maughin says he lived in Pallass for the last decade, after thinking he would not grow competing with smiths from home. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L1051; Book 11, Ch6.31 L447)
- Maughin says the Dwarf Pelt puts out better quality goods than he does. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L445)
## With Erin
- Maughin says he will support the protest if Erin swears Liscor knew nothing of the fines, and she swears. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L637-641)
- Maughin offers to take a commission from Erin. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L673)
- Maughin goes pale when he meets Numbtongue, and then Ksmvr, at The Wandering Inn. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L546-575)
- Maughin shakes Ksmvr's hand mainly to make him go away, and says he is unsure why he is there. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L582-583)
- Maughin watched the end of the Daquin game through a friend's scrying orb. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L1063)
