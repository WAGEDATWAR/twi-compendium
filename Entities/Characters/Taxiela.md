# Taxiela
> Type: person | Also called: Galei (possibly the same being, per the narration) | First appears: Book 19, Ch7.54
> See also: Palt, Elena, Ryoka Griffin, Erin Solstice, Elusive Lot, Wistram
## Overview
- Taxiela is a Lizardgirl [Mage] of the Elusive Lot, a faction of Wistram, who answers Palt's spell. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1137,L1152)
- Taxiela calls Palt her beautiful apprentice and promises him a tome and forwarded lessons as a reward. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1138-1140)
- {belief} The narration says Galei and Taxiela are presumably the same being, and presumably Palt's master and member of the Elusive Lot. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1139)
- Taxiela's eyes widen when Palt saves the most impressive bit of his request for last, and she says she can do it. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1145-1146)
## Palt's call (Book 19)
- {event} Taxiela agrees to help after Palt explains his request, and says Earthers are known to her but all other attempts have failed. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1138-1140)
- Taxiela offers Liscor, Invrisil or Pallass the chance to be among the first to experience Wistram's illusion simulations, a collaboration with Aaron. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1143-1144)
- Taxiela tells Palt to stay ready for a window after an hour or two. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1146)
- {event} Taxiela materialises before Elena holding a glowing phone that is a very good illusion, and winks as she hands it over. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1152-1156)
## Galei (Book 19)
- Galei is a male Centaur of the Elusive Lot, addressed by Palt as Master Galei, who breaks into the call with a friendly male voice. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L225-227,L249)
- {event} Galei says that enchanting a mirror with [Scrying] is safe from Wistram reversing the spell if Palt does it. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L232)
- {event} Galei says the call has about six minutes left and arranges to resume later that night in one of the Elusive Lot's warded rooms. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L239-246)
- Galei says half of Wistram's [Mages] could communicate without anyone in Wistram knowing, since they are the Elusive Lot. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L250)
