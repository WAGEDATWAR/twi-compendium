# Yitton Byres
> Type: person | Also called: Lord Byres; Lord Yitton Byres; Yitton | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.15
> See also: Yvlon, Ylawes Byres, Tyrion Veltras, Lord Gralton Radivaek, Laken, Shallel Byres
## Overview
- Yitton Byres is the head of the small Byres family, a [Lord] whose chainmail is silvery steel. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L13,L23)
- Yitton Byres brought over a thousand retainers to the muster against the Goblin Lord, almost all his house could spare. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L23)
- Yitton Byres has lived over fifty-three years and never took up arms as a [Knight], though he has faced many monsters. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L324)
- Yitton Byres wears a maintained beard and dark brown hair turning grey, and carries himself with the gravitas of his class. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L122)
- Yitton Byres keeps himself clean and smells of rain and metal. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L119)
## Family and House
- The Byres house is regarded by other nobles as a small house with ancient holdings. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L130)
- Byres lands lie past Riverfarm. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L213)
- {belief} Yitton Byres says his family were vampire slayers in another age and the first [Knights] who rode with the Five Families. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L205)
- Yitton Byres says the Byres house has also clashed with [Witches] and other monsters, and many of the Byres line have perished in battle. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L205)
- Yitton Byres is the father of Ylawes and Yvlon Byres. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L328,L331)
- {rel} Shallel Byres is Yvlon's mother and comes from Terandria. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L399-404)
- Shallel Byres says she was the daughter of a very minor noble house and married into money. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L404)
## The Muster against the Goblin Lord (Books 7-9)
- Yitton Byres' political standing rose sharply when images from Liscor showed two of his children helping defeat the Face-Eater Moths besieging the city. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L24)
- Tyrion Veltras respects Yitton Byres as a solid, unpretentious [Lord], though they are not close friends. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L25)
- Yitton Byres has modest holdings and eats the same plain food as his men, as the family cannot afford lavish supplies. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L14,L23)
- Yitton Byres wears a longsword at his side in the nobles' pavilion. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L20)
- {event} When Lord Gralton threatens to confront Tyrion over the delay, Yitton says he would rather fight Gralton than suffer mutiny in the camp. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L19)
- {event} Yitton Byres proposes that a delegation persuade Tyrion of the nobles' discontent, and offers to be the first to join Gralton in it. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L22)
- Tyrion lets Yitton Byres into his protective spell bubble, so that Yitton accepts that Tyrion holds him in some esteem. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L330)
- Yitton Byres reports to Tyrion that two Goblin tribes are about to clash and offers to separate them. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L353)
- Yitton Byres is troubled to see strategy such as pike walls, crossbows and harassing tactics coming from the Goblins. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L336-337)
- Tyrion asks Yitton Byres to ride with him to the front for his input. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L372-373)
- Lord Erill told Yitton Byres over cups that Tyrion would want as many Goblins alive as possible. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L335)
- Yitton Byres was shaken by Tremborag's survival after being speared through the heart, and poured three cups of wine. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L325-326)
- Yitton Byres had fought a Goblin Chieftain and seen a Goblin Lord before, but never laid eyes on a Goblin King. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L330)
- Yitton Byres had not heard from Ylawes or Yvlon in a long while, and Ylawes had sworn to bring Yvlon back. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L331)
- {event} Yitton Byres watches the Goblin Lord's army absorb the defeated Goblins, and Tyrion answers his question with "There are enough to serve". (Book 9, Ch5.56G L313-318)
## Liscor and Laken (Book 9)
- Yitton Byres fears that Yvlon and Ylawes are in Liscor, where Tyrion's army is about to attack. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L142-148)
- Yitton Byres says Liscor's Mage's Guild refuses all [Message] spells that do not come from a Drake city. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L146)
- {belief} Laken hears that Yitton Byres was against attacking Liscor from the start, a position no other noble in camp takes. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L149)
- {event} Yitton Byres asks Emperor Laken for help, saying Tyrion is not open to reason. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L138-142)
- {event} Laken promises to discuss ransom or other means to rescue Yitton's children, and Gralton joins in. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L158-161)
- Laken names Lady Ieka and Lord Erill among nobles he is wary of, while judging Gralton chose his side faster. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L164)
- {event} Yitton Byres charges toward Tyrion with a sword, begging him to hold off the attack on Liscor so he can ransom his children. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L404-412)
