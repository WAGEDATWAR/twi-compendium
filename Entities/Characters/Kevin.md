# Kevin
> Type: person | Also called: Kevin (Earther); stoner guy (Erin's thought) | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.20
> See also: Joseph, Rose, Imani, Galina, Troy, Leon, Hedault, Pelt, Selys, Numbtongue, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Kevin is a young Earther from California who worked at a bicycle repair shop before coming to this world. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L570)
- Kevin is one of the seven Earthers who stay at The Wandering Inn for roughly a week after arriving. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L77,L96)
- Erin thinks of Kevin as the stoner guy, though Joseph knows he is actually very athletic. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L249)
- Kevin used to do the bookkeeping for the bike shop on his laptop, which is why the laptop carries an automatic backup drive. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L255)

## Bicycle and skateboard (Book 18)
- Kevin says he once thought about making a bicycle but Magnolia never went for it, and he has some sketches. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L572)
- Kevin wants to make a bicycle and a skateboard, and names steel, parts, money and the lack of rubber as the problems. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L575)
- Kevin says his mountain bike weighed about thirty pounds and the average speed of a bike is thirty miles per hour. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L704-710)
- Pelt identifies Kevin's metal, aluminum, as Serraleun, one of the metals that goes into Naq-Alrama steel. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L743-746)
- Pelt refuses to work Serraleun, saying the process is a pain, he hates the stuff, and it is poison if it gets into the lungs. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L756-759)
- {event} Kevin rides a steel skateboard down a Pallass staircase ramp from the 9th Floor, wearing a magical ring from Hedault. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L924-926,L934,L942-946)
- Kevin flips near the 5th Floor ramp, and Hedault's ring stops him a foot above the ground before dropping him. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L952-958)
- {event} A [Guard] arrests Kevin after the ride, but a mob of Pallass youth carries him off to ask about the skateboard. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L968-969)
- Hedault says the ring is only safe up to a certain weight and the skateboard itself is quite a projectile. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L967)
- {state} Kevin becomes the new [Tinkerer]-[Boarder], gaining two new classes, and is called the star of Pallass. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L140)
## Enchanted bicycle (Book 19)
- Kevin builds the bicycle with Pelt and Hedault, with a budget granted by Lyonette and Selys. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L971-975)
- The finished bicycle has a polished wooden frame, metal wheels and a wooden tire, with parts forged by Pelt. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L972)
- Hedault says the whole device weighs six point eight pounds and can be made lighter. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L975)
- Hedault says Miss Selys funded the project. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1073)
