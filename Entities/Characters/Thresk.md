# Thresk
> Type: person | Also called: Warmage Thresk of Albez | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.07H
> See also: Magic door, Ceria Springwalker, Pisces
## Overview
- Warmage Thresk's personal sanctuary is a small narrow room holding a rack of unusual weapons, a bookshelf of a mage's library, gold coins and gems, and a two-part staff topped with an aquamarine. (Book 3, Ch3.07H L402-410)
- A guardian voice in the room warns Ceria and Pisces that they are trespassing in the personal sanctuary of Warmage Thresk. (Book 3, Ch3.07H L421)
- A scholar says that Warmage Thresk of Albez is a [Mage] he has studied from unearthed artifacts, whose secret quarters were recently looted by adventurers. (Book 18, ChInterlude_A_Meeting_of_Druids L850)
- {belief} The scholar says the plain door was the final trap of Thresk's apartments, which teleported unwary robbers into a room full of overlapping [Insanity] spells. (Book 18, ChInterlude_A_Meeting_of_Druids L850)
