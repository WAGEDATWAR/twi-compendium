# Viceria
> Type: person | Also called: Viceria Strongheart; Vicci | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.17
> See also: Wailant, Garia Strongheart, Palt, Erin, Ryoka
## Overview
- Viceria Strongheart is Garia's mother and Wailant's wife, a slim, elegant [Mage] with a light brown braid. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L59)
- Viceria looks about ten years younger than her real age, which Garia says is in her thirties. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L98)
- Viceria is a Wistram graduate and a [Green Mage], an expert in plant magic, not a [Geomancer] or [Druid]. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L166,L170)
- Viceria chose farming over competing at Wistram, where disciplines involving growing earned little respect. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L170)
- Viceria's spells grow crops faster than normal, as well as a Skill would, according to Wailant. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L172)
- Viceria says classes change appearance: [Mages] tend to be thin and look young, while [Farmers] grow larger. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L103-106)
- Viceria says Garia inherited her father's build and that Garia's first class was [Farmer], not [Mage]. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L103)
## Events
- {event} Viceria met Wailant when he saved her from Carn Wolves in the Blood Fields, killing two after her escort died. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L166)
- Viceria says the second monster she met outside Wistram nearly killed her, and that she is far better at combat now. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L330)
- Viceria questions Ryoka about her Frost Faerie friend and how she gets contracts for hundreds of gold. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L62,L75)
- {belief} Viceria says Ryoka looks like the girls who bullied Garia, and that Wailant fears Ryoka is taking advantage of Garia. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L99)
- Viceria tells Wailant that people are right about Rhir being the Blighted Kingdom. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L289)
- Viceria is affiliated, at a distance, with Palt's Ullsinoi faction, supplying what they need as a [Green Mage]. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L914-919)
- Viceria grows dreamleaf and offers to sell Erin some as a gift for her husband's behavior. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L923)
- Viceria plants Sage's Grass in Erin's garden while Wailant works too. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L115)
- Viceria and Wailant give Erin an old bag of holding and a basket of about a dozen huge Bullhen eggs. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L72-82)
