# Alrric
> Type: person | Also called: Alrric Kerrfa; Administrator Alrric | First appears: Ch6.32
> See also: Ilvriss, Brilm, Tasilt, Ikrr, Casklal
## Overview
- Alrric Kerrfa is a Gnoll [Administrator], the manager of Wall Lord Ilvriss' company and second to Ilvriss in matters of business. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L626; Book 18, Ch7.33I L66)
- Ilvriss describes Alrric as his [Gemstone Administrator] and executive director in command of all his affairs. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L92)
- Alrric is one of only two Gnolls in Salazsar with this kind of power, [Administrator] being a rare class for a Gnoll. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L66)
- Ilvriss' company, the Gemscale holdings in mining, gem processing and more, employs thousands of Drakes, Gnolls and a few other races in Salazsar. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L107)
- Alrric wears a proper suit tailored to Gnoll specifications, with short-cut, combed fur unlike most Gnolls, and a deferential but only slightly bowed manner to Ilvriss. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L44)
- Alrric wears custom-tailored suits, and his fur shows as a matte chestnut that his co-workers know him by. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L70,L839)
- Alrric uses Ilvriss' name without his title, a mutual respect established through a delicate dance years earlier. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L98)
## History with Ilvriss
- Tasilt introduced Alrric to Ilvriss twelve years before Ilvriss' return from Liscor, and it took Ilvriss over a year to agree to hire a Gnoll. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L667-670)
- Ilvriss credits Tasilt with persuading him that a non-Drake [Administrator] was better than a Drake after a corruption scandal. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L670)
- Alrric and Ilvriss have worked together for seven years, coming on eight, and are used to each other's habits. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L98)
- Ilvriss gives Alrric full control over a development project, saying his claws are off it, and Alrric says that once Ilvriss would never have allowed this. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L661-664)
- Alrric proposes selling the rights to the tapped opal shafts to fund investment in a new seam that has already yielded truegold and magicore. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L656-659)
- Alrric tells Brilm that Ilvriss is not his master but his employer. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L531-532)
- Alrric dislikes the pompous Brilm, and Brilm says the dislike is mutual. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L530,L536)
## Personal life
- Alrric's wife is Ximenes Kerrfa, and he has a daughter, Sidinel, who is fifteen. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L727,L750)
- Alrric has an old family friend, Casklal, a Gnoll with a staff who teaches Sidinel. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L850-854)
- Alrric started working in the mines at age twelve after lying about his age to get into a crew. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L261)
- Alrric came to a desk at the top of a tower from the mines, and says he can teach anyone who doubts him a lesson in mining and fighting. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L261)
- Other Gnolls call Alrric a City Gnoll and a sellout, and in Salazsar they say he does not understand what it is to be a [Miner]. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L257,L369)
## Skills
- Alrric uses [Earmarked Funds - Recompensation], a monetary Skill that produces project-dependent gold from the company's own profits, which cannot be spent except in certain ways. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L272-279)
- Alrric uses the gold from the Skill to buy Ikrr's crews a night out in the city, noting that it cannot increase their pay. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L276,L279)
- Alrric has the Skills [Quick Recollection] and [Compartmentalized Knowledge], which help him recall and handle large amounts of information. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L709)
## Events
- Alrric gains a black eye in a bar fight with Gnolls who called him a City Gnoll and worse, a pet, and keeps the injury for a day or two as a reminder. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L49-56)
- Alrric opens Ilvriss' office door and finds the Wall Lord's chair fallen backwards onto the carpet. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L625-626)
- Alrric sees grey in his fur in a hand-mirror, treats it with a vial from his bag of holding and thinks slip-ups could cost him everything. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L304-307)
- Alrric goes into the shafts, is hit by beams from Stone Starers and has his ring give out, and runs out while Shriekblade charges them. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L444-449)
- Ikrr spots a bit of grey in Alrric's fur, and Alrric says fur dye is a perk of the job and threatens to put Ikrr under Yesh if he tells anyone. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L460-465)
- Alrric reapplies the fur dye after returning to his office, and his nervous shakes stop. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L469)
- Alrric refuses to answer [Gossips] who ask whether Ilvriss is really engaged to a [Princess] of the House of Minos. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L939)
- Alrric watches a scrying orb three days after meeting a group of Gnolls, and says they replaced two idiots. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L940-941)
## Secrets
- {reveal} Alrric was a Plains Gnoll, a fact that matters greatly to him, though Ikrr and his colleagues think he is a City Gnoll. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L370-371)
- {reveal} Alrric tells Ilvriss he is a City Gnoll when asked about leave for the Meeting of Tribes, and the narration calls the lie casual by now. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L680-682)
- {reveal} Alrric's chestnut fur is dyed, and a dye-removing tonic partly strips it, with Sidinel also dyed and Ximenes needing no dye. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L837-842)
- {reveal} Alrric uses fur dye and scent potions to hide his natural fur and smell when he leaves home with Casklal. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L864)
- {reveal} Alrric, Casklal and Sidinel are Doombringers, as are others in the group they meet, and all use dye to hide the common secret. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L865-868)
- {reveal} Ximenes knows the secret and chose to carry it, while Sidinel was born with it. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L865)
