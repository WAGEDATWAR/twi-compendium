# Gamel
> Type: person | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.12E
> See also: Laken, Durene, Riverfarm
## Overview
- Gamel is a young villager who becomes Laken's follower, and later his [Knight]. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L264; Book 7, Ch5.09E L42)
## Book 3: the avalanche
- Gamel was buried upside down in the snow and dug out by Laken, who told him he was safe. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L247-264)
- Gamel did not know the voice that rescued him although he knew everyone in the village. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L260)
- Gamel's father died in the snow, his neck snapped in the tumble. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L304-305)
- Gamel feels ashamed of having thrown things at Durene when she was a child, and part of him is still afraid of her. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L319)
- {rel} Gamel has followed Laken and Durene around since the day of the avalanche and waits outside Durene's cottage. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L329)
- {rel} Laken chose Gamel to accompany him as a helper to Durene, someone who could talk to guards. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L453)
## Books 4 to 5
- Laken and Durene expected to meet Gamel at midday in the plaza, but did not see him early. (Book 4, Ch3.36 L43-48)
- {rel} Gamel calls Laken "sire" and reports what he sees in the plaza. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L64,L77)
- Laken had sent Gamel to approach adventurer teams in the plaza and beg for help. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L423)
- Gamel walks with Laken's caravan and sometimes sits on the wagon, one of Laken's subjects. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L24)
- Laken believes Gamel knows some fire magic and asks him to request mages to melt the snow. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L176)
- {event} Gamel was cut across the chest by a Hobgoblin's longsword when he pushed Laken aside. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L278)
- Gamel survived the night, while those Laken called back from the grave died. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L393-394)
## Later books
- {state} Later, Gamel is Laken's [Knight], and Laken saved his life by ordering him to live. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L42,L58)
- Laken says Gamel waits outside the gate to attend to him, which Laken tells him not to do. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L42)
- Gamel announced Laken at the gathering as Protector of Riverfarm and Durene's Cottage. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L443)
- Gamel was a [Farmer] until a few weeks earlier and is not of noble birth, so some Knights resent him. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L52)
- Members of the Clairei Fields Order have been teaching Gamel personally. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L53)
- Gamel is one of three bodyguards of the Emperor, alongside a wand carrier and an archer. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L44)
