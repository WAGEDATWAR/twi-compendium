# Quexa
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.03D
> See also: Daly, Ken, Quallet, Geneva, Paige
## Overview
- Quexa is a blue-scaled Lizardgirl with deep red neck frills, bordering on purple, who serves in Gravetender's Fist. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L133)
- Quexa is a [Sorcerer], and says [Sorcerers] cast by thinking hard and do not study as [Mages] do. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L173)
- {belief} Quexa says Lizardfolk are the biggest species in Baleros and resemble Drakes without being related to them. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L155)
## Book 5
- Quexa shouts at the Centaurs that Gravetender's Fist are not enemies when Luan is shot. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L395-396)
- Quexa helps carry Luan to Geneva's camp on a stretcher. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L449)
- Quexa spots Ken and Luan running onto the battlefield in red-cross armor and leaps up in fear. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L339)
- Quexa becomes friends with Paige after Ken, Luan and Aiko leave. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L330)
- {state} Quexa loses a leg to a Centaur with an axe, and also part of her tail. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L294-297)
- Quexa tries to smile and chatters as if the loss were nothing when she greets Ken. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L295-297)
- Quexa uses a simple prosthetic leg that Geneva helped design and Daly carved. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L422; Book 5, Ch1.06D L461)
## Book 10
- Quexa uses a pegleg that Geneva helped make, and Geneva hopes to improve it with modern technology. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L101)
- {event} Quexa tells Daly she likes him but is not sure they should be together as she leaves to be a mercenary. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L407-410)
- {state} Quexa leaves the Earthworlders with Quallet's company because she wanted to be a mercenary. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L402-407)
- Quexa limps on her pegleg and keeps up with Quallet's revitalized Gravetender's Fist. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L526)
- Quexa is the first person Daly shows the crossbow to, and he hopes to impress Quallet with it. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L526-527)
- Quexa values Paige and Daly's all-wood crossbow at two gold coins, calling crossbows poor against armor or monster hides. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L530-535)
