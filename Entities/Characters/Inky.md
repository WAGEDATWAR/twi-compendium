# Inky
> Type: person | Also called: Second Mate Inky | First appears: Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1
> See also: Lasc, Trey, Flynn
## Overview
- Inky is a short, dark-skinned [Storm Sailor] with two normal arms, one normal leg and an octopus leg. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L166-170)
- Inky has a cephalopod's beak, an inner set of jaws, inside her mouth. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L171-172)
- Inky has suckers on her hands and the soles of her feet and spits ink, and she climbs the mast using the suckers. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L210-212,L220-221)
- {belief} A sailor named Begal says Inky is the daughter of a Drowned Person and a Human, who did not take the Sea's Bargain. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L221-222)
- {belief} Begal says the Drowned Folk would not take Inky, so she crews with air breathers. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L219)
- {belief} Begal says Inky is good at her job and helps kick the others into shape, though she is only a crew member for the trip and not one of The Emerald Signet's own. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L212,L219)
## Roles
- Inky is one of the [Divers] on the ship and has an Amulet of Waterbreathing. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L286)
- Inky is Second Mate under Captain Lasc, and is a [Storm Sailor]. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L311-313)
- Inky has an octopus tentacle for a leg and a swaying walk. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L309)
- {event} Inky screams at Captain Lasc to turn back from the storm, saying they are about to be destroyed, but he ordered full ahead. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L403-408)
