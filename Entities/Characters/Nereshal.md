# Nereshal
> Type: person | Also called: Lord Nereshal; the Chronomancer | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.02C
> See also: Blighted King, Blighted Queen, Fool, Erille, Tom
## Overview
- Nereshal is a [Chronomancer], a time mage, who serves the Blighted King as palace steward and confidant. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L285-294; Book 18, Ch7.34C L85)
- Nereshal wears dark grey robes lined with silver, which do not quite hide the scars on his arms and left hand. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L266)
- Nereshal has the cold, detached eyes of someone who has seen war. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L266)
- Nereshal is short and slightly pudgy, like a man who casts spells all day and manages the castle, but with a [Soldier]'s eyes. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L86)
## Role and Abilities
- Nereshal acts as steward of the palace until he is released from his duties, and warns the newcomers that guards may not wait for explanations. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L294)
- Nereshal says he can speed up a flight of arrows, slow an enemy's charge, hasten a weather front or slow the collapse of a wall. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L291)
- Nereshal says his most important duty is preventing the aging of the Blighted King, so the king may rule longer. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L294)
- {belief} Zekyria says Nereshal halts the aging of the Blighted King, perhaps the Queen, and that he may use the spell on himself. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L97-99)
- Nereshal is the Blighted King's most trusted confidant, an exceedingly powerful [Mage] who owes nothing to Wistram. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L85)
- {belief} Some wonder whether Nereshal is a match for Wistram's Archmages, or beyond one, but he cares nothing for Wistram. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L85)
- Nereshal is bound to Rhir and its eternal war, like Othius. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L85)
- Nereshal lifts his staff, freezes time, and bursts a bubble of time that makes the exhausted Blighted King look younger. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L145-147)
## Meeting the Americans, Book 6
- Nereshal slows the passage of time around Eddy's mouth, so Eddy cannot speak until it wears off, to teach him manners. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L280-283)
- Vincent and Emily are astonished that Nereshal is a time mage, since they thought time magic was impossible. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L285-287)
- Nereshal answers that lesser [Mages] may think so, but the Blighted Kingdom demands every form of magic in its defense. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L288)
- Nereshal says the Blighted King wants to appraise the group's worth, and notes that two of them reached Level 30. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L310-312)
- {reveal} Nereshal says the king also wants to confirm the rumors of Tom's success with his own eyes. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L313-314)
## The Demon Attack, Book 6
- Nereshal raises his hand and shows the invasion of the courtyard as a floating image above the banquet hall. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L245-247)
- Nereshal explains the Demons arrived by teleportation, likely with a scroll or artifacts, despite the anti-teleportation ward. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L6,L37)
- Nereshal estimates the Demon mage needs between forty minutes and two hours to open a teleport portal. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L34)
- Nereshal says no [Mage], not even an Archmage, could do the calculations for a distant portal in ten minutes or less. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L37)
- Nereshal commands the castle's magic while Tom eats at the banquet table, and the mage lights are put out. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L170-173)
- Nereshal and the other mages cast [Heart of Darkness], which floods the corridors with pitch-black magical darkness. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L173-174)
- Nereshal freezes a corridor full of Demons, leaving Tom only to stab them. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L242)
- Nereshal has a gash on his cheek after the fighting in the corridors. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L242)
- Nereshal and Tom fall from the same tower, and Nereshal survives. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L406)
- Nereshal kills a few hundred Demons yet is almost undamaged, and he asks Tom whether his madness is an act. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L383-384)
- Nereshal snaps that hundreds lie dead in the castle and thousands in the city. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L390-391)
- Nereshal's nose is broken by a juggling ball thrown by the Fool. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L413)
- Nereshal casts [Lightning Storm] at the Fool, who juggles the lightning back and knocks Nereshal unconscious. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L427-431)
## The Summoning, Books 15 and 18
- Nereshal watches Erin Solstice's image beside the Blighted King and grows pale at a word she used, wondering if it relates to the summoning spell. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L1309-1310)
- Nereshal tells the Blighted King he is not sure, but would bet that exactly a thousand came from Earth. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L95-97)
- Nereshal reasons the others would have met hostile monsters, or been teleported into the sea or sky, and ten times that number would have been noticed. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L97)
- {belief} Nereshal believes the summoning ritual of ten thousand lives produced a thousand children from Earth who were thrown across the world, not to their destination. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L93)
- Nereshal tells the king that the Fool had little knowledge of the ritual because he was not there. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L93)
