# Rags
> Type: person | Also called: Chieftain of the Flooded Waters Tribe; Fake Name Goblin (Tremborag's nickname for her); Chieftain of Goblinhome; Great Chieftain | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.24
> See also: Erin Solstice, Pyrite, Redscar, Garen Redfang, Tremborag, Reiss, Goblinhome, Flooded Waters Tribe, Redfang Tribe, Poisonbite, Noears, Quietstab, Ulvama, Greydath, Velan the Kind, Relc, Pisces
## Overview
- Rags is a small Goblin whom Erin Solstice meets at her inn; she is the smallest of the Goblins playing chess there and has already beaten two Antinium Workers back to back. (Book 1, Ch1.24 L310)
- Rags is the Chieftain of the Flooded Waters Tribe of Goblins who live and die in the area around Liscor. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L25)
- Rags is a young female Goblin with dark green skin that camouflages her in long grass. (Book 1, Ch1.39 L270)
- Rags has crimson eyes, as Goblins naturally do. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L39)
- Rags is short and attractive and just past four years of age, which by Goblin standards makes her about twenty, at least developmentally. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L146)
- Rags is not a Hob and has not undergone the growth spurt Hobs do, though by Book 7 she has levelled as high as many Hobs. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L147; Book 6, Ch4.32G L81)
