# Quelm
> Type: person | Also called: Alchemist Quelm | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.02
> See also: Octavia, Mabel the Magnificent, Jeffil, Celum
## Overview
- Quelm is an [Alchemist] in Celum, one of four [Alchemists] in the city, and a competitor of Octavia. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L17-19)
- Quelm is level 20 to 25 and moderately successful. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L93)
- Quelm is less thuggish than Jeffil or Mabel, but he holds a grudge. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L24)
## The match war
- Quelm figures out Octavia's match formula and improves it, selling more reliably ignited matches for less. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L19-21)
- Quelm chases Octavia after she delivers a message to him, and his neighbors do not call the Watch. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L17-18)
- Quelm bought out the muscle Octavia approached, according to Octavia. (Book 11, Ch6.19H L315)
- {belief} Quelm's matches are said to be the best in the city and the only ones sold, with his designs stolen. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L118)
- Quelm is the backer of a new gang that has created a monopoly on matches through low-level racketeering. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L93)
- Quelm's [Thug] seizes Octavia's potions and tells her she will sell through Quelm's store for two-thirds, with Quelm getting a third. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L488-492)
- Quelm's [Thug] says Quelm is concerned and sent him to help Octavia sell. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L479-482)
- Octavia calls the seizure robbery and threatens to go to the City Watch. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L495)
- A thug, shaken by Numbtongue, answers that Alchemist Quelm sent and hired them. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L703)
