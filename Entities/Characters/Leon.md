# Leon
> Type: person | Also called: Leon (Earther) | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.23LM
> See also: Troy, Joseph, Kevin, Rose, Galina, Imani, Grimalkin, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Leon is a young Earther from Poland who is a big fan of tabletop and miniatures games and also likes Magic the Gathering. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L622,L628)
- Leon is one of the seven Earthers who stay at The Wandering Inn for about a week after arriving, and is absent from the kitchen scene that morning. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L77,L96)
- Leon and Troy do their own thing as a team, according to the narration. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L335)
## Arrival and early doubts (Book 17)
- {belief} Leon doubts Erin has a higher level, asking how she has so much after being in this world only a few months. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L915-916)
- Kevin says Leon and Troy wanted a [Gamer] class, but he told them it would only make them better at playing on his laptop. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L246-247)
## Looking for a passion (Book 18)
- Leon and Troy note that Joseph has football and Galina has the actors, and wonder what they themselves are good at. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L603-609)
- Leon and Troy are given free room and board and paid by the hour for help, at slightly less than Ishkr and Drassi. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L608)
- Leon says that Imani gets special treatment and that Kevin would survive anywhere. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L611)
- {event} Leon and Troy discuss making a tabletop or card game, wondering how to design miniatures and whether they could draw cards. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L623-627)
## Dealing with Grimalkin (Book 18)
- Leon and Troy are touring Pallass with a bag of coins and secretly investigating the city. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L933,L989)
- Grimalkin notes that Leon and Troy came from Magnolia Reinhart's estates, which suggests they told her something. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L996)
- Grimalkin gives Leon a bag of coins instead of a bag of holding, and Leon recalls that Magnolia never gave him that much money. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L999)
- Leon tells Grimalkin that he and Troy are just travellers who are not taking sides. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L1000)
- {reveal} Leon and Troy agree to talk to Grimalkin for pay, secretly from Erin Solstice, beginning with their reaction to Master Felkhr. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L1005-1009)
