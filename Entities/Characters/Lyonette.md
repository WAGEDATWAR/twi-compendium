# Lyonette du Marquin
> Type: person | Also called: Lyon; Lyonette Marquin; Lyonette du Marquin; Miss Lyon; the Princess | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.21
> See also: Erin Solstice, Mrsha, Pawn, Krshia, Brunkr, Magnolia Reinhart, Klbkch, The Wandering Inn
## Overview
- Lyonette is a young Human woman who is eighteen years old, and a [Princess] of a Terandrian kingdom. (Book 2, Ch2.35 L2; Book 5, Ch4.14L L10)
- Lyonette thinks of herself as a scion of the Marquin house, one of the Hundred Families of Terandria and Sixth Princess in line to the Eternal Throne of Calanfer. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L7)
- {belief} Lyonette tells Erin she is of a minor Terandrian house, the Clavalettes, and distantly related to the royal family. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L91)
- {belief} Lyonette later claims to be a distant relative of Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L181)
- Lyonette thinks of herself as the third daughter of a small kingdom when she despairs, and as heir to one of the kingdoms of Terandria in another thought. (Book 3, Ch3.05L L216; Book 2, Ch2.35 L2)
- Lyonette is the girl Erin first calls Lyon, and she insists on her full name Lyonette. (Book 2, Ch2.26 L181-182)
- Lyonette has had only one small Skill from her [Princess] class in her entire eighteen years. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L10)
