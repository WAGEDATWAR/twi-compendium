# Chesacre
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2
> See also: Thaina, Purple Smiles, Klbkch
## Overview
- Chesacre is a female Antinium Soldier, female by name and personality even if not by appearance. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1439)
- Chesacre and Thaina are the two female Soldiers, whose paint forms a whole across their two bodies. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L159)
- Chesacre and Thaina are regulars of the inn, and Mrsha almost always sees them holding hands. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L163,L171)
## Book 16: painting
- {event} Chesacre and Thaina wash each other with soap and water before they take their paint, which is something new in the Hive. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1436-1441)
- The other Antinium watching look away as one, and Purple Smiles hangs Fortresses of Fluff blankets on wooden poles to give the two privacy. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1447-1452)
- {event} Chesacre picks up a brush and the two paint a design onto each other's bodies, a magical silver building that is whole only when they stand side by side. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1453-1457)
## Klbkch's attack
- {event} Chesacre, sitting next to Thaina, puts out one of her three remaining arms to block Klbkch as he walks toward Pawn with his sword drawn. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L747-748,L758)
- {state} Klbkch's sword cuts off Chesacre's arm, and Erin cries out at the sight of the green blood. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L759)
- Chesacre then walks in front of Klbkch and just waits, without raising her two remaining arms on her right side. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L761)
## Book 19: signing
- Chesacre and Thaina went into the dungeon, survived Headsnatcher and came back, when a thousand Antinium went in and only they made it. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L162)
- {event} After Thaina asks Mrsha in sign for her words, Chesacre carefully raises her hands and signs yes. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L177-178)
