# Johanas
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.03D
> See also: Ken, Luan, Daly
## Overview
- Johanas is an American from Earth who is stranded in Baleros and is among the Humans at the neutral-zone party. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L186-188)
- Johanas is already drunk when he tells Ken that a friend traded a flashlight to a war vendor for a ton of beer. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L186-187)
- Johanas tells Ken that casting [Repair] on Earth devices also recharges their batteries. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L187)
- Johanas points Ken to the war vendors who will sell to anyone for the right price. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L191-192)
## Death
- {event} Johanas kneels with five girls and two other men, all Americans caught sneaking through the forest, before a Dullahan executioner with an axe. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L261-264)
- {state} Dullahans execute Johanas and the seven other American captives before the battle resumes, and Ken remembers Johanas' corpse. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L274-276)
- Daly later cites the deaths of Johanas and the others when arguing that the Earthworlders must be able to defend themselves. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L375)
