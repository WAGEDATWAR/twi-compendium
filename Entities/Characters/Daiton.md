# Daiton
> Type: person | Also called: Master Daiton; Smith Daiton | First appears: Ch6.13K
> See also: Nawal, Flos, Ulyse, Trey Atwood
## Overview
- Daiton is an older grey-haired [Blacksmith] who calls himself master [Blacksmith] of His Majesty's forges. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L442-443)
- Daiton claims to be the highest-level and oldest smith in the area, and Trey is told no better smith exists in Reim, Germina or Hellios. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L443)
- Daiton makes blades for Flos, while Reim's two other [Smiths] handle more mundane work. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L466)
- Daiton shows Trey a complete new sword from a barrel, with the edge not yet sharpened. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L467-468)
- Daiton hopes to have two hundred swords and four times that many spears made if enough wood arrives. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L468)
- Daiton says his Skills cut the time to make a sword in half and names [Steady Rhythm], [Malleable Metal], [Twicelasting Fires] and [Blazing Forge]. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L524)
- Daiton says he has over a dozen Skills that help him shape metal quickly. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L524)
- {belief} Ulyse says Daiton's blades are beautiful but flawed, and Mirin says they are combination steel, poor for enchantments. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L272-273)
## The sword comparison
- {event} Daiton concedes to Flos that Nawal's sword surpasses his, noting she made it in a day with his steel. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L160-161)
- Daiton says none of his blades could hold such an enchantment and offers to retire his hammer. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L163)
- Flos tells the court that Daiton's art is peerless in its way, because he makes [Smiths] out of apprentices and swords Flos is proud to give his [Soldiers]. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L183)
- Daiton weeps and kneels as Jelaim and the court applaud him. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L184)
- Nawal withdraws her sword from comparison with Daiton's, says she cannot forge as fast as he, and apologises to him. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L177,L186)
