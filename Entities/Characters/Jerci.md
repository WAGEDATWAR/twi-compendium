# Jerci
> Type: person | Also called: Junior Guardswoman Jerci; Guardswoman Jerci | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.61L
> See also: Tkrn, Klbkch, Embria
## Overview
- Jerci is a female Gnoll and a native of Liscor who is in her second week as a Junior Guardswoman of the City Watch. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L490-491,L494)
- Jerci wears an earring in her right ear and has dyed her fur black in artful slashes down one arm. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L491)
- Jerci is issued a baton, and as a Junior Guard she is not allowed to patrol outside of squads. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L519)
## Patrol with Klbkch and Tkrn
- Jerci is uneasy about working with the Antinium Klbkch, and at first refuses even to look at her fellow Gnoll Tkrn. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L494,L504)
- Jerci confirms the scent of a [Thief] when Klbkch asks her, pointing out the direction when Tkrn scents the air. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L502-504,L511)
- Klbkch tells Jerci to observe how he works and to state any thoughts without fear of judgment. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L546,L556)
- Jerci looks scornful at Tkrn's search routine until Klbkch explains it, after which she blushes and mutters an apology to Tkrn. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L566-570)
- Jerci grows nervous when the patrol nearly meets Mister Soot, and Klbkch tells her not to look back over her shoulder. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L608-628)
- Jerci tackles the fleeing [Thief] and pins him with a knee on his back, on Klbkch's advice to rush him. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L664-667)
- Jerci helps Tkrn cuff the thief, and Klbkch corrects her position when escorting the prisoner. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L672-676)
- Klbkch tells Jerci she may ask Tkrn about law enforcement as it pertains to Gnolls. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L710)
- After the shift Jerci walks with Tkrn, asks him questions in a snappish way, and takes her ear piercing out. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L724)
## Later
- Embria scolds Jerci for grabbing a Creler with her paws during cleanup and tells her to use long-handled tongs. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L104-107)
