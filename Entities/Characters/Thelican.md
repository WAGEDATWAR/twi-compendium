# Thelican
> Type: person | Also called: General Thelican | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.53K
> See also: Femithain, Nsiia, Queen Yisame
## Overview
- Thelican is a Stitch-person [General] of Nerrhavia, who commands its nearly three hundred thousand strong army at Oliphant. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L7,L14; Book 13, Ch6.54K L194)
- Thelican reclines on a sofa in a large tent, attended by eight servants, and eats nuts fed by a Garuda [Slave]. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L14,L32)
- Thelican's silk-turned-flesh skin gleams palely. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L778)
- Thelican's officers are of the Silk-caste, and the Stitch-warriors made of Hemp are sturdier. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L14)
## Siege of Oliphant (Book 13)
- Thelican says his Hemp [Soldiers] will take the first wave at the walls, and that he needs only Illivere's Golems. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L31)
- {event} Thelican pulls Nerrhavia back nearly six hundred paces from Oliphant and fortifies when news comes that the Empress of Beasts sought the King of Destruction's aid. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L773-776)
- Thelican tells a [Mage] to tell Yisame he is fortifying and that the armies stay where he says. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L779)
- Thelican orders General Heic of Deimos to hold the left flank or he will break Heic's army himself. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L781-782)
- Alked Fellbow tells Thelican he forfeits his contract if Flos has joined Tiqr. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L879-880)
- Thelican says Nerrhavian pride demands Nsiia be taken as its prisoner, on Queen Yisame's insistence. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L150-151,L190)
- Thelican, tongue-tied, yields Nsiia to Femithain after weighing the wealth of garrisoning Oliphant. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L196-198)
