# Soot
> Type: person | Also called: Mister Soot; Mr. Soot | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.61L
> See also: Bearclaw, Klbkch, Palt, Nokha, Raskghar
## Overview
- Mister Soot is an ashen-scaled old Drake [Fence] of Liscor, one of the city's more notorious. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L599; Book 15, Ch7.07 L1106)
- Soot is very particular about being called Mister Soot. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1107)
- Soot's class is [Mastermind], specifically [Gang Mastermind], a step below a [Crime Lord]. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1108)
- Soot operates a significant portion of Liscor's underworld, with a gang that has magical wands, gear and good numbers. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1109-1111)
- Soot's gang avoids the Watch's dangerous fighters, and Soot is one of the Watch's most wanted. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1111-1112)
- Soot owns several safe houses where a [Thief] could hide for a price. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1106)
