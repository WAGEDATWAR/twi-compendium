# Teliv Witherscale
> Type: person | Also called: Teliv | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.49
> See also: Olesm, Erin Solstice, Lism, Wailant, Liscor Council
## Overview
- Teliv Witherscale is a Drake with black scales spotted with yellow. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L146)
- Teliv introduces himself to the Council as a part-time [Host] at Wishdrinks, available for dinner parties, birthdays and level-up parties but not funerals. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L1003)
- Teliv later calls himself the [Negotiator] in charge of the Esthelm diplomatic trip, and Liscor's Council authorises him to negotiate for the city. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L147; Book 18, Ch7.32D L43)
- Teliv attended Manus's academies with Olesm, and he decided being a [Tactician] was not for him. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L1021)
- Teliv says Manus trains [Diplomats] because of threats that come with the class, such as assassination, poisoning, intimidation and blackmail. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L1023)
- Teliv says he has not yet reached the [Diplomat] class, but hopes work from the Council will help him level up. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L1021)
- Teliv has a likeable manner and a perfect handshake, and Erin cannot tell whether it is a Skill or his personality. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L148)
- Teliv says he has a healthy regard for his life and is allergic to death, trouble and Erin's inn. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L160)
## Esthelm Trip (Book 13)
- Teliv bows to the Council, then pales when he is told the first talks will be with Guardsman Klbkch, saying he does not do Antinium. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L1002-1003,L1027-1029)
- Teliv asks Olesm about the new Council and then decides he needs a suit and a protective amulet. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L1031-1035)
- {event} Teliv collects Erin at her inn for the Esthelm trip, giving her a green flower as an academy custom. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L146-154)
- Teliv tells Erin to leave the bargaining to him, and that her presence is needed because Esthelm's people feel goodwill toward her. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L167)
- Teliv says Esthelm is poor and somewhat traumatized after the Goblin Lord's attack, and suggests a gift of dried fish. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L176)
- Teliv asks Erin not to mention that Liscor is swimming with Gold-rank adventurers. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L176)
- {event} Teliv says Erin came close to a diplomatic incident four times in Esthelm, and says she encouraged a near riot. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L41,L50-51)
- Teliv says Numbtongue sneaked into Esthelm by following the party, and Erin says Esthelm's people were calm, with some knowing Numbtongue. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L41-44)
- Erin says she helped get rid of a jerk who charged travel fees like [Bandits], and Ceria and Yvlon think it was a coup. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L52-55)
- Erin wears a wreath of flowers given to her in Esthelm. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L47)
