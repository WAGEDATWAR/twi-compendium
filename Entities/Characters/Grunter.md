# Grunter
> Type: person | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.19T
> See also: Headscratcher, Badarrow, Garen Redfang, Ylawes Byres, Redfang Tribe
## Overview
- Grunter is a large Redfang Hobgoblin who fights at Esthelm and wields an axe. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L214-217)
- Grunter asked the Horror if she was an innkeeper and learned she had been a flower-grower, not an innkeeper. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L99-103)
- {event} Thirteen Redfang warriors fought twenty-five black-armoured Goblins, and Grunter charged first and threw Goblins into their fellows. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L214-217)
- Ylawes broke Grunter's right arm with his shield. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L239)
- Grunter challenged the Goblin commander, killed a Hobgoblin sent against him, and shouted "Redfang!" (Book 3, Ch3.20T L266-270)
- Grunter's axe smashed the commander's Shield Spider, and the commander's halberd then took his hand. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L273-276)
- {state} Grunter killed the Goblin commander with his remaining arm, and died covered by the black-armoured Goblins' blades. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L277-282)
- Later, Headscratcher and Badarrow mourn Grunter among their fallen. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L197; Book 3, Ch3.20T L287)
