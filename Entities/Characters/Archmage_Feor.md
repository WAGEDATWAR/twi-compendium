# Archmage Feor
> Type: person | Also called: Feor | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.08
> See also: Wistram, Teura, Perorn, Blackmage, Flos, Remi
## Overview
- Feor is an Archmage of Wistram and an aged half-Elf with a long, silvery-white beard. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L722; Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L6)
- Feor has pale blue and silver eyes with unnaturally bright pupils, and is easily twenty times older than Aaron. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L12)
- Feor is one of the few most powerful spellcasters in the world and calls Aaron by the name Blackmage. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L6)
- Feor belongs to the Centrist faction of Wistram and keeps a personal group of half-Elf mages he trusts implicitly. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L44)
- Feor's aide Teura is one of his best [Mages]. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L44)
- Feor is part immortal. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L219)
- Feor is considered to be Wistram's top chess player. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L574)
## Events
- Feor leads Wistram's circle watching the battle at the inn and recognises the young man who cast the rainfall spell and the half-Elf who cast [Ice Wall]. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L722-723)
- Feor notes that Falene Skystrall, a Wistram graduate, was also there, and says inquiries should go through her. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L725)
- Feor wonders aloud what Flos is up to as one of the scrying mirrors shows the King of Destruction. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L727-728)
- Feor orders an investigation of Erin's door, an offer to buy it, or else a task for recent graduates. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L730)
- Feor remembers Perorn Fleethoof as a centaur with a fiery mane who fought beside him on Rhir two decades earlier. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L218-219)
- Feor oversees nearly fifty Wistram [Mages] linked in magical circles, who have over four hundred contacts when the feed opens. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L220-221)
- Feor orders Teura to send a team to find and retrieve Remi, because Canada is a country from the other world. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L803-805)
- Feor tells the team to be ready for conflict, since the Siren of Songs and other rulers are furious at the article. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L806)
