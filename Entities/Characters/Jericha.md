# Jericha
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.48G
> See also: Tyrion Veltras, Yitton Byres, Ullim
## Overview
- Jericha is Tyrion Veltras' personal aide and [Mage], and he relays orders and messages through her. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L448; Book 9, Ch5.51G L331; Book 9, Ch5.60 L432,L451-452)
- Jericha did not study at Wistram but at the mage's academy in First Landing, which causes tension with Wistram [Mages]. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L456)
- Tyrion considers Jericha one of the best spellcasters he employs. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L456)
- Jericha is probably higher-level than a visiting [Assassin] in combat and has more expensive artifacts. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L27)
- Jericha is a nobleman's second-in-command, an elite. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L27)
## Events
- {event} Jericha burns Magnolia Reinhart's [Message] after Tyrion reads it, and Tyrion tells her to send Magnolia his regards. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L338-342)
- {event} Jericha stuns Yitton Byres with a spell when he charges Tyrion, on Tyrion's order to take him alive. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L404-405,L429-430)
- Jericha is disturbed when Magnolia's [Message] names her and warns of consequences if it is not delivered. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L451-452)
- {event} Jericha insults the poisoning [Assassin], who stabs her in the stomach with a dagger, and Jericha heals herself with a wand. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L26-32)
- Jericha hates the [Assassin] who holds Tyrion's sons hostage by poison, as does Ullim. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L22)
