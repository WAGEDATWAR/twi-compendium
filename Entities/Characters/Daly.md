# Daly
> Type: person | Also called: Daly Sullivan; Captain Daly | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.03D
> See also: Ken, Luan, Paige, Geneva, Quallet, Siri, Edima, Calectus
## Overview
- Daly Sullivan is a tanned, blond young man from Australia who carries a sword and later an axe. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L252; Book 5, Ch1.06D L229)
- Daly gains the [Rogue] class by sneaking around the battlefield. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L252)
- Daly's twitching trigger finger shows how tense he is, and he tries to make it stop. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L791)

- {state} Later, Daly is an [Axe Warrior] who is about to become a [Carpenter]. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L512)
