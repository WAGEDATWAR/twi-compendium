# Twisted Queen
> Type: person | Also called: Twisted Hive Queen | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.39A
> See also: Klbkch, Wrymvr the Deathless, Anand, Armored Queen, Xrn the Small Queen, Rasea Zecrew
## Overview
- The Twisted Queen is missing one large mandible, parts of her body and one eye, as if something had eaten away at her. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1217)
- The Twisted Queen speaks haltingly and jerkily with her mouth, but her mental sending is far more eloquent, showing an intact mind. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1217; Book 19, Ch7.48K L774)
- The Twisted Queen is described as having endured the privations of the body, unlike the Armored Queen's broken spirit. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1216)
## Encounter with Klbkch (Book 18)
- {event} The Twisted Queen tells Klbkch that Wrymvr is waiting for him. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L460-461)
- The Twisted Queen apologises to Klbkch for not recognising him and says Wrymvr greeted him with their welcomes. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1218)
- {event} The Twisted Queen lifts Anand and lowers him toward an empty Birther sac to see what his mind has changed. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1225-1230)
- Klbkch says Anand is the highest-levelled individual he has brought and that his death is unacceptable. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1234)
- {event} Klbkch draws his swords and puts the tip of one in the Twisted Queen's side, ordering her to put Anand down. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1248-1256)
- The Twisted Queen asks what Klbkch is doing, saying she is a Queen, and then puts Anand down. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1250,L1254-1257)
- Klbkch's sword only enters the outer layer of the Twisted Queen without drawing blood. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1257)
- {belief} The Twisted Queen says the flaw of the Hives is Queens, and says to remove the Queens. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1320)
- The Twisted Queen sends that her project is complete and that the Antinium are trapped here, with Wrymvr concurring. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1317)
- The Twisted Queen says the Queens are too large to move and too dangerous, and tells Klbkch that he does not need her anymore. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L1326)
## Council of war (Book 19)
- Klbkch asks Wrymvr whether he could kill the Illusionist, and Wrymvr answers yes through their shared link. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L765-768)
- The Twisted Queen says her attempts to create water-capable Antinium have all failed. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L783-785)
- The Twisted Queen sends that the Antinium lack a way to cross the sea, being landlocked among unassailable Walled Cities. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L775)
- Klbkch says the Illuminary could destroy any vessel the Antinium seize, and suggests hiring a [Pirate] of Rasea's caliber or obtaining Greater Teleportation scrolls. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L781,L787)
