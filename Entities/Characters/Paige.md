# Paige
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.03D
> See also: Daly, Ken, Luan, Geneva, Siri, Kirana, Quallet
## Overview
- Paige is an Australian young woman from Earth whom Daly introduces to Ken at the edge of the neutral zone. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L322-323)
- Paige studied aerodynamics, not the engineering of workshops, and had planned to go into engineering. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L475; Book 10, Ch6.04D L478)
- Paige is the appointed treasurer of the United Nations company, under pressure to save coins. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L293)
## Book 5
- Paige relays a rumour that a soldier near Untimely Demise's work area was shot rather than killed by undead. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L329-332)
- Paige says she heard that the Captain was asking about the shooting but not whether he got an answer. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L332)
- Paige becomes friends with Quexa after Ken, Luan and Aiko leave Gravetender's Fist. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L330)
- Paige screams when the Dullahans execute the American captives. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L273)
## Crossbows and money
- {event} Paige photographs a crossbow on sale in the market with her smartphone and works out how its parts fit together. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L491-499)
- Paige plans an almost all-wood crossbow that is cheap to make, with Daly asking his boss for wood cut to her measurements. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L499-505)
- Quexa values Paige's wooden crossbow at about two gold coins, saying it looks poor next to a metal one. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L530-535)
- Paige adds a wooden stirrup to her crossbow so Daly can span it. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L576)
- {state} Paige gains the Skills [Reinforced Frame], [Quick Assembly] and [Advanced Crafting], and levels up quickly. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L577)
- Paige has banned weapons in the house, but treats the Bushrangers as a special case. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L37)
- Paige hands Kirana four gold and three silver coins as the weekly food budget and pays the month's rent through Daly. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L100-103)
## Gunpowder and bombs
- {event} Paige tells the group she can now make gunpowder from charcoal and saltpeter, and that volcanoes supply plenty of materials. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L107-111)
- Paige decides to skip guns, since a [Blacksmith] would expose her work and others would steal them. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L116)
- Paige plans an emergency weapon and grenades, which would be non-magical pots filled with shrapnel. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L119-122)
- Paige builds a papier-mache grenade the size of Daly's hand, and an iron-shelled shrapnel bomb a bit smaller than a basketball. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L402; Book 11, Ch6.20D L442)
- Paige took three days of experiments to find the gunpowder ratio, and less than a week to make the bomb. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L463)
- {belief} Paige fears what would happen if Quallet or a larger company got the bomb. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L463)
- Paige and Daly agree the bombs stay secret and are used only on monsters, and Paige agrees to make three. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L477-481; Book 11, Ch6.20D L508)
- Paige cannot build ballistae because she does not know how torsional force works, which Blake thinks involved skeins of rope. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L486-493)
- Paige and Daly decide not to tell Geneva and the other leaders about the bombs. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L816-826)
- {state} Paige consolidates her classes into [Blackpowder Engineer] and can make a small mound of black powder from her palm daily. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L170; Book 14, Ch6.68 L184-186)
- {reveal} Paige tells the leaders that she and Daly have created grenades and bombs, and Geneva looks deeply betrayed. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L186-189)
## Book 17
- Paige keeps producing black powder every day, agreeing with Daly though she thinks they should have told Geneva. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L340)
- Paige shows Siri a steel hand-crossbow with spring steel limbs, with improved range and punching power. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L362-364)
- Paige says the Greek Earthworlders helped her make torsion crossbows, and that she may mount a mini-ballista on a wagon. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L364)
- Quallet has bought three dozen of Paige's crossbows, and Gravetender's Fist won a battle with crossbow fire at range. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L364-367)
