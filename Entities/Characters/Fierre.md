# Fierre
> Type: person | Also called: Serafierre val Lischelle-Drakle; Fierre the Vampire; Fierre (Serafierre val Lischelle-Drakle) | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.00
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Himilt, Colfa, Bamer, Madain, Belavierr, Teriarch, Valeterisa, Salamani, Kelysta, Gailt
## Overview
- Fierre is a thin, pale girl wrapped in layers of clothing who coughs and sniffs, and who has sharp teeth. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L23,L53)
- Fierre lodges at The Huntress' Haven, Madain's inn in Reizmelt, as does Ryoka. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L25,L47,L53)
- Fierre has lodged at the same inn in Reizmelt as Ryoka for nearly a year when Ryoka first notices her. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L53)
- Fierre's irises are slightly red. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L112)
- Fierre is twenty-five years old but looks about sixteen, and she is a good head shorter than Ryoka. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L214; Book 17, Ch7.24 L909)
- Fierre's full name is Serafierre val Lischelle-Drakle. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L703)
- Fierre grew up on her family's farm, then moved to the city to earn a living. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L213)
## Work in Reizmelt
- Fierre works as an opener from a small room reached by knocking twice and kicking the door once. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L112)
- Fierre's workroom has no windows, a locked and barred back door into a [Carpenter]'s shop, and a magical ward that hums in the air. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L114)
- Fierre opens envelopes and checks their contents without breaking the seal, which makes her a Runner's fence or information broker. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L144-145)
- Fierre does not deal on the wrong end of the law enough to worry the local authorities. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L145)
- Fierre pays Ryoka three silver and six copper for an open-and-check, and offers six gold coins for the information in the letter. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L120-121,L167)
- Fierre also grinds Duskwilter stems for the [Alchemist] Kelysta, who scolds her for working without a mask and thick gloves. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L315-319)
- Fierre hauls cargo for a silver coin an hour, and does not sweat while doing it. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L647-650)
- Fierre works on foundations as a [Laborer] and spends an hour or two at her brokering job beforehand. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L155; Book 12, Ch6.42E L347)
- Fierre's [Opener] class is one of only three in Reizmelt. Other cities usually have an [Informant], [Fence] or [Broker] in that role. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L228)
- Fierre sleeps six hours a night and needs no more. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L82)
## Vampire nature
- Fierre is a Vampire, and Ryoka guesses it after noticing her sharp canines. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L679-686)
- {reveal} Fierre panics when Ryoka asks what being a vampire is like, and begs her not to tell anyone. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L680-687)
- Fierre's senses are far beyond Human, and she can read a woman's embroidery or a person's heartbeat from a distance. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L91; Book 12, Ch6.42E L356)
- Fierre wears a hat and cloth wrappings against the sun, whose rays feel unpleasant on her skin even when weak. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L89)
- Fierre drinks goat's blood as a snack and feels more Vampirish when she has been exerting herself at night. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L156)
- Fierre has trouble getting drunk. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L352)
- Fierre is a Vampire, so she gains no levels, and she thinks of Vampires as animal-like or like the sub-races. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L224)
- Fierre wishes she had a class, because she knows what Skills such people get. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L228)
- {belief} Old Bamer says Vampire Lords had powers such as turning into mist, and even Skills, but Fierre thinks those might be relics. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L82)
- Fierre casts no reflection, which is why she does not appear in mirrors. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L895; Book 18, Ch7.41 L228)
- Modern Vampires live on animal blood, but Fierre and her brother Rivel once bribed a girl for human blood and drank it. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L329-330)
- Fierre tasted human blood two more times before her parents found out, and finds it far more satisfying than animal blood. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L331)
- Human blood did not give Fierre the old Vampire powers or cure her cough. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L332)
- Fierre believes Vampires are all sick, and notes that her father is sick, her mother has a rash and Bamer is dying. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L332)
- Fierre says Vampires are few and mostly related, and that Humans must be drunk and not notice how cold she is. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1343)
- Fierre mixes blood with magical liquor in a shot glass for herself and Ryoka. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1344)
