# Viltach
> Type: person | Also called: Archmage Viltach | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.68
> See also: Feor, Archmage Nailihuaile, Aaron Vanwell, Elena
## Overview
- Viltach is a Human Archmage of Wistram, described as the Archmage of nobility in Terandria. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L294)
- Viltach is described as one of two of the five Archmages currently controlling Wistram, and is Human. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L782)
## Book 14-15: the Archmages
- {event} Viltach visits Amerys with Feor and Nailihuaile, and Amerys greets the three Archmages as having come back. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L293-294)
- Viltach lacks Amerys's intensity in his gaze, as the three Archmages stop before her magical barrier. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L295)
- {belief} Viltach is delighted when A'ctelios claims its leader was assassinated by Gazi Pathseeker and the Quarass of Germina, since he had always known Flos could not keep his oath. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L781-784)
- {event} Viltach tells Mage Rievan Forstrom to send a [Message] to the Drake commentators, and summons Feor and Nailihuaile to meet him at once. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L783)
- {belief} Viltach expects that once Flos is forsworn, Reim will die, and that this moment will be broadcast worldwide by Wistram Academy. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L784-786)
## Book 18: the broadcasts
- {event} Viltach reports that Niers Astoragon, the heads of the Four Great Companies and others have found out Wistram is watching, with word spreading from ruler to ruler. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L812-813)
- Viltach says Wistram is denying the allegations but paying a political cost, and that he has been smoothing feathers in Terandria. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L812)
- Viltach consults the numbers of how many people are watching a broadcast and dislikes Elena's uppity tone. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1127)
- {belief} Viltach murmurs that perhaps Wistram wants the people pacified and that they do not need to know the truth. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1128)
- Viltach's murmur is a bit too loud: the crowd of Earthers quiets, Elena looks appalled and Feor scowls at him. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1129)
- {event} Viltach and Nailihuaile, ordered by Feor to be silent, analyze the Sword Prognugators' combat as ranking them high-silver on talent and Gold with artifacts. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L416-419)
## Book 19: the storm
- {event} Viltach joins Feor and Nailihuaile on a high balcony as the Emerald Signet struggles toward the Isle of Mages through a magical typhoon. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L393,L411-413)
- {event} Viltach, Feor and Nailihuaile link their wand and relic to stretch the Weatherward south, because the ship carries two children from another world. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L419-423)
- Viltach howls at Feor to stop pulling the Weatherward in too close, as the protection spell retreats toward the isle. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L424-426)
- The linked Archmages extend the Weatherward into a corridor of safety nearly three miles southwards, and the Emerald Signet sails in. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L430)
