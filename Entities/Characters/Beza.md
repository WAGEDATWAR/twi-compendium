# Beza
> Type: person | Also called: Bezale; Bezale of Maweil | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.59
> See also: Montressa, Palt, Isceil, Ulinde, Calruz, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Beza is a Minotauress [Spellscribe] and a graduate of Wistram, introducing herself as Bezale of Maweil. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L237,L266)
- Beza is taller than most species and has an incredible physique for a [Mage], second only to Grimalkin. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L366)
- Beza specializes in combat scrolls, and scribing is a rarer specialization even in Wistram. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L252,L269)
- Beza sells [Light] scrolls at about a gold each, and also Tier 4 scrolls, to [Merchants] more than pedestrians. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L237-239,L270)
- Beza says that to scribe a higher-Tier spell, a [Mage] generally has to be able to cast two tiers higher. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L269)
- Beza keeps a small rack of combat scrolls, among them [Stoneskin] and [Haste], watched like a hawk. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L251,L268)
- Beza came eighth in Grimalkin's lifting contest and calls it disgraceful. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L230-232)
- Beza develops a compulsion for working out with Grimalkin's weights. (Book 18, ChInterlude_A_Meeting_of_Druids L388)
## Beliefs about Minos
- {belief} Beza claims Minotaurs are naturally stronger than any other race and make siege weapons that few understand. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L367)
- {belief} Beza says the House of Minos is a peaceful utopia for those who do not wish to leave its islands. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L377)
- {belief} Beza says honor is the one virtue above all in Minos, and that those above judge those below, but everyone also acts by conscience. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L334,L340)
- Beza says Minos's King is a title and the King is a female paragon of honor, and that Queen would make others ask about marriage. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L343-352)
- Beza says Minotaurs are in an ancient conflict with Goblins, and that Goblin power persists after thousands of years. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L361-363)
- Beza lists the paradises of the age as Khelt, the Kingdom of Keys, Heiste the Archmage's Isle, the Silent Dome of the Dullahans and the House of Minos. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L382)
- Beza left Minos because she lacked the magical ability for the role she wanted, and went to Wistram instead. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L370)
- Beza says she left to explore the world, show honor, and return to Minos a hero. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L385)
- Beza says she was carried off the battlefield while the adventurers slew an Adult Creler, and feels she was worse than those who stayed and died. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L387-389)
- Beza says that someday she will return to the House of Minos with a story as a gift to her people. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L387)

## Brokering and Minos at War
- Beza and Montressa act as gatekeepers of the door, brokering businesses between cities, with Cedava of Invrisil as client number six. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L463-471)
- Beza and Montressa's first client was Rufelt and Lasica, then Pelt, then Hedault. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L468-470)
- {event} Beza brings a scrying orb to Calruz's cell to show the House of Minos going to war, and sits cross-legged beside him. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L78-91)
- Beza has visited Calruz twice before. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L83)
