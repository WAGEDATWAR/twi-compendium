# Sillk
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Ch6.22D
> See also: Merrik, Niers, Foliana, Xelic
## Overview
- Sillk is a Lizardman student three years older than Umina, whom Merrik worries about in front of the graduates. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L48-51)
- Sillk is a [Rogue], and Niers calls him one of the officers in training. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L908; Book 11, Ch6.24D L128)
- Sillk tells Merrik that each of them has unique abilities and Sillk has his class. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L50)
- {event} Sillk and Xelic jump a group of Dullahans and take down their leader. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L908)
- Niers says he is glad Sillk and Merrik did not team up, since each fought in his own strengths. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L129)
- Niers praises Sillk for taking out enemy officers but says he was captured the instant Wistram's [Mages] tagged him with a locator spell. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L135)
- Sillk says he should have gone to ground sooner and hidden after being aggressive. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L138)
- Niers rewards Sillk with three days of shadowing Foliana, to learn how she disappears. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L143)
- {event} Niers says the Forgotten Wing does not and never has run an [Assassin] training school, and he is challenged to swear it on a truth spell. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L908-909)
