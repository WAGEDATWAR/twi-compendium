# Luciva Skybreath
> Type: person | Also called: Dragonspeaker Luciva; Dragonspeaker of Manus | First appears: Ch6.33E
> See also: Rafaema, Lulv, Makhir
## Overview
- Luciva Skybreath is the Dragonspeaker of Manus and has ruled Manus for fourteen years. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L29,L33)
- Luciva is a Drake with pale-sky-blue scales mixed with white and, rarely, yellow, which give her an exotic, un-Lizardperson look. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L32)
- Luciva has wings and breath, which mark her as a True Oldblood, the rarest of the Oldbloods. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L48)
- Luciva wears Dragonscale armour, a cold-blue helmet that gives off vapour and boots whose soles never touch the ground. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L29-30)
- Luciva carries a glaive crackling with electricity, known to Manus' people as the Fang of Manus, whose old name is Parentkiller. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L30-31)
- Luciva's breath gives off bright flashes and an acrid smell, and her furled wings stir when she is moved. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L46-47)
## Mourning for Hedica
- Luciva wears white beneath her armour in mourning. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L33)
- Luciva loses her daughter Hedica when reinforcements from Manus take part in Tyrion Veltras' battle at the Bloodfields. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L45-46,L139)
- Luciva tells Wall Lord Makhir that she takes no offence at his remark and that no one at the table is to blame, though she calls the battle beyond all acceptable cost. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L40-43)
## Security Council
- Luciva convenes Manus' Security Council to discuss the Siege of Liscor, the battle at the Bloodfields and the ambitions of Tyrion Veltras. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L33-34)
- Luciva asks whether the Human assault on Manus' people was unprovoked, and the Council agrees it was. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L64-72)
- Luciva looks to the youngest Drake, Rafaema, for her opinion when the Council is divided on replying. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L78-80)
- Luciva decides that the south must answer the Human attack. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L83-85)
- Luciva says Manus will take the lead and may request the best fliers from other Walled Cities, since Manus' children need not bear all the risk. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L87)
- Luciva signs and seals the order for Manus' first plan of ruination with her ring. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L89-94)
- Luciva expects only Oteslia to object, and the Cyclops who drew up the plans says nothing. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L96)
- Luciva tells Aldonss to report on Noelictus, saying the matter concerns a Grade 9 threat, the Witch. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L101-111)
- Luciva says no one kills the Witch, and asks Aldonss for a proposal and for news if the Witch is credibly reported arriving on Izril. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L120,L126)
- Luciva tells Rafaema that a war with the Humans would be disastrous because the Antinium would not be idle. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L134)
## The Wyvern attack on Pallass
- Luciva asks Rafaema what she means when she says the Wyverns could pool their breath attacks. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L133-134)
- Luciva's lips twist at the report of Frost Wyverns attacking Pallass. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L138-140)
- {belief} Luciva wonders whether Bird is a hunting Prognugator designed for anti-air attacks, and says she must change her plans. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L1385-1387)
## Secret of the Dragon
- {reveal} Luciva and Manus' war council know that Rafaema is a Dragon and that she is the hope of Dragons and Drakes. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L119,L221)
- Luciva is believed in Oteslia to have no idea of the different sort of Dragon, Cire, whom the people of Oteslia protect. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L279)
