# Theofore
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.08R
> See also: Lady Magnolia, Ryoka Griffin, Reinhart, Assassins' Guild
## Overview
- Theofore is an [Assassin] who looks so ordinary that he is forgettable, which Magnolia suspects is a skill. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L129-130)
- Theofore clearly dislikes being addressed by his name. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L132)
- Theofore is not a high-level member of the Guild, but is competent enough to be assigned to Lady Magnolia on request. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L283)
## Guarding Ryoka (Book 1)
- {event} Theofore and his associates guarded Ryoka Griffin for Lady Magnolia but lost her when she accepted a request to the High Passes. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L131-133)
- Theofore told Magnolia his group was not equipped to fight monsters, having assumed the job was protection against other humans. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L135)
- {event} After his team's failure, Theofore said Magnolia would be fully reimbursed, but that the Guild would take no further responsibility. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L135)
- {event} Theofore was assigned to watch Ryoka at the inn and found her climbing onto the roof over her room. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L284)
- {event} Ryoka kicked Theofore's right leg just above the knee, numbing it, and then outran him across the rooftops. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L290-299)
- Theofore knew Magnolia would be unhappy with his failure and went back to the inn to report. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L302)
- Theofore uses an unarmed combat skill, [Nerve Strike: Paralysis], and Ryoka swatted his hand down. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L287)
- Theofore lacks the [Sure Footing] skill, which would have helped him fight on the slanted roof. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L288)
## Later appearances
- {event} In Book 2, a report to Magnolia says Theofore has located Ryoka, who will return in two days. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L78)
- Magnolia thinks Theofore perhaps understands Ryoka better than the other [Assassins] and [Scouts] who searched. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L79)
- Theofore reported that two of Ryoka's fingers are missing and that she is not alone. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L90-93)
- Theofore believes Ryoka may have rescued a young Gnoll cub. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L93)
- {state} By Book 11, Theofore is an [Assassin] who stands in Magnolia's entrance hall beside her [Butler] and [Maid], with a bounty put on him by the Guild. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L691,L699)
- Theofore's fellow [Assassins] thought he was dead, and he answers that life is not so kind. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L699-700)
- Theofore draws a throwing dagger and tells three attacking trainees that he is better than all of them. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L701-702)
- {event} Theofore urges the trainees to surrender, and they throw down their blades. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L702-703)
