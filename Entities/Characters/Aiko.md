# Aiko
> Type: person | Also called: Aiko Nonomura | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.02D
> See also: Ken, Luan, Geneva, Quallet
## Overview
- Aiko is a young Japanese woman from Earth, who appeared in Gravetender's Fist's company a week before meeting Geneva. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L300)
- Aiko is a [Nurse] and a [Sketcher] hoping to become an [Artist], and Geneva's assistant in most things. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L57)
- Aiko is Aiko Nonomura, a young Japanese [Nurse]. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L670)
## Arc
- {event} Aiko and Ken befriend Luan, who tells them wars are never safe. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L271,L317)
- Aiko develops a crush on Luan, the older, optimistic South African who takes command. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L230)
- {event} Aiko, Ken and Luan tell Geneva they appeared last week, and Luan says at least forty people from their world remain. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L300,L337)
- Geneva offers food and shelter only to people from their world. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L335,L339)
- {event} Luan, Ken and Aiko leave Gravetender's Fist to go to Geneva. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L293,L357)
- {state} Aiko asks Geneva to let her help, saying she is not brave but wants to help the hurt, and becomes her surgical assistant. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L55-69)
- Aiko threads needles, sharpens scalpels and prepares the healing potion, pouring it with a medieval dropper. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L69,L197)
- {event} Aiko kills a Dullahan with scalpels in his eye and neck, and stares at her bloody hands. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L67-70)
- {event} Aiko confesses her feelings to Luan, who refuses because he is married with a child one year and five months old. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L440-448)
- {event} Gravetender's Fist and the other soldiers leave the valley together. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L371,L389)
- {state} Later, Aiko is the first [Nurse] trampled when a mob of patients rushes in, and her protective glass mask breaks. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L670,L901-903)
- Aiko develops a fever and facial cuts, which Geneva treats without healing potions. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L906-909)
- Aiko is recovering while Blake falls sick with the Yellow Rivers disease. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1022)
