# Ulrien
> Type: person | Also called: Captain of Griffon Hunt | First appears: Ch2.22
> See also: Griffon Hunt, Halrac, Typhenous, Revi, Regrika Blackpaw, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Ulrien is the Captain of Griffon Hunt and a Gold-rank adventurer. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L194)
- Ulrien wields an enchanted greatsword, and several enchanted rings take the force of blows when he fights without armor. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L194-195)
- Ulrien is incredibly strong, strong enough to swing his greatsword around like it was nothing. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L193)
- Halrac once saw Ulrien beat a Minotaur in arm-wrestling. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L193)
- Ulrien's enchanted greatsword can be swung all day without chipping the blade or hilt. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L184)
- Ulrien keeps a calm, dependable manner and never seems to get worried, and Erin thinks of him as being like a rock. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L7)
## Dealings with other adventurers
- Ulrien tells Yvlon that her gear alone could make her Gold-rank and suggests that the Guild may recertify the Horns of Hammerad. (Book 5, Ch4.10 L369-371)
- Ulrien nudges Typhenous with disapproval when the mage asks to buy Pisces's ring. (Book 5, Ch4.10 L375)
- Ulrien uses Mordhau, a technique not meant for repeated strikes, to break the stone floor in the dungeon trap room, taking breaks to massage his hands. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L183-185)
- Ulrien offers to discuss helping the Halfseekers deal with Garen after the dungeon, saying justice should be done for their fallen. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L315-316)
- Ulrien was the first to agree to help Erin set her trap, after she accused a Named Adventurer, though Revi threw a fit and Typhenous worried about their reputation. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L10-11)
- Ulrien looked Erin in the eye for a few moments before nodding and trusting her. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L11)

- {state} Ulrien is dead, headless, with his greatsword still embedded in Regrika's leg. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L3)
