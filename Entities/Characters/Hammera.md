# Hammera
> Type: person | Also called: Hammer of Elbe | First appears: Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems
> See also: Domehead, Nsiia, Femithain, Dellic
## Overview
- Hammera is the nickname of the Hammer of Elbe, the Golem that Crafter Inerta enters in the Testing for the state of Elbe. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1156-1157,L1166)
- Hammera is a titanic Dwarfsteel Golem armed with a huge maul with a spike. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1169)
- Hammera is a female Golem with a face shaped like a statuesque Garuda in armour, and a breastplate that Crafters gave her. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1169-1170)
- Hammera can wipe out a lesser Golem in a single blow. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1171)
- Hammera has a victory routine in which she raises her hammer and struts around the stadium. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1178)
- The winner of each year's Testing keeps their Golem, so Hammera carries two years of innovations. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1164)
## The Testing
- Hammera destroys the upper torso of the spitting-Golem from Unst with one blow of her maul. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1171-1174)
- Crafters say Hammera is faster than last year because of magicore, which gives her a moment of acceleration based on stored mana. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1175-1176)
- Nsiia judges that the two best Golems are Domehead and Hammera, and that they will meet. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1214)
- Hammera takes a crushing blow across her shoulder from a careless swing, which is repaired by the next bout but remains a weak point. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1215)
- Domehead's axe hits Hammera's maul along the haft in their clash, and Hammera stumbles. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1223-1225)
- Domehead buries his axe in Hammera's chest, which is her weak point, but Hammera rises and the axe head falls out of her chest. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1226-1241)
- Hammera corners Domehead after his axe breaks, while the referee, who is from Elbe, refuses to stop the bout. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1243-1244)
- Hammera opens Domehead's metal cavity and destroys many of his magical bindings, though his Golem's Heart remains. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1255)
- Domehead catches Hammera's hand and punches her in the face, copying moves Nsiia made against Dellic. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1256-1262)
- Hammera's face is reduced to a mangled bit of steel, but she keeps attacking, leaking magicore from her head. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1269-1270)
- Hammera is not designed to aim for Domehead's head, where his secondary routines are. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1272)
- {state} Hammera collapses with her chest dented inwards, and Domehead pounds her against a wall until someone calls the deactivation command. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L1301)
