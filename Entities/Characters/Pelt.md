# Pelt
> Type: person | Also called: Pelt of Deríthal-Vel; the mad Dwarf | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.09
> See also: Maughin, Erin Solstice, Emessa, Hedault, Grimalkin, Dawil
## Overview
- Pelt is a short, bearded Dwarf [Smith] who works a forge beside Maughin's in Pallass. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L647-650)
- Pelt is a master blacksmith who can work mithril and other metals beyond steel. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L463; Book 18, Ch7.31 L367)
- Pelt is a Dwarf from Deríthal-Vel, which he calls Dwarfhome, and he travelled far from it. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L293; Book 18, Ch7.31 L366)
- Pelt is a miserable, grumpy drunk who stumbles into his forge late in the morning, badly hung over. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L735; Book 15, Ch7.01 L340)
- Pelt rests in a sling chair of crossed legs and stretched canvas that his apprentice relocates with every move. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L400)
- Pelt has given nearly a century to his craft and says it took him eighty years to reach his level. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L970,L973)
- Pelt is strong enough to curl a two-hundred pound dumbbell effortlessly and out-lift Relc. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L323,L339)
- Pelt is as strong as Maughin despite his size. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L750)
## Pallass and the competition (Book 10)
- Pelt finishes a breastplate in a morning and leaves the engraving and inlays to apprentices, calling them adornment. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L647-657)
- Pelt tells Lasica he does not care who is voted into the seats, so long as the walls do not come crashing down. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L659)
- Pelt inspects each steel bar before heating it, and refuses to use Pallass' steel, forging his own with his apprentice. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L733-735)
- Pelt forges a knife in under an hour with an iron-and-steel sandwich, an edge of steel between softer iron, quenched with clay. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L817-827,L848-853)
- Pelt finds a hairline crack near the edge on grinding the knife, calls it a flaw in his metal, and flees. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L862-865,L875-876)
## Erin's blue fire (Book 15)
- Pelt makes a basket-hilt blade from a mithril alloy he forged while drunk, using three-layer forge-welding. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L459-468,L500)
- Pelt realises the alloy holds truegold and a ghost slayer, and says it will be a fancy magic mithril blade. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L476)
- Pelt wonders whether the alloy holds Dwarfsteel or wolfram, and Emessa notes Dwarfsteel is an alloy that Pelt refuses to talk about. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L470-473)
- Pelt calls the finished blade done and tells Emessa to triple the price. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L514-518)
- Pelt tells Erin he felt her magical fire in his forge all day, and that it would ruin truegold forging. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L689,L697)
- {event} Pelt grabs Erin's arm and crushes it, saying the kitchen knife he made in the competition is flawed and he has to erase it. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L718-742)
- Maughin, Emessa and other smiths pull Pelt off, and Erin drinks a healing potion. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L744-753)
- {belief} Pelt calls the flaw in the knife his sin and says it must be broken. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L726,L795)
- Pelt forges a bardiche head from Grasgil, a metal that melts at room temperature, using Everfrozen Ice powder and Erin's cold blue flame. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L880-891,L925)
- Pelt says Grasgil gives one chance and falls apart if reheated, and the cold from the flames makes his forge freeze. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L886,L896,L937)
- Pelt says Maughin's people invented the way to smith Grasgil, and that levels do not define a smith. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L966-968)
- {reveal} Pelt says he once led a team of Dwarven smiths, and left his home in disgrace over impure steel, never to go back. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L973-979)
- Pelt thanks Erin for her art, and asks her to take the flame away. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L983,L989)
- Pelt knows Dawil of the Silver Swords, but tells Drassi he does not care about another Dwarf. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L358-366)
- Pelt gives Mrsha his complimentary peanuts to make her go away. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L370-373)
## The coin and leaving Pallass (Books 17-18)
- Pelt has hired more apprentices, including the Drake Emessa, so that they do the hammering while he finishes. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L290,L301-303)
- Pelt runs a forge with a glowing circle of powdered gemstone for Dwarfsteel and higher metals. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L286-289)
- Pelt sold the Grasgil axe, which was then enchanted into a low Gold-rank weapon. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L306)
- Pelt says he was banished from home, not forbidden, and is working on a euphonium for Wistram. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L348-354)
- Pelt examines Erin's white mithril coin and says it is monetary and very old. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L369-377)
- {belief} Pelt guesses the coin is so old that Humans lived on Izril in few numbers when it was made. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L377)
- Pelt says mithril should not wear like that, and seems to recognise the coin before keeping it to restore. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L383-395)
- {event} Erin finds Pelt choking on the floor behind the anvil, having restored the coin. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1060-1070)
- The coin's restored inscription reads that Tamaroth leads, and ends 'Protect Us in Our Weakness', with a word scratched out. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1071,L1079)
- Pelt starts breathing once Erin removes the coin, and says it was just an accident at the forge. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1083-1086)
- Pelt says he remembers nothing of the coin, and chokes again when he thinks of it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1090-1093)
- {state} Pelt announces he is leaving Pallass, and Senator Errif offers triple pay and five years. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L239,L266)
- Pelt says he left because of the Golden Triangle protests, and not because of Erin. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L239,L255)
- Pelt tells Maughin he was only the second greatest smith of those who left Deríthal-Vel, and to surpass him. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L233)
- Pelt takes three apprentices, including Emessa, with him. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L234)
- Pelt tricks everyone by changing the door's dial so that it opens on Esthelm. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L303-321)
- Pelt forges a one-edged sword in about an hour in Esthelm, with a Dragonblood Crystal and Truegold edge. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L432-452)
- Pelt gives the blade to Erin for Numbtongue, saying he does not forget his debts. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L457-461)
- Pelt arrives six minutes late and drinks wine, giving Erin until the wine runs out to pitch. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L625-644)
- Pelt says the Grasgil ring he sent Hedault had a slight impurity, and he would have trashed it. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L635-637)
