# Kirana
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.05D
> See also: Paige, Daly, Siri, Priya
## Overview
- Kirana is the [Cook] in charge of feeding the Bushrangers' company, and the second manager of the home front after Paige. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L98)
- Kirana has pretty much taken over household affairs, so Paige can work on more designs. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L98)
- Kirana speaks sharply to a younger girl in Hindi, one of the languages of her nation. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L26)
- Kirana and several of the girls from India joined the company about two months before that scene, and improved the living situation. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L98)
- Kirana's group makes mint toothpaste that is better than the last batch. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L56-58)
## In the company
- {belief} Paige says Kirana is good but mostly bosses around the girls from her country, and suspects she has something on them. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L491)
- Kirana helps hold the Centaur Hastel down during her birth, and is pale as ice afterwards. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1059)
- Kirana serves dum aloo with rice and a steak dinner, with muffins Priya has baked. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L297)
- Kirana turns the meat of giant snakes into something tasty. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L310)
