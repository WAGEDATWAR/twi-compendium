# Montressa
> Type: person | Also called: Montressa du Valeross; Mons | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5
> See also: Pisces, Ceria Springwalker, Calvaron, Beatrice, Cognita, Palt, Beza, Isceil, Ulinde, Erin Solstice, Hedault
## Overview
- Montressa du Valeross is a red-haired Human first-year student at Wistram when she joins Ceria's table. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L38,L44)
- Montressa's parents are of the lesser Terandrian nobility and made a gift to Wistram for her enrollment. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L67)
- Montressa has pale saffron and topaz eyes and red hair. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L53)
- Montressa is an [Aegiscaster], which means she specializes in barriers. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L237)
- Montressa carries a marble-like staff with a hoop head holding a red gemstone and a copper orb circling it that fires [Lightning Bolt]. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L482-485)
- Montressa is a secret-broker partnered with Beatrice, fairly high in the Revivalist faction. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L107)
