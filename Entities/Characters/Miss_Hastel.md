# Miss Hastel
> Type: person | Also called: Hastel; Hasty | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.05D
> See also: Geneva, Daly, Siri, Rital
## Overview
- Miss Hastel is a Centaur [Landlady] in Baleros who owns several properties on the street where the Bushrangers live, and keeps the loveliest home. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L163)
- Miss Hastel has tan fur with dark black spots around her flanks, dark skin on her upper half and dyed blonde hair. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L164)
- Miss Hastel's apartment is made of stone and is magically cool inside. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L163,L167)
- Miss Hastel is heavily pregnant with her first child and hopes to welcome visitors after the birth. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L165,L215-217)
- Miss Hastel keeps a Centaur [Midwife], whom she calls the best in the city, on call. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L215-217)
## The birth
- {event} The foal comes out the wrong way with its legs tangled in the birthing cord, and the [Midwives] cannot deliver it. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1022-1026)
- The [Midwives] push the foal back against the contractions, and Geneva makes up a transfusion from the Centaurs outside and cuts Miss Hastel open to extract it. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1043-1051)
- Miss Hastel stays awake through the operation, since she could not be put to sleep. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1045)
- Geneva uses healing potion sparingly, by dropper, while stitching Miss Hastel up. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1046,L1054)
- {state} The foal cries and the Centauress survives, so mother and child live. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1056-1060)
- The [Midwife] Caleffe Umbalt thanks Geneva, who tells her this was a Caesarian section, which only a [Doctor] can do. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L1064-1073)
