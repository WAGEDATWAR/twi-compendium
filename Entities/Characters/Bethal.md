# Bethal
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Bethal Walchaís; Lady Bethal Walchais; Lady Walchaís; Lady of Thorns; Bethal Aura | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.06KM
> See also: Thomast, Magnolia Reinhart, Laken, Ryoka, Maviola, Rags, Sir Kerrig, Welca Caveis, Tyrion Veltras
## Overview
- Lady Bethal Walchaís is a noblewoman of Izril who leads her own order of knights, the Knights of the Petal, whom she sometimes calls her Rose Knights. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L127)
- Bethal is married to Sir Thomast, a [Chevalier], who stays largely silent beside her. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L328; Book 6, Ch4.39G L127)
- Magnolia Reinhart's letter to Laken calls Bethal a close friend of hers. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L379)
- Lady Rie describes Bethal as commanding a knighthood whose Knights of the Petal are elite and quite dangerous. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L296)
- Bethal is the last of her line, as Sir Kerrig says in Reizmelt. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L97)
- Bethal is tall and thin with short hair, younger than Magnolia, with a spirited, loud and captivating voice. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L588)
- Bethal wears a slashed yellow and red riding dress when she arrives in Reizmelt on holiday. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L588,L592)
- Bethal is famous for her temper, while her husband is known for his silence and lack of temper. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L253)
- Bethal is called the [Lady of Thorns] for her [Aura of Thorns]. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L959)
## Abilities
- Bethal's aura turns spiky, like invisible edged blades, as people around her move away when she is angry. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L317)
- Bethal casts [Tranquil Skies], which parts the clouds and the falling bolts and arrows in a circle of tranquility around her and her escort. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L188-189)
- Bethal's Skill makes the air around her barbed, so anyone touching her against her will cuts themselves on something as sharp as steel. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L110)
- Bethal's [Aura of Thorns] is powerful but cannot stop a crossbow bolt at close range. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L6)
- Bethal wears a Ring of Sufferance on one finger. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L7)
- Bethal's thorny aura turns real when she hisses in fury, cutting Ryoka's legs and Todel's arm until she pulls it back. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L45-48)
- Bethal's [Aura of Thorns] only works if someone strikes at her, and it penetrates flesh and blood but not steel. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L228)
- Laken senses Bethal's aura as a rose or bush covered in thorns that would sting him if he walked into it. (Book 7, Ch5.10E L565)
- {belief} Cimeca says Bethal has a Skill that makes anyone who raises a hand against her suffer, which she learned during the Second Antinium War. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L62)
- {belief} Rumor says Lord Elmha slapped Bethal at a ball six years earlier, tore half the flesh off his hand, and was later cut in the shoulder by Thomast in a duel. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L62)
- Bethal quickly works out the value of Lupp's corn field in her head as about four gold, seven silver and five copper. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L321-323)
- Bethal says that with the right Skills a business venture is hard to fail at, and harder with a husband like Thomast who is good with numbers. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L348)
## Past and the Sacrifice of Roses
- {event} Bethal fought in the Second Antinium War and stood against Velan the Kind during the Sacrifice of Roses, where the Walchaís line ended in a single hour. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L654-658)
- Bethal says the Walchaís line walked through arrows and flames with pride and never stepped back, and First Landing stood at dawn. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L658-660)
- Bethal remembers losing her mother, Maisy, Lady Alvita, Eruvie and her father at First Landing as the Goblin King's charges broke the banners. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L276)
- Bethal remembers hiding among the dead when the Goblin King's hordes attacked First Landing. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L70)
- Bethal thinks that Goblins killed her father, her mother and her sisters. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L148)
- Bethal says she saw the Goblin King and met one of his Lords, and that Velan the Kind knew no mercy. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L202)
- Bethal insists the Goblin Lords all died out after their King fell or led their armies into oblivion. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L429)
- Sir Kerrig has known Bethal since she was a child and knows her passion and noble gestures are only half of her. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L20)
- Sir Kerrig says Bethal can be caring and compassionate or as uncaring as the sun, and is pragmatic. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L20,L24)
