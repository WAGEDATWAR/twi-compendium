# Beatica
> Type: person | Also called: Councilwoman Beatica | First appears: Ch6.36E
> See also: Elmmet, Rie, Prost, Rodivek
## Overview
- Beatica is a [Councilwoman] from Lancrel in her early forties, well dressed, and wearing one large, expensive ring. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L261)
- Beatica was a [Councilwoman] for over two decades and did not give up her class when she left her city. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L249,L267)
- Beatica has a charismatic presence like a torch, which makes the room turn to listen to her. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L263)
- Beatica uses a Skill that silences Rie mid-sentence, and Rie asks her not to use Skills on her. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L268-269)
## Lancrel's claim to authority
- Beatica tells Rie that Lancrel's officials deserve a chance to use their classes, and proposes that she and Mayor Rodivek take over tasks. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L259,L264)
- Beatica says she considers herself in charge of her people though they have lost their home. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L267)
- Rodivek, a former [Mayor] of Hewlat, argues alongside Beatica that Lancrel's people have no say in how Riverfarm is developed. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L249,L264)
## Elmmet's trial
- Beatica objects to Prost on behalf of her Councilman, asking whether the truth stone is valid and whether Elmmet was wrongly accused. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L150-152)
- Beatica argues that a [Thief]'s [Master of Faces] Skill could have been used to impersonate Elmmet. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L154)
- Beatica's [Uninterrupted Monologue] Skill silences Prost while she argues. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L160)
- Beatica stumbles back, looking wounded, when Helm shouts at her and the crowd erupts. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L165-167)
## Later
- Later, Beatica is a [Councilwoman] from Lancrel who jumped into Rie's camp after the drama in the spring. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L60)
- Rie calls Beatica a reliable snake who is better managed than left wandering, and Laken distrusts her duplicity. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L61-62)
- Rie tells Beatica to excavate only twenty feet down, and Beatica hurries to do so. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L58-60)
