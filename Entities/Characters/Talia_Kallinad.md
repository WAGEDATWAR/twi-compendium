# Talia Kallinad
> Type: person | Also called: Dame Talia; Lady Talia Kallinad; Tal; Summer Knight of the Order of Seasons | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.42E
> See also: Calirn, Ser Raim, Rabbiteater, Wil Kallinad, Belavierr, Order of Seasons
## Overview
- Talia Kallinad is formally Lady-Dame Talia Kallinad, Knight of the Summer, and is called Dame Talia or Talia among friends. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L422)
- Talia is a [Summer Knight], twenty-eight years old, and a full member of her Order. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L437)
- Talia is the elder sister of Wil Kallinad, the [Strategist] and pride of House Kallinad, and is potentially heir to the Kallinad family if she marries. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L422; Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L6)
- Talia comes from a coastal duchy, and she is used to seafood rather than pickled food. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L235)
- Talia has her family's colors, gold and orange with hints of white, which she has personalized on her armor. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L437)
- Talia answers to her Grandmaster, the Summer's Champion, but senior members of other Seasons can also command her at need. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L465)
## Abilities and Levels
- Talia has the [Lady] class and a few Skills gained before she became a [Squire]. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L678,L681)
- Talia's Skill [Mark of Worth] tells her when a person is worth attention, helping her choose worthy retainers though not necessarily trustworthy ones. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L681-684)
- Talia says she saw such worthy people in Riverfarm. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L684)
- Talia's [Aura Blade] heats plain steel from red through orange and yellow to white-hot, and the blade burns flesh and sets weapons aflame. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L700-701,L705-706)
- Talia lacks the ability to become a burning fireball like her season's senior Knights, but her sword is as deadly as many enchanted weapons. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L706)
- Calirn tells Talia she is over twenty levels below Ser Raim. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L582)
- Talia has a nearly healed scar on her left leg from an enchanted arrow that went straight through her plain steel armor. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L16)
## Book 12: The Belavierr Expedition
- Talia returns from Baleros after the Titan's game at Daquin and is brought home by mocking [Sailors] who tease her with her title. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L422-423)
- Talia's younger brother Wil calls her Tal before she leaves Baleros. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L422)
- Talia is congratulated by Calirn on the game, and he says the Fall's Sentinel and he himself have reservations about its outcome. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L510)
- Talia is at the stronghold when Belavierr is identified, and she asks Calirn whether it is really the Spider, the enemy of the Order. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L496-499)
- Talia takes part in the ancient magical ritual that sends her across the world to fight for Ser Raim. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L499)
- Talia cries as Raim, before his last charge, touches her shoulder and orders her to return to the Order if he falls. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L1066-1067)
- Talia takes command of the surviving Spring and Summer Knights after each of her seniors has fallen or been too badly wounded to stand. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L93)
- Talia has auburn hair, and her orders are clear: she walks past Belavierr and bows to Riverfarm's people. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L101)
- A wounded senior Knight charges Talia with escorting him to a [Healer] and with returning the younger Knights home. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L94)
- Talia promises that the Order will not forget Raim's sacrifice, and she thanks Durene and Sir Beniar for fighting beside the Knights. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L100,L103)
- Talia carries Ser Raim's ashes and greatsword away without speaking to Ryoka, with her hands clenched so hard that Ryoka thinks the gauntlets bleed. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L103-104)
- Talia leads the survivors, Knights of Spring and Summer, more than half of whom are younger than Ryoka, and the Order's dead. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L90-93)
## Book 14: Izril and the Goblin Slayer
- Talia travels across Izril's dirt roads with her company, tormented by the failure to kill Belavierr. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L15-17)
- Talia argues that Selphids should be allowed into the Order, and cites three Selphid members in its history, with Chalica of Spring holding the gates until the enemy dead piled up. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L80)
- Ser Lorell answers that one in three Selphids proved false, and cites the ruin of the Crowlende Order as a warning against crowding the ranks. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L81-85)
- Talia and the Order of Seasons fight Ogres and Goblins in a dark clearing, and Talia's blade scorches the enemy. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L699-705)
- {event} Talia promises Ser Solstice she will be his ally and stand with him so long as his cause is just and his heart unwavering. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L1133)
- Ser Solstice says that someday he will ask if she remembers the vow, and Talia answers that she will never break it. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L1136-1137)
## Books 18-19: War with Ailendamus
- Talia respects Ser Solstice, who has sworn never to reveal his face, and she says her failure at Riverfarm still weighs on her. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L499-500)
- Talia thinks of her many levels and fast growth as hollow after Raim's death. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L500)
- Talia tells Ser Solstice that war is brewing with Ailendamus and that she believes the Grandmaster will order some Knights to war. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L527-532)
- {event} Talia and Rabbiteater are aboard a transport ship when Ailendamus ships attack with arrows, and a Knight of Spring shouts that Ailendamus has crossed Kaliv's border. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L121-131)
- Talia is startled that war has come a month before Grandmaster Calirn predicted. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L133)
