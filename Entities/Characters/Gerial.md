# Gerial
> Type: person | Also called: vice-captain of the Horns of Hammerad | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.00H
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Calruz, Skinner, Sostrom, Hunt
## Overview
- Gerial is the vice-captain of the Horns of Hammerad, a Silver-rank adventuring group. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L123; Book 1, Ch1.36 L356)
- Gerial has the skill [Dangersense], which went off when a Goblin pulled out a jar of acid. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L150)

- Gerial sits with Calruz before the expedition, discussing the plan. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L4)
- Gerial winces when asked about the sixth captain, and tells that Hendric pulled out at the last moment. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L33-35)
- Gerial stares at Erin in confusion after the adventurers attack the inn at night. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L356)
- Gerial grabs for his belt dagger on seeing the skeleton in the inn, until Ceria stops him. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L122-124)
