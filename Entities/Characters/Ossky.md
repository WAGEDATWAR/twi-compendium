# Ossky
> Type: person | Also called: Lieutenant Ossky | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Cirille, Shellc, Ser Vorn, Oldblood Drakes
## Overview
- Ossky is a Lieutenant and an Oldblood Drake with wings under Commander Cirille. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L805-809)
- Ossky is a rare descendant of Dragons who breathes light, in contrast to Oldbloods who breathe fire, lightning, acid or darkness. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L809-811)
- Ossky is useful for blinding the enemy, so long as she does not turn her head and cough. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L811)
- Ossky flashes light when she speaks, and her tells show light from her nasal cavities or ears when she gets excited. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L807; Book 18, Ch7.35C L246)
- Ossky is yellow-scaled. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L805)
## In the defense
- Cirille tells Ossky to keep her mouth shut at night, since she would otherwise snore with light, and Ossky apologizes. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L806-808)
- Ossky is Cirille's only flight group, and her wing keeps up aerial pressure as the only fliers. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L305,L579)
- Cirille describes Ossky as an expert at Oldblood formations, where one breathes and fights. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L679)
- Ossky commands the [Archers] and blinds the Demons by flying up and breathing light. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L536,L555)
- Cirille calls for a covering flash from Ossky, and the Demons are blinded while the Gnolls fall back. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L560-561)
- Ossky nearly has her scales frozen when Chief Warrior Merish exhales a plume of frost. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L632)
