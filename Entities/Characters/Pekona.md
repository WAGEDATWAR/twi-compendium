# Pekona
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.08T
> See also: Insill, Dasha, Anith, Toren
## Overview
- Pekona is a young Human woman and a [Blade Dancer] in the party Vuliel Drae, carrying a wide, double-edged curved sword. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L215,L260)
- Pekona says [Blade Dancer] is a class from where she is from, with unique footwork and sword techniques, not copyable by just copying the moves. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L350)
- Pekona's people live on the Drath Archipelago and speak a language different from the rest of the world. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L334)
- Pekona is a silent woman, as her command of the common tongue is weaker than her teammates'. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L334)
