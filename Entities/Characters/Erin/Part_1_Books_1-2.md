## Overview
- Erin Solstice is a young woman from Michigan who arrives in the new world with nothing and becomes an [Innkeeper] by cleaning an abandoned inn. (Book 1, Ch1.01 L62-82,L86-89; Book 1, Ch1.07 L93-105)
- Erin is introduced as 20 years old, from Michigan, with a casual interest in video games, a deep obsession with strategy games (chess, shogi, go), hobbies of snowboarding and watching Youtube, and a dream of becoming a professional strategy game commentator. (Book 1, Ch1.02 L177)
- Erin has two first names that could each belong to a girl or a boy, and she thinks Erin sounds more like a boy's name. (Book 1, Ch1.01 L4-5)
- Erin tells Pisces that she cannot drink alcohol because she is underage, being only 20. (Book 1, Ch1.28 L80)
- Ryoka sees Erin as a girl close to her own age, shorter than Ryoka, with fair skin, hazel eyes and only the slightest orange tint to her light brown hair. (Book 2, Ch2.01 L41)
- Erin is 20 years old, and at Lady Magnolia's house the other girls all seem younger than she is. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L194)
