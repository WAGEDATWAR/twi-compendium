# Gazi
> Type: person | Also called: Gazi Pathseeker; Gazi the Omniscient; Lady Pathseeker; Gazi of Everst; Gazi of Reim | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.11R
> See also: Erin Solstice, Ceria Springwalker, Flos Reimarch, Trey Atwood, Orthenon, Pisces, Krshia Silverfang
## Overview
- Gazi is a half-Gazer Named Adventurer, called Gazi the Omniscient, who appears in Liscor in Book 1. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L70-76,L93)
- Gazi serves a sleeping lord and King, later shown to be Flos, the King of Destruction, as one of his Seven. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L343; Book 5, Ch4.03K L600)
- Gazi is a [Scout] of Flos' Seven whose central eye has long been blinded, and she says Erin Solstice injured it. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L324; Book 5, Ch4.06KM L166)
## Appearance and nature
- Gazi is a female adventurer with dreadlocks, dark orange-brown armor that only looks rusty from afar, and a large sword at her side, who has learned to see even in sandstorms. (Book 1, Ch1.29 L192-194)
- Gazi calls herself part Gazer by ancestry: she has one huge central eye with a yellow pupil, four smaller eyes around it, a mouth of sharp teeth, no nose, reddish-brown soft skin, extra-segmented arms and only four fingers. (Book 1, Ch1.29 L315-319; Book 1, Ch1.33 L305)
- Gazi gives Erin Solstice the name Gazi, the name others not of her race use, and says she is an adventurer wandering through these lands. (Book 1, Ch1.29 L315-320)
- Selys Shivertail recognises Gazi as a half-Gazer, a rare species made rarer by mixed blood, and her scales pale nearly to gray. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L70-71)
- Gazi's main eye can see through objects at close range, and shows her a person's name and nature as well as class and level. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L348)
## Abilities
- Gazi tells Erin she sees that Erin is an [Innkeeper], Level 11, simply by seeing it. (Book 1, Ch1.29 L245-247)
- Gazi's eye can cast [Charm] by gaze; Ceria says it hits anyone who meets her gaze, and that Gazers cast spells as easily as looking, a half-Gazer included. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L307-309)
- Gazi's eye makes the yellow pupil widen and glow, so that she becomes terrifying without looking at the person. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L305)
## Classes and levels
- {reveal} Asked by the Quarass for her level and class, Gazi states she is a Level 45 [War Scout], Level 12 [Mage] and Level 18 [Silent Commander]. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L408-410)
