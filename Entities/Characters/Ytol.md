# Ytol
> Type: person | Also called: Death Commander Ytol | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.10K
> See also: Flos, Teres, Mars
## Overview
- Ytol is a [Death Commander] of the Rustängmarder, the commander of the company sent to join Flos. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L487,L491)
- The Rustängmarder have a [Commander] for each company who always has levels in [Necromancer]. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L479)
- Ytol's right arm, right eye and right leg are gone, and he leans on a crutch. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L487-488)
- {state} Ytol lost his limbs the month before, when his company marched to join the King of Destruction. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L488)
- Ytol says that half is always the price for desertion and that he was grateful it was he who bore his company's sins, or they could not fight for Flos. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L489)
- Ytol says the Rustängmarder were pained to honor one vow and forsake the other, so he above others was chosen. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L493)
- Ytol says he would give up his other half to honor the oath, and that the whole company will come when their contract ends. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L493)
- Ytol smiles when he looks at the King of Destruction, though his eyes are otherwise pitiless and empty. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L492)
## Later
- Ytol rides toward Teres to check on her, balancing despite his lack of limbs. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L6)
- {event} Ytol goes still at the sight of the Minotaurs, like Mars, and asks Flos whether to advance or fall back. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L50-51)
- Teres has never heard one of Flos's officers suggest a retreat before. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L51)
- Ytol tells Flos the army did not prepare for a battle with Minotaurs. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L55)
- {event} Flos refuses the retreat, orders Ytol to advance and spread out, and the blank-faced Ytol bows. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L59-60)
