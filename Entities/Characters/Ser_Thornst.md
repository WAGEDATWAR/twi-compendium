# Ser Thornst
> Type: person | Also called: Thornst | First appears: Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia
> See also: Talia Kallinad, Goblin Slayer, Order of Seasons
## Overview
- Ser Thornst is a veteran [Spring Knight] who rides in Dame Talia's company hunting Ogres and Goblins. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L71,L456)
## Role in the Ogre hunt
- Ser Thornst reminds the junior Ser Eldein that diplomacy is the other edge of a Knight's sword and that a Dragon should be addressed with words first. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L264-265)
- A senior knight orders Dame Talia and Ser Thornst to choose four riders each and scout ahead of the company. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L456)
- Ser Thornst agrees with Ser Eldein that their steeds cannot manage the rough, rooted foothills and that the knights must continue on foot. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L458-460)
- Talia asks Ser Thornst to lead the horses back, saying he has a way with them, and he agrees. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L462-464)
- Ser Thornst arrives at the scene after Talia meets the Goblin Slayer and stops on seeing him. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L494,L498)
- Ser Thornst nods in agreement as a knight remarks on the quality of the Goblin Slayer's light, well-fitted armour. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L516-517)
- Ser Thornst frowns and questions the Goblin Slayer's count of fifteen female and ten male dead Goblins. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L543-545)
- During the night fight with the Ogres, Ser Thornst is among the senior knights shouting Skills and concentrating their auras. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L702)
- Ser Thornst eyes a blade and asks the Goblin Slayer why he did not use it before. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L845-846)
- Ser Thornst pretends to study a blade while glancing sidelong at the Goblin Slayer, then sobers after the Goblin Slayer's remarks on enchanted weapons. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L938,L945,L948)
