# Archmage Nailihuaile
> Type: person | Also called: Naili; Nailihuaile; Star Lamia Archmage | First appears: Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage
> See also: Feor, Viltach, Teura, Cognita, Amerys, Aaron Vanwell, Montressa
## Overview
- Archmage Nailihuaile, called Naili, is a slim, smiling Lamia with light white and rose scales and glowing patterns of blue, purple and yellow on her arms and back. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L324)
- Naili says she is a Star Lamia, and her glowing scales store magic, which helps spellcasting but makes sneaking difficult without illusion spells. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L362)
- Naili wields the Serkonian Lance, a treasure of the Lizardfolk. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L334; Book 14, Ch6.60 L168)
- The Serkonian Lance creates a field in which Naili can link spells, change the nature of mana, and fire lances of magic. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L168)
- Naili specializes in enchantments, best at making scales harder than mithril and casting mass-[Invisibility], and claims to be the best in the world. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L373)
- {belief} Naili says she is one of five Archmages in the world, six if Amerys is counted. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L375)
- Naili is the most relaxed of the four Archmages in Wistram, lighthearted and joking, unlike the older, stately Feor and Viltach. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L172)
- Naili is the Archmage of the Revivalist faction. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L98)
- Naili says she learned in Baleros to prefer quick spells, and dislikes Feor's habit of casting only big spells. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L373)
- Naili tells Feor she grew up a little Lizardgirl in a swamp village and earned money trapping young Hydras, which is how she became a Lamia. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L671,L676)
## Alliance with the Earthers (Books 8-15)
- {rel} Naili agrees to protect the Earth people in exchange for access to the Evermote Study. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L380)
- Naili says the Revivalists have the numbers to resist any faction, including Feor and the Centrists. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L380)
- {belief} Feor suspects Naili knows something he does not, and that someone besides Naili helped Blackmage. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L357,L398)
- Naili helped Aaron make an artifact, a sealed battery or orb, which impressed her. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L163,L165)
- {event} Naili hides under Aaron's bed with the Serkonian Lance, telling him she is not there if someone knocks. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L166-171)
- {event} Naili shouts at Cognita through the door that she is having sex, and Cognita punches a hole through the door. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L175,L184-185)
- Naili tells Cognita that Pisces committed more crimes after leaving Wistram, so Wistram has the right to apprehend him. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L214)
- {event} Cognita asks Naili to correct the record naming Pisces and Ceria as Wistram graduates, and Naili relents after a tense moment. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L216-230)
- When Cognita says she would insist, Naili coils cold and dangerous before laughing that she was kidding. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L223-228)
- Cognita tells Naili she is only the academy's protector and test, while the [Mages] control its will. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L232)
- {event} Naili visits Amerys with Feor and Viltach and says the Earthers have helped her make a shock orb nearly as good as a Level 30 [Lightning Mage]. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L294,L310)
- Naili answers that Cognita does not rule Wistram "Not yet", when Amerys asks. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L308-310)
- Naili tries to keep the Earthers under her control and cooperative, and thinks she could dispel Aaron's magic. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L101)
