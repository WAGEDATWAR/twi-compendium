# Cynthia
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 2, Ch1.00C
> See also: Tom, Richard, Chole, Emily, Keith
## Overview
- Cynthia is one of a group of Earthers, including Tom, Vincent and Chole, who were caught by a spell and live together in a village. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L153,L162,L198)
- Cynthia lost her [Hero] class a few days after the group reached the village, and was the first to hear a voice say so. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L198)
- Cynthia is afraid of clowns, and was afraid of them as a child, according to Tom. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L149)
## Fear and distress in Book 2 and 6
- Cynthia screams at Tom, with a red face and shaking finger, when the clown music starts playing around the group. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L144-153)
- Chole pulls Cynthia away from Tom, and Cynthia goes crying that she wants to go home. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L153-154)
- Cynthia tells the group that someone is going to come save them any day now, rocking back and forth, and keeps insisting they will be rescued. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L179-181)
- The last time Cynthia freaked, she broke half the plates and cups the villagers gave the group, and the only glass window in the village. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L185)
- Cynthia points with dismay at a young girl begging with a bowl at the edge of a plaza. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L105)
- Tom expects Cynthia to demand more coins for every child beggar they see. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L122)
- Cynthia runs backwards in a city with her face white after seeing a caged Demon prisoner, and Richard tells her not to scream. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L218-228)
- Cynthia is afraid to enter the room where the King will speak with the group. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L328)
- Cynthia will not be in the same room as Tom after the village. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L372)
- Cynthia made Tom a clumsily stitched bright piece of clothing, before she was too scared to sleep near him. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L169)
- Cynthia lets out a mad, hysterical cackle in front of the King that cuts off quickly. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L37)
- Cynthia screams when Tom enters the banquet hall dressed as a [Clown], and sobs afterwards while Richard explains to the guards. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L205-227)
## Book 18
- Cynthia stays out in the town enjoying the sights and keeping as far from Tom as possible, and has not returned in two days. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L333)
- Cynthia does not contribute much to the group, and distracts herself until help arrives from Earth. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L335)
- A narrator counts Cynthia among those who do nothing, unlike the warriors and helpers such as Keith and Chole. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L652)
- Lord Hayvon says Cynthia, Keith and others who do not fight will have sanctuary in a separate place to work for the kingdom in safety. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L669)
