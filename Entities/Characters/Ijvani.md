# Ijvani
> Type: person | Also called: black skeleton mage; Imenet (disguise as a dark-purple Drake [Mage]) | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.22L
> See also: Az'kerash, Venitra, Oom, Bea, Kerash, Ryoka Griffin, Zel Shivertail, Toren, Pisces, Cognita
## Overview
- Ijvani is an undead skeleton [Mage] and one of Az'kerash's Chosen, with black iron bones carved with glowing runes. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L424-426; Book 13, Ch6.48T L437)
- Ijvani's golden eyes burn in her skull, and her magic is on par with any Gold-rank adventurer. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L424)
- Ijvani's bones shine black, and she weighs far more than she should since her body is a kind of armour. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L425-426)
- Ijvani says she and Oom are the eldest of Az'kerash's creations and that Venitra is the youngest. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L45)
- Venitra says she is Az'kerash's favourite, and that she chose Ijvani as her tracker. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L43,L46)
- Venitra calls Ijvani and Oom their best trackers. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L441)
## Hunting Ryoka (Books 3 to 5)
- {event} A woman of bone and a shadow, Venitra and Ijvani, walked through snow for three days, sent to find the Courier Ryoka. (Book 3, Ch3.22L L377-381)
- Ijvani asked Venitra if they were lost; the narration says they were roughly seventy miles off course. (Book 3, Ch3.22L L381-383)
- Venitra and Ijvani killed Goblins of the Goblin Lord's army who ambushed them, then spoke with the Goblin Lord, whose eyes reminded Venitra of her master's. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L564-567)
- They set off through the snow to Liscor to find Ryoka Griffin. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L578)
- Using a map from a corpse they had killed, they mistook Esthelm for Liscor after crossing a mountain. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L432-436)
- A dark-purple Drake [Mage] named Imenet walked beside Regrika Blackpaw, a Gold-rank adventurer. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L539-542)
- Ijvani cast an eavesdropping barrier, a truth spell and a paralysis on Ryoka. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L12,L15,L39)
- Ijvani put a listening spell on Ryoka, and Ivolethe warned that the spell cannot detect the fae. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L134-137)
- Ijvani carries a staff of bone with a golden gem, and an invisible barrier fractured Ryoka's foot. (Book 5, Ch4.29 L253-257)
- Az'kerash spoke through Ijvani's mouth and healed Ryoka's foot. (Book 5, Ch4.29 L257)
- {event} As Imenet, Ijvani used [Lesser Teleport] to take Zel and Ilvriss away after being hit by Halrac's arrow and Zel's claws. (Book 5, Ch4.29 L574-577)
- Imenet created illusions of himself, raised Golems from the flagstones and rained spells on Celum while Zel fought him. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L62,L91,L207-208)
- Zel found Imenet hard like armour when cut. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L250)
- {state} Zel's claws cut through Ijvani's ribs and Ilvriss cut off her leg, which pained her mentally. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L358)
## Abandoned in the earth (Book 13)
- {state} After Zel Shivertail's death, Ijvani was left to walk back alone because there were no more teleport scrolls. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L442-444)
- Ijvani sits in a pit in the ground, her robes puddled and filthy, for ninety-six days since Az'kerash last contacted her. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L433-436)
- {belief} Ijvani believes that Az'kerash has forgotten or replaced her, and fears she is like Viltraid. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L437,L461-463)
- Ijvani grabbed a healing slime that rolled out of a Shield Spider pit, and the Shield Spiders swarmed her. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L487-491)
- Az'kerash sensed that Ijvani was still absent months after Zel Shivertail's death and found her sitting in a cave. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L542-544)
- Az'kerash ordered her to stay put and set up a surveillance spell that Grimalkin of Pallass could not detect. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L548)
## Watching the inn (Books 14 to 16)
- Ijvani put the healing slime inside her ribcage, held by a [Forcewall] spell. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L976)
- Az'kerash ordered Ijvani to watch Pisces and Montressa, build a teleportation rune usable by two people near the Bloodfields, and prepare for combat. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L980-992)
- Ijvani held a [Bound Spell - Blackfire Fireball] in her jaw. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L995-999)
- Az'kerash let Ijvani keep the slime. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L467-484)
- Ijvani gathered Toren's bones on Az'kerash's order, and later silenced Toren's screaming soul with a smack. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L1160-1162,L1175-1177)
- Ijvani had found the healing slime with the Defenders of the Cave, Shield Spiders and Fortress Beavers. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L98)
- Az'kerash saw that the slime's body uses a minor healing potion as a base. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L214)
- Az'kerash told Ijvani that she and his other Chosen will be altered to give them the ability to level. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L435-436)
