# Merec
> Type: person | Also called: Mister Merec | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.37
> See also: Laken Godart, Durene, Riverfarm
## Overview
- Merec is a [Merchant] who receives Laken in the center of his guild. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L304)
- Merec has never heard of Riverfarm when Laken names it, though he heard of the avalanche there. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L313-319)
- Merec is a ruddy-faced man who turns pale quickly at Laken's revelations. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L417)
## Laken's order
- Laken tells Merec he wants food, supplies, livestock and tools for a village of around a hundred people to last the winter. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L311)
- Laken asks Merec for twelve wagons and guards, at least two adventuring teams, loaded within an hour. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L311,L320)
- Merec tries to retake command of the talk, then turns genial and professionally good-humored. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L322)
- Merec asks Laken to swear an oath on a truth spell, since he cannot be sure where the money came from. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L339)
- Merec makes a very small sound and shrinks back when Durene towers over him. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L343)
- Merec offers Laken a frothy fruit drink, with profuse apologies, after the truth is established. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L417)
- Merec offers Gold-rank teams, but Laken insists on hiring the Celestial Trackers and the Windfrozen Riders. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L418-419)
- Laken explains to Merec that those two teams did not laugh at his man begging for help. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L422)
