# Mavika
> Type: person | Also called: Witch Mavika; the Crow Witch; the Witch of Flocks | First appears: Ch6.34E
> See also: Belavierr, Wiskeria, Eloise, Hedag, Alevica, Califor, Anazurhe, Laken
## Overview
- Mavika is a strong [Witch] who commands crows and ravens, called a great witch by some and among the strongest of her era. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L561)
- Mavika can make her crows talk, carry messages or do things for her, which Wiskeria says is not like being a [Beast Tamer]. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L561)
- Mavika is one of the senior [Witches] of Izril, and Wiskeria says there is no formal ranking but she is among the most important. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L765)
- Mavika leads the Marshlands Coven, which Wiskeria says covers all the [Witches] in about three hundred miles. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L773)
- Mavika looks hunched and bird-like, in grey rags with a dark pointed hat, yellow teeth and bird's eyes overly large in her face. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L871-872,L879; Book 19, Ch7.55E L963)
- Mavika keeps a huge raven on her shoulder, and her raven familiar can turn into her. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L809,L923)
- Mavika speaks in rhymes and in a sibilant voice. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L873-876; Book 19, Ch7.55E L809)
## Pursuit of Wiskeria
- Mavika's crows call "Wiskeria! COME!" in a blended voice that is neither human nor animal, and attack when Wiskeria refuses. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L501-509)
- The crows attack Durene when she intervenes, until Frostwing kills two of them. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L511-516,L527)
- Wiskeria drives the flock off by cracking a wet cloth like a whip and stunning two crows, ordering Mavika to send her crows away. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L522-526)
## Arrival in Riverfarm
- Mavika burns Rehanna's hand with a spell cast on a doorknob, and perches on a roof to claim responsibility to Ram. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L871-873; Book 12, Ch6.40E L802)
- Mavika tells the people of Riverfarm that those who scorn a [Witch]'s gift must pay the toll, with discourtesy matched by discourtesy. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L876)
- Mavika bows to Wiskeria, touches her black hat, and tells her "I am the first". (Book 12, Ch6.36E L878-880)
- Prost fines Mavika two gold coins, which Alevica pays, and Rie gives Rehanna eight silver as compensation. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L802-805)
## The Circle
- Mavika says she was not present when Tillica summoned the coven, that the Circle committed dark deeds in her absence, and that she repaid blood with blood. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L346)
- Mavika took the Circle's offer from her sister's dying lips and the screams of fools before they died. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L346)
- In Califor's view, those who slaughtered the Marshlands Coven died painfully at Mavika's hands. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L347)
- Mavika says the Circle demanded a curse on Magnolia Reinhart and her kin that would boil her by her own skin. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L349)
- Belavierr recalls that she and Mavika last spoke when they ordained the Witch of Molten Stone as a [Witch] unquestioned. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L149-150)
## The crow bargain
- Mavika offers Riverfarm's crows' services for one week in return for a field of freshly grown corn a hundred paces square. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L713)
- Mavika promises her flock will eat no blade or seed, kill the rodents in the fields and devour the insects plaguing them. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L720)
- Mavika demands that no crow be harmed, or she claims the forfeit and three times as many fields. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L736)
- Mavika says her flock have the [Eyes of Many] and will find whatever hides. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L751)
- Mavika sets her raven Sephraic on a four-foot Colony Rat mother with [Ancient Form], and it kills the rat. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L757-763)
- Mavika uses [Primal Fear] to drive the rats out of their warrens into the waiting crows. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L765-766)
## Against Belavierr
- Mavika diagnoses that Belavierr is marked for death by flame, since a single spark now carries the dead [Knight]'s vengeance. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L435-441)
- {belief} Mavika says Belavierr could remake any ward but needs time she does not have. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L447)
- Mavika says each flame saps Belavierr's power and her flame wards will take weeks or perhaps months to mend. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L443,L458)
- Mavika agrees to fly the children and parents out of the fire with Eloise and Hedag, saying her flock will chase pursuers away. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L712,L720-724)
- Mavika tells Eloise she cannot shield them from the flame twister, and leaves when Eloise tells her to go. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L757-760)
- Belavierr catches Mavika's hand as the crow-witch begins to grow, and warns her that she has everything to lose. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L1132-1133)
- {belief} Mavika tells Laken that Belavierr took the life of a fellow [Witch] without cause, and that she is welcome among neither [Witches] nor men. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L184)
- Mavika carries the news to Anazurhe's Molten Stone tribe that Belavierr slew Califor unprovoked and should be given no aid. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L678-681)
## Dealings with Laken
- Mavika refuses gold to cure Gralton's lands but agrees to four gold coins' worth of food for her flock. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L227-231)
- Mavika knows Drake poisons and their venom, and the coven cures Gralton's poisoned water and dogs. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L503)
- Mavika tells the coven that Laken paid his promised price on Day 76 and urges caution. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L735-737)
- Later, Mavika calls her crows against the Humans who stoned Nanette, and says a Goblin defended a [Witch] while Humans threw stones. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1266-1270)
- Laken stops the crows, and Mavika storms into the village to remind the Unseen Empire of its pact. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1277-1278)
- Mavika refuses a seat on Laken's council, which Laken thinks is for the best. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L19)
- Mavika says protecting is not her craft, only killing, when Laken asks her to escort Eloise. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L880-882)
- Mavika asks whether Laken noticed the malice coming from his [Lady] sworn, Rie. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L937-938)
- Mavika says her crow familiar has flown to House Veltras, and that the other [Witch] who could cure any ill would die before reaching the manor. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L949,L957)
- Mavika agrees with Eloise to help, and tells Laken the pact is struck. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L964,L980)
