# Shorthilt
> Type: person | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.15
> See also: Headscratcher, Numbtongue, Badarrow, Rabbiteater, Az'kerash, Reiss, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Shorthilt is a Redfang Hobgoblin and one of the five Hobs who stay at Erin Solstice's inn. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L110; Book 8, Ch5.44 L34)
- Shorthilt is a weapon expert, and his iron sword was the only one that did not break against the Face-Eater Moths. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L110)
- Shorthilt has the Skill [Keener Edge], which lets his iron blade cut flawlessly for the first few blows. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L110)
- Shorthilt is quiet and loves weapons, studying them as poets study love. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L742)
## Levels and classes
- {state} Shorthilt reaches [Warrior Level 24!] and changes to the [Weapon Expert] class, gaining [Sharper Edge] and [Bleed Cuts]. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L589-592)
- Shorthilt and Badarrow shoot out of their bedrolls on hearing their level-ups and throw up their arms in joy. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L598)
- Shorthilt's unenchanted sword cuts as if by magic, aimed at the weak points in Goblin armor. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L23)
## At the inn and in the cave
- The five Redfang Hobs lead at least forty Cave Goblins and stand in a line holding off the Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.44 L28,L34,L49)
- Shorthilt is the Hob in front of a mini-tribe of Goblins who seek the sharpest, most effective weapons. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L17)
- Shorthilt's followers are both armorers and weapon smiths, using a small armory of weapons Shorthilt got from Erin. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L18)
- Shorthilt's Goblins shape edges even on wooden axes, with Shorthilt's help. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L18)
- Headscratcher tackles Shorthilt and pounds on him while the others laugh. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L321)
- Shorthilt and the other four Hobs tell Garen that his orders to kill Erin were bad orders. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L534-535)
- Shorthilt says that every night he sleeps and feels safe, and that Erin gave him his sword. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L629-630)
- Shorthilt is kicked and beaten by Garen when the five Redfangs challenge him. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L616,L639)
- Shorthilt winks at Erin and says the five were "sneaky Goblins" when they return to the hill with her flag. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L658-661)
## Fighting Reiss's army
- Shorthilt cuts through a Hob's club and beheads him in two swings as the Redfangs charge Reiss's Goblins. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L22-23)
- Numbtongue's guitar carries electricity, Badarrow's bell arrows cause pain, and Rabbiteater with his [Grand Slash] stands out as a [Champion]. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L24,L29-35)
- {event} Shorthilt steps forward to face Az'kerash, who is inhabiting Reiss's body, and the other four Redfangs attack alongside him. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L741-746)
- Shorthilt knows Az'kerash's dance of blades, even though he is far behind in it. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L744)
- Shorthilt's sword crosses past Az'kerash's face while Az'kerash is fighting the five Redfangs. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L749)
- Az'kerash cuts open Shorthilt's stomach after Headscratcher is stabbed, and Rabbiteater drags him away. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L756-758)
## Death
- Shorthilt tells Rabbiteater to run and gives him his jade axe, because his own beloved sword is broken. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1086-1092)
- Shorthilt's blood turns Rabbiteater's cloak red as he hands over the axe. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1095)
- {state} Shorthilt walks back with a chipped sword and cuts down Humans who charge him, then falls. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1095-1097)
- Rabbiteater later wears a cloak that holds the blood of a dead Goblin, Shorthilt. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L1068)
- {event} Shorthilt appears to Numbtongue as a ghost, asks to see his sword, and takes Numbtongue's hand. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L1039-1042)
