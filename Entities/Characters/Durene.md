# Durene
> Type: person | Also called: half-Troll; Troll's child; the Emperor's consort | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.00E
> See also: Laken Godart, Riverfarm, Wiskeria, Beniar, Frostwing, Ryoka Griffin, Hedag, Trolls
## Overview
- Durene is a half-Troll girl who lives near Riverfarm and becomes Laken Godart's [Paladin] and later his consort. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L79; Book 3, Ch3.01E L474-475)
- She has a very deep voice that Laken finds soothing. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L50)
- She is at least a head taller than Laken, who is about 184 cm, and she is heavily built. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L247-248)
- Her skin is rough and cracked on the outside of her limbs, while her palm is callused but smoother. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L250)
- Pyrite sees her as a tall grey-skinned warrior whose skin is like stone with cracks, as if her body were still part earth. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L233-234)
- Crossbow bolts fail to puncture her skin in battle. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L236)
- She eats about four times what Laken does, and she can eat a bowl of stew in under a minute. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L253; Book 12, Ch6.33E L287)
- She cannot read: she knows her basic letters and numbers but has never owned a book. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L308)
## Origins
- Durene tells Laken her mother died when she was four and she never met her father. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L70)
- Her father was a wandering Troll who met her mother, a woman living alone on a small farm. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L286,L291)
- She does not know whether her mother liked her father or was attacked, and the villagers only said she was found pregnant and alone, with Troll tracks nearby. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L294)
- An adventurer came and killed her Troll father, and afterwards wanted to kill Durene too, but her mother would not let him. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L296)
- Her mother was too weak after the birth because Durene was so big, and never recovered. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L298)
- After her mother died, the village took her in but she had no real home, sleeping in a barn and eating scraps. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L298-299)
- Laken says the villagers cannot accept Durene for who she is. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L475)
- She calls Drakes "lizard people", has never seen one, and she is excited and overwhelmed by her first glimpse of a city. (Book 4, Ch3.36 L19-20,L32)
## Home near Riverfarm
- {fact} Durene lives alone in a large house of wood and rough stone next to a stream in the forest near Riverfarm. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L79,L128)
- Riverfarm has around sixty people, and the nearest big city, Bells, is over thirty miles away. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L85,L172)
- Her house has no glass in the windows, and she sleeps on a mattress of soft blankets and pillows. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L6-7)
- She raises chickens and pigs and has a large garden, but says she cannot cook well because she has no Skills. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L165)
- She cannot bring herself to butcher one of her pigs when Laken needs food for Frostwing. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L179)
- She swiftly snaps the necks of two squirrels. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L188)
- She says she can kick or push over a small tree, and earns a pittance supplying Riverfarm with firewood. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L218-219)
- Later, she lives in a cottage that she built for herself. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L262)

## Fighting style
- {fact} Durene practices with a club of hammered-together wood and a partially converted door as a shield. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L310)
- She wears a huge stitched leather jerkin, carries a tower shield made from a door and a club as long as her side. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L251)
- She has beaten over a dozen Hobs with the club, and she once broke a greatsword in training. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L251)
- She also uses an old iron greatsword and has killed two Hobs and a score of Goblins with it. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L57)
