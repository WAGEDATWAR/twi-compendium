# Greydath of Blades
> Type: person | Also called: Greybeard; Greydath; Lord Greydath | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.29G
> See also: Tremborag, Rags, Pyrite, Garen Redfang, Velan the Kind, Goblin Lord, Numbtongue, Elia Arcsinger
## Overview
- Greydath of Blades is a former Goblin Lord who served Velan the Kind and was named his servant of arms. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L274-276)
- Greydath lived among Goblins as an old Hob called Greybeard in Tremborag's tribe, with a long grey beard that no other Goblin has. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L66,L69)
- Greydath fights with a rusty, notched greatsword that shines and tears the air when he holds it. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L271,L282; Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L94)
- Greydath has a long grey beard and is older than any other Goblin Pyrite has met, by far. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L122)
- Greydath lacks the pull and aura of Goblinkind, so Rags senses nothing from him and he seems like a ghost in the world. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L279-281)
- Greydath can raise a power that makes Goblins fall to their knees and cut the air and water with his sword. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L275,L297)
- Greydath's crimson eyes show sorrow, a burning hatred that has lasted for ages, and something like regret. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L320)

## As Greybeard in Rags's tribe (Books 4-6)
- Greybeard, an old Goblin, tells Rags that a big tribe like hers can probably remember Chieftain memories. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L314-316)
- Greybeard tells Rags and her Goblins he saw the last Goblin King, Velan the Kind, die and remembers the King before that, Curulac of a Hundred Days. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L222)
- Greybeard compares the Goblin Kings to stars, distant and unreachable. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L227-228)
- Pyrite counts Greybeard among Rags's counselors and has already solved part of the mystery of who Greybeard is. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L121,L124)
- Pyrite thinks Greybeard is important and wonders whether Rags or he will first notice what he really is. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L123-124)
- Greybeard says "Something is coming" just before the horn calls announce the Humans. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L122-123)

- Greybeard holds off the Rose Knight Sir Kerrig Louis with a greatsword and then six of the Knights of the Petal at once. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L222-224)

- Thomast duels Greybeard, who multiplies into several images, an afterimage or a Skill, then opens Thomast's chest and stomach with his greatsword. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L245-249)
- {reveal} The old Goblin declares himself Greydath, and Noears falls to his knees at the name. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L272-273)
- {reveal} Greydath says he served Velan the Kind, who named him servant of arms, and announces himself Greydath of Blades, once a Goblin Lord. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L274-276)
- Greydath tells Rags that Goblin Lords define themselves by overwhelming talent and must stand above all others. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L298)
- Greydath judges Rags too young, too small, too weak and not enough, saying she does not crush others with who she is. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L292-296)
- Greydath says Tremborag is old, lazy and fat on arrogance, and Garen Redfang is complacent in his strength. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L302)
- Greydath says he rode with the Goblin King and that the Goblin Lord is not his equal. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L305)
- Greydath strikes Rags and the Goblins who rush to protect her with a force from his sword, and Pyrite pleads with him to stop. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L306-313)

- Greydath answers Rags's question why he did not lead his people as they died with "Because I cannot be King". (Book 6, Ch4.39G L320-321)
- Greydath tosses Rags aside and walks out of the destroyed camp. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L322)

- Pyrite reveals that Greybeard, who visited Tremborag's tribe with a rusty greatsword, was Greydath of Blades. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L248-255)
- Pyrite says Greybeard cut a Griffon in half, ate, slept, talked and taught him. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L261)

- {belief} Tremborag claims the Lords sent only Greydath with Velan, and that the other Lords were lesser. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L490)

## At the Liscor floodplains (Book 9)
- A grey-bearded Goblin crouches on a distant hilltop and speaks of an army of lost children and five who walk Garen Redfang's path. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L846-847)
- Greydath of Blades is the hilltop Goblin who stares at the inn and senses a strangeness in a cave to the north that drew him south. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L848)
- A tall, lean old Hob with a grey beard and a greatsword offers the five Redfang Hobs a wheel of goat's cheese as a peace offering. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L237-245)
- Greydath tells Numbtongue he dreamed of five not-Chieftains, five seeds not grown. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L332)
- Greydath calls the five incomplete and says their happiness is a trap, because happiness for one Goblin is despair for all. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L332,L403)
- {belief} Greydath claims the Hobs rely on Erin too much and that she is the reason they are holding back. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L345)
- Greydath fights all five Redfang Hobs at once and beats them easily. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L360-371)
- {belief} Greydath claims he met and challenged Garen Redfang, who could not best him, and the Redfangs believe him. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L379-383)
- Greydath threatens to hurt Erin to make the Hobs grow stronger, and leads them in a chase across seven flooded hills toward the inn. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L385-397)
- Greydath dodges an arrow aimed at his eye, howls with grief and rage, and hurls his greatsword into the tower on top of the inn, collapsing it. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L434-437)
- Greydath tells the Hobs they must decide whether they are Chieftains or lost Hobs, then dives into the water and swims away. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L448,L457)
- Greydath answers why it had to be him with "Because we are Goblins". (Book 9, Ch5.53 L455-456)

## After the Battle of the Floodplains (Book 9)
- Greydath leaps down to Rags after the battle, and Rags, Redscar, Snapjaw, Rabbiteater and Badarrow recognize him with fury. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L46-52)
- Greydath admits he saw it all and did nothing, answering Rags's accusation with the question "Should I?". (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L55-60)
- Greydath blocks Snapjaw's sword and tells the Goblins Garen chose to die, calling him a traitor and Reiss a slave. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L64-70)
- Greydath tells Rags that Goblins die, and that this is why her people died, because they are weak. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L77-79)
- Greydath tells Rags he waited and watched for whoever came, Rags, Garen, Reiss or Tremborag, and did nothing. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L86)
- {belief} Greydath says he did nothing because it is meaningless and had to be so, and that Goblins wait only for a Goblin King. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L91,L95)
- {belief} Greydath says he searches the world for Goblins who can rise beyond Lords, tests and goads them, and holds that a true King must rise alone. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L101)
- Greydath tells Rags that Velan chose his death. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L105)
- Greydath tells the Goblins the Goblin King's treasure waits high on the mountain, that two keys unlock it, and that Rags has Garen's key. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L109-111)
- Greydath leaves his greatsword behind and jumps laughing down the mountain. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L124-127)
