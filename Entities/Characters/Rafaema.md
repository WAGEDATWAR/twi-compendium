# Rafaema
> Type: person | Also called: Wall Lady Rafaema; Lady Coloseuvia; the youngest Drake | First appears: Ch6.33E
> See also: Luciva Skybreath, Ferris, Cire, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Rafaema is a Wall Lady of Manus, a young-looking Drake with teal scales that shine and flow like water, wings and yellow neck spines. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L49; Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L115-116)
- Rafaema has wings and breathes lightning, and people say her Oldblood manifestation is the strongest in living memory. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L117)
- Rafaema looks younger than any other Drake at the Manus Security Council by at least a decade, which no one remarks on. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L51)
- Rafaema has heterochromia, with two differently coloured pupils. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L167)
- Rafaema is courted by half of Manus' eligible males and swats suitors away each week. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L118)
- Rafaema says Lady Coloseuvia is a last name she barely remembers. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L134-135)
- Rafaema is believed by most people to be a gifted young Drake who is politically kept from the front lines due to a complicated family dynamic. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L119)
- {belief} People in Manus think Rafaema arrived from abroad at about sixteen, with certified ancestry from the original founders of Manus. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L116)
## Security Council
- Rafaema sits on Manus' Security Council as the youngest member, and the Dragonspeaker asks for her thoughts on whether to reply to the Humans' attack. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L78-80)
- Rafaema says that if the Humans attack, Manus should attack back, and this sways the High Command, except Lulv and General Milka. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L82-83)
- Rafaema asks whether the first plan of ruination will go forward and then says she forgets which plan that is. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L89-92)
- Rafaema recalls that the Witch has not caused major trouble since before she was born, when Luciva raises a Grade 9 threat. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L107-113)
- Rafaema tells Luciva that Humans have always been Humans and that the Antinium bother her more because they are new. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L132)
- Rafaema says that if Manus did not reply, the Humans would get away with it, and asks why Manus would ever allow that. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L136)
- Rafaema offers condolences to Luciva about Hedica before leaving the room. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L139)
- Rafaema tells the council that Wyverns and Oldblood Drakes can pool their breath attacks, and that a Manus flying wing used the tactic eighty years ago. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L132-136)
- Rafaema says she has read older accounts, and that a Drake riding a Wyvern can combine his attack with the Wyvern's, even with different elements. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L136)
## Secret nature
- {reveal} Rafaema is secretly a Dragon, a fact known only to a few people such as Manus' inner war council. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L119)
- {reveal} Only the Dragonspeaker and a handful of others know of the vault holding the document that says there are only two Dragons left in all of Izril, one of them Rafaema. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L217-218)
- The Manus war council knows who Rafaema is and respects her voice, and she is part of their discussions along with Dragonspeaker Luciva. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L221)
- Rafaema keeps a Drake form through transformation magic, with wings but without concealing her shadow, and has the size of a Drake. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L202,L212)
- The narration says the Drake cover would continue until Rafaema 'died' or had an accident in her forties, when a new young Drake with different features and backstory would appear. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L120-121)
- Rafaema asks her minder Ferris how old she is, and he says she is a hundred and twenty-three. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L185-189)
- Rafaema cannot level, which the narration says is just as well, since Dragons able to level would have ruled the world forever. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L220)
- Rafaema keeps her Dragon's strength, speed and toughness and could throw Ferris a long way. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L208,L220)
- Rafaema can cast spells as well as many [Mages] and mastered [Detect Lies] long ago. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L168)
## Childhood and upbringing
- Rafaema asks Ferris if his books mention Dragonspeaker Telimer, then says it took her four decades to understand why they kept putting her back to sleep. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L197-200)
- Rafaema remembers sleeping for seventeen years once and nine years at another time, and her latest slumbers have grown shorter. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L194-195)
- Rafaema last napped twenty-seven years ago. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L190-191)
- Rafaema did not step outside the keep until she was fifty years old, and then an entire army was set to watch her. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L203)
- Rafaema threw the worst adolescent tantrum in Manus' history at age eighty, according to the narration. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L214)
- Rafaema's Dragonspeakers had dealt with her in different ways, and her wrath on finding she had been deceived is written in the history books. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L213)
- Two earlier minders, Kelis and Amessia, assigned to Rafaema fifty and thirty years ago, nearly caused disaster. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L222)
## Ferris and Erin Solstice
- Rafaema spots her new minder, the Gnoll Ferris Thycarl, disguised as a suitor within a second and asks him to speak with her. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L123-147)
- Rafaema tells Ferris that the war council plans to seek joint funding from other Walled Cities for operations against the Hives and to investigate Liscor. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L162)
- Rafaema declares that she wants authority and that she wants the Human Erin Solstice brought to Manus so she can appraise her. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L265-271)
- Rafaema tells Ferris that Manus will send Erin an invitation and that he should offer gold, not kidnap her. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L268,L273)
- Rafaema threatens that her old minder would be sent flying, burnt by lightning, if Ferris refuses, and says she knows where he lives. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L277,L280)
## Later

- Cire says he will tell Rafaema something at their next meeting, in two months at Manus, and calls her uptight these days. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L302-304)
