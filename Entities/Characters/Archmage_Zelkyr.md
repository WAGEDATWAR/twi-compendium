# Archmage Zelkyr
> Type: person | Also called: Zelkyr | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3
> See also: Cognita, Teriarch, Wistram, Calvaron
## Overview
- Archmage Zelkyr is a Drake who ruled Wistram a few centuries ago and held the [Archmage] class. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L349,L352)
- Calvaron says Zelkyr was the best of the Archmages of his time. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L352)
- Zelkyr is famous for damaging one of the Walled Cities' walls and more famous for creating Golems. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L352)
- Zelkyr ordered his Golems to guard the entrances to the upper levels of Wistram so that only he could learn from the mages above. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L361)
- Zelkyr's order caused a huge battle with the other mages, after which his surviving Truestone Construct Cognita imposed a new order. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L361)
- {state} Cognita came down from the upper floors and announced that Archmage Zelkyr was dead. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L370)
- Cognita and the other Golems have maintained Wistram since, still fulfilling his orders, and his order that the upper floors be off limits still stands. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L370)
- Teriarch grumbles that Zelkyr probably wanted funding for his Golems, and recalls that he made a hundred Truestone Golems. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L465-466)
- Teriarch remembers only after ranting that Zelkyr was dead. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L467-468)
