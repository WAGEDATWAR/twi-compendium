# Idis
> Type: person | Also called: the Selphid in Geneva's head | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.22D
> See also: Geneva, Calectus, Okasha, Seve-Alrelious, Selphid
## Overview
- Idis is a Selphid from the Minds, the Selphids' leaders, who takes over sharing Geneva Scala's body in place of Okasha. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1195,L1199-1200)
- Idis tells Geneva she is helping her move because Okasha was trying to take her over, and that the Minds want to meet Geneva. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1200)
- Idis says she won the honor of the task and the Minds thought she would be best, with other Selphids available if she does not suit. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1202)
- Idis says she is strong and can be relied on to kill whoever tries to hurt Geneva. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1200)
- {state} Idis is a Level 32 [Blademaster] and a Level 14 [Barbarian]. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1202)
- Idis says she will help Geneva move and manage her, as Okasha did. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1202)
## With Geneva and Seve (Book 19)
- Idis naps while Geneva treats a patient and lectures, then whispers in Geneva's head as Seve speaks of the Carven City. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L122-123)
- {event} Idis stops Geneva's mouth when Seve asks for the names of those who made the Occillium, saying the information is important and she must run it by Calectus. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L163-168)
- Idis gives in and lets Geneva give a single name, then mutters about breaking the rules. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L172-173,L176)
