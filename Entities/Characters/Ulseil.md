# Ulseil
> Type: person | Also called: Ulseil Greenscale; Councilman Ulseil | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.35H
> See also: Zevara, Klbkch, Krshia, Stale
## Overview
- Ulseil Greenscale is an olive-green Drake [Merchant], unmarried, middle-aged and wealthy by Liscor standards. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L444-445)
- Ulseil is the head of Liscor's Merchant's Guild and a member of Liscor's Council. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L445-446; Book 13, Ch6.49 L13)
- Ulseil is not Oldblood, though his great-great-aunt had vestigial wings, a source of pride for the Greenscale family. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L443)
- Ulseil is content to earn and save money rather than risk much, and his goal is to find a Miss Greenscale. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L445-446)
## Council
- {belief} In the Liscor Council, an unnamed Drake who heads the Merchant's Guild blames the dungeon and the Antinium after another attack. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L546-548)
- Ulseil shudders to think of the Council's recent meetings and wants out of the job after helping Liscor survive possible war with Humans. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L449-452)
- Ulseil plans to appoint his deputy Stale of the Merchant's Guild, a young Drake in his thirties, as his successor. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L452-453)
- {event} Cornered by a crowd, Ulseil blurts that he would support Gnolls on the Council and elections, and the crowd spreads it as a vote. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L540-548)
- {event} Ulseil faints after realising he will be accountable for his words before the whole crowd. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L551-552)
- Ulseil is one of eight rich Drakes of the old Council who sit scowling at each other in the meeting room. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L13)
