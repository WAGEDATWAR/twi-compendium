# Tremborag
> Type: person | Also called: Great Chieftain of the North; Great Chieftain of the Mountain; Great Chieftain of the Mountain City tribe | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.26G
> See also: Garen Redfang, Rags, Greydath of Blades, Ulvama, Velan the Kind, Goblin Lord, Pyrite, Noears
## Overview
- Tremborag is a huge, obscenely fat Hobgoblin with crimson eyes who rules a vast tribe from a stone mountain city. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L167,L186)
- Tremborag stands as tall as a Minotaur and bigger, and sits on a throne in his hall. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L185-186)
- Tremborag speaks the common tongue fluently. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L41)
- Tremborag's shouted command to kneel carries pressure that must be a Skill, and most visiting Chieftains kneel. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L188-189)
- Tremborag calls himself the Great Chieftain of the North. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L187)
- Tremborag's tribe is called the Mountain City Tribe, though Rags hears it more often called the Great Chieftain's Tribe. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L9)
- Tremborag is only the sixth Goblin with a name that Rags has ever known. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L9)
- Tremborag rules his tribe from Dwarfhalls Rest, a mountain he has made his lair. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L88)
- Rags wonders why Tremborag has not become a Goblin Lord, since his tribe is as vast as the Goblin Lord's but she would feel it if he were one. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L16)
- Rags sees Tremborag as a Goblin who rules a palace and acts and speaks like a Human, whom she cannot trust. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L292-293)

## Transformation
- Tremborag changes in battle into a gargantuan eight-foot form where all his fat becomes muscle and he is powerful and quick. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L241-242)
- Tremborag tells Rags there are more things in the world that she does not know than she knows, and that he is one of them. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L247)
- Afterwards Tremborag is fat and normal again, laughing and eating among his warriors as if the change was an illusion. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L251)
- In the siege, Tremborag's form grows larger and his fat becomes muscle as he tears the head off a Hob. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L151)
- Tremborag takes a bestial form with red eyes, yellowed teeth and a body of muscle and fury when he emerges from hiding in a wall. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L55)

## Alliance with Garen Redfang (Books 4-7)
- Tremborag agrees with Garen Redfang that the Goblin Lord is not a true Goblin, then asks again why Garen has come. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L207-209)
- {belief} Tremborag claims he met the Goblin King Velan the Kind ten years ago, when the King came to Izril and to his tribe with his armies. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L187)
- {event} Tremborag agrees to fight beside Garen against the Goblin Lord after Garen shows a small key and Tremborag says he knows what Garen has. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L218-223)
- Tremborag vows that the Goblin Lord will break his teeth upon the mountain should he come to battle. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L223)
- Tremborag tells Rags he will not move against the Goblin Lord unless the Lord comes to the mountain, since it is easy to defend. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L63)
- Tremborag asks which Hob will fight with Rags, and none of the Goblins at his high table moves to give her warriors. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L74-76)
- Tremborag finds his armory emptied of magical arms and four guard Hobs dead after Rags's tribe flees the mountain. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L6-10)
- Tremborag estimates that between two and six thousand Goblins left, including several hundred of his own warriors and some Hobs, plus food, supplies and Garen's wolves and women. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L14,L20)
- Tremborag returns to his enraged form and threatens to kill Garen for bringing Rags, but backs down when Garen says he has the key and knows where the other is. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L28-36)
- Tremborag says he would take the other key even from the heart of Invrisil, behind a Walled City or at the bottom of the sea, and Garen replies it is in a worse place. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L35-36)
- Tremborag vows to take the key all the same, as soon as he hunts down the small Goblin Rags and eats her alive. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L38)
- Tremborag answers Garen's demand to stop sending raids by calling himself Great Chieftain under the mountain, who will bow to no Lord or King. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L15-16,L38)
- Tremborag names two other famed Chieftains, a pyromaniac witch and a brute who loves combat, who refused his request to join against the Goblin Lord. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L29-32)
- Tremborag says the witch also refused the Goblin King, as he did, and the brute accepts no master. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L32)
- Tremborag, eating savagely on his throne, tells Garen that the Goblin Lord wants his strength and will not have it. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L34,L55)
- {belief} Tremborag claims he refused a Goblin Lord who once came to his mountain, and looked the Goblin King in the eye and refused to follow. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L76)
- Tremborag says Velan told the young, old, pregnant and fearful to hide, took only willing warriors, and came across the sea from Baleros. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L72-74)
- Tremborag calls Velan's fight an arrogant waste. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L75-76)
- {event} Tremborag says he too dreams of the secret Velan hid and that he will have it; he and Garen agree to decide who gets it after the Goblin Lord. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L81-84)
- {event} Tremborag orders his armies summoned, the gates barred and swords sharpened, and Garen agrees to wait for the Goblin Lord. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L88-89)

## Siege of Dwarfhalls Rest (Book 8)
- {belief} Tremborag claims to be old, to have lived decades ago when the Goblin King was an unborn seed, and to remember Velan's fall and the King's face. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L120)
- {belief} Tremborag tells Reiss that a slave cannot lead others, that Reiss is a pet and not a Goblin, and that he does not even deserve to be Chieftain. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L126-128)
- {belief} Tremborag mocks Reiss's small army and says he bows to a master and copies Humans' formations and armor. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L131)
- Tremborag eats and laughs on his dirty throne during the siege, an act that boosts the morale of his tribe. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L60)
- {belief} Tremborag boasts that his tribe is greater than the Goblin Lord's and that he has been Chieftain for decades while the Lord has held the title perhaps half a year. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L66)
- {event} Tremborag loses the narrow tunnel entrances to exploding undead, then orders his Goblins to pull back and fight in the open central caverns. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L92-93,L98)
- {belief} Tremborag reasons that the Goblin Lord must fight or run now as the Humans approach, and that fighting Humans in his own mountain suits him. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L89-94)
- {event} Tremborag recognizes Elia Arcsinger, calls her Kingslayer and flees, which causes both Goblin armies to rout. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L479-482)
- {belief} Tremborag claims Elia Arcsinger shot the arrow that killed Velan the Kind. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L479)
- A huge green hand drags a [Light Mage] into the wall, and Tremborag charges out of hiding to strike the [Pyromancer] and a [Rogue] among the adventurers. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L54-57)
- Tremborag tells Garen to run, says his mountain is lost and his tribe flees, and swears to hunt the adventurers. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L61-62)
