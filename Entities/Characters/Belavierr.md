# Belavierr
> Type: person | Also called: the Stitch Witch; the Spider; the Eater of Threads; the Witch of Calamity; the Witch of Webs; the Temptress; Threadstealer; Great Witch of Terandria; Mistress of Strands | First appears: Ch6.37E
> See also: Wiskeria, Califor, Nanette, Az'kerash, Ryoka Griffin, Mavika, Hedag, Eloise, Magnolia Reinhart, Perril Chandler
## Overview
- Belavierr is a seven-foot [Witch] with black hair to her waist, a hat wide enough to shade her whole body, and eyes with multiple orange irises ringed in black. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L506,L515)
- Belavierr rides a huge black stallion and names herself Great Witch of Terandria when seeking sanctuary in Riverfarm. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L530,L560-561)
- Belavierr is Wiskeria's mother and tips her hat to Wiskeria, calling her daughter. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L619; Book 12, Ch6.40E L152-154)
- Belavierr is called the Spider, Temptress, the Eater of Threads, the Witch of Calamity and Mistress of Strands. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L351)
- Belavierr says her craft is stitching, sewing, connection and binding. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L540)
- Belavierr stitches with a pale needle and flesh-coloured thread, with hands too fast for Ryoka to follow. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L504)
- Belavierr's wind-silencing presence makes Ryoka's wind vanish, and she thanks Ryoka as "friend of winds" before staring at her for minutes. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L490-492,L562-565)
- Belavierr wears robes of stitched cloth that are as hard as steel, and says she once made Seven League Boots. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L916-918)
## Immortality and her fall
- Belavierr says her great work, the Helpful Servants, was undone while she slept, nibbled away by strange mice that defiled her soul and craft. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L152-155)
- Belavierr says her death walks the world again and that it burns. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L152)
- Belavierr has many magics shattered, with broken strands trailing behind her, after losing her magic in Noelictus. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L626-629)
- Belavierr says it will take aeons to rebuild, and that she begins thread by thread. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L629)
- Belavierr says what ate her magic was the mice, whose identity matters little. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L662-665)
- Belavierr layered her immortality by binding her soul, eating the heart of the last Phoenix, buying a [Necromancer]'s secret and sending her death to a hundred or a thousand women. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L12-16)
- Belavierr mends the tapestry of her soul, which mice ate holes in while she slept, lonely for her daughter. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L3-17)
- {state} After losing her protections Belavierr says she can be killed with age or blade, and feels more alive and aware. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L138-142)
- Belavierr says she has lost all but a few years of stored time and needs more power to rebuild the workings that hid her heart. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L183)
- {belief} Belavierr says she has survived her death but foresees a second by fire, and stays only until the deal with the Emperor is struck. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L132-135)
- Belavierr speaks like a person, remembers names and faces, and blinks at the sun after her protections burn away. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L136,L139,L173)
- Belavierr tells Ryoka she gained [Immortal Moment] when she last levelled up recently. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L927-930)
- Belavierr later has the lines of her immortality knitting slowly again, though many rings are broken. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L32)
- Belavierr says she has waxed and waned with the world and has not been so exposed in thousands of years, being close to mortality. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L10)
- Later, Belavierr is mortal, learning insight from mortality, and is no kinder for it. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L499-500,L548)
## Crimes and reputation
- A list of Belavierr's alleged crimes includes destruction of a city, murder of a [Prince], murder of a child, summoning of an Old One and mass deaths by it. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L1425)
- Belavierr's threads and garments strangle those who inform on her, and three people died trying to inform the Order of Seasons. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L676)
- One of those informants was strangled by her collar and another pulled in front of a wagon, according to Fierre's knowledge. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L676)
- Wiskeria knows that Belavierr never lies, and Belavierr says she promised to give up only her magic, not her craft of bargains. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L693-694)
- Belavierr says that when the Order sends [Knights] by tens of thousands, they were never enough. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L33)
