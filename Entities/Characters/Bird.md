# Bird
> Type: person | Also called: Bird the Hunter; Individual Bird; Hunter Bird; Knight (former name) | First appears: Ch1.44
> See also: Erin Solstice, Pawn, Belgrade, Anand, Garry, Klbkch, Free Queen, Numbtongue, Mrsha
## Overview
- Bird is an Antinium Worker and Individual who lives at The Wandering Inn, where he hunts birds with a bow from a tower on the roof. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L66-71)
- Bird is both a [Bird Hunter] and a [Singer], and his voice carries across the Floodplains. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L10)
- Bird says that he is two years old and will be three sometime in the fall. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L99)
- Bird says he is named Bird after Henry Edward Bird, a Human chess player who invented Bird's Opening and Bird's Defense. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L86)
