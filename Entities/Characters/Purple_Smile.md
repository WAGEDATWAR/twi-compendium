# Purple Smile
> Type: person | Also called: Purple Smile the Painted Soldier; [Sergeant] Purple Smile | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.40L
> See also: Yellow Splatters, Twin Stripes, Pawn, Anand, Bird
## Overview
- Purple Smile is an Antinium Soldier of the Painted Soldiers who has painted a purple ring around both eyes and a smile across his mandibles and lower face. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L116-117)
- Purple Smile leads the Soldiers who patrol above ground, and two other Soldiers copy his painted markings. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L112-118)
- Purple Smile is relaxed and calm, in contrast to Yellow Splatters, who stands at straight attention. (Book 8, Ch5.25L L26)
## Becoming a [Sergeant]
- Pawn tells Yellow Splatters he is considering Purple Smile as the next [Sergeant]. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L112-114)
- Yellow Splatters thinks the smile looks ridiculous, but Purple Smile enjoys the activity. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L118)
- Purple Smile leads ten Painted Soldiers against zombies, but retreats when two Crypt Lords lurch toward them, to Yellow Splatters' outrage. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L24,L28)
- Purple Smile and his Soldiers then flank the Crypt Lords from behind. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L30)
- Anand praises Purple Smile's efficiency in combat, saying his lateral thinking is impressive though he is less powerful than Yellow Splatters. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L46-48)
- Yellow Splatters doubts Purple Smile is fit to be a [Sergeant], since he does not spar and wanders the barracks playing with a rolled-up ball. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L158-159)
- {state} Pawn congratulates Purple Smile as a [Sergeant], and Purple Smile hugs Yellow Splatters and Twin Stripes. (Book 6, Ch4.42L L260-263)
- Purple Smile touches Twin Stripes' chest as Pawn's blessing restores him from Aberration. (Book 6, Ch4.42L L256)
## Later
- Purple Smile and Yellow Splatters are both [Sergeants] under Pawn, a new concept to both. (Book 8, Ch5.25L L25-26,L193)
- Purple Smile mimes a plan with his four hands, which Pawn interprets as a suggestion to talk to a woman. (Book 8, Ch5.26L L161-163)
- Pawn tells the Queen that Yellow Splatters and Purple Smile show great promise. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L304)
- Purple Smile dances and shows other Workers and Soldiers how to do it, while Yellow Splatters reads a book. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L420)
