# Ishkr
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.14L
> See also: Drassi, Lyonette, Erin, Krshia, Brunkr, Numbtongue
## Overview
- Ishkr is a red-brown-furred Gnoll who works at Erin's inn after Krshia introduced him to her. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L17-18)
- Ishkr is a Silverfang Gnoll, born around the time some Silverfangs came to Liscor, and he is about nineteen. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L120)
- Ishkr was born and raised in Liscor and does not know many of his tribe's superstitions. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L61)
- Ishkr has one sister he grumbles about and two parents he never mentions, who Erin suspects are dead. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L120)
## Classes and role
- {state} Ishkr says he is a Level 16 [Waiter], recommended to work at the inn by Honored Krshia. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L31)
- Ishkr is physically stronger than Lyonette, even with her [Lesser Strength]. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L47)
- {state} Later, Ishkr is a [Head Waiter] and Lyonette's second-in-command, a position he won over Drassi. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L102)
- Ishkr runs the kitchen and common room of the inn while Drassi works night shifts as [Bartender]. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L99,L102)

## Secrets
- Ishkr asks for his week's pay in advance, which Erin ties to a family matter. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L111-113)
- {belief} Erin thinks Ishkr has a secret about money, likely related to his sister, who sends friends to whisper to him. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L113,L123)
- Ishkr is a model employee who accounts for his own mistakes and never seeks advancement. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L114,L122)
- Ishkr is one of Erin's two longest current employees, and calls her Miss Erin more respectfully than before. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L111)
- Ishkr runs the magic door to Esthelm and Liscor, greeting and directing visitors. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L111,L116)
