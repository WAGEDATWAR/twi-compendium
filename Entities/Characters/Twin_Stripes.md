# Twin Stripes
> Type: person | Also called: Twin Stripes the Soldier | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.40L
> See also: Yellow Splatters, Purple Smile, Pawn, Klbkch
## Overview
- Twin Stripes is a new Antinium Soldier with a pink stripe on either side of his face. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L85)
- Twin Stripes spars with Yellow Splatters and goes above ground to patrol with the other Soldiers. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L85,L303)
## Aberration
- {state} Twin Stripes becomes an Aberration after a long day, to Yellow Splatters' surprise since Soldiers almost never become one. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L303-309)
- {event} Twin Stripes lunges at Yellow Splatters and fights off over a hundred Soldiers in the barracks with possessed strength. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L310-311)
- Twin Stripes is restrained with bent iron bars and taken to a holding cell while glaring at Yellow Splatters with hate. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L312-313)
- {state} Pawn's blessing returns Twin Stripes from Aberration to a Soldier, and Yellow Splatters frees him. (Book 6, Ch4.42L L256,L260)
