# Bezha
> Type: person | Also called: Bezhavil | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.12K
> See also: Nawal, Silmak, Hesseif, Clan Tannousin
## Overview
- Bezha is a veiled senior woman of Clan Tannousin, introduced by Silmak under the full name Bezhavil. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L740-743; Book 10, Ch6.13K L7)
- Bezhavil is Silmak's aunt second-removed and oversees the womenfolk and manages the Tannousin caravan. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L7)
- Bezha is the aunt of Hesseif, the caravan's chief guard. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L7,L79)
- Nawal thinks of Bezhavil as her aunt, and Bezha calls Nawal by the affectionate name Nawali. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L5,L19; Book 19, Ch7.46K L216)
- Bezha has tattooed hands, as Silmak does. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L88,L110)
- Bezha is old enough to be lined of face. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L8)
## Caring for Nawal
- Bezha has helped to care for Nawal and her brother since they were children. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L5)
- Bezha, Silmak and Hesseif have all known Nawal since she was a child, and the clan hoped they could rein her sharp tongue in. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L535)
- Bezha tells Nawal to mind her tongue before the King of Destruction, and Nawal bristles that she knows she is a woman. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L740-747)
- Bezha smacks Nawal on the back of the neck for saying the Tannousin are due respect from the King of Destruction. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L92-93)
- Bezha promises Nawal the clan's support, and says she will see to the caravan and find work for its members in Reim if there is any. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L107-108)
- Bezha screams at Nawal outside the forge for challenging Master Daiton to a contest, and takes swings at her face. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L536-541)
## In the forge at Reim
- Bezha studies a puck and bar of Daiton's steel with Silmak and recoils at the soot and grain, which suggests the metal is inferior. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L545,L550-554)
- Bezha closes her eyes but does not argue when Silmak and Hesseif ask Nawal whether she can forge a sword that day. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L561-563)
- Bezha tells Trey that Tannousin smiths are trained to hold their breath, and that Nawal has learned her forging pattern since she could hold a hammer. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L659-660)
- Bezha catches Nawal when she staggers after finishing the forging. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L924,L928)
- After Nawal faints, Bezha wakes her gently with a stamina potion diluted in water, and tells her the King has mentioned her name. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L8-18)
- Bezha gestures Nawal down to her seat as mages walk past in the banquet hall. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L92)
- Trey, who nearly gets tackled by Hesseif and Bezha when he catches Nawal fainting, thinks Bezha may or may not be Nawal's mother. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L250)
## The ruse
- Bezhavil is appalled and afraid when Nawal proposes telling Flos the clan has been pretending, since the King of Destruction's wrath is feared. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L228-230)
- Bezhavil suggests the clan claim that Nawal's youth and inexperience as a female smith is what delays the Naq-Alrama steel. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L235-239)
- Bezhavil kicks the back of Nawal's heels when Nawal babbles a great promise to Lord Venith. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L221)
