# Rehanna
> Type: person | Also called: Rehanna Salesmith | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.22E
> See also: Laken, Wiskeria, Belavierr, Mavika, Durene
## Overview
- Rehanna Salesmith is a tall woman from Windrest who takes refuge in Riverfarm. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L164; Book 12, Ch6.40E L936)
## Book 5: confronting Laken
- Rehanna accuses Laken of breaking his promise to keep the Windrest folk safe from the Goblins. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L154-160)
- Rehanna mocks Laken's blindness, saying a blind man cannot be trusted or tell people apart. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L165; Book 5, Ch4.22E L168)
- Laken tells Rehanna that fear explains but does not excuse her words, and says he will not eject her. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L188-190)
- Rehanna refuses to kneel to Laken. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L192)
- {event} Laken speaks one word, "Kneel", and Rehanna falls to her knees as he walks away. (Book 5, Ch4.22E L198-200)
## Grief
- Rehanna's husband fought at Lancrel and died after following Wiskeria into battle. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L163; Book 12, Ch6.36E L166)
- {belief} Rehanna blames Wiskeria and the mad Emperor for her husband's death, and says Lancrel's people see the truth. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L166; Book 12, Ch6.36E L835)
- {event} Rehanna kicks over a cauldron of soup. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L820)
- {state} Rehanna's hand is scorched when a doorknob turns white-hot, and she screams as skin is torn from her palm. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L845-852)
- Witch Mavika admits she cast the spell, saying Rehanna's pain was justly won by rudeness. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L869-873; Book 12, Ch6.36E L878)
## The cloth child
- Belavierr speaks to Rehanna of an offer, and Rehanna goes white, stumbles and runs. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L936-946)
- {belief} Rie fears that Rehanna accepted Belavierr's offer, though the cost was unknown to Rie. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L950-951)
- {event} Rehanna cradles an infant made of stitched cloth that moves, cries and has black inside its mouth. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L1374-1380)
- {belief} Rehanna believes Belavierr has brought her baby back alive and that he will never leave or die. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L1386-1391)
- {state} Rehanna has aged overnight, her hair grey, and has lost all her hatred for Wiskeria, Riverfarm and Laken. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L17)
- Rehanna says she levelled up as a [Mother] the night before. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L157)
- {state} Rehanna paid forty years of her life to Belavierr for the doll. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L196-199)
- Rehanna names the child Mihka, a name she and her husband chose together. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L551)
