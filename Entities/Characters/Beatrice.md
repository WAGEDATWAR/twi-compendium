# Beatrice
> Type: person | Also called: Dullahan student | First appears: Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1
> See also: Calvaron, Montressa du Valeros, Cognita, Aaron Vanwell, Wistram, Dullahan
## Overview
- Beatrice is a dark-skinned Dullahan student at Wistram whose head and armor are not attached. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L326)
- She is quiet, and at her first meal with Ceria and Pisces she says only about three things beyond greetings. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L326)
- She eats pudding with a spoon into her head while it sits on the table. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L326)
- Beatrice is allergic to mushrooms, and she offers to have them taken away if they bother anyone. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L140-142)
## Faction and class
- {fact} Calvaron describes Beatrice as a Revivalist, in the faction that wants to throw open Wistram's doors and usher in a new era of magic. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L358)
- She is a [Runeshaper] with symbols etched into her armor, and Calvaron says she and he have little clout. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L419-421)
- She wants Calvaron to join the Revivalists, and they quarrel about his future. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L77)
## Friendship with Calvaron
- {fact} A student reports that Beatrice and Calvaron have put money on both Ceria and Pisces passing the exams. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L467)
- {event} She holds the body of Calvaron after the shades kill him in the banquet hall. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L385)
- She tells Ceria it is her fault and that the cause was Pisces. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L386-389)
- Beatrice later attacks Ceria for defending Pisces, and Montressa saves Ceria. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L427)
- Beatrice silently watches Charles de Trevalier after he drops Illphres' spellbook into the sea, and says nothing. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L436-438)
## Later at Wistram
- {state} Beatrice passes her final test and becomes an official mage of Wistram, a [Runeshaper] and one of the secret brokers of the academy. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L209)
- She inherited Calvaron's network of connections after he passed, but not his easygoing air. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L204)
- People still come to her because she has a lot of influence, not because she is easy to work with. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L204)
- Montressa du Valeros is her friend, a full student on the verge of being recognized as a [Mage]. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L184,L209)
- {state} Later, Beatrice is a Dullahan [Runemistress] who is escorting Aaron to Archmage Nailihuaile. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L752,L755,L761)
- Beatrice backs up as Cognita stops them and says another Archmage, Amerys, requests Aaron's presence. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L756-760)
- She calls Amerys a traitor to the academy, and Cognita dismisses the opinion. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L762-764)
- Beatrice relays that Cognita insists on the visit when guards of Amerys's cell hesitate to let Aaron speak with her. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L873-875)
