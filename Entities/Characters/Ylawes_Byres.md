# Ylawes Byres
> Type: person | Also called: Sir Ylawes; Ylawes; the Savior of Esthelm; Yvlon's older brother | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.18T
> See also: Yvlon Byres, Falene, Dawil, Erin Solstice, Himilt, Toren, Grunter
## Overview
- Ylawes Byres is a tall blond Gold-rank [Knight] who wears silver plate and carries a shield and sword. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L92,L183)
- Ylawes leads the Silver Swords, an adventuring group dedicated to fighting monsters. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L47)
- Ylawes is the older brother of Yvlon Byres. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L296)
- He works mainly in the far north near the family estates, and is quite famous there. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L296-298)
- Ylawes is a [Lord]'s son and has no knighthood order, saying he fulfilled the spirit of his class. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L30-33)
## Skills and gear
- Ylawes uses the Skill [Shield Breaker] against Toren. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L138)
- Magic twists a blade away from his head before it can strike. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L146)
- His arms are enchanted but his plate mail is not. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L155)
- He uses [Shield of Valor] to push zombies and Goblins back in a cone. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L159-160)
- His armour is silvery with the Byres crest, and in Book 8 he carries a silver-alloyed longsword. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L26-31; Book 8, Ch5.35H L536-537)
- Ylawes wears silver and steel armour that, per the old stories, no Vampire could touch. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L1280)
## Esthelm (Book 3)
- Ylawes says he came to Esthelm to put the dead to rest after hearing too late and finding other cities and the Adventurer's Guild doing nothing. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L124,L176)
- He is not a Named Adventurer and could not drive off hundreds of Goblins alone. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L131)
- {belief} Ylawes suspects the Goblins spared Esthelm's people so there would be many bodies to raise as an undead horde. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L129)
- Ylawes killed a man who charged him with a sword. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L168)
- He shamed a gang of Esthelm men preying on each other into following him to protect the city. (Book 3, Ch3.18T L176-186)
- He organised close to a thousand survivors into building barricades that sealed off nearly a quarter of the city. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L13-16)
- Ylawes carries a scroll that shows a reply from someone, simply "Soon". (Book 3, Ch3.19T L263-264)
- Ylawes told a man he had sent for help the night before and that the reply said help is coming, but not when. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L121-123)
- Ylawes nearly killed the Redfang Goblins who attacked him, until the Horror intervened. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L225-231)
- {belief} Ylawes wondered why the Redfang warriors were unlike ordinary Goblins. (Book 3, Ch3.19T L233)
- {belief} Ylawes suspected the Redfang Goblins played a role in Esthelm's victory, having seen their leader kill the Goblin commander. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L332-333,L363-367)
- Ylawes holds the main entrance of the palisade, where he is struck by a javelin. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L104,L155)
- Ylawes remembered the dead girl as a monster, but saw only a smiling young woman, and let the Goblins leave with her body. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L360-370)
- Ylawes is called the Savior of Esthelm in talk, and Yvlon says Esthelm is just another of his triumphs. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L291,L298)
## Book 6 to 9: with Yvlon
- Ylawes' team sent a request seeking confirmation that Yvlon Byres lived in the city. (Book 6, Ch4.37O L477)
- Ylawes arrived at Erin's inn with a half-Elf [Mage] and a Dwarf in full plate, and greeted Yvlon. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L493-496)
- Erin threw a cake at his face when the Redfang Goblins drew weapons. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L497-498)
- Ylawes sheathed his sword after learning the sign outside says No Killing Goblins. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L26-31)
- House Byres supports itself with silver and merchants, has a mine, and is in decline. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L391-394)
- House Byres was famous for rooting out Vampires, and its silver was key to their destruction. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L397-400)
- {belief} Ylawes believes Magnolia's army will not engage the Goblin Lord, while Tyrion's levy would sweep the Goblins. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L316-321)
- Ylawes' family has fought undead and those who command them for generations. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L94)
- Ylawes committed his team to finding a lost Minotaur adventurer in Liscor's dungeon. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L100-102)
- Ylawes said the largest Face-Eater Moth he had seen was mounted on a wall and only Troll-sized. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L412)
- A giant Face-Eater Moth carried Ylawes aloft, and he cut into its belly as Falene's magic helped him survive. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L517-528)
- Erin sold Ylawes advice for five copper and told him not to ask Yvlon to come home. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L95-100)
- Ylawes explained to Embria that House Byres made arms against Vampires, last sighted four hundred years ago. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L536-540)
- Ylawes argued with Yvlon about joining Tyrion's army and going north, and she refused. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L802-843)
- He told Yvlon he outranks her as her brother, and she told him adventurers do not follow military command. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L828-830)
- Ylawes demanded Yvlon leave Liscor for the north, and Falene bound her with [Binding Cords - Iron]. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L186,L250-252)
- Ylawes fought Pisces and others until Halrac and Bevussa and the other adventurers stopped them. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L260-270)
- Ylawes' team left Liscor going north. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L298)
- {state} Later, Ylawes returned to the walls and told Yvlon he had a change of heart and would not abandon her. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L271-276)
