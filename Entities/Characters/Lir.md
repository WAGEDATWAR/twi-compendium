# Lir
> Type: person | Also called: Lir the mage | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.01H
> See also: Cervial, Gerald, Ceria Springwalker, Skinner, Yvlon Byres
## Overview
- Lir is a mage and one of the Silver-rank captains who joins the expedition into the Ruins of Liscor. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L52; Book 2, Ch2.21 L364)
- Lir is the only mage at the captains' table besides Ceria. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L282)
## Before and in the Ruins
- Lir shakes his head when Calruz blusters about commitment, and Yvlon winces. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L52)
- Lir tells Calruz that the captains agreed to come but are all equals and will go in properly or not at all. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L281-282)
- Lir wants to bring in good Silver-rank adventurers from the city and agrees with Cervial that they need time. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L283)
- {event} Lir raises his staff and casts [Wall of Flames], which burns a curtain of fire across the corridor against the charging undead. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L93-95)
- {event} Lir and two more mages raise [Ward of Safety], [Lesser Barrier] and [Sanctuary of Light] at the entrance, all Tier 1 and Tier 2 spells working together. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L99-103)
- {state} Lir's head emerges from the folds of Skinner's body of skinned corpses, and he is killed and absorbed like Gerald and Cervial. (Book 1, Ch1.02H L279-282)
