# Zamea
> Type: person | Also called: Shepherd Zamea; First Shepherd Zamea | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.08K
> See also: Flos, Teres, Orthenon, Esiela
## Overview
- Zamea is the leader of the Nomads of the Sky and the tallest of the half-Giants, with the strongest bloodlines. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L789)
- Zamea is about thirty feet tall, or ten meters, and the ground trembles where she walks. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L789; Book 15, Ch7.10K L371)
- Zamea holds the title Shepherd, and is called First Shepherd of the Nomads of the Sky. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L818; Book 16, Ch7.13K L80)
- Zamea's skin is rugged and mineral-hard, her eyes are pale orange, and she looks like a scaled-up Human woman. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L395)
- Zamea is twice as large as the average War Walker. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L80)
- Zamea wields an axe and her skin can stop steel. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L245,L250)
## Book 15: with Flos's army
- Zamea punches a Manticore in a battle, and the rest flee her. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L789)
- Flos calls Shepherd Zamea over to hear news of subjects arriving from Jecrass. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L817-819)
- {belief} Zamea tells Teres that half-Giants have skin like armor, and that she would break only after mountains do. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L426)
- {belief} Zamea says she is one of the last of the tallfolk and tells Teres that they are not equals, while lifting her in her hand. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L450-457)
- Zamea says the Nomads number only hundreds, while smallfolk are like dust, everywhere. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L453)
- Zamea carries a frightened Teres on her shoulder, choosing her steps with care. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L460-461)
## Book 16: the Belchan battle
- {event} Zamea strides against Belchan's first line, her axe gleaming, smashing through dirt, stone and pits as soldiers vanish before her swings. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L244-247)
- Zamea shouts for the smallfolk to bring her down for Reim in Flos Reimarch's name. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L248)
- Zamea is struck in the cheek by an enchanted javelin and is threatened by elite soldiers with enchanted blades. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L254-256)
- {event} Zamea uses [Cleaving Arc], widening her axe's blade, and cuts half of the elites apart. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L257-259)
## Book 17: Jecrass
- {event} Zamea, the First Shepherd, puts on a helmet and tells Orthenon to order his [Soldiers] back, as she will not stop until the enemy breaks. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L38-40)
