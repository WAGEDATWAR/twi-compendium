# Fool
> Type: person | Also called: the Fool; royal [Fool] of the Blighted King | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.03C
> See also: Tom, Erille, Blighted King, Nereshal, Lady Zekyria
## Overview
- The Fool is the royal [Fool] of the Blighted King, a small man in a colorful costume who entertains the court and its children. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L93,L251; Book 6, Ch1.04C L121)
- The Fool says his class is rare and there is scant work for a [Fool] in hungry villages, and he has served the Blighted King for over a decade. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L251)
- Before coming to Rhir, the Fool was a noted entertainer who travelled across Baleros and Terandria performing grand feats. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L121)
- {belief} Nobles at court think the Fool is high-level. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L121-125)
## Abilities
- The Fool juggles seven balls at once, weaving them into a double pattern. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L93-94)
- The Fool juggles twelve balls in the garden when Tom is seated with the nobles. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L120)
- The Fool has a Skill that copies objects, and the copies last a while if handled gently. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L232-234)
- The Fool says his copy Skill is good for pranks and trickery, and not for much else. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L234)
- The Fool has a magic scroll for talking to people, which he rolls up when Tom and the Princess approach. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L348-351,L364)
- Tom cannot hear the voice in his head whenever he is near the Fool. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L431)
## Court life
- The Fool performs for the Princess and other children, and Erille asks Tom whether he can backflip as the Fool can. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L340; Book 6, Ch1.04C L63)
- {belief} The Fool tells Tom that a plague killed the royal household's other daughter, and that the plague was thought sent by the Demons. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L299)
- The Fool tells Tom that the royal household once had seven members, and that two sons died in battle. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L299)
- The Fool tells Tom that assassins killed the last Queen and the one before that. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L299)
- The Fool tells Tom he was talking to his true love back in the courtyard by scroll. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L334)
## The Demon attack
- {event} A Demon slashes the Fool's belly open, the Fool grips the Demon, and the Demon kicks the Fool in the face. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L544-548)
- The Fool is taken hostage during the attack, and the nobles say he is regrettably injured. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L123-125)
- {belief} The Fool says the Demons are not right but are not wrong either, since they fight to live. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L294)
- {rel} The Fool and the Demon teleportation [Mage] stare at one another, and Tom realises the Fool loves her. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L333-334)
- Tom holds the Fool at the edge of the battlements as a hostage, and the Fool tells him the Demon mage will not betray her mission for him. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L335-337,L342)
## The Fool's rebellion
- {event} The Fool falls from a tower, lands on his feet, and breaks Nereshal's nose with a thrown juggling ball. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L406-413)
- {event} The Fool declares himself the world's greatest [Fool] and says he seeks the Blighted King's life for the king's sins and his own. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L414)
- {event} The Fool fights the royal guards with daggers, tripping Richard and tripping the Blighted Queen with a thrown juggling ball. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L419-423)
- {event} The Fool grabs a fireball and makes it vanish, keeping it hidden in a space of his own. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L423-424,L471)
- {event} The Fool catches Nereshal's lightning in his hand, juggles it, and throws it back, knocking Nereshal out. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L429-431)
- {event} The Fool holds a knife to the Blighted King's throat and demands the king tell everyone what he did. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L432-442)
- The Fool says he could understand the king's war crimes, but never the ritual and its cost. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L439)
- {event} Tom throws a knife at Erille to distract the Fool, who catches it and throws the daggers back at the king and Tom. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L458-461)
- {state} Tom kills the Fool with the Fool's own hidden fireball, after telling him he is a fool and Tom is a clown. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L468-472,L503)
