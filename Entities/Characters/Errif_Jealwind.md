# Errif Jealwind
> Type: person | Also called: Senator Errif | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.01
> See also: Pallass, Assembly of Crafts, Erin Solstice, Embria
## Overview
- Errif Jealwind is a Gnoll [Merchant] who heads the Merchant's Guild in Pallass. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L478)
- Errif is one of the Head Speakers of the Assembly of Crafts and wears a skullcap, the first hat Erin has seen on a Gnoll. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L477-478)
## Events
- Errif tells Erin the Assembly is considering impounding her door for its security risk and economic advantages. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L475,L478)
- {belief} Erin accuses Errif of lying about security and says he just wants her door because it is cool. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L516)
- Errif tells Embria that the Assembly will not act until a vote is called, and that Pallass is not at Liscor's beck and call. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L488-491)
- Errif says the Assembly is on break, and that Pallass's own [Strategists] will review Embria's report. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L491)
- Erin calls Errif a jerk [Senator], and Lasica says that Pallass backing the non-Gnoll candidate would bother his voters. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L758-759)
- Errif is a Gnoll [Senator] of the Assembly of Crafts who joins Pallass's delegation of four [Senators]. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L486)
- Pallass claims custody of the surviving Raskghar, some of whom will go with Senator Errif to the Meeting of Tribes. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L504)
