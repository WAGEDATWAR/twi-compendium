# Olesm Swifttail
> Type: person | Also called: Olesm; Strategist Olesm; Oresm (a misspelling on mail) | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.19
> See also: Lism, Erin Solstice, Pisces, Ceria Springwalker, Zevara, Ilvriss, Maviola El, Niers Astoragon, Antinium, Selys
## Overview
- Olesm Swifttail is a young Drake of Liscor, thinner than his uncle but taller than Erin, who plays chess and is the city's [Tactician]. (Book 1, Ch1.19 L76-80,L147)
- {state} Later, Olesm is Liscor's [Strategist]. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L159)
- Olesm is meticulous and careful, unusual among the Drakes Erin has met. (Book 1, Ch1.19 L90)
- Olesm has blue eyes patterned like falling raindrops. (Book 1, Ch1.37 L312)
- Olesm has light blue scales. (Book 2, Ch2.04 L497)
- Olesm is Lism's nephew, whom Lism helped raise. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L136; Book 11, Ch6.30 L587,L600)
