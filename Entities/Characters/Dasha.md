# Dasha
> Type: person | Also called: Dasaha | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.08T
> See also: Insill, Anith, Pekona, Toren, Ylawes Byres
## Overview
- Dasha is a short, part-Dwarf woman and an [Axe Fighter] in the adventuring party Vuliel Drae. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L194,L260)
- Dasha was raised by Dwarves and swears by a beard she does not have, such as "Burn my beard". (Book 5, Ch4.08T L229-234)
- Dasha has a bit of Dwarf blood in her family and is part Terandrian, according to Insill. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L262)
- Dasha often calls Dwarves her people despite having more Human blood than Dwarvish. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L336)
## Events
- {event} Dasha mocks the battered state of Toren's old sword, saying it deserves to be melted for scrap. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L229)
- Dasha and Pekona often argue, with Dasha grumbling about Toren's sword while Pekona is wary. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L261)
- Dasha insults Griffon Hunt and the String-folk, and praises the Silver Swords because they have a proper Dwarf. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L335)
- Dasha admires Ylawes Byres, the Silver Swords' leader, and wants to meet him. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L335-337)
