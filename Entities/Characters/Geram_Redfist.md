# Geram Redfist
> Type: person | Also called: Geram; Captain Geram | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.35E
> See also: Lady Rie Valerund, Reynold, Ressa
## Overview
- Geram Redfist is the brawny captain of Lady Rie Valerund's guard, a man who was an accomplished [Street Brawler] before she met him. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L268,L273)
- During the Goblin attack he carries no weapons but wears two gauntlets on each hand, and he beat a Hob to death with his bare fists. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L277)
- Geram tells Rie that the Hobs are breaking the doors bit by bit while a [Shaman] builds something big outside. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L278)
- Geram is bald and wears armor when he tries to follow Rie to Magnolia Reinhart's meeting. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L259-260)
- Reynold, Magnolia Reinhart's [Butler], intercepts Geram and pivots him out of Magnolia's path, and Ressa steps in front of Magnolia. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L259,L262-265)
