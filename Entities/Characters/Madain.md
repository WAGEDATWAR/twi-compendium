# Madain
> Type: person | Also called: Mad Madain; the Madman | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.00
> See also: Fierre, Ryoka Griffin, Alber
## Overview
- Madain is the owner and [Innkeeper] of The Huntress' Haven in Reizmelt, and people call him Mad Madain behind his back. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L23,L25)
- Madain is a huge burly man who sets out bowls of last night's soup for breakfast. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L19-21)
- Madain is a retired adventurer who was part of a Gold-rank team, though that is not the same as being certified Gold-rank himself. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L48)
- Madain opened an inn as many retired adventurers do, but has the opposite temperament to a good [Innkeeper]. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L48)
- Madain was a [Javelineer], and his arm is still strong enough for hunting. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L49)
## Running the inn
- Madain wakes his guests at his own hour, sometimes before dawn, then serves food and leaves his inn to go hunting. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L49)
- Madain calls Ryoka by her nickname because he cannot be bothered to learn her real name. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L52)
- Madain's inn survives on meat served almost every night and on cheap alcohol. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L329)
- Madain gets a lot of dining custom, and is in a good mood after a successful hunt. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L328-329)
## Fits and temper
- {belief} Fierre says Madain likes her and that his friends drink with him only because he is good for free alcohol. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L128,L132)
- Madain has a fit at least once a month, smashing things, fighting with friends and cursing until he passes out. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L90)
- Ryoka worries about Madain's rages, and says she has seen him throw his axes. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L34,L130)
- Alber calls Madain crazy but strong, and says he faced the inn's owner once. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L43)
## Later
- {belief} Madain tells Ryoka that Fierre's old office was destroyed by sewing needles, and guesses Ryoka's [Witch] was responsible. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L593-596)
- {belief} Madain says Fierre is fine and has some high-level defensive Skill, and may have used healing potions. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L599)
- Madain does not tell Ryoka that Fierre is fine, and grins when Levil points out he could have. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L597-602)
