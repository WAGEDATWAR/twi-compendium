# Himilt
> Type: person | Also called: Himilt val Lischelle-Drakle; Himilt Drakle; Lord Himilt val Lischelle-Drakle | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.01
> See also: Fierre, Colfa, Bamer, Rivel, Ryoka Griffin
## Overview
- Himilt is Fierre's father and Colfa's husband, and he dresses as a working farmer with muddy clothes and callused hands. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L102,L110)
- Himilt is a vampire and farmer who keeps sheep and other animals on the old Drakle estate. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L478-479; Book 12, Ch6.42E L226-227)
- Himilt is the most Vampire-like of Fierre's family, though he seems to Ryoka to be pretending to be normal. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L934)
- Himilt's neck has been swollen since about six years before Ryoka meets him, a symptom Ryoka compares to thyroid cancer. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L598; Book 19, Ch7.45 L14)
- Himilt casts no shadow. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L478)
## Meeting Ryoka
- Himilt argues that Ryoka should not have been abducted, and suggests letting her learn the family's secret. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L100-106)
- Himilt shakes Ryoka's hand gently. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L107-110)
- Himilt warns Ryoka he will find and kill her if she reveals Fierre's secret or puts her in harm's way. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L465)
- Himilt shows Ryoka the ruined estate, says the Drakles moved for thousands of years, and stands briefly proud before returning to a farmer's manner. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L474-480)
- Himilt tells Fierre that Ryoka is someone to watch, though he means it as a warning. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L281)
- Himilt holds a hatred of [Knights] who hunted down his family, according to Fierre, especially old houses like the Byres family. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L456)
## The Vampires' history
- {belief} Himilt tells Fierre it is better to be what they are, people in hiding, because Vampires once ruled part of Izril and fell. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L226)
- Himilt says Vampires once could change forms, cast magic and command lesser beings, and that the greatest feared only dragonfire. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L881)
- Himilt says a sickness began after a long war, that many powers were lost in the first generations and the affliction stabilised within eight generations. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L883-888)
- Himilt says Colfa has a rash because she became a Vampire late in life, and that Bamer, Rivel and Fierre were born with the signs. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L885)
- {belief} Himilt says Vampires committed crimes that made other species recoil in horror, but he would never accept that this is just. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L1313-1316)
- Himilt remembers with Bamer when House Byres' plan was put forward, so some places for Vampires to live are off the table. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L5-7)
## Fierre's illness and afterwards
- Himilt sits by his daughter's bedside while she suffers from fever, rash and pain, holding her hand. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L9-14)
- Himilt runs a great distance to bring Fierre packets of meat and blood for lunch and is embarrassed in front of Ryoka. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L33-37)
- Himilt says the family boiled water rather than drink from the well, now freshly topped up with silver. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L38)
- Himilt says he will try charcoal pills first, and refuses to flee since that is what House Byres wants. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L47-51)
- Himilt says the family bought breeding pairs from overseas and needs new flocks that were not raised in Izril. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L54)
- Himilt says there are several thousand Vampires in Izril, in scattered families, settlements, clans and towns. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L69-73)
- Himilt says Vampires of Drakes and Gnolls were hunted more bitterly by their own kind, and contact with them was lost. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L77)
