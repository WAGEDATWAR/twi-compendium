# Beniar
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Beniar; Sir Beniar | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.20E
> See also: Laken, Wiskeria, Durene, Gamel, Fabiel, Windfrozen Riders, Blacksky Riders
## Overview
- Beniar is a [Rider] who leads the Windfrozen Riders, one of two Silver-rank adventurer groups Laken hired to escort him and protect the village. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L26)
- Laken finds Beniar a chatterbox and is glad to be rid of his company. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L27)
- Beniar is a solid adventurer and Laken's cavalry leader who sometimes gives good advice, counted among Laken's advisors. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L215)
## Classes
- {state} Later, Beniar is a [Cataphract] and [Captain] in the service of Laken Godart, wearing heavy mail with a scale-barded horse. (Book 7, Ch5.11E L94-95)
- Beniar is a former Silver-rank adventurer who is now a [Captain] and [Cataphract]. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L425)
- Beniar wears full-body plate armor as a symbol of his class, and his steed wears mail too. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L405)

- {belief} Beniar tells Laken that average Goblins are no threat, but those around Invrisil are nasty because adventurers purge every tribe they find. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L333)
