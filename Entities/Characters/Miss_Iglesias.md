# Miss Iglesias
> Type: person | Also called: Iglesias Theroben | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.35
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Charlay, Lady Bethal Walchaís
## Overview
- Miss Iglesias Theroben is a female [Innkeeper] who runs the Rose's Retreat, an inn in Walta. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L7)
- Miss Iglesias is described as accommodating, strict, somewhat portly and very gracious. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L7,L12)
- Miss Iglesias told Ryoka she wanted her rooms not sullied by impropriety or untoward advances toward her all-female staff. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L7)
- Miss Iglesias thinks of Lady Bethal Walchaís as a paragon of virtue. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L8)
## The inn
- Miss Iglesias serves a breakfast buffet of goat's cheese, sausage and vegetables from her own garden. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L12)
- Miss Iglesias stands on no customs aside from forbidding hanky-panky and horrifically graphic displays of sex. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L24)
- Miss Iglesias' inn has no stairs, and she gets oats especially for the Centauress Charlay. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L72)
- Miss Iglesias shakes Ryoka's hand as she leaves, and steps aside when Charlay clatters into the inn. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L43,L67)
