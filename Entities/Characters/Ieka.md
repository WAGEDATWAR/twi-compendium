# Ieka
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Ieka; Lady Ieka Imarris; Ieka Imarris; Lady Imarris | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.30G
> See also: Tyrion Veltras, Magnolia Reinhart, Ryoka Griffin, Rie, Archmage Valeterisa, Salamani
## Overview
- Lady Ieka Imarris is a [Lady] and [Mage Lady], one of the few nobles able to perform true magic. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L27,L57)
- Ieka attended Wistram Academy, a rarity for a first-born noble scion. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L29)
- {belief} Rumour says Ieka was expelled from Wistram for unknown reasons after four years. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L27)
- Ieka believes in magic and has instituted magical reforms in her lands, such as self-drawing wells powered by magic. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L29)
- Ieka reclines in a chair made of Deguree Rabbit pelts and tails, among the softest substances in the world. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L18)
- Ieka copies good ideas from her peers, so her [Spellarmor Warriors] copy Lady Bethal's Knights of the Petal. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L403-404)
- Ieka's bodyguards are all women [Spellarmor Warriors], led by Captain Kelda, who wears thick ensorcelled plate and carries a mace. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L400-401,L438-439)
- Ieka hired the half-Elf [Aide] Zenna about two decades ago, copying the House of El's use of half-Elven guides. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L401,L405)
## Politics and Allies
- Ieka and Tyrion Veltras were childhood friends or acquaintances, though it is still poor manners for her to use his name. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L57)
- The Imarris family is a traditional ally of House Veltras, and Ieka is more allied than most. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L892)
- Ieka has fought the Goblin King and lost her sister at the Sacrifice of Roses. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L894)
- Ieka dislikes Magnolia Reinhart, thinking her shallow, manipulative and controlling, and believing Izril does not need her. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L891,L894)
- {belief} Ieka is certain Magnolia and Ressa are in a relationship, though she cannot prove it. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L67)
- Laken regards Lady Ieka as a noble who makes him nervous. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L164)
## The Circle of Thorns
- Ieka is a member of the Circle of Thorns, which asks only loyalty and treats private affairs as meaningless so long as they stay secret. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L892; Book 17, Ch7.24 L66)
- Hatred of Magnolia Reinhart is one reason Ieka joined the Circle of Thorns. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L66)
- {belief} Ieka believes past Circles fought off invasions from Chandrar and Baleros and humbled Khelt, but that the group mostly waxes and wanes into uselessness. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L38-39)
- Ieka has doubts about a Circle that tries to kill without hesitation like the group that slew Suvelta. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L105)
- Ieka is a higher-ranking Circle member with the power to pull strings for Lady Rie's requests. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L17-18)
## Private Life
- Ieka is attracted to women, a secret she keeps from the public. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L402)
- Ieka has an unhealthy relationship with her [Maids], many of whom are lovers, which is deviant by Izril, Terandrian and Drake standards. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L65)
- Ieka's fondness for [Maids] comes from Magnolia and Ressa. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L404)
- {reveal} Lady Rie tells Ieka that her new [Maid] Sidel is in the employ of Wistram, and Rie's [Gracious Invitation] stops Sidel from fleeing. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L117-121)
