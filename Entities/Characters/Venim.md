# Venim
> Type: person | Also called: Watch Captain Venim | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.02
> See also: Zevara, Erin Solstice, Kel, Pallass, Nevvim
## Overview
- Venim is a Drake Watch Captain of Pallass's City Watch. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L351; Book 11, Ch6.32 L406)
- Venim is Erin's appointed liaison, as is Sergeant Kel, and is assigned to the 8th floor every day. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L150)
- Venim has two days off a week, one of which is spent with his daughter, who is allergic to grapes. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L152,L161)
- Venim tends to have a bad day whenever he meets Erin. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L134)
## The door and the permit
- Venim and Zevara say the three Watch Captains of Pallass agree that a trade war or war with Liscor is unacceptable. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L351)
- Watch Captains of the two cities keep in touch and sometimes bypass the chain of command, and they will deny meeting Erin. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L354-356)
- Venim says a war with Liscor is not in Pallass's interests and that the door could be more dangerous to hold than not. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L362)
- Venim says Erin's earlier paperwork authorises her portal and inn as a legal residence in Pallass, and that clerks signed approvals by accident. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L385-390)
- Venim says the permit fees are twenty gold for both leases and six gold for inspecting a premise with monsters and magical artifacts. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L415)
- Venim expects a [Message] spell from Erin by way of Liscor within the week. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L6)
- Venim says he can tell the Assembly of Crafts that a Gold-rank team present most of the time will deter anyone from taking the door. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L11)
- Venim stands at the Pallass end of the door, which now opens onto a side street instead of an alleyway. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L219-220)
- Venim sits in his office with Selys and takes her statement after the thugs who kidnapped her are captured. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L175)
- Venim tells Selys that Zevara has requested her immediate return to Liscor. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L254)
## Goblins and the Antinium
- Venim tells Erin that any Goblin entering Pallass will be killed under standing Pallassian law. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L165)
- {belief} Venim says the law must be incorruptible but thanks Erin for her part in keeping Liscor standing. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L167)
- Venim tells Nevvim and Kel to leave Erin alone, since Pallass and Liscor owe her a debt. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L178)
- Venim threatens to close the door over the Antinium, and Erin shames him by calling him a scaredy cat. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L524-529)
- Erin grabs Venim's armor collar through the door and tells him Antinium are Liscor citizens until they break the law. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L532-533)
- Venim tells Erin that Skill-forced sales are legal in Pallass, since the buyer cannot be forced to name a price. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L592-596)
- Venim objects that Erin cannot change the checkpoint without permission because it is sovereign Pallassian territory. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L132-135)
- Venim says he would have granted Erin's request by day's end had it come through Sergeant Kel the next day. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L155)
