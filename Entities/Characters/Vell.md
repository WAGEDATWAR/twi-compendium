# Vell
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Vell | First appears: Ch6.30
> See also: Embria, Wikir, Pielt
## Overview
- Vell is a sharp-tongued, yellow-scaled female Drake [Captain] in 4th Company under Wing Commander Embria. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L31,L47)
- Vell's squad includes Lieutenant Kesa, a Gnoll who is her subordinate. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L31,L49)
- Embria threatens to feed unprepared soldiers to Captain Vell for breakfast. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L10)
## Habits and relationships
- Vell carries a pouch of fried green beans that she uses as a natural wake-up supplement when the tea runs out. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L36-39,L55)
- Vell says she bought the beans in the other Oteslia, not the Walled City, and fried them to get rid of mold spots as they began to rot. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L39-46)
- Vell says only her own squad gets to share her belongings and tells Pielt he should have bought his at Oteslia. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L60)
- Vell mocks Pielt's romantic attempts and laughs at Wikir's jokes about spiders. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L88,L135-142)
- Vell tells Embria that her squad fears being stuck in Liscor for another month with no action. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L68-69)
- Vell openly nods in agreement when Embria says Lism is not the right candidate. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L110-111)
## Skills and the Antinium
- Vell mutters "Yet" when Embria insists that the Antinium have no magic or Skills, which frightens Embria more than anything else. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L107-109)
