# Caddin
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.18H
> See also: Alais, Stan, Ceria Springwalker, Yvlon, Thunder's Solace
## Overview
- Caddin is a Silver-rank adventurer in the Adventurer's Guild, and a member of Alais' team, Thunder's Solace. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L400,L416)
- Ceria and Yvlon's old teams knew Caddin back in the day. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L400)
- Caddin is a young man who wears leather armour like the rest of his team. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L392,L559)
## Meeting the new Horns
- Caddin halts in shock when Ceria and Yvlon walk up to his team's table in the guild. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L384-387)
- Caddin jumps when Ksmvr extends an Antinium hand to shake, and shakes it gingerly, flinching at the exoskeleton. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L395-397)
- Caddin asks Ksmvr exactly what he is while Pisces listens to Alais introduce her team. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L415)
## The quarrel over Liscor
- Caddin snorts at Ceria's proposal to combine teams against a [Bandit] group, and says fourteen of them would face thirty or more [Bandits]. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L549-551)
- Caddin is angry at Ceria and Yvlon, and refuses to join with them. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L557-560)
- Caddin shoves back from the table and confronts Pisces and Ksmvr, asking if they know what Ceria and Yvlon did to their previous teams. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L567-573)
- {belief} Caddin says that Ceria and Yvlon both lost their teams, and that all the Horns of Hammerad died except Ceria. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L575)
- Caddin recoils from Pisces as if infected when Stan names him a [Necromancer], and says no one else would partner with Ceria or Yvlon. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L594-598)
- {belief} Caddin shouts that Ceria hid and Yvlon ran, while Yvlon retorts that everyone ran. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L631-636)
- Caddin spits on the ground after Alais says Ceria's and Yvlon's teams were the ones who brought everyone to Liscor. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L685-686)
- Caddin tells Ceria she will get no help from his team when she takes the [Bandit] bounty for the Horns alone. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L695-702)
