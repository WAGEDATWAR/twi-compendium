# Garen Red Fang
> Type: person | Also called: the Red Fang Chieftain; Garen | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.02G
> See also: Rags, Goblin Lord, Hobgoblin
## Overview
- The Red Fang Tribe's Chieftain, a Hob Goblin, leads sixty Goblins riding rust-red giant Carn Wolves and carries a red sword. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L67)
- He is a tall Goblin, around the height of a normal Human man and not particularly big for a Hob, covered in battle scars, with a gaze like a red beacon. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L70)
- His name is Garen, and Rags thinks of him as a legend in his own right. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L293)
- His sword is red-forged and far too well made for a Goblin. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L292)
## The campaign against Rags
- {event} The Red Fang Chieftain demands in the Goblin tongue that Rags submit to his tribe or die, Rags refuses, and he attacks. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L91-98)
- He takes an arrow in the shoulder without blinking, and the arrowhead snaps off, which Rags takes to mean he has some kind of skill. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L104)
- As Rags marches her tribe away he strikes it eight more times that day and again on the following days, until over a hundred Goblins are lost and half again as many wounded. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L123,L131)
- He stops his wolf at the last moment before a gorge where Rags has hidden boom bark and turns away, and he spots every ambush team she sets. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L143-146)
- The Red Fang Chieftain is warlike but not stupid, and he avoids fighting head-on. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L147)
- {event} In the duel both Chieftains hear a scream of a thousand Goblin lives saying that this is Goblin, while Rags also hears the voice of a King telling her "This is not who we are". (Book 2, Ch2.02G L239-251)
- Rags rolls into a pit, he leaps after her and is struck by a stake, and she breaks a jar of acid over both of them, then heals him with her last potions. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L253-257,L266,L272-274)
- {state} The Red Fang Chieftain tells Rags she is Chieftain, that the Red Fang Tribe will submit, and orders both tribes to stop fighting. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L281-284)
- Rags says afterwards that he let her win, and she is certain she should not have won even with a thousand Goblins against his sixty. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L290,L293)
## After the battle
- Garen speaks in the Human tongue and says he learned it long ago when he was an adventurer, claiming he became the strongest of a special party that trusted him and then killing them. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L312-322)
- Garen says he came to find a Chieftain to unite the tribes against a great enemy to the south, a bad Goblin, the Goblin Lord. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L324-326)
- {rel} Garen is the leader of his tribe and of several hundred miles of territory north of Liscor, and he calls Rags "Chieftain" the next day despite having part of his face melted off in the fight. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L9-11)
- {belief} Lady Magnolia says she very much doubts that Garen Red Fang will bow so easily to the Goblin Lord. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L66)
