# Jurix
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5
> See also: Illphres, Bastam, Ophelia, Qum, Ceria Springwalker
## Overview
- Jurix is a tall Dullahan mage of Wistram, and a member of the Isolationist faction along with Illphres and Bastam. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L333; Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L256)
- Jurix is a powerful mage specializing in combat magic and physical reinforcement magic. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L253)
- Jurix is cheerful for a Dullahan, and sets his head on the table to eat with floating plates and cups. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L334)
- Jurix prefers to eat with his head on his shoulders, which is an oddity for a Dullahan. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L205)
- Jurix knows Illphres' secret, the reason she covers her mouth when she smiles. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L344)
## Wistram
- Jurix sits at Illphres' table at the banquet and talks with her, ignoring her glare. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L333-334)
- Jurix takes one enemy ship while Bastam takes another during the pirate attack on Wistram. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L472-476)
- Jurix lands on the enemy ship's deck with impervious armor, striking pirates with [Tremor Impact] and pursuing them with [Flash Step]. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L480-482)
- Jurix introduces himself at a table with Bastam, Ophelia and Qum, five mages each holding influence within Wistram. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L253-256)
- Jurix laughs, puts his head on the table to shake Ceria's hand, and later shouts at her during spell practice. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L259,L386)
- Jurix gives Ceria a wand to practice with. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L386)
## The challenge to Zelkyr's Golems
- Jurix is one of a group of five, with Illphres, Bastam, Ophelia and Qum, who train to challenge Zelkyr's Golems. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L155)
- Jurix opens the double doors with his own hands as the five mages enter the room of Zelkyr's Golems. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L236-240)
- {state} Jurix is one of five mages who were alive less than an hour earlier whose remains Pisces finds in the room where the Golems will disassemble them. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L298-299)
- Jurix is remembered as brave, a true mage with courage, in Ceria's angry speech afterward. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L365)
