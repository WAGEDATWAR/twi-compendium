# Elena
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.14T
> See also: Cara, Aaron Vanwell, Ryoka Griffin, Feor
## Overview
- Elena is a young woman from Earth whose Greek name descends from Helen, and she is a [Beautician] by class. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L504; Book 18, Ch7.29B L96)
- Elena graduated from a university in Greece in a two-year program, and is older than Aaron by nearly half a decade. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L97)
- Elena is stunning, and brought a beautician's gift to this world, where her Skills made her one of the most attractive people Aaron had met. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L519)
- Elena fabricated the first beauty products by herself before Wistram's teams found her. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L520)
- Elena is a member, or rather an asset, of the Centrists faction under Archmage Feor. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L520; Book 18, Ch7.29B L97)
- Elena is one of seven young people from Earth in the Centrist faction's grasp, and is especially stubborn. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L104)
- Elena is better than Aaron at subtly influencing people. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L105)
- Elena is a friend of Cara, who found and saved her, and Elena was the one the band sent to Wistram. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L270,L462-464)
- Elena has a strong throwing arm and a temper almost as bad as Cara's. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L23)
- Elena has only recently begun to learn magic and has weak command of it, though she can cast [Light]. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L41-42)
## At Wistram
- {event} Elena fills a plate at the Wistram buffet with Aaron, teasing him about his shock glove, when all of Wistram's Golems freeze for about a minute. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L502-504,L522-530)
- Elena keeps her voice light about the freeze, then mouths the word Robots to Aaron, reflecting the Earthers' worries about artificial intelligence. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L531-535)
- Aaron and Elena consider Wistram full of idiots who have never heard of a robot uprising. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L537)
- Elena and some other Earthers think the Golems serving food are effectively slaves. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L509)
- Archmage Naili suspects the Ullsinoi faction leaked the cake recipe, which had once been a special treat available only among some factions. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L512-513)
- {event} Elena lectures a room of Wistram [Mages] that the world is round, using the shadows on the moons, an orange lit by a [Light] spell, shadow lengths and cliff height. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L37-68)
- The Wistram [Mages] stay unconvinced and many leave, and Magus Telim says her proofs are circumstantial. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L51-52,L55,L79)

- {reveal} Elena says she marched with Cara on a village out of hell ruled by an immortal [Witch]. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L466)
- Elena says that powerful people ruling the world and deciding how others live or die is what Cara was afraid of. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L466)
- {event} Elena, as spokesperson for two thirds of Wistram's young Earthers, demands better journalism from Feor and Viltach, saying the Drake commentators spread misinformation and downplay the disease. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1111-1123)

- Viltach dislikes Elena's tone, and when he mutters that the people do not need the truth, Elena looks appalled. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1127-1129)
