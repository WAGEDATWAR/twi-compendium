# Alime
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.00
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Reizmelt's Runner's Guild
## Overview
- Alime is a [Receptionist] at the Runner's Guild in Reizmelt. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L591,L599)
- {belief} Alime tells Ryoka that someone has told every Runner's Guild north of Invrisil about her, and not in flattering terms. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L592)
- Alime says she feels she owes Ryoka an apology over rumors that she thinks were wrong. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L592)
## Dealings with Ryoka
- Alime greets Ryoka cheerfully at the counter of the Reizmelt Runner's Guild one morning. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L172-174)
- Alime plans Ryoka a circuit of a bulk-letter delivery to Nonsfru, two deliveries in Ingli and a pickup at Enam. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L204)
- Alime estimates the circuit takes six hours, or eight with longer breaks. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L204)
- Alime warns that the deliveries are safe and non-priority and pay only six silver coins and eight bronze for the lot. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L207)
- Alime says bigger guilds sometimes take contracts from further away, so Reizmelt sometimes loses contracts to Invrisil. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L222)
- Alime says Reizmelt's guild loses funding when contracts go to Invrisil, so it needs its Wind Runner to stay. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L222)
- Alime asked Celum's Guild for a dossier on Ryoka, and says Ryoka is different now from what it described. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L225)
