# Teriarch
> Type: person | Also called: Grand Magus Eldavin; Grand Mage Eldavin; the Dragon; Starfire Dragon of Iltanus; Ward-Regent of Voltail; Lord of Flame | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.09R
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Ryoka Griffin, Az'kerash, Ivolethe, Dragons, Rags, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Teriarch is a Dragon who lives in a cave in the High Passes and first appears to Ryoka as a silver-haired old mage. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L16,L32)
- In human form Teriarch has silvery-white hair, a statuesque body, and eyes that are blue and violet like nebulas. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L16-23)
- Teriarch's human form is nearly six foot six with faintly bronze skin, white hair and pointed ears. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L266-267)
- In true form Teriarch is a massive bronze-scaled Dragon with a mane like molten bronze and mismatched eyes. (Book 2, Ch2.38 L246-250)
- His bronze scales look golden in the light, and his mismatched eyes are heliotrope and cerulean. (Book 2, Ch2.38 L247-250; Book 5, Ch4.31 L15)
- Teriarch is about the size of a passenger airliner in Ryoka's estimation. (Book 2, Ch2.39 L42)
- Later, the Dragon is called a brass Dragon with a copper-like mane. (Book 15, Ch7.00 L7-8)
- Teriarch lives in a cave of marble, art, weapons and treasure that he insists is a collection and not a hoard. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L7-8,L73-76)
- His human illusion still casts a dragon-shaped shadow, which he removes later. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L35; Book 2, Ch2.38 L156)
- Teriarch swears by "Tamaroth's beard" and "Darkscale's breath". (Book 2, Ch2.16 L90; Book 15, Ch7.00 L27,L66)
- Teriarch casts spells without speaking them aloud. (Book 2, Ch2.39 L324)
- Teriarch remembers ten thousand years ago, when four true Dragons flew, and watched one fall in the Creler wars. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L798)
