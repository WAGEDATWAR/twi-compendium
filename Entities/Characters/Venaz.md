# Venaz
> Type: person | Also called: Venaz of Hammerad; Venaz the Minotaur; Sir Bull; Sir Sea Cow | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.07D
> See also: Luan, Wil Kallinad, Feshi, Peki, Niers, Calruz, Marian, Umina, Fetohep
## Overview
- Venaz is a Minotaur [Strategist] who studies at the Titan of Baleros' school, in the Titan's private class. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L383; Book 11, Ch6.21D L51-52)
- Venaz is the tallest member of his class, and only Marian comes close to his height. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L138)
- Venaz says he is no [Warrior] but a [Strategist], and a [Strategist] to the core with only a few levels in anything else. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L383; Book 11, Ch6.23D L479)
- Venaz says he won the competition among everyone in the House of Minos under thirty years old for the honor of learning from the Titan. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L493)
- Venaz says he beat a Level 28 [Armored Veteran] in a duel with bared swords to win that place. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L497)
- Venaz says that he beat the [Prince] to earn a place with the mercenary forces of Minos. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L344)
- Venaz says Minos was too safe for him, and that being a [Strategist] is his ticket to joining the mercenary forces. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L344)
- Venaz speaks for Hammerad, and charges into battle shouting for the honor of Hammerad. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L176)
## Hiring Luan
- {event} Venaz offers the Runner Luan a job in a tattooist's shop, saying he used an artifact so that no one noticed him come in. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L348,L383)
- Venaz says those watching him will not report the meeting, and that Luan will go out the back after they are done. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L383)
- Venaz offers twenty gold to deliver a small object to him alone in a port city north of the city, and warns Luan may be roughed up if caught. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L424)
- Luan raises the price to sixty gold after Venaz insults him, and Venaz agrees to ten up front and fifty on delivery. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L433-440)
- Venaz shrugs off Luan's punch, which feels like hitting a mildly rubbery rock, and grins. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L416-417)
- {belief} Niers says Venaz has not read the signs and thinks the coming game will be a little event. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L539)
- Luan tells the Bushrangers that Venaz hired him for a delivery three weeks away and that the worst case is being lightly beaten and held for a day. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L775-778,L812)
- Luan says he checked and learned Venaz is a student of the Titan of Baleros. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L778)
## The Titan's game
- Venaz shows a Mossbear pelt to a teacher and says his team eliminated it and two others. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L57-58)
- Venaz says he contracted the Silver-rank team Thunri Dwarves to deal with a trio of Mossbears. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L91)
- Venaz argues that his strategy was sound and says he could block one volley with his [Arrowguard Formation] Skill. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L399,L402)
- Venaz favours a lead-from-the-front philosophy and argues with the Professor about it. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L506)
- The Professor forbids Venaz from fighting in training matches, and Venaz says he crushes opponents with his mind. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L506-507)
- Venaz tosses Luan over his shoulder and flees dock soldiers, and Luan and Venaz end up in a communal septic tank. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L957-968)
- Venaz is found wearing armor, and gives Umina anti-scrying and confusion ward talismans of Drath make. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L191,L569-573)
- Venaz casts painted paper beasts against Dullahans, saying the House of Minos and the Drath Archipelago fight with more than spells. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1044-1047)
- Venaz uses [Unit: Enhanced Strength] and [Unit: Unstoppable Advance] in the final battle, with a dozen Knights of the Summer behind him. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1286-1288)
- {belief} Venaz claims he found his own way to the end and will be the one to finish the game, which Feshi mocks. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1144-1146)
- Niers praises Venaz for reaching the end of the game unscathed, and gives him a reward. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L157)
- Venaz credits what he learned to the City Runner he hired and listening to sound advice from anywhere. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L159)
- Niers assigns Venaz to accompany General Felk, an expert in urban warfare, when Felk returns to duty in a month. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L164)
- Venaz asks whether Armor Captain Shailt is recovering. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L163)
