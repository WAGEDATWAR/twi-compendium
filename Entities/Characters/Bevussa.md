# Bevussa
> Type: person | Also called: Bevussa Slenderscale; Captain Bevussa | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.36
> See also: Keldrass, Bird, Erin Solstice, Issa, Zassil, Kin, Grimalkin, Halrac
## Overview
- Bevussa Slenderscale is a Garuda adventurer of the Wings of Pallass with spring-green feathers patterned with white and pink down her arms and legs. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L18; Book 19, Ch7.53 L705)
- Bevussa is adopted: her Garuda people live almost entirely on Chandrar, but Drakes in Pallass took her in after she was abandoned as a chick. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L23)
- Bevussa says she was born and raised by Drakes, and heard stories of the sky all her life from the library and from Garuda she met. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L163)
- Bevussa is Gold-rank and the real captain of the Wings of Pallass. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L209,L469; Book 13, Ch6.48T L290)
- Bevussa's feet are sharp talons, and she kicks with them. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L213)
- Bevussa has a recall Skill that lets her call her thrown sword back with "Return!". (Book 8, Ch5.40 L215-217)
- Bevussa plucks a feather from herself when she has stray ones she expects to lose. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L224)
- Bevussa notes that alcohol runs through Garuda faster than other races. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L4)
## The Wings of Pallass
- Bevussa says she got onto the team because she can fly, and that the other members are Oldblood Drakes. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L27)
- Bevussa's team members include Issa, Zassil, Kin and others, and Issa admires her greatly. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L209-217,L469; Book 15, Ch7.03 L316)
- {reveal} Issa publicly poses as captain of the Wings of Pallass, since it would not do for a Garuda to lead a team of Oldblood Drakes. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L469)
- Later, Bevussa introduces herself as only second-in-command of the Wings of Pallass. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L64)
- {reveal} Later, it is an open secret that Bevussa is the real captain and Zassil is the official Drake captain, for reasons of Pallass politics. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L699-702,L721)
- A Pallassian Drake strokes Bevussa's feathers at a reception, and Bevussa keeps a perfect smile while hating such events. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L705-707,L726)
- Bevussa tells Grimalkin she is only a "member" of the Wings, not Captain, outside the room. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L504)
## The Dungeon (Books 8-13)
- {event} Bevussa introduces herself to Erin as an adventurer sent by duty to the dungeon her team had held back from entering. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L20)
- {belief} Bevussa tells Erin the dungeon had been unnaturally quiet, with little action for her team. (Book 8, Ch5.38 L62-64)
- {event} Bevussa drops from the ceiling to kick a Stone Starer's eye while Issa and Zassil kill a second Stone Starer. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L209-217)
- {belief} Bevussa says Halrac is not far from Named Adventurer. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L503)
- Bevussa says she cannot stand the inn her team stays at, though Keldrass defends the Tailless Thief's food. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L510-512)
- {state} Bevussa is jailed for a day for punching people at the Adventurer's Guild over the lottery, with Keldrass in the next cell. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L281-285)
- Bevussa negotiates artifact shares with the Halfseekers and complains to Erin that Jelaqua is suddenly calm about gold. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L5-8)
- {event} Bevussa trades one plucked feather for all the goose feathers Bird holds, and he says he will treasure it forever. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L222-231)
- Bevussa tells Bird she has been known to snack on ants. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Bird L275)
- Bevussa tells Earlia's team that three horn blasts mean run for the exit as Shield Spiders come in waves, and one long blast means the Facestealer. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L313-317)
- Bevussa says the Facestealer rarely strikes, and that Gold-rank teams plan to pin it down and melt it at range. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L317)
- Bevussa tells Ceria the Wings are sticking with the dungeon, where the real gold is, instead of the Council's new bounties. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L41,L57)
- {event} Bevussa sets up the door to the purple Bloodfields stone and addresses workers at the edge of the Bloodfields. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L534,L556)
- {belief} Bevussa warns that all the Bloodfields' creatures, even the rabbits, are dangerous, and orders workers to stay two miles from its nearest visible edge. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L621)
## Views on Goblins
- {belief} Bevussa tells Keldrass she has fought Goblins as much as he has, but not all Goblins are bad, so not all [Necromancers] need be. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L1016)
## Battle of Pallass (Book 15)
- {event} Bevussa orders civilian fliers to land because flying makes them targets, and leads the Wings against Wyverns. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L315-322)
- {event} A Wyvern drops on Bevussa and she falls tangled with it as the other Wings fight a second Wyvern. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L326)
- Bevussa reports no casualties among the Wings, with Kin's torn wing expected to heal, and says she leveled from the fighting. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L507)
- Bevussa waits in a comfortable chair in a waiting room to be heard before Pallass's Command. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L502)
