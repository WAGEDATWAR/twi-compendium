# Grimalkin
> Type: person | Also called: Fist Mage; Grimalkin of Pallass; Muscle Mage of Pallass; Physical Magic Master; Sinew Magus | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.09
> See also: Erin Solstice, Rufelt, Lasica, Zevara, Ceria Springwalker, Pisces, Jelaqua, Archmage Feor, Cognita, Maughin
## Overview
- Grimalkin is a bulky, muscular Drake of Pallass who teaches what he calls physical magic as his own personal school. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L779-781)
- Grimalkin's class is [Sinew Magus], and he is nicknamed the Fist Mage. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L469; Book 11, Ch6.31 L772)
- Grimalkin is almost pure muscle from head to toe, with arms twice as large as a normal Drake's. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L200)
- Grimalkin declares himself Pallass' greatest [Mage], and says he has dueled Archmages. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L772)
- Grimalkin graduated from Fissival, but his unusual beliefs make him a pariah in the magical community. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L147)
- Grimalkin visited Wistram though he never attended it. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L147)
- Grimalkin emphasises physical enchantment spells, which let him fight on par with a Gold-ranked [Warrior] but make him neglect other magic. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L162)
- Grimalkin's motto is that a [Mage] should be able to keep up with [Warriors]. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L162)
- Grimalkin fought in both the First and Second Antinium Wars. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L581)
- Grimalkin has killed Adult Crelers, and still bears scars from one. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L646; Book 15, Ch7.02 L262)
