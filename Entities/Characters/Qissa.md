# Qissa
> Type: person | Also called: Watch Captain Qissa | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.03
> See also: Saliss, Pallass
## Overview
- Qissa is a Drake Watch Captain in Pallass who carries an enchanted spear. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L548,L556)
## Book 15: the Wyvern attack
- {event} Watch Captain Qissa tries to hold a dozen Wyverns that have occupied the 4th Floor, while her [Mages] fail to hurt them even with a [Fireball]. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L548)
- Qissa asks where 1st Army and General Duln are, and a Senior Guard answers that they are fighting below and working their way up. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L549-551)
- All twelve Wyverns breathe at once, shattering a magical barrier, and Frost Resistance potions fail to protect Qissa's [Guards]. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L553-554)
- Qissa orders her [Guards] to keep the Wyverns pinned until reinforcements arrive and decides to charge in herself despite attempts to restrain her. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L555-559)
- {event} A thrown jar of red oil bursts on a Wyvern, and the oil-covered Wyverns go berserk and attack their fellows, saving Qissa and her [Guards]. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L560-565)
- Qissa recognizes Saliss's voice and turns to find the naked Drake alchemist standing in the street. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L566-569)
- {state} Qissa survives the battle and is later seen working in a window of the Watch House. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L470)
