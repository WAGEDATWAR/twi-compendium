# Rémi Canada
> Type: person | Also called: Rémi | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.52K
> See also: Femithain, Flos Reimarch, Erin Solstice, Chandrar International
## Overview
- Rémi Canada is a [Reporter], later called a [Journalist], who writes reports on the war against Tiqr. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L773; Book 18, Ch7.34C L46)
- Rémi Canada is a young man from Earth who keeps a smartphone and uses a scrying mirror like a camera. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L894; Book 16, Ch7.13K L632-635)
- Rémi Canada is an Earther from Canada, listed alongside the Singer of Terandria as safe bets for being from Earth. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L167)
- {reveal} Erin pales at the surname Canada and the class [Reporter] and suspects the author comes from Earth. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L777-781)
## Reporting
- {event} Rémi Canada's article carried by the Mage's Guild, which disclaims its authenticity, describes the end of the Laughing Brigade and warns of Tiqr's likely fate. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L753-771)
- Rémi writes that he fears his articles have attracted a bounty on his head and that he will try to reach Oliphant. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L770)
- Pisces finds the author's report fascinating and is unsure whether the writer is male or female. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L775-778)
- {event} Rémi Canada watches from a hidden vantage point as Femithain advances, raising his smartphone while he cribs notes. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L894)
- {event} Rémi holds the scrying mirror like a camera behind Flos of Reim and Teres at Belchan, while a Garuda assistant whispers beside him. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L632-635)
- Rémi tells his assistant to stop whispering during the filming. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L635)
- King Raelt of Jecrass looks into the scrying mirror that Rémi holds, so that his words are heard by all. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L674)
- Rémi has appeared in Levrhine, probably to witness the battle, and broadcasts both sides with a night-and-day difference. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L735)
- Rémi watches the King of Jecrass raise one hand and produce a roar of sound. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1130)
## Chandrar International
- {event} Rémi Canada follows a group of Earthers into A'ctelios as one of fifteen who enter. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L337)
- Rémi Canada is organizing another newspaper while others ride south, and tells merchants, a [Mayor] and backers that Wistram does not hold a monopoly on news. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L44-45)
- Rémi argues that Chandrar needs a paper of its own, since with one or two papers the news can be controlled. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L45-47)
- {state} Rémi is the Editor-in-Chief of the newspaper Chandrar International, one of three international newspapers in circulation. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L4)
- Chandrar International has the weakest initial sales on the first day, but by day's end Runner's Guilds are asked for it. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L8-9)
- Customers cite layout, more impartial reporting, attention to detail, and the Editor-in-Chief's own fame as reasons to buy Chandrar International. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L9)
- Chandrar International has paid for itself, its equipment and salaries by the end of the first day. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L10)
- Rémi reports the Clash at Medain without the victorious tones of the other two newspapers. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L5-6,L15)
