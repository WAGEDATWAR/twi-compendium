# Emperor of Sands
> Type: person | Also called: Empress of Sands; Emira Saet-rei (one of her heads); Emperor/Empress | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.00K
> See also: Flos, Drevish, Khal, Orthenon
## Overview
- The Emperor of Sands rules the largest nation in Chandrar, an empire built in the years after the King of Destruction's fall. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L146)
- The Emperor of Sands has expanded the empire across the western nations in the last eight years, and the Zeikhal desert has stopped further expansion. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L232-234)
- {belief} Orthenon believes the Emperor of Sands wants to consolidate the west before expanding, and may conquer north and south rather than cross the desert. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L234)
- The Emperor of Sands rules from a silver throne, and veiled attendants fill the audience hall. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L134,L145)
## Nature and heads
- The Emperor of Sands is a Stitchman who changes bodies, appearing first as a young, impatient man with stitch-marks around his fingers. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L134-135)
- {event} Attendants cut the stitches at the Emperor's neck, and the head is sewn onto a new body behind a wall of cloth. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L167-169)
- {state} After the swap the Emperor of Sands becomes a tall, elegant woman with a low, soft voice who says she has a new perspective. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L171-173)
- The Emperor of Sands is a String-person who can sew and replace any body part, including the head that most String-people cannot replace. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L11-13)
- The Emperor of Sands styles herself [Empress] when wearing the female head and [Emperor] when wearing the young man's head, though both are the same being. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L9-10)
- The Emperor of Sands has nearly a dozen heads, all String-folk, including a young man who had been a fearsome [Lieutenant]. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L26)
- Another head of the Empress is a grinning, scarred head that makes her a genius but too ambitious, debauched and murderous. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L51-52)
- The Empress keeps a female head on a pedestal that she considers the most complete and reasonable, though not the best for combat or war. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L56-57)
- {reveal} The female head the Empress wears was once Emira Saet-rei of Doran, who vanished in a deal with the then-Emperor to protect her defeated folk. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L24-25)
- Doran was a small nation subsumed early by the Empire of Sands, and Emira Saet-rei had led its defence well. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L24)
- The Empress, when wearing Saet-rei's head, loves poor puns, which makes half her other heads groan. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L83)
- The Empress wears an old [General] head that she will not wear for a long time, regretting that she executed Drevish while wearing it. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L70-72)
## Skills and rule
- The Emperor of Sands has an empire-wide Skill, [Sanctuary of the Desert], that draws water from deep aquifers and springs into dry soil. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L203)
- {event} The Empress proclaims that a new oasis will begin at a chosen spot within two days, and her people cheer. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L201-202)
- The Empress remarks that an [Emperor]'s duties include more than war, though Chandrar thrives on war. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L204)
- The Emperor of Sands is advised by disembodied voices speaking from the ceiling and walls, who counsel patience against the Garuda. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L151-157)
- The voices tell the Emperor that war is not the only tool of empire, and he asks for a different perspective. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L161-164)
## Conflict with Flos
- {event} The Emperor of Sands sends Flos a box by Courier containing the head of Drevish, one of the King's Seven. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L125-127)
- {event} Flos declares war on the Emperor of Sands, vowing not to rest until he is dead. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L127-128)
- {reveal} The Emperor admits he should not have slain "the Architect" and provoked Flos so soon. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L159)
- The Empress says Flos used [Army of the King] at Reim and would have lost but for a grand enchantment on his city. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L67-68)
- {reveal} The Empress did not know that Drevish had created the grand enchantment on Reim, and regrets executing him. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L68-70)
- The Empress believes that to defeat Flos she must have him use [Army of the King] and then slay him before he can use it again. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L72)
- {event} The Empress resolves that the Empire of Sands will grow while other nations burn themselves against Reim, and she will strike Flos when the time is right. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L75-77)
