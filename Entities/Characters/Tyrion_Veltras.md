# Tyrion Veltras
> Type: person | Also called: Lord Tyrion; Lord Tyron (variant spelling); Lord Veltras | First appears: Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Jericha, Ullim, Hethon Veltras, Sammial Veltras, Laken Godart, Pellmia, Zel Shivertail, Circle of Thorns, Veltras Family
## Overview
- Tyrion Veltras is the scion of the Veltras family, one of the Five Families of Izril, and technically as powerful as Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L274)
- Tyrion commands one of the most powerful private armies on the continent, and some see him as a legend embodying the pride of Izril. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L2)
- He has defended Izril from every threat on its soil for decades and protects Humans, his subjects, first and foremost. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L3)
- Admirers do not love him, and others call him the closest thing Izril has to a tyrant. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L3-4)
- Tyrion is a war hawk as pro-Human as the worst of the Drakes are pro-Drake, and he sees diplomacy as a weaker alternative to war. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L4)
- Tyrion has a sense of honour: he does not use [Assassins] as Magnolia does, and he keeps his word. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L5)
- Tyrion fought against Velan the Kind and the Antinium and is known as the most powerful martial [Lord] in existence. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L244)
- Ullim reflects that only Lord Hayvon, Lord Belchaus and Lord Seagrass might rival Tyrion as a martial [Lord]. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L244)
- Tyrion says the Goblin King was a threat that could be dealt with, and he had a plan to bleed him by killing his Goblin Lords. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L100-103)
## Appearance, abilities and equipment
- Tyrion is tall, with black hair, a trimmed goatee, a clean-shaven upper lip and cold dark blue eyes. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L425)
- Tyrion holds himself strictly upright on his horse, and wears armor that glows slightly. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L425)
- Tyrion dresses in black, and the artifacts he carries could lay waste to a whole room. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L282)
- Tyrion wears rings that turn rain and arrows aside, though he dislikes rain as too nostalgic. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L11,L43)
- Tyrion's longsword, shield and armor are Veltras heirlooms infused with ancient magic. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L327)

- Tyrion rides faster than a horse and rider should be able to move, using the Skill [Lightning Hooves]. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L571-572)

- Tyrion has the Skill [Peilspear Formation]. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L386)
