# Belgrade
> Type: person | Also called: Belgrade the Tactician | First appears: Ch2.11
> See also: Anand, Pawn, Bird, Garry, Klbkch, Xrn, Erin Solstice, Yellow Splatters
## Overview
- Belgrade is an Antinium Worker and Individual, one of the five Antinium who survived protecting Erin from undead at her inn. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L231)
- Belgrade is a [Tactician] who was a Worker, and has four arms like other Antinium. (Book 3, Ch3.21L L33; Book 5, Ch4.13L L347)
- Belgrade is a stickler for tactics and the openings Erin taught the Antinium, and plays chess defensively and cautiously. (Book 3, Ch3.21L L40)
- Belgrade favours fixed formations and chokepoints, whereas Anand prefers roving strike forces, and Anand is considered the better player and [Tactician]. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L42-43)

- {state} After the battle with Skinner, Belgrade is still too injured to move. (Book 2, Ch2.11 L186-187)

## Defence of the Hive (Books 5-8)
- Belgrade holds the Hive's tunnels against monsters from the dungeon, using his [Battlefield Eye] Skill and sending Workers by tunnel code such as L4A and R98. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L344-355)
- Belgrade's front is made of countless tunnels, and he wishes he had a clear chess board to fight on instead. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L347-348)
- Belgrade faces Crypt Worms and Shield Spiders, and two suits of enchanted armor that fell into the tunnel with his Soldiers. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L345-346,L351-353)
- A Worker turns Aberration beside Belgrade, kills another Worker and cuts off one of Belgrade's antennae in the struggle. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L372-378)
- {state} A monster takes both of Belgrade's left arms in the fighting, and he feels no pain. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L391)
- {state} Xrn stops Belgrade's bleeding with a warm touch that closes his injuries, and then leads him on while pushing monsters back. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L418-419)
- Belgrade and Anand coordinate the Hive's defence in place of Klbkch and the Queen. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L36,L41)
- Belgrade reports that his static defences were overwhelmed in the lowest southeastern breach, but Anand had already redirected Pawn's division. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L39)
- Belgrade supervises Workers who dismantle tunnels and bring blocks of stone, an unusual material for the Hive, to reshape the area near the dungeon. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L19-22)
- {state} At Level 20, Belgrade gains the Skills [Simple Trap Construction] and [Basic Fortification Construction], which suit static defence. (Book 6, Ch4.42L L218)
- Belgrade credits Erin's lesson that practice can defeat talent, and says preparation wins battles in advance of reactionary tactics. (Book 6, Ch4.42L L226-228)
- Belgrade and Anand share a strategy room with a chess board, an improvised Go board and Hive maps. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L233-234)
- Belgrade nearly drowned two years ago when water burst through the Hive, and survived by holding his breath, as Antinium cannot swim. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L261-265)
- Belgrade and Anand take over management of the Hive's defences from the Queen, freeing her mental space. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L67-68)
