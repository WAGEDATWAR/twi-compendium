# Venitra
> Type: person | Also called: woman made of bone; Regrika Blackpaw (disguise); Fortress Blackpaw (disguise nickname) | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.24
> See also: Az'kerash, Ijvani, Kerash, Bea, Ryoka Griffin, Erin Solstice, Brunkr, Teriarch
## Overview
- Venitra is a servant of the Necromancer Az'kerash, a woman with pale skin and green eyes who reports to him in his castle. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L5-7)
- Venitra feels fulfilled whenever she speaks to her creator and feels his attention on her, and she dreads failing him. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L6,L15)
- Venitra was taught to mimic the gestures of the living, though she was never alive. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L12)
- Venitra was designed by Az'kerash personally and created from nothing by his mind, unlike Kerash and Bea, who were not made that way. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L124)
- Venitra is unique among Az'kerash's servants because she serves him and receives his commands in person. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L124)
- Venitra is the first-born of Az'kerash's creations and his loyal servant. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L418)
## Appearance and abilities
- Venitra is an undead knight whose skin, face and armor are all white bone, with a great sword of bone. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L315-316)
- When Ryoka first meets her, Venitra's eyes glow silver with rage. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L329)
- Venitra's body is an armored frame of bone that any Dullahan would envy, woven with spells crafted by her master. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L419)
- Venitra's face is a perfect carved face of white bone, and when she is unmasked her eyes burn green. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L567)
- Seen up close in the alley, Venitra's eyes are burning yellow flames set in her sockets, and she is as perfect as a sculpture. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L2-4)
- Venitra's voice is deep and beautiful, and Ryoka judges that she was manufactured to be perfect both as a weapon and as a work of art. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L20)
- Venitra can cast the spell [Mists of Madness], an enchantment her master built into her, which makes those caught in the cloud go insane. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L292-294)
- Venitra's sword and shield are enchanted bone, and her body is warded against lesser spells and attacks. (Book 5, Ch4.31 L28)
- In battle Venitra's mind is cool and calculating, and she thinks with perfect clarity amid spells and arrows. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L37)
- {belief} Venitra believes she was enchanted to withstand spells from Named Adventurers, and that Halrac's arrows cannot injure her bone body. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L36-38,L311)
## Book 2: the castle
- Venitra reports to Az'kerash that another being has breached the outer web of his castle. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L5-7)
- Venitra describes the intruder to Az'kerash as a human female, six feet tall, with black hair, an athletic build and highly magical equipment. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L10-13)
- Venitra does not mention the Frost Sprites her watchers saw around Ryoka, because her master long ago insisted they were unimportant. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L15)
- Venitra blocks Ryoka in a dark corridor of the castle, with a group of altered skeletons behind her. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L314-317)
- Venitra's skeleton troops have far too many ribs and redesigned frames that make them look like walking tanks. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L317)
- Ryoka's flour dust explosion goes off around Venitra, who strides out of the smoke unharmed. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L319-329)
- {belief} After Az'kerash lets Ryoka go, voices identifiable as Venitra, Kerash and Bea discuss it; one says his decision may be in error, and another says he never errs. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L402-407)
- The same three agree they should hold Ryoka until their master is finished. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L406-407)
- Venitra stands in the castle watching as Az'kerash's great flesh-and-bone creation finishes forming overhead. (Book 2, Ch2.41 L123-125)

- Zel notices a pattern of deaths or accidents following Regrika's travels. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L30)
- {reveal} An investigation traces Regrika's visits across six years and finds that half the cities she visited lost a notable person under dubious circumstances. (Book 10, Ch6.11 L390)
