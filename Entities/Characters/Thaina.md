# Thaina
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2
> See also: Chesacre, Purple Smiles, Mrsha
## Overview
- Thaina is a female Antinium Soldier, one of the two female Soldiers along with Chesacre. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1439; Book 19, Ch7.50 L159)
- Thaina's paint forms a whole with Chesacre's across their two bodies. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L159)
## Book 16: painting
- {event} Thaina and Chesacre wash each other, with Thaina dipping her cloth into a bucket, before they take their paint. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1436-1441)
- {event} Thaina and Chesacre paint a joint design of a silver building on each other's bodies, in the privacy made by blankets that Purple Smiles hangs. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1452-1457)
- Thaina sits next to Chesacre when Klbkch walks toward Pawn, and stands up after Chesacre's arm is cut off. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L758-760)
## Book 19: signing
- {event} Thaina uses Mrsha's sign language for the first time to say she and this one would like a drink too, pointing instead of using a name. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L163-166)
- Thaina is trembling when she signs a request to Mrsha for her words, and Mrsha agrees. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L173-176)
- Soldiers could never talk, and their identities came when they painted themselves, but they can have words. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L172)
