# Palt
> Type: person | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.57
> See also: Montressa, Beza, Isceil, Ulinde, Erin Solstice, Elusive Lot, Ullsinoi faction
## Overview
- Palt is a Centaur [Illusionist] and a [Mage] of Wistram on Montressa's team. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L810; Book 14, Ch6.61L L337)
- Palt is a [Fairday Illusionist], and good times are his specialty. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L50)
- Palt is also a [Smoker], whose class reflects that his life revolves around smoking. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L353-354; Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L318)
- Palt can exhale a cloud of smoke with a Skill so that everyone within fifteen feet shares his vibe. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L353)
- Palt says his smoke strengthens calming spells. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L50)
- Palt is an amateur [Cook] with only six levels. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L385)
- Palt is a member of Wistram's small Ullsinoi faction, led by the Elusive Lot, of under a hundred members. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L828; Book 14, Ch6.65 L164-165)
- Palt says the Ullsinoi faction is secretive and made almost entirely of the illusion school, and its members use nicknames or classes for master [Illusionists]. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L828-829)
- Palt's superior is a master named [Phantasmal Trickster]. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L813)
- Palt smokes cigars, dreamleaf, and a blue cigar of Mela's Kiss, a sea plant that gives off almost no smoke. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L812; Book 15, Ch7.06 L63-64)
- Palt can chew a nicotine gum and make a chewable dreamleaf toffee. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Burning_Alcohol L218; Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L329-331)
- Palt says Centaurs are nervous, spooked by thunderstorms, and that Centaurs, Lizardfolk and Dullahans leave mountains alone. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L99,L111)
- Palt can name four holders worldwide of the Inheritance Skill [Garden of Sanctuary], including Erin. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L164)
