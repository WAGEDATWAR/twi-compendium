# Therrium Sailwinds
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Therrium; Depth Captain Therrium; Therrium | First appears: Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships
> See also: Emerald Signet, Diamond Swords of Serept, Drowned People, Seborn, Wil Kallinad
## Overview
- Therrium Sailwinds is a half-Eel Drowned Man and a Depth Captain of the Undersea Crews, who commands the Drowned ship The Passing Shadow. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L431)
- The Passing Shadow is feared above and under the sea. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L431)
- Therrium has three sons, a ship of his own and a good deal of gold, and is used to risky ventures though he hates it when they fall through. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L434)
- Before he is named, Therrium appears as the unnamed half-Eel captain of a Drowned ship with a half-starfish [First Mate]; he can walk through lightning and generate it, and his fish-half is rubbery. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L365-366; Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L431)
- Therrium's enchanted sword shocks any enemy [Depth Sailor] whose blade it locks with. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L137)
- Seborn, a Drowned [Rogue] with a half-Crab body, recognises Therrium on a broadcast and the narration calls Therrium his father. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L524-530)
- {belief} Seborn describes Captain Therrium as a lawless [Thief], despised even by other Drowned Folk for not honouring his word. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L533)
## Shadowing the students
- The unnamed half-Eel captain of a Drowned ship follows the Emerald Signet, saying that everyone has heard the rumour of a treasure map and that whether it heads into Seagrass' waters or sails with an armada of storm-ships, they follow. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L363-367)
- Therrium's scouts find nothing in the first shipwreck that the landfolk did not find, and he curses the landfolk who plundered it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L430-435)
- A second Drowned ship follows the Emerald Signet along with his own, and its captain is the other [Depth Captain] who is making the same calculation as Therrium. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L447-448)
- Therrium watches the jellyfish attack on the Emerald Signet and then the giant crab, and he and the other Drowned captain decide not to take the guarded wrecks, saying "Nope". (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1044-1049)
- Therrium curses on seeing Rasea Zecrew's ships, since he knows her and her ship spells trouble. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1493)
## The Swords of Serept
- {event} Two Drowned ships clash, and Therrium defeats the other [Depth Captain], who surrenders her blade and signs a document placing her under his command for six months. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L136-142)
- The quarrel was over whether to attack at once and take the Swords of Serept, and Therrium insists on waiting for the final shipwreck. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L143-144)
- {event} Therrium, with the Sinking Light behind him, surrounds the Emerald Signet from beneath, and demands that the students surrender the treasure; he tells Wil that he does not intend to spill landfolk blood. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L500,L507)
- Therrium has the casket opened with an oar, sees the diamond blades flash, and takes the casket onto his ship. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L512-515)
- Therrium tells Wil, after the theft, that it is nothing personal and that the adventure was failed from the start. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L516)
- Therrium blows off the head of Fetohep's sand form with a wand and orders the chest lashed to the side of his ship, planning to dispel Fetohep's magic underwater. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L565-566)
- {event} Therrium advances with his Drowned crew on the deck of Prince Khedal's warship, and tells Wil that it would have been better if he had given over the blades at the beginning. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1135-1136)
## The chase and Zeres
- Therrium places a half-Blowfish [Archer] on the prow of The Passing Shadow, and Lasc warns that one dose from her arrows is deadly. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L791-793)
- Therrium gives the Emerald Signet a last chance to stop or die, and raises a hand as the archer sights. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L794-795,L799)
- {event} Zeres, a Walled City, fires on the Drowned ship with spells and its Drake navy sails out, and Therrium, in a rage, curses the landfolk and their Walled Cities. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1375-1380)
- {event} Therrium orders Glisal, a half-Blowfish [Venom Archer], to aim at the [Lord] and kill him, and she spits venom onto an arrow, which the narration calls his one last vengeance, and the ship then sinks under the waves. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1386-1391)
