# Redit
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.15R
> See also: Grev, Maviola El, Invrisil, Player's Retreat
## Overview
- Redit is a [Bouncer] who guards the door of the Players' inn in Invrisil. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L633; Book 17, Ch7.20 L1220)
- Redit carries a club, and keeps his hand on it when trying to be unobtrusive. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1223; Book 18, Ch7.30 L1107)
- Redit is a man strong enough to hold a boy in one arm. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L633)
## At the door
- Redit asks Grev whether he knows the Griffon Hunt, and Grev confirms they are the original Griffon Hunt. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1088-1089)
- Grev tells Redit that a boy trying to kick past him is a pal, so Redit lets him in. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L633-634)
- Redit stumbles back and flinches when Maviola El enters with fire racing up her arm. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1220-1223)
- Redit closes the door after the [Lady] walks in with the Earthers. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1240)
- Redit recognizes Maviola, lets her in, and blocks the mundane patrons. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L266)
- Redit is kicked in the rear by Maviola after he looks abashed at being passed. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L560)
- Redit has the door while the other [Bouncers] lock down the fancy inn. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L50)
