# Pawn
> Type: person | Also called: individual Pawn; Pawn of the Free Antinium | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.26
> See also: Erin Solstice, Klbkch, Ksmvr, Belgrade, Bird, Anand, Garry, Lyonette
## Overview
- Pawn is an Antinium Worker who took his own name after Erin Solstice told him he was an individual. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L194-205,L264)
- Pawn plays chess with Erin and has a [Tactician] class from the game, and later becomes the Hive's only [Acolyte], then a [Priest] who leads the Painted Antinium in prayer. (Book 2, Ch2.26 L359-362; Book 5, Ch4.13L L8; Book 14, Ch6.62L L136)
