# Emessa
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.01
> See also: Pelt, Maughin
## Overview
- Emessa is a Drake [Smith] apprentice who works for the Dwarf Pelt. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L414,L434)
- Emessa is the longest-serving of Pelt's apprentices, working under him for about a year, longer than the last six apprentices he had over that year. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L414)
- Pelt calls Emessa only "apprentice", and she wonders whether he knows her name. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L414)
## Under Pelt
- Emessa loves being Pelt's apprentice because his corrections let her avoid worrying about the quality of her blade, which ends up as good as Maughin's work. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L424-425)
- Emessa is willing to accept near-perfection, since Pelt makes almost no mistakes and only microscopic flaws. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L426)
- Emessa adores her master and does not understand why other smiths mock him and other apprentices scorn her. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L435)
- Emessa is a Drake girl too afraid to be her own [Smith]. (Book 15, Ch7.01 L436)
## Book 18: Esthelm
- {event} Emessa is one of three apprentices who follow Pelt out of Deríthal-vel, leaving her home without hesitation. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L234)
- Emessa walks taller after Pelt lit a spark in her soul. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L234)
- Emessa tells Pelt that they could make a proper forge to his specifications even in the inn. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L290-293)
