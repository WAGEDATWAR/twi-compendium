# Ulinde
> Type: person | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.57
> See also: Montressa, Palt, Beza, Isceil, Halfseekers, Jelaqua, Seborn, Moore
## Overview
- Ulinde is a Selphid [Spellslinger] of Wistram who fights with two dueling wands. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L438; Book 14, Ch6.64 L546,L566)
- Ulinde is a dangerous duelist, regarded by Palt as good as Isceil. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L546)
- Ulinde's gender follows the Selphid's body, so Ulinde is female when wearing a female Drake's body and male in a male one. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L377)
- Ulinde's Selphid faction is tied to the Revivalists. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L560)
- Ulinde grows up on tales of the Halfseekers in Selphid communities and admires them. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L768-773)
## Book 13: The Wistram Team
- {belief} Ulinde guesses that Palt learned the spliff from the guests. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L770)
- Ulinde pushes her body to move faster than all but Seborn when she Rampages, and she survives Seborn's flaming dagger stabs, which perforate her chest. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L404-406,L438,L444)
- Ulinde uses her second dueling wand on Seborn's chest with [Reduced Fireball] while Rampaging. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L444-445)
