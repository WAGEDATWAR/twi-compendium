# Delivan
> Type: person | Also called: First Mate Delivan; Mister Delivan | First appears: Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1
> See also: Lasc, Wil, The Emerald Signet
## Overview
- Delivan is the [First Mate] of The Emerald Signet, under Captain Lasc, a [Storm Captain]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L165,L271,L477,L547)
- Delivan is one of the crew whom Wil Kallinad knows by name, along with Captain Lasc. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L165)
- Delivan carries a sword and a miniature crossbow at his sides. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L542)
- Delivan has a nastily precise aim with the hand-crossbow he carries. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L626)
- Delivan has a [Dangersense] Skill, which Lasc asks him about when something seems off. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L965-966)
- Delivan uses the few ship-spells Lasc has available through his bond as [First Mate]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L618)
## Aboard The Emerald Signet
- Delivan tells the bored students that the ship is nearly at the next spot and that Captain Lasc wants to know who will watch. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L270-271)
- Delivan shows the other students how to calculate their course during a small lesson in Lasc's cabin. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L477,L482)
- Delivan tells Merrik that [Storm Sailors] fight as well as any landfolk, and asks to try Merrik's armour. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L542-545)
- Delivan assures Wil that Captain Lasc will not lose a duel, and nods to Peki and Merrik who bet on the outcome. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L589-592)
- Delivan grins at Wil when Lasc's fist lays Venaz flat and unconscious in their duel. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L615-616)
## Sea battles
- Delivan stands in front of the cabin with a wand, rallying the [Sailors], when slugs swarm the deck. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L764)
- Delivan warns Lasc that thousands of slugs in the water might sink the ship. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L821-823)
- Delivan steers the ship when the crew approaches the wreck site, and Lasc asks him to take them away at the first sign of danger. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L921,L965-970)
- Delivan's eyes go round when the name The Archmage's Favor is seen on the shell of a giant hermit crab. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L986-988)
- Delivan turns the ship and steers out of range of the crab's claws. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L990-992)
- {event} Delivan swears when over two thirds of the crew draw their blades in mutiny against Lasc. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L441-445)
- Delivan fights beside Yerranola and Feshi against the mutineers. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L468)
- Delivan takes the helm and steers into the storm at Wil's call to escape pursuing ships. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1261-1262)
- Lasc trades places at the helm with Delivan and then Inky as they break clear of the storm. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1343)
