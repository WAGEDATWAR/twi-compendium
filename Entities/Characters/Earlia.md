# Earlia
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Earlia; Earlia of Gemhammer | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.27
> See also: Nailren, Numbtongue, Bevussa, Facestealer
## Overview
- Earlia is the Captain of Gemhammer, a Silver-rank adventuring team from the north made up of six Humans. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L300,L356)
- Earlia carries a sledgehammer on her back, and her team carries ropes, crossbows, swords and a net. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L300)
- Earlia is a former [Miner] who knows gold, and her team of former miners became adventurers after monsters attacked their shaft. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1051,L1076)
- Earlia calls herself a former [Mining Leader] when she asks Numbtongue about mining. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L20)
- Gemhammer's members have strength Skills and heavy weapons, and Earlia fights with a warhammer. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L715,L717)
## Arrival and the Dungeon (Books 8-13)
- {belief} Earlia says Gemhammer heard rumours of an inn near Liscor with Hobgoblin guards and a magic doorway. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L324)
- Earlia thought she would have to travel four more days to reach Liscor when the team heard the inn rumours. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L324)
- {event} Earlia's team is ambushed by Raskghar in the dungeon and a masked woman saves them by slashing the Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L443-445)
- {event} A Raskghar archer's invisible arrow strikes Earlia through her chainmail and she falls as Goblins appear behind the Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L448)
- Earlia's shoulder is healed but tender, and Gemhammer rests after the ambush. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L124)
- Earlia says Gemhammer is a dungeon team and is chummy with the Pride of Kelia. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L126)
- Earlia plans to return to the dungeon at once with proper equipment, since the layout is now known. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L126,L129)
- {belief} Earlia believes the Raskghar have no Skills and can be prepared for, and she wants the armoured Raskghar and the invisible bow. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L129)
- Earlia wonders how the masked woman survives in the dungeon and suggests bringing her extra supplies. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L131)
- {event} Earlia wins the scramble for a gold nugget thrown to the adventurers in the inn, ending with a swollen lip and two bruises. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1067)
- Earlia judges the nugget to be a substantial sum, as she knows gold from her time as a [Miner]. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1051)
- {belief} Earlia tells the table of the Golden Goblin, an adventurers' legend from north of Celum, as a myth like seeing a Unicorn. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1073)
- {belief} In Earlia's telling, teams who nearly caught the Golden Goblin saw him drop treasure and flee while they scrambled for it. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1079-1082)
- {belief} In Earlia's telling, the dropped treasure sometimes led to avalanches, pitfalls or a Creler's nest, and the tale popped up in distant places. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1085-1087)
- Earlia leads Gemhammer in prying enchanted trap blades from de-trapped dungeon rooms, and her team trades with Anith. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L148,L228-235)
- Earlia estimates the enchanted trap blades at forty gold pieces each or ten times that if keenly enchanted, then at least six-fifty each after a bone test. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L232,L235)
- Earlia says her team were [Miners] and that adventuring pays better for about the same danger. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L191)
- {event} Gemhammer cracks a trap-room wall and harvests fire-elemental magicore, which explains why the room heats up so fast. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L285-286)
## The Facestealer (Book 17)
- {event} Earlia's team and Nailren's Pride of Kelia explore the dungeon together as Silver-rank teams that cannot advance as fast as the Gold-ranks. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L718)
- {event} Earlia finds a gold ring with a sapphire on a diseased Raskghar corpse, and her team cuts it from the finger. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L728-734)
- Gemhammer also finds a bag of cut gemstones and an enchanted-looking knife on the Raskghar. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L739-748)
- Earlia suspects the ring is an armour enchantment, since the Raskghar had something like armour made of water. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L736)
- {event} Both Silver-rank teams meet the Facestealer, which holds severed heads on sticks around a corner, and Nailren orders them to spread out. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L761-776)
- {event} Both teams are paralyzed by the Facestealer, and Earlia sobs, until an explosion through the dungeon distracts it. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L798-807)
## Mining with Numbtongue (Book 19)
- {event} Earlia asks Numbtongue for good mining spots while Gemhammer is on break from the dungeon, after Fea gets hurt removing a trap. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L17-23)
- Earlia offers Numbtongue a fifteen to twenty percent cut of what Gemhammer mines and asks to borrow the door. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L27)
- {event} Numbtongue points Earlia to a good vein in the cave by the door and warns of what may be acid maggots. (Book 19, Ch7.43G L28-32)
