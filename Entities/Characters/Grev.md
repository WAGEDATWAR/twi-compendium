# Grev
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.37
> See also: Jasi, Wesle Salkis, Players of Celum, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Grev is a scrawny, underfed boy in Celum who looks no more than thirteen years old. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L180)
- He has a charming gap-toothed smile that makes Erin like him at once. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L178,L180)
- Grev lives with the Drake Jasi in a squalid hut next to Celum's walls. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L370)
- The hut belonged to their parents, both of whom were dead or had simply left. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L370)
## Meeting Erin
- {event} Grev offers to guide Erin to an [Alchemist] when she enters Celum to sell her bees. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L177,L181)
- {event} He leads her down a side street into an alley where three armed men in dark clothes wait. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L192)
- He takes a few silver coins from one of the men and runs when Erin accuses him of tricking her. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L194-195)
- He still tells the men not to hurt her, saying she carries the money in a pouch. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L196)
- {event} Erin spots him cutting a young woman's purse in a shop queue, and he runs from her in terror. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L321-326)
- Erin chases Grev through Celum's poorer streets and catches him by the hand outside a door. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L329-332)
- Grev shrinks away with an arm raised as if he fears being hit. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L333)
- Jasi shields him and begs Erin not to report him, saying he is one crime away from being exiled from the city. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L337-338,L351)
- Erin tells Jasi that Grev helped a group of thugs ambush her when she first came into the city. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L354)
- Grev denies the pickpocketing, but Jasi knows he is lying. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L347-350)
## Dreams and the Players of Celum
- Erin feeds Grev to the point of bursting to calm his restlessness, and he can barely finish his last bite of hash browns with cheese. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L200)
- {belief} Asked what he would do if he could have any class, Grev says he would be a famous adventurer and a Named Adventurer. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L231-232)
- He adds that he would carry a flaming sword and slay Dragons, while Jasi asks Erin not to encourage him. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L232-234)
- Grev joins the staged Romeo and Juliet with faulty lines and overenthusiastic acting. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L475)
- Grev runs around the Players of Celum carrying a rucksack full of props, costume changes and scripts. (Book 3, Ch3.25 L96)
- The bag Grev carries has to be emptied now and then because the actors' cut of the profits fills it. (Book 3, Ch3.25 L175)
- {event} Grev hovers backstage with water and scripts during the rehearsal of My Fair [Lady]. (Book 4, Ch3.39 L289)
- Jasi asks Erin to let her buy a gift for Grev in the secret gift exchange. (Book 4, Ch3.39 L296)
## Street Rat
- {fact} Later, Grev says he is a [Street Rat] with a Skill that lets him find out street news. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L569)
- {event} He tells Jasi and Wesle that Greenblade's Boys, a new gang, are the group that attacked Kilkran and Andel. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L563-569)
- He explains that flash gangs run in, take what they can and run out, and that Bandits are all flash. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L572)
- He says builders give back to what they take so the take is bigger, and protect their clients, like the Brothers. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L575)
- He says rats are never noticed and strike rarely, and viners are gangs as big as the Watch, like the Sisters. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L575)
- He says Celum's Brothers and Sisters both got wiped out in the bad job with Miss Erin's inn. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L575)
- {event} Grev is among the Players of Celum who arrive at The Wandering Inn when riots cancel the evening's play. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L39)
