# Termin
> Type: person | Also called: Mister Termin | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.34
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Horns of Hammerad, Zel Shivertail
## Overview
- Termin is a [Wagon Driver] who tells Pisces that wagon drivers commonly have Skills like [Continuous Rolling], since around there one moves fast or gets eaten. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L509)
- Termin drives a cart at the head of a convoy of more than a hundred adventurers, [Guards], [Healers], [Masons] and [Hunters]. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L504)
- {belief} Termin says no army the Human cities could assemble would be half as powerful as one led by Zel Shivertail with Gold-rank teams and Antinium Soldiers. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L512)
- Termin takes the Horns of Hammerad to Esthelm, then heads north as fast as he can, preferring to be closer to Invrisil if a Goblin Lord is coming. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L283,L293)
- {state} Later, Termin says he levelled up after Ryoka rescued him from Wailer Frogs, and owes her a bit of a debt. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L17-19)
- Termin runs a passenger shuttle with goods, with Invrisil as his last stop, pulled by two mules. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L16,L20)
