# Bastam
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5
> See also: Illphres, Jurix, Ophelia, Qum, Cognita, Ceria Springwalker
## Overview
- Bastam is a Drake [Mage] of Wistram Academy and one of the five allies of Illphres. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L472; Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L253,L276)
- Bastam is the oldest of Illphres' group of five mages, in his sixties, although he does not look it. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L255)
- Bastam is an Isolationist, like Jurix and Illphres. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L256)
- Bastam has a tail that wags faster when he is tense, even when he otherwise appears calm. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L201)
## Fighting the pirates
- Jurix says he will message Bastam and Ophelia so the group can teleport to the fight, which will take several leaps. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L367)
- Bastam arrives with Jurix and Ophelia in the air above the sea, and lands on the choppy water as if it were solid ground. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L471-472)
- Bastam stands on the ocean waves with Ophelia and hurls [Fireball] and [Lightning Bolt] spells hundreds of meters at a pirate ship. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L484)
## The challenge to the Golems
- Bastam angrily asks Illphres why she has not told her apprentice Ceria what the group is about to do. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L268-269)
- Bastam is named by Jurix as one of five mages who will challenge Cognita at the end of the year. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L276)
- Bastam trains on the rocks by the ocean at dawn with Jurix, Ophelia and Qum, while Illphres trains separately with Ceria. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L332)
- Bastam took three tries to work out that one of the Golems was a Flesh Golem enchanted to look like a Shadow Golem. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.6 L367)
- Bastam warns Jurix not to eat too much before the challenge, as the mages will probably be moving around a lot. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L200-201)
- Bastam eats sparingly from a bowl of oats and honey on the morning of the challenge. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L201)
- Bastam tells Ceria before the challenge that her exam showed courage and daring, which older mages have forgotten. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L218-219)
- {state} Bastam dies in the challenge, and his remains are laid out in the catacomb room with those of Illphres, Jurix, Ophelia and Qum. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L298-299)
- A Golem reaches down for Bastam's face in the catacomb room as Pisces hesitates over what to do. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L299-302)
- Ceria later cites Bastam, with Illphres and Jurix, as having shown the courage of true mages. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L365)
