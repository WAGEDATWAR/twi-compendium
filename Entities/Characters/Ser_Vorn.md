# Ser Vorn
> Type: person | Also called: Sir Vorn | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Cirille, Merish, Commander Uxel, Delezza, Viri
## Overview
- Ser Vorn is a [Knight] of the Order of the Thirsting Veil, deployed to Rhir to honour ancient pacts. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1015)
- Ser Vorn is decades older than the other officers at the 5th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L695)
- Ser Vorn's Order of the Thirsting Veil does not induct non-Humans. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L894)
- Ser Vorn is an even-tempered man with a good sense of humour, normally a font of patience. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1238; Book 18, Ch7.35C L347)
## At the 5th Wall
- Ser Vorn introduces himself to Commander Cirille after his knights sing of their homeland, and says the singing has been cleared with Bastion-General Quiteil. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1015-1018)
- Ser Vorn's knights are held back for heavy engagements, and he hopes to work harmoniously with Cirille's forces. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1018)
- Chief Warrior Merish asks Ser Vorn not to have his knights sing before morning, and Ser Vorn is visibly taken aback by the request. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1020-1024)
- Delezza says Ser Vorn is sworn to slay undead, which becomes a point of argument when necromancers join the defence of the 5th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1191)
- Ser Vorn joins the officers' card games and has a terrible tell, his fingers dancing on his cards, and half the table folds the moment he smiles at a hand. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1208,L1226-1228)
- Ser Vorn loses three gold coins in one hand and takes it in good humour, and later loses five times as much as Cirille in one night. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1238,L1303)
- Ser Vorn says the 5th Wall must be defended, and calls it a different war from the one he prefers but one he respects. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1249-1250)
- Ser Vorn's knights are mounted and ready to reinforce any group in trouble at the wall. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1322)
- Ser Vorn's knights serve as the heavy cavalry that no other species at the wall has. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L579)
- Ser Vorn is discouraged when the joint Gnoll and Drake skirmish exercises go poorly, saying he cannot understand why the two species fight. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L347-348)
- Ser Vorn dodges all the Drake spear formations in one skirmish exercise and carves up their archers. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L332)
- Ser Vorn rides to complain to Cirille after a clash with Drake [Riders] leaves paint all over his armour. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L555-558)
- Ser Vorn declines a night of drinking with Uxel and Merish, citing an aging body. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L584)
- Ser Vorn assures Cirille that a tall armoured figure among the group approaching the wall is Human, not a half-Giant. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L716-717)
- Ser Vorn is astonished when Cirille suggests bringing Lizardfolk to Izril, and says his Order is no stranger to crusades abroad. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L893-894)
- Ser Vorn notes that Ailendamus would never send him as a mercenary, though other knights of his Order might be interested. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L894)
## The battle and death
- Ser Vorn leads the Order of the Thirsting Veil and four other Knight Orders in a charge into the Demons' flank. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L595)
- After nearly two dozen charges, Ser Vorn calls the disengage, as a fifth of his knights have fallen. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L598)
- Ser Vorn charges a Demon commander on horseback with a spear, and Uxel hurls a scimitar as the Demon's glaive spins towards Ser Vorn. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L839,L856-858)
- Ser Vorn is struck by a heavy, fast projectile fired by a young human woman, whom the Demon calls Flora, and Cirille later sees him fall. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L876-881,L899)
- Ser Vorn is later counted among the fallen, a casualty of an encounter that only Cirille and Uxel witnessed. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1287)
- Ser Vorn's ghost walks forward in formation with the other fallen defenders of the 5th Wall, glancing back at his body. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1326)
- Ser Vorn kneels before his [King], answering frantic questions until he vanishes. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1382)
