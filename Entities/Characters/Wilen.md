# Wilen
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Ch1.00C
> See also: Tom, Troll
## Overview
- Wilen is a boy in the village where Tom's group stops. He is too young for a job beyond helping his parents but a bit too old to play with the other kids. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L213)
- Wilen asks Tom whether he plans to leave, and Tom says not unless the King sends for them. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L214-215)
- Wilen asks how [Clowns] level up, wondering whether juggling pins for long enough does it, and notes that [Tumblers] level up if they get a large crowd. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L221)
- Wilen is native to this world and seems perfectly at ease in talking to Tom. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L220)
## Death
- {event} Badly wounded, Wilen tells Tom that he is going to die and that Tom should run because a Troll is coming. (Book 2, Ch1.01C L11-12)
- Wilen holds out a kitchen knife that he must have used to defend himself, and it falls from his fingers. (Book 2, Ch1.01C L15-16)
- Wilen asks Tom for a joke, and Tom tells him one about a fat [Clown] sitting in front of a Troll, with the punchline "the main course". (Book 2, Ch1.01C L17-28)
- Wilen giggles at the joke, saying it is good, and then dies in Tom's arms mid-sentence. (Book 2, Ch1.01C L29-31)
- {state} Wilen is dead. (Book 2, Ch1.01C L31)
- Later, Tom says he had enough of fighting after the night Wilen died. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L126-127)
