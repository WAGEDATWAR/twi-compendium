# Seer of Steel
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.06D
> See also: Tulm, Xol, Iron Vanguard
## Overview
- The Seer of Steel is the leader of the Iron Vanguard, a company of northern Dullahans. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L608-612)
- {belief} Ken says the Seer of Steel is a [Juggernaut] whose body is made of the most expensive armor money can buy. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L612)
- {belief} Geneva says the Seer of Steel never goes into battle with his head, but leaves it in his fortress and controls his body through a scrying orb. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L616)
- {belief} Geneva says that way only his body can be hit, so he is practically impossible to kill, or so it is said. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L616)
## Dealings with Tulm and Xol
- The Seer of Steel speaks through a scrying orb in a booming, rumbling voice, in capital letters. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L551-553)
- The Seer of Steel tells Tulm not to regret a defeat, because he quite enjoyed it. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L550-552)
- The Seer of Steel tells Tulm to uncover the Titan's answer, saying he will await him in person. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L593)
- The Seer of Steel says that if the question is worth answering, then it is worth almost any cost, and tells Tulm to pay it. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L595-596)
