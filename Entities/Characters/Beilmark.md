# Beilmark
> Type: person | Also called: Senior Guardswoman Beilmark | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.54
> See also: Jeiss, Klbkch, Zevara, Tkrn, Relc
## Overview
- Beilmark is a Gnoll Senior Guardswoman of the Liscor Watch. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L54-55; Book 14, Ch6.61L L684-692)
- Beilmark is paired with the Senior Guardsman Jeiss on the walls and bets with him on chases. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L684,L733-738)
## Events
- {event} Beilmark's patrol catches Rabbiteater, and she reports to Olesm that the Hobs did not draw steel but a fifth Hob escaped. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L54-59)
- {event} Beilmark bets five silver that the little bee escapes the pursuit, and welcomes a junior [Guardsman] to the Watch with Jeiss. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L733-738)
- Beilmark takes reports at the desk, and swears at news that Soot has the criminal Bearclaw, lamenting Relc is not Klbkch's partner. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L684-687)
- Beilmark says she will alert the patrols and take the report to Captain Zevara, who will be displeased at a Wistram visit. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L689-697)
- {belief} Beilmark calls Antinium "big kids" but doubts they are really children. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L1025-1028)
