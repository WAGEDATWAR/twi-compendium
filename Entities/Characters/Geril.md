# Geril
> Type: person | Also called: Head Servant Geril | First appears: Ch6.15K
> See also: Raelt, Jecaina, Lyfelt, Perric
## Overview
- Geril is an old [Servant] respected above the disgruntled nobility of Raelt's court, whom Raelt asks to read the King of Destruction's letter. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L20-21)
- Geril is Raelt's [Head Servant] and old retainer, who has been at his side for over four decades, from boy to man. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L252)
- Geril helped raise Raelt as a child. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L208)
- Geril clears his throat beside the throne to quiet the court. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L111)
- Geril guesses that the gathering of rulers Raelt mentions is in Pomle. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L248-250)
- Raelt asks Geril to contact Minister Lyfelt about condemning Flos' letter. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L151)
- {event} Geril has Jecaina locked in the palace vaults, guarded by General Lael's [Soldiers], so she cannot escape to the camp. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L408-411)
- Geril reports to Raelt that Princess Jecaina started a brawl with Nerrhavia's [Master Charioteers] after they made remarks she objected to. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L616-617)
- {belief} Geril tells Raelt he wishes Raelt had not gone to Terandria, because Raelt's father sent him to learn fencing to be safe abroad. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L882-885)
- Geril adds that Raelt might have joined the King of Destruction's army or stayed in Chandrar and become a great [King]. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L888)
- {event} Geril fights Perric with [Full Power Blow] and [Bellhammer's Impact] to free Jecaina's horse, then holds off the Golden Ranks with [My Life, My King]. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L396-405)
- Geril tells Jecaina that her father is captured and that Jecrass needs her as its [Queen]. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L410-413)
- Geril calls Jecaina his lovely child and tells her not to blame herself. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L415-416)
- {state} Geril dies covering Jecaina's escape, having been dead the moment the High King swung his sword. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L399,L418)
