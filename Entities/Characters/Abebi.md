# Abebi
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.21KQ
> See also: Cara, Singer of Terandria
## Overview
- Abebi is a young woman from Nigeria who works as the [Manager] of the Singer of Terandria's band. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L806; Book 19, Ch7.50 L780)
- Abebi is an Earther travelling with Cara's band, whose members were all found by Cara or came to her after hearing her music. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L780)
- Abebi is one of the oldest members of the band, together with Thien. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L781)
- Abebi did not know Cara from the very beginning, only after the star had already risen a fair way on her own. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L781)
## Role in Book 17
- Abebi keeps a clipboard at concerts and ticks off the Earth songs Cara performs, noting the Bruno Mars number during the Phel's Light show. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L732)
- A stagehand warns Abebi that the ship she wanted watched is arriving, and she hurries to the side of the stage to reach Cara. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L735-740)
- Abebi passes Cara gelato and preserved food in the wagon after the concert, and teases her about bursting out of her dress. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L802-804)
- Abebi learns from Cara that Cara lied to Rae about once being fat, because Rae needed the confidence. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L807-813)
- Cara tells Abebi, as the person who has known her longest, that her stage persona is a mask worn because the band is not safe. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L823-825)
- Abebi knows the band is touring to find the best place to settle, and says so to Cara. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L825-827)
## Role in Book 19
- Abebi leads the band north through Pheislant after war breaks out in the south between Ailendamus and the Dawn Concordat, since the band wants no part of it. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L788)
- Abebi takes the band towards Oztera, which has no personal quarrel in the war. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L789)
- Abebi has seen real fights, and doubts the band members from Earth have the stomach to kill people. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L790)
- Abebi orders the caravan forward once the broadcast is back on after a mob disrupts the concert. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L833)
- Abebi sets up a real wooden stage with a draped background for the broadcast concert. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1089)
- Abebi directs the shot for the broadcast and holds five amulets, which a [Huntress] discusses with her. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1099,L1125-1127)
- Abebi watches the delayed scrying-orb broadcast, which shows Drassi and Noass, while Cara is already performing. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1146,L1154)
