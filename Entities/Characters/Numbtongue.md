# Numbtongue
> Type: person | Also called: the Bard; Redfang Warrior; Hobgoblin [Bard] | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.34
> See also: Pyrite, Headscratcher, Rabbiteater, Shorthilt, Badarrow, Erin Solstice, Octavia, Mrsha, Bird, Yellow Splatters, Garen Redfang
## Overview
- Numbtongue is a Hobgoblin of the Redfang tribe, one of the five Redfang Warriors who live at Erin Solstice's inn. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L211-212; Book 9, Ch5.58 L519-535)
- Numbtongue is a [Bard] who plays a guitar and calls lightning with his music. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L142-145; Book 9, Ch5.53 L34-46)
- Numbtongue is the most literate Goblin in the setting: he reads and speaks the common tongue better than Garen Redfang, and may write Human and Drake scripts. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L151)
- Numbtongue speaks more fluently than any Goblin Pisces has met. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L622)
- Numbtongue got his name because, as a child, he talked so much that he bit his tongue. (Book 8, Ch5.38 L467-468)
## Background
- {fact} Numbtongue learned to speak as a small Goblin by stealing words, hiding in the sewers of a town far to the north. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L6)
- Numbtongue listened outside the Mage's Guild and other guilds, thinking that if Humans knew he could talk they would stop hunting Goblins. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L8-10)
- Numbtongue was swept out of the sewers, was hunted when he spoke and pleaded, and survived, and then he met Garen Redfang. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L10-12)
- Numbtongue was one of thirteen Redfangs sent by Garen to kill an innkeeper, but the group got lost. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1108-1111)
- Numbtongue fought at Esthelm, where thirteen Goblins fought and only six walked away. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1118-1119)
- Numbtongue recites the names of Redfangs fallen before the battle of Liscor, including Grunter, Orangepoo, Rocksoup, Leftstep, Bitefly, Patchhelm and Justrust. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L412-414)
- Numbtongue tells Garen the group met the innkeeper Erin Solstice, who was nice and has a name, and that Garen's orders to kill her were bad orders. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L519-534)
