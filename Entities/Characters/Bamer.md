# Bamer
> Type: person | Also called: Old Bamer | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.01
> See also: Fierre, Himilt, Colfa, Rivel
## Overview
- Bamer is an elderly Vampire of the Drakle clan who sits in the rafters of the keep with the family when Ryoka is brought there. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L80,L107)
- Bamer stares down at Ryoka with a frown but says nothing when Himilt asks whether the family disagrees. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L106-107)
- Bamer has coughed all his life, according to Fierre. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L598)
## Beliefs and stories
- {belief} Bamer thinks that the Vampires' frequent sickness is a curse. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L334)
- {belief} Bamer says Vampires once preyed on people and controlled much of Izril until they were nearly wiped out. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L379)
- {belief} Bamer thinks the Byres family cursed the Vampires centuries ago, after they went into hiding. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L608)
- Bamer told Fierre stories of the Vampires of old, who used blood magic and shape shifting. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L225)
- Himilt says Bamer could tell more of the Vampires' history but likes to exaggerate. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L881)
- {belief} Bamer says there were Vampire Lords, royalty with powers such as turning into mist, and even Skills. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L82)
## Health and later events
- Fierre says Bamer is dying, and counts him among the sick Vampires. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L332)
- Bamer was born with the signs of the Vampires' malady, as were Rivel and Fierre. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L885)
- Bamer arrives with Rivel when Colfa confronts Ryoka about Fierre's collapse. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L877)
- Bamer remembers, with Himilt, when House Byres' plan was put forward, so some places to live are instantly off the table for Vampires. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L5-7)
