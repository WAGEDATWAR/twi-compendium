# Charles de Trevalier
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1
> See also: Timor du Havrington, Rievan, Ceria Springwalker, Pisces, Illphres, Wistram
## Overview
- Charles de Trevalier is a young noble student who calls himself the scion of his house and demands respect. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L103)
- He wears rich clothes and an ornamental shortsword, and has a gaggle of other teens following him. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L102,L104)
## Voyage to Wistram
- {event} Charles approaches Ceria and Pisces on the ship in the manner of a prelude to harassment. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L102-103)
- He rests a hand on his sword as if wanting to draw it, but is clearly unprepared for actual violence. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L104,L125)
- {belief} Charles warns Pisces that Timor du Havrington is an experienced warrior with many levels in [Warrior] who would cut him to bits. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L126)
- Ceria thinks Timor does not hold himself like a warrior. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L127)
- Timor claims to be a [Warrior] and a [Lord] when confronting Pisces. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L130)
- Charles and his cronies back off when Pisces draws his rapier faster than the sword-wielding student had. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L121-123)
## Wistram politics
- {rel} Calvaron says Charles is heavily in Rievan's camp and certainly part of the Libertarian faction. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L141)
- Calvaron expects the Libertarians to welcome his family's deep pockets and influence. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L141)
- {event} Charles caused a commotion in Illphres' classroom by calling for her to teach him. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L134)
- {belief} Calvaron says Charles got in only by having someone else melt Illphres' ice, so she ruled it did not count. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L138)
- Calvaron guesses that Rievan, who argued with Illphres about it, probably did the melting. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L138)
- {event} Charles buys Illphres' spellbook from the Drake student who was carrying it to Ceria, for gold and then secrets. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L434-436)
- He drops it from a balcony into the sea, where it freezes the water around it and sinks encased in ice that will not melt. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L436-437)
- Beatrice sees him smiling afterward and says nothing. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.7 L438)
