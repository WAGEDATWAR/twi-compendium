# Pyrite
> Type: person | Also called: Gold Stone Chieftain; Goldstone Chieftain; Sir Pyrite | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.02G
> See also: Rags, Numbtongue, Goblin Lord, Garen Red Fang, Tremborag, Greydath of Blades
## Overview
- Pyrite is a fat Hobgoblin who was the Chieftain of a small mining tribe, the Gold Stone (Goldstone) tribe, and later calls himself Rags's second. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L43,L322; Book 8, Ch5.22G L396)
- Before he is called Pyrite the Gold Stone Chieftain has no name, because his tribe had no [Shaman]. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L127)
## Early appearances
- {event} In Garen's first attack the Gold Stone Chieftain steps in front of Rags and takes a one-handed sword slash meant for her, which breaks his shield and cuts through his armor. (Book 2, Ch2.02G L99-100)
- The Gold Stone Chieftain knows more Goblin history than Rags does. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L175)
- Rags sees the Gold Stone Chieftain as a classical Hob, fat and trusting his body mass to shield him, fairly good with an axe and shield, and chewing pine needles. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L121-123)
- The Gold Stone Chieftain refuses Rags's order to go and be nice to the strongest Redfang Hob in order to gain his trust, saying "Not Goblin". (Book 4, Ch3.28G L355-365)
- The Gold Stone Chieftain tells Rags that he once lived in Tremborag's mountain long ago. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L387-389)
- {belief} The Gold Stone Chieftain says he is strong but not smart, that he cannot be a Lord and does not want to be. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L441-443)
- {event} The Gold Stone Chieftain attacks Tremborag's Goblins with the Hobs Rags sent him. (Book 4, Ch3.28G L464)
- Pyrite has deep slashes on his belly and arm, chews a stick and spits out pieces of wood, and says chewing rocks keeps his teeth sharp. (Book 4, Ch3.29G L61,L63,L259-263)
- Pyrite drags Rags to safety miles from the village and tells her that fewer than a hundred Goblins died. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L182,L192)
## Habits and thoughts
- Pyrite eats soap, smooth stones from the lake bed and slabs of bark. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L42,L113-114)
- Pyrite stays awake at night while Rags sleeps, so that he is there in case something goes wrong. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L302)
- To Pyrite smells are like colors, and he cannot write. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L42,L110)
- Pyrite's first memory of a Human is being stabbed by a farmer with a pitchfork on a raid. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L163)
- Pyrite wonders why the last Goblin King, Velan, was called Velan the Kind, and how Tremborag took on the giant form he used to slaughter Gold-rank adventurers. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L83,L86)
- Pyrite wonders what the key-like thing Garen Redfang showed Tremborag unlocks, since it convinced the Great Chieftain to ally with Rags's tribe. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L87)
- {event} To lift morale Pyrite throws Goblins into the lake for fun, then throws Rags herself in, and she swims out quickly. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L279-300)
- {belief} Pyrite is sure the nearby Human town sent the adventurers who killed the Undercrawler tribe, though there is no proof. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L233,L236)
## Book 6: Rags's second
- {event} Pyrite composes verses in his head, including lines beginning "The wretched few gather" and "We are not monsters yet", and dreams of Goblins living freely at peace with other races and of Rags becoming a Goblin Lord and someday a King. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L99-106,L304-320)
- Pyrite tells the grumbling Goblins that Rags is aimless and young but "Will grow", while he crunches a giant Barkeater Beetle. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L107-115)
- {event} Pyrite drowns a Rose Knight in the lake, fights on with his axe broken, and is stabbed in the side by a frost dagger. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L198-210)
- Pyrite tells the Goblins that Greydath taught him some old things and stories, that he left the mountain long ago and made his own tribe, and he punches a Hob who suggests he could be Chieftain. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L338-350)
## Book 7: the Knights
- Rags regards Pyrite as a big, classically fat Hob who is deceptively placid and sometimes quite sharp, and he is respected by all factions of her tribe. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L156,L216)
- {belief} Pyrite says Greydath was an old Goblin who liked to talk but was very secretive and that he knew little, then passes gas and walks off. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L232-233)
- {event} Pyrite beats six Hobs and about nine smaller Goblins from Tremborag's mountain who are attacking Welca, together with Sir Kerrig. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L263-267)
- After Pyrite offers the older Knight a muddy dandelion, Sir Kerrig thanks him and asks to talk honestly, saying he had scarcely believed Goblins could be peaceful after Velan the Kind. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L286-290)
- Pyrite tells the Knights that Goblins want only to live. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L161-165)
- Pyrite says his tribe attacked caravans and one settlement, took only from caravans, did not kill, and was not like the Mountain City tribe. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L199)
- Pyrite says a Human town killed Goblin adults and children, and that the Goblins then killed the same number of Humans because they counted. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L207-218)
- Pyrite tells Welca that he does not kidnap or rape. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L232)
- Pyrite says he was the Chieftain of the Goldstone Tribe, which never fought Humans and gathered gold stones for fun. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L244-250)
- Pyrite carries an old hemp sack of pyrite nuggets and gems, and he treats a real gold nugget as worthless and trades it to a Goblin child for tadpoles. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L261-287)
- Pyrite tells the Knights that his tribe threw gems at attacking armies as "Human bait" to make them stop and fight each other. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L297-299)
- {event} Pyrite gives Welca a polished, cut sapphire the size of an egg that he made himself, and gives Sir Kerrig a piece of turquoise. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L309-326)
- {event} Pyrite asks the Knights how magic works and what it wants. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L333-364)
- Pyrite says he wants to be a [Mage] rather than a [Shaman] because [Mages] cast spells easily, and because Humans kill Goblin [Mages] first. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L393-401)
- {event} Pyrite crunches a topaz, says "Sink", and turns the soft ground to mud so that he and Welca sink into the earth. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L424-426)
- {event} In the battle Pyrite cuts a Human [Captain] down with his fiery battleaxe and orders his Hobs not to chase the fleeing Humans because the Chieftain said so. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L600-605)
- {belief} At a crossroads Pyrite tells Rags that the Human Knight who stayed wants to learn and thinks Goblins are like people, and Rags orders them to watch him. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L701-710)

- Pyrite calls himself second and orders Redscar to obey, and Redscar does. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L394-397)
- {event} Pyrite tells Bethal "Tears are a waste of water" and that his tribe is not the Goblin Lord's army and does not raid Goblins. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L142-157)
- {event} While Rags lies unconscious, Pyrite fights off the Unseen Empire's raids for three days, keeping the tribe marching, and takes five arrows in the chest. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L304-310,L365-366)

- Pyrite tells the Goblins to put their backs to the walls of the shattered city, with rows of twenty-foot pikes and a line of a hundred veteran Hobs behind them. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L191-197)
- Pyrite's enchanted battleaxe burns through flesh, sets Humans ablaze and sets the club of Durene alight. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L215,L258)

- {belief} Lord Tyrion's army identifies the fat Hob with the enchanted battleaxe as the probable Chieftain, though Welca cannot be sure. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L268-273)

- Pyrite finds a nest of burrowing rats with his gem-finding ability, roasts them and shares them with Eater of Spears and the other Goblins. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L145-154)

- Pyrite tells Eater of Spears that he can see when and where someone is going to hit by reading muscles, which he learned from Greydath and which is not a Skill. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L312-320)
- Pyrite privately doubts Greydath's secrets about Goblin Kings, which drove him from Tremborag's mountain. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L49)
- {event} When Reiss's army turns on Rags, Pyrite throws Eater of Spears' axes and cuts off Reiss's hand, saying "Told you". (Book 9, Ch5.55G L471-474)

- {event} At the mountain slope Pyrite blocks the Humans, hurls Tyrion Veltras down the mountain, and kills Humans with his battleaxe. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1119-1139)
- {state} Pyrite dies sitting on the slope after being run through by Tyrion and struck by magic and steel, reaching for a snack, with Rags far above him. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1130-1141)
## After his death
- Numbtongue finds Pyrite's body lying on its back with a smile on his face, takes a glowing blue gemstone from his mouth, and buries the head on a hill across from the inn. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L717-727)
- Pyrite had guessed that if one could remember a scene, one could pass on a sword technique or spell, and he tested it on Numbtongue in his last moments. (Book 10, Ch6.03 L89-91)
- Pyrite sent Numbtongue knowledge he deemed important, including the secret of the two keys, one of which Garen has. (Book 10, Ch6.03 L91)
- Pyrite consolidated his [Mining Chieftain] class into a [Magestone Chieftain] one, and his memories include carving an emerald marble for the Goblin Stickhair. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L50,L59)
- {event} Through the Skill [A Minute, Reborn], Pyrite wakes in Numbtongue's body for about a minute and scratches questions into the floorboards, ending with "Find the others if they remain. Find our King." (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L1156-1173)
- In Numbtongue's body Pyrite carves a smoky quartz marble with [Rock Polishing] and [Miner's Claws] and rolls it to Mrsha, thanking her for the good food. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L228-233)
- {event} Pyrite again speaks through Numbtongue to Mrsha, and uses a knife on a chair leg to hollow a hidden compartment to test how much an [Innkeeper] Skill enlarges it. (Book 18, ChInterlude_A_Meeting_of_Druids L146-155)
- {state} The Greystone Chieftain is told that the Goldstone Chieftain is dead, killed by being impaled, "Sacrifice death". (Book 19, Ch7.55E L474-478)
