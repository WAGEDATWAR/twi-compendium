# Zalaiss
> Type: person | Also called: Zalaiss Holmfyre; Councilwoman Zalaiss | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.49
> See also: Tismel, Lism, Krshia, Olesm, Liscor Council
## Overview
- Zalaiss Holmfyre is a [Merchant] elected to Liscor's new Council. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L28,L140,L243)
- Olesm describes Zalaiss and Tismel as wealthy, safe picks in their districts. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L243)
- Zalaiss counts on the Council as one of the Drakes in Lism's party, along with Tismel, Jeiss and Alonna. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L243; Book 13, Ch6.59 L25)
- Zalaiss and Tismel are in the same bed as the old Council, as Olesm sees it. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L520)
## On the new Council
- Zalaiss and Tismel sit between Krshia and Lism across the table at the first meeting, apprehensive about the old Council. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L272,L281)
- Zalaiss timidly objects that having the Council announcement shouted repeatedly would be lazy, and falls silent when Lism and Krshia agree the matter is unimportant. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L593-599)
- Zalaiss asks hesitantly whether the Council meeting time means right at dawn. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L615-616)
- Zalaiss votes in favour of executing the Minotaur in the dungeon, when Tismel hesitates, in Lism's proposed vote. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L628,L643,L656-657)
- Zalaiss, being a [Merchant], looks sharply at Lism and knows when not to trust someone's immediate word. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L808)
- Zalaiss objects to Lism's motion to limit rent prices, claiming she has a right to be heard as a Councilmember of equal rank. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L839-843)
- Lism answers that the Council has already decided, and Zalaiss pales as the other Councilmembers back his rent motion. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L845-850,L856,L863)
- Zalaiss tries to yank Jeiss's arm down when the Council raises hands for Lism's motions, to no effect. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L884-885)
- Zalaiss shouts that Lism's actions are mutiny, and flees the room to tell the old Council as Lism urges. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L887-892)
- Zalaiss returns with the old Council, and Relc blocks the way, telling Zalaiss and Tismel they may listen if they promise not to interrupt. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L910-915)
## Later
- Zalaiss rarely shows up to Council meetings, and is either drunk when she arrives or with Tismel and the old Council, relaying the current Council's decisions. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L26)
- Zalaiss and Tismel lack the votes to sway any but contentious issues. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L26)
- Zalaiss and Tismel are the largely useless traditionalist members of the Council, who tend to be overridden in majority votes. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L366)
