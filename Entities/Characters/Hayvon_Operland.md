# Hayvon Operland
> Type: person | Also called: Lord Hayvon; Lord Operland; Lord Hayvon Operland | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.68
> See also: Blighted King, Richard, Tom
## Overview
- Lord Hayvon Operland is known to some as the fifth most powerful [Lord] in the world, appearing in fifth place on rankings whatever the criteria. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L2-3)
- Lord Hayvon is a [Lord] in service to the Blighted King of Rhir and is considered largely boring compared to the other top-ranked [Lords]. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L5-6)
- Lord Hayvon is very rich, though not as rich as the Emir Yazdil Achakhei of Chandrar. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L4)
- Lord Hayvon does not care about being ranked fifth and would not blink if told so. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L7)
- Hayvon Operland is described as the foremost [Lord] of Rhir and of the Blighted King's court. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L785)
## Home and habits
- Lord Hayvon's mansion balcony overlooks wheat fields worked by [Farmers], right beside the home, so close that a hop past the tiny garden lands in wheat. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L8-9)
- Lord Hayvon deliberately keeps [Farmers] working next to his mansion because he does not believe in wasting space in Rhir. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L9)
- Lord Hayvon owns Pegasus boots, made of ancient hide from the animals and decorated with feathers, that let him walk in the air. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L25)
- Lord Hayvon demonstrates his Pegasus boots by walking an eighteen-step lap through the air from the balcony, over the garden and back to the railing. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L25-26)
- Lord Hayvon says flying is simple, as simple as buying the right artifact, while growing wheat is far harder than flying. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L24,L27)
- Lord Hayvon has a son and a young daughter. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L56)
- Lord Hayvon says he has ordered tens of thousands of men and women to their deaths with a single word, and has overseen at least a million deaths. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L56)
- Lord Hayvon's hands are scarred from battle. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L57)
- Lord Hayvon takes one deer on a hunt to feed his mansion and gives the other six bagged to the public. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L221-222)
- Lord Hayvon's mansion is rich but not opulent, and has fewer servants than the nobility of other continents. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L222-223)
- Richard reads that Krsysl Wordsmith's treatise ranks Lord Hayvon fifth and Lord Belchaus Meron first among the world's [Lords]. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L215-217)
- {belief} Krsysl Wordsmith's treatise calls Lord Hayvon largely practical and a staunch commander of Humans and the mixed forces sent to Rhir, with levels unknown. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L219)
- Richard thinks the Drake [Writer]'s rating of Hayvon as average is complete nonsense. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L216,L220)
## The [Historian] class
- Lord Hayvon says he was something of a [Historian] in his youth and even gained the class, out of a passion for literature. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L10)
- Lord Hayvon says his father beat him within an inch of his life when he found out he had gained the [Historian] class. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L10)
- {state} Lord Hayvon says the Blighted King removed his [Historian] class, so he no longer levels in it. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L12)
- Lord Hayvon says that he now indulges his love of history and understanding the world through his [Lord] class. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L12)
