# Pielt
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Pielt | First appears: Ch6.30
> See also: Embria, Wikir, Vell, Relc
## Overview
- Pielt is a male Drake [Captain] in 4th Company under Wing Commander Embria. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L85,L146-147)
- Pielt commands a squad that is mainly made up of Drakes, including Sergeant Fika. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L132)
- Wikir says Pielt's squad has to clear Shield Spider nests all by hand and torch. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L24)
## Personality and relationships
- Pielt boasts that he is as smooth on a tavern floor as he is on the battlefield, and Vell mocks his attempts at flirting. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L135-139)
- Pielt tells Wikir to go to hell after Wikir jokes that Pielt's group, lacking a strong [Mage], will be picking baby spiders out of its ears. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L83-87)
- Pielt's tail lashes under his table when Embria says Lism is not their man and that she cannot approach Krshia instead. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L110-111)
- Pielt's scales turn beet red when Kesa whispers a joke at his expense, and Embria tells him to stop bringing up his failed romances. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L146-147)
- Pielt tells Embria her test pick-up line is terrible, and the other officers cannot meet her eyes except Wikir. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L165-170)
- Wikir says he went into the pit rather than let Pielt call him a coward. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L22-23)
## Stories about Relc
- Pielt tells a crowd of listeners that all of 4th Company heard the stories about Sergeant Relc. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L328)
- Pielt says Relc was 4th Company's secret weapon who would charge in, cut his way to an enemy officer, and escape again, sometimes against a [Commander]-class opponent. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L328)
- Pielt says Captain Wikir served with Relc, and that Embria learned [Spearmaster] skills from him. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L328)
