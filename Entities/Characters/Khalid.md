# Khalid
> Type: person | Also called: Khalid who has no last name | First appears: Ch6.12K
> See also: Quarass of Germina, Trey Atwood, Vaitsha
## Overview
- Khalid is a wiry young street boy with a burning courage, who kneels before the new Quarass in the mud-brick court. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L293,L299)
- {state} Khalid swears to be the Quarass' strength and to do whatever else she asks, naming himself Khalid who has no last name. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L299-300)
- Khalid has blood on his skin and clothes from where his arm bled. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L301)
- Khalid tries to drink from his cup like Vaitsha and declares himself the Quarass' protector and dagger, saying she lives forever. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L476-477)
- Khalid calls himself Khalid who has no other name in the Quarass' court. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L477)
- The Quarass says Khalid is a young boy whose classes are hard to level so young, and he has begun training with the [First Warriors]. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L717)
- {belief} The Quarass says she expects Khalid to show her in time which class suits him and to become a champion like no other. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L717)
