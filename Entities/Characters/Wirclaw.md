# Wirclaw
> Type: person | Also called: the Gnoll [Farmer] | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.33
> See also: Erin Solstice, Krshia, Mrsha
## Overview
- Wirclaw is a Gnoll [Farmer] with dirty blonde fur who lives in a small village near Liscor. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L246,L249)
- Wirclaw keeps many cows and sells milk, which Krshia buys from him. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L257-258)
- Wirclaw has a son, a small Gnoll with light blonde fur. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L361)
## Farm and village
- Wirclaw says Gnolls settle near cities after learning farming from the Drakes, and that Liscor would starve without farmers. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L247,L254)
- Wirclaw says the village's defenses are a ten-foot wall, his bow and its villagers, and the Watch patrols nearby. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L249,L252)
- Wirclaw says Rock Crabs do not attack walled settlements, and that Shield Spiders can be heard when they tunnel. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L250-252)
- Wirclaw already knows of Mrsha when Erin mentions the little Gnoll she is looking after. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L260-261)
- Wirclaw recommends goat milk for a young Gnoll, and sells Erin milk. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L265)
- Wirclaw owns a pair of Fischer Cows, which like meat and whose rich milk comes from cows that bite. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L265-267)
## The goat attack
- {event} Wirclaw shoots an arrow into a charging goat as the villagers barricade themselves in their houses. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L26)
- {event} Wirclaw catches the goat as it leaps for Erin and hurls it aside, and he and Erin kick and stomp it to death. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L30)
- {event} Wirclaw leads the villagers after the fight, holding bloody weapons, and none of them is hurt. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L100)
- Wirclaw half-raises his bow when he spots Bird, then glances toward the Hobs in the distance. (Book 6, Ch4.34 L100)
