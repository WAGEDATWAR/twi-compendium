# Brilm
> Type: person | Also called: Wall Lord Brilm | First appears: Ch6.32
> See also: Ilvriss, Alrric, Tasilt
## Overview
- Brilm is a Drake Wall Lord of Salazsar and a friend of Ilvriss, who is described as a childhood friend. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L78,L80,L91)
- Brilm is a heavier Drake who dresses in a flamboyant red-and-orange outfit and is as energetic as Ilvriss is reserved. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L79)
- Brilm wears rings on his claws and carries a bag of holding. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L117,L137)
- Brilm sniffs Balerosian powder from a box and sprinkles it on the carpet. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L538)
- Later, Brilm is portly and jovial and toasts Ilvriss in Ilvriss' tower after the corruption scandal in the teleportation network is uncovered. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L53-55)
## Visit to Ilvriss
- Brilm visits Ilvriss less than a week after Ilvriss returns from Liscor, and mentions a dinner party he is hosting. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L73,L80,L126)
- Brilm sympathises with Ilvriss over losing Periss, his best subordinate. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L82)
- {belief} Brilm says Zel Shivertail's army cut apart Salazsar's army and that Zel threw the Drakes over for the Humans at the end, and Ilvriss tells him it was not like that. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L85-86)
- Brilm complains that Alrric showed him in with no courtesy and used his name, and asks why Ilvriss keeps him around. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L90)
- Brilm says the magic door would be a treasure, useful for moving miners and trade, and that he and others leaned hard on Pallass over Ilvriss' travel. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L124)
- Brilm shows Ilvriss a pink-shimmering quartz that holds a recording of a loud song, which Ilvriss finds horrendous. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L127-136)
- Brilm plays a second, blue-glowing quartz holding a slow, soulful song that deeply moves Ilvriss. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L139-143)
- Brilm says the song is called My Way, originally by Frank Sinatra, performed by the Queen of Pop, who is also called the Baroness of the Beats. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L145-148)
- Brilm says the Queen of Pop is a nickname the singer chose for herself and that she is the [Singer] of Terandria. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L148)
- Brilm notices the groove in Ilvriss' ring finger and asks whether he is getting married, and Ilvriss refuses to answer. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L189-194)
## Later dealings
- Brilm presses Ilvriss to say how he and Tasilt came to suspect the [Cargomaster], and Ilvriss refuses to say. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L56-61)
- Brilm tells Alrric that the other Children of the Walls are wondering what activated Ilvriss' rings and that there are rumours. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L536)
- Brilm asks Alrric whether Ilvriss has given the ring to a Gnoll, a Human or a Selphid, and says Alrric does not like him and he does not like Alrric. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L533,L536)
- Brilm says Ilvriss marrying a Gnoll would be bad for his reputation, remarking that old Zail would kill him. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L539-541)
- Brilm advises that Ilvriss take a mistress, which would be a scandal but acceptable, and suggests Navine could be the one to marry. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L549-551)
