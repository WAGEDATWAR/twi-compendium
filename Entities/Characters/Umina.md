# Umina
> Type: person | Also called: Umina Caxical; Dancing Serpent | First appears: Book 8, ChInterlude_Niers
> See also: Niers, Marian, Wil Kallinad, Tulm the Mithril, Venaz, Geneva, Foliana
## Overview
- Umina Caxical is a Lizardgirl from Baleros with yellow scales patterned pink and violet frills around her neck. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L18; Book 11, Ch6.24D L3)
- Umina is a nervous Lizardgirl and a student of Niers Astoragon, the Titan. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Niers L181; Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L87-89)
- {state} Umina is a Level 27 [Strategist], with a dozen Skills, at nineteen years old, one of the youngest students in Niers' advanced class. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L632)
- Umina has beaten every classmate in practice war games and is especially good at taking down Venaz. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L632)
- Umina got into the class on pure talent in the one-year program, while the other students paid more. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L688)
- Umina has beaten Venaz three times in practice, which no one else has done, while Marian's best is two wins in a row. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L690)
- Umina is the only Lizardfolk in Niers' current class, and some of her family's friends helped pay for her to attend. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L706)
- Umina has barely twenty silver coins and two gold coins reserved for rent and food, so she cannot afford preparations for the game. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L633-640)
- Umina prefers listening and understanding before acting, which has earned her a reputation for timidity in class. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L194)
