# Thomast
> Type: person | Also called: Thomast Veniral; Sir Thomast; Chevalier Thomast | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.06KM
> See also: Bethal, Magnolia Reinhart, Ryoka, Sir Kerrig, Eliasor Melissar
## Overview
- Thomast Veniral is the [Chevalier] husband of Lady Bethal Walchaís and is known as Sir Thomast. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L328; Book 11, Ch6.26 L118)
- Thomast is renowned for his silence and lack of temper, in contrast to his wife's moods. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L253)
- Thomast has warm black hair, a mustache, yellow-green eyes, a light red coat and a long, cat-like stride. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L11)
- Thomast carries a long, thin duelist's blade and an enchanted rapier. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L11; Book 6, Ch4.48 L228)
- Thomast is a [Fencer], signing his open letter with that title. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L295)
- Thomast fights better on foot than on horseback. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L101)
- Thomast and Bethal ride without riding Skills, though both have excellent horses. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L118)
- The Knights of the Petal look to Thomast rather than Bethal for orders in the field. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L116-117; Book 11, Ch6.26 L80-82)
## Skills and fighting
- Thomast uses [Affair of Honor] to force a fleeing [Assassin] to turn and duel him. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L486-489)
- Magnolia notes that few can match someone of the [Duelist] class in a one-on-one bout. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L506)
- Thomast cuts the air with his rapier from a distance, wounding Hobs and testing Greybeard. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L242; Book 6, Ch4.48 L228)
- Thomast uses [Double Cut] against Greybeard, thrusting with a second silvery blur at the same moment. (Book 6, Ch4.39G L244-245)
- Thomast sends wind slashes along his enchanted rapier and uses [Flawless Dodge] and [Lunge] against Assassins. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L211,L214,L221-222)
- Thomast uses [Flicker Onslaught] to teleport between spots, striking down daggers, an arrow and an [Assassin] at once. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L236-237)
