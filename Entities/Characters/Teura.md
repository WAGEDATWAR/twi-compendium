# Teura
> Type: person | Also called: Mage Teura | First appears: Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage
> See also: Feor, Blackmage, Aaron Vanwell
## Overview
- Teura is a half-Elf with bright red hair and sable eyes, and Blackmage flushes around her. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L34)
- Teura is senior to many [Mages], having lived in Wistram for over six decades, and belongs to Archmage Feor's personal faction. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L40)
- Teura guards and escorts Blackmage through Wistram. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L34-40)
- {belief} Teura tells Blackmage that the Council deliberated on waiving fees, a Revivalist notion rejected as too drastic. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L64)
- Feor tells Teura that Wistram is in a race to collect otherworlders. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L413)
- {event} Teura holds the mists back around herself in the plaza, brightening the air. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L992,L1009)
- Teura is a half-Elf High Mage, a Council member and a Centrist under Archmage Feor. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L25)
- Teura still holds a grudge against Aaron for giving her the slip when he was under her authority. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L100)
