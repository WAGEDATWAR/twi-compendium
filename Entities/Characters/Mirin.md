# Mirin
> Type: person | Also called: Mage Mirin | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.13K
> See also: Ulyse, Palke, Esiela, Parasol Stroll, Trey Atwood
## Overview
- Mirin is a [Mage] of Parasol Stroll, quiet and soft-spoken, and she is about six years older than Trey Atwood. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L267,L274)
- Mirin carries a parasol, crimson and indigo in Book 10 and indigo and teal at the banquet. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L312; Book 10, Ch6.14K L101)
- Mirin is the second-in-command of Parasol Stroll. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L272)
## Service to Flos
- Ulyse asks Mirin to take part in the Parasol Stroll performance for the King of Destruction. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L91)
- Mirin conjures a river from the stone floor and air that rushes up to the ceiling, and the water proves real. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L101-103)
- Mirin agrees with Ulyse that Smith Daiton's combination-steel blades are imperfect and poor for enchantments. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L272-273)
- Ulyse asks Mirin whether she can feel that a pure blade will hold an enchantment. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L946)
- Ulyse orders Mirin to take her group and burst acid orbs over the enemy during the battle. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L231)
- Mirin, Ulyse and Esiela of Parasol Stroll approach Flos as the group's [Mages]. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L409)
- Mirin is told that Roshal's mage-collars will not work on Grand Mage Esiela, so she must be prevented from escaping. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L382)
## With Trey
- Mirin and Palke escort Trey south through Nerrhavia's Fallen from Reim, teaching him magic on the way. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L276)
- Mirin casts a cooling spell on Trey and reminds him to remember to use magic. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L272-273)
- Mirin watches with Palke as Trey boards the ship. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L305)
- Flos' commanders ask who leads Parasol Stroll with Ulyse gone, noting that Mirin is with Trey. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L60)
