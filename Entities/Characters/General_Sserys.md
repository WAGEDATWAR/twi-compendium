# General Sserys
> Type: person | Also called: Spear of the Drakes; General Sserys of Liscor | First appears: Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1
> See also: Zel Shivertail, Magnolia Reinhart, Antinium Wars
## Overview
- General Sserys is a Drake and the hero of the First Antinium War, and he is called [Spear of the Drakes]. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L416,L421)
- Sserys tells Zel Shivertail that Magnolia Reinhart, a child at the time with vomit dripping off her chin, offered him her hand and proposed that together they could end the war and save the world. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L417)
- {reveal} Sserys confesses to Zel that he was afraid of her peace and of what she saw, and he says he would never admit it to anyone else. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L420-422)
- Sserys's account says that other Human [Lords] interrupted him before he could answer her. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L420)
- A diary of General Sserys of Liscor records the army of Liscor, one of the strongest forces on the continent, meeting the Antinium on a guard mission. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L43-45)
- At Shivering Falls Pass he alone refuses to retreat, gathers coalition armies, and personally leads fifteen charges against the Antinium. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L96-100)
- In Liscor, 4th Company soldiers watching Antinium march in a parade ask what happened to pride and to General Sserys. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L420,L424)
- Zel recalls that Sserys once ordered a set of magical armor made for him, which Zel refused. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L371)
- Sserys coined the saying "Drakes do not run". (Book 7, Ch5.18S L462)
- Selys describes Sserys as Liscor's greatest hero, who died long ago, and his grave has a grand tombstone with his name. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L889-890,L911,L919)
- {belief} Selys believes that Zel and Sserys were lovers, since Zel's will asks to be buried with his love; she scatters Zel's ashes beside Sserys's coffin. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L896,L911-914)
## Command and the Antinium Wars
- Sserys's diary records that his Liscorian Army had been subjugating a local Goblin Lord's forces around the city of Ussls before meeting the Antinium. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L46)
- The army of Liscor is renowned as one of the strongest on the continent and sells its services to any nation that will hire it. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L44)
- Only a few people of note knew of the Antinium early, including young Magnolia Reinhart, Lord Tyron Veltras and General Sserys. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L40)
- Sserys writes that a patrol of twenty Drakes and Gnolls met fifteen black individuals harvesting trees in a forest, and six of his soldiers died. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L47)
- Sserys is deeply troubled that equal losses were taken against a numerically inferior foe. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L49)
- Sserys forms heavy ranks with defensive classes and assigns [Tacticians] and [Strategists] along each column. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L101)
- Sserys collapses tunnels that appear behind his lines, and his relentless advance leaves the Antinium no time to dig under his forces. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L102)
- Sserys sends kill-teams of high-level warriors to devastate Antinium lines and retreat with few or no casualties. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L103)
- Sserys writes "We will not retreat" and "Drakes do not run", and the next day the First Coalition Army of Liscor counterattacks the besieging Antinium. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L111-115)
- {state} Sserys unifies every standing Drake city, forges a non-aggression pact with the Gnoll Tribes, and is named High General of the war. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L5)
- {event} Sserys personally leads a mounted elite group to rescue Geir's survivors, retrieving over forty Drakes and Gnolls above Level 30. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L30-31)
- {event} Sserys pushes to the southern entrance of Liscor, where the Black Tide encircles his back and traps him. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L42)
- Sserys bans the water-flooding tactic except as a last resort, because mages warn of its effect on the weather. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L106)
## Death
- Sserys's last diary entry says a new breed of Antinium is destroying his army from the tunnels, and that Zel must be told. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L129-131)
- Sserys's last entry ends "To Liscor, and the world, fight on," and he says he will die as he lived, a soldier. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L134-135)
- {state} Sserys dies at the Antinium Grand Hive, and his journal and corpse are found in the First Unified Army's camp twenty-four days later. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L137)
- Sserys's body lies fully armored in his command tent, sword in hand, killed by a single strike through armor and bone to the heart. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L141)
- No other soldier of the First Unified Army is found. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L140)
- {rel} Zel calls Sserys his mentor and friend, and tells Xrn he has not forgotten that the Antinium killed him. (Book 4, Ch3.42 L350)
- {belief} Sserys tells Zel that young Magnolia seems like a god, or the opposite of one, comparing her to a Demon from Rhir. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L396-400,L417)
