# Persua
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.02R
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Garia Strongheart, Fals, Celum Runner's Guild, Courier
## Overview
- Persua is a City Runner in Celum with a sallow, shrewish face, brown hair and a strong perfume, who tosses her head often and annoys Ryoka on sight. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L145-146,L148-150)
- Ryoka considers Persua slow, lazy and an idiot who takes few long-distance contracts and only delivers light items like flowers or letters. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L163)
- Persua has Street Runner friends, mainly new or low-ranking ones, who watch Ryoka for her. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L158)
## Rivalry with Ryoka
- {event} Persua claims a Magnolia request that was to go to Ryoka, saying Ryoka has already done one this week and should switch, and Ryoka takes it instead by saying it is urgent. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L154-180)
- {event} Persua, sweaty and tired from running back to the city, insists she can make the run in time, and then stomps her foot and tells Ryoka she will regret it. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L175-190)
- {event} About twenty Street Runners surround Ryoka on a run, trip her and drive her onto a small street where a heavy mule-drawn cart and a patch of solid air hit her; Ryoka sees a familiar sallow face grinning. (Book 1, Ch1.03R L158-179)
- Persua stands facing Ryoka and Garia with at least eight Street Runners behind her. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L98)
- {belief} Persua tells Ryoka she is terribly sorry she got in the way of a runaway cart and says the other Runners tried to warn her. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L107)
- {belief} Persua says Ryoka does not listen to her fellow Runners. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L107)
- {event} Persua tells Ryoka she will die in the High Passes alone because she is a fool, saying experienced Runners avoid such requests and work together. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L145-148)
- {event} Persua backs the adventurer Arnel against Ryoka, telling her she no longer has a Minotaur and a half-elf to protect her. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L147-148)
- Ryoka says she still owes Persua a shattered leg bone and thinks Persua still has it out for her. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L131)
- Persua hates Ryoka because the popular Runner Fals seems to like her, while Garia likes Fals. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L119)
## Double Step and the fight
- {event} Ryoka is passed on a snowy road by a pale, pinched-faced Runner at a speed she cannot match, and recognises her as Persua. (Book 3, Ch3.03 L390-393)
- {state} Persua gains the movement Skill [Double Step] before Level 20, is delivering faster than any Runner, and is leaving Celum for Invrisil or another northern city as a likely Courier. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L247-263)
- {event} Ryoka breaks Persua's nose and parts of her face in a fight in the Runner's Guild, and Persua swears she will have Ryoka raped and killed. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L424-433)
- {event} Persua is kept overnight by a [Healer] even though potions mended most of her wounds. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L79)
- Fals expects Persua to run cross-continent deliveries if she becomes a Courier, or to move north if she does not. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L179-181)
## Aftermath
- {state} Persua has left the Celum Runner's Guild, which Ryoka feels let her act as she did, and her gang revolved around her. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L164-167,L271)
- {state} Garia says Persua lost almost all of her clique after she got in trouble, and that Claudeil and Toriska vanished. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L234)
- Garia says she might be on track to becoming a Courier now if she had trained earlier, rather than Persua. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L230)
- {belief} A young City Runner in Invrisil accuses Ryoka of causing an avalanche in a guild, stealing contracts and being violent, and Ryoka recognises the accusations as Persua's. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L321-323)
- {belief} Delanay says Persua has won over half the Runners in Invrisil with sob stories of Celum mistreating her. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L366)
- {event} Ryoka had heard Persua was being considered for Courier status after going north from Fals. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L323)
