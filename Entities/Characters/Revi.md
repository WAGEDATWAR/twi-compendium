# Revi
> Type: person | Also called: Revi Cotton; the Stitch-Girl; the Stitch-Woman; Revi of Griffon Hunt | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.01T
> See also: Halrac, Typhenous, Ulrien, Griffon Hunt, Erin Solstice, Regrika Blackpaw, Pisces, Jelaqua
## Overview
- Revi is a Stitch-girl, a person made of cloth, and a Gold-rank [Summoner] of the adventuring team Griffon Hunt. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L221-229; Book 7, Ch5.05 L109)
- Revi is in her late twenties and is impatient. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L104)
- {state} In Book 2, Revi is the newest addition to Griffon Hunt. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L229)
- Revi is not a [Necromancer], as she tells Halrac. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L221)
- Revi is a [Mage] of a different specialisation from Typhenous, who is a generalist focused on defensive magic and wards. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L109)
- Revi changes her hairstyle to short-cropped hair for the wet season, though she still tosses her head as if she had a ponytail. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L205)
- Revi sews her own ears back onto her head, and Typhenous takes over the needle. (Book 8, Ch5.38 L77)
- Revi says you cannot kill a Stitch-Girl with a sword. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L146)
## Summoning
- Revi summons constructs from the memory of souls past by throwing a scrap of fabric into the air. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L220-224,L230)
- Revi says her constructs bear some of their previous bodies' power, but have no minds and are not the souls themselves. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L230)
- Revi's first summon is a tall man in bronze armour with a mace and shield, whom she names as Kelthor. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L224,L232)
- Revi says not all String People are related and that Kelthor is only a shadow of the warrior he was in life. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L232)
- Revi says she got Kelthor from her grandmother, and that he has declined in power since her grandmother used him. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L243)
- Halrac estimates Kelthor at around Level 25. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L240)
- Revi plans to capture the skeleton's spirit, since she can use a spirit whose memory of life is strong. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L218,L221)
- Revi says that once a construct dies it is gone, though she could restore it if it were only injured. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L271-272)
- Halrac says Revi can conjure spirits of Stitch-warriors or summon other creatures, but her summons cannot think for themselves. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L216)
- Revi throws five talismans to raise glowing figures, then three String People, two warriors and a staff-wielding mage, against Regrika. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L8,L15)
- Revi calls on a flaming spectral Corusdeer using a bit of calcified horn. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L181-182; Book 7, Ch5.05 L139-141)
- Revi summons spectral warriors and animals to do her bidding. (Book 7, Ch5.05 L109)
- Revi summons ghostly warriors and archers, a flaming Corusdeer and two eagles of light on Liscor's wall. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L386-387)
- Revi summons a glowing green Face-Eater Moth from a wing in amber and rides it down the street. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L382)
- Revi's tools are pieces of fallen monsters in amber and talismans made from fallen Stitch-People. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L168)
- Revi summons eight glowing Stitch-Warriors who hold a line behind Jelaqua against Shield Spiders. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L203)
- Revi cannot call on the Corusdeer for a week if its spirit is destroyed. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L535)
- Revi's Corusdeer falls into the monster pit, and she says later she lost it. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L542-544; Book 8, Ch5.29 L182)
- Revi uses a wand that shoots weak glowing missiles of light. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L202)
- Revi's summoned warriors and Corusdeer fight zombies while Halrac shoots a charging Ghoul. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L395)
