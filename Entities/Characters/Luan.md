# Luan
> Type: person | Also called: Luan Khumalo; the Rower; Luan the City Runner | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.02D
> See also: Ken, Aiko, Daly, Geneva, Paige, Siri, Venaz, Marian, Xol
## Overview
- Luan Khumalo is a tall South African man in his twenties who is transported to Baleros from Earth. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L263-265; Book 5, Ch1.02D L316)
- Luan is twenty five, several years older than Ken and Aiko. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L316)
- Luan speaks four languages: English, Afrikaans, Zulu and Tswana. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L278)
## Earth background
- Luan was an Olympic rowing contender for South Africa rather than an Olympian, and would have competed in 2020. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L304)
- Luan aimed at gold in the single scull, having first trained for a coxless pair with his best friend Ben. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L304-307)
- Luan was in Australia on a break when he was transported to Baleros. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L307)
- Luan says Ben will get a chance at the Olympics in his place. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L308)
- Luan has a wife and a baby son, whose names are later tattooed on his arm. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L142; Book 10, Ch6.07D L397-398)

- Luan teaches Aiko to row and is followed by Lizardfolk who are taken by his skill on the water. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L154)
- {belief} Rumour says Luan is a married man, while Aiko is clearly deeply attached to him. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L157)
- Luan decides to earn money for the United Nations company by running messages in his canoe. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L172)
- {state} Luan is an [Expert Rower] at Level 27 after only two months in Baleros. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L190)
- Luan's Skills include [Boat: Spray Cutter], [Power Strokes], [Greater Endurance], [Lesser Strength], [Enhanced Durability - Oars], [Boat: Lightweight Craft] and [Wave Sense]. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L186)
