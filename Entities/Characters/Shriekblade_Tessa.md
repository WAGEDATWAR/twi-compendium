# Shriekblade (Tessa)
> Type: person | Also called: Shriekblade; Tessa; Miss Tessa; Adventurer Shriekblade | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.50I
> See also: Ilvriss, Alrric, Akral the Adamantium, Healer of Tenbault, Assassin's Guild
## Overview
- Shriekblade is a Named Adventurer who works for Ilvriss in Salazsar, and money dictates whom she works for. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L573; Book 18, Ch7.33I L182)
- Shriekblade's real name is Tessa, but she and everyone else prefer not to use it. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L167)
- Shriekblade is one of two Named Adventurers currently resident in Salazsar, and the more dangerous and unpredictable of the two; the other is the steady Gnoll Akral the Adamantium. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L146)
- Shriekblade is a female Drake, younger than Alrric, and is called a prodigy of an adventurer. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L171)
## Appearance and manner
- Shriekblade wears long, tight-clinging black silk, probably enchanted, with no armor, and her grey-green scales are scarred wherever they are exposed. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L169-170)
- Many of Shriekblade's scars are self-inflicted, and the split scales do not regrow. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L170)
- Shriekblade can cling to a ceiling like a gigantic, demented gecko, and lands on a desk as lightly as a cat. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L162,L164)
- Shriekblade swings abruptly between laughing and looking tired and drawn, and her fits of shuddering include a full-body shake. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L175,L180,L185,L188)
- Shriekblade fights with a long dagger, drawn so quickly that Alrric only sees a blur. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L197)
- Alrric judges that she is not a [Rogue] or [Assassin] but a close-quarters combat specialist who has seen more combat than Captain Shieldscale four times over. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L216)
## Record and condition
- Shriekblade has a terrible record of failure and success, and is a good mercenary if the job is killing something. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L182)
- Shriekblade was imprisoned twice for murder, though both times were counted as self-defense. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L183)
- Shriekblade depends on potions and calming tonics, including high-quality ones that Ilvriss provides, and says they do not help enough. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L186-191)
- Shriekblade says that the Healer of Tenbault makes her feel better for a month, and then it is worse. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L191)
- Shriekblade has visited the Healer of Tenbault many times, but whatever the Healer does does not stick. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L193)
- Shriekblade asks Alrric whether Ilvriss has someone for her to kill yet, as casually as asking about weeding a garden, since killing people or monsters is all the same to her. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L204-205)
- After drinking three tonics, Shriekblade becomes completely without emotion and reports for duty, asking whether she should kill the [Weapon Master] she is sent to test. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L209-214)
## Work for Ilvriss
- Shriekblade is on retainer to Ilvriss, who hired her despite Alrric's objection. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L181-182)
- Ilvriss sends Shriekblade to test a half-Elf [Weapon Master]; later he explains how to fight Antinium while she yawns openly, and Alrric guesses his bruises come from a duel with a Named Adventurer. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L212,L342,L352-354)
- {event} Shriekblade charges screaming at the Stone Starers at a barricaded shaft, killing them without tactics, before Ilvriss leads Gold-ranks and [Mercenaries] in behind her. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L446-452)
- Killing the monsters does not improve her temper, and she sits in a void of space ignoring the blood and singe marks, as killing just is for her. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L467)
- {event} The Assassin's Guild sends Shriekblade a contract as black letters tracing themselves on her arm; the Guild normally leaves the Named Adventurer alone, so the target must be big. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L376-379)
- Shriekblade is calm after drinking too many potions, and answers the contract with a lodestone, saying "I am working. No." (Book 18, Ch7.37 L380-382)
