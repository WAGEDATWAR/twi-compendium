# Queen Yisame
> Type: person | Also called: Queen Yisame of Nerrhavia; Yisame | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.11
> See also: Raelt, Perric of Medain, Flos
## Overview
- Yisame is a [Queen] of Nerrhavia, who sees the truth of battle for the first time while watching Flos's attack on Belchan. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L3,L5)
- Yisame's horror at the scene is genuine, which earns her appreciation for her sincerity. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L5)
- {event} Yisame, pale, declares war again on Belchan. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L3)
## Support for Jecrass
- {event} Yisame covertly tells Raelt that she has sent a small gift and that at least forty thousand Nerrhavian mercenaries may head north. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1168-1172)
- Yisame says the mercenaries require payment, hence the gift, which the Merchant's Guildmaster estimates at about three hundred thousand gold pieces. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1172-1173)
- Raelt has also just received money from the Empress of Sands. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1174)
