# Pisces
> Type: person | Also called: Pisces Jealnet; the necromancer; comrade Pisces | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.06R
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Erin Solstice, Toren, Cognita, Az'kerash, Montressa du Valeross, Olesm Swifttail, Horns of Hammerad, Wistram Academy
## Overview
- Pisces is a young Human [Necromancer] and [Mage] who studied at Wistram Academy before being expelled over what he wanted to do with necromancy. (Book 1, Ch1.09 L188-192; Book 1, Ch1.06R L95-98)
- His true name is Pisces Jealnet, and he calls himself a Silver-rank adventurer on the team of the Horns of Hammerad. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L942; Book 7, Ch5.18S L129)
## Background and Character
- Pisces is a pale young man with unkempt brown hair who smells bad, and Erin Solstice first meets him in grimy grey robes that look as if they have never been washed. (Book 1, Ch1.09 L10)
- When Erin first looks at him closely, his robes hang thin on his frame and he is in rough shape, though he eats with the energy of two men. (Book 1, Ch1.09 L113-114)
- The huge skeletal creature wreathed in slime and darkness that frightened Erin was an illusion: struck by her flung pot it vanished, leaving Pisces unconscious on the floor with a bruise forming. (Book 1, Ch1.09 L5)
- He introduces himself as Pisces, practitioner of magic and student of Wistram Academy, specialised in the Elementalist and Illusionary schools with additional competencies in multiple spell schools; when Erin asks for a hobby, he admits it is Necromancy. (Book 1, Ch1.09 L188-192)
- Pisces is wary of the Wistram title system: he insists "mage" is the proper general term and defines wizards as arcane researchers who study the mainstream branches, sorcerers as self-educated casters with little formal education, warlocks as drawing power from sources such as summoning, and witches as practising alchemy along with specialised schools. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L46-51)

## Magic and Necromancy
- Pisces can turn invisible, and mumbling and touching his belt makes him vanish with a booming echo. (Book 1, Ch1.09 L21-23)
- Pisces escapes the guards by an illusion double: Relc's spear passes through him and Relc concludes he cast an illusion spell and walked off while they chatted. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L305-311)
- Spells Pisces shows in his first fight with Relc and Klbkch include gathering shadows around his hands like a cloak, pushing the air to throw Relc off him, setting a leather cord on fire, a small explosion of lightning that blasts Klbkch into a wall, and a flickering barrier of wind in front of Relc. (Book 1, Ch1.11 L43,L95-100,L124)

- Pisces says the acid flies have few predators and are nearly limitless in number; the specimens in jars are only males, whose purpose is to gather food and absorb nutrition for the substantially larger females. (Book 1, Ch1.22 L95-98)
- Pisces knows [Invisibility], which he calls a 3rd Tier spell acquired after much practice and study, and one of his fortes. (Book 1, Ch1.28 L103-105)
