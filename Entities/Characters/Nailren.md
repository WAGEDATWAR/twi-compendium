# Nailren
> Type: person | Also called: Nailren of the Fletchsing Tribe | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.27
> See also: Earlia, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Nailren is a Gnoll of the Fletchsing Tribe and the leader of the Pride of Kelia, a Silver-rank adventuring team from the south. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L254,L357)
- Nailren's pelt is light grey, and the nine armed Gnolls of his team range in colour from that grey to russet red with black stripes. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L241)
- Nailren says all-Gnoll adventuring teams are common further south, and that the Pride of Kelia came north to tackle Liscor's dungeon. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L257)
- Nailren has the Skill [Double Shot], which drops a Raskghar with two arrows in the chest. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L355)
- The Pride of Kelia includes a Gnoll who wears beads and feathers and may be a [Shaman]. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L143)
## Dungeon Work
- {event} Nailren leads the Gnolls in covering Gemhammer while Earlia's team searches Raskghar corpses in the dungeon. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L743-748)
- {event} Nailren spots the Facestealer ahead, aims his bow, and orders both Silver-rank teams to spread out and send emergency [Message] spells. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L759-772)
- Nailren was paralyzed by the Facestealer alongside Earlia's team, and the Facestealer bent toward him as he snarled. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L799-805)
