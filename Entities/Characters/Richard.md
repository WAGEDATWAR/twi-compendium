# Richard
> Type: person | Also called: Richard Davenport; Sir Richard; the Champion of Rhir | First appears: Ch1.00C
> See also: Emily, Tom, Lord Hayvon, Cirille, Keith, Chole
## Overview
- Richard is a [Knight] from Earth who leads the group summoned to Rhir, and Tom calls him the strongest person in the group by far. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L19)
- Richard grew up in Mississippi on a ranch, and knows how to ride a horse and tends his own. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L271)
- Richard wears a cowboy Stetson over his armor, and Tom admits he pulls it off. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L10)
- Richard says "Ma'am" and "Sir" to everyone no matter how often he is corrected, since in Rhir people are called Miss or Mister. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L147-148)
- Richard is a young man with dark skin who wears a wide-brimmed hat and plate armor. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L199)
## Level and class
- Richard is a Level 26 [Knight]. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L19)
- {state} Later, Richard and Emily are over Level 30. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L84)
- {state} Later, Richard is a [Hero] as well, like Edward, who has decided to fight the Demons. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L258,L317)
- {state} Later, a report says that some of those under Lord Hayvon, like Richard, are approaching Level 40. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L172)
- Richard is Muslim, which differs from Lord Hayvon's view that the gods are dead. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L430)
## Equipment and abilities
- Richard flies in the air with Boots of Flight, which are slower than a horse. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L229-231)
- Richard wears an enchanted red cloak and carries a Ring of Featherfalling. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L230,L260)
- Richard wields a massive sword as though it weighed nothing at the breach. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L151)
