# Quallet
> Type: person | Also called: Quallet Marshhand; Captain Marshhand | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.02D
> See also: Ken, Luan, Daly, Geneva, Exara, Xalandrass, Gravetender's Fist
## Overview
- Quallet Marshhand is a Human Level 32 [Mercenary Captain] who commands the suppression company Gravetender's Fist. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L15; Book 5, Ch1.04D L117)
- Quallet has the Skill [Talent Seeker], which lets him find the best recruits in any group. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L19)
- Quallet uses [Commanding Presence] to stare down a challenging recruit. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L97)
- Quallet has light skin tanned from days in the sun, and later bears several scars. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L97; Book 10, Ch6.04D L98)
## Gravetender's Fist
- {belief} Quallet reflects that only Niers Astoragon or Valka Cerethrian could win every recruit, and the Four Great Companies' leaders never recruit in such grounds. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L13; Book 5, Ch1.02D L53)
- {event} Quallet signs over eighty recruits in one night, doubling the size of Gravetender's Fist. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L53)

- {event} Quallet punches a recruit who drops his sword in drills and threatens to leave shirkers in the forest to rot. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L106-108)
- Quallet kills a Ghoul with his axe at blinding speed during a night patrol in the valley. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L113-114)
- Quallet carries the company flag, a fist shooting out of soil on glowing cloth, so neither army treats suppression companies as enemies. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L182)
- {event} Quallet pulls the whole company back within range of the banner and orders them to move together after losing soldiers. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L21)
- {event} Quallet thanks Geneva for saving one of his soldiers and offers silver and copper coins, and she refuses fees. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L117-126)
