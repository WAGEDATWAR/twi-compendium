# Agnes
> Type: person | Also called: Miss Agnes | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.15
> See also: Erin Solstice, Jasi, Maran, Frenzied Hare, Celum
## Overview
- Miss Agnes is the [Innkeeper] and owner of the Frenzied Hare, an inn in Celum. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L7)
- Agnes works hard to support her sick husband, and Erin sees her as good to friends and family but no better than that. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L46; Book 5, Ch4.12 L423)
## Book 3
- Agnes says she has little free time and no coin for books, and that she has a pint when the dinner rush is slow. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L225,L234)
- Agnes says she closes her inn most of the day on city holidays. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L240)
- Agnes told Erin she wanted to help Jasi find another job, suggesting that being a [Barmaid] was not for everyone. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L22-26)
- Erin realised Agnes was not the person she had thought, merely a woman good to her friends and family. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L45-46)
- Erin offered to take over Agnes' inn for a while. (Book 3, Ch3.16 L172)
## Book 5
- Maran and Safry say Agnes docks pay, cannot cook, and screams at staff over wrong orders. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L416)
- They say Agnes is better when her husband is well, and that her workers get no tips. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L418-419)
- Agnes was away from her inn with her husband, having hired another [Barmaid] and a [Cook] because business was good. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L386-388)
- {rel} Agnes stormed into Erin's inn and screamed that Erin had stolen her best workers and her business. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L4,L7,L45)
## Book 19
- Agnes greeted Jasi at the Frenzied Hare, calling her the star of Invrisil. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L996-998)
- {belief} Agnes says Celum will never be the same after the Bloodfeast Raiders attack, and that she and Jerom could not sleep for weeks. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1016-1018)
- {belief} Agnes says Maran and Safry are at Ulna's, who took them in. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1025)
- Agnes and Erin had not parted on good terms. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1022)
