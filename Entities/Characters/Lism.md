# Lism
> Type: person | Also called: the Drake shopkeeper who cheated Erin; Councilman Lism | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.19
> See also: Krshia Silverfang, Olesm Swifttail, Erin Solstice, Selys, Liscor Council, Zevara
## Overview
- Lism is a Drake [Shopkeeper] who runs a stall on Market Street in Liscor and is known as a vocal anti-Human voice. (Book 1, Ch1.31 L21-22; Book 8, Ch5.41 L512)
- Lism is Olesm's uncle. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L136; Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L217)
- Lism is Krshia Silverfang's long-standing rival on Market Street, the two being the top salespeople there. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L586)
## First meeting with Erin, Books 1-2
- {event} A Drake shopkeeper who sold Erin food and a bag refuses her a refund, pointing to a sign saying the store gives none, and when she produces his own written price list he wagers a chess game instead: if she wins against his chosen player, he repays her money. (Book 1, Ch1.19 L41-48,L63-68)
- Erin identifies this shopkeeper as Lism, and Krshia says Lism gave her the heavy bag of flour on purpose without warning her. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L135-136)
- Lism screams at his store and his nephew Olesm after the chess game that Erin wins. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L136)
- {event} Lism accuses Erin of robbing him of merchandise and of cheating his nephew at chess, after a guardsman says Shopkeeper Lism was the latest victim of petty thefts in the city. (Book 1, Ch1.31 L13-22,L43)
- {belief} Lism claims that humans have caused only trouble since arriving, that they steal, will take Drake jobs, and attack people on the streets. (Book 1, Ch1.31 L36)
- Lism ducks back into his stall when Krshia glares at him. (Book 1, Ch1.31 L58)
- {event} Lism turns around and walks away when he sees Krshia unwrap huge broadhead arrows. (Book 2, Ch2.03 L173-174)
- {event} After a fire destroys a stall, Lism blames the Humans for destroying parts of the city twice, first by unleashing the undead; Krshia tells him to be quiet, and Erin tells him he is not a nice person, unlike Olesm. (Book 2, Ch2.19 L258-281)
- {event} Lism grins and laughs with friends while eating nuts as the crowd watches the thief girl being exiled. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L112)
## Rivalry with Krshia, Books 7-10
- {event} Lism and his Drake friends plan to bid on all the good meat at an auction, but Krshia outbids him, boasting that he could not tell a prime cut from entrails. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L127,L164)
- {event} After the second Raskghar attack, Lism stands on his stall on Market Street and tells a crowd the Watch failed them and that Human-lovers are in charge. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L509-514)
- {belief} Lism accuses Erin of employing undead and letting Goblins work at her inn, and suggests the Goblins told the Raskghar where and when to strike. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L515)
- {belief} Lism says Erin may be a Human spy and that he heard she met Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L521)
- {event} Lism shouts from his stall that the inn is a den of criminals when Lyonette walks through Market Street, blaming it for riots and for nearly drowning him. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L279-283)
- Krshia says Lism has been angry since someone tried to drown him in the baths, and the Watch warned him twice to be quiet while he argued they favoured others by not investigating; she wears wax earplugs against him. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L292-297)
## The Council election, Books 11-12
- {belief} Lism tells Krshia that Gnolls have lived in Liscor only about a decade and that Drakes built the city and have lived in it for centuries. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L582)
- {event} Lism objects to Krshia's election plan, saying the Council has led Liscor well and that elections are a power grab, and he rejects her proposal that the Antinium build larger walls. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L588-613)
- {event} Lism rushes to the Mage's Guild and demands a highest-priority [Message] to Liscor's High Command, but the receptionists refuse without the Watch Captain's or Council's authorisation. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L623-637)
- {belief} Lism tells a rally the army defended Liscor and promises three more companies stationed in Liscor permanently, claiming he has spoken with High Command, which has approved funding the walls with army money. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L662-664)
- {event} Lism consults supporters including the old [Seamstress] Shazzi and Cellic, a Drake veteran in his mid-thirties with a scarred skull, who is half-asleep at the meeting. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L688-696)
- {event} Lism adopts a "Jobs for Liscor" platform of hiring Drakes and Gnolls rather than the Antinium to expand the wall, a path through the hills around the Bloodfields, and funding the Watch. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L794,L816-825)
- {belief} Lism claims three Walled Cities have each pledged to cover five percent of the wall costs. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L833)
- {event} Lism presents Senior Guardsman Jeiss and the Mage's Guild head Alonna Swiftwing as candidates alongside himself. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L860)
- {event} Lism fields three Gnoll candidates in Gnoll-heavy districts and demands that the Antinium swear under a truth spell that the Hive has no plans against Liscor. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L602-604)
- {belief} Lism calls the Dullahans traditional allies of the Drakes, and Maughin corrects him that the species were great enemies in the time of Dragons. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L678-680)
- {event} Lism argues against trusting the Antinium in a debate, while his nephew Olesm whispers to him. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L212,L217)
- {belief} Lism promises to execute the Minotaur Calruz if elected, claiming Calruz's team released the undead from the crypts and that he sent the Raskghar against citizens. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L237,L243,L250)
- {event} Hooded, Lism visits the inn the night before the election, advises Rufelt and Lasica to consider adopting a child since Liscor has children needing homes, and says he will tax the inn if elected. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L363-381)
- Lism tells Rufelt he came to see the inn because his nephew talks about it so much, and says he will do what is right for Liscor but disagrees with Silverfang about what that is. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L374-379)
## Councilman, Books 13-19
- {event} Lism concedes the Council cannot resist Wistram's mages, saying they have been outmanoeuvred like a Level 50 [Merchant] with thugs, but he moves to send Hawk to Fissival's Draconae Scholarium to ask about replacing Wistram's services and to co-sign Zevara's complaint. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L147-150,L170-174)
- Lism sits with Olesm at the inn instead of pestering Drassi about Erin. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L68)
- {rel} Lism started the [Carers] initiative, which pays volunteers to check on vulnerable residents across Liscor and which he has run for years; Krshia helps organise it. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L907-919)
- Lism pays about forty people to run the [Carers] project and wants a place for the many children who live on the street. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L911,L933)
- {event} Lism proposes selling more land to Pallassian [Senators] and Salazsar Wall Lords at a 23% markup and expanding the city a second time to supply it. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L995-1008)
- {event} Lism offers the Antinium six square miles above ground, with digging limited to ten feet, and Belgrade agrees to relay the offer to the Free Queen and Xrn. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L1134-1146)
- {reveal} Krshia kisses a distracted Lism in the conference room, revealing her attraction to him, part dislike and part odd infatuation. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L1201-1202)
- {event} Lism tells Krshia the Raskghar are held by Watch Captain Zevara, and when Zevara objects to killing every Raskghar, asks what she proposes; the Council defers to her authority as Watch Captain. (Book 19, Ch7.52 L423-448)
- {event} Lism slaps and punches Scalespeaker Yisht of Hectval for slandering the Gnolls, tells him he owes Krshia, Elirr and Raekea an apology, and bans Pallass from Liscor for a week. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1124-1137)
- {event} Lism puts to a Council vote the end of negotiations with Hectval, and the Council agrees and has the delegation escorted out. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L1147-1154)
