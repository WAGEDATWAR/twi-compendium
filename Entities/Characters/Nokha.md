# Nokha
> Type: person | Also called: awakened female Raskghar | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.43
> See also: Calruz, Ceria Springwalker, Elirr, Mrsha, Raskghar, Bearclaw, Soot
## Overview
- Nokha is a female Raskghar, taller and bigger than the others, who keeps her clarity of thought even during the full moon. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L25,L160)
- Nokha carries a glowing yellow enchanted blade that bites into steel. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L25; Book 8, Ch5.44 L60)
- Calruz calls Nokha quite intelligent for a Raskghar and a competent warrior who led the raid on Liscor. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L356)
- Nokha is one of the awakened Raskghar, created in a ritual in which Gnolls were killed on a stone altar and their hearts eaten. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L148-152,L160)

- {event} Nokha kills a Gold-rank adventurer, a member of the Reldiev Scalers named Cassa, by slashing his throat with the glowing blade. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L25,L81,L160)
