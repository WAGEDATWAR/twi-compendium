# Vaitsha Zectiou
> Type: person | Also called: Highborn Vaitsha; Vaitsha | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.54K
> See also: Quarass of Germina, Trey Atwood
## Overview
- Vaitsha Zectiou is a Highborn of Germina, a woman in her thirties and clearly noble, who has pledged to be the Quarass's companion. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L460-461)
- Vaitsha is one of the three protectors sworn by blood to serve the Quarass. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L393)
- Vaitsha is a beautiful woman of Germina who is white as a sheet and wobbles a little when she greets Trey. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L64)
## The Poison Change
- Vaitsha's class is [Highborn of Coin], which the Quarass calls weak and tells her how to change into a rare and deadly class. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L393)
- Vaitsha wears gloves and sips from a vial, looking paler than the boy beside her. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L473)
- {reveal} The Quarass tells Trey that Vaitsha ingests poison and antidote daily, in growing quantities, so that her blood becomes poison. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L704-706)
- Later, the Quarass says Vaitsha will become a [Viperborn], a [Serpent Lady], or another class depending on factors outside her control. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L234)
- Vaitsha must take another poison as a numbing medicine to stay sane, and will soon be bedridden and hand-fed. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L198)
- The Quarass says Vaitsha chose the risk herself, and her chance of death is one in sixty. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L204,L208-210)
- Khalid, a boy of Ger and a [Street Tough] who is the Quarass's protector, trains with tinctures and baths to gain twice a normal man's strength. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L213-216)
