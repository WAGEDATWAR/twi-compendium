# Eloise
> Type: person | Also called: Witch Eloise; Eloise du Havin; the Tea Witch | First appears: Ch6.37E
> See also: Hedag, Mavika, Califor, Belavierr, Rehanna, Laken
## Overview
- Eloise is an old [Witch] who arrives in Riverfarm describing herself as a travelling trader specialising in teas, flowers and herbs. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L306,L313)
- Eloise carries an heirloom porcelain cup and wears a grey travelling hat, and her teas and biscuits put Rie and others at ease. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L298-299,L303-307)
- Eloise serves tea at a table with a [Carpenter], a Lancrel [Councilwoman] and a mother from one of the towns. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L299-302)
- {belief} Eloise tells Miss Safey she has a village of her own and no children or relatives who would care for her. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L322)
- Wiskeria says Eloise is very skilled at herbal magic and tea, lives in one village and generally helps people out. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L577)
- Wiskeria says Eloise has no secret and is just nice. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L585)
## Craft
- Eloise says her talent is tea, sociability and medicines, and that she puts her magic into her tea. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L406)
- Eloise says that when she sits with people and offers tea and advice, she makes them feel better and takes a little of their good feeling. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L433)
- Eloise's magic tea made from local roots tastes far better than the identical plain tea. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L434-440)
- Eloise says her tea loses its effect within a few days, and neither [Preservation] spells nor bags of holding preserve it. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L441)
- Eloise prepares a second pot of tea merely by touching it while Nanette struggles to ignite sticks. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L418)
- Eloise says kindness asks questions and that spreading it is her craft, her reason for existing and the root of her magic, unlike Belavierr's. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L903)
## Against Belavierr
- Eloise warns the circle that Belavierr offers charms that can be very good, but that no one should accept a deal she seeks out. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L887)
- Eloise tells Rie that she seeks the same person Belavierr seeks, and asks Rie to convince Rehanna to sit with her. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L890-892)
- Eloise sits with the grieving Rehanna, who had a husband, a home and a child before fleeing the Goblins. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L893-899,L909)
- Eloise, with Hedag and Mavika, helps take about fifty children and parents out of the fire under fire-resistance charms. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L712,L716,L721-723)
- Eloise slaps Alevica and tells her that flying with a spent mana pool would get her killed by the Drake. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L709-712)
- Eloise tells Alevica to come with them, since Mavika has agreed to fly with the group. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L712)
- Eloise trembles with fury and swears by her hat that no [Witch] in the world will give Belavierr shelter or aid after Califor's death. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L1135-1136)
- Eloise is carried by Charlay to Gralton's lands in two days. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L251-261)
## Riverfarm and the Unseen Empire
- Eloise, an old [Tea Witch], judges that the Halrac team is honest because the boy Cade laughs and talks freely. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L248-251)
- Eloise notes the strong artifact of an invisible bow on Halrac's back. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L248)
- Eloise and Hedag represent the [Witches] on Laken's council, having accepted when asked. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L19)
- Eloise signals Laken by tipping her hat up for a smile and down for a frown, and uses it when giving advice. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L33,L139)
- Eloise names herself the most suited healer in the region to cure the poisoned Lords, and says she knows of someone as accomplished at poisoning and curing who would not consent. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L867-869)
- Eloise says she could cure two poisoned [Lords] in time, but that the journey is too dangerous for her to undertake. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L872-875)
- Mavika says protecting Eloise on the journey is not her craft, which is killing, not protecting. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L880-882)
- Eloise says she and Mavika spoke no untruths, since it is unwise for [Witches] to lie. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L946)
- Eloise says she would never risk the trip to House Veltras, even with its army, as it would escalate into open conflict. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L946,L953)
- Eloise offers to decipher the poison afflicting Lords Hethon and Sammial from Riverfarm, warning that there will be reprisals. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L957-960)
- Eloise asks that the Unseen Empire declare itself the beneficiary of [Witches] and that [Witches] receive land in perpetuity, perhaps a small forest. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L970-976)
- Laken now regards Eloise as his most knowledgeable and trustworthy advisor. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L951)
