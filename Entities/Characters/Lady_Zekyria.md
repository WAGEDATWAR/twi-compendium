# Lady Zekyria
> Type: person | Also called: Zekyria | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.02C
> See also: Fool, Blighted King, Blighted Queen
## Overview
- Lady Zekyria is a [Lady] of the Blighted King's court and the twin sister of Lady Theras. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L102,L105)
- A [Chamberlain] says Lady Zekyria and her sister lost their children unexpectedly three months earlier, and both have been in mourning since. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L341)
- Lady Zekyria wears a pink and red outfit like a riding dress with trousers, and Tom finds her fierce even in mourning. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L67-69)
- Lady Zekyria has a husband and had a child. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L126)
## Opinions
- Lady Zekyria argues that using [Assassins] against the Demon King is a mistake, since Rhir has no Assassin's Guild. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L65,L70)
- Lady Zekyria dismisses the notion of contracting an Assassin's Guild on another continent. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L72-74)
- Lady Zekyria reassures her twin and reminds her that Lady Reinhart is an ally of the kingdom. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L105-110)
- Lady Zekyria dismisses the Fool's sufferings, noting he was taken prisoner while Tom fought. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L124-125)
- Lady Zekyria would like Lord Nereshal to halt the aging of more individuals in the realm. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L96-97)
## The ritual
- {reveal} Lady Zekyria stands with the other ladies in light red, nearly pink and white, mourning her dead child, after the Fool explains crimson. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L490-491)
- {reveal} The Fool reveals that the miscarriages the ladies mourn were not a curse but the price of the summoning ritual. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L489,L494)
