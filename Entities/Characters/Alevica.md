# Alevica
> Type: person | Also called: the Witch Runner; Alevica of the Glokhen Coven | First appears: Ch6.35
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Charlay, Mavika, Hedag, Eloise, Califor, Belavierr
## Overview
- Alevica is a City Runner who is also a [Witch], a young woman in her late twenties with light blue hair and bright pale pink irises. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L227-228)
- Alevica wears robes and a hat like a [Mage]'s, and moves like an athlete. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L228)
- Ryoka feels a strange tugging in her chest, like the wind refusing to obey her, when Alevica walks in. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L229)
- Alevica is sent by her mentor Oliyaya of the Glokhen Coven, who is busy cleaning up attacks on [Witches]. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L106)
- Alevica holds a Runner's Seal, which she shows Califor. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L109)
- Califor calls Alevica the most gifted [Witch] of her generation at her age, whose magic is self-destructive, and says such [Witches] often end up in early graves. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L474)
- Califor says Alevica is a prodigy who took six years to learn to fly. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L389)
## Reputation as a Runner
- Alevica is one of the least-trusted Runners though a good one, and Charlay says she opens mail and threatens other Runners. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L258)
- Alevica will throw away important packages rather than deliver them if a client insults her, and no Courier or local Guildmaster will sponsor her. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L258)
- Charlay is terrified of Alevica, who calls her "horse-girl", and Charlay tells Ryoka to do as Alevica says. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L231-234,L394)
- Alevica takes the Steel-box delivery worth eighteen gold and seven silver, with a double penalty, and counts out gold at once. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L261-263)
- Alevica later rides in a hired travel-carriage with three other passengers. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L371)
- Charlay says Alevica once cut in line at the guild, and that her tail later caught fire on the road, taking four healing potions to stop. (Book 12, Ch6.35 L401-404)
## Abilities
- Alevica flies standing on a broom as if it were a skateboard. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L486)
- Alevica fights from her broom with a crossbow, thrown vials of fire and smoke, and a sword, and raises skeletons from the earth. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L508-525)
- Alevica dismisses the skeletons with a sign and they collapse into dust. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L525)
- Alevica kills the bandit group, and Charlay says she took their money pouches and perhaps their potions. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L526,L535)
- Alevica dries herself and her clothes by magic after swimming in the river in her undergarments and hat. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L390,L448)
## Visit to Riverfarm
- Alevica tells Califor and Nanette she has not seen them in two or three years and says she is a City Runner these days. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L92-93,L109)
- Califor tells Alevica she knows she killed sixteen [Bandits] and advises burning the bodies next time. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L121)
- Alevica tells Califor that Agratha may not attend because a [Witch] was killed and she lost her schoolhouse. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L113)
- Alevica fails to cut the thread sewn through Ryoka's lips with a charmed knife and hurls the knife into the floor. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L883-889)
- Mavika removes the thread with Alevica's knife and a Griffin charm, and Alevica tells Ryoka she now owes her a favour. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L897-922)
- {belief} Alevica says Hedag has a bigger bounty than hers, and that Hedag leaves the dead in villages she passes. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1170-1173)
## The Witch Hunter and the fires
- Alevica confronts a smiling man who calls himself a scout or hunter, and accuses him of being a [Witch Hunter] who put a [Knight] on Belavierr. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L590-591)
- Alevica fires a bolt into the man's side, and he stabs her in the stomach with a poisoned spear. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L596-600)
- {state} Alevica is left poisoned and screaming as he walks away, saying she can hope someone finds her. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L600-604)
- {reveal} A man matching the hunter's description paid Fierre to alert the Order of Seasons, according to Fierre's report to Rie. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L699)
- {reveal} The narration shows a Drake arsonist who set the fires paid the [Bandits] to attack the [Witches] and had spared a [Witch] earlier. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L822,L830)
- Alevica sleeps under Eloise's watchful gaze after being saved. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L697-698)
- Alevica stumbles up after the ritual with no power left, and Eloise slaps her and tells her flying would get her killed. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L709-712)
- Alevica thinks Eloise and Hedag are not powerful [Witches] by her standards, and that only Mavika and Califor made her come. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L481-482)
