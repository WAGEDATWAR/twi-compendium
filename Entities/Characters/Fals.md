# Fals
> Type: person | Also called: Fleetfoot Fals | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.01R
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Garia, Persua, Magnolia Reinhart, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Fals is a tall, blonde, tanned senior Runner who wears red clothing of better quality than the other Runners around him and carries two daggers at his belt. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L61)
- Fals is one of the most influential Runners in all the northern city-states, and the local Guildmasters listen to him. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L66)
- Fals is twenty-three years old and is nicknamed Fleetfoot Fals. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L84,L100)
- Garia says Fals can read and has been seen reading books, which most folk cannot do. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L61)
- Ryoka sees Fals as dirty blonde, handsome and athletic, and thinks he is Garia's type; he has straight, nearly white teeth. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L18-20)
- Fals's Skills are geared towards sensing danger and preserving stamina and he has no movement Skills, so he admits Ryoka can beat him in a race even without levels. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L162)

## Book 1 and 2: Celum's Runners and Ryoka
- Fals tells Ryoka that Magnolia's request is a good delivery and asks her to let other Runners take it next time, since Runners like Magnolia, who tips well and shares her deliveries. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L77-90)

- {event} Fals apologises to Ryoka on behalf of the Guild and himself after her delivery, offering to let her take any Magnolia request she likes, and warns her that Persua left the Guild with her group that morning and will probably try something. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L56-61,L90)
- {belief} Ryoka reports that Fals says Runners must band together to survive; Cervial replies that Fals is no coward but represents much of what he dislikes about the Runner's Guild, though he is not wrong. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L227-229)
- {event} Fals tells Ryoka that if she never levels up she will never become a Courier, that she is one of the Runners' best but nowhere near fast enough, and that in a year or two even Garia could outrun her. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L148-150)
- {event} Fals says the Runner's Guild in Esthelm might have requests going down to Liscor and that he will talk to the Guildmaster there, then asks Ryoka if she would like to get a bite at a pub with a chef who just learned [Advanced Cooking]. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L123-130)

## Book 3: Persua and Celum
- Fals is absent from the party in Celum, which is conspicuous because Persua follows him about wherever possible. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L242)
- {state} Fals avoids Celum on purpose because Persua took work from the City Runners after getting the [Double Step] Skill, and he could not make her listen. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L163)
- Fals tells Ryoka that he has been a [Runner] for about sixteen years and was the best in the area until she showed up, with all his Skills stamina-based and defensive. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L113,L183)

## Book 15 to 19
- Fals is a Human City Runner who works around Celum, with the Skills [Surefoot], [Efficient Run] and [Thousand-Step Sprint]. (Book 15, Ch7.00 L78)
- Fals has been to Erin's inn before and knows the [Innkeeper]. (Book 15, Ch7.00 L179)
- {event} Fals delivers a heavy wrapped package that arrived from the north to Erin Solstice at the inn, getting a seal so that he can swear it was delivered; Erin opens it in her room and finds a Go board that is a twin of her chess board. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L942-991)
- {event} After the Bloodfeast Raiders attack, Fals finds Ryoka shot through the shoulder in the ash, gives her a potion that closes the wound, and tells her it is not her fault. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L266-269,L387)
- {event} Fals reports that Liscor and Esthelm are sending aid through Wailant's door after word reached Wailant. (Book 17, Ch7.25 L380)
- {belief} Fals fears that Kevin's bicycle will be the end of Runners; Ryoka answers that it will change running but not end it and that Runners will simply become Couriers. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1082-1094)
- Erin jokes that the bicycle might take Fals' job away. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L1117)
