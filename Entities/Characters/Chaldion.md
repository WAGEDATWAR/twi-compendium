# Chaldion
> Type: person | Also called: Grand Strategist Chaldion; the Grand Strategist of Pallass | First appears: Ch6.38
> See also: Grimalkin, Saliss, Erin Solstice, Lyonette, Edellein Blackwing, Duln
## Overview
- Chaldion is an old, nearly completely grey Drake with several facial scars and an eye patch over his right eye, who is as old as Tekshia and as tough as steel. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L674)
- Chaldion is the [Grand Strategist] of Pallass, in charge of its armies, and is over Level 50 according to Sir Relz. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L1204; Book 15, Ch7.03 L787)
- Chaldion is one hundred and six years old and looks to be in his seventies, with many potions behind that appearance. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L192,L342)
- Chaldion has a missing eye replaced with magical gemstones that can kill with a look, and swaps them under his eye patch. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L276; Book 15, Ch7.06 L182,L706)
- Chaldion wears a blue gemstone eye for ordinary use that flashes bright light and a [Truesight] effect. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L695-697; Book 15, Ch7.07 L32)
- Chaldion swaps in a fiery red gemstone for the thunderous city-wide announcement, and a white clouded gemstone when he casts [Scrying]. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L1205; Book 15, Ch7.06 L706)
- Chaldion uses a magical cane for support, which gives him a jolt of stability. (Book 16, ChInterlude_The_Hangover_After L31)
- Chaldion uses a ring and his eye-patch gem to create a bubble of privacy for whispered conversations. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L131; Book 16, Ch7.11 L40)
- Chaldion's favourite drink is the Chaldion's Eye, a Firebreath Whiskey and Ultasian bitter cocktail with an ice eye holding sweet wine. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L18-20)
## Skills and chess
- Chaldion beats Erin at chess with a split attack that wins her queen, and names his Skill as [Path to Victory]. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L702-706)
- Chaldion flips up his eye patch to confirm with [Truesight] that Erin is not using magic or a Skill. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L695-697)
- Chaldion asks Erin about the games Go and Shogi. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L706)
- Chaldion says his Skill shows only one route to victory, not the optimal one, and Erin changed strategy to defeat him on the third game. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L41)
- Chaldion says that a sleeping Skill of Erin's means she is at that level, and is not very powerful. (Book 16, ChInterlude_The_Hangover_After L17)
- Chaldion plays Bird at chess and says he wants to talk about his Hive. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L208-215)
## Meeting Erin in Pallass
- Chaldion tells Warden Grasfur to pretend Saliss' incident never occurred, and takes over Erin's case. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L918)
- Chaldion tells Saliss that his rank gives him privilege and uses an aura on him before it fades. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L960-968)
- Chaldion releases Erin on condition she play another chess game with him. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L951-953)
- Chaldion agrees to drink at the inn with Lasica and Rufelt after the arrest. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L1062-1069)
- {belief} Chaldion's look convinces Erin that the attack is nobody's fault, since the gods were dead and a Dragon meddled. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L1202)
- Chaldion puts out an inquiry on Erin and finds nothing. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L44-47)
## The Wyvern attack on Pallass
- Chaldion issues a city-wide alarm on his authority as [Grand Strategist] and orders 1st Army to the walls. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L1204-1208)
- Chaldion opens a gateway to the walls, bringing General Duln and nearly four hundred [Soldiers] through. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L720-732)
- Chaldion uses [Path to Victory] and says he sees one road. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L749-751)
- Chaldion orders all fliers to land within forty seconds and aims Pallass' wall lightning spells inside the city. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L785,L799)
- Chaldion counts deviations from his vision and murmurs "Stay alive, Miss Erin Solstice", having not seen her in it. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L783,L802-804)
- Chaldion orders Noass and Sir Relz restrained, the scrying mirror confiscated and anti-divination wards cast, ending the broadcast. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L17-23)
## Dealings with Lyonette and Grimalkin
- Chaldion visits the inn with Grimalkin and greets Lyonette as Lady du Marquin. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L174-175)
- {reveal} Chaldion tells Lyonette in private that she is Princess du Marquin and does not hide her heritage well. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L185)
- Chaldion advises Lyonette to change her hair colour and name and buy a charm against [Appraisal], and promises to keep her secret. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L185-188)
- Chaldion fails to find Mrsha by [Scrying] around the inn. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L706-721)
- Chaldion tells the [Generals] of the 1st and 4th armies to form experimental regiments based on Grimalkin's training plans. (Book 16, ChInterlude_The_Hangover_After L32,L53)
- Chaldion foresees commuters from Esthelm using the magic door and compares Erin to a quarter of Saliss. (Book 16, ChInterlude_The_Hangover_After L44-48)
- Chaldion appears on the scrying broadcast with Noass and says he would retreat to the capital and pull back from the Jecrass and Medain fronts. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L98-114)
- {belief} Chaldion calls Belchan's chosen defensive position a mistake and says the army will be slaughtered. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L101-118)
## Injury and Saliss

- Chaldion tells the Walled Cities by [Message] to go to Rhir's hell and stop spreading gossip. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L161)
- Chaldion flips up his eye patch at Saliss, blasting light around the room. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L268-271)
- Chaldion begs Saliss to become Pallass' hero, and Saliss refuses, saying he wants to choose. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L382-403)
- Chaldion calls Saliss a disgrace after he insists on being himself. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L409)
- Chaldion says he knows enough about Turnscales to say it is not a choice to act like what you are. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L396)
- {belief} Chaldion suspects the Goblin Lord used necromancy, since a single Goblin Lord spellcaster beat the Tidebreaker unseen. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L372)
- Chaldion is de-aged by two years by the treatment, and has a hunch he does not share. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L373)
