# The [Enforcer]
> Type: person | Also called: Brother of Serendipitous Meetings | First appears: ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1
> See also: Numbtongue, Yellow Splatters
## Overview
- The [Enforcer] is an unnamed, high-level criminal who goes only by his class, [Enforcer], and strolls into Celum unnoticed. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L25-26)
- The [Enforcer] is a member of the Brotherhood of Serendipitous Meetings, whose branches turn up in unexpected places and whose members keep standards. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L27,L61)
- The [Enforcer] wears a cap he treats with care, and brushing something off it relaxes a tense confrontation. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L60-64)
- The [Enforcer] has a [Grudgescent Terrier] Skill that lets him see the deaths of his associates on the person responsible. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L877)
## Meeting the Sister of Chell
- The [Enforcer] refuses to budge before a [Nightstalker] of the Sisters of Chell, and takes his hat off. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L60)
- A [Blackguard] with the [Nightstalker] freezes mid-strike when the hatless [Enforcer] looks at him. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L61)
- The [Enforcer] says it was just a bit of dust on his cap, and the [Nightstalker] realises she misjudged his level and nature. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.1 L61-64)
## Numbtongue
- The [Enforcer] asks Numbtongue about three Humans who wore caps and died during the siege of Liscor. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L860-861)
- The [Enforcer] hums a rhyme about slow thieves dying that Numbtongue recognises from the thieves who took Erin's door. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L863-868)
- The [Enforcer] asks how Numbtongue crossed paths with his brothers and why he killed them. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L872)
- The [Enforcer] says his Skill [Grudgescent Terrier] lets him see the deaths of his associates on Numbtongue, and asks him not to lie. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L877)
- Numbtongue admits he killed the three men. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L878-879)
- The [Enforcer] concludes that Numbtongue had no warning of the group's arrival and simply saw the boats and the door. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L881)
- The [Enforcer] tells the [Nightstalker] that Numbtongue is a tribe Goblin, and she tells him to step off. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L891-894)
- The [Enforcer] says Numbtongue answered truthfully or his Skills would have said so. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L925)
- The [Enforcer] says his association does not as a rule work with Goblins or non-Humans, but has a few such lads in its ranks. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Numbtongue_Pt.2 L928)
