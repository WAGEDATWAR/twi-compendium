# Nsiia
> Type: person | Also called: Empress Nsiia; Empress Nsiia of Tiqr; Empress of Beasts; Animalfriend Exile | First appears: Ch6.15K
> See also: Flos, Femithain, Vasraf, Thef, Orjin, Revine, Yinah, Alked Fellbow
## Overview
- Nsiia is the Empress of Beasts, ruler of Tiqr, who wears no crown but a headband of feathers from birds she has caught. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L159)
- Nsiia's headband holds a Roc's feather, a magical heirloom of Tiqr, and a bright yellow feather from a sparkling shock-bird. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L159-160)
- Nsiia's headband feather lets her use the powers of the bird it came from when she plucks it. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L160)
- Nsiia carries the Tusk of Tiqr, a spear enchanted as a fairly standard relic-class weapon. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L593,L601)
- Nsiia grew up alongside Thef, a Grand Elephant she calls her friend and protector. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L174; Book 13, Ch6.53K L624)
## Pomle and the old alliance
- {belief} Nsiia tells the other monarchs at Pomle that as a girl she opened her borders to Flos and joined her armies to his because she believed in his cause. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L514)
- {belief} Nsiia says she no longer calls herself Flos' subject, but will not join a plot to attack him and calls the other monarchs cowards hiding in the dunes. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L521)
- Nsiia leaves Pomle with her animals roaring and trumpeting, telling Orjin she enjoyed talking with him most. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L590-591)
## The war on Tiqr
- Nsiia uses [Empire: Wild Riot] as the Wildkeepers prepare a caravan to leave. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L657-658)
- {event} Nsiia loses Thef when Alked Fellbow, a Named Adventurer, brings him down. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L622)
- Wildkeeper Jvaile and the [Druids] tell Nsiia they are leaving because half their circle has died. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L621-625)
- Nsiia tells the [Druids] to take her people, the herds, the young and the elephants to the Kilalle Steppes. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L627-630)
- Nsiia says Tiqr will not yield and that surrender to Savere or Nerrhavia would mean giving her people to slavery. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L633-636)
- Nsiia dons her cracked war-mask, which Fellbow's arrow cracked, and tells her warriors they may leave with her blessing. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L650-654)
- Nsiia's honour guard has lost over half its number, and only a few war elephants remain. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L652)
- Nsiia tells Flos that Thef is dead, slain by an arrow from Alked Fellbow that gave her time to flee. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L625)
- Nsiia says General Vasraf holds the walls of Oliphant while the coalition's ten armies encircle it. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L632)
- Nsiia says six hundred thousand citizens remain in Oliphant and its food will last about two more days. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L662,L670)
- Naili notes Nsiia's headband has one feather left. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L593)
- {event} Nsiia asks Flos to take Tiqr as his subject, pledging her life by blood and asking to be made one of his Seven. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L679)
- Nsiia says Flos can revive the Sorcelled Blades from a hundred enchanted weapons. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L683)
- {belief} Nsiia believes her people are no less than the Dreamers who brought nations to ruin and could become unbreakable if turned into Dreamers. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L688)
- {event} Nsiia sends Femithain a message that makes Viltach say she told him to run. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L847-848)
- {state} Nsiia plucks the Roc's feather, the last gift of her father, and grows as tall as one of her Grand Elephants. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L857-859)
- {state} Nsiia throws down the Tusk of Tiqr and her headband, shrinks, and surrenders Tiqr unconditionally to the Illivere Federation. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L911-915)
- Nsiia tells Femithain she gave Vasraf leave to do as he wished and does not know his plan. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L40)
- Nsiia says she would have surrendered to Deimos or Nerrhavia, but never to Savere. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L47)
- Nsiia says she chose Illivere because Femithain tried to warn her and Illivere has few slaves. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L48,L52)
