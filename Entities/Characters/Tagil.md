# Tagil
> Type: person | Also called: Foreign Hunter Tagil | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.44E
> See also: Gaile, Ser Raim, Belavierr, Sylind
## Overview
- Tagil is a [Witch Hunter] who introduces himself as the Foreign Hunter leading the Hunters of Noelictus on business of the Crown of Noelictus. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L189)
- Tagil wields iconic scissor-swords that he commissioned to fight Belavierr. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L957)
- Tagil has a daughter who is very sick, and even the Healer of Tenbault could not cure her. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L953)
## The Bargain
- {reveal} Tagil says he was captured by Belavierr during the attack on her village, and she made him an offer. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L949)
- {reveal} Tagil says Belavierr saw right through him, and that he had given up hope after a meeting for his daughter failed. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L953)
- {reveal} Tagil tells Sylind that the offer was never for then, but for now. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L958)
- {state} Tagil shoots Sylind with a hidden crossbow, then kills Coroise with his sword, and fires bolts at Faigen and Erashelle, killing all four. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L964-975)
- Erashelle calls Tagil a traitor and hopes he suffers forever before she dies. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L976-979)
- Tagil walks out of Riverfarm past the Knights, whom he placates with lies, following a thread to Belavierr on a hill. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L981-983)
- Tagil tells Belavierr that Raim lives and that he has done his part, and she confirms she has sworn the pact. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L984-987)
- Belavierr says she will keep her promise in the hour after his death, whatever happens to Tagil, and does not promise to tell Raim or his daughter. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L991,L995-996)
- {state} Tagil curses Belavierr, asks her to save his daughter, and hangs himself from a tree on the hill. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L997-998)
## Aftermath
- Prost oversees the burial of Tagil, and the villagers bury him where he fell as a traitor in silence. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L110-111)
- No one asks for Tagil's remains, while the Order carries away the ashes of the other fallen Hunters. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L110)
