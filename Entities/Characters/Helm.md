# Helm
> Type: person | Also called: Master Helm; Mister Helm | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.20E
> See also: Laken, Halrac, Prost, Windrest, Riverfarm
## Overview
- Helm is a [Blacksmith] from Windrest, a village fourteen miles east of Riverfarm by a big prairie. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L377)
- Helm arrives at Riverfarm frozen and nearly falling from his saddle, brought by the Windfrozen Riders. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L375)
- {event} Helm weeps when he sees Riverfarm and hears about the avalanche, and hugs Prost. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L376)
- Helm reports that Windrest is burning. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L377)
## Riverfarm and Windrest
- Helm and Tessia hurry to the larger trebuchet when Laken calls them to demonstrate it to the visiting lords. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L91)
- Helm is a huge, middle-aged [Blacksmith] who greets Halrac with a hug and is proud of him like a father. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L179-182; Book 18, Ch7.40ER L198)
- Helm tells Halrac that most Windrest folk survive, though Old Kessy died with an arrow in her during the Goblin raids. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L183)
- Helm says Eldrim is alive, Turc is now married, and Talaya wrote that she was fine. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L183)
- {belief} Helm says a Goblin Lord nearly overran Riverfarm, but His Majesty dealt with her right quickly. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L201)
- {state} Helm is now a [Royal Blacksmith] with new Skills, including [Perfect Temperature Control], so his steel never overheats. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L205)
