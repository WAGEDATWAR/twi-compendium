# Garusa
> Type: person | Also called: Garusa Weatherfur; General Garusa | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.16
> See also: Thrissiam, Osthia Blackwing, Zel Shivertail, Az'kerash
## Overview
- Garusa Weatherfur is a tall female Gnoll [General] of the Longstride Clan, with light brown fur that is almost blonde. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L10,L15,L60)
- Garusa is one of the lead generals of the Trisstral Alliance, sent as one of its best after Zel Shivertail took leave. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L5,L8)
- Garusa is a specialist in brutal assaults, a reason she was chosen to face the disorganized Goblin armies. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L20)
- Garusa rides a large warhorse stallion and fights with a long spear. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L6,L221)
- Garusa has a clear, deep rumble of a voice. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L10)
## The campaign against the Goblin Lord
- Garusa is high level, higher than Thrissiam, but she declines to lead the joint army and cedes command to him. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L19)
- Garusa asks Thrissiam to leave the attacking to her, since his formations suit fighting Goblins. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L19)
- Garusa says Zel Shivertail warned her a day earlier not to underestimate this Goblin Lord, so she requested extra City Runners and [Scouts] as a reserve. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L24)
- Garusa recalls that Liscor's fabled army was defeated in the Second Antinium War by a single Goblin Lord's army and not by the King. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L28)
- Garusa says she has fought against Oldblood Drakes on occasion but never alongside them. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L81)
- {rel} Garusa carries Thrissiam into a bed and shares it for an hour, after which the soldiers all know what happened and joke about it quietly. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L184,L212-213)
- Garusa tells Thrissiam a [General] must think of victory and not affection. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L202)
- Garusa notes that the [Mages] cannot cast [Message], which would require a mage of far greater ability than a Goblin Lord's forces should have. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L185-188)
- {belief} Garusa says she smells Goblins everywhere but cannot find their scent, and thinks the Goblin Lord retreated to make sure they could not run. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L140)
- {event} Garusa rescues Osthia from the Goblin ranks by riding in, killing a Hobgoblin and pulling her into the saddle. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L220-221)
- Garusa defends Osthia against Thrissiam's anger, saying she might have gone for the Goblin Lord in her place. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L231)
- {event} Thrissiam and Garusa plan an ambush on Day 66 against the Goblin Lord's alternating forward camps. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L252-259,L267)
- {event} A black lance of magic kills Garusa's stallion, and she charges the Goblin Lord and faces him with his black blade in hand. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L272-275)
## Death
- {state} Garusa is killed by a black bolt from the Goblin Lord, whose body a voice calling his apprentice now speaks through. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L283-288)
- {state} Garusa and those the black scythe killed rise as undead, and Thrissiam sees the hole in her chest and the light in her eyes. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L289-292)
- Thrissiam realizes Garusa has been raised as a Draugr, a higher undead that only a very high-level [Necromancer] could animate. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L334-336)
- {state} Thrissiam kills the undead Garusa with his spear and the [Crescent Arc] Skill. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L339-343)
- Hawk later reports that Garusa Weatherfur is dead. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L500)
- {belief} A red-scaled Drake at a table says Garusa and Thrissiam are two of the finest [Generals], dead with over twenty thousand Gnolls and Drakes, and that vital information about undead was not passed to them. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L385)
