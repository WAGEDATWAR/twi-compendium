# Levil
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.00
> See also: Tally, Ullica, Pithfire Hounds, Ryoka Griffin
## Overview
- Levil is a [Pyromancer] and the Captain of the adventuring team the Pithfire Hounds. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L445; Book 12, Ch6.41E L713)
- Levil and his team keep war dogs, including Makki and Dassa, the latter belonging to Bram. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L538)
- Levil's team includes the [Mage] Ullica, Tally, Keima, Lamont and Bram. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L538-541; Book 11, Ch6.26 L593)
## The Wailer Frog fight
- Levil sees Ryoka racing across the grasslands, and throws himself to the ground when she signals him. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L445-455)
- Levil's team puts down the Wailer Frogs with fire after the frogs' lungs are filled with dirt, so the Runner who answered their call saved them twice over. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L539)
- Levil carries a bag of charred Wailer Frog tongues as proof of the kill. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L537)
- The dog Dassa dies to a frog, and the Pithfire Hounds are most torn up about her. (Book 10, Ch6.00 L538; Book 10, Ch6.01 L651)
## Later with Ryoka
- Levil argues with Tally while the Pithfire Hounds wait at Reizmelt's gates, telling her to aim left and wait for his signal. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L578-593)
- Levil conjures an orb of fire while waiting to hit whoever comes out of the city. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L597)
- Ryoka misses Levil and the Pithfire Hounds when far from home. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L713)
- Levil hugs Ryoka, beaming, when she brings the team a new dog from Lord Gralton Radivaek's kennels. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L519-527)
