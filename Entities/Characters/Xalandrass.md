# Xalandrass
> Type: person | Also called: Xal; the war vendor; Naga Xalandrass | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.05D
> See also: Ken, Quallet, Exara, Niers, Peclir Im, Geneva
## Overview
- Xalandrass is a male Naga [Merchant] and war vendor with golden scales and jeweled clothing, at least nine feet tall. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L110-112)
- Xalandrass invites Ken to call him Xal, since he knows Humans are not as adept at speaking as other races. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L120)
- Xalandrass enjoys being admired and wiggles his tail appreciatively when Ken stares at him. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L116)
## Book 5: the valley war
- Xalandrass recognises Ken at once as a Human he has seen before, though they have never spoken. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L110-114)
- Xalandrass says he sold Evercut arrows to the Roving Arrow company and shares some blame for an attack on those who hired them. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L126-128)
- {event} Xalandrass buys an Evercut arrowhead back from Ken for red, white and black paint plus coin. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L125-146)
- Xalandrass offers Ken other wares, including a charm that amplifies the voice, a favourite of commanders like Quallet. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L142)
- Xalandrass fishes for information about the Last Light, and Ken declines to say anything he has no right to. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L142-146)
- Xalandrass hopes Ken will speak of his fairness and send the Last Light to him for healing potions or needles. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L146)
- Xalandrass noted that another person had come to him with a device that made light. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L136)
- Xalandrass has a deep cut down his tail after his camp was attacked and heals it with a potion. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L251)
- Xalandrass crushed at least six soldiers who attacked his camp, breaking their bones with his tail. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L246)
- {event} Xalandrass throws open his stores to supply Gravetender's Fist and others with enchanted weapons and potions. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L251)
- Xalandrass joins forces with the neutral captains and tends his wounds in a corner of Quallet's tent. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L242; Book 5, Ch1.06D L246)
- {belief} Xalandrass suspects both companies attacked Geneva's camp, though he would bet the Dullahans broke conduct first. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L262; Book 5, Ch1.06D L277)
- {belief} Xalandrass warns that both sides will want no witnesses to their attacks on neutral parties and will kill Geneva. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L277-279)
- Xalandrass lends Ken his most powerful voice-amplifying ring so Ken can address the battlefield. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L351-357)
- Ken names Xalandrass, along with Quallet and Exara, as a neutral party in the conflict. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L365)
## Book 10: Niers
- Xalandrass's fortunes turned south after being caught up in the incident with the Razorshard Armor company. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L559-560)
- Xalandrass tries to bribe his way to the front of a queue with two hundred gold pieces, as much as he can afford. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L561-563)
- Xalandrass tells Niers that the Last Light of Baleros took part in the battle, and that he observed her work. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L597)
- Xalandrass shows Niers a flashlight that he obtained from strange Humans on the battlefield. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L605)
- Xalandrass explains he got the flashlight when mercenaries from a suppression company bartered it for alcohol. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L628)
- Xalandrass also shows Niers iPhones, which he took from the dead by trading with Dullahan soldiers. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L639; Book 10, Ch6.07D L650)
- Xalandrass learns the iPhones can be [Repaired] and tells Niers so. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L639)
- {event} Niers buys Xalandrass's information, the artifacts and his silence, offering a price in gold plus business deals. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L690-693)
- Niers has Peclir bring a purple cloth-covered item as payment for the silence of Xalandrass and his [Mage] friend. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L694-703)
