# Dekass
> Type: person | Also called: Prognugator Dekass | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.68
> See also: Tersk, Pawn, Klbkch, Xrn, Free Antinium, Armored Antinium
## Overview
- Dekass is a Prognugator of the Armored Antinium, and one of the few Antinium of his Hive deployed out of it on a non-confrontational mission. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L392; Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L6,L39)
- Dekass has four arms with fingers, a more reinforced build than a typical Soldier, and shorter than Tersk, who has higher status. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L387,L392)
- Dekass does not wear his customary steel armor, which leaves him feeling almost naked. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L392; Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L6)
- Dekass reasons that the Armored Antinium do not do stealth and that Prognugators are too valuable to waste. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L39)
## Arrival in Liscor
- {event} Dekass and Tersk, in disguise, are the first visitors to Liscor after the access tunnel is finished, and Dekass asks Tersk about cats. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L387-392,L411)
- Dekass does not comprehend why they were sent to establish relations before the Grand Queen's envoys to the Free Antinium. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L395)
- Tersk says they are a personal greeting from the Armored Queen and a warning, and Dekass does not understand Tersk's connection to Pawn. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L397-401)
- Tersk asks a Drake and a Gnoll for the nearest Antinium location and for Klbkch, claiming to be normal Workers. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L402-403)
- Dekass reports that Liscor is ignorant of their presence and that the Armored Antinium could take it with a single legion thanks to the tunnel. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L410)
## Visit to the Inn (Book 15)
- {event} Dekass and Tersk eat with Pawn and Yellow Splatters at The Wandering Inn as the first visitors to use the new passage, only two having made the trek. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L9-10)
- {belief} Dekass believes that only two making the trek shows the Free Antinium are not that useful to the other Hives. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L10)
- {belief} Dekass believes the Free Antinium's only valuable assets are their location under Liscor and Klbkch the Slayer. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L21-22)
- Dekass notes that all Hives serve the Grand Queen, and that the Armored Queen has no quarrel with the Twisted Queen or Flying Queen. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L11)
- Dekass says the two-week delay on arriving in the city was regrettable, but that he toured the Free Antinium's facilities thoroughly. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L25)
- Dekass is convinced that his cover is perfect, though the Human did not look twice at the Antinium. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L38)
- Dekass stops cold and shakes after tasting acid fly soup, and Pawn says "Finally." (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L44-46)
- Dekass says that after the food he begins to agree with Tersk's view of the Free Antinium. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L99-100)
- Dekass leaves a tall pile of dishes next to his chair. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L56)
- {event} Dekass and Tersk tell Drassi they are ordinary Antinium and ask to peruse her drinks. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L74,L81)
- Dekass asks Drassi what drunkenness is, says he is undecided about her gender, and pays with gold from a bag of holding issued for the mission. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L91,L108-111)
- Dekass enjoys the rum, which he finds pleasant. (Book 15, ChStory_2_Tersk_and_Dekass L101)
## Book 18
- Dekass and Tersk show up at the inn every day, eating, and then leave, doing nothing else for weeks. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L47-50)
- Xrn has given the Antinium her anti-food-poisoning charm on a wooden amulet, so they can eat without fear of gluten. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L42)
