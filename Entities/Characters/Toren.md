# Toren
> Type: person | Also called: Tor; the masked swordswoman; Miss Swordswoman | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.35
> See also: Erin Solstice, Pisces, Az'kerash, Lyonette, Mrsha, Skinner
## Overview
- Toren is a skeleton created by the mage Pisces and bound to protect and obey Erin Solstice. (Book 2, Ch2.00T L23-31)
- Ceria notes that Toren is quite intelligent for a skeleton, which means Pisces is closer to his goal of an undead that levels. (Book 1, Ch1.39 L35-36)
- Toren cannot speak. (Book 2, Ch2.01T L32)
