# Alais
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Alais; Thunder's Solace captain | First appears: Ch6.19H
> See also: Stan, Ksmvr, Yvlon
## Overview
- Alais is a Silver-rank captain and [Aeromancer] who leads the team Thunder's Solace. (Book 11, Ch6.19H L211; Book 14, Ch6.66H L34-35)
- Alais has imprecise control of lightning, so her team wears wood and chitin armour. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L667)
- {event} Alais tells Ksmvr the guilds' bounty on a bandit group was raised by eighty gold, to a hundred gold. (Book 11, Ch6.19H L266-271)
- Alais says her team will be the group that takes the bandits out. (Book 11, Ch6.19H L271)
- Alais warns Ksmvr that too many enchantments on his gear will explode. (Book 11, Ch6.19H L216)
- Alais explains Stan's caution to Ksmvr, saying he has two kids and a lot saved. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L685-686)
- {event} Alais calls Thunder's Solace to her and raises her staff to hold the line against the Crelers. (Book 14, Ch6.66H L34-35)
