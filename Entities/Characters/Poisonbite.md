# Poisonbite
> Type: person | Also called: female Goblin warrior leader | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.31G
> See also: Rags, Redscar, Noears, Snapjaw, Pyrite, Shineshield, Redfang tribe
## Overview
- Poisonbite is a female Goblin warrior known by her nickname for the two poisoned daggers she wields. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L76)
- Poisonbite is a typical Goblin warrior who likes fighting but not practicing fighting. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L266)
- Poisonbite wears chainmail at all times as proof of her rank, which has saved her life eight times and has only three holes. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L35)
- Poisonbite is a [Venom Warrior] who is a Hob in the making, growing by the day and eating a massive amount of food. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L203)
- Poisonbite likes female Goblins. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L215)

## Under Rags (Books 4-8)
- Rags puts Poisonbite in command of a group of warriors after Redscar takes the Redfang vanguard. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L76)
- Poisonbite objects to Rags's training plan, saying Goblins do not train, and asks whether it is Goblin. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L265-271)
- Poisonbite tells Rags that Goblins must be strong, and answers Rags that training is not Goblin. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L270-271)
- Poisonbite recalls Lady Bethal's attack that killed hundreds of Goblins, and says Greydath's identity reveal helped save them. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L228-229)
- Poisonbite leads an all-female unit of Goblin raiders, who were good within Tremborag's mountain. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L18-19)

## Floodplains and the inn (Book 9)
- Poisonbite unhorses Snapjaw by killing her mount with poisoned daggers, and the two face off with their warriors behind them. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L68-69)
- Poisonbite's female warriors engage Reiss's warriors from the pikes with poisoned blades, but are useless against undead, which are immune to poison. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L132-133)
- Poisonbite follows Redscar toward Liscor and the inn with Noears. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L264)
- Poisonbite tells Erin in English that she is Poisonbite and draws her coated daggers when Erin jokes about her bite. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L355-360)
- Erin tells Poisonbite to keep her daggers sheathed and to follow the inn's rules. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L361)
- {belief} Poisonbite insists instantly that Rags, called Chieftain, is alive. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L538-539)
- Poisonbite tells Erin that Rags rebelled from Tremborag, fought the [Emperor], defeated the pink-death [Knights] and spoke to Greybeard. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L540)
- {state} Poisonbite survives the battle by hiding and being overlooked by the Humans, then climbs the mountain toward Rags. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L2-7)
- Poisonbite watched Noears die after he called lightning from the sky, and he stayed so more Goblins could escape. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L5-6)

## In Goblinhome (Book 16)
- Poisonbite breaks up with Shineshield in her own mind, but Shineshield, a Redfang [Shield Maiden] who survived Garen's last charge, had decided she had enough. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L206-209)
- Poisonbite hops mad around Shineshield until her warriors drag her off, and Snapjaw says Shineshield likes one Goblin at a time while Poisonbite likes many. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L212-214)
