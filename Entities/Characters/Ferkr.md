# Ferkr
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Ch6.38
> See also: Grimalkin
## Overview
- Ferkr is a female Gnoll apprentice of Grimalkin and the only Gnoll apprentice he has ever had. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L265,L279)
- Ferkr is shorter than Grimalkin and far less muscular, though she has twice Erin's muscle definition. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L265)
- Ferkr is a Level 6 [Mage], though Grimalkin says she is far too nervous. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L279,L307)
- Ferkr was born in Pallass and is not a Plains Gnoll. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L376)
## With Grimalkin
- Grimalkin gives Ferkr a two-hundred-pound weight to carry as she attempts to become a [Mage]. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L279)
- {belief} Grimalkin says the rumour that Gnolls cannot become [Mages] is completely false, and that some idiot started it at Wistram. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L299)
- Ferkr has been studying under Grimalkin for six months. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L335-336)
- Ferkr casts a ball of yellow light over her paw as a [Mage] spell. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L349-350)
- Grimalkin tells Ferkr she will go to the Meeting of Tribes and show that she can cast magic. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L351)
