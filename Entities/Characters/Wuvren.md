# Wuvren
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Wuvren; the Eternal Beauty | First appears: Ch6.27M
> See also: Pryde Ulta, Magnolia Reinhart, Belavierr, Zanthia
## Overview
- Lady Wuvren is a [Lady] of Izril who looks about twenty-eight although she is nearly sixty years old. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L618-619)
- Wuvren's skin and hair are as youthful as they ever were, though she sometimes seems older in her manner, voice and the age in her eyes. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L620-621)
- Wuvren has a son, Gilliad, a stately man with a trimmed chestnut beard who looks older than she does and calls her mother. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L631-635)
- Wuvren is called the [Eternal Beauty], and people who meet her fall in love with her. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1323)
- Wuvren is called the most beautiful [Lady] in all of Izril, with an appearance that always belies her age. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L934)
- Wuvren has held her place on the lists of the top ten [Ladies] to marry since she turned sixteen, about fifty years. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L12)
- Wuvren is said to be over sixty and to be always third place at worst in the beauty rankings. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L887)
## Skills and charm
- Wuvren charms people with her voice and eyes, and an [Assassin] warns his companions not to look at her because she has "charmed eyes". (Book 11, Ch6.27M L643-644)
- Wuvren uses [Heartbreaker Kiss], flicking two fingers from her lips to leave an [Assassin] clutching her chest without killing her. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L651-653)
- Pryde says Wuvren claims to have charmed a Troll once. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L400)
- Pryde later refers to Wuvren's Troll, called the Baron, as someone Wuvren could have brought as a bodyguard. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L964-965)
## Assassin attack on her estate
- Wuvren is entertaining guests at an estate open to callers when [Assassins] leap in through windows and demand her head. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L617,L636-637)
- Wuvren pushes her son back, pushes the leader's blade away from her chest and talks the female [Assassin] into lowering her weapon. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L638-643)
- Wuvren's guests and guards catch three [Assassins], and she remarks that she nearly had the young woman. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L648-649)
- Wuvren says she is not like Bethal, Magnolia or Zanthia and prefers to break hearts rather than kill, and sends Gilliad to learn who the attackers were. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L653)
- Wuvren asks for word to be sent that she needs to speak to Magnolia. (Book 11, Ch6.27M L653)
## Confrontation with Belavierr
- Wuvren stands with Zanthia and Ieka in the semicircle of [Ladies] facing Belavierr. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L980)
- Wuvren shudders and nearly steps back when Belavierr turns on her, and Pryde and Bethal steady her. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L1003)
- Wuvren's voice trembles with the strain as the [Ladies] circle Belavierr. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L1067)
- Wuvren helps Zanthia lift the stricken Pryde after Belavierr strikes her down. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L1111-1112)
## At Erin's inn
- Wuvren visits the Wandering Inn with Magnolia and Pryde in modern Earth-style dress, with her hair braided in Earth fashion. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1314-1318)
- Wuvren blows a kiss at Grimalkin, who is suddenly polite and formal, and apologises to him for it. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1355-1358)
- Wuvren, as the coach pulls away, says Erin has charm-related Skills, probably [Crowd Control], and could start a riot if she wished. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1688-1689)
- Wuvren looks closer to her actual age as the coach leaves Liscor. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1688)
