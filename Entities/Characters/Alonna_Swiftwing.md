# Alonna Swiftwing
> Type: person | Also called: Alonna; Guildmistress Alonna; Councilmember Alonna | First appears: Ch6.30
> See also: Lism, Jeiss, Raekea, Krshia, Zevara
## Overview
- Alonna Swiftwing is a Drake [Mage] and the Guildmistress of Liscor's Mage's Guild. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L860; Book 11, Ch6.31 L754)
- Alonna is new to her position as Guildmistress and did not inherit anything that put her in the Mage's Guild's leadership. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L37,L525)
## Liscor election
- Lism Swifttail marches through Liscor promoting Alonna and Senior Guardsman Jeiss as his candidates for the Council. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L859-860)
- Alonna stands at a podium in Dragondance Plaza opposing Raekea, and glares at her during the debate. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L754)
- Alonna is named among the winners of the election with Lism, Jeiss, Tismel Lischscale and Zalaiss Holmfyre, alongside the Gnolls. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L31,L140)
- Alonna sits flanked with Jeiss at Lism's side of the Council table and rubs her claws to make a magical spark. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L272,L281)
- Olesm judges that Alonna, Lism and Jeiss are firmly together as a group on the new Council. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L519)
- Alonna, addressed by Olesm as Councilmember, demands to know why the agents who attacked powerful nobles were not spotted. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L336-338)
## The Wistram bounty
- Alonna says Liscor's Mage's Guild is affiliated with Wistram, as every Mage's Guild is, because [Message] spells and information go through a central authority. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L92,L96)
- Alonna says she is not from Wistram but her guild must distribute information from the academy, including a bounty sheet. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L96-97)
- Alonna warns that Wistram could direct other Mage's Guilds to ignore Liscor's guild if she refuses to distribute the bounty. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L110)
- Alonna explains that this would mean Liscor's [Messages] to guilds elsewhere would be shoved to the bottom of the piles and delivered last. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L113)
