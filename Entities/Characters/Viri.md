# Viri
> Type: person | Also called: | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Commander Uxel, Cirille, Merish, Ser Vorn, Lizardfolk
## Overview
- Viri is a small, multicoloured Lizardman of Commander Uxel's company, armed only with a staff strapped to his back. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1062-1063,L1070)
- Viri's class is [Longstick Jumper], and he vaults along the ground and walls with his pole. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1075,L1080-1082)
- Viri calls himself one of the best [Scouts] and offers to jump ahead of the army to the 5th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1080)
- Viri has an ingratiating way about him. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L347)
## At the 5th Wall
- Viri greets Cirille's Drakes cheerfully as fellow scale-folk and is surprised that Izrilian Drakes hate Lizardfolk. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1064-1067)
- Viri explains that he is not the commander, who is napping, and demonstrates a pole vault of about twenty feet. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1070,L1082)
- Viri invites Cirille to meet Uxel and says she will get to play cards that night. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1086)
- Viri talks his way into the nightly officers' card games even though he is not really an officer, and drinks only goat's milk. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L247; Book 18, Ch7.36C L347)
- Viri leads a wing of Uxel's Lizardfolk skirmishers who catch Merish's group and wipe it out in a practice exercise. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L331)
- Uxel offers Cirille Viri and a [Mage] division, saying he wants to see what Drake spears do. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L569)
- Cirille patrols with Viri and other [Scouts] hunting for Demons. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L858)
- Viri leaps onto the wall to tell the officers that Delezza is dying. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L740-743)
## The battle
- Viri acts as a messenger to Cirille during the Demon assault, hopping across the wall with his staff. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L639)
- Viri warns Cirille from the walls of a second wave of Demon soldiers. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L779-781)
- Viri flies high into the Harpies, using a Demon as a foothold and lashing out with his staff. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L950-951)
- Uxel fights towards where Viri has fallen. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L999)
- Viri is found alive with one leg severed, among the survivors lying on the ground. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1290)
- Viri hops after the ghost of Uxel using his staff as a cane, and Uxel tells him to stay there. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L1320-1323)
