# Spiderslicer
> Type: person | Also called: Spiderbite (ledger key) | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.56G
> See also: Garen Redfang, Redscar, Headscratcher
## Overview
- Spiderslicer is a scarred veteran Redfang Goblin who rides a Carn Wolf and wields a thin falchion. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L8; Book 9, Ch5.58 L654)
- Spiderslicer was Garen's second and had been the third-strongest Redfang in the tribe. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L8)
- Spiderslicer resents Redscar, who beat him repeatedly and then left the tribe for Rags. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L11)
