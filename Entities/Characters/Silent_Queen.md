# Silent Queen
> Type: person | Also called: the Silent Queen of the Antinium | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.54
> See also: Free Queen, Grand Queen, Klbkch, Bird, Anand
## Overview
- The Silent Queen is an Antinium Queen with a genius for shaping Antinium forms. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L66; Book 18, Ch7.39A L690)
- {belief} The Silent Queen is described as the smallest and most timid of the Queens in one account, and as second-smallest next to the Free Queen in another. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L66; Book 18, Ch7.39A L690)
- The Silent Queen's hive lies in the shade of the cliffs, uses darker soil and dim corridors, and has camouflaged attendants. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L74; Book 18, Ch7.39A L684-686)
- The Silent Queen is fond of Klbkch and is practically vibrating when he visits. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L683)
- The Silent Queen says her Hive is not True Antinium. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L713)
## Events
- {event} The Silent Queen, via mirror, directs the Free Queen's surgery on Bird, ordering gel, warmth and splicing of a blood-sac organ with flesh and chitin. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L186-198)
- Bird survives the surgery and says he is brave. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L200-206)
- {event} The Silent Queen contacts the Free Queen and accuses her of withholding the design of her new Bird-Prognugator. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L72-75)
- The Silent Queen offers to trade and to send a Prognugator through the new passageway, misunderstanding the Free Queen's explanations. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L75-79)
- The Silent Queen's elite Infiltrator Antinium are six-legged, scythe-armed and shell-camouflaged, with blades able to cut weak enchanted armor. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L688-689)
- Each Infiltrator takes two months to create, and the Silent Queen calls her Hive the smallest but with elite soldiers. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L691-693)
- {event} The Silent Queen shows Klbkch a light-emitting Antinium built to blind enemies, based on Sii the Glorious. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L721-728)
- The strobe Antinium flickers its lights while others attack the blinded foe, though it must rest after a minute. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L731-733)
