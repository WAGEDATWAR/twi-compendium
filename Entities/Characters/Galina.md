# Galina
> Type: person | Also called: Galina (Earther) | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.26
> See also: Joseph, Kevin, Rose, Imani, Relc, Temile, Emme, Andel, Yaina, Players of Liscor, Players of Celum
## Overview
- Galina is a young woman from Earth who loves the theatre and is one of the seven Earthers staying at The Wandering Inn. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L77,L96; Book 18, Ch7.38 L334)
- Galina performed Macbeth twice back in high school. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L514)
## Theatre (Books 17-18)
- Galina asks Relc whether the Players know Wicked, Phantom of the Opera, Mean Girls and Chicago, while sitting at the back of the inn. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L507-514)
- Galina says she knows many plays and musicals, including Anastasia and To Kill a Mockingbird, though not perfectly, and asks how Erin remembered them. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L514,L517)
- {state} Galina, too shy to confess her addiction to theatre, bonds with Relc and Temile over the stage. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L511,L515-519)
- {event} Galina pitches A Doll's House to Emme, Andel and Temile, arguing Jasi should not be cast as the lead. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L524-531)
- Galina wants an older woman who can speak to unhappy married women as the lead, and Emme suggests Yaina. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L533-535)
- Galina says she cannot write the script out well because she lacks Erin's memory and hopes Andel can polish it. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L537)
- {event} Emme and Andel agree to try A Doll's House in the inn, and Emme says hiring Galina was never in doubt. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L539-546)
- {state} Galina is hired by the Players, and the narration says she belongs there as her Earther friends split up. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L544-547)
- Galina asks Rose to help brainstorm plays to adapt, and says she wants to act rather than just produce. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L333-341)
- Galina says some plays, such as Angels in America, do not work because they are too tied to home. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L342)
