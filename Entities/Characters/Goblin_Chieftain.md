# Goblin Chieftain
> Type: person | Also called: the Chieftain; Goblin Chief | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.15
> See also: Erin Solstice, Klbkch, Selys, Goblin Lord, Goblins
## Overview
- The Goblin Chieftain is the leader of the Goblin tribe that lives near Erin's inn, and Erin concludes he is out for her blood. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L88-90)
- The Goblin Chieftain is nearly as tall as Erin, very big for a Goblin but still small, with a wide muscled body, a rusted helmet and mismatched bits of metal armour. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L88)
## The attack on the inn
- {event} The Goblin Chieftain blows a horn from a hill near Erin's inn, then fires arrows at Erin from a bow when she tries to run. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L87-94)
- The Goblin Chieftain carries a sword and a bow and has levels, which makes him a better fighter than Erin. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L150)
- {event} The Goblin Chieftain keeps blowing his horn as he walks toward the inn, then batters at the barricaded door until it splinters. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L115,L127,L160-170)
- {event} The Goblin Chieftain smashes the door and enters the inn. (Book 1, Ch1.15 L174)
- {event} The Goblin Chieftain charges Erin, rams a table into her, and tries to tear her clothes off to assault her; she punches him in the groin. (Book 1, Ch1.16 L7-16)
- {event} The Goblin Chieftain follows Erin into the kitchen, loses his sword in the wall, and is hit by Erin's rolling pin. (Book 1, Ch1.16 L21-23)
- {event} A kitchen knife is driven into Erin's stomach in the kitchen fight; she pulls it out herself, and the Goblin Chieftain then lunges at her with the knife. (Book 1, Ch1.16 L24-34)
- {state} Erin throws a pot of boiling oil in the Goblin Chieftain's face, and he sinks to the floor screaming silently from the burns. (Book 1, Ch1.16 L35-38)
- {state} The Goblin Chieftain dies slowly of his burns while Erin sits beside him until his breathing stops. (Book 1, Ch1.16 L41-52)
## Aftermath
- Klbkch finds the Chieftain dead beside Erin and calls him a great threat. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L15-16)
- Klbkch disposes of the Chieftain's body but warns that his tribe may seek vengeance. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L85,L161)
- Klbkch asks Erin what caused the Chieftain to attack her, noting that the tribal leader rarely takes any aggression himself. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L166)
- {belief} Erin answers that the Chieftain attacked because Relc killed three Goblins and beheaded them. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L168)
- A bounty notice signed by Klbkch awards forty silver coins for the destruction of a Goblin Chieftain within the area of Liscor. (Book 1, Ch1.19 L4)
- {belief} The people of Liscor say that Erin killed the Goblin Chieftain by herself. (Book 1, Ch1.21 L187)
## Chieftains in general
- {belief} Selys tells Erin that Goblin Chieftains are too dangerous to send most adventurers after, since Watch fighters like Relc and Klbkch are probably better fighters but a Chieftain who calls his tribe well would wipe out an average group. (Book 1, Ch1.21 L217-220)
- Only Goblin Chieftains can access far-back memories. (Book 10, Ch6.03 L90)
