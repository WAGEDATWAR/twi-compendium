## Background and arrival in the world
- Ryoka Griffin is a Runner who runs barefoot, and she says she was born in New York, which she calls the greatest city in the world. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L4,L20-21,L89-90)
- Ryoka dislikes her own name and enjoys running more than talking to people; her listed hobbies include not mentioning her name, running, hunting and drinking coffee. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L91)
- {state} Ryoka knows she is in another world and has no way to get back. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L89)
- Ryoka calls herself a girl from Wisconsin and a college freshman, and says she found herself in this world while out on a run, carrying her iPhone, her wallet and her watch. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L120)
- Ryoka says she looks Asian and so stands out in a world where few people have skin darker than tan. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L121)
- Ryoka won a track-and-field scholarship, which she cites as why she outpaces the local Runners. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L136)
- Ryoka has been in the world only about a week and a half, though it feels much longer to her. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L128-129)
- Ryoka's left ear has been torn at some point in the past; the lower part of the earlobe is missing and the rest healed jaggedly around the gap. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L96)
- Ryoka has Japanese grandparents and describes her heritage as Japanese-American; her grandparents wanted her to appreciate tea, but her American side insists on coffee, which she has not yet found in this world. (Book 1, Ch1.03R L53)
- Ryoka describes her Japanese side as half of her heritage. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L285)
- Ryoka reflects that she was accepted to Columbia University on a Track and Field scholarship for the 400-meter run, with a 3.89 GPA, but never set foot on campus. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L300)
- Ryoka lists among her past interests hunting, sports shooting, reading, Youtube videos and video games, and she has not chosen a major. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L300)
- Ryoka thinks of herself as an unfriendly daughter who does not like her dad, holds a 4th Dan in Muay Thai though she prefers Parkour, refused Mensa membership and was kicked out of two high schools. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L301)
- Ryoka loves running music, favouring rock and disliking pop, techno and opera; her favourite artists include Meatloaf, Imagine Dragons, Fueled by Ramen, Lady Gaga and Five for Fighting. (Book 1, Ch1.06R L302)
- Ryoka's iPhone 4 is black, holds 32 GB that is almost entirely music, and has no password lock, only a few books, saved files and an empty contact list besides. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L321-322)
- Ryoka listens to "Heathens" by twenty one pilots while running, and notes that Suicide Squad, the film it was written for, came out a month before she came to this world and that she never saw it. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L5-6)
- Ryoka says that thinking of the world she came from hurts, but like a thorn pulled from her foot long ago. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L7)
