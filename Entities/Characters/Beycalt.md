# Beycalt
> Type: person | Also called: Beycalt Newman; Forewoman Beycalt | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.34E
> See also: Durene, Ram, Prost, Laken, Riverfarm, Hedag
## Overview
- Beycalt Newman is a woman [Builder] and [Forewoman] who leads a crew of builders working in Riverfarm. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L32,L57)
- Beycalt says her name is Newman, but that she is without a man. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L57)
- Beycalt has calluses nearly as tough as Durene's own skin. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L56)
- Beycalt is a former Street Runner, though that was years ago. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L130)
## Meeting Durene
- Beycalt raises an eyebrow when Ram says Durene and a few hands can lift a load-bearing beam without ropes, and asks if he is sure. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L31-33)
- Beycalt blinks as Durene pushes the heavy beam upright by herself, and then stares at her with amazement and fear. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L42,L52)
- Beycalt recognises Durene from the army at Lancrel and says she saw her smash at least thirty Goblins before the survivors got away. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L57,L61)
- Beycalt thanks Durene when Durene apologises for Lancrel. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L59-61)
- Beycalt tells Mister Felp that rain does not affect building, and that a house can be raised in an hour with Durene's help. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L215-218)
- Beycalt leads builders who came to Riverfarm from Lancrel, and Durene dislikes how quick most of them are to complain about the rain. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L223)
## Riverfarm politics
- Beycalt answers "Death" with a hard voice when Hedag asks what should be done to the one who struck the blow, in the judgment of the [Thief] Elmmet. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L225-227)
- Beycalt is among the people Prost calls on to get their folk working when he clears the street. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L121)
- Beycalt keeps a spear-fishing friend from wandering off to pursue his passion, arguing good-naturedly with him. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L131)
- Beycalt, Ram and Beniar's [Riders] follow Prost as he confronts the [Witch] Belavierr in the street. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L391)
- Beycalt drinks and eats with Hedag and other laborers, and brightens at the generous drinks Hedag pours. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L998)
- Hedag challenges Beycalt to an arm-wrestling competition. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1027)
- {state} Emperor Laken names Beycalt his [Construction Supervisor] in front of Riverfarm, confirming the authority she already held under Prost. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L116-126)
- Beycalt's eyes bulge and she stammers when Laken addresses her by name, saying he has heard good things of her. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L118-125)
