# Feor
> Type: person | Also called: Archmage Feor | First appears: Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage
> See also: Archmage Nailihuaile, Teura, Viltach, Amerys, Blackmage
## Overview
- Archmage Feor is a half-Elf of two hundred years and one of the Archmages of Wistram. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L294)
- Feor used to teach one of the highest-level magic courses for eighth-year students and still advises apprentices and mages. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L35)
- Feor leads the Centrists, a faction opposed to the Revivalists, and Teura belongs to his personal faction. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L40; Book 18, Ch7.29B L25)
- Feor is a generalist, according to Naili, who says he wants to be a proper [Archmage] of old but lacks the class. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L373)
- {belief} Naili says Feor prefers big spells in battle and everywhere else. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L373)
## Books 8-14

## Books 18-19
