# General Duln
> Type: person | Also called: 1st General Duln | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.03
> See also: Chaldion, Pallass
## Overview
- General Duln is a Dullahan and the 1st [General] of Pallass, who arrives with a huge red-hot glowing mace and a tower shield. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L722-723)
- General Duln is remarkable for being a non-Drake on Pallass's inner war council. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L535)
## Book 15: the Wyvern attack
- {event} General Duln arrives apologizing for the delay, saying he was giving orders and equipping a strike force, and his [Soldiers] form up on the walls. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L723-724)
- General Duln uses [Volley Fire] on the Wyvern Lord and then [Phantom Volley], whose copied arrows blast the Wyverns overhead to pieces. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L727-730)
- General Duln uses [Shieldwall Formation] and [Rapid Advance], and his Skill makes the soldiers' shields repel the Wyverns' frost. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L741-742)
## Book 19: the Raskghar
- {event} General Duln proposes putting the young Raskghar through military training once their numbers replenish, to see how they react to discipline. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L536)
- General Duln says the Walled Cities clashed with the tribes often before the Antinium wars and have used unorthodox methods before. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L540)
- {event} Chaldion cuts General Duln off, which shows his remark was a slip of the tongue, and calls the matter classified. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L541-545)
