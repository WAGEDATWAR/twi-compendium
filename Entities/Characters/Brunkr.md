# Brunkr
> Type: person | Also called: Brunkr of the Silverfang Tribe | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.21
> See also: Krshia, Mrsha, Erin Solstice, Lyonette, Venitra, Regrika Blackpaw, Silverfang Tribe, Octavia
## Overview
- Brunkr is a tall, muscular Gnoll warrior of the Silverfang Tribe, armed with a longsword and a large shield. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L236,L241)
- Brunkr is Krshia's nephew, and he calls her Aunt. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L235,L246; Book 2, Ch2.33 L223)
- The narration of Mrsha's viewpoint calls Brunkr Krshia's cousin once, though Krshia calls him nephew in the same scene. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L331,L353)
- Brunkr ends sentences with "yes?" or "no?" tags when he speaks. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L237,L241; Book 2, Ch2.22 L73)
- Brunkr is older than Lyonette, though not much older. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L339)
## Arrival and the attack on the inn, Book 2
- Brunkr arrives in Liscor with his brethren to guard and assist Krshia, his honoured aunt and the Silverfang clan leader's sister. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L241)
- Brunkr grabs Erin's hand and calls her an enemy for harbouring the thief, whom he wants to see punished. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L236-244)
- Krshia orders Brunkr to let Erin go and says Erin has made her choice. (Book 2, Ch2.21 L238,L245)
- Brunkr leads Gnolls against Erin's inn despite orders not to go after the thief. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L70-75,L339)
- Brunkr takes a [Power Strike] from Erin, gets back up and tells her she cannot do it more than once. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L66-70)
- Brunkr says his aunt wants Erin alive but not unharmed, and he plans to teach her a lesson before dealing with the thief. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L73-75)
- Brunkr tells Zevara that the Silverfang tribe will go to war against Liscor if anything happens to him. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L331)
- Krshia seizes Brunkr by the ear, asks if he wants to become Chieftain already, and drags him off. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L338-340)
- Brunkr shrinks in front of Krshia, though he is taller than her. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L336)
- Brunkr introduces himself to Ryoka as a warrior of the Silverfang tribe at Krshia's shop. (Book 2, Ch2.33 L223,L246)
- Brunkr tells Mrsha that her tribe is gone and never coming back, because she brought disaster and is cursed. (Book 2, Ch2.33 L454)
- Mrsha attacks Brunkr in response and bites him. (Book 2, Ch2.33 L445-450)
## The infected hand, Books 4-5
- Krshia calls for Brunkr when Mrsha is in her apartment, and Mrsha goes very still at his name. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L330-331)
- Brunkr enters with a foul-smelling bandaged hand, oozing and raw, and introduces himself to Klbkch. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L335-339)
- Brunkr blames Mrsha for his hand and calls her Doombringer, Cursed one, Deathbringer and Calamity. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L351,L355)
- {belief} Brunkr says Mrsha took his hand just as she took her tribe, and that she is cursed. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L355)
- {belief} Brunkr tells Erin to take her cursed doombringer away unless she wants to end up as he has. (Book 4, Ch3.38 L146)
- Brunkr tells Erin that her cursed one bit him. (Book 4, Ch3.38 L156)
- Brunkr's right forearm is swollen and oozing yellow pus from a bite, and the infection covers a good part of the forearm. (Book 4, Ch3.38 L152-158)
- Brunkr asks Krshia whether it is time to cut his hand off. (Book 4, Ch3.38 L150)
- Brunkr says a [Healer]'s poultice, his tribe's meat and his aunt's stews all did nothing, and he burnt the wound with fire. (Book 4, Ch3.38 L488)
- Brunkr agrees to try Erin's treatment, saying he has nothing left to lose. (Book 4, Ch3.38 L530)
- Brunkr keeps a honey-slathered bandage over his arm while Gnolls gather in Krshia's apartment. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L173)
- Brunkr removes the bandage and holds a sword in his good hand when the Gnolls go out to face armed Drakes. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L267)
- Octavia's mould, the honey and regular cleaning stop the gash leaking pus and it starts to heal. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L377)
- Erin checks on Brunkr all week, and he is soon fit and eats half a cow. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L377-378)
## Hopes to be a Knight and friendship with Lyonette, Book 5
- Brunkr is a [Warrior], and says he is one of the best in his tribe. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L387)
- Brunkr aspires to become a [Knight], but says the class requires something he does not have. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L387)
- Erin asks Brunkr to train Lyonette, and perhaps Bird, with the sword, and hopes he makes up with Mrsha. (Book 5, Ch4.18 L389-392)
- Brunkr teaches Lyonette how to hold and swing a sword, outside the inn in the cleared snow. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L326-328)
- Brunkr is changed by his injury, no longer self-confident but a recovering [Warrior], grateful for his healing arm and quietly guilty. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L330)
- Brunkr and Lyonette have both apologised, and a friendship begins once Brunkr learns Lyonette made his honey salve. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L332)
- The narration says Lyonette is the girl Brunkr had tried to kill. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L331)
- Brunkr found a book in his youth about Human [Knights] in shining armour, and trained with sword and shield to be like them. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L336)
- Lyonette says a [Knight] needs a [Lord] or other nobility to make him one. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L337)
- Brunkr says no Gnoll has the [Knight] class, and he came north to help Krshia and to see if it was possible. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L336-338)
- {state} Brunkr gains the class [Knight] and says so proudly in front of Ryoka. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L48)
- {state} Brunkr's right arm has completely healed and he no longer wears a bandage. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L64)
- Brunkr says he has his arm and debts to pay, and will be a [Knight] worthy of his class. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L65)
- Krshia and Brunkr hold a celebration of his new class at Erin's inn. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L117)
- Erin gives Brunkr a pink-frosted cake with [Knight] written on it. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L408-411)
- Brunkr tells Erin that she saved his arm and that he misjudged her badly. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L521-525)
- Brunkr apologises for what he said when Erin first came to him. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L525)
- Brunkr says a life as an adventurer might be worth living after all, but he would not enter the dungeon. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L506-510)
- Brunkr says he owes too much to Erin, Krshia and Lyonette to risk his life so easily. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L510)
