# Wailant
> Type: person | Also called: Wailant Strongheart; Farmer Strongheart | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.17
> See also: Viceria, Garia Strongheart, Numbtongue, Seborn, Erin, Ryoka
## Overview
- Wailant Strongheart is Garia's father, a [Farmer] near Celum and a retired [Pirate], married to Viceria. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L54,L153; Book 11, Ch6.18H L89,L94-96)
- Wailant has black hair, green eyes, stubble and a circular scar on his jaw. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L57,L78; Book 15, Ch7.07 L673)
- Wailant has scarred, tattooed arms, including a tentacle gripping a ship and a fanged fish that seems to swim. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L54-55; Book 15, Ch7.07 L674)
- Wailant wears a sleeveless light blue shirt gilded with a golden wave edge. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L56)
- Wailant carries an enchanted cutlass looted from plunder, with a wand in his off-hand. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L165; Book 15, Ch7.07 L721-722)
- {belief} Garia says Wailant coats his cutlass with a paralysis poison. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1088)
## Past
- {reveal} Wailant first claims to have been a [Sailor], then admits to Garia and Viceria that he was a [Pirate]. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L143-153)
- Wailant was a Level 27 [Pirate] from a Baleros family and sailed to all five continents. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L143,L155,L157)
- Wailant's father died at sea before he was born, and his mother died in a battle when he was five. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L155)
- Wailant retired as wealthy as a Gold-rank adventurer, and spent most of it clearing the bounty on his head. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L157,L162)
- Wailant bought a farm far inland, tired of water and wary of enemies, and struggled for the first years. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L162-163)
- Wailant got a savage scar from killing a Creler. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L155)
- Wailant claims he could have become a [Storm Sailor] or [Captain], and Garia and Viceria roll their eyes at the embellished story. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L158-159)
