# Demon King
> Type: person | Also called: Demon King of Rhir | First appears: Book 2, Ch1.00C
> See also: Blighted King, Demons, Rhir, Hayvon, Az'kerash
## Overview
- The Demon King is the leader of the Demons and monsters on Rhir, who is not acknowledged as an actual king by the Blighted Kingdom. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L175)
- {belief} Tom's account says that the Demon King, as people call him, is a mystery, and that a group of monsters is organised and raids the Kingdom. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L92)
- The Demon lands are the other half of the continent, where another King rules. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L89)
- The Demon King's army never got past the First Wall of the Blighted Kingdom, though the Fourth, Third and Second walls have been breached in war. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L87)
- Rhir has been at war with the Demon King for thousands of years, with the Blighted King battling him. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L175)
- The Demon King and his minions have Skills as powerful as those of the Blighted King, according to a Blighted Kingdom lord. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L409)
## Forces
- The Demon King uses the captured Fearless Demons as shock troops when he wants to win, according to a Drake. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L247)
- {belief} A Blighted Kingdom lord says that when the Demon King's Deathless take to the field, armies die. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L44)
- Az'kerash says the Demon King has Deathless, and that the world forgets the peril of assailing him. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L433)
- Az'kerash thinks his four chosen undead could not match one of the Demon King's Deathless. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L135)
- Lord Hayvon says a plague of Vorepillers was created by one of the Demon King's Deathless and unleashed on Rhir's fields about three thousand five hundred years ago. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L33)
## Events
- The Demon King sends two of his best warriors, an [Archer] and a teleportation [Mage], to hold ground and open a gate for his army at the Blighted King's palace. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L6,L31)
- A noble asks how the Demon King knew to strike the Blighted Kingdom at its weakest moment. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L53)
- Tom faces the Demon King's soldiers in the palace courtyard, and a speaker later remarks that one more of the Demon King's limbs has been taken away. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L378,L397)
- A [High Magus] says she thought the Blighted King was better than the Demon King, in a debate about a ritual. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L48)
- {belief} A character lists the Demon King of Rhir as an enemy of the Blighted Kingdom and of the world. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L84)
