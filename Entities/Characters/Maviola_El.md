# Maviola El
> Type: person | Also called: Lady El; Lady Firestarter; Maviola; Maviola Imarris | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.18M
> See also: Olesm, Erin Solstice, Saliss, Magnolia Reinhart, Lyonette
## Overview
- Maviola El is the matriarch of the House of El, one of the Five Families of Izril, and is known by the nickname Lady Firestarter. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L134,L1244; Book 18, Ch7.30 L767)
- Maviola El is over ninety years old, and both she and Lady Gresaria Wellfar are over a century old by another account. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L1237; Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L413)
- Maviola has a Skill that tells apart little girls who lie to her, even when their parents give them tricks to avoid truth spells. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L215)
- Maviola has the Skill [Like Fire, Memory], and says she was the first to have it, so Erin's fire is the same as hers. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L700,L704,L714-715)
- Maviola can call forth an aura of fire, suited for combat, which she uses as a [Lady]. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L1009; Book 18, Ch7.31 L826)
- Maviola has orange eyes and hair that is black mixed with orange or red. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L925; Book 17, Ch7.23LM L1071; Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L166)
- Maviola's old Skill is [Deft Hand], with which she shoves people off a hill. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L974,L981)
## Matriarch of House El
- {state} Maviola El sits in a magical wheelchair, old, with white hair and papery skin, in the study of House El, whose ironwood doors were carved over nineteen thousand years ago. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L49-51,L63-65)
- Maviola controls the distribution of wealth among the El family, so she could cut children's families off from gold if she chose. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L134-135)
- A half-Elf aide, Zedalien, serves Maviola, closes the study doors and pushes her chair, and is older than she is. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L71,L234,L239,L241)
- Maviola says her brother Fulviolo El and the Terland twins Petria and Ulva would have been magnificent leaders, but losing Petria broke Ulva. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L185)
- Maviola says that too many nobles were lost during the Sacrifice of Roses, and that the Goblin King took a terrible toll. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L192)
- Maviola says she has a Skill that tells liars apart and tells Zeci El to put her hand in the fire. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L215)
- Zedalien asks whether Maviola is tormenting children with illusionary fire. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L235)
- {event} Maviola tells Zedalien that she plans to announce her retirement within the week, sooner if she can do it with one last triumph. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L240)
- {event} Maviola tells Deilan that he will be the next patriarch of the House of El, with Zedalien as her witness for months. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L286)
- Zedalien says he will retire with her and will stay with Deilan until his replacement is ready. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L298)
