# Perorn Sadiluc (Fleethoof)
> Type: person | Also called: Perorn; Fleethoof; Professor Perorn | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.24D
> See also: Perorn Sadiluc, Niers Astoragon, Tulm the Mithril, Forgotten Wing Company
## Overview
- Perorn is a Centauress [Strategist] known as Fleethoof, and one of the foremost [Strategists] and commanders in the Forgotten Wing Company, one of the Four Great Companies of Baleros. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L110; Book 11, Ch6.24D L530)
- Perorn is a famous [Strategist], almost as famous as Niers Astoragon, who teaches joint classes and speaks in a crisp, no-nonsense tone. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L7)
- Niers calls her one of his first winners and says she sat in a chair in the class years earlier. (Book 13, ChInterlude_The_Titans_Question L435)
## Tulm
- Perorn says Tulm was the one who took away her leg, and asks Niers why he brought her. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L321)
- {state} Tulm hamstrung her back right leg in a past battle, and when Niers introduces her to him she says a single word, "Mithril", laden with over a decade of feeling. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L339-340)
## Geneva's invitation
- {event} Perorn authorizes bringing Geneva to the academy, with a formal invitation and the cost of travel expenses. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L500)
- She gallops out of Daquin at great speed, filled with hope, even though her leg hurts. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L516-517,L529-532)
