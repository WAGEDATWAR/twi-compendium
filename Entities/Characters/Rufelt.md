# Rufelt
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.09
> See also: Lasica, Erin Solstice, Grimalkin, Lism
## Overview
- Rufelt is a City Gnoll with dark, near-black fur streaked a bit with grey, and he works as a [Bartender]. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L314,L426; Book 10, Ch6.10 L92)
- Rufelt introduces himself to Erin as 'City Gnoll', and co-owns the Pallass bar Tails and Scales with his wife Lasica, a Drake who runs the kitchen. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L452,L761,L818)
- Tails and Scales is a large tavern just off the main stairwell on the fifth floor of Pallass. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L817)
- Rufelt says that he is reasonably high-level. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L50)
- Rufelt has an advanced version of the [Bartender] class, and can make drinks with effects. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L140,L221)
- Rufelt has the Skill [Immunity: Alcohol], though two shots of a black Dwarf spirit still knock him off balance. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L2-3)
- Rufelt's Skill [Bar: Selective Admission] can keep anyone below his level from casually walking into his bar. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L98)
- Rufelt did a few years in Zeres, which allows in Pirates who have paid off their bounty under Drake law. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L211-213)
## Views and advice
- {belief} Rufelt says City Gnolls do not bow to tribe loyalty, and are free rather than born into a family of hundreds. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L461)
- Rufelt calls the Plains Gnoll view of City Gnolls that of a domesticated fool without tribe or loyalty. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L461)
- Rufelt cautions Erin not to rush rare ingredients, saying he and Lasica once sold for far too little. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L158-159)
- Rufelt tells Erin to cultivate signature dishes, saying he and Lasica boast the widest variety in Pallass thanks to Balerosian ingredients. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L188)
- Rufelt says an [Innkeeper] is a generalist class and that he can make better alcohol, including drinks that cheer, energise or knock out. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L220-221)
- Rufelt tells Erin that he and Lasica could have been affiliated with a large Gnoll tribe if they had not let the chance slip. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L192)
- Rufelt warns Erin not to mention Skills, saying he and Lasica could have been the wrong couple to tell. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L173,L176)
- Rufelt serves Erin a drink of floating Dwarfrain liquor, which rises toward her lips. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L130-139)
- Rufelt says he and Lasica back the Unitail group and [Senators], and mixes blue fruit juice with rum, chilled with [Chill Paws]. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L742,L775-776)
## Pallass and the Wandering Inn
- Rufelt serves Pisces an amber drink with a green energy mix, based on a stamina potion recipe he worked out with Xif. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L67-73)
- Rufelt and Lasica dance to an invisible cello at The Wandering Inn, and the other guests pair off to follow them. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L278-290)
- Rufelt agrees to look at fertility spells and talk about it when Lasica has not had four drinks. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L354-360)
- Rufelt takes offence at a hooded Drake who suggests adoption, and later guesses the Drake has a child, because he sounded like a father. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L364-365,L385)
- Rufelt knows Hawk as a customer at Tails and Scales, and says Lasica has done carrot dishes for him. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L108-111)
- {belief} Rufelt thinks Hawk lays off the carrots when he likes someone, to find a Drake date who will eat them. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L113)
- Rufelt and Lasica bail Erin out of jail with Chaldion and Saliss, and Rufelt is amused. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L914,L933-934)
- Rufelt stops making a molotov and presses Lasica down, refusing her alcohol until Erin guesses the reason. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L1013-1022)
- Rufelt provides the drinks at a get-together while Lasica sits with a juice, and blushes when Erin congratulates them. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L334,L345-349)
- Erin says Rufelt told her Gnolls have no problem with chocolate, which Mrsha jokes about by pretending to choke. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L251,L255-266)
- Rufelt is overprotective of Lasica during her pregnancy, and keeps trying to do her work in the kitchen. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L636; Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L364,L375)
