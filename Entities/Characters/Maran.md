# Maran
> Type: person | Also called: Maran and Safry (paired with the barmaid Safry) | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.15
> See also: Agnes, Safry, Erin Solstice, Lyonette, Frenzied Hare
## Overview
- Maran is a [Barmaid] at the Frenzied Hare in Celum who later works at Erin's inn together with her co-worker Safry. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L384-410)
## Book 3
- Maran told Erin that she had bought quartz earrings, and mentioned dancing in taverns and looking forward to city festivals. (Book 3, Ch3.15 L228,L235,L239-241)
## Leaving the Frenzied Hare
- {belief} Maran and Safry say Agnes docks pay for breaks, pays little and gives no tips, so they asked Erin to hire them. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L413-421)
- Maran and Safry said Erin's inn is larger and Erin is easier to work with than Agnes. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L413)
## At Erin's inn
- Maran and Safry cannot tell Drakes from each other, and served the wrong Drake the wrong food. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L64-71)
- They served Gold-rank adventurers before the Drake Zel in the hope of tips. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L146)
- Maran and Safry are uncomfortable around Bird and the Antinium, and Maran says the Antinium in the Horns of Hammerad was one thing. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L110-112)
- Maran and Safry flirted with Halrac, Ulrien and Typhenous. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L146)
- Maran admired Pisces until Lyonette told her he is a [Necromancer]. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L158-160)
- Maran refused to fetch water from the stream, citing fish with teeth, Rock Crabs and Shield Spiders. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L167)
- {belief} Maran claimed that having water delivered costs a few silver and is how civilized folk do it. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L174)
- Maran took coins from Erin's money jar and had six huge barrels of water delivered through the Celum door. (Book 5, Ch4.14L L181-184)
- Maran and Safry brought about two dozen Human friends from Celum and took a thirty-minute lunch break during the rush. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L107-117)
- They claimed the Innkeeper's Guild's policy gives them a break. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L117)
- {state} Erin fired Maran and Safry and her [Inn's Aura] Skill drove them out through Octavia's shop. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L233,L249)
- Agnes later claims Maran and Safry are at Ulna's, who took them in. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1023-1025)
