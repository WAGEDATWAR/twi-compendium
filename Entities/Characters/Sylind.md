# Sylind
> Type: person | Also called: Hunter Sylind | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.44E
> See also: Ser Raim, Tagil, Belavierr, Calirn, Riverfarm
## Overview
- Sylind is a half-Elf [Archer] and [Hunter] among the Witch Hunters who pursue Belavierr alongside Ser Raim. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L193,L763,L844)
- Sylind has the Skill [The Eternal Hunt], which lets her mark a quarry she will never lose once marked. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L311)
- Sylind joins the hunt because she sees Belavierr's crimes as unforgivable, according to Ser Raim. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L844)
## The hunt for Belavierr
- Sylind fires a Phoenixfire arrow at Belavierr on the order of the Witch Hunters' leader, and is later told to mark Belavierr as she flees. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L75,L158)
- Sylind says she will never lose Belavierr again because she is marked, and Coroise tells her that her life is as precious as Raim's. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L178-181)
- Coroise orders the others to protect Sylind, because if she dies Belavierr escapes. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L227)
- Ser Raim tells Knight-Commander Calirn that the Skill worked this time because he had weakened Belavierr's protections. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L311)
- Sylind aims up at a huge shadow as the hunters take their position by the orb. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L320)
- Sylind argues with Tagil that she and Raim together make a doubly large target, and proposes moving Raim to a warded house in Riverfarm. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L763-768)
- Sylind says Belavierr cannot be perfect if her Skills work on the Witch, and that Raim can be warded just as well elsewhere. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L772)
## Death
- Sylind sits upstairs in the slanted house meditating and checking her arrows while five Witch Hunters remain. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L915-917)
- Tagil tells Sylind that Belavierr once captured him during the attack on her village and made him an offer. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L949)
- Tagil says the offer was never for then but for now, and Sylind urges him not to go through with it. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L958-959)
- Tagil kills Sylind with a hidden crossbow, shooting her twice despite her protective charms, after she reaches for her bow. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L963-965)
- Later, the Order carries Sylind's ashes home with the ashes of the other fallen Witch Hunters. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L110)
