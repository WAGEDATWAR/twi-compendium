# Trey
> Type: person | Also called: Trey Atwood; Lord Atwood; Troy Atlas (alias) | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00K
> See also: Teres, Flos Reimarch, Gazi, Fetohep, Mars, Orthenon, The Quarass, Parasol Stroll, Flynn Patel
## Overview
- Trey Atwood is a sixteen-year-old from England who was summoned to this world and became Flos Reimarch's ward and personal attendant. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L78,L87-93)
- Trey is the fraternal twin of Teres, and they are both 5'10 tall with moderately dark blonde hair and fair skin. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L91-94)
- Trey is a young man from London. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L4)
- Trey is afraid and confused by Flos, who shattered everything he believed in, and whose waking Trey can feel as a shock to the spine. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L87-88; Book 5, Ch4.00K L91-92)
- Trey is not by his own estimation cut out to walk with legends. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L4)
- Trey eats crunchy bacon bits at breakfast and dislikes vegetables even in another world. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L92,L98)
- Trey and Teres do not hold a servant class; Orthenon tells them they are aides, expected to become bodyguards and assistants. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L121)
- Orthenon introduces Trey to Clan Tannousin as a companion of the King of Destruction, chosen to follow him. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L30)

## Arrival and early months (Book 2-5)
- Trey and Teres fell into Flos's throne room after stepping off the ledge of a Tube subway car, and found Flos sitting alone in a small chair. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L104-105)
- Orthenon nearly ran Trey through before Flos stopped his men. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L106)
- Trey has an iPhone that he keeps switched off ever since a call, and Flos insisted he and Teres could keep their phones. (Book 2, Ch2.00K L135)
- Trey had been in this world little more than a month at the time of the Emperor of Sands' war. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L102)
- Trey and Teres openly refuse Flos's order, and Flos thanks them for the truth and apologizes. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L452-457,L475)
- Trey wakes at dawn when Teres takes his pillow, trains with Flos and Mars in slow-motion sword swings, then hits a cloth dummy. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L2-3,L12,L24-26)
- Orthenon doubts a [Warrior] is the class for Trey, and trains Teres instead. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L46-47)
- Flos tells Trey to call him Flos rather than "my King". (Book 5, Ch4.03K L452)
- Trey takes a [Geomancer]'s staff after the battle at the hill, and his badly cut arm is closed by a potion from Mars. (Book 5, Ch4.05K L448,L476-481)

## The break over slavery (Book 5-7)
- Trey realizes that Chandrar's kingdoms keep or sell slaves, and sells captives to slave traders to feed Flos's poor kingdom. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L19-20; Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L65)
- Trey and Teres fight bitterly over Flos selling slaves, with Trey throwing a chair at a wall and calling Teres a monster. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L26,L49)
- Teres argues Flos must be changed gradually and draws her sword, and Trey runs. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L41-50)
- Trey and Teres had not spoken for over a month after the fight. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L20)
- Trey tells Flos that Flos is not his king and that he still has a Queen, and stops speaking to him. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L74-75)
- Orthenon refuses to speak to Trey unless he apologizes, and Trey feels a pulling to give in that he compares to addiction. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L78-79)
- Trey is alone, eating and sleeping apart from Teres and no longer training with Mars. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L78)
- Months later, Trey's rift with Flos and Teres persists, and Gazi is his only friend because he objected to slavery. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L64-66)
- Trey is a [Mage] who casts [Flash], [Sand Arrow] and [Sand Veil] under Gazi's instruction. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L51,L58,L70)
- Trey is unsure how he feels about being a [Mage] or about his sister becoming a warrior. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L66-67)
- Trey catches Nawal as she faints on realising Flos is the King of Destruction. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L485)

## Book 10: Magic and the Quarass
- Trey practices magic because Gazi expects it, and casts [Lightsand Arrow] by combining [Light] and [Sand Arrow]. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L341-343)
- Trey casts [Sand Sprite], which creates toy-like golems with big eyes and spears modeled on Earth figurines, which Gazi calls the ugliest creations in history. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L345-348)
- Ulyse tells Trey the staff taken from the [Geomancer] is useful but needs spells and experience to control. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L328)
- Gazi tells Trey she will find him tomorrow to practice and he will learn [Featherfall] with the staff. (Book 10, Ch6.13K L336)
- Trey and Teres feel like friends or siblings but not like twins, and have not yet figured out how to come back together. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L259-260)
- Teres tells Trey that Gazi was a slave whom Flos found and raised, and Trey says he knew. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L273-275)

## Khelt and Fetohep (Book 13)
- Flos sends Trey with the Quarass to meet Fetohep, since Trey has a talent as a [Diplomat] and has the phone. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L598,L618)
- Trey orders a shepherd's pie, then orange juice, at Fetohep's table, and Fetohep disapproves of the low-born dish. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L163-180)
- Trey later asks for bubble and squeak, a fried egg, strong tea with no sugar and a treacle tart, and Khelt's [Chefs] reproduce them exactly by a Skill. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L276-277,L326-333)
- Trey tells Fetohep he comes from England, a country in the United Kingdom, and Fetohep says no such country exists. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L487-490)
- The Quarass tells Fetohep that Trey and his sister come from another world. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L502)
- Trey tells Fetohep about Egypt, pharaohs, pyramids and mummification. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L586-590)
- Trey gives Fetohep his iPhone to keep overnight and tells him the passcode, 4467. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L619,L629-633)
- Trey has all of Series 1 and two episodes of Series 2 of Sherlock on his iPhone, and Fetohep watches through the night. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L673-675)
- Trey proposes a selfie, and Trey and Fetohep take the first royal selfie in this world. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L736-741,L824)

## Wistram (Book 18-19)
- Trey rides through Chandrar with two Parasol Stroll [Mages], Mirin and Fellif, on a mission incognito while learning magic. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L34-35)
- Fellif says the Quarass taught Trey more than they have, and Trey carries a mini-Gazi golem made of the Quarass's red lifeblood sand. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L37-39)

- Trey was escorted south from Reim by Mirin and Palke, and Mirin, second-in-command of Parasol Stroll, casts a cooling spell on him. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L272-276)
- Trey had not seen the Quarass since they parted after A'ctelios, and misses Nawal. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L267)
- Teres tells Fetohep that Gazi is gone and Trey is heading with two Parasol Stroll [Mages] to a harbor in Nerrhavia's Fallen. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L184)
- Trey boards the Emerald Signet as Troy Atlas, saying he is seventeen from Hellios, and swears under a truth spell that he is bound for Wistram Academy. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L293-304)

- Trey decides not to tell his teachers or the other Earth-people that he can make Mini-Gazi. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L644)
