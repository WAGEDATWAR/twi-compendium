# Eater of Spears
> Type: person | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.48
> See also: Reiss, Goblin Lord, Zel Shivertail, Pyrite, Headscratcher, Rockbreakers Tribe
## Overview
- Eater of Spears is a gigantic Hobgoblin and one of the Goblin Lord's lieutenants. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L362)
- Eater of Spears is a huge Hob with an unusually precise, well-chosen way of speaking. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L388)
- Eater of Spears is nine feet tall or more and looks like a mass of muscle. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L215)
## History
- Before he joined Reiss, Eater of Spears led a tribe of fewer than eighty Goblins, almost all Hobs. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L167)
- That tribe was the Rockbreakers Tribe, which terrorised the cliffs and beat Wyverns to death for food. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L166-167)
- Eater of Spears says his tribe fought monsters often and had too little food to build fat. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L387-388)
- Eater of Spears says his old tribe fought Stone Starers, which have stone skin, strong arms and little meat. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L390)
- Eater of Spears says Stone Starer eyes tasted horrible and were partially acidic. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L390)
- Eater of Spears once went to a small Drake city for healing potions after a fight with Wyverns, and had to scare Drakes off the walls. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L168)
- Eater of Spears was a [Thrower] before and can hit flying Wyverns with rocks, though he no longer levels the class much. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L303)
## Abilities and equipment
- Eater of Spears carries two weakly enchanted throwing axes that Reiss gave him as a reward. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L297-300)
- Eater of Spears carries three healing potions on his belt, a sign of how important he is. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L297)
- Eater of Spears has a Skill and class for throwing and says the axes are good against [Mages]. (Book 9, Ch5.55G L303)
- Eater of Spears dodges Erin Solstice's thrown [Unerring Throw] bottle, saying the Skill only works if he holds still. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L228-231)
- Arrows from Badarrow mostly fail to penetrate Eater of Spears's skin. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L233-235)
