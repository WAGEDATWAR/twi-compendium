# Kerrig
> Type: person | Also called: Sir Kerrig; Sir Kerrig Louis; Kerrig Louis | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.19G
> See also: Welca Caveis, Bethal, Rags, Pyrite, Laken, Thomast
## Overview
- Sir Kerrig Louis is a Knight of the Petal, sworn to the Walchais family, and an older [Knight]. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L172-174; Book 7, Ch5.20G L4)
- Sir Kerrig is of the landlord nobility: his claim to aristocracy is a small, worthless inherited plot that once held a village. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L5-6)
- Sir Kerrig became a [Knight] despite being a first-born son, and served over three decades, fighting Drakes, Goblins and monsters. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L6)
- Sir Kerrig carries an enchanted battleaxe that sparks with lightning. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L122; Book 11, Ch6.26 L784)
## Captive of the Flooded Waters Tribe (Book 7)
- {event} Sir Kerrig and Welca Caveis are captured after trees are dropped on them and the armoured [Knights] survive being knocked out. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L184)
- Lady Bethal abandoned the two captured Knights rather than keep fighting in the trapped woods. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L184)
- {event} On their first night as prisoners, Kerrig and Welca overwhelmed a guard, stole a sword, and were flattened by a dozen Hobs. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L185)
- Rags ordered that nobody may take advantage of her prisoners, male or female, and the Redfang warriors back her. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L187)
- {event} Sir Kerrig thanks Pyrite, saying the tribe is unlike what he had been told. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L286)
- {event} Sir Kerrig asks Pyrite to talk honestly, saying he had scarcely believed Goblins could be peaceful after Velan the Kind. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L288)
- {event} As Welca's superior, Sir Kerrig orders her to take no action against the Goblins and not to escape, but to watch and listen. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L35)
- {event} Watching the Goblins argue and care for their children, Sir Kerrig fears he has been blind, and that he may have killed people rather than monsters. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L45)
- {belief} Sir Kerrig suggests the Flooded Waters tribe may be to Goblins what Knights are to Humans, hunting those who kill Goblins. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L612-615)
- {event} Sir Kerrig sends Welca away on a horse to reach Lady Bethal while he stays to influence Pyrite and Rags. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L636-641)
- Sir Kerrig believes his order alone could not subdue this tribe, which would escape repeatedly and begin to retaliate. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L641)
- {event} Sir Kerrig fights five Hobs and eight Goblins barehanded in a boxer's stance and leaves them all unconscious. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L652,L658)
- {event} Pyrite kicks Sir Kerrig against a cart and knocks him out with a punch for six hours. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L671)
## Release and the Message to Laken (Book 8)
- {belief} Lady Bethal believes Sir Kerrig and Welca perished, until Rags tells her both are prisoners. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L159,L173-177)
- {state} Later, the tribe returns Sir Kerrig to Bethal's Knights without ransom, telling her to take the Human and go. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L124-129)
- Sir Kerrig tells Bethal he helped Welca escape less than a day before, and describes the poison-gas night attack on the camp. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L85)
- {belief} Sir Kerrig tells Rags that Laken Godart's attacks are preemptive retaliation for Goblin raids, and that Laken cannot tell her tribe from the Goblin Lord's. (Book 8, Ch5.23G L402-404)
- Sir Kerrig tends the poisoned Goblins, having them gargle salt water, and watches the wounded with pity and regret. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L241)
- Sir Kerrig tells the Goblins only that the Unseen Emperor is named Laken Godart, and does not know where he comes from. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L243)
- {event} Sir Kerrig first refuses to trust Rags or promise to persuade the Emperor of her message. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L226)
- {event} After Pyrite argues the tribe wants peace, Sir Kerrig agrees to carry Rags' message and try to broker peace with Laken. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L239-241)
- {state} Sir Kerrig rides out of the city on a horse supplied by the Goblins. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L246)
- Tyrion Veltras says he met Sir Kerrig in Riverfarm, where he was well and unharmed. (Book 8, Ch5.37G L250-253)
## Knights of the Petal and Clairei Fields (Book 11)
- {event} Sent by Sir Thomast, Sir Kerrig confronts six Knights of the Order of Clairei Fields, including Sir Molte, in an inn. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L771)
- {event} Sir Kerrig pours his ale over Sir Molte's head and smashes a mug into his face. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L778-779)
- {event} Sir Kerrig declares the Clairei Knights enemies of his order until Lady Bethal receives redress, and challenges them to fisticuffs. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L781)
- {event} Sir Kerrig draws his lightning battleaxe when a Clairei Knight draws a sword, and she sheathes it. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L784-786)
- {event} Sir Kerrig throws Sir Molte through a window, smashes another Knight through a wall, and proclaims war. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L788-791)
- Sir Kerrig says the Order of the Petal draws no blood unless necessary, as Sir Thomast has proclaimed. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L789)
