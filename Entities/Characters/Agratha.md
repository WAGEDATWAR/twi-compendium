# Agratha
> Type: person | Also called: Witch Agratha | First appears: Ch6.37E
> See also: Hedag, Califor, Laken
## Overview
- Agratha is a [Witch] and [Teacher], described as a [Witch Teacher] who wears a little red pointed hat. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L127-129)
- Agratha is not among the most powerful [Witches] in Riverfarm, but her coven is large. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L129)
- Agratha leads nearly a dozen [Witches] who were the first to take up the Emperor's bargain. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L129)
## Events
- A [Witch] is killed and Agratha loses her schoolhouse, so Alevica tells Califor that Agratha may not attend. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L111-113)
- Califor hopes Agratha can attend a meeting of [Witches]. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L111)
- Hedag calls on Agratha to sweep up spilled honey with a spell, and Agratha asks Hedag not to shout. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L126-128)
- Agratha has her apprentices join in sweeping the honey as a test, and she and Hedag marshal the young [Witches] to get it into the jar. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L131-133)
- Laken thanks Agratha and Hedag with a pot of honey, and Agratha bows slightly. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L142-145)
- Agratha is the first [Teacher] Laken has met in this world. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L73)
- Agratha's coven appeared first among the covens seeking Riverfarm's protection. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L72)
- Rebeca says that she, Agratha and Devay are trying to create a school of magic for young [Witches]. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L138)
