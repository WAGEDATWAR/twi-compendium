# Ullim
> Type: person | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.45E
> See also: Tyrion Veltras, Jericha, Magnolia Reinhart, Eloise
## Overview
- Ullim is the [Majordomo] of House Veltras and serves Lord Tyrion Veltras. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L48)
- Ullim has a high-class servant's demeanor, dress and manners, and he tells a [Messenger] that Tyrion is riding and a missive can be left. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L47-49)
- Jericha is Tyrion's personal [Mage] and first assistant, who rushes in when he calls for her and Ullim. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L451; Book 18, Ch7.42M L105-106)
## Investigating the Rain (Book 12)
- {belief} Ullim reports that villages recall the passing of a [Witch], Eloise, the Tea Witch, around the time the rains began, and many believe she cursed the region. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L452)
- Ullim discovers by discreet inquiry that the Tea Witch is Lady Eloise of House Havin, a Terandrian [Lady] considered dead to her family for over three decades. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L458-461)
- {belief} Ullim and Tyrion both think of Magnolia Reinhart, who loves tea and is fond of using agents, as the possible hand behind the Tea Witch. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L463-465)
- Ullim notes that Lords Erill, Pellmia and Lady Ieka suffer weather disturbances while Invrisil does not. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L466)
- Ullim reports that Lord Pellmia's [Manservant] Kilmet saw another [Witch], Hedag or Hedag the [Executioner], pass through his lands. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L466)
- Tyrion orders Ullim to send word to Pellmia and the villages the [Witch] passed through, and to check whether Bethal, Pryde and Wuvren are affected. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L471)
- {belief} Tyrion says that if Bethal, Pryde and Wuvren are unaffected, he will treat Reinhart's interference at Liscor and this rain as an insult. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L473-474)
