# Pivr
> Type: person | Also called: Revalantor Pivr; Flying Antinium Prognugator | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.23L
> See also: Tersk, Xrn, Klbkch, Free Antinium, Belgrade
## Overview
- Pivr is a Revalantor of the Flying Antinium who introduces himself to Klbkch by that title. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L406)
- Pivr has jade-colored wings, four sets of eyes, a hunched body, twin daggers at his side and poisonous spit. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L407)
- Pivr produces acidic bile from his mandibles when upset, a trait he was engineered to have. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L32)
- Pivr has a higher-pitched voice than other Antinium and fans his wings restlessly when he stands still. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L377)
- The Free Antinium Queen regards Pivr and the Flying Queen's winged soldiers as half-baked copies of the true winged soldiers she saw long ago. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L377)

## Arrival at Liscor (Book 3)
- Pivr asks Klbkch whether his Queen has been creating variations of the Worker and Soldier form without authorization. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L406)
- Pivr brings six warriors of his Queen: five regular warriors and one larger heavy-combat variant with huge wings and a beetle-like horn. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L411-412)
- Pivr says the Flying Antinium came to see what Klbkch's Hive has wrought. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L411)
- {event} Pivr announces that his orders were to obey Prognugator Xrn only until the Antinium reached Liscor, and that the Flying Antinium will enter the city by the main gates. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L459)
- Pivr tells Xrn her input was not required and that she has no authority over his Queen's orders. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L464,L468)
- Pivr promises the Flying Antinium will not fight even if provoked, but will maintain a show of force, and invites Klbkch to join them. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L468)
- Pivr and his six warriors spread their wings and skip across the snow toward Liscor, too fast for Klbkch to follow on foot. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L469-470)

## Visit to the Free Antinium Hive (Books 4-5)
- Pivr tells the Free Antinium Queen's chamber that he has surveyed the Hive and found only four Individuals with formal designations and no significant levels, Skills or classes. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L375)
- Pivr asks, on behalf of his Queen, what formal plan the Free Queen would put before the Hives for converting a whole Hive to Individuals. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L380)
- Pivr ignores the Free Queen's questions about how many Prognugators and Individuals his own Hive has produced. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L378-379)
- Pivr refuses Klbkch's offer to join an expedition and says he will stay to observe the Hive and the dungeon. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L420-421)
- The Free Antinium Queen privately considers arranging for a monster to break through and slaughter Pivr by accident, noting that Flesh Worms are quite lethal. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L422)
- {belief} Tersk reports that Pivr fought in the dungeon and was wounded, that three of his five accompanying warriors died, and that his heavy-combat variant was badly damaged. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L263)
- {belief} Tersk considers Pivr a failure of the Prognugator class who does not deserve the title of Revalantor, lacking Klbkch's presence and battle experience. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L267)

## The Antinium Wars history book (Book 6)
- Pivr stares at Tersk as Tersk holds a history book describing an entire Antinium army destroyed by a Goblin. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L22-23)
- Pivr protests to Xrn that the Antinium cannot be defeated so easily and notes that the book says she led the first army. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L33)
- Pivr's wings fan wide and acidic bile leaks from his mandibles when Xrn confirms the book's account; Tersk snatches the book away before acid lands on it. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L31-32)
- Pivr asks Xrn how the Antinium fought and won against Goblin Lords, since the Antinium do not retreat. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L53)
- Xrn thinks that for all his faults Pivr is brave, and answers his question truthfully. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L54)

## The assault on Liscor (Book 9)
- Pivr flexes his wings with his four Prognugators as Xrn addresses fourteen Prognugators and one Revalantor of the Flying, Silent and Armored Hives. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L387-388)

## Staying in Liscor (Book 18)
- The narration calls Pivr the Flying Antinium's Prognugator and shows him accompanying Xrn, fluttering his wings in a slight challenge at Xeu. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L58)
- Pivr notes his own inefficient motion compared with the perfectly hexapodal Xeu of the Silent Antinium. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L57)
- Pivr follows Belgrade around for three hours asking why he does things and calling them inefficient. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L137-140)
- Pivr's constant refrain to Belgrade is to ask whether Prognugator Xrn gave him orders to do something, and if not why he does it. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L141-142)
- Pivr is always mystified when Belgrade answers that he chose to act on his own because it seemed useful. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L143-144)
