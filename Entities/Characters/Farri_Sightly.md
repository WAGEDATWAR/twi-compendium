# Farri Sightly
> Type: person | Also called: Farri; Merchant Farri Slightly | First appears: Ch6.17S
> See also: Selys, Pisces, Bezale, Montressa
## Overview
- Farri Sightly is a Human [Merchant] who introduces himself to Selys and has an apprentice [Trader] named Febin. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L184,L187,L226)
- Farri is a thirty-something [Merchant] who counts Selys as something of a friend. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L42,L45)
- Farri has the Skill [Evaluation of Wealth], which he notes is handy for a [Merchant] or a [Thief], and reads Selys as having new money. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L199)
- Farri also has [Prolonged Negotiations], which Selys notices. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L206)
- Farri says he can pick his clients and plans to stay at the Firehearth inn. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L227)
## Dealings with Selys
- {event} Farri sells Selys a magical vault for eight hundred gold, a bag of holding for six hundred and sixty gold and other items at fair prices. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L224,L264)
- Farri has a Drake-style full-toothed smile and makes the extra trip to Liscor for Selys, saying one good client makes up for many miles. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L45-46)
- Selys praises the cooling charm she bought from Farri, which does not work all night but gets her to sleep. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L47)
- Farri offers Pisces and Selys credit worth twenty percent of what he thinks they can spend. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L203)
- {belief} Pisces believes Farri painted over the gem on his ring finger so it would shine only towards him. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L215)
- Farri sells Selys nearly twelve hundred gold's worth of items, to be delivered to her apartment, and adds a tracking charm to her bag of holding. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L210)
- Farri refuses to place a large order with Bezale of Maweil's scrolls, because of recent events involving his client Selys Shivertail. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L266-283)
- Farri describes Selys as new to her money, owner of the Heartflame Breastplate, niece of Zel Shivertail and granddaughter of the Adventurer's Guildmistress. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L281,L283,L289)
