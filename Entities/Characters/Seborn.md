# Seborn
> Type: person | Also called: Seborn Sailwinds; the Drowned Man | First appears: Ch2.22
> See also: Moore, Jelaqua Ivirith, Halfseekers, Garen Redfang, Wailant Strongheart, Captain Therrium, Ulinde
## Overview
- Seborn is the [Rogue] of the Halfseekers, a Gold-rank team of part-humans also called the Half Freaks. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L390-392,L410)
- Seborn is a tall, gaunt man covered across half his body in a shell, and he has a crab arm. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L248,L414)
- Seborn is a Drowned Man, and he says he has been like this since he was a young man. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L410-412)
- Seborn explains that Drowned is the name for everyone who suffered the fate of a sea creature latching onto and melding with their body. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L415)
- Seborn's left side looks merged with a sea crustacean, including his left hand as a crab's claw, his face and his leg. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L615)
- Seborn has a Human eye on one side of his face. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L832)
- Seborn seldom laughs and speaks little, though Erin notes he talks more than usual when nervous. (Book 8, Ch5.28 L198; Book 9, Ch5.57 L615)
- Seborn is of the Sailwinds family and is a Gold-rank adventurer. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L376,L530-533)
## Abilities and equipment
- Seborn wields enchanted daggers and prefers to wound rather than kill. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L145)
- Seborn's daggers carry acid and [Frostbite] enchantments, and he says acid, a thousand cuts that grow worse, suits a [Rogue]. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L95-102)
- Seborn says he hopes to change his other blade to lightning if the team gets a big payout, since lightning paralyses and weakens muscles. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L107-109)
- Seborn can slip into shadows and vanish from view even when watched. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L810; Book 9, Ch5.58 L84)
- Seborn uses the skills [Shadowsteps], [Blur Leap], [Cat's Evasion], [Flurry Blades] and [Rearward Cut] against Garen. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L91-94)
- Seborn uses [Blur Leap] again in his brawl with Wailant. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L858)
- Seborn is a trap expert, in the same role in his party as Halrac in his. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L161,L205)
- Seborn recognises the dungeon door's pressure mechanism and suggests a Bronze-rank adventurer could use a protected door as a shield. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L204-206)
- Seborn explains that an enchanted mace shrieks like a Death Wailer, affects everyone but its wielder, and may also immobilise. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L89-90)
- Seborn asks Ceria to consider selling the Horns' dagger when Typhenous and Revi bid for the ring. (Book 5, Ch4.10 L381-387)
- Seborn scouts ahead of the Horns of Hammerad in the dungeon and tells them to stay about ten paces behind. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L504)
## Pirate past
- Seborn says he is a retired [Pirate], not a [Sailor], and a member of the Undersea Crews. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L209-210; Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L380)
- Seborn orders a Narwhal's Knife, a double, in the Pallass bar and says it tastes like a port-city bar's, only cleaner. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L195-201)
- Seborn lists the worst waters to avoid: the Iron Vanguard's port cities, Wistram, Roshal, the Siren's territories and Zeres. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L214,L218)
- Seborn says he left the sea because he was sick of it, since land monsters are mild next to ship-eating whales. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L221)
- Seborn says Crelers are as bad on land as in water. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L221)
- Seborn says he sailed with The Siren's Depths, a crew of the Undersea Crews. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L825,L830)
- Wailant Strongheart says he sailed with the Steelsails Armada, whose ships The Siren's Depths sank in the past. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L827,L833)
- Seborn replies that the Steelsails Armada clashed with some of the Drowned Folk. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L834)
- Wailant throws his drink into Erin's face and knocks the table over, and Seborn punches him, starting a brawl. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L852-860)
- Seborn and Wailant, both [Pirates], agree that people who cast [Clear Skies] are idiots because it delays a storm and makes it worse. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L226-232)
- On Noass's show, Seborn recognises the crew of The Passing Shadow and its [Depth Captain] Therrium Sailwinds. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L525-530)
- Seborn says Therrium is a lawless [Thief], despised even by other Drowned Folk for breaking his word, and a backstabbing shrimp. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L533)
- Seborn answers Noass's question about relation with a flat "Relation". (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L531-533)
- Captain Therrium addresses Seborn as "my boy" when Seborn locks eyes with him through the orb as if he were his father. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L525-529)
- On the show, Seborn says he is there to advertise his team and tells viewers to hire the Halfseekers. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L380)

## Garen Redfang
- Seborn joins Jelaqua and Moore walking north with a door when they meet the Redfangs and Garen in the pass. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L613-618)
- Seborn utters an oath on seeing Garen and straightens when Jelaqua names him. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L619,L637)
- Seborn shakes hands with Jelaqua before the team attacks Garen, and reaches for his daggers. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L644-649)
- Seborn charges silent as Jelaqua leaps and Moore bellows. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L654)
- Seborn leaps over the Carn Wolf riders to fight Garen and cuts a horse and a Goblin with both blades. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L82)
- Seborn is roughly Garen's level but, as a [Rogue], cannot win head-on, yet he fights on without fear. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L86)
- Garen uses [Frenzy Cuts] and then his transparent Redfang to cut open Seborn's side and carapace. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L90,L115-117)
- Seborn shatters a potion across his wounds and goes on fighting, shouting "traitor" at Garen. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L118-120)
- A flashback shows Garen arguing with Seborn over an acid dagger, with Seborn the cold voice of reason in the team. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L95-106)
- Seborn tells Garen that the old teammates tried to take the key, but Garen struck first and stabbed three of them before they could move. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L480)
- Seborn agrees that threatening to kill Garen was absolutely justified to stop a Goblin King. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L487-488)
- {belief} Seborn says Halassia and Ukrina were right about a Goblin King, because one cannot appear again, but that Garen was wrong to attack them. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L648-657)
- Seborn says Garen attacked them and that what he remembers was murder. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L655-657)
- After Garen dies, Seborn says Garen was a traitor regardless of how he died, and looks away from the body. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L530-532)
- Seborn says that Garen's key is gone and that he has debts and obligations, and he is unsure whether to quit. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L551-562)
- The Halfseekers burn Garen's body, find no key on him, and Jelaqua proposes one more adventure. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L540,L546,L563)
