# Peclir Im
> Type: person | Also called: Peclir | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.25N
> See also: Niers, Foliana, Xalandrass
## Overview
- Peclir Im is a Human [Chamberlain], an experienced high-level servant who works under Foliana. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L68; Book 5, Ch4.25N L77)
- Peclir is a [Chamberlain] because [Stewards] are rare and require royalty to appoint them. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L68)
- Peclir has worked for four years and notes his anniversary is that month. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L88-89)
- Peclir fears dying of a heart attack like his predecessor, whom people still discuss as the previous [Majordomo]. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L69; Book 5, Ch4.25N L78)
- Peclir cleans Niers's dishes and offers to poison rats in the Fraerling passages. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L73-77)
- Peclir is the [Chamberlain] of the Forgotten Wing company who keeps much of Niers's citadel academy and home running. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L454)
- Peclir brings Niers a purple cloth-covered item on a pillow when Niers summons him. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L696-701)
