# Moore
> Type: person | Also called: Moore (Halfseekers); the half-Giant | First appears: Ch2.22
> See also: Jelaqua Ivirith, Seborn, Halfseekers, Typhenous, Garen Redfang, Maughin
## Overview
- Moore is a part-Giant [Mage] of the Gold-rank adventuring team the Halfseekers, who stands eight or perhaps nine feet tall. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L234,L410)
- Moore wears patched clothing and carries a massive staff crowned by an orb of light. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L234)
- Jelaqua describes Moore as the team's only spell caster, unless Ceria joins them. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L410)
- Jelaqua says Moore is a quarter-Giant or eighth-Giant, though he is not sure which, and is used to stares. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L377)
- Moore speaks politely and gently even in a fight, and sits cross-legged on the floor because no chair is large enough. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L237-240; Book 6, Ch4.43 L31)
## The Halfseekers
- {rel} The Halfseekers, called the Half Freaks, are a Gold-rank team led by Jelaqua Ivirith, and Moore and Seborn are her companions. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L390-392,L410)
- Jelaqua says the team has lost a few members recently and came to the new dungeon to explore it and scout for a recruit. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L392)
- Lyonette sees Seborn drink like a sailor, a pace only matched by Jelaqua's enthusiasm and Moore's capacity for alcohol. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L36)
- After a party, Moore, Jelaqua and Seborn are hungover, and Moore offers to look after Mrsha with the other two. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L26-36)
- Seborn tells Moore that he is thirty-one, and that Moore, Jelaqua and Halassia were all romantics. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L150,L154)
- Moore remembers Halassia as an old friend of the team, and he can now talk about the past without the old tension. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L151)
- Moore argues that the Halfseekers were founded to chase a dream, which is why he defends Jelaqua's romance. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L152)
## Spells and abilities
- Moore is a [Green Mage] who works with plants and earth. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L53; Book 9, Ch5.58 L39)
- Moore kicks Typhenous before the older mage can finish casting a binding spell, without raising his staff. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L242-243)
- Moore taps his staff so that a line of grass sprouts from the floorboards, as a safe and accurate way to measure mana use. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L219-220)
- Moore proposes this grass cantrip because a flame spell would risk Erin's wooden inn. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L215-219)
- During the inn fight, Moore thrusts his staff into the ground and raises a wall of earth to block Regrika. (Book 5, Ch4.30 L100-101)
- Moore calls vines covered in huge thorns from beneath Erin's floorboards to cover himself, Jelaqua and Seborn. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L78)
- Moore's spells [Barrier of Thorns] and [Thorn Spray] appear against spiders in the dungeon, and he says he has no Tier 5 spell. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L171,L239)
- Moore wears a [Thorn Armor] spell of thorns and vines in the dungeon fight against the Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L298)
- Moore fights Garen's tribe in [Armor of Thorns] and his skin resists Garen's cut with [Barkskin]. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L41)
- Moore says [Mass Armor of Thorns] covers himself, Jelaqua and Seborn, cuts what they run into, and does not restrict movement much. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L65)
- {state} Moore casts [Earthen Spire] to seal a cave of bees in solid stone, a spell four times as thick as Ceria's or Pisces' spells. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L164-165)
- {state} Moore says [Earthen Spire] is the first spell he earned after the battle with the Redfang tribe, though he levelled in the dungeon. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L163,L185)
- Ceria and Pisces judge [Earthen Spire] to be a Tier 4 spell because the stone stays in place. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L163,L189-190)
- Ceria gives the reward for sealing the cave to the Halfseekers, because Moore sealed it. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L216-218)
- Moore teaches the spell to Mrsha over two days, and Mrsha finds it easier than learning from Krshia's spellbook. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L53)
## The magic door
- Moore asks whether the door will be used as an involuntary translocation spell or a portal effect, since that affects mana cost. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L204)
- Typhenous says Moore had the idea of a mechanism to attune the door to each lodestone, creating multiple connections. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L122)
- Moore recharges the door, and Jelaqua says he has managed to juice it up once already. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L94-96)
- Moore says recharging the door takes a lot of mana and he is nearly tapped out, but he could cross using a mana potion. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L96)
- Pisces works a long time with Moore and Typhenous to let Erin switch the door's portal by tapping a mana stone against it. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L234)
- {event} Moore charges the door so that he and Seborn travel to Pallass, which tires him out, because Seborn refuses to walk to Invrisil. (Book 13, Ch6.56 L283)
