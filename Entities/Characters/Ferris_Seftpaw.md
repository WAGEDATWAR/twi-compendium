# Ferris Seftpaw
> Type: person | Also called: Ferris; Ferris Thycarl | First appears: Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons
> See also: Rafaema, Erin Solstice, Lyonette du Marquin
## Overview
- Ferris is a Gnoll agent trained in the City of War, who performs secret duties, and gives the name Ferris Thycarl to Rafaema. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L146-147)
- Ferris has no rank or affiliation when he introduces himself, and Rafaema knows what he is. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L147)
- Ferris is one of Manus's top agents and an expert in covert operations, infiltration and combat. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L108-109)
- Ferris can detect truth in three ways: a Skill, an artifact that helps him read people, and training in detecting lies across species. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L114)
## Book 15: Rafaema's minder
- {reveal} Ferris is the newest of Rafaema's protectors, and she spots him within a second of meeting him, so his disguise needs work. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L142-144)
- Ferris was chosen for Rafaema because he is very good at his job and has the right personality. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L222)
- Two earlier agents assigned to Rafaema, Kelis and Amessia, fifty and thirty years ago, nearly caused disaster. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L222)
- Ferris is aware of the war council's decisions in which Rafaema, known only to two Walled Cities, has a respected voice. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L221-222)
## Book 18: Izril's Wonders
- {event} Ferris appears at the inn as a Gnoll [Coachman] for the company Izril's Wonders, wanting to add the inn to a tour of the Walled Cities. (Book 18, ChInterlude_A_Meeting_of_Druids L419-429)
- Ferris tells Lyonette his company takes people to each Walled City and that he is no [Negotiator], only closest when orders came. (Book 18, ChInterlude_A_Meeting_of_Druids L423,L429)
- {reveal} Ferris is revealed as a Manus agent who came to the inn under the guise of Izril's Wonders, a real company with which he actually holds a charter. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L108-109)
- Ferris reports by two-way scroll to Rafaema, who is unhappy that he has failed to get Erin to go on the tour. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L129)
- {event} Erin forgets Ferris's offer and declines the Walled Cities tour, saying she should stay with the inn and will not leave for days. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L113,L117,L124-126)
## Book 19: Lyonette
- Ferris has been sent to obtain Erin Solstice, but finds she is too well connected for him to spirit her away. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L317-318)
- {belief} Ferris concludes that Lyonette, whom he identifies as the 6th Princess of Calanfer, is the real power behind the inn, and that Erin is a mascot. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L323-327)
- {event} Ferris changes targets from Erin to Lyonette. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L328)
- Ferris is not completely right: the text says his conclusion is half-wrong. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L329)
- Ferris tries to enter the [Garden of Sanctuary] a dozen different ways, and its Skill foils him. (Book 19, Ch7.48K L330)
