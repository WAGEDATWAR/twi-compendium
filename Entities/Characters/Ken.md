# Ken
> Type: person | Also called: Kenjiro Murata; Kenjiro | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.02D
> See also: Luan, Aiko, Daly, Geneva, Quallet, Xalandrass, Paige
## Overview
- Ken, full name Kenjiro Murata, is a Japanese university student who is twenty one when he arrives in Baleros. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L288; Book 5, Ch1.06D L2)
- Ken was studying business at university, in the same classes as his lifelong friend Aiko Nonomura. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L288-291)
- Ken is polite and deferential, applying the etiquette of his own culture to Baleros, and people tend to like him. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L44-46)
## Background
- Ken's grandfather died the year Ken entered university, at the age of 88. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L2)
- Ken's grandfather was fifteen when Japan surrendered and lived through the American bombing raids, which he sometimes described to Ken. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L3-5)

- Ken and Aiko befriend Luan on the march with Gravetender's Fist, since Luan is the first person they get to know. (Book 5, Ch1.02D L271-272)

- {event} Ken fights a zombie Centaur with Luan, Aiko and two Dullahans, cutting at its side while Luan, who leads their patrol, delivers the final blow. (Book 5, Ch1.03D L60-68)

## Leaving for Geneva and the Red Cross Company
- {event} Luan, Ken and Aiko tell Geneva that they appeared in Baleros only last week and ask whether she is from their world. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L300)
- Geneva offers Luan, Ken and Aiko food and a bed, but only for people from their world, and Luan says at least forty such people remain. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L335-339)
- {event} Luan declares he cannot be part of the war, and Ken answers that he will go too, hoping the others follow. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L293; Book 5, Ch1.04D L357)
- Ken goes alone through the valley under a white flag to meet the war vendor Xalandrass, escorted by two tall Lizardmen. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L109-110)
- {event} Ken barters an Evercut arrowhead, taken from a friend, to Xalandrass for red, white and black paint plus some coin. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L124-146)
- Xalandrass says he once sold Evercut arrows to the Roving Arrow company and hopes Ken will spread word of his fairness. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L128-146)
- {event} Ken and Luan wear white armor marked with red crosses, plant a flag and carry a shield with the same cross onto the battlefield. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L335-339)
- Ken and Luan carry wounded soldiers back, making seven trips, and soldiers from both sides hesitate rather than attack them. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L339-340; Book 5, Ch1.05D L364)
- Ken points to a flying red-cross banner and says the Red Cross is a company, and Geneva later finds a Red Cross Company sign above the camp. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L156; Book 5, Ch1.05D L308)
- {event} Ken leaves a blistered, gassed young Dullahan woman behind to save burn victims he can still help, and feels deep guilt afterwards. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L408-415)
- Ken and Luan begin acting as [Medics], and are cornered by eight Centaurs six days after starting. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L447-448)
- {belief} Ken thinks Dullahans fight with a Japanese-style ideal of victory at any cost, using suicide attacks, traps and prisoner killing. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L462-463)
- {belief} Ken thinks Centaurs regard ambushes as honorable but abandon honor once they suspect the other side of underhandedness. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L461; Book 5, Ch1.05D L464)

## Later: the United Nations company
- Ken has taught his companions how to behave with Baleros races, such as not touching Dullahans and chatting with Lizardfolk. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L442)
- {event} Ken persuades a Dullahan company, a Gold-rank [Mage] heading the Mage's Guild in Talenqual, the Adventurer's Guildmaster and a ship's crew to fight giant serpents. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L926-931)
- Ken and Daly organized the Talenqual operation, gathering adventurers, Runners and sailors rather than inviting just one company. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L928-930)
- Ken has no set job in the United Nations company, but visits allies from Hastel to Silver-rank teams and shopkeepers at least weekly with small gifts. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L328-329)
- Ken proposes saving for Paige's emergency fund, then giving twenty percent of spare money to repairs, books, beds and tools, and the rest to gear for Daly's team. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L342-345)
