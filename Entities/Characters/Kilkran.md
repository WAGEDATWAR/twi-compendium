# Kilkran
> Type: person | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.63P
> See also: Jasi, Wesle Salkis, Emme, Grev, Pralcem, Players of Celum
## Overview
- Kilkran is a former [Blacksmith] with a golden voice, a bald head and a full beard, which he shaves when a role demands it. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L15)
- Kilkran is an [Actor] of the Players of Celum, a troupe over forty strong at the time. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L99-100)
- Kilkran wears wigs, which Mrsha finds very adaptable, over his excellent bald head. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L39)
## Events
- Kilkran sighs and drinks while Pralcem argues about the troupe's version of the classic plays. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L14-16)
- Wesle laughs at Kilkran's ire over the lesser [Actors] in the troupe. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L99-100)
- {event} Kilkran plays King Duncan in Macbeth, and Emme thinks he can emote decently, while Wesle and Jasi have reached another level. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L302)
- {event} Kilkran and the other Players of Celum enter The Wandering Inn when the riots cancel the evening's play. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L39)
