# Kel
> Type: person | Also called: Guardsman Kel; Desk Sergeant Kel; Sergeant Kel | First appears: Ch6.31
> See also: Venim, Erin Solstice, Saliss
## Overview
- Kel is a surly, hot-tempered Drake [Guardsman] of the Pallass City Watch who guards the Pallass end of Erin Solstice's magic door. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L192-197,L212)
- Kel carries a spear and resents Erin's repeated unauthorised crossings, which Erin says have happened about eight times. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L197,L208)
## Guarding the door
- Kel tells Erin that Watch Captain Venim requires everyone passing from Liscor to Pallass to be authorised. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L198)
- Kel says one [Guardsman] was judged enough to watch the door despite two unauthorised entries several days earlier. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L202)
- Kel lets Erin through rather than poke her with his spear, and she believes she is wearing him down. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L212)
- Kel spars with Erin over members of the Watch having free tea, which he insists is reserved for the Watch. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L208-209)
- Kel glares at Erin from the Pallass side after she leaves with Grimalkin and Maughin through the door. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L484-485)
- Kel pursues Grimalkin and Relc through the door, until Relc's clawed hand blocks him. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L864)
## Promotion
- Kel is the one who believes Erin when she warns that something is going to attack the inn, and orders Watch Captain Venim fetched and the alarm sounded. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L1032-1039)
- Later, Kel is promoted to Desk Sergeant two weeks before Erin's arrest, after the Creler attack, and is openly proud of it. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L38-39)
- Erin says that Kel was the one who believed her warning to Pallass about the attack. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L39)
## Erin's arrest
- Kel tells Erin that she was cleared by a truth spell, so he lists her account as completely accurate. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L51)
- Kel says Watch Captains review cases each evening before nightfall and any of them would agree the incident was an accident. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L90)
- Kel has Erin placed in the temporary holding area, the punishment cell, rather than the non-magical section. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L99)
## The attack on Pallass
- Kel holds the 8th-floor street in front of his Watch House against a Wyvern, ordering shields and long pikes forward. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L458-464)
- Kel uses [Protector's Order] to send a Gnoll family running past his squad's line. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L465-470)
- Kel's scales freeze when the Wyvern breathes frost on the squad, though he is nearly but not killed. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L472)
- A Dullahan [Guardswoman] in the first rank takes the brunt of the frost and dies, her head dropping from her body. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L472)
## Door duty as Sergeant
- Kel, as Sergeant, commands the Pallass Watch squads posted at the door on the 8th floor, which are the worst posting. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L12-13)
- Kel gives new arrivals a serious speech about the Crazy Human of Liscor, and looks sorry for them when they laugh. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L13-14)
- Kel tells a new squad that they will collect the food Erin sends through the door, and eats a cookie himself. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L88,L102,L105)
- Kel says he really hates Erin and tells the squad to file their complaints, while sitting in a comfortable armchair at the door. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L96,L106)
