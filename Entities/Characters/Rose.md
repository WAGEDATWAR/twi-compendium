# Rose
> Type: person | Also called: [Rose] (chat handle) | First appears: ChInterlude_The_Call
> See also: Joseph, Kevin, Imani, Galina, Ryoka Griffin, Maviola El, Temile
## Overview
- Rose is an Earther from California who was transported to this world and later stays at The Wandering Inn with six other Earthers. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L126; Book 17, Ch7.26 L96)
## Arrival and the group chat
- {event} In the Earthers' group chat, Rose says she is alone in a forest in the middle of nowhere and has seen only monsters. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L32)
- Rose says she is from California. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L126)
- Rose says that she has been in this world only one day, and that she is flipping out. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L132-134)
## Travelling with the Earthers (Book 17)
- {event} In Invrisil, Rose finds Kevin begging and snaps at the group for gathering instead of looking for jobs, since they have run out of money. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1126-1134)
- Rose blames herself partly for tipping their [Wagon Driver] and escorts, though she says everyone mismanaged the coin. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1134)
- One of the group says he sold his sword for a good price. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1138)
- Rose is told they cannot afford a play, and that Ryoka took their electronics and left them in the city. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1152,L1159)
- {event} Maviola El sees the despairing Earthers and offers to treat them to a meal at an inn. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1173-1177,L1194-1199)
- Seven Earthers have stayed at The Wandering Inn for roughly a week, and Rose is one of them. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L96)
- A young man from Spain growls at Rose over breakfast while drinking a morning cup of gin and blue fruit juice. (Book 17, Ch7.26 L77,L81)
## The Romeo and Juliet incident (Book 18)
- {event} Rose pitches an all-male version of Romeo and Juliet, called Romeo and Romeo, to Temile of the Players of Celum. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L372-374)
- {event} A crowd in the inn shouts "Disgusting" at the idea, and a Drake woman angrily accuses Rose of something called Turnscales and punches her. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L377,L385-388)
- Rose finds she stands alone against the crowd, and Onieva wants to protect her until Erin stomps her foot. (Book 18, Ch7.38 L384,L389-390)
