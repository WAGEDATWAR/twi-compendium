# Velan the Kind
> Type: person | Also called: the last Goblin King; Velan; Goblin Kings | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.03G
> See also: Goblin Lord, Tremborag, Rags, Greydath of Blades, Tallis the Stormbreaker
## Overview
- Velan the Kind became a Goblin King, and Rags speaks of the Goblin King of long ago as the last one. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L26; Book 4, Ch3.31G L209)
- The Gold Stone Chieftain tells Rags and Garen that the old Goblin King had a tribe larger than any other, was largely peaceful, and traded with Humans. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L178,L181)
- The Gold Stone Chieftain says the Goblin King was a Chieftain first, then a Goblin Lord, and then others called him Goblin King. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L181)
- {belief} The Gold Stone Chieftain claims the Goblin King was beloved by Goblins, and that something bad later happened and there was terrible war. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L177,L182-183)
- Greybeard tells Rags that the Goblin King ruled peacefully for six years, that Goblins made peace with Humans, Gnolls and Drakes on this continent and forged alliances on Baleros, and that Goblins were hunted in only a few places while he lived. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L213,L218)
## Kings in general
- A historian writes that Goblin Kings emerge every few centuries at most, each time heralding destruction and death. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L21)
- Almost every nation has signed a treaty requiring aid in destroying a Goblin King, and Goblin Lords are actively hunted to prevent them from becoming Kings. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L23)
## Before the war
- In the year 14 A.F., Velan the Kind was a Goblin Lord who lived in a peaceful relationship with the nations of Baleros, and his company of Goblins was rumored to be the fifth Great Company of Baleros. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L25)
- Velan led the Clean Grass tribe on Baleros, learned to make medicines from the plants and animals, and began making poisons when adventurers and mercenary companies came to slaughter his tribe. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L5-6)
- Velan led Hobs to shatter the Screaming Vine company, but after four companies were sent he lost his tribe and fled. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L7-9)
- {event} Velan flung himself into the ocean and swam for days, weeks and months without dying. (Book 9, Ch5.47G L9)
- Before he became a Goblin Lord, Velan was a [Healer] and an [Alchemist], strong enough to forage alone in the jungle, and his tribe used draughts that let a Goblin run for three days straight. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L245-248)
- Rags learns that Velan used crude funnels to pour blood into other Goblins. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L302)
## The war
- {state} In the waning months of spring, Velan fulfilled an unknown criterion and became a Goblin King, then appeared with his tribe in the Balerosian city of Zexil and razed it in a single day, breaking eighteen treaties of nonaggression. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L26)
- His only message to the leaders of Baleros was one word brought by a half-dead Courier: "Vengeance". (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L27-28)
- Every company on Baleros agreed to a truce, but Velan was joined by many Goblin Lords and their tribes, seized and leveled the eastern port cities, built thousands of ships and sailed at the start of summer. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L29)
- The historian says his seizing of the ports was a plan to flee across the sea to an easier target while the Four Great Companies advanced slowly. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L226-227)
- Nine Goblin Lords sailed with him, including Greydath of Blades and his right hand, the Goblin Lord [Shaman] Tallis the Stormbreaker. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L231)
- {event} His armies won two sea battles, one against a Minotaur fleet and one at Wistram, where Tallis the Stormbreaker killed the Archmage Balkizat after a nine-hour magical duel. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L229,L232)
- {event} The Goblin King landed at Cape Heathshell, and within about ten minutes his soldiers routed an army of about a hundred thousand led by Fulviolo El and the twins Petria and Ulva Terland, and he beheaded Fulviolo and Petria personally while Ulva escaped. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L233-235)
- {event} He vanished for eight days in the High Passes and reappeared south of them, opening a gateway between north and south Izril that bypassed Liscor. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L317-318)
- {belief} Reiss tells Rags that Velan did not clear the High Passes but hid his treasure there, locked away with a key. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L290)
- Reiss says that he and Garen belonged to the Ghostly Hand tribe, whose Chieftain remembered Velan's last months. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L286)
- {event} Velan led five thousand Goblin elite Hobs, as strong as Gold-rank adventurers and some as tall as half-Giants, against Terandrian ships landing over two hundred thousand troops. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L122-124)
- {event} Velan surrounded an Antinium Hive, wiped out the surface Antinium and killed its Queen himself, after which the Antinium stopped attacking his armies and pulled back to defend their remaining Hives. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L128-129)
- {event} In Tremborag's throne room Velan asks Tremborag to join him, accepts his refusal, and tells him the war has no purpose but vengeance against everything. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L582-602)
- In that scene Velan shows Tremborag two ordinary-looking steel keys and says there will one day be a King again who follows in his footsteps. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L605-606)
- Velan tells Tremborag to live. (Book 9, Ch5.51G L610)
## Death
- {state} Velan charged alone at the Archmages' mage detachment through the spells of three Archmages until a Silver-rank half-Elf adventurer, Elia Arcsinger, loosed an arrow with [Piercing Shot] through his eye and killed him instantly. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L138-148)
