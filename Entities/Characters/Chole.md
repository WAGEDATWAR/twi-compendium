# Chole
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 2, Ch1.00C
> See also: Tom, Richard, Cynthia, Emily, Vincent, Keith
## Overview
- Chole is one of the Earthers in Richard's group, and a [Nurse]. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L75; Book 18, Ch7.34C L494)
- Chole is from California. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L476)
- Chole did not complete her nursing education, but is still the most medically-qualified person in the world, by her own account. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L494)
## Early days
- Chole pulls Cynthia away from Tom and gives him the stink-eye when Cynthia screams at him over the clown music. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L153)
- Chole argues with Vincent, wanting to use his iPhone to call the others again. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L162-164,L189)
- Chole and another girl get between Vincent and Kevin before their argument comes to fists. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L178)
- Chole backs her horse up to ride behind Tom and Richard as the group rides into Paranfer. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L80)

- Chole opens Tom's door while he spins a knife, and screams and runs when he gestures at her with the blade. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L502-505)
## Book 18
- Chole approaches Richard to say she is worried that Tom has shut himself in his soundproofed room for two days. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L473-479)
- Chole tells Richard that Tom needs help and that she has been trying to work with him. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L483-485)
- Chole says Emily and Tom hate each other, and that no one talks to Tom. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L489)
- {belief} Chole thinks Tom's condition is not just mental illness, and that Tom is choosing to be the way he is, or has one of the sacrificial classes Lord Hayvon described. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L496-501)
- Richard tells Tom that Chole says the other Tom is not to blame for everything. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L582)
- Chole is working for Hayvon on blood transfusions and other non-magical Earth medicine. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L507; Book 18, Ch7.35C L770)
- Chole's blood-transfusion equipment is at Hayvon's mansion when it is needed to save Huntress Delezza, and the Champions try to improvise without it. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L766-784)
- Chole tells Tom how to test blood compatibility by mixing blood and watching for platelets, and Emily relays the method. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L780-782)
- After Huntress Delezza dies, the Champions feel guilt and think that things might have been different if Chole, their [Nurse], had been there. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L798-800)
- Emily shields Chole when Richard's group falls back from a battle at the wall. (Book 18, Ch7.36C L713)
