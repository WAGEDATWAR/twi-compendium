# General Lael
> Type: person | Also called: Lael | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.08K
> See also: Raelt, Jecaina, Zamea, Flos
## Overview
- General Lael is the high commander of the armies of Jecrass, known for emphasis on cavalry and speed, and guards the wide, open realm. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L135)
- Lael is a Level 27 [General], one of the highest-leveled assets of Jecrass, and young, in her early thirties. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L135)
- Lael was hired from the Republic of Belchan, a democracy known for its mage-schools and gold mines. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L147-148)
## Background
- Raelt promoted Lael after his daughter Jecaina suggested her, and hired her from the nation next door. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L147-148)
- Lael served with distinction in Belchan as a [Commander] until she was disciplined for striking a [Politician] over what she claimed was a bribery attempt, and she was demoted until Raelt contacted her. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L148)
- Raelt hired Lael for loyalty over levels, and she has proven incorruptible, at least to the River Wardens. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L149)
## Book 15-17
- {belief} Lael tells Raelt's court she cannot fathom a scenario where Jecrass's armies would triumph over Reim's, even at three times their size. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L136)
- {belief} Lael says that all things being equal, she would negotiate with the King of Destruction for a favorable surrender of Jecrass's forces. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L167)
- {event} Lael finds Nerrhavia's chariots retreating without her signal and Medain's adventurers already gone, as the battle against Reim goes badly. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L59-64)
- Lael orders the [Archers] safeguarded and a fall back, with Warden Emile's van to hold the line and then retreat. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L65)
- Jecrass's soldiers die rather than the foreign reinforcements, as in the last two battles, and Lael has a terrible taste in her mouth. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L66)
- Lael thinks that this is not the foreign forces' war and that Jecrass could have won if they had held and brought down Zamea. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L67)
