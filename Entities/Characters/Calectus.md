# Calectus
> Type: person | Also called: Calectus-san | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.04D
> See also: Okasha, Geneva, Ken, Luan, Selphids
## Overview
- Calectus is a Selphid who inhabits dead bodies and oversees the Selphids volunteering as Geneva's assistants. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L8)
- Calectus is the most senior and highest-level of the Selphids with Geneva, a Level 36 [Honor Guard]. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L145-148)
- Calectus first appears inhabiting a pale, nearly white Lizardman's body and is more at home among Dullahans. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L28; Book 5, Ch1.04D L158)
- Calectus is troubled by Okasha's possession of Geneva and speaks to her brusquely as a senior to a junior. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L146; Book 5, Ch1.04D L155-157)
## Geneva's camp
- Calectus tells Ken to leave the wounded Luan and not make things worse, and tells him the [Doctor] is sleeping. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L29; Book 5, Ch1.04D L79)
- Calectus says Geneva is valued above Okasha's or his own life, which the Selphids have agreed. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L157)
- {belief} Calectus believes that if any person can save the Selphids from the Wasting, it is Geneva. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L161)
- Calectus intends to stay with Geneva until she reaches Level 30, then suggest leaving, since both armies grow more savage. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L161)
- Calectus tells Geneva that Luan, Ken and Aiko are deserters who will be killed if they leave her camp. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L11)
- Calectus says he can keep the three safe only inside the camp, which Geneva's reputation as the Last Light protects. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L15-20)
- Calectus says that no potion could have saved the dying Dullahan War Walker, except those a Named Adventurer would use. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L218)
- Calectus warns that Geneva, having saved a War Walker and changed the battlefield, is now a powerful ally or a dangerous enemy. (Book 5, Ch1.05D L223)
## The camp attack
- {event} Calectus fights eight Dullahans alone in Geneva's burning camp, wounded and half-burned. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L22-23)
- Calectus kicks one armored Dullahan eight feet through the air, leaving an imprint in the iron armor. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L31-32)
- A Dullahan shouts that the Selphid is rampaging, and Calectus leaps six feet to crush a Dullahan's helmeted head. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L34-36)
- Calectus kills all the Dullahans, cutting the last in half with his halberd. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L37-39)
- Calectus is left with arrows in his shoulder and thigh, bloodless cuts and a deformed skull, yet only exhausted. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L44)
- Calectus lowers his halberd when Luan shouts his name and Ken calls him Calectus-san. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L24; Book 5, Ch1.06D L40-43)
- {belief} Calectus judges that the Centaurs sent Zalthia and that the Dullahan assassins were experienced soldiers, not hired mercenaries. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L49)
- {state} Calectus says he has torn this body's muscles and must find another. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L47)
- {state} Calectus returns from scouting in a female Dullahan's body that he killed himself. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L120)
- {reveal} Calectus warns Ken, Luan and Aiko that a Selphid living in a living host is forbidden by every nation and would mean death for Okasha and Geneva. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L119-126)
- Calectus gives Okasha a quarter bottle of healing potion for Geneva's torn muscles and tells her to use it sparingly. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L221)
- {belief} Calectus explains that sharing a body between two minds with their own Skills is forbidden because an army of them would be unstoppable. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L222)
- Quallet makes room around his map for Geneva's group on hearing that Calectus is a Level 36 [Honor Guard]. (Book 5, Ch1.06D L242)
## Later

- {event} Calectus holds a jar containing Okasha after the Selphids pull her out of Geneva's body. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1181-1182)
- {belief} Calectus tells Okasha she has committed the sin for which the Selphids were destroyed and that the Minds will sentence her. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1183-1184)
- Calectus says the holding-jar will serve as Okasha's prison for now. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1183-1184)
- Calectus, still an [Honor Guard] in a Dullahan body, tells Geneva that Selphid decisions are for the Minds and that Selphids are not trusted as a species. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L289-293; Book 18, Ch7.32D L299)
- The Minds are the governing body of the Selphids, according to Idis. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L300)
