# Falene
> Type: person | Also called: Falene Skystrall; Pointy (Dawil's name for her) | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.45
> See also: Dawil, Ylawes Byres, Ceria Springwalker, Pisces, Numbtongue
## Overview
- Falene Skystrall is a half-Elf [Battlemage] with light red hair and a dignified bearing, a member of the Silver Swords. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L56; Book 8, Ch5.24L L112)
- Falene graduated from Wistram three decades ago and remembers the academy fondly. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L271)
- Falene greets Ceria with ancient half-Elven gestures, and Ceria does not return them. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L58-60)
- Falene uses her staff to dry and knock the frosting off Ylawes' armor. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L67)
- Falene says the standards have lowered at Wistram since she attended. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L278)
- Falene is a [Battlemage], not an [Enchanter], but as the only fully Wistram-trained [Mage] present offers a free preliminary assessment of the Goblins' artifacts. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L112-115)
## Wistram contacts
- Falene receives a note from a Wistram colleague confirming Ceria and Pisces are graduates who left prematurely, and she suspects a cover-up. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L88-92)
- Wistram confirms Falene Skystrall is in Liscor, and she refuses to give confidential information on the two expelled students, saying it conflicts with her team's interests. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L404-406)
- Falene tells Ceria she was not at Wistram at the time and cannot comment on incidents, but thinks the two are inexperienced for graduates. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L96-97)
- {belief} Falene says her contacts in Wistram refused to explain unless she did them favors, and that Ceria and Pisces were expelled in their third year. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L838-841)
- Falene says several senior [Mages] challenged the academy's higher levels and failed, around the time the two left. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L841)
- Falene speaks with her old roommate Bashea by a private [Message] variant, and Bashea keeps telling her to come back. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L98,L125-128)
- Falene says Wistram gave her a blanket ban on any information regarding this team. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L125)
- {rel} Falene belongs to the Centrist faction, while the Revivalists, led by Archmage Nailihuaile, back Montressa du Valeross' team. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L137,L154-155)
## Magic and adventuring
- Falene draws on the inn's strong mana to cast [Lightning Shroud], and later casts [Blue Lightning], [Flash Fireball], [Water Arrows], [Sand Spray] and [Aerial Burst]. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L66,L83,L99)
- Falene casts [Grand Fireball] and [Featherfall] against the giant moths. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L516,L527)
- Falene, Dawil and Ylawes stop Yvlon from entering the dungeon, with Falene arguing she would get herself killed. (Book 8, Ch5.39 L264-268)
- Falene pitches a burning fastball that sets Dawil's glove and beard alight, and a swerving ball. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L726,L733)
- Falene argues that placing bounties has historically culled problematic populations, and Dawil rebukes her. (Book 9, Ch5.59 L577-579)
- Falene leaves Liscor north with Dawil and Ylawes, telling Ceria they offered a chance. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L286,L298)
- Falene transmits what she sees from the walls by scrying, so the entire world watches the battle. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L118-119)
- Falene offers to buy Pyrite's blue mana stones from Numbtongue, naming a price that stuns Erin, and Numbtongue refuses. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L456-462)
- {belief} Falene tells Numbtongue that mana stones are of no use to him, and Dawil calls her a half-wit. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L463-466)
- Falene complains of wasting a month rushing to Yvlon's aid, and says Ksmvr and Pisces are rolling in treasure. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L505-509)
## Private life
