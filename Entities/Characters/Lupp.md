# Lupp
> Type: person | Also called: Farmer Lupp; Mister Lupp | First appears: Ch6.25
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Bethal, Kamine
## Overview
- Lupp is an old [Farmer] who works a farm three miles south of a village, with a main cornfield and a smaller field of other crops. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L232,L253)
- Lupp's corn is ripe in spring, which Ryoka judges a Skill with limited effect because the field is small. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L232-233)
- Lupp's corn stalks stand sixteen feet high, with thick, heavy ears of golden kernels. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L234)
- Lupp keeps one cow, Helli, and dresses like a scarecrow, with straw sticking out of his ragged clothes. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L253,L295-296)
- Lupp has a [Dangersense] Skill that screams at him to stay quiet when something rustles outside. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L344)
## Family
- Lupp's daughter is Kamine, who sends him a package from Reizmelt at least every month. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L301-304)
- Kamine sends Lupp a whittler's knife because he wrote that his old knife had grown too dull. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L305-306)
- Lupp glances back at the farmhouse when he mentions the knife, and Ryoka wonders what happened to Kamine's mother but resolves never to ask. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L307)
- Kamine is a [Shoemaker] in Reizmelt, and Ryoka delivers milk and corn to her. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L431)
- Lupp says Kamine is not a high-level [Shoemaker] but might make something of herself with better materials and a bigger shop. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L384)
## Life on the farm
- Lupp bars his door and waits with a knife and healing potion on bad nights, sending a [Message] spell by scroll if it gets worse. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L344)
- Lupp once found pieces of an adventuring team that failed to scare off a monster when he opened his door afterward. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L344)
- Lupp says he used to drink, gave it up because it made him too miserable, and then devised his own method of coping. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L348)
- When Lupp starts staring at shadows or stops talking to himself, Lupp leaves the farm, taking corn or bread to the road or going to Reizmelt. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L348-350)
- Lupp says a friendly hand, a smile over some corn, or even a cursing match with a drunk [Wagon Driver] helps, though sometimes he needs to be alone. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L352)
- Lupp tells Ryoka of a hateful [Innkeeper] who brewed the best ale he ever tasted and hanged himself in his own inn using a keg as a stepstool. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L358)
- Lupp says the villagers found the [Innkeeper] the next day, argued over whether he was murdered, and then drank the ale rather than let it go to waste. (Book 11, Ch6.25 L360-363)
## The ambush on the road
- Lupp, disguised as a scarecrow, shoots an [Assassin] through the head just as the man is about to attack Bethal. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L239-241)
- Lupp puts a second arrow in the air, and the two remaining [Assassins] flee. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L242-244)
- Lupp checks the road and puts an arrow in all four bodies on the ground, which Bethal finds funny and Lupp does not. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L248)
- Lupp says no one enters his fields, animal or person, without his knowing, and that only six strangers had been about besides Bethal and Thomast. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L259)
- Lupp says even a Level 40 [Thief] cannot hide on his lands while standing on them. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L261)
## Selling corn to Bethal
- Lupp says his winter price is a copper for two ears, or one copper for three ears in bulk. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L319)
- Lupp plants about six hundred stalks and harvests twice a season, sometimes three times if he is quick. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L319)
- Lupp says he can harvest his whole corn crop in two days, or one if need be. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L384)
- Lupp accepts Bethal's offer of seven copper pieces for an ear of corn, saying he has his old age to think about. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L381-384)
- Lupp suggests the City Runner known as the Wind Runner when Bethal needs speed, though he has only seen her run for a few seconds. (Book 11, Ch6.26 L451-454)
## Later visits
- Lupp is paid gold for corn by Lady Bethal Walchaís, and says he never expected a [Lady] to keep her word. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1103-1106)
- Lupp asks Ryoka to bring him gin, which she delivers along with Bethal's payment and a jar from his daughter. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1104-1105)
- Lupp has made three harvests of corn and is surprised Lady Walchaís has not tired of it. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1110)
- Lady Walchaís sends a fast wagon by Lupp's farm each month, and Lupp says the corn is going to a market. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1111-1112)
- Lupp tells Ryoka she is as miserable as a [Farmer] he once knew and that her only choice is when she goes. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1216-1218)
- Lupp admits he was close to a darker day when Ryoka first met him than ever before, and says she has been a comfort to him. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1222)
- Lupp says the miserable [Farmer] became a famous adventurer named Eldert, who fought the Goblins in the Second Antinium War and joined a Named-Rank team. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1236-1239)
- Lupp says Eldert used to grow beets and probably changed his name to something fancier. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1239)
