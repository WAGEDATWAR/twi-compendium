# Garry
> Type: person | Also called: Garry the Chef; Antinium [Cook] | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.42
> See also: Erin Solstice, Free Queen, Pawn, Bird, Belgrade, Anand, Klbkch, Pebblesnatch
## Overview
- Garry is an Antinium Worker who cooks, and his four-armed carapace lets him handle hot and cold objects easily. (Book 4, Ch3.42 L33-36)
- Garry carries Erin's ultra-fluffy bread out to the common room of her inn while helping in her kitchen. (Book 4, Ch3.42 L36)
- Garry is one of five Individual Antinium (with Bird, Belgrade, Anand and Pawn) who survived the Workers' defence of Erin's inn against undead. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L231)
- Garry helped Erin at the Christmas party, though Erin had hardly had a chance to chat with him. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L232)
- Garry is the most timid of the five Individual Workers and prefers to work alone in isolation. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L31)
- Only Anand, Belgrade and Klbkch know Garry's name, apart from Erin. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L45)
## Role, levels and classes
- {state} Garry is a Level 17 [Cook] who has levelled exponentially fast. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L29)
- Garry is the only [Cook] in the Hive, since what other Antinium do to make their nutritious paste is not cooking. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L25)
- Garry was assigned to the Queen's chambers to cook for her and has worked and slept ever since. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L24)
- Garry feels he is slowing down, because he mass-produces what his Queen likes best and has no inspiration and no one to learn from. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L29)
- {state} Later, Garry is a [Chef] who has learned the Skill [Non-Allergen Cooking], which lets him eat gluten and serve pasta and bread to fellow Antinium. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L585-586)
- {state} Later, Garry gains his first apprentices, two Flying Antinium. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L713)
- Garry teaches his two new Flying Antinium assistants how to mix dough. (Book 17, Ch7.19 L924)
- {state} Later, Garry's assistants Pisca and Runel are both nearing Level 10 as [Assistant Baker] and [Assistant Cook]. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L272)
## Relationships
- Garry never forgets Knight, Calabrian, Milner-Barry and the other Workers, who had their names for less than an hour before dying against Skinner and the undead. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L28)
- Garry was as happy the first time he ate Erin's cooking and played chess with her as he is on his day off. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L27)
- Garry greatly admires Erin, believing she is the best at everything, and hopes to learn from her cooking. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L29)
- Bird moved into the Queen's chambers and talked about Erin's food, which helped Garry find the nerve to ask for time off. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L26)
- Garry takes acid flies he bought from Lyonette to the Free Queen, who thanks him and acknowledges him, which she would not have done before. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L46-51)
- The Free Queen is growing fond of Garry's attention to the presentation of food before it is eaten. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L54)
