# Hedag
> Type: person | Also called: Hedag the Executioner; Witch Hedag | First appears: Ch6.37E
> See also: Elmmet, Belavierr, Eloise, Mavika, Agratha, Alevica, Durene
## Overview
- Hedag is a huge woman who is both a [Witch] and an [Executioner], who carries an iron, nicked axe and wears a plain brown hat. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L186,L256; Book 12, Ch6.43E L1005)
- Hedag travels the further villages performing the oldest task where there is no jail, watchman or law in sight but her. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L255)
- Hedag comes from a line of [Executioners], a family trade that ties into her [Witch] side, and her craft is being the law. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L572-575)
- Hedag has a booming laugh and is jovial. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L108; Book 12, Ch6.43E L1005)
- Hedag has eyes in the back of her head, which she describes as a Skill, not magic. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1104)
- Belavierr says Hedag was a name spread across Izril, borne by village [Executioners], and that this Hedag is the last. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L144-147)
- Hedag grins when Belavierr asks about her predecessors, and asks Belavierr how many Hedags she killed. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L145-148)
## Skills
- Hedag uses [Revelation of Sin] to pull a black shadow out of a criminal that replays every crime he has committed, from back to front. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L199-213)
- Hedag says her Skill reveals only the truth of criminals and cannot be swayed by magic or lies. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L187,L213)
## The execution of Elmmet
- Hedag's Skill shows Elmmet stealing purses, changing faces, robbing a sleeping man, breaking into a house and slapping his wife while drunk. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L205-210)
- Hedag's shadow-replay shows Elmmet beating his daughter, whose neck and shoulder bear marks, and then something worse that makes onlookers vomit. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L216-217,L253-255)
- Hedag cuts off Elmmet's hand, grips the stump to stop the bleeding, and punches him, saying that the punch is for a child beaten and the hand for a [Thief]. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L230-236)
- {state} Hedag says "I see" and beheads Elmmet with her axe. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L256-258)
- Hedag says she delivered the justice she had in her axe and swears before the crowd that her Skill revealed Elmmet's sins. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L43,L58)
- Prost grants Hedag leave to act as judge and arbiter in Riverfarm, but never to execute, and only alone. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L105-115)
## In Riverfarm
- Hedag says her axe is a last resort and that she will cut no man or woman down but will not promise to leave those who need her craft unscathed. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L109,L112)
- Hedag helps build houses in Riverfarm with a hammer and no magic. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1003-1006)
- Hedag says she will break the arm of a man who strikes a child, and that her craft is justice, not mercy or changing people. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1118-1121)
- {belief} Alevica claims Hedag's bounty is bigger than her own and that Hedag leaves the dead in the villages she passes. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1170-1173)
- Alevica says Hedag's bounty is only about three hundred gold pieces, because poor folk cannot afford more. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1173)
- Later, Hedag patrols Riverfarm by night with Durene, whom she has taken under her wing, and answers every child's call for help. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L124-125)
- Hedag sweeps spilled honey back into vats with Agratha and four other [Witches], and Laken gives her a pot of honey as thanks. (Book 18, Ch7.40ER L125-142)
## Against Belavierr
- Hedag, with Eloise and Mavika, agrees to take about fifty children and parents out of the fire under fire-resistance charms, saying it is for the parents to decide. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L712-713,L716,L721)
- Hedag holds an infant in her arms with the same terrible light in her eyes as when she swung her axe. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L720)
- Hedag is among the [Witches] who hesitate when Belavierr warns them off after Califor's death, and Eloise alone swears no [Witch] will aid Belavierr. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L1133-1135)
- Hedag says Hedags seldom go after [Witches], and that covens look away from a [Witch]'s sins unless a [Witch] kills another. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L423)
- Hedag says a [Witch] who kills another [Witch] is hunted by the others, and that if she meets Belavierr, she owes her a Hedag's justice. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L423)
## Laken's council
- Hedag and Eloise represent the [Witches] on Laken's council, having accepted when asked. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L19)
- Hedag asks Laken whether he intends to swing at the Circle, saying that if words were deeds there would be no need for the Hedag. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L855-856)
