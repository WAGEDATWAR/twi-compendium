# Nicoletta
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.05D
> See also: Lorenzo, Diana, Daly, Kirana, Siri
## Overview
- Nicoletta is an Italian Earthworlder around nineteen years old, introduced alongside Lorenzo and Diana. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L514-519)
- Nicoletta, Lorenzo and Diana were riding a crowded bus when it passed through a tunnel and they found themselves in a forest. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L554)
- Nicoletta is one of eight people who appeared there, plus a pair of two other confused people. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L554)
## In Liscor
- Nicoletta greets Siri with Salve and asks whether she is from Earth. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L519)
- Nicoletta whispers to the other Italians and translates for them. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L529,L570)
- Nicoletta takes Daly's hand when he reaches out to reassure her. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L539)
- Kirana hugs Nicoletta as the Earthworlders of the United Nations company surround the overwhelmed newcomers. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L625)
- Daly says people from countries like Nicoletta's are not used to war. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L378)
- Nicoletta and Lorenzo brighten at something Daly points out, but Kirana fends them off. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L287)
