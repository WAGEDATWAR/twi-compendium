# Bearclaw
> Type: person | Also called: Miss Bearclaw | First appears: Book 14, Ch6.61L
> See also: Soot, Klbkch, Relc, Mrsha, Nokha, Raskghar
## Overview
- Bearclaw is a large scarred Gnoll woman with dark brown fur mixed with red and patches where fur never regrew. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L587; Book 15, Ch7.07 L1121)
- Bearclaw is called Bearclaw because of the metal claws she fights with, and Mrsha notes it is not a Gnoll name. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1121; Book 15, Ch7.06 L402)
- Bearclaw is a Plains Gnoll who says she came to Liscor for business after leaving the tribes. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L626; Book 15, Ch7.06 L408)
- Bearclaw smells of old blood, like the Wanderers the Stone Spears Tribe warns children about. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L388)
- Bearclaw keeps steel in the pockets of her leather vest. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L419)
- Klbkch estimates Bearclaw's level at anywhere from Level 20 to Level 30 and quite dangerous. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L685)
## Arrival in Liscor
- {event} Bearclaw opens a door to Klbkch in Greas Street, with steel-clawed knuckles on one paw, meeting Mister Soot. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L583-586,L685)
- Klbkch orders her to remove the knuckles, says she is not under arrest, and withdraws when it would be a risky fight for the junior guards. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L586,L627)
- Klbkch reports to Beilmark a possible career criminal known as Bearclaw. (Book 14, Ch6.61L L685)
- {event} Bearclaw meets Mrsha and the other cubs in the street, walking with two Gnolls and a Drake. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L385-388)
- Bearclaw hears there is a white Gnoll in the city and recognizes Mrsha, and a companion says Soot said nothing about her being white. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L399,L409)
- Bearclaw cuffs the Gnoll who calls the white fur a Doomed One's sign, calling it superstition. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L397-411)
- Bearclaw tells Krshia she has heard of her, and Krshia answers politely while backing away. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L404-407)
- Klbkch and Relc tell Bearclaw to move along, and she leaves as Relc calls for her to tell Soot hello. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L421-437)
## The Bounties
- {event} Bearclaw arrives from the south on foot, not through the magic door, to take the bounties on a white Gnoll alive, an [Innkeeper], the magic door and the Heartflame Breastplate. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1119,L1161)
- {event} Bearclaw tortures and breaks two Senior Guards of the Watch in Soot's safe room, one of them a Gnoll woman, and a hooded figure carries them off. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1117-1131)
- Bearclaw tells Soot that she knows how this works and that the Guard will not die. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1126-1129)
- Bearclaw scorns Soot's warnings about the Brother of Serendipitous Meetings and the Elusive Lot claims on the inn, saying her gang does not answer to [Mages]. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1149-1156)
- Bearclaw leads a gang of Liscor's most violent criminals, whom she taught a new level of violence, and kills two Senior Guardsmen in a week. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L507)
- Bearclaw's mission is to abduct Mrsha, kill or kidnap Erin Solstice, and steal the magic door. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L508)
- Bearclaw snarls about who the Gentlemen Callers are and why they guard the inn. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L505)
- {event} Bearclaw decides to take the bounty on Relc for fun, saying she remembers him and the Ant-monster who challenged her. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L538-551)
- Bearclaw keeps Soot in place with a paw on his shoulder so that they fail or succeed together. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L555-556)
## Death and Aftermath
- {event} The Raskghar Nokha has Bearclaw held down while she screams and begs, intending to make her a pack-sister. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L1094-1097)
- Nokha asks that Bearclaw's skin and skull be saved for her. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L1109-1111)
- Zevara says Liscor owes thanks for dispatching Bearclaw's gang, that Soot is dead, and that Bearclaw will be found, and she has posted a thousand-gold bounty on her. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L313)
- {event} 'Bearclaw' grins in the crowd during the day of delegations and slips away. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L1349)
