# Noass
> Type: person | Also called: Noass of Pallass | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.08
> See also: Sir Relz Hauscale, Eltistiman Verdue, Drassi, Wistram, Errif Jealwind
## Overview
- Noass is a Drake of Pallass who becomes a [Commentator] with his companion Sir Relz. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L459)
- Noass and Sir Relz gain a class from their commentary on the attack on Liscor and become minor celebrities. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L201)
- Noass is less expensively dressed than Sir Relz, and the pair are well fed. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L155)
## Broadcasts
- Noass spots Seborn, the Crab-type Drowned Man and [Rogue] of the Halfseekers, while he and Sir Relz comment on the battle at the inn. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L373-374)
- Noass and Sir Relz commentate with Eltistiman Verdue, a [Magician] for hire who provides the [Scrying] spell. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L459-460)
- Noass reports that Senator Errif is arguing with a Wing Commander from Liscor, and doubts the claim that Liscor will be attacked. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L461-463)
- Noass weighs what is best for Pallass if Tyrion Veltras's army with two hundred thousand soldiers drives the Goblin Lord toward Liscor. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L465)
- Noass and Sir Relz broadcast live from Pallass during the Wyvern attack and take cover from the Wyverns. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L157-160)
- Noass says a massive explosion and a chill came before the Wyverns attacked, and that Grand Strategist Chaldion shouted the alarm. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L166)
- Noass and Sir Relz commentate on the advance of Flos's army, which has been slower than projected. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L4-5)
- Viewers send Noass angry [Message] spells when the scrying of the Couriers' run is cut off. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L785)
- Noass pays Drassi a gold piece for transport through the inn door to Invrisil and goes live as soon as he arrives. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L528-533)
- Noass ignores Numbtongue's warning that the riots in Invrisil are dangerous, and says he knows what he is doing. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L540-546)
- Noass opens the broadcast from Invrisil by announcing a riot over The Golden Triangle fraud. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L551)
- Numbtongue calls Noass and Sir Relz idiots through the door, and his voice is broadcast worldwide for a moment. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L552-553)
