# Tulm
> Type: person | Also called: Tulm the Mithril; second-in-command of the Iron Vanguard | First appears: Ch6.22D
> See also: Niers, Perorn, Xol, Wil, Umina, Foliana
## Overview
- Tulm the Mithril is a Dullahan, second-in-command of the Iron Vanguard, a Great Company, and Niers' former student. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L260,L286)
- Tulm's entire body is mithril, brighter and deeper than silver, and he carries his head under one arm. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L280,L292)
- Tulm commands the whole Great Company as a [Strategist] answerable only to the Seer of Steel. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L288)
- Tulm won Niers' game in years past, and he agreed to seek this time. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L260)
- Tulm does not hide his presence, and Dullahans and others kneel as he appears. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L290-291)
- Tulm's aura presses on the teachers and Perorn until Niers tells his [Strategists] not to kneel, lifting the weight. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L295-306)
- Tulm's class is [Dragonslayer Strategist], known only to Tulm, the Seer of Steel and Niers. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L639-640)
- Tulm's appraisal Skill let him read the rough levels and Skills of all the students and several Forgotten Wing officers. (Book 11, Ch6.24D L558)
## The game at Daquin
- Tulm says Niers called him by a letter sent into the Iron Vanguard's mightiest stronghold, carried over two thousand miles by one of the best Couriers in the world. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L351)
- Perorn says Tulm took her leg in a past battle, and she hates him. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L321,L339)
- {event} Tulm orders his chief [Mage] to activate the Screamer Dust. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L924-927)
- Tulm uses [Battlefield - The Cold Iron Mists] and [Legions of Steel], whose mist hampers enemies and makes his soldiers' skin steel. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L990-994)
- {state} Tulm dons his helmet for the first time and uses [The Twice-Born Warlord], matching Xol's strength. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1279,L1296-1303)
- Tulm catches Venaz's fist, and Venaz lands behind Marian with torn armour. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1297-1298)
- Tulm leaps eight feet into the air to catch Cameral's head, and Niers rules "Out!" (Book 11, Ch6.23D L843-849)
- Tulm goes white with fury when Umina surfaces from her tunnel through the privy. (Book 11, Ch6.23D L1326-1327)
