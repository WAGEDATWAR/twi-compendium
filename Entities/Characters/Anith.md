# Anith
> Type: person | Also called: Jackal Beastkin of Vuliel Drae | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.08T
> See also: Insill, Dasha, Pekona, Toren, Earlia
## Overview
- Anith is a Jackal Beastkin from the Duskclaw Tribe, with sleek black fur and an elongated, dog-like head. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L244,L271)
- Anith is a [Mage] and the leader of his adventuring party, carrying a faintly glowing book. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L245,L267)
- Anith leads the eclectic Silver-rank team Vuliel Drae, which includes Insill, Dasha, Pekona and a Gnoll [Archer], Larr. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L146,L193-195)
## Events
- {event} Anith introduces himself politely to the masked Toren in the dungeon as the [Mage] and party leader. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L267)
- Anith casts [Repulsion Barrier], which sends a wave of leeches flying away from him and Larr. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L320-321)
- Anith has a [Dangersense] Skill that went off when the stranger Toren thought his party were foes. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L459)
- {belief} Anith believes the masked adventurer is clearly of a higher level than his party. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L459)
- Anith's party is hired for the first time to guard another adventuring team, Earlia's Gemhammer, in Liscor's dungeon. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L146,L198)
- Anith agrees to guard Gemhammer out of curiosity and a sense of obligation, and says they are being paid. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L197-198)
- Anith assigns Insill and Dasha to watch the entrance, with Larr and Pekona on guard at the other. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L193-195)
