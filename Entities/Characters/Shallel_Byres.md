# Shallel Byres
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Shallel; Mother (to Yvlon) | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.37
> See also: Yvlon, Yitton Byres, Ylawes Byres, Pisces, Ceria Springwalker
## Overview
- Shallel Byres is Yvlon's mother, and Yvlon refers to her as Mother. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L1072-1074; Book 19, Ch7.45 L399)
- Shallel is a former [Scribe]. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L1073)
- Shallel comes from Terandria, where Yvlon says her mother is from. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L399)
- Shallel grew up in the big cities of Terandria and knew a few half-Elves, as much as any Terandrian Human. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L401)
- Shallel says she was the daughter of a very minor noble house and married into money. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L404)
- Shallel says Terandria has a group of young [Ladies] whose purpose is to earn a large dowry or marry upwards. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L404)
- Shallel is married to Yitton Byres, who coughs as she tells how she reached Izril. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L403)
## Yvlon's arms (Book 18)
- {reveal} Shallel pinches the tip of Yvlon's glove and pulls it off, exposing Yvlon's silver-flesh hand in front of a gasping crowd. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L1073-1075)
- Shallel asks Yvlon in a pointed tone to explain, and Yitton looks at his daughter with wide eyes. (Book 18, Ch7.37 L1074-1076)
