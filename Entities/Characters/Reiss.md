# Reiss
> Type: person | Also called: the Goblin Lord; the Goblin with magic; the Necromancer's apprentice | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.49
> See also: Az'kerash, Garen Redfang, Rags, Zel Shivertail, Snapjaw, Eater of Spears, Tremborag, Pyrite, Osthia, Reiss' army, Reiss's lieutenants (Entities/Organisations/Reiss_army.md)
## Overview
- Reiss is a Goblin Lord who is also a [Necromancer], and he has black eyes with white pupils instead of the crimson of other Goblins. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L4; Book 9, Ch5.48G L378,L641)
- Reiss is thinner than Garen Redfang, almost able to pass for a Human apart from his green skin and pointed ears. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L371)
- Reiss speaks the common tongue like a native speaker, with a reverberation of power in his voice. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L381)
- Reiss rides a dead Shield Spider reanimated by magic, torn open and headless. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L105; Book 9, Ch5.48G L369-370)
- Reiss is the apprentice of Az'kerash the Necromancer and calls himself the one student of Az'kerash. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L146)
## Zel Shivertail and the naming
- {event} After the Goblin Lord's duel with Zel Shivertail, Az'kerash orders his apprentice to finish the Drake and secure the corpse, then teleports away. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L418-419)
- Reiss kneels by the dying Zel and apologises, calling the death cowardly and unworthy. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L422-425)
- Zel asks the Goblin Lord's name, and the Goblin Lord answers that he has none, only Goblin Lord and Apprentice. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L444-445)
- Zel suggests the Drake name Reiss, which his old friend Sserys always wanted to give a son. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L449)
- {state} The Goblin Lord takes the name Reiss and swears to avenge Zel and his people by killing the Necromancer. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L450-452)
- Reiss orders his Goblins not to touch Zel's body on pain of death, then orders a withdrawal. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L467-472)
- {belief} The Humans believe the Goblin Lord killed Zel Shivertail, though Reiss knows that is untrue and cannot say so. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L461-464)
- Reiss keeps the name given by a dying Drake secret from his master. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L583)
## After Invrisil
- {belief} Rumour spreads that the Goblin Lord slew Zel, defeated an army backed by Magnolia Reinhart and destroyed two War Golems alone. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L4)
- Reiss's injuries from the duel with Zel were healed within minutes of his victory. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L3)
- Reiss rides a smaller Shield Spider after Zel killed his former mount. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L22)

- {belief} It was said later that the Goblins spared Invrisil because Zel's army had damaged them, a credit claimed by both Drakes and Humans. (Book 7, Ch5.02 L27)
