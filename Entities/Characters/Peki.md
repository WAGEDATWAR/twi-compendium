# Peki
> Type: person | Also called: Peki of Pomle | First appears: Ch6.22D
> See also: Merrik, Wil, Orjin, Xol, Venaz, Feshi
## Overview
- Peki is a Garuda [Martial Artist] with bright purple-and-green feathers, and a Lieutenant and student at Niers Astoragon's academy. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L838-844; Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L183)
- Peki tells Xol that Pomle's Strongest taught her to fight. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L842-844)
- Orjin recalls Peki, a Garuda he used to train, who challenged him each time and promised to return when she met the Titan. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L184-185)
- Peki cannot swim, because she is a Garuda and flies. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L189-193)
- Peki says she came to see the Walled Cities before going home to Pomle, and to find treasure. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L276)
## Skills
- Peki uses [Aerial Dance: Waterfall Strikes] to strike down Xol's armour from the air. (Book 11, Ch6.22D L838-839)
- Peki has trained to fight in the air and dodges pirates' spells and arrows. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L665)
- Peki uses a [Galewinds] scroll to blast a pirate ship's sail around. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L667-668)
