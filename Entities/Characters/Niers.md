# Niers Astoragon
> Type: person | Also called: Niers; the Titan; Titan of Baleros; the Professor; Lord Astoragon | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.17
> See also: Foliana, Forgotten Wing Company, Erin Solstice, Olesm Swifttail, Magnolia Reinhart, Tulm the Mithril, Flos Reimarch, Wil Kallinad, Fraerlings
## Overview
- Niers Astoragon is second in command of one of the Four Great Companies of Baleros. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L359)
- Niers is a Fraerling, one of the Small Folk, and is only six inches tall. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L157,L159)
- Niers is called the Titan, an ironic nickname that only some people understand. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L141,L157)
- Narration calls Niers the highest-level [Strategist] on the continent, perhaps in the world, and says he keeps his exact level hidden. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L157)
- Niers led his army as a [Strategist] without a single level in [General] or any other class, and is infamous for it. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L141)
- Niers is the most famous Fraerling in the world and a hero to the children of his kind. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L160)
- Niers led armies to victory using his mind rather than a sword, outmaneuvering foes up to three times his army's size. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L156)
- Niers has fought as a mercenary in Baleros for over fifty years. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L184)
- Niers is loyal to his company and to his commander, whom he calls a long-time friend, almost to a fault. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L174,L300)

## Appearance and habits
- Niers looks like a middle-aged Human apart from his height, with dark black, slightly grey hair and a powerful build. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L158)
- Niers wears armor as expensive as any [Lord]'s, since [Blacksmiths] find forging anything so small difficult. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L158)
- Niers sleeps in his command tent on a small cot set on top of a table, to avoid being stepped on or attacked by wildlife or bugs. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L216)
- Niers considers a dagger as large as he is still a dagger and is strong enough to lift it, though it is awkward. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L363)
- Niers does not interfere in battles his lieutenants are winning, because interfering would impede their growth. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L360)
