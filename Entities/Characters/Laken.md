# Laken
> Type: person | Also called: Laken Godart; Emperor Laken; Emperor Laken Godart; Emperor of the Unseen; the Unseen Emperor; Emperor of Riverfarm; L | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.00E
> See also: Durene, Ryoka Griffin, Prost, Gamel, Frostwing, Riverfarm, Ivolethe
## Overview
- Laken Godart is a blind young man from Earth who is transported to the Wandering Inn world holding his bacon quiche and walking stick. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L3,L10)
- Laken is blind from birth and sees nothing at all, neither blackness nor a difference between light and dark. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L11)
- Laken becomes an [Emperor] by declaring himself one, and his class lets him sense all the land he claims. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L436,L445-446; Book 5, Ch4.20E L118)
## Background on Earth
- Laken's best friend on Earth is Zoe, and he was with her in San Francisco just before he was transported. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L13,L35,L81)
- Laken is the son of two fairly affluent parents, one a lawyer and the other a businessman. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L177)
- Laken says he once wanted to be a professional chef and then a food critic, but gave that up because a blind Masterchef already existed. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L229)
- Laken says he studied many professions and at one point wanted to be a billiards player. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L234)
- Laken is about 184 cm (6'1") tall, shorter than Durene by at least a head. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L247)
- Laken has a fiberglass walking stick that astonishes Durene because the material is unknown in her world. (Book 3, Ch3.00E L129)
- Laken has an iPhone in his pocket that his best friend Zoe loaded with a flashlight app as a joke. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L364,L368)
- Laken is twenty-four, not American; his mother is German, she married his father in France, and he grew up all over Europe. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L220,L223)
- Laken says Ryoka is twenty-one and Erin is twenty, and he asks whether Ryoka is around his age. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L219-221)
- Laken speaks German and some French, and answers Ryoka's German in German. (Book 4, Ch3.35 L343; Book 4, Ch3.36 L131-139)
