# Blighted King
> Type: person | Also called: Othius IV; Othius the Fourth; the Demon King (named so once in a history text, see Overview); King Othius | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.07R
> See also: Nereshal, Rhir, Demons, Tom, The Fool, Antinium
## Overview
- The Blighted King wages an eternal war against monster tribes and demons on a continent far to the east across the seas. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L305)
- His continent has known war for two hundred years, and he fights a daily war that he is losing over the course of years. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L305)
- Other nations send their own armies to support the Blighted King. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L305)
- The Blighted King's name is Othius the Fourth. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L16)
- The Blighted King rules the kingdom of Rhir, and its [King] is known throughout the world. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L53-54)
- {state} In Book 18 the Blighted King is named King Othius IV and is shown as an extremely old Human who prolongs his life by magic and other methods. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L73)
- The Blighted King's hair and eyes are pale grey-silver, and he looks faded. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L102)
## The Fifth Wall and the summoned Heroes
- {event} The Blighted King greeted the summoned Earthers and told them they would be the heroes who drove the demons out of his kingdom. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L16)
- {event} The Blighted King gave the Earthers weapons and sent them out into battle, where they were slaughtered. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L16)
- The Blighted King wants to build a Fifth Wall to push back the monsters and Demons, and for that he summoned the Earthers. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L97)
- With powerful [Heroes] the Kingdom would have enough power to build another Wall and nearly double its available land. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L98)
- The King's magicians summoned a group of children instead of legendary heroes, but all of them held the unknown class [Hero], which the King still pinned hopes on. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L99)
- {event} After the Earthers proved unable to fight, the King gave up on them and effectively exiled them from the capital city. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L17)
## The Antinium Wars
- {event} In 5 B.F., news from Rhir said that the Blighted King's armies were shattered in defeat after defeat and every Wall but the one protecting his capital was stormed. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L14)
- The same history text calls the Blighted King the Demon King and says he was also pushed to the very brink of his territory. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L14)
- {event} After the Antinium vanished from the continent, news came that the Blighted King was rebuilding and his armies were already retaking his lost kingdom. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L16)
## The court of Rhir (Book 6)
- The palace of Rhir rises above the capital and is described as taller than any other building of its kind in the world. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L53)
- The palace was designed by Dwarfs, added to year after year, and made never to be taken. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L53)
- At the feast, the Blighted King and his Queen sit at one end of the room at a table with an ornate golden chair. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L321)
- The Blighted King's voice is an old man's voice, tired and not grand. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L13)
- Tom suspects Nereshal has been keeping the King young, and sees few grey hairs in his beard. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L13)
- The Blighted Queen is younger than the King and, though not yet thirty by Tom's estimate, her towering height makes her seem older. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L18)
- {event} The King greeted the Earthers as the heroes of prophecy returned "diminished". (Book 6, Ch1.03C L12)
- {event} The King said that over sixty Earthers were sent to fight the Demons and now less than a third remain. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L19)
- {event} The King noted that one Earther has reached Level 30 in three months as a [Knight] sworn against darkness, yet no [Lord] or [Lady] has given him a title. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L25)
- {event} The King noted that a water [Mage] among the Earthers has taught herself. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L25)
- {event} The King offered the Earthers a place in his halls, food and his protection so long as they hold back the scourge of darkness. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L27)
- {event} The King said he had heard of a warrior who slew a Demon raiding party single-handed, and turned his eyes to Tom. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L27-28)
- The King's throne room has black and white marble floors and stone beams overhead, on which guards with bows, arrows and wands stand. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L23)
- The Blighted King's throne is made of twisted ivory, the bones of some ancient monsters carved into a huge seat and enchanted. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L25)
- {event} The King asked Tom whether [Clowns] are truly entertainers rather than warriors, and whether they fight. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L48)
- {event} Tom answered that [Clowns] have tricks suited for combat but are not warriors. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L53)
- The King said the court searches for Skills and classes that might aid its people, and thanked Tom the [Clown]. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L55)
- {event} The King invited Tom to join him in the gardens and said a suitable reward would be deliberated that night. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L57)
- {event} The King ordered Nereshal to summon the commander of the palace guard, saying failure must be met with appropriate punishment. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L57)
- {event} The King's young daughter, a Princess, said her father sent the soldiers away, all of them, and she did not like it. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L370)
- {reveal} The Fool told Tom that the King decided two days earlier to deploy every soldier to the Fourth Wall for an upcoming battle, except a few foreign regiments and the King's own guard. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L376)
- The Fool said the decision was a secret known only to the King, his advisors and the Fool himself. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L379)
## The attack on the palace
- During the attack on the palace, the Blighted King's orders fill defenders of many species with strength so that their flesh is like iron. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L215)
- {event} The Fool held a knife at the King's throat in the courtyard while a hundred bows and [Mages] were trained on him. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L434)
- The King stood still and calm and asked the Fool to explain why he betrayed his pledge of loyalty. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L435-436)
- {belief} The King answered the Fool's accusation about the ritual by saying he does it for his people so that they may live. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L439-440)
- {event} Two of the Fool's thrown knives struck the Blighted King in the chest and the shoulder. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L461)
- {state} After the Fool's defeat, the Blighted King lay on the ground in the courtyard, breathing heavily but alive. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L480)
## The second ritual
- {event} In Book 9 the King, listening to his steward, advisor and personal mage Nereshal, hears that several [Mages] protest the scope of a second ritual. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L710-711)
- The King says Rhir's population can sustain ten times the cost of the ritual if needed. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L715)
- {event} The King decides the ritual must be done, citing the recorded Antinium evolving in strength and Demon spies and traitors in his kingdom. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L720-722)
- {belief} The King says the summoned chosen ones erred as weak, timid children instead of warriors, but that they grow with guidance and must be raised with care. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L725)
- {event} The King tells Nereshal that those protesting the ritual will obey or be dealt with. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L727)
- The King dreams that the Earther children's weapons that rain death from the sky will end the war with the Demons and purify Rhir. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L729-730)
- The King sighs at the smell of rot and death on the air, which is the smell of home, and dreams of a day of only spring. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L731)
## Later view of the King (Book 18)
- {event} The first ritual sacrificed ten thousand of Rhir's children, in the King's words, for a hundred heroes. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L84)
- {reveal} Nereshal later concludes the ten thousand lives produced about a thousand children from Earth who were thrown across the world instead of arriving at their destination. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L93)
- The King's earlier heirs, including his [Queens] and offspring, all died in battle, treachery or accident. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L74)
- The Blighted King has a new [Queen] and two daughters, kept as insurance though their levels are too low. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L75)
- Nereshal the [Chronomancer] is the Blighted King's most trusted confidant. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L85)
- The King wears the deaths of his family on his face and body, which they have aged to grey. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L77)
- The King has a hatred of the Demons that has allowed him to live so long and fight the endless war. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L78)
- The King spat the name of the Fool like a curse, calling him the traitor who nearly ruined the kingdom. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L92)
- {event} The King refuses a test of the spell and decides the second ritual will use a hundred thousand lives. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L163)
- The King dreams of a day when he can walk past the walls and see no Demons and no Blight. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L153)
- The King's [Mages] say the ritual cannot be altered and that it must use the unborn, after criminals, Demons, animals and slaves failed. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L159)
