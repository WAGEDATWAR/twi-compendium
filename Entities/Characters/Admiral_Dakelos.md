# Admiral Dakelos
> Type: person | Also called: Dakelos | First appears: Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions
> See also: Yerzhen, Belchaus Meron
## Overview
- Admiral Dakelos is the [Admiral] of the warship The Foehammer, part of the Ailendamus fleet blockading trade to the Dawn Concordat. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L18,L38,L42)
- Dakelos has a poor way with drink. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L53)
## The Itorin's Pride deception
- Dakelos had received several coded [Messages] from Ailendamus telling him to watch for a message or persons on the day the Itorin's Pride arrived. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L39,L42)
- A man in a [General]'s uniform named Yerzhen boards Dakelos' ship, claiming they met when Dakelos was barely a [Captain]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L46-52)
- Dakelos does not remember Yerzhen and is embarrassed. A [Fleet Mage] casts a truth spell on both men, and Yerzhen's statements all prove true. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L53-59)
- Yerzhen tells Dakelos the fleet must land in Nadel by nightfall, and Dakelos protests that Nadel is the territory of Lord Belchaus. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L68-70)
- Yerzhen claims Lord Belchaus is secretly allowing the landing as part of a plan to flank the Dawn Concordat when war begins in six days. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L81)
- Dakelos thinks the Itorin's Pride was supposed to be much further north, and is uneasy at how quickly it moves. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L90)
- Yerzhen promises to call for Dakelos to become [High Admiral] if he is made a [Great General] of Ailendamus. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L93)
- Dakelos takes a drink with Yerzhen in his cabin and leaves an hour later somewhat intoxicated, and the two give the crew a speech. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L99-103)
- Dakelos reaches Nadel's waters after nine hours, drunk on courage and dreams of promotion. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L105-107)
- Dakelos shouts a demand for passage through a voice-amplifying spell, using notes Yerzhen gave him, and Lord Belchaus Meron answers that Nadel bows to no nation. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L119-123)
- Dakelos orders his [Mages] to throw [Fireballs] at Lord Bel's flagship, aiming to miss. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L127)
- Lord Belchaus boards The Wrath of Sel, a twin of Dakelos' own warship, and kills its [Captain] and [First Mate]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L130-132)
- {reveal} Dakelos sees that Yerzhen has vanished and the Itorin's Pride has been replaced by a tiny trading boat, and realises he may have been tricked. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L135-139)
- {reveal} The Admiral abducted by the illusion Yerzhen was itself an illusion cast by the Elusive Lot, a group of the Ullsinoi Faction of Wistram. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L141-149,L171)
- The fake Yerzhen says he wrote the speech with some help from the Admiral himself. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L159-161)
- The disguised mages, watching from their hidden ship, say Dakelos is already surrendering and practically screaming that he was duped. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L153)
