# Krshia
> Type: person | Also called: Krshia Silverfang; Krisha | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.20
> See also: Brunkr, Tkrn, Elirr, Lism, Mrsha, Erin Solstice, Lyonette du Marquin, Ryoka Griffin, Silverfang Tribe, Liscor
## Overview
- Krshia is a female Gnoll [Shopkeeper] who sells goods on Market Street in Liscor and introduces herself to Erin Solstice as a shopkeeper and seller of goods. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L93,L120)
- Krshia leads the Silverfang Tribe in Liscor and is one of the leaders of the city's Gnolls; Tkrn, a junior [Guardsman], is under her unofficial command. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L125)
- {state} Krshia becomes a [Royal Shopkeeper], a class she thinks she may be the first Gnoll in history to have, thanks to Lyonette. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L30)
- {state} Krshia later wins a seat on Liscor's Council, a huge change that she had not imagined a year earlier. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L358)
