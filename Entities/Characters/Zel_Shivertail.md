# Zel Shivertail
> Type: person | Also called: Tidebreaker; General of the Line; Lineholder; Shivertail; Uncle Zel | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.24
> See also: Ilvriss, General Sserys, Magnolia Reinhart, Klbkch, Xrn, Az'kerash, Goblin Lord, Selys Shivertail, Tekshia Shivertail, Reynold, Venitra
## Overview
- Zel Shivertail is a Drake known as the legendary General of the Line and the famous Tidebreaker, though few call him by his first name. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L266)
- Zel wishes at times that people believed in him less, because one day he will lose a battle and his life. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L267)
- Zel is widely regarded as the greatest defensive general on the continent. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L415)
- Zel is a hero of both Antinium Wars, and Drake children all know his name. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L6)
- The Drakes of Liscor remember Zel just as often because General Sserys, whom they do not forget, came from their city. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L6)
- Zel tells young Selys that fame is a one-way street, and once you go down it you cannot go back. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L21)
- Zel tells Selys he does not like being famous and finds having everyone's eyes on him a heavy responsibility. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L29)
- Zel tells Selys he takes command because there is no one else, because it is his duty, and because it must be done. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L35)
