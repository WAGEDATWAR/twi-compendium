# Raelt
> Type: person | Also called: Raelt Leysars; King Raelt of Jecrass; King of Duels; Duelist King; King of Challenges | First appears: Ch6.15K
> See also: Jecaina, Geril, Lyfelt, Flos, Orthenon, Mars, Perric, Yisame, Zamea
## Overview
- Raelt Leysars is the King of the Realm of Jecrass, a small country north of Hellios and Germina, and is regarded as a decent but unremarkable [King]. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L2-6)
- Raelt is unmarried, with no title beyond his own name, and raises Jecaina as his daughter. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L5,L136-137)
- Raelt dislikes his court, oranges, horses, bells, his River Wardens and war, though Jecrass breeds horses. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L99,L106; Book 16, Ch7.13K L467)
- Raelt eats a half-rotten orange slice that Geril peeled, because his daughter loves oranges. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L105-107)
- Raelt is exactly forty and not young any more, and he exercises in private. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L217-218,L492)
- Raelt's father, King Meriden Leysars, was a good, popular [King] who pledged Jecrass as a vassal to Flos and died of heart failure a few years after Flos' slumber. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L219-222)
- Raelt's mother passed within a year of Meriden. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L524)
- Raelt was a young [Prince] enjoying peace in Terandria when his father died, and he took the throne with a new class. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L222-223)
- Raelt's half-sister abandoned her royal class for a love-marriage and left her baby, Jecaina, in the palace. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L223)
- Raelt fought with his father under Flos' banners twice, according to Flos. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L491)
## Abilities
- Raelt is a Level 26 [King], average and low-level, with higher levels in another class. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L655-656)
- Raelt carries an enchanted cup rapier with cutting enchantments and a parrying dagger enchanted with a five-pound [Weight] spell. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L489-492)
- Raelt fights as a traditional [Fencer] and taught his daughter to fence. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L492-493)
- Raelt wears light, enchanted, flexible leather armour in preference to royal mail. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L437,L488)
- {state} Raelt's [Duelist] class reaches Level 35 and [Quick Step] changes to [Flash Step] while he is knocked senseless, and he gains [King Level 28]. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1031-1033)
- {state} Raelt gains [Skill - Aura of the Lion] after a thirty-foot fall, at [King Level 28]. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1051-1053)
- {state} Raelt reaches [King Level 30], becomes a [King of Challenges] with [Duelist] removed, and gains [Skill - Army: Flicker Charge] and [Skill - Royal Arms]. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1082-1086)
- Raelt's army glows with [Aura of the Lion] when he leads it. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L367-368)
- {state} The world calls Raelt the King of Duels or the Duelist King after his duel with Flos. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1129-1131)
