# Gershal
> Type: person | Also called: Lieutenant Gershal; Gershal of Vaunt | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.47
> See also: Salvia, Zel Shivertail, Magnolia Reinhart, Tyrion Veltras
## Overview
- Gershal is a [Lieutenant] and low-level officer from Vaunt, a small city north of Invrisil. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L2)
- Gershal commands disciplined but fairly low-level [Archers] and [Swordsmen] and has never fought in a war. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L2)
- Gershal and his men fight monsters, patrol and keep the peace, and had never been levied for the annual battles against Drakes. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L3)
## Before the battle at Invrisil
- Vaunt's [Mayor] panicked when both Magnolia Reinhart and Tyrion Veltras sent levy requests, since the city could not satisfy both. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L4)
- Vaunt sent four hundred [Soldiers] to Tyrion Veltras, a battle-ready [Lord], and two hundred under Gershal to Lady Magnolia. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L5)
- Gershal thinks of himself as the sacrificial lamb sent to Magnolia. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L6)
- Gershal knows Salvia because her city, Nonelmar, lies close to Vaunt. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L24)
- Vaunt is known for producing some of the best cheeses on the continent, including the spicy, fragrant Peppermort. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L24)
- Gershal shares his Peppermort cheese with Salvia over cheap wine. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L23-24)
- Gershal wraps cheese in wax paper and tucks it into his armor, following a Vaunt superstition that a cheese once stopped an arrow. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L73)
- Young men and women leaving Vaunt carry cheese into battle, where it also serves as a field ration. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L73)
- {belief} Gershal argues that a local [Commander] is no match for a Goblin Lord and that an offensive would be suicide. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L35)
- {belief} Gershal says Tyrion Veltras is gathering an army twice as large and should deal with the Goblin Lord instead of Magnolia. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L35)
- Gershal fears that fighting without a true leader or twice as many soldiers would only level up the Goblins and create more undead. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L53)
- {belief} Gershal says Tyrion Veltras called for six thousand horse from the Terland family, at least a quarter of them [Knights]. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L50)
- Gershal dismisses the Knights of the Petal as arrogant hotheads until Salvia warns him of their temper. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L43-45)
- Gershal reassesses the two Rose Knights once he hears their armor is Dwarf-made, though they do not ease his concerns. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L49)
- Gershal regards Magnolia Reinhart as cunning and manipulative from rumour, though never a fool, and hopes today will not prove the rumours wrong. (Book 6, Ch4.47 L75-76)
## The battle
- Gershal is assigned with his unit to the front ranks of Magnolia's army. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L112)
- Gershal sees his line begin to step back before the Hobgoblins' charge and orders his men to hold. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L116-117)
- "Cheese-rotted heads" is a petty childhood insult from Vaunt, and it snaps Gershal's soldiers out of their fear. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L117-118)
- Gershal steadies himself by looking at Zel Shivertail, who stands unmoving before the charge. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L119-120)
- Zel Shivertail calls [Stonewall Formation] and [Chargebreaker Guard], and Gershal feels his shield warm and his footing firm. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L121-122)
- Gershal's iron and wood shield turns aside a gigantic Hob's massive hammer, and he holds his ground against repeated blows. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L123-127)
- Gershal wounds the Hob in the thigh with his sword and thrusts the hammer away with one arm. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L126-127)
