# Raekea
> Type: person | Also called: Raekea Silversmith; Raeky | First appears: Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia
> See also: Krshia, Mrsha, Maughin, Jekss, Lism, Pelt
## Overview
- Raekea is a Gnoll [Armorer] in Liscor, married to the best Drake [Blacksmith] in the city, and her arms are patterned with burns. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L518)
- Raekea is a Plains Gnoll of the Silverfang Tribe, and her husband is the Drake Jekss. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L152,L168-169,L194)
- Raekea is called the best [Armorer] in Liscor. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L735)
## Events
- {event} Maughin is attracted to Raekea at the forges until Erin whispers that Raekea is married. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L906-909)
- {event} Raekea takes Mrsha to the city, saying she will be an honored guest of the Silverfang Tribe at the Meeting of the Tribes. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L189,L194)
- {belief} Raekea says Gnolls thought magic was a curse and hopes Mrsha and another Gnoll [Mage] may weaken that belief. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L198)
- Raekea says Krshia might ask the Chieftain, her sister, to name Mrsha a Silverfang. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L200)
- {event} The Council votes 6-0 to authorise Raekea to offer the Dwarf smith Pelt a forge in Liscor. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L373-381)
- Raekea says Liscor gets most of its ore cheaply from Esthelm, and that Pelt is interested. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L373)
- Raekea posts a 272 lb lift on Erin's gym list, claiming one of the higher numbers. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L385-388)
