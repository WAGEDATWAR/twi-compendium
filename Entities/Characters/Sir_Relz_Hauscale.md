# Sir Relz Hauscale
> Type: person | Also called: Sir Relz; Relz | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.08
> See also: Noass, Eltistiman Verdue, Drassi, Wistram
## Overview
- Sir Relz is a Drake of Pallass and, with Noass, one of the world's first [Commentators]. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L459)
- Sir Relz wears a monocle on a chain looped around a neck-spine and dresses expensively. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L155)
- Sir Relz gained a class with Noass from their commentary on the attack on Liscor. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L201)
## Broadcasts
- Sir Relz identifies Jelaqua Ivirith as a Gold-rank adventurer who leads the Halfseekers while he and Noass watch the battle at the inn. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L366)
- The two Drakes say the Halfseekers were once a group of eight on the verge of Named status and are now three Gold-rank adventurers. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L371)
- The two Drakes identify Moore as an earth-magic specialist using a mass variant of [Armor of Thorns]. (Book 7, Ch5.08 L371-372)
- Sir Relz joins Noass in broadcasting through Eltistiman Verdue's scrying orb about the warnings of an attack on Liscor. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L459-464)
- Sir Relz comments that the Liscorian Wing Commander stormed up to Senator Errif in a huff. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L462)
- Sir Relz broadcasts live from Pallass during the Wyvern attack, with a portable scrying mirror and [Spectator's Concealment]. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L157-164)
- Sir Relz is inept at angling the scrying mirror and shows the audience a close-up of his own face. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L161)
- Sir Relz says the pair's class gives them [Spectator's Concealment], which works so long as they do not interfere. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L164)
- Sir Relz tells Noass that Flos's army is systematically destroying each group it meets rather than racing. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L5)
- Sir Relz adjusts his monocle as the pair arrange transport through the inn door to Invrisil. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L526,L532)
