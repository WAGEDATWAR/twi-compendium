# Princess
> Type: person | Also called: [Princess]; the filthy girl | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.40
> See also: Ksmvr, Calruz
## Overview
- In Book 1 a filthy blonde girl with pale skin, hiding behind a shopkeeper's stall, rushes into the street when her [Invisibility] spell fails, and fires a web-shooting emerald ring that pins Gerial and Sostrom and cocoons Ceria. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L277-281)
- Gazi severs the web with her sword, and the girl has already fled down an alleyway. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L282-284)
- In Book 2 a starving [Princess] steals from market stalls in Liscor's winter chill, using a few remaining charges of [Invisibility] and spells to open locks. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L482-484)
- The [Princess] believes richness is her birthright and that she deserves everything she takes. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L484)
- A Gnoll, Krshia, catches her, noting that invisibility is not enough against the Gnolls' sense of smell. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L487-491)
- Her invisibility breaks, and in fear she fires a ring and screams "Ignis!", whose fireball destroys a stall and engulfs the Gnoll. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L493-500)
- Gnolls seize the [Princess] from behind, capturing her hand and ring. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L502-503)
