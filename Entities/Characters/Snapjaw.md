# Snapjaw
> Type: person | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.49
> See also: Reiss, Eater of Spears, Badarrow, Poisonbite, Rags, Calescent
## Overview
- Snapjaw is a female Hobgoblin with an oversized head and teeth fused with steel or something harder. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L368; Book 16, Ch7.12G L146)
- Snapjaw is shorter than a regular Hob, and her classes are [Eater] and [Rider]. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L146)
- Snapjaw bites through things better than Eater Goats and often does so in combat. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L146)
- Snapjaw was Reiss's lieutenant, so she is fluent in the common tongue and prefers it. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L143)
## With Reiss
- Snapjaw runs at Zel Shivertail with Eater of Spears, but a Human woman's whispered voice holds her in place for three seconds. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L103-106)
- Snapjaw rides a horse beside Reiss at his first meeting with Rags's tribe. (Book 9, Ch5.48G L368)
- Snapjaw speaks for the first time at the council fire to say that Reiss can make a nation and keep Goblins safe. (Book 9, Ch5.50G L327-328)
- Poisonbite kills Snapjaw's horse with poisoned daggers and the two female Goblins face off. (Book 9, Ch5.56G L68-69)
- Snapjaw leads at least fifteen thousand mounted Goblins against the Redfangs. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L157)
- Snapjaw is one of the last two lieutenants Reiss has left, since Zel slew his best officers. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L156)
- Snapjaw snaps Spiderslicer's sword with her metal teeth and bites a Redfang Hob in half. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L160-162)
- Snapjaw screams when Tyrion Veltras's lance strikes Reiss. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1010)
## Later
- Snapjaw sits with Badarrow in his tower whenever she is not working, leaning on him to calm his nerves. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L144-145)
- Snapjaw and Badarrow are lovers. (Book 16, Ch7.12G L176)
- Snapjaw and Badarrow met because both wept for Eater of Spears and other fallen friends. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L828)
- Badarrow is taciturn while Snapjaw is outgoing, yet they trust each other. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L828-829)
- Calescent walks in on Snapjaw and Badarrow mid-act and says he will return with a less spicy dish. (Book 16, ChMating_Rituals_Pt.2 L835-838)
