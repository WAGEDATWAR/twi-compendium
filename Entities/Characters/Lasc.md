# Lasc
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Lasc | First appears: Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships
> See also: Inky, Wil Kallinad
## Overview
- Lasc is the [Storm Captain] and captain of the ship The Emerald Signet. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L165,L603)
- Lasc fights with a shortsword in his right hand and a metal gauntlet on his left. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L603-604)
## Book 16: the Drowned Folk
- {belief} Lasc says he would rather meet a storm than a monster, and a monster rather than a [Pirate] ship. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L246)
- {belief} Lasc calls the Drowned Folk saltsucking bastards and says he will be grateful if they do not see a Drowned Ship on the errand. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L246)
## Book 17: the voyage
- {event} Lasc parries Venaz's thrusts with his shortsword and strikes him across the face twice with his left hand so fast that the Minotaur cannot pull his head back. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L604,L610)
- Lasc strikes Venaz twenty times before landing from a leap, with a Skill-fast left hand. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L612-613)
- {event} Lasc lays Venaz out unconscious with his fist in a duel on the deck, and the sailors and students roar in amazement. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L614-616)
- Lasc tells Merrik and Peki that at sea he would dump them in the water, since there is no sense fighting folks who cannot swim in armor or with feathers. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L629-632)
- Lasc offers to use a [Storm Captain]'s first and last Skill at Lord Kallinad's order, calling it an act of desperation that could sink them all. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1118)
- {event} Lasc calls a storm that obeys no one, a terrible [Storm Captain]'s Skill of last resort that can crack a ship like The Emerald Signet in a moment. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1325-1327)
- A [Storm Sailor] is blown over the sides in the storm and cannot be found. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1330)
- Lasc stays at the helm, navigating the ship and refusing to budge as the waves shake it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1329,L1331)
- {belief} Begal says old Lasc took Inky on as a crew member for the trip because she is a [Storm Sailor]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L212)
## Book 19
- Lasc commands his repaired ship while coordinating the loading of cargo and people, with Inky as Second Mate. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L311-313)
- Lasc tells Inky that until the ship is re-enchanted and the crew up to snuff, he does not want complaints, and that the alternative is dancing with Lord Seagrass, the Undersea Crews and half the [Pirates] in the sea. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L313)
- Lasc ties himself to the wheel and fights the storm to reach the Isle of Mages, telling Inky they never had a choice. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L407-408)
