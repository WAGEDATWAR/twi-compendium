# Hexel Quithail
> Type: person | Also called: Hexel; Architect Hexel; Master Quithail | First appears: Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn
> See also: Elirr, Mrsha, Krshia Silverfang, Lism, Liscor Council, Lamia, Naga
## Overview
- Hexel Quithail is a male Lamia [Architect] hired to build Liscor, and an anomaly in southern Izril even among Lizardfolk. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L977-981)
- Hexel is of average height when upright, with larger scales than a Drake, a long serpentine lower half and two human-like arms. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L978-979)
- Hexel has a scar on one cheek that runs down his face. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L984,L1002)
- The narration calls Hexel one of the Naga, a broad term for the higher forms the Lizardpeople of Baleros can become. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L293)
- Hexel is one of four Lizardfolk living in Liscor at the time of Mrsha's meeting with him. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L84)
- Hexel and Elirr are around the same age, though a Lamia would live possibly twice as long as a Gnoll. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L430-431)
## Arrival in Liscor (Book 17)
- Hexel lands at Zeres, sails north to Human lands, and is escorted by Lady Reinhart to Invrisil before reaching Liscor. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L985)
- Hexel arrives at the inn with three nervous Lizardfolk assistants carrying bags, and introduces himself to the Council. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L981-984)
- Hexel says he was delighted to be offered the job of building a city like Liscor. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L998)
- Hexel says he would love to see maps of the Antinium tunnels so he can rework the city to avoid sabotage. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L987)
- {event} Hexel tells the Council that Drakes slaughtered half his [Assistants] and gave him his scar on his journey north from Zeres. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1001-1002,L1006)
- {event} Hexel demands non-Drake [Bodyguards], Gnolls or Humans preferably, and threatens to return to Baleros without them. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1003)
- The Council promises Hexel bodyguards, and Lism protests that the attackers were bandits and not Liscor's people. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1009-1013)
- Hexel says he was forced to kill the rest after the Drake ambush. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L1006)
- Erin sees Hexel naked at his window the morning after he arrives. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L41-42)
- Hexel asks Lyonette about entering Liscor from the inn and complains that his bodyguards have not arrived, telling Yemmi not to worry. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L314-315)
- {event} Hexel tells the Council he wants multiple bodyguards, one per assistant, and that Baleros' Guild of Builders will hear if they fail. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L350,L383)
- Hexel insists on Level 20 or higher if the Watch is used as bodyguards. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L383)
## Designs for Liscor (Book 17)
- {event} Hexel uses [Ambition Visualized] to present a first design that expands Liscor with high-occupancy housing, two parks and a larger sewer network. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L371-377)
- Hexel's second plan adds a second tier of aerial walkways and streets, and he says it will triple the city's capacity. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L387-388)
- Hexel's second plan replaces the walls with fifty-foot sloped walls and doubles the landmass by expanding west and south. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L391-393)
- Hexel's second plan places a deep lake in the city centre, nearly three times as deep as Liscor is tall. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L394-406)
- Hexel says the lake could be drained and used as a last-resort flood against the Hive. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L409-412)
- Hexel says the lake would draw on the Floodplains' groundwater, perhaps raised by a few [Hydromancers]. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L433)
- The Council gasps at the revised budget, which Tismel calls outrageous, and Hexel offers to start only after funding is secured. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L416-423)
- Hexel says he devised the plan in one night while drinking with Councilmember Elirr. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L423)
- Elirr appoints himself liaison to Hexel, and Jeva Silverfang says Hexel is already smoothing out the Floodplains for a football pitch. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L316,L361-366)
## Daily life (Books 18-19)
- Hexel's three Lizardfolk apprentices stay afraid of Drakes and take their breakfast in their room. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L72)
- Hexel tells Erin he is going to breakfast with Councilman Elirr. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Meetings_and_Friendships L76)
- Hexel tells Mrsha the Council has him working so hard that his tail is raw. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L98)
- Hexel's team of builders once numbered eighteen of the finest of Baleros, and now he has only three apprentices. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L294-295)
- Hexel scolds his Lizardfolk assistants, telling Zekky and Vile to stop cowering when a Drake server approaches. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L279-281)
- Hexel packs a wooden bento-style lunch box into a bag of holding for breakfast with Elirr. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L156,L200-201)
## Relationship with Elirr (Book 19)
- {reveal} Hexel and Elirr are in a secret relationship, and Mrsha, caught sneaking out of the shop by Elirr's cats, sees it. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L427-444,L477-479)
- {rel} Hexel coils his lower half around Elirr and later twines his tail around Elirr's foot. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L438-439,L508)
- Elirr asks Mrsha to tell no one, including Erin and Numbtongue, because it could mean danger for him and Hexel. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L483-489)
- Hexel says some people will not like it, and that he might leave but Elirr has to live in Liscor. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L493)
- Hexel says Dullahans hate same-sex couples, Naga are not opposed, and Centaurs are very bad, sometimes. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L509)
- Hexel helps make sure Mrsha understands why she must not tell, and she nods her agreement. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L524)
