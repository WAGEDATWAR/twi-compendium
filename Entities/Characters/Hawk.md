# Hawk
> Type: person | Also called: the Courier of Liscor; Rabbit Beastkin Courier; Rabbit-man; Hare of Liscor | First appears: Ch2.13
> See also: Selys Shivertail, Valceif, Erin Solstice, Ryoka Griffin, Baiss, Relc, Lasica, Rufelt
## Overview
- Hawk is a Rabbit Beastkin of the Long Ear Tribe, with light brown fur marked by dark spots, a pink nose, two long floppy ears and a ripped build. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L144-146,L231)
- Hawk is a Courier, a class rarer than City Runners, and says there are fewer than a hundred Couriers on the continent. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L160,L167)
- Hawk's deliveries cover around a thousand miles, sometimes two thousand on a round trip, and he takes big jobs or none at all. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L167-169)
- Hawk is the only Beastkin Runner on the continent, according to the narration of another Courier's visit. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L102)
- Hawk is the lone Courier living in Liscor, and can do deliveries south of Liscor where City Runners cannot. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L23; Book 10, Ch6.11 L141)
## Background and character
- Hawk says his parents were from Liscor but did not know what names his species used, so they chose Hawk, though he would have preferred Hakss. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L154)
- {belief} Hawk later says that his original parents left him in Liscor and a Drake couple took him in, and that Liscor is his home. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L180)
- Hawk is afraid of loud noises and pays for soundproofing spells at home. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L172,L183)
- Hawk is trying to become a [Chef] as well as a Courier. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L186)
- Hawk eats little meat and makes carrot dishes, which Selys says bore the Drakes he courts. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L215-220)
- Selys says Hawk likes Drakes, not Gnolls or Humans, and that she dated him for only two weeks. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L204,L257)
- Selys says Hawk killed a Crypt Lord and many undead during the undead attack and carried over a hundred people to safety. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L207)
- Hawk says Humans seem fleshy to him, and he would rather kiss a Minotaur than a Human. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L303)
- Hawk offers Erin a Courier discount through the Runner's Guild, especially if she gives him more burgers. (Book 2, Ch2.13 L195)
- Hawk normally makes Liscor's Courier runs, and Valceif covered one because Hawk was busy with the undead. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L195-198)
## Warnings to Ryoka (Book 2)
- Hawk reaches Ryoka and Valceif within seconds of leaving the city gate, and shakes hands with a large paw. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L195-202)
- Hawk warns that southern warzones use area spells and shoot at any Human running past, Runner or not. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L236-239)
- Hawk says the Necromancer's leftover undead near the Blood Fields include wraiths, ghosts and new types. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L266-271)
- Hawk says he ate eight hamburgers at the inn the night before and then went to bed with a Drake. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L296-298)
## The Goblin Lord and the door (Books 4-8)
- Hawk sits with Halrac and Ulrien, whom he has met on deliveries, and asks whether a room is free at the inn. (Book 4, Ch3.42 L98-102)
- {event} Hawk runs south and back carrying a dead Drake soldier and demands that the Council and Watch Captain Zevara be summoned. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L490-495)
- Hawk refuses to try the road to Salazsar because of the Goblin Lord's armies, having seen two Drake armies destroyed. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L23-25)
- Hawk delivers a thick sealed letter to Olesm, picked up in Invrisil and probably sent from First Landing on another continent. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L294-300)
- Ilvriss orders the Courier of Liscor to carry the door's anchor to Pallass that day, whatever the Goblin threat. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L170-172)
- {event} Hawk runs four days to Pallass, sets up the door with a stone Pisces gave him, and tells Erin of his run. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L286-297)
- Hawk charges Pallass triple for the job and ignores the Runner's Guild regulations. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L556)
- Jelaqua reports that Hawk flatly refuses to run near the Goblin Lord's army or wait for an [Enchanter]'s appraisal of artifacts. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L37-40)
- Hawk can cross the flooded land by jumping from hill to hill instead of swimming, because some hills are just under the water. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L107-109)
- Hawk thinks that Klbkch, whom he has known over a decade, is well liked in Liscor. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L161)
