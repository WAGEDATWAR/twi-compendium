# Elirr
> Type: person | Also called: Mister Elirr; Councilmember Elirr | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.42
> See also: Krshia, Mrsha, Hexel, Lyonette, Relc, Nokha, Calruz, Jekle
## Overview
- Elirr is an old Gnoll of the Silverfang Tribe who runs a pet shop in Liscor, and he is the oldest Gnoll held captive by the Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L579; Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L481-482; Book 19, Ch7.51 L317)
- Elirr is a [Beast Trainer], not a [Beast Tamer] or [Beast Master], and his shop is one of five pet stores in Liscor. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L481-482; Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L463)
- Elirr trains dogs, cats and small caged birds, and keeps war dogs, cats, small dogs, birds and a crab in his shop. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L482,L484)
- Elirr is a Plains Gnoll but less so than Krshia, and had been in Liscor as a retired scout before the Silverfang Clan arrived ten years earlier. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L316,L350)
- Elirr is a Councilmember of Liscor and finds the new laws and progress make him feel young. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L293; Book 19, Ch7.51 L383)
- Elirr's level and the Raskghar issue would make him a distinguished speaker at the Meeting of Tribes, according to Krshia. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L351)
## Captive of the Raskghar (Book 8)
- Elirr is the leader of the Gnoll captives in the Raskghar's cages, and the others defer to him. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L579)
- {belief} Elirr says old legends held that the Raskghar broke away from the Gnolls underground and became beasts that hunted them. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L580)
- {belief} Elirr says the legends were incomplete and that the Raskghar performed rituals that gave them strength. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L580)
- {belief} Elirr says the Book of Levels does not mention such things, and that Gnolls have forbidden blood magic as the worst of deeds. (Book 8, Ch5.42 L584)
- Elirr is shocked that Mrsha can cast [Mage] magic, because no Gnoll has done so in living memory. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L624-625,L637)
- {event} Elirr kicks Nokha, breaks out of his cage and tears open Mrsha's cage bars, then flees carrying her as Nokha and awakened Raskghar chase them. (Book 8, Ch5.43 L712-716)
- {event} Elirr tells Zevara that Mrsha howled, bringing the monster down on the Raskghar, and ran back to buy time when the Hobs would fall. (Book 8, Ch5.44 L249-251)
- The Raskghar made Elirr eat foul monster parts in the cage, which still haunts him. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L352)
## The Pet Shop (Books 9-12)
- Elirr's mastiff war dog is wary of him, because he starved for two days when Elirr was kidnapped just after acquiring him. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L488)
- {event} Mrsha faces down Elirr's mastiff by eating from its bowl, which cows the dog. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L497-499)
- {belief} Elirr tells Lyonette that a [Beast Tamer] over Level 40 is exceptionally rare, and that some animals, such as Crelers, cannot be tamed. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L518)
- Elirr says he could barely tame a Griffin, only to the most basic of social functions. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L518)
- Elirr says a [Beast Tamer] who tried to train a Creler was found dead in his home, half-devoured. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L521)
- {belief} Elirr believes Skills can make animals only so intelligent, and that a truly intelligent creature would be a person. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L528)
- Elirr refuses to sell Relc a trained attack sheep and keeps a killer cat named Miss Twekla who is trained to kill pests. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L460,L483)
- {event} Elirr bans Mrsha and the others from his shop after Mrsha ties two rats together, until they learn to respect animals. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L583-585)
- {event} Elirr calls his dogs and cats to kill the rats' basement nest, which none survive. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L612-616)
- Elirr is angrier at the two bound rats than at the death of the nest. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Two_Rats L621)
- Elirr sits with Mrsha at the bar and gets a herbal cream liqueur from Rufelt on the house, and comforts her. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L184-193)
