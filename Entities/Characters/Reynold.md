# Reynold
> Type: person | Also called: Reynold Ferusdam; Mister Reynold; Combat Butler | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.30
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Ressa, Sacra, Ryoka Griffin, Erin Solstice, Zel Shivertail
## Overview
- Reynold is a [Butler] of the Reinhart house, in the direct employ of Lady Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L486)
- Reynold is a very silent man who tells Erin his name only after a few tries. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L17)
- One of Reynold's duties is overseeing Magnolia's wine collection. (Book 2, Ch2.30 L151)
- Reynold is also one of Magnolia's personal aides and drives her carriage. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L138)
- Reynold introduces himself to Ryoka in Invrisil as Reynold Ferusdam, a humble servant of Lady Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L102)
## Abilities and background
- Reynold says he has a few levels in auxiliary classes that inform his main class, though he was never an adventurer. (Book 4, Ch3.36 L272-275)
- Reynold fights with a sword and has the Skills [Sickle Cut], [Burst Speed] and [Invisibility Field]. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L326-339)
- Reynold was once a soldier and says he was a [Lieutenant] commanding a cavalry unit. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L334; Book 6, Ch4.44M L147)
- Reynold says he fought twice against Drakes in the Bloodfields. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L147)
- Reynold sets bones in battle and has treated soldiers who cursed his name. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L408)
- Reynold is called a [Combat Butler]. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L324; Book 14, Ch6.63P L806)
- Reynold uses Magnolia's pink magical carriage as a weapon, running down assassins with it. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L868-870)
