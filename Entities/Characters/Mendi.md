# Mendi
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling
> See also: Salthorn, Orjin, Pomle, Iratze
## Overview
- Mendi is a young Dullahan in her mid-twenties and the apprentice of the Selphid Salthorn, who has taught her for eight months. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L186-191)
- Mendi wears a new, well-tended cloth uniform with a badge of a falling giant, Salthorn's symbol, sewn on one arm. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L188)
- Mendi is new to Pomle and fights as a light-footed warrior in cloth armor. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L188,L295,L303)
- Mendi is a disciple of Pomle and gets to spar fellow disciples even when she cannot fight outside challengers. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L203)
## Sparring against the Hurricane's Fists
- Mendi spars a Stitch-Man Hurricane's Fist, blocking without taking a direct punch and landing two hits in the first minute. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L318)
- Mendi does not press her opponent, which mystifies the other masters, and she backs away from his pressure-point attacks. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L319-326)
- Mendi later blocks punches and two kicks, then hits her opponent sharply and apparently cracks his femur. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L361-362)
- Mendi grapples a second Hurricane's Fist, kneeing him in the chest and breaking his ribs with a punch. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L376-382)
- Mendi is described as a bit overconfident, or tired of foreigners, when she agrees to spar Iratze. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L444-448)
- {event} Iratze beats Mendi on the ground with an arm bar, and Mendi taps out, rises panting and bows to him. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L452-457)
