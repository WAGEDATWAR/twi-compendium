# Tom
> Type: person | Also called: Thomas | First appears: Ch1.00C
> See also: Richard, Emily, Wilen, Nereshal, the Fool, Blighted King, Cirille, Alternate Tom
## Overview
- Tom is the name Thomas prefers to be called, and he is the class clown of his group of people summoned from Earth. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L24)
- Tom says no one ever laughs at his jokes and that he is not actually that funny. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L24)
- Tom has clinically diagnosed depression and no medicine in the new world, where the only drugs are different kinds of alcohol. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L30)
- Tom says depression makes him feel worthless. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L31)
- Tom wears white face paint with red on his mouth and juggles pins, performing prat falls and bad jokes for children. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L32,L53-56)
## Levels, classes and skills
- {state} Tom is a Level 19 [Clown], who took the class as a sign. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L32)
- Tom has skills for clownish things like juggling and prat falls, and a [Loud Voice] skill that he thinks is odd for a [Clown]. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L49-50)
- Tom's main trick is pulling objects seemingly out of nowhere, which works within a 5 ft radius if he has them on him. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L58)
- Tom has an annoying skill that plays a cheerful carnival tune from him unbidden. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L49,L139-141)
- {state} After the village massacre, Tom reaches [Clown Level 24!], gains [Skill - Greater Pain Tolerance] and [Skill - Devil's Luck], and receives the Conditions [Horrific Laughter] and [Lesser Insanity]. (Book 2, Ch1.01C L45-49)
