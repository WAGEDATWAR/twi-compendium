# Esiela
> Type: person | Also called: Grand Mage Esiela | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.13K
> See also: Zamea, Ulyse, Fultoolm
## Overview
- Esiela is the Grand Mage of Belchan and head of Belchan's Mage Schools. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L158)
- Esiela is the highest-level individual on Belchan's side of the battlefield, able to become invisible and levitate. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L159)
- {belief} A professor addressing the class estimates that Esiela is most likely over Level 40, and at the very least in her high thirties. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L82)
## Book 16: the battle with Reim
- Esiela's students stare at their headmistress in trepidation but also with trust. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L160)
- Esiela is dueling the [Mages] of Parasol Stroll when General Fultoolm looks for her. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L253)
- {event} Esiela is defeated and has her magic suppressed by Parasol Stroll's [Mages], and she imagines a mage collar around her neck. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L375-377)
- Ulyse of Parasol Stroll says Esiela is powerful, with six [Mages] killed, and that the Slavers of Roshal's mage-collars would not work on someone of her caliber. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L378,L382)
- {state} Ulyse offers Esiela six years in Parasol Stroll under a magical blood oath, one year per dead [Mage], or slavery. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L387)
- {state} Esiela accepts, drawing her own blood and signing the contract, and feels the magic bind her. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L404,L415)
- {event} Esiela takes a parasol and marches with the [Mages] of Parasol Stroll upon Levrhine, her home. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L416)
## Book 17: with Parasol Stroll
- Later, Esiela is a Grand Mage of Parasol Stroll, bound by blood magic, and casts [Featherweight] on Zamea's axe with the linked spell of the other [Mages]. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L32-33,L35)
- Esiela casts [Haste] on Zamea, which nearly wipes out her reserves. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L35-37)
