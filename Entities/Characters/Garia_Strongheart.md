# Garia Strongheart
> Type: person | Also called: Garia; Garia Strongarm; Celum's Martial Artist | First appears: Ch1.01R
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Fals, Persua, Wailant Strongheart, Viceria Strongheart, Grimalkin, Numbtongue, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Garia Strongheart is a City Runner who is shorter than Ryoka Griffin but wider, sturdy without the obvious muscles of a man. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L21)
- Garia is friendly to Ryoka and greets her cheerfully, slapping her on the shoulder. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L20-23)
- Garia is a good-hearted but slow Runner whom Ryoka considers, for her heart, the best Runner of the lot. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L193)
## Early Runner years (Books 1-3)
- Garia visits Ryoka at her table, and cheerfully sits down with her despite Ryoka's cool reception. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L35-36)
- Garia admits she cannot read books and can only manage signs and math, and says Fals can read. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L57-61)
- {event} When Persua and a gaggle of Street Runners taunt Ryoka, Garia grabs the lunging Ryoka by the shoulder and, one-handed, pins her to her chair while placing herself between Ryoka and Persua. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L94-100)
- Garia says she is too slow to run like Ryoka and can only carry very heavy loads instead of fast requests; she says she could not run at all if not for the way the Runner's Guild works. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L180)
- {event} Garia hugs Ryoka hard enough to threaten a rib, promises to keep the other Runners busy while Ryoka flees, and tells her not to die. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L191-192)
- Garia normally takes the heaviest Runner deliveries, saying they are safe because nobody steals raw ore or sugar; the costliest goods she carries are weapons and spices. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L12-21)
- Ryoka notes that Garia jogged a pack of roughly thirty to forty kilograms of spices all the way from Wales; Garia says it is not that bad if the weight is distributed properly and that she sometimes walks. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L15-17)
- {event} In a brawl, Garia punches the adventurer Arnel, whose feet leave the floor, and knocks him out cold; the blow deforms his chain mail. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L184-189)
- {reveal} Garia tells Ryoka that she was a [Farm Worker] before becoming a [Runner] and reached Level 14 before changing classes. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L226)
- Garia has the Skill [Enhanced Strength], which she says normally comes to warrior classes around Level 30, so she is much stronger than normal; she barely feels thirty kilograms. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L237-239)
- {event} Garia arrives in Esthelm just before midday with several bags of sugar for a bakery and sees the commotion around Ryoka at the Adventurer's Guild. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L2-4)
- Ryoka remembers that Garia was Level 11 when they first met and wonders whether she will get faster as a [Runner]; Ryoka also thinks of her as shorter and stockier than Fals, with terrible running form. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L18,L152-153)
- Garia elbows Ryoka in the stomach hard enough that Ryoka had forgotten how strong she is. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L32)
- Garia says she looked at a tripvine bag for protection, but even that was out of her price range. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L129)
- Garia carries a horseshoe that appears to keep the faeries away from her. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L281)
- {event} Garia stumbles into the Runner's Guild with a bloody head wound; she says bandits cut her off from over a hill, some shooting arrows and one with a club, and she tried to outrun them. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L134,L156)
- {event} Ryoka checks that Garia's skull is not broken and closes the wound with a healing potion that Fals hands her. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L141-147)
- Garia has no healing potion and cannot afford one; Ryoka notes she is one of the slowest City Runners, even if she can carry a lot, and earns only a few silver pieces at best, usually coppers. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L173-174)
- Garia strides into Erin's inn with a huge smile, greets Ryoka, and asks where Fals is and whether anything is new; Ryoka tells her about Persua. (Book 2, Ch2.40 L481,L490-491)
- Garia lowers her voice to warn Ryoka that Persua is going to be a Courier; she does not like violence. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L250-251)
- {event} After Persua is hurt in the fight in the Guild, Garia, her nose bleeding, holds Ryoka back. (Book 3, Ch3.04 L422-423)
- Fals tells Ryoka that Garia is a good Runner who can haul as much as a horse but is slow. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L192)
- {reveal} Fals explains that Garia loses the road in snow because she cannot tell white from grey, so winter is hard for her; Ryoka identifies it as colour blindness. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L192-203)
- Fals says Garia has invited him to her parents' farm to thank Ryoka for helping her after her injury, and asks Ryoka to come too. (Book 3, Ch3.09 L208)
## Parents and the farm (Book 5)
- Garia apologises to Ryoka that she is skipping runs because she sprained her ankle, and says her healing potion was low-quality. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L180)
- Fals joins Ryoka at the Runner's Guild, mentions Garia told him Ryoka was in a meeting with Erin, and plays along to get food for her guests. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L235-245)
- Garia's parents tell the story of two fights at the inn, in which Garia knocked out opponents with one hit each time, including a Bronze-rank adventurer brought by Persua, and fought to defend Ryoka. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L127-129)
- Garia's father Wailant says that her [Enhanced Strength] at her age means she could knock out an ox. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L133)
- Garia refuses to let her parents buy her magical artifacts or things; Wailant says she wanted to fit in, not get ahead, and thought it would be cheating. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L184-187)
- Wailant and Viceria say Garia has no talent with a sword, magic, a spear or a bow, and is too clumsy and wrong in temperament to be a warrior. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L190-195)
- {state} Garia tells Ryoka, delighted, that she is a [Martial Artist] and has gained the Skill [Basic Footwork]. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L442)
## Martial Artist (Books 10-12)
- {state} Garia, now toned and muscular, greets Ceria and the Horns in Celum; Ceria does not recognise her because she was stockier before. She says she lost weight and has a new class, [Martial Artist], and still calls herself a City Runner. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L990-1002)
- Garia says she has made Celum her home base because it is close to her family's home, she has become much faster and earns more, and she is a regular at The Drunken Swordsman, where the innkeeper is Timbor. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L5,L13-15)
- {state} Garia says she is a Level 14 [Martial Artist], has been levelling very fast, and shows a six-pack of abdominal muscle. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L71-72)
- Garia says she got the class nearly a month and a half before, and that [Weight Control] was among her first Skills, at Level 6. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L38)
- Garia says she knocked out her father with a lucky punch to the head during a sparring session after Ryoka left, when he made her practise a hundred of Ryoka's punches; she gained the [Martial Artist] class that night. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L100,L115)
- Garia confirms she had [Enhanced Strength], and says she was a [Farmer] until she quit. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L102-110)
- Garia says she has learned a very fast kick and a dodging Skill, and that she is faster partly from weight loss; she notes almost nobody on Izril has her class. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L129)
- Garia is researching [Martial Artists] and similar classes among adventurers, consults [Mages] and travellers from Chandrar, and notes that Pomle in Chandrar has [Martial Artists] but no Mage's Guild for a [Message] spell. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L132-134)
- Garia says [Martial Artists] also use weapons in some styles, practises daily punches, kicks and a dodging routine, and wants Ryoka to teach her flips. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L82,L134)
- Garia tells the Horns that she is the best City Runner in the area now, though not as fast as Ryoka. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L242)
- Pisces says Garia punched a Rockdevil to death; Garia tells how she realised she was a lot better when she killed a monster whose bounty was worth more than her delivery. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L192-194)
- {event} Visiting Erin's inn with Fals through the door from Celum, Garia reflexively kicks Numbtongue with [Quick Kick] and knocks him back, mistaking him for a Hobgoblin enemy. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L390-392)
- Grimalkin recognises Garia as a [Martial Artist] and a Runner with [Enhanced Strength]; she tells him she wasn't taught by a martial artist and wants to learn more, and he urges her to become a [Mage] instead. (Book 12, Ch6.38 L397-408)
## Later appearances (Books 15-19)
- Garia, now a slim [Martial Artist] and City Runner, runs through Celum with Fals searching for Mrsha, and answers Erin's call. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1076-1078,L1092)
- Garia says her parents farm near Celum, that her mother sent a [Message], and that her father fights with a paralysis-poisoned magic cutlass. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1086-1093)
- {reveal} Garia says her parents drink like retired [Pirates], which her father is, and the farm grows produce including Sage's Grass. (Book 15, Ch7.07 L1096-1099)
- {event} Garia drags a door tied to her waist with a mana stone on it from Celum to the farm. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L376)
- Garia explains that Erin took the door from Celum after a trade dispute with the Runner's Guild, and that the Players of Celum are moving to Liscor. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L489,L505)

- {state} Garia says she is a Level 17 [Martial Artist]; she has [Basic Footwork] and [Flash Kick], learned the kick from Grimalkin, and has beaten monsters including a rabid bear. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L572,L580,L594,L604)
- Garia says she levelled quickly but has slowed despite a thousand punches and kicks per day, and asks Numbtongue to spar. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L604)
- {event} Garia spars Numbtongue in the barn, and her [Flash Kick] sends him into the wall; he is put to bed with a healing potion. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L631-636)

- Ryoka sees that Garia has become a completely different person, with abs like Numbtongue's and greater confidence. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L666-668)
- Fals says Garia now has the nickname Celum's [Martial Artist] after she beat a bandit group and dragged four of them into town for the bounty; Garia says she is not yet Level 20. (Book 19, Ch7.54 L676-678)
