# Wikir
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Wikir; First Captain Wikir | First appears: Ch6.30
> See also: Embria, Pielt, Vell, Relc
## Overview
- Wikir is a Gnoll [Captain] of 4th Company, called First Captain and second-in-command to Wing Commander Embria. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L17)
- Wikir is twenty years older than Embria and has dozens of scars hidden under his fur, and one of his ears is tattered. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L74)
- Wikir fights with a mace, and the narration compares his turning head to the slow wrath of the mace, which is not slow in battle. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L128)
- Wikir leads 1st Squad and is described as the oldest [Captain] in 4th Company. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L11-13,L23)
- Wikir served with Relc in 4th Company, according to Captain Pielt. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L328)
## Role in 4th Company
- Embria spars with Wikir first each morning and then with two more of his [Captains] while the others practise. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L17)
- Embria orders Wikir to take the squads out to practise and later leaves him in charge of parade marches on her day off. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L13,L100-101)
- Wikir commands the [Lieutenants] and [Sergeants] under his direct command and grins when Embria announces the Shield Spider culling. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L77-80)
- Wikir tells Pielt his group will be picking baby spiders out of its ears unless it has a strong [Mage] like Wikir's own. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L83-86)
- Wikir tells Embria that he does not run with the civilian Gnoll crowd and that they do not trust him. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L120)
- Wikir says he knows Krshia, that she is smart and has authority, and that many Gnolls will follow when she howls. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L120)
- Wikir asks a Sergeant whether having more Gnolls than Drakes on Liscor's Council would be a bad thing. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L127-129)
## Shield Spider nest culling
- Wikir smashes a Shield Spider's head with his mace as 1st Squad drops into a nest while seven more members wait above. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L11-13)
- Wikir orders the squad to kill the adult spiders and use torches, and the nest burns with its egg sacks. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L14-15)
- Sergeant Gna helps Wikir out of the pit, and tiny Shield Spiders scuttle from his paw to her fur. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L17)
- Wikir stomps back to the pit to shake off the baby spiders, as the squad's [Mage] adds to the flames. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L20)
- Wikir says he went in himself so that Pielt could not call him a coward, and that other nests are burned from above as planned. (Book 11, Ch6.30 L21-24)
