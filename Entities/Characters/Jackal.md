# Jackal
> Type: person | Also called: Jack | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.32G
> See also: Minerva, Halrac, Tyrion Veltras
## Overview
- Jackal is a [Ranger] who leads an adventuring team that infiltrates the Goblin mountain, and he carries a shortbow. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L15,L24,L29)
- Jackal's infiltrators are nearly a dozen strong, in black, dark green or grey, and arrive by magic circle alongside [Mages] and a [Witch]. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L15,L26-28)
- Jackal's team includes Minerva, who calls him her leader. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L13,L24)
## Infiltration (Book 8)
- {event} Jackal waits with a shortbow beside two Hobs he has shot through the eye, and chides Minerva for being late. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L15-19)
- Jackal tells the [Mages] to cancel their light spells, since the arrival must stay a stealth mission. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L29-30)
- Jackal says he holds a pass for a personal audience with the Healer of Tenbault, so captured allies who are alive can be made whole. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L50)
- {event} Jackal leads Minerva and four others deeper into the mountain while the [Mages] cast ward spells. (Book 8, Ch5.32G L52)
## At Liscor (Book 9)
- Jackal is a Gold-rank captain whom Tyrion's messenger orders to shoot the Goblins defending Liscor. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L590-598)
- {event} Jackal readies [Farseeker Arrows] and [Double Arrow] against the Hob archer Badarrow, until an enchanted arrow detonates in front of his team. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L599-605)
- Jackal recognises Halrac on Liscor's battlements as the archer who shot the enchanted arrow. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L612-613)
- {event} Jackal lowers his bow and refuses Tyrion's order, saying Halrac has the angle and uses enchanted arrows. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L620-622)
- Jackal tells the messenger to get Tyrion's own [Archers], or the Kingslayer, to do the job. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L622)
