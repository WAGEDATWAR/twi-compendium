# Osthia Blackwing
> Type: person | Also called: Captain Osthia; Osthmissa Shieldscale; Asrira Shieldscale; Captain Shieldscale | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.16
> See also: Thrissiam, Garusa, Ilvriss, Reiss, Az'kerash, Snapjaw
## Overview
- Osthia Blackwing is a Drake [Soldier] and [Captain] who leads the Ancestor Guard (Oldblood) company assigned to General Thrissiam. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L46-48,L59)
- Osthia's wings are dark yellowish-green, her scales are bright yellow, and the spines on her head crest are dyed bright blue. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L46-47)
- Osthia has wings, which most Drakes do not, because of the old blood in her veins. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L48-49,L64)
- Osthia is a graduate of Pallass' military academy with high marks. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L71)
- Osthia is Thrissiam's niece, technically, though only about ten years younger and a very distant cousin. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L74)
- Osthia has liked Thrissiam since meeting him as a hatchling, never told him, and tried to be assigned to his command. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L75)
## Abilities
- Osthia does not breathe fire but spits a corrosive greenish acid from glands in her mouth. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L50,L166)
- Osthia's wings and breath attack work without the problems that afflict some Oldblood Drakes. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L86)
- Osthia cannot fly long, becoming very tired after about ten minutes of flight. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L85-90)
- Osthia drinks a foul-tasting mana potion to fly longer, and can do so only four times before she throws up. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L107)
- Osthia can melt a Ghoul's face with her acid, and her own body is immune to it. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L166-167)
