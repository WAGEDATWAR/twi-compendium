# Salthorn
> Type: person | Also called: Master Salthorn | First appears: Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling
> See also: Orjin, Mendi, Iratze
## Overview
- Salthorn is a Selphid [Martial Artist] and one of Pomle's masters, an expert in grappling. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L181,L183)
- Salthorn is the only Selphid in Pomle, since few Selphids could survive there given how hard it is to find new bodies. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L182)
- Salthorn's body is old and desiccated, and her Skill lets it move and keep active with minimal wear. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L181-182)
- Salthorn wears a carelessly worn uniform on a worn, half-rotten corpse, and her symbol is a falling giant. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L188)
- Orjin would rather jump into a nest of giant flame ants than let Salthorn get an arm around him. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L183)
## Apprentice Mendi
- Salthorn brings a young Dullahan apprentice, Mendi, in her mid-twenties, to Orjin. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L184,L187)
- Mendi says Master Salthorn has taught her for eight months. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L191)
- Salthorn pushes forward to find her apprentice on the ground after Iratze beats Mendi. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L454-455)
- Salthorn teaches her pupil Mendi, a [Grappler], holds and submissions, including how to subdue a Dullahan by grabbing the head. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L51-54)
- Salthorn says that if she touched the Death of Magic and broke her magic shields, she would never let go. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L52)
- Salthorn teaches Iratze, a new pupil, to stop a Selphid, who a Human would need to break down by tearing muscles. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L55-59)
## Advice to Orjin
- {belief} Salthorn advises Orjin that if he wants to fight enemies like the Death of Magic, he should buy a flying artifact and train with it. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L52)
- {belief} Salthorn tells Orjin she cannot help him fight the Death of Magic, and that flying is the only reason a [Mage] would survive an encounter with him. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L64)
