# Flos
> Type: person | Also called: Flos Reimarch; King Flos Reimarch; King of Destruction; Flos of Reim; King of Reim; King Reimarch | First appears: Ch1.07R
> See also: Orthenon, Gazi, Mars, Trey Atwood, Teres Atwood, Reim, Quarass of Germina, Raelt, Fetohep
## Overview
- The King of Destruction is a warlord whom Ryoka reads about as having conquered a whole continent before his empire suddenly collapsed; the books she read did not say why, and he was neither killed nor apparently harmed. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L307)
- {belief} Ryoka's book says the King of Destruction abandoned his dreams of world conquest and now sits in his crumbling kingdom while other countries pick apart his empire: "The King of Destruction sleeps." (Book 1, Ch1.07R L307)
- Pisces tells Ryoka that Flos was a king once poised to rule nearly half of the civilized world, who abandoned his kingdom and, he says, has slumbered for ten years in his decaying capital. (Book 2, Ch2.05 L343)
- Pisces tells Ryoka that the Wistram mages feared him even inactive: a gathering of the greatest living mages listed him as the eighth likeliest disaster to destroy civilization, with a 30% chance that he conquers the world, depending on factors. (Book 2, Ch2.05 L347-371)
- An Izril speaker tells Erin that Flos conquered nearly all of Chandrar and that his armies were crossing the sea and landing on Izril's southern shores before he vanished. (Book 2, Ch2.10 L124-126)
- Flos is entirely Human, with red-gold hair, a strong nose and a grin, and is a huge man of muscle without fat, apparently over forty but looking in his prime. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L16,L75)
- Flos is one of the highest-levelled [Kings] in the world, perhaps the highest; his true level is a closely guarded secret and his Skills are all geared to war, making his kingdom prosperous in strife and impoverished otherwise. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L117)
- Flos likes simple food and eats Yellats stew, a poor man's staple of Chandrar that he grew up enjoying; his servants object to it. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L119-124)
