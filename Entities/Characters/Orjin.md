# Orjin
> Type: person | Also called: Strongest of Pomle; Orjin of Pomle | First appears: Ch6.15K
> See also: Salii, Peki, Nsiia, Xil, Revine, Iratze
## Overview
- Orjin is the Strongest of Pomle, a [Martial Artist] who has trained in Pomle all his life, since before he could walk. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L516; Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L2)
- Orjin trains by balancing on his toes on gravel and broken stones, without using his arms. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L531-535)
- Orjin knows eight different weapons well enough to use them in combat. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L178)
- Orjin's Skills include a tremor stomp, [Spear Dance: Crashing Rapids] and [Aeriform Shockwave]. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L354,L538,L543)
- Salii acts as Orjin's [Secretary]. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L532)

## Book 13, Tiqr's refugees
- {event} Orjin stands at Pomle's ring of coloured stones facing a Nerrhavian force of about six thousand and a Savere force of about two thousand pursuing Tiqr's refugees. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L516-519)
- Orjin thinks the armies want the refugees as slaves. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L526)
- Orjin tells the two armies that Pomle claimed this land thirty years ago, and the refugees are guests under Pomle's peace. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L535-537)
- {event} Orjin tells the armies to leave, warning that stepping across the line will remind them why Pomle is free. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L540-549)
- Orjin has barely a hundred [Martial Artists] and [Warriors] with him, on the high ground with stone spears. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L532,L550)
- Both armies withdraw, and Salii leads the refugees to temporary camps. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L552-558)
- {event} That night Orjin helps kill a raiding party of Savere rogues, kicking one off the canyon. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L564-567)
- Salii tells Orjin the latest group of refugees is a thousand two hundred and eighteen strong, and Pomle may run out of food. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L572-575)
- Orjin says it is not Pomle's duty to feed the refugees, but lets Salii try to handle it. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L577-584)
- Salii tells Orjin that Pomle has taken in over sixty thousand of Tiqr's citizens during the war. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L557)
- Orjin smells blood on the wind and knows Tiqr's end is near. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L530)
## Book 16, the Windcaller's Wrath
- Orjin has allowed many outsiders, refugees fleeing the war in Tiqr, to settle in Pomle, and some have built a small settlement. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L176)
- Salii warns Orjin that the Windcaller's Wrath, a foreign martial arts group, is a cult. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L223-226)
- {event} Orjin beats the Fury of Skies by clinching, throwing him and punching him in the face. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L426-430)
- Orjin mounts the Fury of Skies, locks down his arms and punches until he stops moving, stopping at the jaw and teeth. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Dancing_and_Brawling L430-434)
## Book 18, the Death of Magic
- Orjin wonders how he would kill a Kraken, or a foe that attacks from beyond the clouds like the Death of Magic. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L5-10)
- Orjin imagines the Death of Magic killing him. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L10)
- Orjin fought and beat a Grand Elephant in his youth, killed Crelers bare-handed and fought sand worms. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L7)
- Orjin's [Aeriform Shockwave] reaches about three hundred feet at best, far short of the clouds. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L13-16)
- Lacten of Pomle was weaker than Orjin and died without killing a single foe, fighting illusions. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L11)
- Orjin tells Xil he beat the Fury of Skies but the Fury would do better against an aerial opponent, as Orjin cannot fly. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L21-27)
- {belief} Salthorn tells Orjin he cannot help him fight the Death of Magic and that flying is the only reason a [Mage] would survive him. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Sand_and_Notes L64)
