# Headscratcher
> Type: person | Also called: Redfang Berserker | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.20T
> See also: Badarrow, Grunter, Numbtongue, Rabbiteater, Shorthilt, Garen Redfang, Az'kerash, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Headscratcher is a Redfang Hobgoblin warrior who was the first Goblin to come for the Horror at Esthelm. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L36)
- Headscratcher is the strongest of the five Hobs, passionate and prone to fury in combat. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L110)
- Headscratcher is seven years old and had been a warrior for six. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L2)
## Early appearances
- The Horror removed ticks from Headscratcher's head using a hot stick and her nails. (Book 3, Ch3.20T L36-40)
- At the inn, Headscratcher mimed asking Lyonette for the leftover eggs and ate food the Antinium refused. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L193-197)
- Erin slapped Headscratcher by mistake after he was standing on the ladder listening. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L85-87)
- {belief} Headscratcher showed he was willing to die with a dagger at his throat if it would help the tribe. (Book 7, Ch5.14 L154-155)
- Grief at losing Bugear and the fury of battle made Headscratcher stronger. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L136)
- Headscratcher carried the wounded Badarrow through deep water and refused to let go. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L268-272)
## Grief and class change
- Headscratcher, Badarrow, Numbtongue, Shorthilt and Rabbiteater all levelled while staying at Erin's inn. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L6)
- Headscratcher weeps over Grunter, Bugear, Orangepoo and Leftstep, and his grief turns to rage. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L197-199)
- {state} Headscratcher reached Warrior Level 20 and changed to the [Berserker] class, gaining [Fury Strength] and [Overpowering Blow]. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L545-548)
- His new class was born of grief, and he threw a Raskghar across the room. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L551-554,L574)
- [Berserker] and Numbtongue's [Bard] are unique classes among Goblins. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L147)
- Headscratcher was badly wounded in the side by Nokha's sword while holding off the Raskghar. (Book 8, Ch5.44 L50-51)
## Book 9
- Headscratcher hit a home run in Erin's baseball game with a roar and glowing red eyes, and Erin gave him a place on a team. (Book 9, Ch5.46 L454-455,L792)
- Headscratcher's faction are fighters who train relentlessly and carry an enchanted axe. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L15-16)
- Headscratcher won the axe in a brawl among the Hobs and cried with joy. (Book 9, Ch5.49 L555-559)
- Headscratcher and others challenged Garen about his order to kill the Innkeeper, and were beaten bloody. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L519-524)
- {state} Headscratcher and the four other Hobs effectively took over the Redfang tribe's leadership. (Book 9, Ch5.58 L781-783)
- Headscratcher took Erin's white flag and said he was going to leave but then saw it. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L636-641)
- Headscratcher carries a golden axe whose jade edge cuts through Goblins' steel shields. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L19-20)
- {state} Az'kerash's rapier pierced Headscratcher's chest just below the heart, and he died in Erin's arms. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L755-756,L773-779)
