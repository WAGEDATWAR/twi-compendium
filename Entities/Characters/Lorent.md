# Lorent
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.09
> See also: Maughin, Erin Solstice, Pelt
## Overview
- Lorent is a Dullahan [Sharpener] in Pallass who sells knives from a permanent stall built into a building. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L192; Book 11, Ch6.31 L226)
- Lorent wears a full set of polished iron armor and speaks in a dour, flat voice. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L193,L195)
- Lorent sells knives crafted by Maughin and says a blade could skin a Wyvern. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L200)
- Lorent removes his head, puts it in a basket on the counter and lets his body fetch stock from the back of the shop. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L232-235)
- Lorent says a knife should fit the hand and offers to have a [Carpenter] sand down the grips for Erin. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L234)
## Later
- Lorent greets Erin as Hello and says business could be better, doubting a Gnoll's claim about good knives. (Book 11, Ch6.31 L224-225)
- Lorent splits a wooden block in half by dropping it on an upturned knife in front of a Pallassian and Liscorian crowd. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L462-464)
- Lorent is among the Dullahans who put their heads on their shoulders to join the dance after Maughin does. (Book 19, Ch7.49 L761-762)
