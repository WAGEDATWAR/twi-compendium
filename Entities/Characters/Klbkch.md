# Klbkch
> Type: person | Also called: Klb; Klbkchhezeim; Klbkch the Slayer; the Slayer; Revalantor Klbkch; Senior Guardsman Klbkch | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.07
> See also: Relc, Xrn, Erin Solstice, Pawn, Ksmvr, Anand, Bird, Wrymvr, Zel Shivertail, Zevara, Antinium (Free Antinium of Liscor), Rite of Anastases, Centenium
## Overview
- Klbkch is an Antinium who introduces himself to Erin Solstice as a Senior Guardsman in the employ of the city of Liscor, partner to the Drake Relc. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L126)
- Klbkch is a giant ant-like being with large black bulbous eyes, a dark brown chitinous body, a pair of swords at his sides, a huge abdomen, two long pincers by his mouth and, in his first body, four arms. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L2)
- Relc names Klbkch as a [Swordslayer] with some levels in [Guardsman], fewer than in his primary class. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L174)
- Klbkch is known as "Klbkch of the Free Antinium" and, in the oldest history, as Klbkch the Slayer of the Antinium Wars, one of the Centenium. (Book 2, Ch2.06 L20; Book 5, Ch4.07 L29)
- {state} Klbkch dies defending Erin from Goblins in Book 1, is reborn in a new two-armed body through the Rite of Anastases, and afterwards rises to the rank of Revalantor of the Free Antinium. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L187-188; Book 1, Ch1.44 L291-292; Book 2, Ch2.15 L5)
## Appearance, body and manner
- Klbkch has compound eyes, brown and fractal like an ant's, which Erin finds creepy but decides she can trust. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L255)
- In his first body Klbkch fights with two swords and two small daggers, one in each of his four hands. (Book 1, Ch1.11 L105)
- Klbkch carries two swords at his waist and draws two daggers with his lower set of hands when he expects a fight. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L5,L9)
- Klbkch stands still to rest, which Relc calls his "creepy standing hibernation thing"; Klbkch agrees that it is very restful. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L307-308)
- Among his own kind Klbkch has no name that can be expressed in words; "Klbkch", and sometimes "Klb", is what members of other species call him and what he calls himself, because he thinks it important to have a name. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L2)
- Klbkch values doing things properly, keeping good relations with other species and polite posture, and his thoughts repeatedly return to these ideas. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L3-4,L193,L202)
- Klbkch's blood is green. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L144)
- {state} Klbkch's reborn body has only two arms and a slim, graceful build unlike a Worker's or a Soldier's, and he holds two swords. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L279)
- Klbkch tells Erin the new body is swifter and more mobile, with anatomical changes not incorporated into other Antinium, and calls the older four-arm design inefficient. (Book 1, Ch1.45 L73-77)
- Ceria Springwalker notes that she has never seen an Antinium with only two arms and takes it to mean he is a special leader, since the other Workers obey his commands without question. (Book 2, Ch2.11 L255)
- Klbkch's Queen is still bothered that he has only two hands, but it was something he had requested. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L21)
- Klbkch keeps his two silver swords at his side even inside the Hive. (Book 3, Ch3.06L L47)
- Klbkch's office in the Hive is a small dirt room with a table, two chairs and a coatrack that holds only a single scarf. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L110)
- Klbkch thinks he was made for war and not for kindness, a point Xrn presses on him. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L447-448)
