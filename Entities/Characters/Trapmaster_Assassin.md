# Trapmaster Assassin
> Type: person | Also called: Sithorn; the older Trapmaster | First appears: Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations
> See also: Mire, Assassin's Guild, Ryoka Griffin, Fierre, Karsaeu-Dequoa
## Overview
- The Trapmaster Assassin is an older [Assassin] and [Trapmaster] of the Assassin's Guild who rides the Unmarked Coach and is a full-timer as a Face. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L443-444,L455)
- The Trapmaster Assassin is named Sithorn, the oldest of the [Assassins] in his group. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L200,L298)
- The Trapmaster Assassin leaves the coach and walks into the forest, vanishing out of sight and mind. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L443)
## Forest camp (Book 18)
- The Trapmaster Assassin finds two other Faces of the Assassin's Guild already camped among the trees, not full-timers but just as good. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L444)
- Mire asks about the Trapmaster's kids, and her own eldest is having trouble in his woodworking business. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L445-446)
- The Trapmaster Assassin says the Unmarked Coach is the way to travel and that he does not know how the other two stand to go on foot. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L449-450)
- The Trapmaster Assassin tells Mire he met a Vampire on his ride, new, who had a strong reaction to even a bit of garlic. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Carriages_and_Conversations L456-462)
## Ambush on Magnolia's carriage (Book 18)
- The Trapmaster Assassin is the white-haired man that Ryoka and Fierre had met, and is one of five Faces sent by the Assassin's Guild. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L188,L198)
- {event} The Trapmaster Assassin's wires between posts stop Magnolia Reinhart's pink carriage at full speed and hold, flipping it over. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L176-178)
- {event} The Trapmaster Assassin leaves cover with four other Assassins to approach the downed carriage. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L180)
- {event} The Trapmaster Assassin throws long mithril wires anchoring them in the air and uses [Create Field: The Steelthread Web]. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L198-202)
- {event} The Trapmaster Assassin gives Mire the wires and a glove for the combination Skill [The Web Closes], which Sithorn and Mire perform together. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L297-299)
- A warning is called that Ressa used to be a Face, and the Trapmaster eyes her before withdrawing his wires. (Book 18, Ch7.42M L197-198)
