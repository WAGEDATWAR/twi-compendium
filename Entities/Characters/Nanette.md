# Nanette
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Ch6.41E
> See also: Califor, Belavierr, Eloise, Hedag, Wiskeria, Pebblesnatch
## Overview
- Nanette is a young [Witch], Califor's apprentice, who got her class when she was six. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L859)
- Nanette says Califor raised her from a baby, and that she loves being a [Witch], which is all she ever wanted. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L808)
- Nanette is a girl almost as small as a Cave Goblin, with brown hair in braids, who wears a little blue hat. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1112,L1114; Book 12, Ch6.46E L481)
- Nanette needs eight tries to ignite sticks with the basic spell [Hot Hands] while Eloise prepares a second pot of tea. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L411,L418)
## Stories of Califor
- {belief} Nanette says Califor has talked down a rampaging Cyclops, out-stared a Gazer and made peace between Drakes and Gnolls. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L839)
- {belief} Nanette says Califor carries a Cloak of Balshadow, which can turn her as invisible as night, and a staff from one of the Great Forests. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L843)
- Nanette says Califor had a cottage by the High Passes, kept goats, and raised her there before they travelled. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1234)
- Nanette says she wandered off as a small child with a goat named Belfaus and Gargoyles took her to a cave, where one ate Belfaus. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1236-1240)
- Nanette says Califor found her in the cave, brought her back, and then taught her to use magic to defend herself. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1244-1248)
- Nanette says Califor still has a scar from that day, and that nobody knows how Califor defeated so many Gargoyles. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1248,L1253)
## Belavierr and Califor's death
- Nanette gives the burnt Belavierr a half-empty jar of burn cream that Califor made, and tips her hat to her. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L478-479)
- Belavierr pats Nanette on the head and weaves a brown ribbon into her cut braids, restoring them. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L480-484)
- Nanette cries "Mother" at the burning body of the dead [Witch] and tries to leap onto the pyre until Hedag seizes her. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L1090-1091)
- {state} Nanette loses her mother Califor, who dies stopping the wildfire. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L1089-1091; Book 12, Ch6.47E L10)
- {reveal} Califor was Nanette's mother by blood as well as by parenthood, and Hedag says Califor pretended otherwise to spare Nanette attention. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L10,L428)
- {reveal} Belavierr stole a strand or two of Nanette's hair when they met, and offered Califor her daughter's life or her own. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L419-421)
- The brown ribbon at Nanette's throat unravels and slithers back to Belavierr, who rides in wearing Califor's shape. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L1098-1107)
- Nanette sits silent in grief and does not leave the coven's place after learning Califor stopped the fire. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L9)
- Nanette crawls to the circle, takes Califor's charred hat from the altar and hugs it, saying "Mommy". (Book 12, Ch6.47E L878-881)
## In Riverfarm
- Eloise and Hedag say Nanette is alive and needs time, and that [Witches] do not run from their pain. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L975-983)
- Nanette sits blank and doll-like in the grass past Riverfarm's unfinished wall, with a basket of herbs and a sickle, while onlookers whisper "Witch". (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1113-1118)
- Eloise scolds Nanette about where to sit and escorts her back. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1119-1121)
- Children throw stones at Nanette and cut her cheek, and she does not move. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1159-1170)
- Nanette looks less blank for a moment and tips her hat to the Cave Goblin Pebblesnatch. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L1283-1286)
