# Feshi
> Type: person | Also called: Feshi Weatherfur | First appears: Ch6.23D
> See also: Wil, Venaz, Yerranola, Merrik, Peki, Talia Kallinad, Perorn
## Overview
- Feshi Weatherfur is a Gnoll of the Weatherfur tribe who, as her tribe's custom, dyes parts of her fur different colours. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L301)
- Feshi came to the Titan's Academy to become one of the world's best [Strategists], and is better at laying traps than anything else. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L301-302)
- Feshi says her tribe colours its fur because it knows where and when to send its large flocks and predicts the weather. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L366)
- Feshi has the [Shaman] class and boils water by closing her eyes over a pot. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L803-806)
- Feshi uses [Bursting Arrow] to detonate arrows in slugs. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L816)
- {belief} Feshi fears her people's hatred of the undead since the Necromancer slew the Great Chieftain Kerash. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1401)
