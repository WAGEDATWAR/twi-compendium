# Quarass of Germina
> Type: person | Also called: the Quarass; Dragonslayer of Ger | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.53K
> See also: Fetohep, Flos Reimarch, Trey Atwood, Raelt, Germina
## Overview
- The Quarass of Germina is the ruler of Germina, protector of Ger, and a Dragonslayer, one of the rulers of Chandrar's four Shield Kingdoms. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L378; Book 15, Ch7.09K L92)
- The Quarass dies and is found again as a new young girl, while her memories pass on to the next incarnation. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L483; Book 15, Ch7.09K L91)
- The current Quarass has a child's body of perhaps nine to thirteen, pale yellow eyes and black hair with a widow's peak. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L88)
- The Quarass's eyes look as old as forever, and her voice carries adult intelligence through a child's voice. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L486-487)
- The Quarass has lived eighty-seven lifetimes, some brief and some long. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L731)
- The Quarass says she chooses to be Quarass and give herself for Germina, though she could flee. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L731)
## Memories and Past Lives
- The Quarass remembers lifetimes upon lifetimes and can compare the best [Strategists] and builders of all ages. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L376-377)
- The Quarass remembers Reim's castle being built about fifteen hundred years ago, first as a keep. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L379)
- The Quarass ranks Drevish the [Architect] in her top twenty, probably the top ten, though the previous Quarass disliked his personality. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L380)
- The Quarass remembers her previous death, a woman who feared the King of Destruction, with red-gold hair and a sword. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L381)
- The Quarass says she was once a [Carpet Racer], a [Skyrider of Ger], and that she died at age thirty-eight flying a carpet into a Dragon's maw. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L301,L307)
- {belief} Fetohep says that when the world rose against Vampires, the Quarass was possessed by their power and her Champion of Ger sacrificed his life to wake her. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L62)
- {belief} Fetohep says she drank poison, poisoned the Vampires' bloodwells with her own ichor, and fought for hours until she died. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L62-63)
- {belief} Fetohep says the reborn Quarass led an army that staked every remaining Vampire and burned the rest from the toes up in molten stone. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L64)
- The Quarass says the current girl's only notable event was killing a viper with her father's dagger, and the viper bit her four times. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L808)
## Dealings
- The Quarass arrives at Reim with about forty Germina citizens, who were enemies of Flos months ago, and she greets Flos as the King of Destruction. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L481-485)
- The Quarass travels with sacred protectors, including a street-tough boy and a noblewoman, and her private entourage includes hidden [Assassins]. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L483; Book 15, Ch7.09K L87)
- Trey Atwood is a friend of the Quarass, who tolerates him on her trip. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L92)
- The Quarass flies a carpet with Trey and Gazi, and says most carpet riders die within a month. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L300-305)
- {event} The Quarass walks into Jecrass's palace sworn to peace by Ger and is besieged by servants seeking her advice. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L749-759)
- The Quarass tells King Raelt she comes in peace, not at the King of Destruction's command, and only seeks to rest an hour or two. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L763)
- {event} The Quarass tells Raelt she would have sent twenty thousand riders to raid and burn villages that did not declare against Flos. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L912-914)
- The Quarass advises Raelt to choose whichever answer he can live with. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L922)
- {event} The Quarass spots the adventurers' wagon from Medain, and her bodyguards kill the [Mage] and capture another adventurer. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L1119-1135)
- The Quarass cannot cast lightning magic in her current body. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L1129-1130)
- The Quarass orders a [Message] sent to Jecrass that their [Princess] has been kidnapped. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L1144)
