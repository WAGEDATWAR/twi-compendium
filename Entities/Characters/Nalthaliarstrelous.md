# Nalthaliarstrelous
> Type: person | Also called: Nathal (Magnolia's suggested short form; he refuses it) | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.20
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Mrsha, Teriarch, Shassa Weaverweb, Erin Solstice, Garden of Sanctuary, Druid (class)
## Overview
- Nalthaliarstrelous is a [Druid] with a tangled beard and ragged clothing stained with soil, and he smells earthen rather than filthy. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1369-1371)
- Nalthaliarstrelous carries a long curved staff that is still growing buds of flowers and leaves, with a bit of blood on the bottom. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L1371)
- Nalthaliarstrelous is introduced as the keeper of Magnolia Reinhart's gardens. (Book 18, ChInterlude_A_Meeting_of_Druids L572)
- Nalthaliarstrelous can shift into a hulking beast with envenomed claws and layered teeth, and later into a Dire Bear form. (Book 17, Ch7.28 L976-977; Book 18, ChInterlude_A_Meeting_of_Druids L1255-1257)
