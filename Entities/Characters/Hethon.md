# Hethon
> Type: person | Also called: Hethon Veltras; Lord Hethon; Lordling | First appears: Book 17, Ch7.24
> See also: Tyrion Veltras, Sammial Veltras, Ullim, Jericha, Eliasor Melissar, Circle of Thorns
## Overview
- Hethon is the elder son of Tyrion Veltras and is fourteen years old, described by others as studious, diligent and level-headed. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L470,L473)
- Hethon is interested in books and does not have his mother's eyes, instead having the eyes of Tyrion's father. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L405,L473)
- Hethon's mother Salva died four years before the lance practice, and Hethon and Tyrion are strangers to each other. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L370,L392)
- Hethon excitedly tells Ullim about hitting the hoop on his first try. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L408-409)
## Lance practice (Book 17)
- {event} Tyrion helps Hethon hit a hoop target on the practice dummy, using one Skill to improve aim while guiding the horse. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L372-387)
- {event} Hethon snaps the wooden hoop off the dummy on his first try, and Tyrion tells him it was not luck. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L394-403)
- Tyrion tells Hethon that he did very well, may have hidden talent as a lancer, and that it was exemplary work. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L407)
- Tyrion has the ring kept as a trophy, and Hethon beams at the applause of the Veltras folk. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L410-413)
- Tyrion says Hethon hit the target six times out of ten, even without his Skills. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L415-417)
- Ullim says Hethon's eyes are excellent and he will have the tutors focus on it in lessons. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L419)
- Hethon has not seen his friend Eliasor in a few months, whose mother was killed by [Assassins]. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L458)
## Illness (Book 19)
- Hethon hears Tyrion's account of the battle with the Assassin's Guild and listens agog while Tyrion downplays it. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L468)
- {state} Hethon falls ill ten minutes before an [Assassin] from the Assassin's Guild arrives, claiming a stomachache, and then does not respond. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L131-133)
- {state} Hethon and Sammial remain gravely ill, with Hethon's breathing shallow and laboured, while Tyrion sits with the blood contract. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L1336-1338,L1344-1346)
