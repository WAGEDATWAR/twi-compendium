# Cessic
> Type: person | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Pisces, BlackMage, Wistram
## Overview
- Cessic is a Lizardman new student at Wistram who gravitates toward Ceria, the half-Elf, in class. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L330)
- Cessic fans out his neck frills when he spots Ceria and Pisces, and tells them they are about to get their first class in destruction magic. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L334-335)
- Cessic is claustrophobic and paces in panic, hyperventilating, while the new students wait. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L397)
- Ceria tells Cessic to stop pacing and look out a window. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L398)
- Later, Cessic has rainbow scales, a bright red neck frill, a hat and a wand, and welcomes the new students to Wistram. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L83-84)
- Blackmage recognizes Cessic instantly when he strides in at the head of the new students. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L84)
