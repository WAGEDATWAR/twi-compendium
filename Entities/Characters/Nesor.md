# Nesor
> Type: person | Also called: Laken's [Mage] | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.35E
> See also: Laken, Rie, Wiskeria
## Overview
- Nesor is a young [Mage] who attended Wistram but failed to receive his mage certification. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L270)
- Nesor serves in Lady Rie's company and kneels beside her and Geram when she kneels to Laken. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L365)
## Role in Riverfarm
- Nesor is a poor caster, so it takes him laborious minutes to send Laken's words by [Message] to Wiskeria's [Mage]. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L405)
- Nesor is pale-faced and sweaty with nerves, and Laken makes him sit so he cannot faint. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L6)
- Nesor says he can send further [Message] spells if he has a mana potion. (Book 8, Ch5.22G L415-416)
- Rie says she will send [Message] spells to Invrisil with Nesor's help. (Book 7, Ch5.09E L146)
- Nesor attends Rie's meeting with Geram and is silent, since the magic discussed is beyond him. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L9)
- Nesor copies the [Message] Ryoka receives. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L42)
## The forged message
- Nesor is found among the older [Ladies] at Lady Bevia's house, who have stolen him away with their company, and Laken calls him back. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L372-377)
- Rie is mortified at how Nesor was sought out and promises to keep an eye on him. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L378-380)
- {event} A [Message] ordering the army to attack is sent in Laken's name, and Laken says he sent no [Message]. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L405)
- {event} Nesor is gone from Rie's cottage, which shows signs of a scuffle. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L412)
- {reveal} Laken finds Nesor unconscious with a lump on his head, dumped behind trees far outside the village, too late to stop the attack. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L412)
