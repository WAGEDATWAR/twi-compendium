# Colfa
> Type: person | Also called: Lady Colfa val Lischelle-Drakle | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.01
> See also: Fierre, Himilt, Bamer, Ryoka Griffin
## Overview
- Colfa introduces herself to Ryoka as Lady Colfa, Mistress of House Lischelle-Drakle. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L35)
- Colfa speaks in an archaic accent and plays at being Vampiric royalty at their first meeting. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L21)
- Colfa is Fierre's mother and Himilt's wife. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L226,L281)
## Background
- Colfa was Human until Himilt turned her, and she likes being a Vampire. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L349)
- Colfa married into the family and begged to be turned for years. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L351,L355)
- Fierre says her mother insists on calling the family Lischelle-Drakle because she thinks it sounds better than Drakle. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L351)
- Colfa was a [Shepherdess] of the Lischelle family, who are like royalty among herders, and gave away her levels and humanity to become a Vampire. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L22)
- Colfa knows [Breeders] and [Herders] across the continent, and her family can supply animals raised outside Izril. (Book 19, ChInterlude_Experiments_in_Golems L54)
## Powers and temper
- {event} Colfa tries to compel Ryoka three times to swear herself a thrall, and Ryoka resists each time. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L77-90)
- Colfa's eyes flash when she uses her compulsion, and she angrily puts all her energy into the third attempt. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L78,L89)
- Ryoka's anger at the claim of immortality brings a wind into the room, and she tells Colfa she has met true immortals. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L91-94)
- {belief} Fierre says that Colfa's claim of Vampire immortality is just what her mother says, and that Vampires die. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L337)
- Colfa has a rash that has lasted months, which Fierre links to the Vampires' sickness. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L332; Book 10, Ch6.01 L598)
- Colfa likes telling stories of grand Vampire castles and balls. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L226)
## Fierre's illness
- {event} Colfa grabs Ryoka by the neck, blaming her for Fierre's sickness after Fierre came home saying she had been cured. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L873-878)
- Colfa drops her accent and slams Ryoka into the stone wall of Fierre's room, claws digging into her flesh. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L22-26)
- Colfa insists that Vampires do not get infections. (Book 19, Ch7.45 L855)
