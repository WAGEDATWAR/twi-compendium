# Valceif
> Type: person | Also called: Valceif Godfrey; Val; Courier Valceif | First appears: Ch2.14
> See also: Ryoka Griffin, Hawk, Garia, Lady Magnolia Reinhart, Mihaela Godfrey
## Overview
- Valceif Godfrey is a Courier from First Landing, a tall and tanned Human with black hair and a crooked nose that was never set properly. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L314,L319; Book 2, Ch2.15 L218)
- Valceif, who goes by Val, is a confident man whose presence sets him apart from other Runners. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L314)
- Valceif says he earns forty gold coins per delivery, and up to one or two hundred for danger or speed. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L183)
## Arrival in Celum (Book 2)
- Valceif introduces himself to the Runner's Guild in Celum as a Courier from First Landing on a delivery to Liscor. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L319)
- Valceif says he has not stopped running since passing Invrisil. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L331)
- Valceif killed the bandits who attacked Garia and reported the incident to the local Watch. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L308,L325)
- Valceif uses [Double Step] to run at twice Ryoka's pace and is a hundred meters ahead in seconds. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L385-388)
- Valceif says he needs to reach Liscor by that night and wants to meet the Drake [Tactician] said to be the best chess player in Liscor. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L383,L390)
- Valceif delivers a package to Olesm, the best chess player in Liscor, as a Courier from First Landing. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L218-223)
- {event} Valceif gives Ryoka a small metal dreamcatcher charm, which he says dispels magic, worth several thousand gold, to free her from a spell. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L413-418)
- Valceif says he paid over a thousand gold coins for the charm, which Ryoka's use destroyed. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L145-147)
- Valceif waives repayment and asks Ryoka, from one Runner to another, whether it was worth it. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L148-152)
## Running with Ryoka
- Valceif was brought in because Hawk, the usual Courier near Liscor, was busy with the undead. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L195-198)
- Valceif avoids Skills when he can, because [Double Step] and [Quick Movement] tire him too much for everyday use. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L104)
- Valceif says the last Runner who helped him took two arrows pulling him to safety after a [Bandit Lord] jumped him. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L129)
- Valceif dislikes Crelers. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L139)
- Valceif says he has met other people who choose not to level and compensate with magic. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L167-170)
- Valceif carries a stone inscribed with glowing runes and calls Hawk to join the run. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L190-194)
- Valceif knows Lady Magnolia Reinhart, who greets him warmly as Valceif and has not seen him in two months. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L394-398)
- Valceif delivers Ryoka's folded letter to Magnolia in Invrisil, wondering what she will make of it. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L396-401)
## Death
- {state} A Celum receptionist tells Ryoka that Valceif is dead, killed two weeks earlier on a delivery. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L442)
- {reveal} Surviving bandits say they used a [Sleep] spell on the road to take him unawares, which is rare for a Courier who normally has wards. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L451)
- {reveal} Ryoka admits that Valceif's dreamcatcher could have shielded him from spells, and that she broke it. (Book 4, Ch3.37 L459)
- {event} Mihaela Godfrey says Val is buried on the Courier's Road, and that he will be there forever. (Book 19, Ch7.44 L823)
