# Rievan Forstrom
> Type: person | Also called: Rievan | First appears: Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2
> See also: Ceria Springwalker, Pisces, Wistram
## Overview
- Rievan Forstrom is a tall, dark-haired senior [Mage] in robes who runs the new students' magic lesson at Wistram. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L9,L22)
- Rievan teaches the Magical Foundations class, which Ceria and Pisces take as probationary students. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L29)
- Rievan teaches basic or pure magic, covering telekinesis, magical composition and spellcraft, a branch most mages find boring. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L35-37)
- Rievan seems to have it out for Ceria, who suspects he is a Human from Terandria who dislikes half-Elves. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L29-30)
- Rievan snaps at Ceria that her [Ice Shard] mixes the cold and water aspects and that she freezes water instead of forming a shape and solidifying it. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L9-10)
- Rievan evaporates Ceria's [Ice Shard] with heat from his hand. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L11)
- Rievan has taught the whole class [Electric Jolt], but insists anyone who knows a higher-Tier spell practice that instead. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L39)
- Rievan expects Pisces to make a mistake with his [Electric Jolt], but nods with a hint of respect at the immaculate control. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L52-54)
- Rievan tells the class to emulate Pisces's way of diverting the whole spell into his other palm. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L54)
- Rievan asks Pisces to repeat his statement on the dangers of mana overabundance. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L228)
- Rievan stares hard as Pisces gives a perfect summary of the previous day's lesson, then grudgingly says "Very good". (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L230-233)
- Rievan scolds a student named Elkrin for an abysmal [Fire Orb] and tells Ceria to attend to detail. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L234)
