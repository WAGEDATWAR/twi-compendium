# Dawson
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.04D
> See also: Daly, Geneva, Siri, Bushrangers
## Overview
- Dawson is an Australian Human from Earth who is one of the Bushrangers, and a friend of Daly's from home. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L24,L227)
- Dawson dons mud camouflage and jungle makeup so that only his eyes show. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L227)
- Dawson shares a movie night with Siri and other Bushrangers, and Siri elbows him when he asks about The Hobbit. (Book 10, Ch6.04D L736-741)
## Early days and the rappelling accident
- Daly says that on their first day in a big city, Dawson paid eight silver coins for coloured alchemy water. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L156)
- {state} Dawson falls when his gri-gri belay device snaps during a rappel from the trees. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L36-38,L81)
- Dawson dislocates his shoulder and twists his right leg in the fall. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L40,L81)
- Dawson insists that Geneva set his shoulder rather than have a healing potion poured on him. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L92-93)
- Dawson later says Doctor Geneva set his arm and fixed his foot, and that he needs two days of rest. (Book 11, Ch6.20D L276)
