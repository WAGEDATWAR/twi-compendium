# Cade
> Type: person | Also called: none | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.15R
> See also: Briganda, Griffon Hunt, Halrac, Mrsha, Grev
## Overview
- Cade is the young son of Briganda, a [Shield Maiden] of Griffon Hunt, and looks like his mother, with a head of dark violet hair and fair skin. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L641,L664,L727)
- Cade is about four years old, and talks, which he had not done when Revi last saw him. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L641,L649)
- Briganda had Cade in the course of being an adventurer, and did not notice she was pregnant until close to delivery. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L664,L668)
- Briganda has no idea who Cade's father is. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L695)
## Book 16
- Cade points out that Revi has threads in her face, and asks why she has them in her neck. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L642-650)
- Cade keeps himself entertained with a magical box of simulacra that releases an illusory Dragon, which perches on his hand. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L655-657)
- Briganda says the toy cost a fortune but is the only thing that keeps Cade's attention while she works. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L662)
- While travelling with Griffon Hunt, Briganda kept Cade in the care of [Carers] and [Nursemaids], and left him at safer places. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L665,L722)
- Briganda says Cade has tried to climb off the balcony more than once. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L733)
- Briganda says she will not leave Cade again, because he nearly forgot she was his mother, and makes taking him along a deal breaker for rejoining Griffon Hunt. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L724,L733)
- Cade looks confused when Briganda hugs Revi and Typhenous after agreeing to rejoin Griffon Hunt. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L786-789)
## Book 17
- Cade's box makes illusory [Knights] that chase Goblins around a table, and he glares at Halrac when Halrac taps the box and makes them vanish. (Book 17, Ch7.20 L611-620)
- {event} Cade, apparently forgotten by Briganda, wanders into The Wandering Inn through the door, and Grev asks Mrsha to help find his mother. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L456-458)
- Cade cries in the inn until Grev introduces Mrsha, and he stops crying as Mrsha takes him and Grev into the Garden of Sanctuary. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L465,L472)
- Cade screams when he sees Numbtongue and runs into a burr-bush, and Numbtongue picks him up to pull the burrs off. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L501-508)
- Briganda charges Numbtongue with her axe and buckler when she hears Cade screaming, and crashes into the barrier of the Garden of Sanctuary. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L513,L519,L524)
- Lyonette serves Cade a smiling egg-and-bacon breakfast at The Wandering Inn. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L256,L260)
- Briganda says she might stay behind at the inn because Cade needs his mother. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L270)
