# Nemor
> Type: person | Also called: Great Mage Nemor | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.13E
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Ressa, Circle of Thorns
## Overview
- Nemor is a Great Mage who once cast ward spells for Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L21)
- Nemor wears yellow robes with green slashes and has a groomed goatee. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L6,L16)
## Attack on Magnolia's estate
- {event} Nemor blasted the wall of Magnolia's sitting room and announced that the Circle of Thorns seeks her life. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L591-599)
- Magnolia and Ressa stood untouched inside a glowing shield while assassins emerged from the dark. (Book 3, Ch3.13E L595,L603)
- Nemor walked across the courtyard wreathed in a gold and sapphire shield. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L74)
- Nemor cast a blue fireball with a white-hot center. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L97)
- {belief} Nemor claims the Circle of Thorns has roots everywhere, even in Magnolia's own home. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L92)
- Magnolia says Nemor bypassed her wards, with assassins, and subverted control spells for her Golems. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L106)
- {state} Nemor dies when Magnolia fires a thin line of light from her ring that expands into a pillar of fire. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L115-130)
