# Emme
> Type: person | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.04
> See also: Players of Celum, Wesle, Jasi, Kilkran, Erin Solstice
## Overview
- Emme is the stage manager and later [Director] of the Players of Celum. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L202; Book 14, Ch6.63P L53)
- Emme is a short, heavy woman with Dwarf blood, shorter than anyone else in her troupe. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L202,L207; Book 14, Ch6.63P L53)
- Emme is beardless, and says she is relieved not to have the bushy beard some Dwarven descendants grow. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L226)
- Emme often played the lower half of an animal or a moving prop, since few parts suit her height. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L207)
## Events
- Emme plays Olaf the Snow Golem in a costume during the troupe's performance at the Wandering Inn. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L241)
- Emme does not rise to the bait when Drakes in the front row cheer against the Players of Celum. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L168)
- Emme rules that the rewritten scripts are used for most plays and the original text only sparingly. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L85)
- Emme dreams of having the Players perform for nobility, where they would earn more money. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L388)
- Emme agrees a restaurant deal at forty-sixty of the takings after the owner's ingredients. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L675-679)
- Emme challenges rival Men of Invrisil [Actors] to recite a Hamlet soliloquy, and Kilkran, Yimur and Pralcem each perform part of it. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L688-700)
- The Men of Invrisil cannot do the same and retreat while the crowd cheers the Players. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L701)
- Emme mutters that the riots may smash up the Solstice Theatre, after the Players' performance is cancelled. (Book 18, Ch7.31 L39,L95)
