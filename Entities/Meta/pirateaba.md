# pirateaba
> Type: meta (real-world author) | Also called: the author; Author | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.07
> See also: Andrea Parsneau
> Note: pirateaba is the real-world author of The Wandering Inn, not an in-world character. These claims come from the e-book Author's Notes and from two meta references in the text.
## Overview
- pirateaba is the name under which the author signs the Author's Notes of the e-books. (Book 3, ChMeta_B03_Authors_Note L7)
- pirateaba is described as an anonymous writer who has written ten million words in the ongoing web serial The Wandering Inn. (Book 8, ChMeta_B08_About_the_Author L2)
- Less flattering rumors say pirateaba is three Goblins in a trench coat, a stupid artificial intelligence, or a creature with too many hands. (Book 8, ChMeta_B08_About_the_Author L2)
- pirateaba likes to write, plays video games, eats popcorn with nutritional yeast and compares the readerbase to the endless quacking of ducks. (Book 8, ChMeta_B08_About_the_Author L2)
- pirateaba's identity is anonymous at the time of the Book 11 note, and pirateaba has not attended any conventions. (Book 11, ChMeta_B11_Authors_Note L4)
- In Book 2, Erin and Ryoka joke that "pirateaba" is obviously a fake name while drafting a petition. (Book 2, Ch2.07 L64)
- In the same scene Erin and Ryoka argue over whether the author is a she, a he or an it, and settle on writing "pirateaba". (Book 2, Ch2.07 L86-90)

## Writing process and team
- pirateaba says the author is better at writing new chapters than at revising, fixing typos and releasing the old volumes as e-books. (Book 3, ChMeta_B03_Authors_Note L2)
- pirateaba says e-books also allow the audiobooks to be made. (Book 3, ChMeta_B03_Authors_Note L2)
- pirateaba writes most days of the week, treating the story as something of a live production. (Book 6, ChMeta_B06_Authors_Note L4)
- pirateaba says Book 7 was written without any support group beyond online typo-finders. (Book 7, ChMeta_B07_Authors_Note L4)
- pirateaba says a team of beta-readers and editors has since developed to catch spelling mistakes and wrong names and to help with revisions. (Book 7, ChMeta_B07_Authors_Note L5-6)
- pirateaba says finding people to help with the writing is the biggest change in the writing process over time. (Book 7, ChMeta_B07_Authors_Note L6)
- pirateaba jokes about not having any handy Skills to level up as a writer. (Book 7, ChMeta_B07_Authors_Note L6)
- pirateaba says that Volume 8 is over a million words long and could become eight books. (Book 8, ChMeta_B08_Authors_Note L3)
- pirateaba notes that the narrator Andrea Parsneau does not want to record a single million-word audiobook. (Book 8, ChMeta_B08_Authors_Note L3)
- pirateaba says nine books of The Wandering Inn correspond exactly to five volumes, split because a million words cannot be offloaded into one e-book. (Book 9, ChMeta_B09_Authors_Note L2)
- pirateaba says Andrea Parsneau could not be made to read a million words in one go. (Book 9, ChMeta_B09_Authors_Note L2)
- pirateaba is rewriting Volume 1 and writing Gravesong, whose audiobook should come out soon, alongside the serial. (Book 11, ChMeta_B11_Authors_Note L5)
- pirateaba plans to rest for a month after the year ends, then come back to write more. (Book 11, ChMeta_B11_Authors_Note L7)
- pirateaba says Book 12 was revised extensively to fix characterization and add gravitas, and the revisions took one month because of a scheduling mistake. (Book 12, ChMeta_B12_Authors_Note L4-5)
- pirateaba thanks Diana Gill especially for helping to improve the story. (Book 12, ChMeta_B12_Authors_Note L6)
- pirateaba likes [Witches] as a class and says the originals of the revised Book 12 chapters may go on the author's website. (Book 12, ChMeta_B12_Authors_Note L7-8)
- pirateaba says the first week of a less writing-intensive schedule began in April 2024, meant to give more energy to write better. (Book 13, ChMeta_B13_Authors_Note L2-4)
- pirateaba likes the cover of Book 13. (Book 13, ChMeta_B13_Authors_Note L7)
