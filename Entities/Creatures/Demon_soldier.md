# Demon soldier
> Type: creature | Also called: Demon soldiers | First appears: Book 2, Ch1.01C
> See also: Demons, Rhir, Erille
## Overview
- Demon soldiers are fighters of the Demons, and one wears leather armor and has a strange, half-melted-looking face. (Book 2, Ch1.01C L170-171)
## Appearance
- A Demon soldier has a face like leather, two horns curving downwards and bright green eyes. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L538)
- A Demon soldier has no whites in his eyes, only green sclera with yellow diamond-shaped pupils, and cloven feet. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L548,L571)
## Encounters
- {event} The narrator finds himself standing in a ring of dead Demon soldiers, and the last one flinches when the narrator turns to him, and he dies with the word "Monster". (Book 2, Ch1.01C L167-174)
- {event} A Demon soldier with a bloody sword slashes the Fool's belly open, picks up a key near a second gutted body in armor, unlocks the door the Fool was guarding and rushes in at Princess Erille. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L539-544,L554-556)
- A second Demon soldier with two knives attacks the narrator, and they fight on the floor of the room. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L566-571)
