# Hobgoblin
> Type: creature | Also called: Hob; Hobgoblins | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00G
> See also: Goblins, Rags, Durene
## Overview
- Hobgoblins are Goblins that enter a sudden growth spurt, begin eating twice their weight each day, and grow constantly until they are as tall as Humans and in some cases twice as wide. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L145)
- The Goblins who become Hobgoblins are Chieftains or Goblins stronger, faster or tougher than normal. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L145)
- Hobgoblins are the leaders of tribes, the shock troopers of a Goblin group and the only way Goblins can take on deadly monsters. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L146)
- Most Goblin tribes have around one Hobgoblin, and the Flooded Waters tribe's single Hob was their Chief until Erin killed him. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L146)
- Rags has leveled as much as any Hob but has not grown bigger, which sometimes happens. (Book 2, Ch2.00G L144,L147)
## Book 5
- {event} In the attack on Durene's village a Hobgoblin, round and made of muscle and taller than anyone but Durene, smashes the wooden barricades with brute strength and cuts down a Celestial Tracker. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L245-246)
- The Hobgoblin fights Durene with a sword and a shield and moves with trained grace, and he wounds her deeply on the side and along one breast. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L303-304)
- {event} Durene lets his sword bite into her left arm and shatters his shield, arm and head with her club, killing him. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L305-306)
