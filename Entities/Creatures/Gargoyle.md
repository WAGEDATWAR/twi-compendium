# Gargoyle
> Type: creature | Also called: Gargoyles | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.08R
> See also: High Passes, Eater Goats, Carn Wolves, Redfang Tribe
## Overview
- Gargoyles are stone monsters that hide among rocks in the High Passes and were named as the most prevalent type of monster there. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L148-150,L155)
- A gargoyle looks vaguely like a bird crossed with an ogre, with a curved beak-mouth and skin that looks like stone but moves like flesh. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L159,L163)
- Gargoyles unfurl massive stone wings; they cannot really fly, being heavy, but they can angle a leap from a cliff to land like bombs. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L155,L180)
- Gargoyles spit stone fragments that cut like shotgun fire, and their roar makes rocks tumble down. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L163-165)
## Behaviour and encounters
- Ryoka ran into a nest of gargoyles in the High Passes while wolves and goats chased her, and the gargoyles tore the goats apart and ate them. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L155-159)
- Gargoyles have a sense of smell: they retreated from Ryoka after she covered herself in a terrible stench. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L221-222)
- The Redfang Goblins lure Gargoyles by tying an Eater Goat to a pole, and they fight Gargoyles in the passes. (Book 8, Ch5.36 L248; Book 9, Ch5.58 L65)
- The Redfangs plan to push Gargoyles out of territory in the High Passes as part of their expansion. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L137)
- A man named Ambriel said Gargoyles followed Eater Goats and troubled his sister's village near Orefell, eating half a flock, and Ylawes said the Adventurer's Guild had a bounty on them. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L644-651)
- Six Gargoyles attacked Falene, Dawil and Ylawes; they were huge, as tall as small trees, with sensitive eyes and orange-red blood. (Book 10, Ch6.16 L781-785)
