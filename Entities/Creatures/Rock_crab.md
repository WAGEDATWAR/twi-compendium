# Rock-crab
> Type: creature | Also called: Rock Crab; Rock Crabs; Hollowstone Deceiver; Hollowstone Deceivers | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.02
> See also: Floodplains, Seed cores, Goblins
## Overview
- Rock-crabs are giant land crustaceans that hide under a rock-like shell, shuffle at prey, and make loud clicking sounds; the name Hollowstone Deceiver is what local people call them. (Book 1, Ch1.02 L149,L153-154; Book 1, Ch1.23 L106-107)
- Rock-crabs have two enormous claws and a pair of dark antennae that may be their eyes, and are longer and more elongated at the back than a round crab. (Book 1, Ch1.02 L149; Book 15, ChStory_1_Mrsha_and_Relc L36)
- Rock Crabs are immune to most acid, according to Erin's reckoning, and she thought one could level her inn. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L160-161)
## Encounters
- Erin threw a seed core at a rock-crab's antennae, and it panicked, scraped at its antennae and made a loud cricket-like noise while Erin retreated. (Book 1, Ch1.02 L150-155)
- Erin later pasted two rock-crabs with the slimy seed cores, and they retreated with loud clicks. (Book 1, Ch1.08 L36-37)
- A rock crab at her inn swiped at Erin and tried to grab her head, and she smashed it with a chair; Goblins hunted and ate one, ripping the shell apart for its entrails. (Book 1, Ch1.18 L35-36; Book 1, Ch1.23 L26-41)
- Selys warned that a Hollowstone Deceiver sneaks up on adventurers outside, is silent in the rain, and hunts aggressively in spring. (Book 7, Ch5.03 L352)
- {event} A baby Rock Crab uncovered by Relc outside Liscor was twice Mrsha's size when newly born and had no rock shell yet; the rainy season let Rock Crabs spawn a brood. (Book 15, ChStory_1_Mrsha_and_Relc L36-38)
## The Earthers' hunt
- {event} Six rocks stood up and stalked seven Earthers in the Floodplains, while Gnoll [Hunters] normally avoid the telltale rocks; the Earthers did not know they were Rock Crabs. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L933-934,L944,L952-954)
- Erin drove the crabs back with seed cores, but they turned on her when she ran out of cores, and her aura failed to keep a crab away. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L973-979,L985-989)
- Bird shot a [Ricochet Arrow] from the tower through a crab's exposed antennae, and Halrac's glowing arrow detonated under another crab's shell, killing it. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L991-994,L1004)
- Ceria raised ice walls, a [Shatterbolt] broke a shell, Ksmvr fired crossbow bolts, and Grimalkin punched a third crab; Typhenous's orb finished it. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L1006-1010)
- The three remaining Rock Crabs fled from the inn's defenders. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L1011)
