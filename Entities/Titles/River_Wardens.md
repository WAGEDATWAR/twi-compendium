# River Wardens
> Type: title | Also called: River Warden; Warden | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.15K
> See also: Jecrass, Raelt, General Lael, Prime Minister
## Overview
- River Warden is the name for the nobility of Jecrass, who control the water in the kingdom's sprawling plains and pastures. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L49-50; Book 15, Ch7.08K L117)
- The River Wardens are heirs to land that holds water, and anyone who owns a stream can build farmland, raise animals and dole out the resource. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L50)
- King Raelt privately calls them River Robbers, because it is hard to tell a [Lord] from a [Bandit Leader]. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L50)
- The River Wardens are peers of the realm, below only Raelt in stature, and they can hold votes and drag their feet or oppose his decisions even though he is a [King]. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L48; Book 15, Ch7.08K L128)
## Politics
- Raelt hates his River Wardens, oranges and horses, even though Jecrass has all three in profusion. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L200)
- Lyfelt remarked that the River Wardens could be shortsighted, and that half of the minor parties in his own parliament were the same. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L198)
- The River Wardens argue over their horse breeds versus Terandrian ones but cannot consider the King of Destruction for ten minutes without gossiping; when asked what happens if he wins, they fall silent. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L118-123)
- Raelt told Warden Dulfe that his people might leave his duchy to join Flos's empire because he had ignored Raelt's demand to improve living and working conditions. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L51)
- The River Wardens were annoyed by the caravans of Flos's people passing through their borders and forgot Raelt's decree to aid them. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L184)
- General Lael, who is not a River Warden, told them that Jecrass's armies could not triumph over Reim's, and Raelt told her to let the Wardens stew on the thought of an invasion for another week while she prepared. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L134-137,L191)
