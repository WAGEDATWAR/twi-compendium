# Bastion-General
> Type: title | Also called: none | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Bastion-General Quiteil, Bastion-General Zavarial, Blighted Kingdom, Cirille
## Overview
- Bastion-General is the rank of the officer who commands one of the Walls of Rhir; Bastion-General Quiteil is described as the person in charge of the entire 4th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L702)
- Subordinate commanders such as Cirille address a Bastion-General by that title and authenticate with codes over a speaking stone. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L686,L692,L703)
- Bastion-General Zavarial commands 5th Wall, and Bastion-General Quiteil can place divisions under him. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L749; Book 18, Ch7.35C L177)

- Zavarial and Quiteil each report to the Blighted King's command and use speaking stones and authentication codes. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L686; Book 18, Ch7.35C L156-159)
