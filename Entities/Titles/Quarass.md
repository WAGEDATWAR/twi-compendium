# Quarass
> Type: title | Also called: The Quarass; the Quarass of Germina | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.06KM
> See also: Fetohep, Flos of Reim, Trey Atwood, Germina
## Overview
- The Quarass is the ruler of Germina, a position revered and acknowledged across Chandrar, and one of four leaders counted among the Shield Kingdoms of eastern Chandrar. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L377)
- Bethal explains that the Quarass and the other Shield Kingdoms were each once powerful empires, capable of challenging Dragons. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L377)
- [Quarass] is a unique class, and the first Skill its holder obtains is [Memory of the Quarass]. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L365)
## Rebirth in a child
- {reveal} A pale-yellow-eyed girl of no more than ten is presented to the crowd as the Quarass; the [Councilor] doubts she can be the ruler until she wakes from a collapse with the old presence in her gaze. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L159-160,L183-188)
- {state} The crowd, including sixteen men, kneels and proclaims that the Quarass lives, and the girl is accepted as the Quarass of Germina. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L185-188)
## The previous Quarass
- The previous Quarass remembers her own death as a woman who feared the King of Destruction enough to risk everything to kill him, and the face of her death was Flos with red-gold hair and a sword. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L381-382)
- The previous Quarass was a foolish, vain woman bitter at kneeling to Flos Reimarch, who had poisoned her mother to take the throne and then learned her mother's contempt for her. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L396)
- {belief} The current Quarass calls her predecessor a fool and a matricide who was forced to live through slaying herself, and she says she herself will be one of the greatest Quarasses. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L557)
- {belief} Meeting other rulers, the Quarass admits her predecessor was a fool, says she is not that woman, and says it was good she died before more could be ruined. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L879,L885)
- The other rulers are reserved with the Quarass, and Baosar says a kinkiller and matricide was a poor Quarass. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L883,L886)
## The current Quarass in Book 13
- The current Quarass is a girl barely thirteen who nevertheless counts as a ruler beside Flos. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L388)
- She can recall being a man in a number of her lives. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L397)
- Flos tells her that Trey and Teres come from another world, and she says she has never heard of such a thing in all her lifetimes. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L451-454)
- {event} She examines Trey's iPhone and accepts it as proof that the twins come from another world. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L565)
- She says plastic has never been made in any of her lifetimes and judges it weaker than metal. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L568-572)
- She cannot cast the Tier 5 spell [Pestilence's Touch] in her young body, since she has little magic now. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L682-693)
- She remembers fighting monsters but says her body cannot lift a sword properly or kill a single adult, because the knowledge of her body is gone. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L693-695)
- {belief} She says undead are not farmed elsewhere because the undead crave death, and necromancers could lose control of them to other undead. (Book 13, Ch6.54K L838)
- {belief} She says the Siren of Savere may be a sign of the resurgence of an old breed of [Bandit Lady], which she has seen rule many kingdoms in times past. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L302)
## With Fetohep
- The Quarass has dined with Fetohep across eight lifetimes and orders the same first dish each visit: roasted eels, white grape juice, and eight glossberries sprinkled with nali powder on crushed ice. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L157,L196)
- She says eels and glossberries were her favourite dishes in past lives and her happiest meals, so the memory makes her seek them out each time. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L198)
- {event} She bets Fetohep that the Antinium will lead an attack within six years, expecting the Drakes to hold back after losing the Tidebreaker. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L243)
- {event} Fetohep bets that the Antinium will be attacked within six years by Drakes or another side; they settle on four hundred pounds of Khelt's gold against sixteen assassinations by Germina's highest-leveled [Assassins], Level 30 at minimum. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L245,L248-255)
- {rel} Fetohep notes that the previous Quarass lost two bets to him across two incarnations. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L257)
- She tells Trey that she failed to convince Fetohep to offer Flos something of value, since he would rather use force than seem Reim's ally, and she plans to use Trey's device to sway his vanity. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L572-580)
## Training Trey
- The Quarass gets back a flying carpet from Germina's treasury, which Flos's armies looted when they took Ger and which he returned in exchange for her aid. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L123)
- She has not flown in forty-two years and is still adjusting to the carpet. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L149)
- She keeps a double, a little girl with yellow eyes, to show her face once a day in Ger or Reim in her absence. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L104-105)
- {event} Trey shows her [Light Arrow], taught to him by Gazi, [Sand Wall] and [Featherfall]; she corrects his mana control twice and says King Reimarch intends her to help shape his class. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L365-379)
- {event} She asks to see his Skill [Basic Golem Shaping] and watches his sand golems, noting his [Magical Specialization: Sand]. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L382-393)
- {event} As training, the Quarass cuts Trey's throat with a ring-blade of the kind used by Germinan [Assassins], then forces him to move a golem covered in his own blood and sand, calling the result lifesand and the foundation for his golems. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L1091-1096,L1104-1112,L1121)
- She heals Trey with a potion afterwards, and he shouts that he hates her and wants to leave. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L1114-1116,L1131)
