# Archmages
> Type: title | Also called: Archmage; Archmagus; Wistram Archmages | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.35
> See also: Tier Magic, Cleric
## Overview
- Krshia tells Erin that archmages are the rarest of spellcasters, and that she knows of only a few and none on this continent. (Book 1, Ch1.35 L102)
- {belief} Krshia says there are several archmages: one in the Human lands to the north, two on other continents, and at least three with homes in Wistram, the Isle of Mages. (Book 1, Ch1.35 L105)
- Pisces recalls the saying that a [Cleric] in the earliest levels could do what an [Archmage] could not. (Book 1, Ch1.34 L107)
## Book 17 and 18
- By Book 17, Wistram counts six great [Mages] of Archmage rank: Terandria has two, Izril one, Chandrar one and Baleros three living Archmages, with none from Rhir. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L5-6)
- Archmage Amerys is no longer numbered among them. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L7)
- Izril's sole Archmagus lives on an island and seldom interferes in her home's affairs. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L8)
- Archmagus Verdan Blackwood, a Human raised by Dullahans, is old and effectively retired from Wistram politics. (Book 17, ChInterlude_The_Gecko_of_Illusions L9)
- Feor is a white-bearded half-Elf Archmage, head of the Centrists; Viltach is a Human Archmage who leads the Libertarians; and Nailihuaile is an Archmage with a staff. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L25,L214,L798,L804,L841)
- When the three confronted Amerys, she reportedly called down a storm of lightning bolts over Wistram's exterior. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L799)
- {event} Feor, Viltach and Naili show Aaron Vanwell paintings of the interiors of the Antinium Hives and vote not to send the images to the Drakes, preferring to keep observing until they have something worth more. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L800-804,L839-845)
- The Archmages say Wistram's access to the Hives runs both ways and that they have limited their observation after rumors began to spread. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L809)
- Wistram's higher floors are sealed behind the Truestone Golem Cognita's test, and she has killed every [Mage] who attempted to challenge her. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L750-751)
