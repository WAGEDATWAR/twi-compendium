# Revalantor
> Type: title | Also called: none | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.11
> See also: Klbkch, Prognugator, Antinium, Pivr, Free Antinium
## Overview
- Revalantor is an Antinium rank that stands a step above Prognugator; Klbkch is the Revalantor of the Free Antinium in Liscor's Hive. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L5; Book 2, Ch2.27 L51)
- The Revalantor is technically above Prognugator and could ignore even the Queen's instructions, such was the nature of his duties. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L5)
- Within his Hive, a Revalantor walks in a bubble of his own, and no one would get in his way. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L51)
- Klbkch had become Revalantor only a few weeks earlier, and was expected to report less to his Queen and work more with Pawn and the new Individuals. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L7)
## Klbkch as Revalantor
- Ksmvr became the new Prognugator in the position Klbkch once held, and Klbkch as Revalantor is famous among the Antinium. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L233)
- Pawn noted that the Hive now had a Revalantor who also acted as a Prognugator in the form of Klbkch. (Book 3, Ch3.05L L249)
- The Revalantor monitors the new Individuals, and Pawn was told to report changes in his level or class to Klbkch. (Book 3, Ch3.06L L55)
- The Revalantor Klbkch instructed the Antinium to rebuild Erin's inn, in return for letting Antinium visit the inn to learn chess. (Book 2, Ch2.11 L200,L206)
- Revalantor Pivr of the Flying Antinium visited Liscor's Hive with the leaders of other Hives and questioned the Individuals, speaking in his role as Revalantor for his own Queen. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L373,L375)
