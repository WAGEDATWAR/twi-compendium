# Desk Sergeant
> Type: title | Also called: none | First appears: Book 15, Ch7.02
> See also: Kel, Senior Guardsman, Watch
## Overview
- A Desk Sergeant is a position, not a rank, in a Watch House, and it is temporary; a Senior Guard can fill the role and often does. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L7)
- The Desk Sergeant manages the Watch House and the patrols coming in when the Watch Captains on duty are not present. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L6)
- At Pallass's 8th floor Watch House, the Desk Sergeant on duty was Kel, to whom Patrol Leader Tanic reported. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L7-8,L37)
- Desk Sergeant Kel said it was within his authority as Desk Sergeant to let Erin walk free for her accident, and was enjoying the moment. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L95-96)
- During the Wyvern attack, Desk Sergeant Kel led the 8th Floor's defenders and saluted Olesm when Olesm assumed command of the sector. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L457,L524-525,L539)
- A Drake Desk Sergeant waved Lyonette up to see the Watch Captain after she slipped him a tart. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L332)
