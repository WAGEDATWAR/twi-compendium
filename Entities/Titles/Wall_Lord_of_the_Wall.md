# Wall Lord of the Wall
> Type: title | Also called: Wall Lord; Wall Lady; Holders of the Wall; Lords of the Wall; Children of the Walls | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.43
> See also: Ilvriss, Salazsar, Walled Cities, Ilvriss's ring
## Overview
- {belief} Ilvriss says that a Wall Lord's lineage extends back to the age of Ancestors and Dragons, that they built the Walled Cities with the Dragons' help, and that their role is to lead and inspire Drakes and defend their home. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L378)
- Magnolia says Zel deserves as many artifacts as a Lord of the Wall, and Zel scoffs at wearing a hundred rings and amulets at all times. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L369-370)
## The Holders of the Wall of Salazsar
- {event} The Holders of the Wall of Salazsar gather in a room that only those whose hearts belong to Salazsar can stand in, and they present rings of copper and brass that turn gold and glow. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L799-800)
- Wall Lady Calistoca, the oldest of those gathered, presides and opens by naming complacency, noting that an Antinium called Bird the Hunter appeared on the walls of Pallass. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L798-803)
- The room is divided into camps: Ilvriss, a traditionalist, sits opposite his sister Navine Gemscale. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L760,L805)
- Wall Lord Brilm, a continual drinker and collector of what is new and trending, offers Ilvriss support for his plan. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L764,L770-772)
- Wall Lord Tasilt, who married a Gnoll, reads the plan, doubts it at first, and then agrees to talk with Ilvriss over a drink. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L776,L784-796)
- Ilvriss tells Tasilt his plan is the chance to create a unified force of all the Walled Cities, to replace what Zel Shivertail was. (Book 15, Ch7.04 L791)
- {belief} Brilm tells Alrric that the other Children of the Walls wonder what activated Ilvriss's rings, and guesses the recipient is a Gnoll, a Human or a Selphid, and that Ilvriss will marry. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L516,L533-541)
