# Dragonspeaker
> Type: title | Also called: Dragonspeaker of Manus | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.33E
> See also: Luciva Skybreath, Manus, Rafaema, Wall Lords
## Overview
- Dragonspeaker is a title in Manus, held by Luciva Skybreath as Dragonspeaker of Manus. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L33)
- The Dragonspeaker carries the glaive known in Manus as the Fang of Manus, whose old first name is Parentkiller, and those who wield it hold it as a symbol of authority. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L30-31)
- The Dragonspeaker presides over a council of Wall Lords and Ladies of Manus, where she hears opinions, asks the youngest member Rafaema for her thoughts, and signs and stamps decisions with her ring. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L33-35,L80,L94)
- Luciva is a blue-scaled Drake with white and yellow in her scales, and her heritage is among the purest of all Drakes. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L32; Book 15, Ch7.03 L170)
- Ilvriss named the Dragonspeaker of Manus among the big names he wished to alert about the Necromancer once he was sure she was not under his control. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L493-494)
## Role
- The Dragonspeaker sat down with the Lords and Ladies of the Wall to discuss the Siege of Liscor, the battle at the Bloodfields and the ambitions of Tyrion Veltras. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L33-34)
- Luciva wore white in mourning, as the reinforcements from Manus in the battle had cost her everything. (Book 12, Ch6.33E L33,L45)
- When Wyverns attacked Pallass, Dragonspeaker Luciva watched the scrying orb in Manus's inner keep and led the war room's response. (Book 15, Ch7.03 L82,L101,L113)
