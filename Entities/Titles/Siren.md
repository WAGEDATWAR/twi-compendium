# Siren
> Type: title | Also called: Siren of Savere; Siren of Tides | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.15K
> See also: Revine, Savere, Drowned People, Nsiia, Yisame
## Overview
- Siren is the title of the ruler of Savere, a nation of Drowned People and chaotic warriors, which Raelt thinks of as a unique country with a long history and culture. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L4,L425-426)
- Revine is the Siren of Savere; she calls water and rain with her hands, and the waters formed a path in the sands as she walked. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L423,L427,L433)
- Revine is also known as the Siren of Tides or, unofficially, one of the [Bandit Queens] that plague the world. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L760)
- Savere is described as home to ships and theft from every part of the world. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L503)
## Revine
- Revine is open about her hatred for Flos, and she attended the monarchs' gathering at the watering hole to speak of the King of Destruction. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L427,L480)
- At the gathering the Siren glared at Empress Nsiia and then pointed jets of water at her, which Orjin and a blast of air stopped. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L504-507,L541-542)
- The Siren of Savere offered the leader of Tiqr torture and her citizens as slaves, according to the leader of Tiqr, who would prefer death. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L676)
- Revine's full name is Revine Zecrew. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1455)
## Other use
- The Baroness of the Beats is also called the Siren of Songs, a nickname for a [Singer] of Terandria. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L148)
