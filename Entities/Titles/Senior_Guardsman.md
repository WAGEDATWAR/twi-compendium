# Senior Guardsman
> Type: title | Also called: Senior Guardsmen; Senior Guard; Senior Guardswoman | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.08
> See also: Relc, Klbkch, Zevara, Desk Sergeant, Liscor City Watch
## Overview
- Senior Guardsman is a rank in Liscor's City Watch, and Relc insists it is an important and highly prestigious rank. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L230; Book 7, Ch5.13 L646)
- A Senior Guard is a rank, whereas a Desk Sergeant is a temporary position that a Senior Guard can fill. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L7)
- Relc and Klbkch are the best-known Senior Guardsmen of the Liscor Watch; Zevara called Klbkch one of her best Senior Guardsmen. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L88; Book 2, Ch2.20 L127)
- The Watch sends out Senior Guardsmen against Goblins and posts bounties to keep their numbers down. (Book 1, Ch1.21 L214)
## In the Watch
- Relc and Klbkch were not the only pair of Senior Guardsmen in the city, but between them they were the Watch's greatest fighting strength. (Book 2, Ch2.22 L160)
- Klbkch, as a Senior Guardsman, could take Erin up onto the battlements where citizens are not allowed. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L594)
- Klbkch told Zevara he was visiting not as a Senior Guardsman but as Prognugator of his Hive. (Book 3, Ch3.21L L209)
- In the fight against the Raskghar, the Senior Guardsmen moved independently while the rest of the Watch fought shoulder to shoulder. (Book 8, Ch5.44 L701)
- Senior Guardsmen include Gnolls, such as a Gnoll in his mid-forties with black and grey fur. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L113)
