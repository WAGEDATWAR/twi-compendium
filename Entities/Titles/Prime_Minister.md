# Prime Minister
> Type: title | Also called: [Prime Minister]; Minister | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.12K
> See also: Belchan, Lyfelt, Raelt, Jecrass
## Overview
- Prime Minister is the elected ruler of the Republic of Belchan, a parliamentary government north of Germina. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L32)
- Lyfelt was elected [Prime Minister] by Belchan's people, which makes Belchan a democracy and rare among nations; he is a career politician who consolidated a [Politician] class into [Minister]. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L192-193)
- Lyfelt rose to rule purely by political skill, not by birth, unlike the River Wardens of Jecrass. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L193)
- Lyfelt was new to his position though he had served six years as [Prime Minister], and had held it less than a decade. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L208; Book 15, Ch7.08K L536)
## Lyfelt and Jecrass
- Lyfelt speaks with King Raelt of Jecrass through a pair of magical marble statues, and Raelt considers him a friend who sometimes thinks too much like a [Politician]. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L186; Book 15, Ch7.08K L728,L750)
- Lyfelt uses a Skill with a soothing voice to calm Raelt, and Raelt dislikes how he can play with lives like numbers. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L734,L764)
- Lyfelt recommended that Flos Reimarch hunt Manticores in Belchan, and invited Reim to dine and cross Belchan's borders. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L737,L845; Book 15, Ch7.10K L763)
- Flos Reimarch accused Lyfelt of letting the Saltstone Tribe be slaughtered inside Belchan, and declared war on Belchan and on Lyfelt personally. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L1099,L1175)
