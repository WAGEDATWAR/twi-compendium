# Knight-Commander
> Type: title | Also called: Knight-Commander of the Order of Seasons | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.42E
> See also: Order of Seasons, Calirn, Talia Kallinad, Belavierr
## Overview
- Knight-Commander is the title of the head of the Order of Seasons, a major Terandrian knighthood; Knight-Commander Calirn holds it. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L462,L466)
- The Order of Seasons has four Seasons, each with its own Grandmaster, and at least two thousand Seasonal [Knights] in its headquarters at any time. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L463,L465)
- Even so, no [Knight] in the Order is beyond any other full member, even the Knight-Commander, so Talia spoke up to Calirn when she asked leave to hunt Belavierr. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L515)
## Calirn
- Calirn is a Knight of the Winter with pale grey eyes, and the room around him grows freezing when he is troubled, though he can warm the air again. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L470,L474,L483)
- Calirn rules from the Order's keep, whose cathedral is the domain of the Knight-Commander, and he leads the Order in command duties such as speaking with monarchs and settling arguments among the Seasons. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L466-467)
- When Belavierr was reported to be in Filk, Calirn said the Order could slay her, but that it was one of many orders and tempered its strength with humility. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L599)
- {belief} It is said that Calirn uses nothing but ordinary steel at times to challenge himself into levelling up. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L939)
- The Order of Seasons is headquartered in Pheislant, on its own lands but allied with the Terandrian Kingdom. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L504)
- Calirn personally attended a vigil on the docks for Ser Raim's funeral barge, blaming himself for leading the Order to war, and had white hair as white as the snow he could call. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L504,L513-514,L519-520)
