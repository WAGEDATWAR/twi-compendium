# Chief Warrior
> Type: title | Also called: none | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Merish, Plains Gnolls, Cirille
## Overview
- Chief Warrior is the title by which Cirille addresses Merish, a Plains Gnoll who commands a Gnoll contingent sent to Rhir under the pact. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L752,L825-826,L831)
- Merish's contingent counts two thousand one hundred and three Gnolls, and he personally brought sixty heavy-infantry warriors. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L752,L854-855)
- Chief Warrior Merish is a [Shamanic Warrior] of the Plain's Eye tribe, a class he described as a magic warrior suited to heavy fighting. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L864,L868)
- Merish is over seven feet tall with dyed fur, carries a bone axe, and his Gnolls show little interest in Drake military discipline. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L827,L830,L869)
