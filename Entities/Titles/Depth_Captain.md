# Depth Captain
> Type: title | Also called: [Depth Captain] | First appears: Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships
> See also: Therrium Sailwinds, Drowned People, Undersea Crews, The Emerald Signet
## Overview
- Depth Captain is the title of a Drowned Folk ship [Captain] in the Undersea Crews who sails an underwater ship; a Drowned Ship's [First Mate] addresses its captain as "Depth Captain". (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L366-368; Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L430-431)
- Depth Captain Therrium Sailwinds is a half-Eel Drowned Man who commands The Passing Shadow, a ship feared above and under the sea. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L431)
- Crews under the waves follow the precept to make no sound, lest they attract what draws to it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L432-433)
## Rivalry at sea
- A second Depth Captain, a half-Shrimp Drowned Woman, followed the same treasure rumour about The Emerald Signet and argued with Therrium across the barrier of their ships. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L440-444,L447)
- When the two crews fought, the rival Depth Captain surrendered her blade, and Therrium made her sign a document placing her ship under his command. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L139-141)
