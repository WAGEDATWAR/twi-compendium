# Bronze-rank adventurers
> Type: concept | Also called: Bronze-rank; Bronze-ranks | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.10R
> See also: Adventurers, Adventurer's Guild, Named-rank adventurers
## Overview
- Bronze-rank adventurers appear alongside Silver-ranks and Gold-ranks in the Invrisil Adventurer's Guild, where Bronze-ranks are described as doing errand-work. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L489-490)
- Ryoka Griffin was said to be far stronger than the average Bronze-rank adventurer in hand-to-hand combat. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L286)
- Countless Bronze-rank adventurers crowd the Liscor Adventurer's Guild to turn in bounties or take the few requests on the board. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L136)
## Work and strength
- In Invrisil's guild, Bronze-ranks come in covered in muck from killing rats in sewers or gathering [Alchemist] ingredients, which is typical errand-work to make ends meet. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L489)
- Ulrien said that Bronze-rank adventurers who die do so because they overestimate their abilities or underestimate their opponents. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L33)
- The narration says each Goblin in an elite band matched any Bronze-rank adventurer by levels, and exceeded one in experience. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L115-116)
- Prost told Laken that a pooled village purse could hire only a few Bronze-rank adventurers for a few weeks. (Book 3, Ch3.14 L406)
- Over forty Bronze-rank adventurers and six Silver-rank adventurers attacked the Furgather Tribe's camp at midday and killed its Chieftain. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L310)
- Halrac told Erin that beating a Gnoll warrior barehanded was as good as any Bronze-rank adventurer. (Book 2, Ch2.26 L161)
