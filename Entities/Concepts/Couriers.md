# Couriers
> Type: concept | Also called: Courier | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.09
> See also: Runners, Ryoka Griffin
## Overview
- Couriers are special long-distance Runners, and Ryoka thinks of the job as roughly the Runner equivalent of a Gold-rank adventurer. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L59,L135)
- Fals tells Ryoka there is a highest-priority special request coming all the way from First Landing and that a Courier is delivering it. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L57-60)
## Culture
- Val tells Ryoka that Couriers do not answer to Guilds in the way small Guilds expect, that they back each other up in danger but no one bosses anyone else around. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L139)
- Val says "We're a community, not a cult." (Book 2, Ch2.17 L142)
- Val says not all Couriers are fast, that some run slower than Ryoka but cannot be stopped, and that one Courier is a [Marksman] who rides a horse and snipes anyone in her way from two hundred feet away. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L175)
## In Book 16
- {event} Three Couriers, Tritel, Salamani and Hawk, are ambushed on the road north by [Assassins] in enchanted coaches and on horseback. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L712-724)
- Tritel's horse Ci and Tritel use [Flicker Dodge] and [Blinding Flash] to blind the Assassins, and Tritel later uses [Rope Trick] to drag an Assassin off her horse. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L727-730,L740-743)
- Salamani, a [Mage Runner], uses a spell to summon hundreds of [Light Arrows] that destroy a coach. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L731-736)
- Hawk, a Beastkin Rabbit Courier, uses [Physical Overdrive] to run twice as fast, crashes through a coach and escapes the box-in. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L751-761)
