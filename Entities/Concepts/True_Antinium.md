# True Antinium
> Type: concept | Also called: true Antinium; True Antinium Soldiers | First appears: Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3
> See also: Antinium, Individual, Free Antinium, Klbkch, Centenium
## Overview
- Xrn thinks of the Antinium of the present Hives as poor copies of true Antinium who will never know the grandeur of the true Hives. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L141)
- The Free Queen judged Garry, Anand, Belgrade, Pawn and the fifth Individual to be not true Antinium, because they failed her test. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L88)
- Klbkch thought of the Individuals as strange new Antinium who were not True Antinium but more than the half-made Soldiers and Workers the world knows. (Book 8, Ch5.39 L533)
- The Free Queen said she would rather have created True Antinium than Individuals, because Individuals shape the Hive but True Antinium are all Antinium. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L792)
## What they were
- True Antinium were linked, both broadcasters and receivers, unlike the tenuously held half-Antinium drones the Queens make now. (Book 8, Ch5.39 L561)
- An army of True Antinium Soldiers moved as one being, thought with a thousand minds, and could outmaneuver armies of Soldiers by coordinating over long distances. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L800,L807)
- The Free Queen said True Antinium could work through a Unitasis Network, which the few remaining Queens formed again during the First and Second Antinium Wars. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L807)
- Klbkch told Soldiers that in the past, a dying Antinium was remembered by all, and that he is one of the last True Antinium, remembering all of them. (Book 8, Ch5.39 L576-577)
- In ages past Klbkch could communicate with another True Antinium a hundred miles away with ease. (Book 8, Ch5.39 L581)
- Klbkch remembered that the Free Queen argued against making new forms and called only for rediscovering how to make True Antinium and for creating Queens. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L657)
- The Free Queen said that Xrn, Klbkch, Wrymvr and the remaining Queens remember the True Antinium, from Rhir. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L816)
