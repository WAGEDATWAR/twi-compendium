# Yellow Rivers
> Type: concept | Also called: Yellow Rivers disease; Yellow Rivers illness | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.06D
> See also: Yellow Rivers disease, Geneva Scala, Healing potions, Niers Astoragon
## Overview
- Yellow Rivers is a new disease that Quallet reports to Geneva as one of the new ones, which he thinks is called Yellow Rivers. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L793)
- Two Human men in Quallet's care caught a severe infection at a brothel in the last town, and one used a healing potion on it, after which it hurt much more. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L775-779,L810-811)
- The infection is on the men's genitals, Quallet calls it the worst case of it he has seen, and he has kept the men isolated as Geneva advised. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L782-790)
## Spread
- {belief} Niers Astoragon reads a report that the Last Light of Baleros is treating Yellow Rivers victims, and concludes that it spreads through brothels and armies in major cities. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L28-36)
- {belief} Niers suspects the sickness has multiple ways of being contracted, because two children are being treated for it, and fears a magical plague like the Sporepits Plague, in which millions died. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L38-41)
- A broadcast reports six cases in Pallass and over a thousand in Zeres, mainly from ships, and says the illness is not spread solely by intercourse. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L88)
## Nature
- Geneva says the disease is bacterial, not viral, and that the mortality rate if treated is not high, but it provokes fevers and diarrhea, which are killers where clean water and medical treatment are lacking. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L127)
- Geneva calls it airborne and mutated. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L109)
- Geneva would hold a quarantine without a cure and would advise city leaders to expect a 20% mortality rate. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L139)
