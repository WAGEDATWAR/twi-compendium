# Law of Liscor
> Type: concept | Also called: Liscorian law | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.11
> See also: Liscor guards, Goblin child
## Overview
- The Liscor Watch cannot arrest someone for an attack unless the victim gives testimony, and Klbkch confirms that when Erin withdraws hers, the Watch cannot arrest Pisces. (Book 1, Ch1.11 L167-174)
- Relc argues that being a [Necromancer] is a crime no matter what the person does, but cannot name any way to check a person's class on the spot. (Book 1, Ch1.11 L176-182)
- Klbkch states that under Liscorian law, Goblins are considered monsters and bandits for the purposes of determining crime. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L93)
