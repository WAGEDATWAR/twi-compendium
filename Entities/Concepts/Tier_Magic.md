# Tier Magic
> Type: concept | Also called: Tiered Magic; magic tiers | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.05R
> See also: Message spell, Illumination, Cleric
## Overview
- Tiered Magic organizes spells by tier, in contrast to the undisciplined magics. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L185)
- A spell that a mage below Level 16 can cast is generally called 1st Tier or 0 Tier Magic, one up to about Level 24 is 2nd Tier, and up to Level 32 is generally the bar for 3rd Tier. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L186)
- Ryoka is told of a friend of the speaker who can cast [Restoration] and other 4th Tier spells. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L183)
## Examples from Ceria
- Ceria says she cannot cast [Fireball] and uses a wand for it, but knows [Ice Spike], which is Tier 3. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L173-175)
- Ceria says [Detect Magic] is Tier 3 as well, and the [Haste] spell Ryoka wants is Tier 4, or perhaps Tier 5. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L177,L179)
- Ceria says she can cast Tier 3 magic at best, and that Tier 4 would need a spellbook and weeks or months of study. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L181)
- Ceria says Tier 3 magic is good for fighting, [Ice Shard] is Tier 2, and she would call [Light] Tier 0 and [Spark] another example of it. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L191-193)
- Ceria says Pisces knows one Tier 4 spell, [Invisibility], which took him at least two hours of study every night for a month. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L187)
