# Sharphorns
> Type: concept | Also called: untested Sharphorns | First appears: Book 19, Ch7.47K
> See also: Ozem, Minotaurs, House of Minos, Flos Reimarch
## Overview
- Sharphorns are the new, untested young [Warriors] of the House of Minos, in contrast with the veterans whose horns are decorated. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L204-206)
- Of the House of Minos's army, four thousand [Warriors] were new and two thousand were complete veterans. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L207)
- The Sharphorns' horns are plain white ivory, and they are green [Warriors] who have seen few battles but are at least Level 16. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L204,L473)
- The Sharphorns trained for the battle against the King of Destruction and were sent to win the King of Minotaurs' victory alongside the veterans. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L491,L775)
## The battle against Reim
- General Ozem called the veteran Minotaurs the House of Minos's failures who could not live in peace, and told the young Sharphorns to look at the King of Destruction and not scorn him. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L484,L490)
- When Ozem advanced the Sharphorns alongside the Axebearers, who were all Level 30 or higher, Reim's momentum stopped. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L749,L773-775)
- A quarter of the Sharphorns had fallen by the time Ozem ordered the retreat to the ships. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L941-942)
- Ozem ordered the Sharphorns to drop armor, secondary weapons and shields so they could outrun horses, valuing them for their future. (Book 19, Ch7.47K L978-980)
