# Chess
> Type: concept | Also called: Lightning Chess | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.19
> See also: Law of Liscor
## Overview
- Olesm tells Erin that chess was invented in this world just a year earlier. (Book 1, Ch1.19 L91)
- Erin proposes playing Klbkch and Olesm at once with only five, or rather ten, seconds per move, and calls the style Lightning Chess. (Book 1, Ch1.24 L259-261)
- Erin explains that tournament chess can give anywhere from an hour's total thinking time to three minutes; she says that without a clock she will explain as they play. (Book 1, Ch1.24 L261)
- Erin wins the first two rounds of the Lightning Chess games handily, but in the third game Olesm scores a draw. (Book 1, Ch1.24 L265)
