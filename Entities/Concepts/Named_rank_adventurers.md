# Named-rank adventurers
> Type: concept | Also called: Named-rank; Named Adventurers; Named adventurer | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.17S
> See also: Bronze-rank adventurers, Adventurers, Saliss of Lights
## Overview
- Named-rank is a level of adventurer above Gold-rank; Named-rank individuals and teams are described as another level, even compared to Gold-ranks. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L622; Book 18, Ch7.31 L915)
- A Gold-rank adventurer is described as someone who might be close to Named-rank, with only one thing, or recognition, holding them back. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L497)
- Even Named Adventurers might walk into the large Adventurer's Guild at Invrisil, and its Guildmaster was part of a Named-rank team without being a Named individual himself. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L486-487)
- Named-rank teams could be as powerful as a Named-rank person, though their individual members might not be as formidable. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L487)
## Examples
- Saliss of Lights is a Named Adventurer and is Pallass's only permanent resident Named-rank adventurer. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L994)
- Eighty thousand gold pieces were said to be enough that one might think about becoming a Named-rank adventurer by outfitting oneself with it. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L558)
- A bounty notice rated Belavierr a Named-rank threat and compared her kill of elite [Hunters] and a [Knight] to a Named Adventurer and a Named-rank team. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L1483,L1485)
- Selys thought a particular artifact looked worthy of a Gold-rank adventurer, maybe even a Named-rank one, before she knew what it really was. (Book 7, Ch5.17S L187)
- Farmer Lupp told Ryoka that a former [Farmer] became a famous adventurer who fought in the Second Antinium Wars against the Goblins and ended up in a Named-Rank team. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L1230-1236)
