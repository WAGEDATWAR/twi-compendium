# Aberrations
> Type: concept | Also called: Aberration | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.25
> See also: Antinium, Klbkch, Pawn, Individual
## Overview
- In the Antinium Hive, an Aberration is a Worker who has become an individual and turned violent against the Hive, and the Hive treats such a Worker as a failure to be destroyed. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L8,L17)
- Antinium speakers say that those who are Aberration kill, reject the Hive and destroy. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L305)
## Chess Workers
- After Workers brought home the game of chess from Erin's inn, other Workers first wondered whether the new game was itself an Aberration, but concluded it was not because Klbkch had not eliminated the twelve who played it. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L5-7)
- During the fifth round of chess, one Worker fetched a butcher's knife and stabbed his opponent to death, and six more Workers were killed before the Propugnator was summoned. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L8-9)
- Klbkch found the killing Aberration sawing at another Worker's body, and the Aberration told him he had not failed, that he refused to serve the Queen, and that he refused to take a name. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L11-28)
- The Aberration stabbed Klbkch twice before Klbkch cut him to pieces and burned the remains. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L30)
- Afterwards only a quarter of the Workers were allowed to play chess at once, in case of further Aberrations. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L33)
- The Queen complained that three Workers had already become Aberration from the experiment and called it a waste. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L35)
## Individuals and the test
- An Antinium Worker told Erin that no Worker who has become individual has ever not been Aberration, and that Pawn would be executed if he proved to be one. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L300-302,L312)
- Ksmvr reported that of twenty-five Workers tested, four became Individuals and the rest all became Aberration. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L256)
- Ksmvr reported that among those who did not meet Erin Solstice the rate of becoming Individual was one in one hundred and sixty-three, and few Soldiers became Aberration. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L261)
- Klbkch told Erin that asking a Worker its name was dangerous because most Workers become Aberration when it happens. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L451)
