# The Black Tide
> Type: concept | Also called: black tide | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.44
> See also: Antinium, Antinium Wars, Klbkch, Zel Shivertail
## Overview
- The Black Tide is the name used for the Antinium swarming forward in force; guardsmen in Liscor shouted that the black tide marched when Antinium appeared in the streets. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L189-193)
- In the history of the Antinium Wars, the Black Tide is the Antinium army, which swept across Izril as it had across Rhir and which put cities under siege. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L15,L77; Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L3)
- Zel Shivertail was hailed as 'Tidebreaker' because his army repeatedly withstood the Black Tide. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L33)
- Klbkch ordered his Hive to call the Black Tide when he learned a Goblin and Human force was marching on Liscor. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L82-84)
- The Antinium are called the Black Tide of Izril, and they are seen politely eating ice cream at the gathering in Erin's inn. (Book 17, Ch7.24 L615)
- A Reinhart house head watching a vast horde of Antinium thinks the Black Tide is reforming. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L837)
