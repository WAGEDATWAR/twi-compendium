# Hemp
> Type: concept | Also called: Hemp caste; Hemp warriors; Hemp soldiers | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.26G
> See also: String-people, Stitch-people, Nerrhavia, Thelican
## Overview
- Hemp is a plant fiber used for ropes and bags in the world, including a hemp rope that lit lanterns in a cavern and sacks that adventurers and goblins carry. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L149; Book 7, Ch5.15 L98; Book 9, Ch5.56G L363)
- Among String-people, Hemp is also the name of a caste, the rough-skinned warriors of Nerrhavia's society. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L254-256)
## The Hemp caste
- Femithain understood the Hemp warriors to be made from the strongest materials, which gives them a rough, callused appearance closer to hide than skin. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L251,L254)
- Nerrhavia's society is caste-based, and Nerrhavia's silk-skinned officers shout insults at the Hemp foot-soldiers marching in the chariots' dust. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L251,L254)
- General Thelican's Hemp [Soldiers] formed his pike lines and foot, and he planned to send them first at the walls. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L369,L420; Book 13, Ch6.53K L31)
- Thelican felt nothing for a fallen Hemp warrior, even though beheading is death for a String-person unless they are saved immediately. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L348)
- String-people fear fire, and Tiqr's mages tried to burn the Hemp warriors, who have thick skins that make this difficult but possible. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L346-347)
- Alked Fellbow, a Stitch-person with rough, plant-fiber skin who is not quite Hemp, is treated as an outcast in Thelican's tent. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L17)
