# Aberration
> Type: concept | Also called: Aberrations | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.25
> See also: Free Queen, Chess, Ksmvr
## Overview
- An Aberration is an Antinium Worker that has become an individual and rejects the Queen's will; Workers say those who are Aberration kill, reject the Hive and destroy. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L305)
- A Worker tells Erin that no Worker who has become individual has ever failed to become an Aberration. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L312)
## Book 1
- {event} Klbkch finds a Worker sawing at another Worker's body, who says he corrects the fault in the others, who play games against the will of the Hive and deserve death. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L12-15)
- The Worker refuses to serve, claiming "I am", calling the Queen's words madness and heresy, and refuses a name, saying the Experiment is a failure; Klbkch kills him. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L19-30)
- The Free Queen tells Erin that three Workers have already become Aberration from gaining useless levels, and calls the experiment wasted. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L35)
- The Queen orders her Prognugator to investigate an aberration on the surface and speak with him if he is truly free, and the Prognugator says he will dispose of any Aberrations he finds. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L256-258)
