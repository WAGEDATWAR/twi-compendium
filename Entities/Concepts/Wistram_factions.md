# Wistram factions
> Type: concept | Also called: the factions of Wistram | First appears: Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1
> See also: Wistram, BlackMage, Feor, Nailihuaile
## Overview
- Calvaron tells new students that Wistram is not one unified place but divided into factions, and that mages either band together or go it alone depending on their personalities. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L346)
- There is no formal ceremony for choosing a faction, and Calvaron advises students to tell no one if they join one. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L350-352)
- Calvaron says most mages do not care about new students, and only the Expansionists and Revivalists are interested in ensuring there is a system. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L343)
## Factions named
- Calvaron points out a small group of Isolationists, who would prefer that Wistram stop accepting new mages and let aspiring mages find their own way there. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L353-354)
- Beatrice is a Revivalist, part of the faction that thinks Wistram should throw open its doors and usher in a new era of magic like centuries past. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L358)
- Calvaron says he himself is undeclared, at least for now. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L361)
- Almost any powerful mage belongs to one of the major factions that control Wistram's Council or is allied to one of the big players. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L44)
- Feor belongs to the Centrists, and also keeps a personal group of half-Elf mages he trusts. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L44)
- Welaiat is a Libertarian, and Failess is a Preservationist. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L139-141)
- Montressa du Valeros says she is a Revivalist too, and Beatrice is also called a Revivalist. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L300-305)
