# Antinium Wars
> Type: event | Also called: Antinium War; Incursion Wars; First Antinium War; Second Antinium War | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.00H
> See also: Antinium, The Black Tide, Klbkch, Sserys, Zel Shivertail, Magnolia Reinhart, Az'kerash
## Overview
- The Antinium Wars, also called the Incursion Wars, were two wars between the Antinium and the Drakes, Gnolls and Humans of Izril; Ceria recalls hearing frightening stories of them even though she was not on the continent. (Book 2, Ch2.00H L366; Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L4,L7)
- Krsysl Wordsmith wrote the history of the First Antinium Wars, saying Izril survived two wars with the Antinium and that the peace had lasted nearly a decade when he wrote. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L4,L7-8)
## First Antinium War
- Before the war, the Antinium were driven out of Rhir in the year 5 B.F. after the Blighted King's armies shattered; only four ships of some two hundred greatships reached the south-west of Izril. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L14,L17,L20-21)
- Krsysl Wordsmith calls the Antinium's underestimation of the sea their one real mistake, because it cost them nearly their whole fleet. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L10-12,L21-22)
- The Antinium hid for months, attacked patrols around the port city of Nasserous and the highlands, and were first identified when a Gold-rank [Ranger] brought four corpses to a guild. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L23-35)
- Leaders of the Walled Cities argued for nearly a month over whether the Antinium were in Izril, and only a few notables such as Lady Magnolia Reinhart, Lord Tyron Veltras and General Sserys knew. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L36,L40)
- About six and a half months after the landing, fifteen villages and a small town were destroyed in a single night. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L51-52)
- In less than two hours the Antinium killed or maimed over 80% of the population of each of three attacked cities, and the Drake cities then fell to raising separate armies instead of one. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L58,L60-61)
- Drake armies adopted a standing policy of never putting more than one Level 30+ soldier in a squad, because Antinium tunnelling raids targeted concentrated high-level units. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L72-73)
- The Antinium besieged the Walled City of Zeres after taking nearly thirty percent of the southern half of Izril, and were rebuffed with heavy casualties. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L77-78)
- Sserys unified the Drake cities and secured a non-aggression pact with the Gnoll Tribes, and was named High General of the war. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L5)
- Magnolia Reinhart worked as a diplomat holding the Human allies together; her sword from the fight at the Liscor pass is displayed in her mansion. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L69; Book 2, Ch2.30 L123)
- The Drakes, Humans and Gnolls formed the Southern Alliance, which pushed the Antinium back until the Queens answered with a second offensive that used the Prognugators. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L70-71)
- Nine months into the war, the allies discovered that the Antinium cannot swim and drown, after Antinium were drowned in a valley by a [Rain] spell. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L90-94)
- Sserys led the First Unified Army against the main Hive nearly a year after the war began, despite Zel Shivertail's objection, and the army vanished overnight. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L112-114,L118-121)
- {state} Sserys died in the attack; his diary and corpse were found in the encampment, and the diary said a new breed of Antinium was hunting his army. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L123-131,L137,L141)
- The First Antinium Wars ended with an accord signed by the Walled Cities, the Antinium Grand Queen and the Human coalition led by Magnolia Reinhart. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L144)
## Second Antinium War
- {belief} Klbkch said the Antinium Wars were setbacks, and that the true disaster for the Antinium was losing their people on the oceans. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L326)
- Klbkch explained that the Antinium types other than Soldiers and Workers were revealed during the Second Antinium War, but that only Soldiers and Workers are allowed inside the Hive in Liscor. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L271)
- The Second Antinium War overlapped with the war against a Goblin King and the Necromancer, and began with a Drake army besieging an Antinium Hive at the Jessal Highlands; Krsysl Wordsmith's history of it opens in the year 13 A.F. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L13,L31-32)
- Klbkch and Xrn recall that the Drakes flooded the besieged Hive with rain, and the history says the Antinium freed the youngest Queen, who later led the Hive in Liscor. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L57-59,L89,L119)
- Wrymvr the Deathless, the Flying Antinium and the Silent Antinium were deployed against the Drakes in the Second Antinium War. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L136,L149-151)
- Xrn said the historian exaggerates but does not invent events, and that the Goblin King's forces under Tallis Stormbreaker destroyed her army. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L31,L35)
- The war ended in a peace treaty with the Grand Queen after Klbkch's Antinium helped Zel Shivertail kill the Necromancer at Liscor; Zel and Klbkch then fought each other. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.5 L9,L30-32,L39,L53,L58)
