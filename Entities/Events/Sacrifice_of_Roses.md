# Sacrifice of Roses
> Type: event | Also called: Sacrifice of Roses | First appears: Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Antinium Wars, Goblin King
## Overview
- The Sacrifice of Roses is the name for the slaughter of Izril's noble [Ladies] during the Second Antinium War, after they joined the front lines. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L241,L244)
- Until then the [Ladies] of the realm had refrained from fighting, but two weeks into the slaughter Magnolia Reinhart and every [Lady] in the city appeared on the front lines to lend their Skills against the Goblin King. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L241)
- The [Ladies] went into combat with their personal escorts, and their combined low-level Skills let the Humans push back the surprised Goblins. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L243)
## The slaughter
- {event} The Goblin King ordered his vassals to target the relatively undefended [Ladies], and the Goblin Lords led charges in person against them. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L244-245)
- The Humans refused to run when death was imminent, and held their ground long enough for their soldiers to take vengeance on the Goblins. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L245)
- One of the lesser Goblin Lords, Murmar, was confirmed slain as a result of the defence. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L245)
- Over 60% of the [Ladies] who took to the front lines perished along with their escorts. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L308)
- The Sacrifice of Roses became a rallying cry across Izril and is lauded as a defining moment of courage. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L246)
- The Goblin King withdrew his forces after the loss of the first Goblin Lord, and the Goblin advance stalled for the first time. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L307-308)
