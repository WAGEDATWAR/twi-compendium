# Liscor election
> Type: event | Also called: Liscor Council election; election results | First appears: Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt
> See also: Krshia Silverfang, Lism Swifttail, Ulseil, Liscor's Council
## Overview
- Liscor's election replaces the current Council with a democratically elected group of eight for the next two years, with votes verified by magical spell. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L128)
- Voters answer under a truth spell before voting, and the City Watch guards the ballot boxes in the streets. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L318)
- Each district has at least two candidates, sometimes three or four, and the hottest debate is a split between Lism and Krshia, with almost all Drake and Gnoll candidates on each side. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L318-319)
## Results
- {state} Ulseil announces that the new Council by popular vote is Raekea Silversmith, Elirr Fultpar, Krshia Silverfang, Lism Swifttail, Alonna Swiftwing, Jeiss Sielmark, Tismel Lischscale and Zalaiss Holmfyre. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L134,L138-140)
- The Gnolls leap for joy and Lism's camp shouts, but the crowd goes still when they realise the seats were not allotted by district. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L139,L141-144)
- Ulseil explains that the Council chose the most popular candidates because no official backing was ever given to the proposed districts, and calls it the fairest distribution of seats. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L147)
- Erin calls it cheating, and Lyonette answers that it is clever and the only move the Council could make. (Book 13, Ch6.49 L151-152)
