# Expedition
> Type: event | Also called: the adventurers' expedition into the ruins | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.00H
> See also: Horns of Hammerad, Calruz, Crypt Lord
## Overview
- Five adventuring teams assemble before the dark doors at the head of the expedition: the Horns of Hammerad, the Flawless Flight, Kyrial's Pride, the Circle of Reneë and the Silver Spears. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L143,L147-151)
- Every adventurer in the crowd is Silver-rank. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L142)
- Cervial says that, counting the extras, the five teams are over forty people strong and all Silver-rank. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L386)
## Course
- Gerial says the expedition has enough supplies for at least four days and that he doubts they will be down there that long unless they need to do excavation. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L44)
- {event} Deep underground the expedition is ambushed when the walls slide open and the dead pour out. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L409-410)
- {event} Calruz kills a Crypt Lord with his [Hammer Blow] skill, after which the undead lose their controlling intelligence and the adventurers press them back until they are ground into paste against a wall. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L278-279)
- {event} After the battle Ceria says the adventurers will now get the treasure. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L284-286)
