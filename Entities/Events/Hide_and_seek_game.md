# Hide-and-seek game
> Type: event | Also called: Daquin game; the Titan's war game | First appears: Book 11, Ch6.21D
> See also: Niers Astoragon, Tulm the Mithril, Wil Kallinad, Umina
## Overview
- Every few years, sometimes once a decade and once for eight years straight, the Titan of Baleros announces a hide-and-seek game with his students, which never happens during war or unrest. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L589)
- He takes his students to a location, whether a city, a patch of jungle or an artificial maze, and gives them a day to hide while an entire army searches for them. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L589)
- Over a hundred students nearing graduation and some recent graduates hide, while the seekers are Niers's older [Strategists], his first students, who are veterans of dozens of wars. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L590)
- The hiders try to fulfil a criterion that changes every year, and the seekers must capture them by whatever means necessary short of permanent bodily harm. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L591-592)
- Students may fight back, set traps and form alliances, and some of Niers's soldiers carry clubs and nets. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L592)
- Umina suspects the game is meant to teach students what it is like to be cut off from their forces and hunted, and that in practice it is also the Titan's way of having fun. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L593)
## At Daquin
