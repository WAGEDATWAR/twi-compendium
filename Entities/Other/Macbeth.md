# Macbeth
> Type: work | Also called: the play Macbeth | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.25
> See also: Hamlet, Players of Celum, Erin Solstice, Wesle, Temile
## Overview
- Macbeth is a play from Erin's world, and Erin shuddered at the idea of performing it when choosing her next play for the Players of Celum. (Book 3, Ch3.25 L100)
- Erin explained Macbeth to a customer by saying that a line was from Macbeth, a play, before explaining what a play is. (Book 4, Ch3.33 L375)
## Staged by the Players
- A Gnoll [Actor], Yimur, played the lead in a Gnollish Macbeth with 'hand' changed to 'paw', and Director Temile told him the dagger is a vision in Macbeth's mind. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L128-140)
- The Players of Celum ran through Macbeth and then put on a full performance for an audience that was not heckling them. (Book 11, Ch6.28 L424; Book 11, Ch6.29 L559)
- Relc delivered Macbeth's dagger soliloquy without a flaw. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L957)
- In Sovvex, the Players of Celum led with a comedy of a [Jester] and then performed Macbeth as the second play of the night, with Wesle as Macbeth. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L271,L278,L299)
- The Players' Macbeth later used props, armor, blood, costumes, a stage and sets, with revised, modern scripts instead of the original, though Erin had first taught it in Celum. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L307-308)
