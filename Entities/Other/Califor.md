# Califor
> Type: person | Also called: Witch Califor; Califor Weishart; Califor's ghost | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.37E
> See also: Nanette, Belavierr, Wiskeria, Coven, Mavika, Ryoka Griffin, Riverfarm
## Overview
- Califor is a tall, thin [Witch] who dresses in plain black with a worn hat, and her apprentice is Nanette, a round-cheeked twelve-year-old with a fresh dark blue hat. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L3,L18,L77)
- Califor had never had an apprentice before Nanette, which is famous among witches. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L433)
- Califor keeps numbered rules for Nanette, and rule eight is that a smart woman looks up now and then. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L83-85)
- {belief} Wiskeria reports tales that Califor is tougher than nails, and that she is the most powerful [Witch] in generations. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1228)
## Arrival at Riverfarm
- {event} On the road to Riverfarm, a [Bandit Leader]'s axe seems to cleave Califor's skull, but the body is one of his own bandits, and her illusion saves her. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L42-46)
- Califor tells Nanette that she survived her first bandits well and that she may trust her teacher but must rely on herself. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L76)
- Califor says she had heard Riverfarm was safe from [Bandits] of any kind, and Rie replies that the method they use to detect them is not working as usual. (Book 12, Ch6.37E L355-357)
- Califor says she is the coven's leader when asked, and she stops a quarrel between Alevica and Mavika by slamming her hand on the table and saying "Enough". (Book 12, Ch6.40E L394-395,L445-448)
- Califor tells Ryoka that she feels like the wind and has an unusual affinity for it, though she is no [Mage]. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L377)
- Califor says her cloak is a flea-market bargain and her staff a gift from a [Shaman], but Durene thinks she sees the staff turn leafy and the cloak swirl like shadow. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L1147-1150)
- Califor loads a pony cart with far more goods than it could hold, and says the cart does not know how much it is carrying. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L1126-1133)
## Past
- {belief} Prost says Califor may be the woman who broke the Wellfar dam at Lake Roisemead, and Rie confirms that the incident happened eighteen years ago and that Wellfar is still hunting for the culprit. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L86-90)
- {belief} Prost wonders whether Califor ran Yellats across the north after the 2nd Antinium War, and says that woman had brown hair and nobody called her a Witch. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L1138)
- Califor says she once wore happiness, then passion and great deeds, and that these days much of her craft is Nanette and teaching. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1311)
## The Hunters of Noelictus
- {event} Califor rides down three fake [Hunters], conjures a storm of leaves, and cuts a fireball in half with a knife. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L318,L327,L336)
- {event} She catches a thrown glass orb filled with bright yellow liquid, binds it with string, and sends it back at one of the fleeing riders, and she makes a tree explode around another. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L341-349)
- A third rider is knocked from his horse by a thunderclap of wind, and when Califor finds him he is only ash, which she guesses was suicide to avoid being traced to Noelictus. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L350-351,L363-367)
- {event} Califor stands between the Hunters and Knights and Wiskeria, catches a crossbow bolt bare-handed, and says Belavierr is part of her coven. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L494-499)
- Califor buries a [Spring Knight] up to his waist with one blow of her staff that sounds like a gong, while Mavika turns into a giant bird. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L522,L528-529)
- {belief} Califor tells Nanette that if she had known Belavierr still lived and her crimes in Noelictus were as heinous as suspected, she would have hunted her. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L679)
## Belavierr
- {event} Califor sacrifices her Cloak of Balshadow to hide Riverfarm in shadow from the undead, and the cloak tears away. (Book 12, Ch6.44E L896-901)
- Belavierr recognises the hiding of the village as a craft of witches of old. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L166)
- {event} Califor tosses a glowing thing upward and makes multicoloured rain fall, interrupting Belavierr. (Book 12, Ch6.43E L1391-1400)
## The wildfires
- {event} Califor drives her quarterstaff, a relic of an ancient forest, into the ground to burn in the place of the forest, and it erases three wildfires before it crumbles to ash. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L501-507)
- Califor shares sake from Drath porcelain with Ryoka and says the secret is being able to do great things, so that one seldom has to. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L538-545,L582)
- {state} On Day 70 Califor leaves Riverfarm with Nanette, saying that Nanette comes first and that she is a coward that day. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L633,L644,L653,L668)
- Two-thirds of the people who tried the river perished, including Mayor Rodivek and many of Lancrel's folk. (Book 12, Ch6.46E L1056)
## Death and ghost
- {state} Califor's story ended when her thread was cut, and the Stitch Witch had plucked it to trick her own death. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L801)
- {event} On Day 77 the coven summons her ghost with Nanette's blood and her handkerchief, and Mavika says she was the most powerful in generations. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L797,L802-806)
- The ghost calls Nanette Weishart her daughter and curses Belavierr, and says to Nanette that in the end all must walk alone. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L827-835)
- The ghost says the other side is empty and that something hungry has devoured the land of death. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L843-847)
- {belief} She says the nameless one among the dead pursues her and must never return. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L847)
- The ghost seizes Wiskeria in flame, charges her to keep Nanette safe, and whispers one word into her ear. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L862-873)
- {state} Califor is gone, and Wiskeria's arms are marked with raw handprints that no potion or charm removes. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L874-875)
