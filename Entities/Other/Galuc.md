# Galuc
> Type: person | Also called: Galuc the Builder | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.06L
> See also: Klbkch, Centenium, Antinium
## Overview
- Klbkch tells Pawn that Galuc was one of the Centenium, called the Builder, and that it is Galuc's form that all the Antinium wear. (Book 3, Ch3.06L L572-575)
- Klbkch says his own original form was lost when he died at sea, while Galuc's was saved and remains in each Antinium, smaller and weaker. (Book 3, Ch3.06L L578)
- Klbkch knew Galuc, who felt great emotion and never broke or gave in, even at the end. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L98)
- The Queens believed in a false version of Galuc and did not think copies of him have morale. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L97)
