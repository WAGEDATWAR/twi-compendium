# Reiss's lieutenants
> Type: org | Also called: Reiss' lieutenants | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.30G
> See also: Goblin Lord's lieutenants, Reiss, Snapjaw, Eater of Spears
## Overview
- Snapjaw, the Goblin Lord Reiss's fierce lieutenant, is a Hobgoblin with metallic teeth and too large a head who rides a horse beside him. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L105)
- Eater of Spears, a Hob nearly as tall as Tremborag, is all muscle and fearlessly meets Tremborag's eyes. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L106)
- Snapjaw orders Tremborag to show respect to the Goblin Lord, who is not a Chieftain. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L117-118)
- Reiss's best officers, the ones who inspired and defined his army, were slain by Zel Shivertail. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L156)
- In the later battle, only Snapjaw and Eater of Spears remain, and Snapjaw leads at least fifteen thousand Goblins on horseback against the Redfangs. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L156-157)
