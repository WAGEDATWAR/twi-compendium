# Yellow Rivers disease
> Type: concept | Also called: Yellow Rivers infection; Yellow River strain | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.07D
> See also: Yellow Rivers, Geneva Scala, Healing potions, Okasha
## Overview
- Quallet says that anyone who comes down with the sickness cannot use healing potions, which just make the rash worse very quickly. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L82)
- {belief} Geneva considers it a distinct possibility that the infection is partly magical, in which case she could not stop it, and she treats her two patients by cleaning the infections, bandaging them and giving them an antibiotic treatment based on the [Healers]' recipes. (Book 10, Ch6.07D L67)
## Mutation
- {belief} Paige says that the Yellow Rivers patients are growing in number, that Geneva fears an epidemic, and that the bacteria have mutated across all of them, so it is not just spread by sex now. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L375)
- Geneva shows Paige a petri dish of the Yellow River strain that she cultured from her own infection, which proves it is bacterial because a virus would not survive in dead matter. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L633-638)
- Geneva needs a broad-spectrum anti-bacterial agent, with penicillin as one example. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L639-641)
- {event} Geneva drops a single drop of a weak healing potion onto the culture, and the bacteria instantly cover the whole dish. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L833-834,L837)
- She explains that using a healing potion turns a small infection into an open sore and a preventable illness into full-blown fever, diarrhea and pus that infects anyone who does not wash their hands. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L838)
- {state} Within three more days the disease lands in First Landing, Lailight Scintillation and two Terandrian ports, and it becomes a true pandemic spreading by sea and land. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1011,L1016)
