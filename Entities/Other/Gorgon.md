# Gorgon
> Type: species | Also called: Gorgons | First appears: Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage
> See also: Lizardfolk, Naga, Lamia, Medusa, Uxel, 06_Species_Other
## Overview
- Gorgons are one of the main evolutions of Lizardfolk, alongside Nagas, Lamias and Medusae; general Lizardfolk-evolution facts are held in the species file. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L365; Book 14, ChInterlude_Foliana L402)
- A Medusa says Medusae are not as strong and tough as Gorgons or even Nagas. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L61)
## In Rhir's war
- Commander Uxel is a Gorgon who commands a mixed army of Nagas, Gorgons, Lamias and Lizardfolk at the Walls of Rhir. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1209; Book 18, Ch7.35C L262,L392)
- Gorgons and Nagas act as the heavy punch of the Lizardfolk forces, while Lamias and other spellcasters batter the enemy with magic. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L354)
- Gorgons are individually strong and tough, more than Gnolls or the average Drake. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L356)
- Uxel has venom fangs, enhanced scales, great height and brawn, and infrared vision with which he sees the heat of a nervous horse. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L363,L580)
- Gorgons and Medusae share sight-attacks, though a Gorgon's only makes the enemy's muscles lock for a second or two, which Uxel used to break Drake formations. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L581)
