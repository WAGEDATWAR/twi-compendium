# General Sserys
> Type: person | Also called: Spear of the Drakes; General Sserys of Liscor | First appears: Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1
> See also: Zel Shivertail, Magnolia Reinhart, Antinium Wars
## Overview
- General Sserys is a Drake and the hero of the First Antinium War, and he is called [Spear of the Drakes]. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L416,L421)
- Sserys tells Zel Shivertail that Magnolia Reinhart, a child at the time with vomit dripping off her chin, offered him her hand and proposed that together they could end the war and save the world. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L417)
- {reveal} Sserys confesses to Zel that he was afraid of her peace and of what she saw, and he says he would never admit it to anyone else. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L420-422)
- Sserys's account says that other Human [Lords] interrupted him before he could answer her. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L420)
- A diary of General Sserys of Liscor records the army of Liscor, one of the strongest forces on the continent, meeting the Antinium on a guard mission. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L43-45)
- At Shivering Falls Pass he alone refuses to retreat, gathers coalition armies, and personally leads fifteen charges against the Antinium. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.1 L96-100)
- In Liscor, 4th Company soldiers watching Antinium march in a parade ask what happened to pride and to General Sserys. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L420,L424)
- Zel recalls that Sserys once ordered a set of magical armor made for him, which Zel refused. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L371)
- Sserys coined the saying "Drakes do not run". (Book 7, Ch5.18S L462)
- Selys describes Sserys as Liscor's greatest hero, who died long ago, and his grave has a grand tombstone with his name. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L889-890,L911,L919)
- {belief} Selys believes that Zel and Sserys were lovers, since Zel's will asks to be buried with his love; she scatters Zel's ashes beside Sserys's coffin. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L896,L911-914)
