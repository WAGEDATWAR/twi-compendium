# Ilvriss's ring
> Type: item | Also called: Ilvriss' ring; the Salazsar ring | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.50I
> See also: Ilvriss, Erin Solstice, Wall Lord of the Wall, Salazsar
## Overview
- Erin wears a ring that Ilvriss gave her, which bears the single word "Salazsar" in golden letters. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L189,L202)
- The ring looks gold to Erin, but Lyonette says it is not gold, and Mrsha, Teliv and Pisces agree that it looks like copper, which Pisces calls polished. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L199-210)
- Lyonette says she has never seen Erin wear the ring in her life, though Erin insists she wore it the day before. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L192-195)
- Erin puts the ring back on before anyone can see the faint indentation of flesh it leaves, and later she notes that no one remembers her wearing it. (Book 13, Ch6.50I L213,L215)
## Emergency signal
- {event} A Wall Lady tells the Administrator Alrric that one of the family rings broadcast an emergency signal from Invrisil, and as it was Ilvriss's ring, all his family and the Holders of the Wall were alerted. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L507-508,L511)
- Salazsar nearly sent a force out before learning the ring had activated in Invrisil. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L511)
- {reveal} Ilvriss admits privately to Alrric that he gave the ring to a woman because he thought she needed it, and says it is a mark of respect as well as nuptials. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L591-592,L595)
- Alrric understands that the rings have powers beyond sending an alarm, and Ilvriss says that the ring activating in Pallass could cause a massive incident. (Book 18, Ch7.33I L593-595)
