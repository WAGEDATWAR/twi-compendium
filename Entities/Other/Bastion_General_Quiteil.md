# Bastion-General Quiteil
> Type: person | Also called: Quiteil | First appears: Book 18, Ch7.34C
> See also: Bastion-General, Bastion-General Zavarial, Cirille, Merish
## Overview
- Bastion-General Quiteil commands the 4th Wall of Rhir and is the person in charge of the entire 4th Wall. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L686,L702)
- Quiteil has a secretary's build, spectacles, ink-stained fingers and blue eyes, and Cirille found him looking like a [Scribe] rather than a war hero. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L735-736,L754)
- Quiteil has a luminescent white patch of skin that covers his wrist and moves up his arm. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L741-742)
## Command style
- Quiteil asks his officers many questions and sends them repeated priority messages that need authentication; he makes no small talk and does not look up while giving orders. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L702,L740,L751)
- Quiteil told Cirille that he has no time to explain himself to every officer who questions his command. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L758)
- Cirille's contacts said he runs a tight ship, requires subordinates to know how many arrows they hold, and loves reorganizing units. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L785,L1130)
- Quiteil assigned two thousand one hundred and three Gnolls under Chief Warrior Merish to Cirille's command, and kept the arrangement in force when objections were raised. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L749,L752; Book 18, Ch7.35C L362)
- Quiteil defended the use of Rhirian [Necromancers], saying necromancy has never been outlawed in Rhir and that he would gladly take ten thousand more like Mage-Captain Hetarria. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1173,L1178)
- {belief} A [Huntress] said he came closest to panicking when someone shouted "Deathless" for fun, and that he had the Lizardperson arrested and shipped back to Baleros. (Book 18, Ch7.34C L1302)
- During the serpent-titan attack, Quiteil opened a Tier 7 scroll box from the capital with the [Mage-General] and teleported the scroll to Zavarial. (Book 18, Ch7.35C L180-188)
