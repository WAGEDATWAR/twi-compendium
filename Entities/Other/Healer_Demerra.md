# Healer Demerra
> Type: person | Also called: Demerra | First appears: Book 16, Ch7.17S
> See also: Chaldion, Pallass, Yellow Rivers disease, Geneva Scala, Healer of Tenbault
## Overview
- Healer Demerra is a female Gnoll [Healer], a City-Gnoll born and raised in Pallass, in a leadership that is mostly Drakes. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L162-163)
- Demerra runs a personal treatment clinic where Grand Strategist Chaldion is treated, and the text calls her the best [Healer] there, and a Pallass broadcast later presents her as Pallass' own top [Healer]. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L162; Book 18, Ch7.32D L86)
## Book 16: Chaldion's fall
- Demerra tells the crowd of officials that Chaldion is fine for someone of his age falling down the stairs, but needs rest and no visitors. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L164)
- Demerra says Chaldion is too old for potions to work perfectly and that she told him so last time. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L169)
- Demerra says Chaldion should have a personal [Healer] or [Bodyguard] with the right specialization on him at all times, a [Minder] at the very least. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L169)
- Demerra says that, even with her best crystals and recovery Skills, Chaldion must sit on his broken leg for at least two days. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L169)
- Demerra tells General Edellein that bodies develop resistances to healing potions, and that he recovered faster because he is half Chaldion's age. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L170-171)
- Demerra laughs at General Edellein's suggestion of the Healer of Tenbault and says that getting her to visit Pallass would be a miracle. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L173-175)
- Demerra insists that Chaldion needs an aide and that someone has to order him to have one. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L175,L179)
- Demerra tells Edellein that a Ring of Health would help Chaldion but is relic-class, and that he would quickly be dependent on it. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L187)
- Demerra reminds the officials that Chaldion is one hundred and six years old, then sends them to the waiting room and seals her clinic against intruders. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L192-194)
- Demerra's clinic offers expensive treatments such as healing crystals placed around the bed, which naturally boost recovery, and floating trays, though she cannot cure old age. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L260-261)
- Demerra allows no pests in her clinic. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L345)
- Demerra gives Saliss a look of envy and annoyance because she knows, or guesses, what he can do. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L418)
- Demerra organises the gatherings of a wealthy group that bids on collections, and she has a collection of fine healing crystals that rejuvenate the body. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L669,L672)
## Book 18: the Yellow Rivers disease
- Demerra is interviewed by Sir Relz on a Pallass broadcast about the Yellow Rivers disease, of which Pallass has reported six cases while Zeres has over a thousand. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L86-88)
- {belief} Demerra says that her magical crystals have reversed the illness' effects in her patients and that a crystal should enhance the body's natural capabilities and lower the chance of catching the disease. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L99-101)
- {belief} Demerra says her healing beds have cured three of the sick people, who are completely healthy, and that she is selling the crystals. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L101)
- Demerra gifts a healing crystal to the broadcasting Drakes, who display it on their desk. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1103)
- Drassi argues on air that even Demerra admits a full recovery still takes a week in her healing beds, and that Demerra, with six infected people in Pallass, is not on the front lines of the problem. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1149,L1159-1161)
- Demerra is present as Grimalkin and Chaldion read Geneva Scala's correspondence, there at Grimalkin's request and to follow her patient Chaldion, who snuck out of her clinic. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1036)
- Demerra has two patients with the disease in a room in her clinic. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1038-1040)
- Demerra protests that her crystals are enough, but Chaldion tells her to treat the patients as the [Doctor] prescribes, and she bows after a moment's hesitation. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1042-1058)
- Demerra has questions for Geneva Scala about one patient who is doing poorly, and Geneva spends the day helping with the clinic's patients, someone having just broken a leg. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1274)
