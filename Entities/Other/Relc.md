# Relc
> Type: person | Also called: Relc Grasstongue; Gecko of Liscor; Senior Guardsman Relc | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.07
> See also: Klbkch, Embria, Erin Solstice, Zevara, Senior Guardsman, Liscor City Watch
## Overview
- Relc is a Drake guardsman of Liscor's City Watch, a Senior Guardsman over six and a half feet tall with light green scales, sharp claws, a forked tongue, a spiny crest, a bass voice and eyes like a snake's. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L12,L79,L83,L212; Book 1, Ch1.42 L346)
- Relc is a [Spearmaster] with levels in [Guardsman] as well, and Drakes like him can breathe fire. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L174,L219)
- Relc has the skills [Dangersense], which makes his scales itch near danger, and [Thick Skin] and [Iron Skin], the last skill from his [Soldier] class, though he thinks [Spearmaster] may also grant it. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L266; Book 1, Ch1.14 L28-35)
- Relc has ear-holes on the sides of his head and dislikes the Captain of the Watch. (Book 1, Ch1.08 L85-88)
- Relc says his spear throw never misses and he is strong enough to peel Erin off with two fingers. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L179,L217)
- Relc says he is a former soldier of the 1st Wing of the Liscorian Army and has killed more mages than Pisces has levels. (Book 1, Ch1.11 L132)
- A named appraisal gives Relc as Relc Grasstongue, a former sergeant of the 1st Wing of the Liscorian army, with Level 33 in [Spearmaster], Level 12 in [Guardsman] and negligible [Archer] and [Carpenter] levels. (Book 1, Ch1.38 L157)
- Selys said Relc is the strongest active city guard on the continent, perhaps the world, and that he quit the army to become a guardsman. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L143-145,L157)
- Relc says he is the highest-level [Guardsman] in the city. (Book 1, Ch1.31 L304)
- Klbkch said Relc is a higher-level [Guardsman] than he, though Relc lacks [Detect Guilt]. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L122)
