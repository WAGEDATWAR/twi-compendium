# Hobs
> Type: species | Also called: Hobgoblins; the Redfang Hobs | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.41
> See also: Redfang Warriors, Goblins, Raskghar, Numbtongue
## Overview
- Five Redfang Hobs form a strike team with the mission to track down the Raskghar, get Mrsha and Ceria, and escape the dungeon. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L197)
- The Hobs take Badarrow's cursed bell of pain but leave the cursed necklace for fear of what it might do. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L198)
- The Hobs once belonged to the Redfang Tribe in the High Passes, which fought dangerous monsters at the base of the mountain range. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L194-195)
## In the dungeon
- {event} The Hobs ambush a four-Raskghar patrol that is moving behind Cave Goblin scouts, kill them, and persuade the freed Cave Goblins that the Hobs are Goblins too. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L324-327)
- The Hobs then find a Raskghar camp of two dozen Raskghar with at least fifty Goblin slaves, whom the Raskghar torment and eat. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L330-333)
- {event} Badarrow ties the cursed bell to an arrow, and its clang drops the Cave Goblins in pain and cripples the Raskghar before the Hobs charge shouting "Redfang!" against twenty-six of them. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L346-350)
- The Raskghar all fall to the Hobs, with Badarrow picking off enemies with arrows from behind. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L350,L372-373)
## Afterwards
- An old Hob who is offered garlic bread hopes that the Chieftain needs old Hobs, and Headscratcher answers that the group has no Chieftain. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L273-275)
- The Redfangs wonder whether Erin might be the Chieftain. (Book 9, Ch5.53 L276)
- {state} Zevara agrees to let the other Redfang Hobs out of Liscor's prison as a sign of goodwill, and they climb down from the wall to meet the Cave Goblins. (Book 9, Ch5.57 L580-583,L593-595)
