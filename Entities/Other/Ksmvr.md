# Ksmvr
> Type: person | Also called: Ksmvr of the Antinium of Liscor; Prognugator Ksmvr | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.30
> See also: Free Queen, Aberration, Calruz
## Overview
- Ksmvr is an Antinium, named at the end of the Queen's orders in Book 1 as her new Prognugator, who walks to the surface with twin swords at his waist to deal with the Council and investigate the aberration. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L256-262)
- Ksmvr looks exactly like Klbkch in every small detail and has Klbkch's voice, but he is slimmer than the Workers, with four arms, large multifaceted eyes and a slightly darker carapace. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L210-215)
- Ksmvr introduces himself to Erin as "not Klbkch", of the Antinium of Liscor, and serves his Queen as Prognugator. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L218)
- Ksmvr carries two swords and two daggers. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L186)
## Book 1: Prognugator of the Free Antinium
- Ksmvr states that Antinium who refer to themselves as individuals or have names are prone to fits of violence, and that his role is to judge such individuals and terminate them. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L236)
- Ksmvr takes Pawn into custody, cutting off Pawn's antenna when he speaks, and tells Erin that interfering with his duties is a crime. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L230-232,L251,L256)
- {event} When Erin refuses to stand aside, Ksmvr strikes her hard enough that she sees stars, and decapitates Toren when the skeleton attacks him. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L263-265)
- Ksmvr says the Hive within Liscor has been in disarray and that his appointment was out of necessity. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L240)
- Ksmvr waits on a hilltop outside Liscor with four Soldiers, since pacts forbid the Soldiers above ground inside Liscor except in emergency or war. (Book 1, Ch1.38 L99-102)
- Ksmvr privately believes his Queen was wrong to appraise Erin as insignificant, thinking her important and perhaps unique. (Book 1, Ch1.38 L103-104)
- Ksmvr calls himself Prognugator of the Free Antinium of Liscor, and the narration says he became Prognugator before his training was done. (Book 1, Ch1.38 L150,L177)
- Ksmvr decides not to capture Erin because Gazi the Omniscient and Relc make the cost too high, and has no idea what to do for the first time in his short life. (Book 1, Ch1.38 L178-180)
- In Liscor's battle against the undead, Ksmvr brings fifteen Soldiers and tells Zevara that the Watch Captain may command at most fifty Soldiers and a hundred Workers in a crisis declared by the Council. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L186,L198)
- Ksmvr tells Zevara that his position will cease in a few hours and another will take his place. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L234)
- Ksmvr pulls the Soldiers back to guard the Hive, telling Zevara that Liscor may fall but the Hive shall never be taken. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L66,L86)
- Ksmvr reasons that the Queen's safety during the Rite of Anastases outranks the Bargain with Liscor. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L144-146)
- {event} Ksmvr is knocked down by a punch, feels his Queen's anger as she completes the Rite, and a figure stands over him. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L171-176)
- {state} Ksmvr kneels trembling before his wroth Queen. (Book 1, Ch1.45 L164)
