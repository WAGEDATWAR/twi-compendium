# Kerash
> Type: person | Also called: Commander Kerash; Kerash the Chieftain | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.25
> See also: Az'kerash, Silverfang tribe, Krshia Silverfang
## Overview
- This entry gathers two uses of the name: an undead Gnoll commander in Az'kerash's service and a Gnoll Chieftain remembered as the greatest of his people. The texts cited here do not say whether they are the same person. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L273-274; Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L440)
## Commander of the Endless Legions
- {event} An undead Gnoll skeleton in armor announces itself as Kerash, Commander of the Endless Legions of Az'Kerash, and raises a battleaxe to attack the living soldiers with the undead Bea. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L273-276,L286)
- Kerash, Venitra and Bea stand around Ryoka when she leaves the Necromancer's room. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L402)
- {belief} Venitra, Kerash and Bea discuss that the Master has let Ryoka go, and one says his decision may be in error, though another notes that he does not make mistakes and they agree to hold her until he is finished. (Book 2, Ch2.25 L403-407)
## The Gnoll Chieftain
- {belief} Krshia says Kerash was the greatest of the Gnoll Chieftains and died a century ago. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L440)
