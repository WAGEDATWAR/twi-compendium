# Xrn (the Small Queen)
> Type: person | Also called: Xrn, the Small Queen; Small Queen | First appears: Book 9, Ch5.60
> See also: Free Queen, Klbkch, Grand Queen, Prognugators
## Overview
- Xrn, the Small Queen, stands on an earth platform and addresses fourteen Prognugators and one Revalantor from three Hives: the Flying, Silent and Armored Antinium. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L387)
- Xrn tells them to kill only Goblins and Humans and not Drakes unless ordered, and says that she, the Free Queen and Klbkch will lead in battle, with their commands superseding those of the other Queens. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L386)
- Xrn's eyes shine with several colours, red for battle excitement, blue for anticipation, and bright yellow and white for hope. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L389)
- Workers from all three Hives dig a vast tunnel north to Liscor, so that an army will be there to take back the city from the Humans when Liscor falls and its people retreat into the Free Antinium's Hive. (Book 9, Ch5.60 L392-394)
## Later
- Xrn is azure blue, and the last of the three Centenium of Izril, with eyes of dancing pink, yellow and a hint of orange mischief. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L30)
- Xrn tells the Grand Queen that the Free Queen must explain an incident to the Liscorian government, and the Free Queen is grateful for Xrn's lie. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L31-35)
