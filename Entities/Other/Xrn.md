# Xrn
> Type: person | Also called: the Small Queen; Azure; Aunt; Xrn of the Centenium | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.23L
> See also: Klbkch, Wrymvr, Pawn, Galuc, Antinium, Centenium
## Overview
- Xrn is an azure-carapaced Antinium with swirling multicolored eyes, a slim body and only two arms, who stands out from every other Antinium in Liscor's Hive. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L13; Book 3, Ch3.23L L418)
- Xrn is the Prognugator of the Grand Queen of the Antinium, known by her enemies as the Small Queen. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L14)
- Xrn's class is [Thaumaturge], and what she works is Wonders rather than ordinary magic. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L9; Book 5, Ch4.13L L420-421)
- Xrn carries a staff, which she raises to cast her spells. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L455; Book 5, Ch4.13L L404)
- Her eyes change color with her mood, such as orange and red, cold blue-grey, or a swirling vortex of colors. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L24,L34,L37)
## Appearance in Liscor
- Xrn tells Klbkch that the Custodium are partial Prognugators with natural blades of a dark substance, four of whom accompany her. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L431-434)
- {event} Pawn blocks Klbkch with twenty Soldiers as Klbkch and Xrn stand ready to fight in the street, while Guardsmen form a line. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L536-542)
- Xrn casts [Featherfall] to slow a fall, and she is named "Xrn the Small Queen" when she meets Zel. (Book 4, Ch3.27M L455,L463,L491)
- Xrn finds Klbkch at the inn at Christmas and watches the mistletoe kisses with fascination. (Book 4, Ch3.41 L226-227)
- Xrn scolds Klbkch for running off to the inn and avoiding her for the fifth time, and asks whether his Queen knows. (Book 4, Ch3.42 L45-48)
- Xrn tells Zel that of all those who fought in the Antinium Wars she was the one who never fell. (Book 4, Ch3.42 L344)
- Xrn tells Zel that she was there when General Sserys died but did not kill him, and that a single thrust to the heart was dealt by Wrymvr the Deathless. (Book 4, Ch3.42 L351-365)
- Xrn tells Klbkch that her class is [Thaumaturge], and she conjures ice, fire, lightning and magma on his desk. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L9,L54-55)
## The Antinium plan
- Xrn says she has regained almost all her lost levels but has not told the Grand Queen of her leveling for a long period. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L73,L76)
- Xrn says she came against the Grand Queen's wishes, that the Queen would have preferred Tersk to lead, and that only she, Klbkch and perhaps Wrymvr are competent Prognugators. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L80-83)
- Xrn says the Grand Queen does not care whether Klbkch's Hive has created Individuals, and that the Queen of the Silent Antinium sent no envoy. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L85,L88)
- {reveal} Xrn reveals that no new Antinium Queens have been born since their arrival on this continent over two decades ago. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L121-125)
- Xrn says she has tried repeatedly to create Queens, burned what she created, and proposes that they return to Rhir to recover their lost knowledge. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L152-160)
- Xrn plans to learn to sail, pass the Blighted King and the Demon King, and bring back a younger Queen with the necessary knowledge. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L181-185)
- Xrn wants the [Strategists] Belgrade and Anand for the plan, and intends to use the flawed Prognugator copies the Grand Queen made, which she encouraged. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L188-191)
- {belief} Xrn believes the Antinium who fought on in Rhir swore to fight for a thousand years and have continued to do battle. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L178)
- Xrn says she told Zel to find Wrymvr and thinks Wrymvr and his Queen have gone insane. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L170-175)
## Fighting and spellcasting
- {event} Xrn shields Belgrade with her magic, announces herself as the Small Queen, casts [Burning Lightning] against the Crypt Worm, and heals Belgrade's injuries. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L403-405,L418-419)
- Xrn tells Pawn that the duty of the Antinium is to survive and that they go to war and die so that some may live. (Book 5, Ch4.15L L407-409)
- Xrn tells the Queen of the Free Antinium that the Grand Queen wants her to return to the Hives. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L340-344)
- Ryoka recognizes Xrn as a legend of the Second Antinium War, and Xrn casts [Stomach of the Cow] and [Powerful Digestion] before going to eat. (Book 5, Ch4.28 L365,L393-394)
- In the Antinium Wars Xrn casts [Venomous Snow], [Thunderstrike Volley], [Chaos Flamewheel] and [Glorious Radiance], and is struck in the chest by an arrow. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L98-102)
- Xrn tells the others that Klbkch was created as a warrior and assassin, she was meant to cast magic, and Wrymvr was meant to defend the Grand Queen. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L133)
- Xrn says the Centenium were made to be heroes. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L142-144)
- Xrn thinks of Tersk as an ill-made clone of Galuc with armor, and of her escort as poor copies of true Antinium. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.3 L141)
- Xrn says she, Klbkch and Wrymvr were ordered to break the Goblin King's might, and that Tallis Stormbreaker could not repeat his grand spell when the second and third armies were sent. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L55)
- The Grand Queen lost three armies to the Goblin King's forces before she stopped fighting in open engagements. (Book 6, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.4 L60)
