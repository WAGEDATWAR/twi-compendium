# BlackMage
> Type: person | Also called: Blackmage; Aaron Vanwell; Aaron | First appears: Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call
> See also: Chat, Wistram factions, Feor, Nailihuaile, Elena, Ryoka Griffin
## Overview
- [BlackMage] is the screen name of the user who sets up the magical phone chat connecting Earth people in the new world. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L13,L19)
- BlackMage's real name is Aaron Vanwell, but within Wistram Academy he goes by Blackmage and hardly anyone uses his real name. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L5)
## The first chat
- BlackMage says he thought up the phone connection but that it takes a lot of magical power, and that he used a chat because many voices at once would be too confusing. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L13,L19)
- BlackMage says he is a Level 11 [Engineer] and a Level 8 [Mage], and that this is how he set up the call. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L54)
- BlackMage says he got the [Engineer] class by taking apart his iPhone and some of his electronics. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L56)
- BlackMage relays that some people on his end say no one has gotten the [Hero] class in centuries. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L74)
- BlackMage tells the chat that a person needs to be activated by another magic-user, because not everyone has the potential to do magic. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L234)
- BlackMage warns the chat that the mage he is with says first and last names can be used to scry on a person's location and appearance. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L318)
- {reveal} Kent Scott deduces that only one group could cast a spell reaching across the world and tells BlackMage he is in Wistram Academy. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L322)
## Calling Ryoka
- {event} A young man with an English accent calls Ryoka Griffin's iPhone, introduces himself as [BlackMage] from the chat and asks if she is [batman], and Ryoka angrily rebukes him for making a call that could reveal locations. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L355-370)
- BlackMage tells Ryoka that he and the mages of Wistram are trying to find people across the world and bring them to Wistram, and that they are using magic in secret. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L375-377)
- BlackMage says he contacted Ryoka first because she uncovered [Kent Scott] as a fake and warned everyone. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L383)
- When Laken takes the phone he tells BlackMage to call him "L", says he is from the same world and has just met [batman]. (Book 4, Ch3.40 L390)
## Life at Wistram
- {reveal} The narration gives his real name as Aaron Vanwell and says that months earlier he opened his college dorm door to get a pizza and found himself standing in a room with Golems. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L5,L47-49)
- He was brought to Cognita, who demanded to know how he came there, then summoned four Archmages who debated, cast magic on him and accepted his story, after which he entered Wistram and has not left. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L50)
- Over eighty mages linked to perform the spell that he and Feor worked on for two weeks, after he tinkered with his phone to call other iPhones in this world. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L51)
- {state} By the time of the Halloween-style banquet he has reached Level 18 in [Mage]. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L55)
- Feor told Aaron never to use his real name in Wistram's halls once it was discovered that someone was scrying others by their real names. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L54)
- His [Engineer] class comes from painstakingly taking apart his watch and iPhone and relying on Feor's [Repair] spell to put them back together. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L131)
- {event} Blackmage pays the Dullahan Beatrice with a secret written on a piece of paper, which she burns after reading, for distracting Teura for about twenty minutes so he can slip away. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L211-224)
- {state} Feor decides to cast a lock spell that stops Blackmage from speaking about confidential matters, but Archmage Naili tells him he is now with the Revivalists and has protectors and allies. (Book 8, ChInterlude_Blackmage L319,L385)
## Magitech and the Earthers
- Aaron Vanwell is Wistram's first Earther guest and builds a battery combining magicore crystals with the principles of a lead-acid battery. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L160-161)
- Archmage Nailihuaile helps Aaron make the orb, and he miniaturizes the battery into a glove painted red and gold. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L163-164)
- Aaron puts on the glove and lightning arcs from it along a path he has created. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L249-250)
- Aaron counts eighteen Earthers at Wistram so far and nine more on the way, and knows they are prisoners or well-treated guests who cannot leave the island. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L247)
- {event} Blackmage stands on a table aiming a wand at his own head and threatens to blow his head off to stop Nailihuaile introducing commercials and pay-per-view, and she gives in. (Book 15, Ch7.10K L95-105)
- {event} Aaron tells Elena about trying his shock glove, which sends lightning to the nearest metal thing, and they are at the Wistram buffet when every Golem freezes for about a minute before moving again. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L502-504,L514-516,L523-530)
- Elena, a beautician and member of Feor's Centrist faction, mouths the word "Robots" to Aaron after the Golems freeze. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L520,L533-534)
- Naili is unhappy that the cake recipe leaked and suspects the Ullsinoi faction. (Book 16, Ch7.14T L512-513)
- Aaron has found a secret room that he told no one about, including Nailihuaile, and which tracking spells cannot follow into. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L612)
- Aaron hides artifacts under a floor hatch opened by an invisible latch that took him six hours to find, but he says he had been told it was there. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L613-614)
- {belief} Aaron tells the other Earthers he thinks a ghost of an Archmage named Emerrhain, whom he met during the winter, is hiding in Wistram. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L675-685)
- Archmage Amerys helped Aaron with magicore batteries, and he builds a magical sailboat for the [Sailor] Lamont using a Naili wind spell powered by a magicore battery. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L929-935)
- Aaron pitches Mage Galei and Mage Taxiela on illusion combat scenarios and videogame-like entertainments, and Telim helps trial an undead scenario in which Aaron is declared out. (Book 18, Ch7.29B L1109-1115,L1117-1133)
- Elena says Aaron Vanwell, called Blackmage, was the first Earther at Wistram, has never left it, and is about nineteen, with around forty-one Earthers there, about half of them over eighteen. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L708-710)
