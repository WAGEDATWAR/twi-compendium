# Lord Erill
> Type: person | Also called: Erill; Lord Erill of House Fienst | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.30G
> See also: Lady Ieka, Lord Gralton, Lord Yitton, Lord Tyrion Veltras, Circle of Thorns
## Overview
- Lord Erill is a merchant-lord, a noble who built his fortune and amassed enough power and land to be considered true nobility. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L25)
- He carries no sword and is not a warrior. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L25)
- Erill and Lady Ieka are protected by enchantments that keep the rain from falling on them as they leave a tent with Gralton and Yitton. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L28)
- Yitton sees Erill as one of the richer [Lords] and newly come to his nobility. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L334)
- Tyrion Veltras presents Lady Ieka of House Imarris, Lord Gralton of House Radivaek, Lord Erill of House Fienst and Lord Yitton of House Byres as representatives of the larger gathering of nobility. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L421)
- {event} Erill gives Lady Ieka a bag of holding with two hundred thousand gold pieces' worth of gold, gems and goods as a donation for the Circle of Thorns, which he says is repayment to Magnolia Reinhart, who is to know nothing of it. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L636-642)
- He wants nothing to do with the Circle of Thorns and leaves quickly once his task is done. (Book 9, ChInterlude_B09_1 L640)
