# Knight
> Type: person | Also called: the Worker Knight | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.42
> See also: Erin Solstice, Pawn, Chess, Antinium
## Overview
- Knight is an Antinium Worker who names himself, and who looks and sounds like Pawn but seems different to Erin. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L296-300; Book 1, Ch1.43 L51)
- Knight is one of Erin's chess-club Workers, who all choose names that night and come to protect her inn. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L297-300,L316-327)
## Naming and the chess club
- {event} Knight kneels to Erin, declares that he has named himself and chosen to be an individual, and says he is there to protect her. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L296-300)
- Knight says Pawn was kept in the Hive by the Prognugator and remains there. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L312-313)
- The Workers around the inn name themselves Bishop, Vladimir, Calabrian, Emanuel, Garry, Milner-Barry, Belgrade and Jose, and Erin realizes they are her chess club. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L316-327)
- The Worker Magnus bears a name taken from the World Chess Champion Magnus Carlsen, and Garry is named after the Grandmaster Garry Kasparov. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L22,L227)
## Defence of the inn
- {event} After a deranged Worker who speaks in mixed upper and lower case, claims to be individual yet rejects everything and blames Erin, attacks her with a sword, another Worker throws him off and breaks his neck, and that Worker is Knight. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L266-288,L291-297)
- {event} Knight uses [Defense Formation] and the Worker Magnus uses [Attack Formation], and Erin feels the effects of both Skills, feeling lighter and aware of everything around her. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L30-35)
- Knight tells Erin that thirty-four undead have been killed and that the rest have retreated and will return in greater numbers. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L43-45)
- Knight tells Erin that a horde of the undead emerged from the Ruins and besieged Liscor about an hour earlier. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L52)
- Knight tells Erin that several adventurers reached the city alive, that none of them were Minotaurs or elves, and that the only survivors were humans, including a captain who is a woman in silver armor. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L58-62)
- The Worker Calabrian reports that over four hundred undead have attacked Liscor and that the Watch is fighting them in the streets. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L69-70)
- Knight reflects that the [Tactician] class levels with chess but that chess does not teach fighting or account for unpredictability. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L194)
- Knight orders the Workers to close ranks into a wall around the inn, tells Erin to stay inside, closes the door and puts his back to it. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L197-202)
## Death
- {state} Knight dies standing in front of the inn's door, stabbed and bitten by the undead while trying to cover it, after asking Garry if Erin is still safe. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L218-243)
- Knight's last wish is to play one more game of chess with Erin, opening with the Danish Opening. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L241-242)
- Only Erin notices the moment Knight dies. (Book 1, Ch1.43 L243-244)
