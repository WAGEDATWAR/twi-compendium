# Free Queen
> Type: person | Also called: Queen of the Free Antinium; the Free Antinium Queen; the Queen of the Antinium under Liscor; Klbkch's Queen | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.24
> See also: Ksmvr, Aberration, Chess, Law of Liscor
## Overview
- The Free Queen is the Queen of the Free Antinium hive beneath Liscor, a massive creature who sits in a dark cavern. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L232)
- In Book 1 she is a large female Antinium in the darkness whom Klbkch addresses as "my lady" and "my Queen". (Book 1, Ch1.24 L5-8,L52)
- She calls Klbkch her Propugnator and champion, and addresses him as "Klbkchhzeim of the Free". (Book 1, Ch1.24 L11,L31,L39)
## Appearance
- Erin sees the Queen as gargantuan and bloated, so huge she cannot move from her spot, and completely insectile unlike her humanoid-looking subjects, with wide faceted eyes that glow dim orange-red. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L10-11)
- Two silent guard Antinium nearly twice Klbkch's size flank the doors, with massive forearms and spiked gauntlet-like arms and no real digits. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L14-18)
## Book 1: Klbkch, the inn and the Aberrations
- Klbkch asks the Queen to let ten or twenty Workers visit a local inn for several hours, and she asks whether the idea is his own. (Book 1, Ch1.24 L6-11)
- {event} The Queen tells Klbkch the Others are moving, speaking of envoys and an army, and allows the risk: he may take twelve Workers to visit the Solstice child. (Book 1, Ch1.24 L31-35)
- The Queen tells Klbkch she needs success, warns that the Plan has faltered too long and that if the next generations fail more Antinium will fall back into the old ways. (Book 1, Ch1.24 L39-46)
- The Queen asks Klbkch how the acid flies taste, and he promises to bring her a sample. (Book 1, Ch1.24 L51-52)
- Below Liscor the Queen senses the events at the inn, sends her soldiers marching to the surface at her command, and whispers "Klbkchhzeim?". (Book 1, Ch1.25 L202-203)
- After Klbkch's death, the Queen has Erin brought before her and says she holds Erin accountable for the death. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L12,L19)
- The Queen says Klbkch's death in itself means little because individuals die in service to the whole, but that dying to Goblins was wasted, and that Klbkch was more than a match for a hundred Goblins. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L28-30)
- {belief} The Queen tells Erin she sees no reason Klbkch would have wasted his life saving her, and says he died a failure. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L32,L39,L43)
- The Queen complains that her Workers play games in their rest periods and gain useless levels, and that three have already become Aberration, calling this experiment wasted. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L35)
- Hearing what Klbkchhzeim told Erin, the Queen calls him a fool as well as a failure and says the Antinium are not free. (Book 1, Ch1.26 L49)
- Far below Liscor, the Queen is busy on a project that needs all her attention, and orders the Antinium reporting to her to deal with the Council while she finishes. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L249-255)
- The Queen tells her Prognugator to investigate the aberration on the surface, and he promises to dispose of any Aberrations he finds. (Book 1, Ch1.30 L256-258)
- Relc says the Queen spoke of "time lost" and needless valuable resources expended when speaking with the Captain, but never about Klbkch. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L99)
- Ksmvr tells Zevara that the Queen is in the middle of a critical process, hours or less from completion, and that the Antinium will cooperate in defending the city once she finishes the Rite of Anastases. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L204,L210)
- {state} Later, Ksmvr finds the Soldiers streaming back out of the Hive, the Queen having finished the Rite and returned, and he feels her anger in his mind. (Book 1, Ch1.44 L160-173)
## Book 2 and the rivalry with the Great Queen
- In Book 2 Ksmvr reports to the Queen, who is called the Queen of the Free Antinium within Liscor, and who thinks of him as inexperienced and not Klbkch. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L232-233,L272)
- The Queen says it seems Klbkchhzeim was correct that Erin Solstice is needed for now. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L264)
- She regards Erin as important but not vital, since the Individuals can be replicated, and so the Antinium can continue the experiment. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L273-274)
- Rivalry exists among the Queens: the Free Queen has endured the ridicule and scorn of her fellow Queens for years and expects the Great Queen to learn of her success. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L276-277)
- She is in sore need of another assistant but is barred from making another Prognugator or Queen by the Great Queen and the pact made with Liscor. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L280)
- She thinks Pawn has levels in [Tactician], perhaps not as high as her own. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L281)
- Klbkch has argued for years that adventurers should help with the dungeon, thought he had convinced the Queen after Skinner, and finds she wants one Named Adventurer and twice as many Gold-rank ones. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L13,L33)
- Klbkch thinks the Queen is old and might need the Rite of Anastases, which would require bringing her to another Queen. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L39)
## Books 4 to 5: the other Hives
- {event} In Book 4 the Queen receives envoys of four Hives: Prognugator Tersk with three Armored Antinium, Revalantor Pivr of the Flying Antinium with six, two Silent Antinium soldiers, and Xrn of the Centenium, now Prognugator to the Grand Queen. (Book 4, Ch3.34 L373)
- Xrn tells Klbkch that the Queen has no resources, denied to her by the Hives, and is surprised she managed to alter his form, let alone resurrect him. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L143)
- Klbkch tells Xrn that the needed reagents can be obtained, with difficulty, by purchasing from [Alchemists]. (Book 5, Ch4.07 L145)
- {event} Klbkch tells the Queen what Xrn told him: that the Grand Queen is not trying to create more Queens, which breaks her faith in the Antinium on Izril. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L173-175)
- The Queen drags herself down a tunnel no Antinium but Klbkch enters, past airtight rooms she built for mixing enzymes and reagents. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L178-181)
- One room holds corpses and embryos of failed experiments; the Queen rediscovered the Rite of Anastasis there, used it to save Klbkch's life, and created Ksmvr there. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L183-184)
- {event} The Queen begins experiments to create a new Queen, using her own eggs, vowing to sacrifice her Hive and body if she must. (Book 5, Ch4.24 L187,L195,L198)
## Book 8: Bird, Garry and the sealed chambers
- The Queen speaks through Klbkch by a link that works through rock and water, so that when she speaks he speaks and she hears what he hears. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L2,L59)
- Through Klbkch, the Queen tells Zevara the Antinium first became aware of the dungeon a few months ago. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L17)
- Klbkch states for the Queen that the agreement between Liscor's Council and the Free Antinium exempts the Hive from Liscor's legal restrictions and that the dungeon would not count as Liscorian property. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L29)
- She has lived in the Hive for over a decade, can feel thousands of Antinium around her, and she and Klbkch have labored for a decade toward true individuality. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L61-62,L89)
- {belief} The Queen considers the Individuals who failed her test incomplete, not true Antinium and not people, only useful tools for Klbkch and herself. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L88)
- The Queen is lonely, since only Klbkch keeps her company and only two real Antinium are in the Hive. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L90)
- She eats cooked food prepared by Garry, savouring a fried Shield Spider abdomen stuffed with lentils and egg after a decade of eating the Antinium's paste, and likes roast pork. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L74-78)
- She has a sealed, airtight chamber for her experiments, with sacks of fluid on the wall holding failed creations. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L92,L165-170)
- She tells Klbkch she spent four years reconfiguring muscle fibre and two more on balance to build his new form, and will not mass-produce it because it takes too much work and too many resources. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L275,L280)
- She calls the Flying Queen's Antinium forms flawed, and says the Silent Queen has improved on her designs. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L272-273)
- She says alchemical goods are too complex to work with without rendering. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L265)
- {event} The Queen declares Bird a flawed creation whose mind is broken, assumes an imperfection in the growing stage caused it, and says it was not his fault. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L592-605)
- She lifts Bird in her feelers and tightens them until his carapace cracks, but when he sings she freezes and loosens her grip, having heard no song in the Hive since coming to Izril. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L618,L621-624)
- She tells Bird she finds him worthy and will allow him to live in her chambers and sing for her each day. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L648-650)
- She thinks that Bird may be close to Galuc the Builder, or unique. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L679)
- She gives Bird a bag of holding full of gold coins to buy a bow. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L714-715)
- {event} A vessel delivered by the Grand Queen restores contact and the Free Antinium are told they have made adequate progress and will rejoin the larger Hive through this relay. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L216)
## Books 9 to 11: Bird's tests
- In Book 9, Drones with six feelers and no arms tend an injured Bird, using gels and a living blood sac designed as a store of organs for the Queen to harvest. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L90-95)
- The Queen has learned Garry's name and been kinder to him, and gives him time off to visit the inn on condition that he bring back the choicest morsels. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L17-19)
- She eats Garry's pizza, sweet potato hamburgers and cake, saying chemical imbalances were allowed in Galuc's modified form but not in Queens, and tastes concentrated sugar for the first time. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L185-200)
- She tells Bird the Antinium will fail on purpose to destroy more than one or two trebuchets, since Klbkch must speak only truths to Drakes who use truth-detection spells. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L196,L202)
- She says that when the Hive must be defended, Pawn will lead the Painted Soldiers and other Antinium in to move Liscor's citizens into it. (Book 9, Ch5.61 L215)
- {event} When the Painted Soldiers march to save Erin, the Free Queen denies ordering it, and then pretends she cannot hear the Grand Queen's orders over a malfunctioning vessel and scrying glass. (Book 9, Ch5.62 L469-498)
- In Book 11 the Queen tests Bird and Garry a second time on the purpose of the Antinium, and Bird's answer is judged wrong. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L736-743)
- She explains that the test is not about the question but about hearing the answer she thinks, which she can send to them through her feelers, while they can only be heard. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L752,L757-763)
- {belief} The Free Queen states that the purpose of the Antinium is to return to Rhir, destroy the thing in the darkness and avenge the Antinium, in the name of "her". (Book 11, Ch6.29 L780)
- The Free Queen tells Bird and Garry that she was a barely created Junior Queen on the fleet sent to take Terandria. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L830)
- The fleet's troops were sealed in stasis aboard ships and died asleep, pursued by Rhir's warships, then attacked by pirates, then storms, and then a Kraken. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L836-838,L840-843)
- Sixteen of the Centenium died there, Klbkchhezeim lost his true form, and fewer than twenty juniors landed on Izril. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L845-848)
- Her mentor Bessachidia, a Shaper Queen who could weave forms as well as any but the First Queen, was her teacher and one of the finest Queens ever created. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L867)
## Books 14 to 18
- In Book 14, when Klbkch leads wounded Antinium against Flesh Worms and Ghouls, she takes direct control and speaks "Forwards!" into their minds. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L254-263)
- Klbkch tries a Skill he lost in the Rite of Anastases and stumbles; the Free Queen directs each Worker and Soldier individually. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L269,L273-275,L296)
- She is at least four decades old, was newly formed when she left Rhir, and learned shaping from Bessachidia alongside the Silent Queen. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L3,L6,L9)
- She sees through Klbkch's eyes, and the Free Antinium have been in exile for nearly a decade. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L35,L37)
- The Grand Queen argues that Bird is a Prognugator; the Free Queen replies that the Individual and Autonomous Antinium need no Antinium lives for their formation. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L23-26)
- {belief} She suspects the Silent Queen thinks she is withholding experimental designs that let Bird hit the Wyverns, and that other Queens misunderstood Bird. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L87,L107)
- {state} After a decade of neglect, the other Hives now look on the Free Antinium with wariness or approval. (Book 15, ChInterlude_Queens_and_Dragons L107)
- In Book 16, Bird asks the Free Queen if Garry may come with him; she says "Go, little bird". (Book 16, ChInterlude_Lifting_Ants L642,L651)
- In Book 18 the Free Queen spends three days in her laboratory while Xrn, Yellow Splatters, Pawn, Belgrade and Purple Smiles run the Hive, and Garry's helpers Pisca and Runel are Flying Antinium. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L173,L181-190)
- She feels this is how the Queens of Rhir once lived and that she is being treated like royalty. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L194,L197)
- Klbkch notes she is still experimenting with creating new Queens, while the Grand Queen says none of the remaining Queens are Shaper Queens and refuses to waste resources on it. (Book 18, Ch7.39A L507,L512)
