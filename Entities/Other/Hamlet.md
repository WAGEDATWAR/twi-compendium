# Hamlet
> Type: work | Also called: the play Hamlet | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.24
> See also: Macbeth, Players of Celum, Erin Solstice, Wesle, Jasi
## Overview
- Hamlet is a play from Erin's world that she taught to the Players of Celum, the [Actors] who perform at her inn and in Celum. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L223)
- The audience at the Frenzied Hare cheered the performance of Hamlet, including the Watch [Guardsman] with the fuzzy mustache who played Hamlet. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L215-218)
- Erin taught Hamlet after Romeo and Juliet, and the Players performed it each night to great success. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L220-223)
- Erin and the Players finished learning Hamlet and kept it for occasional performance, and then looked for another play. (Book 3, Ch3.25 L98)
- Colina told Wesle she loved his Hamlet calling his uncle a false [King]. (Book 7, Ch5.04 L99)
## Later performances
- A Dullahan watched Hamlet being shown on stage at the inn. (Book 11, Ch6.32 L1000)
- Grev listed Hamlet among the plays the Players of Celum have done. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L639)
- The Players put on Hamlet as a classic, with Jasi as Queen Gertrude, Wesle as Hamlet and Kilkran as Claudius. (Book 19, Ch7.50 L1421-1422)
