# Lord Gralton Radivaek
> Type: person | Also called: Lord Gralton; Gralton; Gralton of Lycit; the Dog Lord | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.30G
> See also: Laken Godart, Yitton Byres, Tyrion Veltras, Riverfarm, Dogs
## Overview
- Lord Gralton Radivaek is a large, roughly-dressed [Lord] in his early forties with a tattoo of a snarling dog on his cheek, who smells of wet dog. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L10)
- The nobles call Gralton the [Dog Lord] behind his back in jest, and he raises dogs. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L12; Book 9, Ch5.52 L612)
- When first seen at Tyrion Veltras' gathering he is introduced as Lord Gralton of Lycit. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L10)
- Gralton is of House Radivaek. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L421)
- Gralton is rumoured to be more beast than man, wild and savage compared with many [Lords], though Ryoka finds that rumour twisted fact and exaggerated truth. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L357-358)
## The Goblin Lord campaign
- Gralton rages in Tyrion Veltras' pavilion that the army has sat idle while Magnolia Reinhart took the fight to the Goblins, and Yitton Byres answers by asking whether he means to assault their field commander, saying he would rather come to blows than suffer mutiny. (Book 8, Ch5.30G L11-12,L19-20)
- Yitton Byres thinks Gralton is an excellent fighter and leader in his own way, a hothead who smells of wet dogs but who can evaluate good strategy. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L380)
- When Tyrion presents Laken Godart as an [Emperor], Gralton demands to know where he came from, half-rises with a low growl when Laken refuses to answer, and then sits down and laughs when Laken stares him down. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L604-612,L615-622)
- Gralton grins madly at Tyrion's talk of war, with a burning fever in his eyes, and Tyrion tells him that he has a way to open Liscor's walls. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L639,L654,L660)
## Companion of Laken
- Gralton and Yitton Byres ride with Laken as his companions, and a speaker at Riverfarm is shocked that Gralton is among them. (Book 12, Ch6.34E L832,L835)
- Laken thinks Gralton is impetuous and thoughtless at times, but higher-level than Yitton or Laken, and that he does not scoff at Laken's dreams or feelings. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L202)
- Gralton gives advice through stories of his own failures, such as a Creler nest in his lands for which he hired three Gold-rank teams and Baudin the Brass. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L203-204)
- Gralton and Yitton pay for the escort, the [Messages] and the food of Laken's caravan, mostly out of Gralton's money. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L184)
- Laken calls Gralton one of the richer [Lords] in the area and one of his new allies. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L687)
- Laken asks Gralton to ride ahead toward his lands if things get worse, in case he needs to bring back his army. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L211-212)
- {event} When Laken senses danger at Riverfarm, he tells Gralton to ride, and Gralton and his escort race ahead; Yitton says Gralton can muster thousands, and Laken thinks he should have sent Gralton the instant the coven arrived. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L779,L786,L791,L793)
## The plague and poison
- {event} Oldblood Drakes attack the nobility's lands, and Gralton races into his kennels to find the dogs sick and poisoned while a Drake spits acid into the water supply. (Book 12, Ch6.45E L838)
- Laken tells the [Witches] that the Drakes engineered a plague, or perhaps poison, in his friend's lands, and that Gralton's dogs and people lie dying while his [Healers] are stymied. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L203)
- Laken offers four hundred gold coins to each [Witch] who heals Gralton's sick, and tells Yitton to have Gralton put up the eight hundred pieces. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L217,L237)
- Many dogs die, and some people too, but Gralton cares more about the dogs. (Book 12, Ch6.47E L506)
- When Ryoka visits his lands, Gralton is kneeling in his kennels with red, weeping eyes, feeding a wheezing puppy goat's milk. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L359-362,L376)
- The kennels, built as a paradise for dogs, could house hundreds, but they are deathly silent and half-empty, since death swept through them. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L365,L377-380)
- The Drake came while Gralton was absent and spread plague and poison, getting the wells and the water, and poisoning the air; no one noticed except the dogs, and he says many people died, as well as dogs. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L368-369,L382)
- Gralton says he would never have known it was Drakes because the Drake was invisible. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L391)
- Gralton vows to kill all the Drakes who did this. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L382)
- Gralton tells Ryoka that the [Witches] already left, saying there was nothing more they could do. (Book 14, Ch6.60 L373)
## Gift to Riverfarm
- Gralton shows up in Riverfarm with nearly four dozen dogs of many breeds, as a gift for the Unseen Empire. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L728-730)
- Gralton says the kennels are filling up and that the dogs will be ratters, herders and guards, and that there would be no dogs to give if Riverfarm had not sent the [Witches]. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L730)
- Gralton introduces a blind dog, Draugmot, named after a Draug that its mother brought down. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L719)
- The narration calls Gralton surly and even oafish to anyone who walks on two legs, but a friend to four-legged animals everywhere. (Book 19, Ch7.55E L718)
