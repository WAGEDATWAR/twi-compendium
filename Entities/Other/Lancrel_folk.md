# Lancrel folk
> Type: org | Also called: people of Lancrel; Lancrel villagers | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.36E
> See also: Elmmet, Durene, Hedag, Riverfarm
## Overview
- {belief} A female Lancrel [Builder] tells Durene that Elmmet is a good [Councilman] and that people are saying there might have been a mix-up, since the [Thief] could change faces and might have set Elmmet up. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L232-233)
- Durene answers that she saw the thief change his face and that Beniar caught him. (Book 12, Ch6.36E L234)
- {belief} After Hedag's axe kills Councilman Elmmet in front of everyone, a woman from Lancrel shouts that she killed him. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L8-9)
- {belief} A Lancrel man shouts that the Witch murdered Elmmet, who was only accused of theft and had not been proven guilty. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L61-62)
