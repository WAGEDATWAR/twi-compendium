# Listeners
> Type: creature | Also called: Antinium Listeners; Antinium Listener | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.15
> See also: Antinium, Klbkch
## Overview
- Klbkch says Listeners are a specialized breed of Antinium developed within the Hive that can listen to movements and sounds at extreme range and, given instructions and time, monitor individuals. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L268)
- Klbkch says a few Listeners were allocated to his Hive, that their existence is not a secret, and that they were revealed during the Second Antinium War along with a few other types. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L271)
- Klbkch says only Soldiers and Workers are allowed within the Hive in Liscor. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L271)
- Klbkch tells the Queen that Listeners to the south report a group of Gnoll warriors of the Silverfang Tribe heading toward Liscor, led by a high-level warrior. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L36)
## Nature
- An Antinium Listener is a rare subtype of Antinium used by the Hive, built only to listen to sounds, and can pick up a conversation five miles away in a quiet room. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L10)
- Listeners have short lives because their sensory organs overload and they die, quickly even for Antinium. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L10)
- The Listeners' scrawls record conversations in full, along with marching feet, animals, magic and underground water, and Klbkch scans them each day. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L11)
