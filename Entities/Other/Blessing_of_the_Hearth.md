# Blessing of the Hearth
> Type: skill | Also called: Laken's Skill [Blessing of the Hearth]; [Skill - Empire: Blessing of the Hearth] | First appears: Book 3, Ch3.12E
> See also: Laken, Durene, Emperor
## Overview
- Laken obtains [Skill - Empire: Blessing of the Hearth] when he reaches Emperor Level 9. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L392-393)
- After the night he receives the Skill, Laken wakes rested despite sleeping far less than six hours. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L395)
- The grieving villagers who slept nearby slept like logs, woke refreshed, and found that minor cuts had scabbed over and healed almost overnight. (Book 3, Ch3.12E L398-399)
- Durene tells Ryoka that Laken's Skill makes beds soft, so that one sleeps like a baby even on hay. (Book 12, Ch6.40E L196-198)
