# Skeleton
> Type: person | Also called: Toren (probable; see hand-off); [Skeleton Warrior]; Erin's skeleton | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.32
> See also: Ksmvr, Calruz
## Overview
- Erin Solstice's skeleton stands in a doorway, taller than her, with yellow-white bones and twin burning blue-white flames for eyes. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L172)
- Erin notices that the skeleton has a human head but its ribs and some other bones are slightly different from the human bones in her biology books. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L251)
- The system labels it [Unnamed, Level 1 Skeleton Warrior]. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L290)
## Early days at the inn
- Erin orders the skeleton to clean the whole upper floor; it first tosses the rag aside and wipes with its foot, until Erin shows it how to scrub. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L272-277)
- The skeleton cleans every upstairs room until the floorboards regain their original lustrous color. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L33-34)
- The skeleton struggles to lift both full buckets at once. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L66)
- The skeleton charges Pawn and two Workers with a wordless rattling shout, and Erin's punch knocks its skull off; its body finds the head and puts it back on. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L99-106)
- {state} The [Skeleton Warrior] is now Level 2, though skeletons are not supposed to have free will or to level. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L382)
