# Alternate Tom
> Type: person | Also called: Tom (mirror reflection); Tom (alternate personality) | First appears: Book 6, Ch1.02C
> See also: Tom, Fearless, Blighted King
## Overview
- The retired [Clown] Tom is haunted by a second personality, who appears as the Tom in his bedroom mirror and speaks in his own voice. (Book 6, Ch1.02C L68; Book 6, Ch1.04C L161-168)
- The reflection tells Tom that it is not over and that he is insane but not an idiot. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L170-172)
- {belief} The mirror Tom argues that the Demon soldiers arrived too neatly: they appeared at night and knew where the princesses slept, two for one princess and six for the other, and none were sent for the Blighted King, his Queen or anyone else. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L175-179)
## Taking over
- {event} Before the Blighted King and his court, Tom drives a fork through his own other hand to silence the voices in his head, and the narration then continues in a wilder voice that calls Tom stupid. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L93-95,L127)
- {event} The alternate holds a dagger to the time mage Nereshal's throat to command the room's attention. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L113)
- {event} He points at the Fool as the culprit, deducing that the Fool marked the spot in the courtyard for his allies to come through and tried to abduct the princesses beforehand. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L127-140)
- {reveal} He shows that the glowing ward stone is fake, made with a Skill of the Fool. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L141-148)
- {event} He kisses the Blighted King on the cheek, and the Blighted Queen backhands him, knocking out a tooth. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L136,L154-156)
- {event} The alternate asks the Blighted King for a map, a [Mage] who can cast a few spells for him, knives, healing potions and the King's sword, in order to hunt the Demons. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L206-208)
- {event} In the dark, he cuts down Demon warriors, grabs one and vanishes with him, and reappears behind the others with his wounds closed, laughing. (Book 6, Ch1.05C L224-232)
