# Mossbear
> Type: creature | Also called: the bear | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.20E
> See also: Wiskeria, Laken Godart, Celestial Trackers
## Overview
- {event} On Day 59 the bear that the Celestial Trackers found wakes up and charges into Laken's village at incredible speed, and the adventurers recognise it as a Mossbear. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L330-337)
- Arrows bounce off the Mossbear's fur, and Wiskeria's fire spell and thrown stone shards fail to stop it because of the thick, wet fur. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L338,L342-343)
- The Mossbear is easily three times as large as the biggest warrior in the village. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L340)
## Missing fur
- A patch of the Mossbear's fur near the nape of its neck is missing, and Durene and Laken judge from the lack of blood and the neatness of the cut that it was no accident. (Book 5, Ch4.21E L404-412)
- Later, Wiskeria's emergency spell calls the Mossbear from its cave, which is the reason she had wanted its fur. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L347-348)
- {event} The Mossbear charges the Goblins from behind, smashes one flat and bites another. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L302)
- The Mossbear leaves after eating some of the dead Goblins, possibly wounded but not deeply. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L347)
