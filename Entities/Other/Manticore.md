# Manticore
> Type: creature | Also called: Manticores | First appears: Book 2, Ch1.00C
> See also: Flos Reimarch, Monsters
## Overview
- Manticores are monsters grouped with Goblins, Trolls and Dragons as mindless, vicious killers, in a description of how monsters differ from Demons. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L88)
- Manticores are part lion, part scorpion and part bat, can fly despite being five times as large as lions or larger, and have a stinger with lethal venom. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L714)
- Manticores are named as the typical monster of Chandrar, as Wyverns are for Izril and Griffins for Terandria. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L710)
- Teres judged Manticores to be above her level, and she was kept out of the fight with them. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L782)
## The Belchan hunt
- A pack of Manticores had been troubling Belchan and Jecrass for months, and a Gold-rank team slaying a Manticore was the kind of thing Terandrians discussed. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L730; Book 12, Ch6.42E L427)
- Flos Reimarch, the King of Destruction, fought a hundred or more Manticores with his army's elites, who blasted them from the sky with magic and held the line. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L712,L715-716)
- Flos stopped a Manticore's charge on his shield, shoved it back and killed it with a blow to the head. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L718-722)
- Zamea, a half-Giant thirty feet tall, punched one Manticore in the battle and the rest fled her. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L789)
- Lyfelt said Flos might take the Manticores' hides and parts and leave the meat to feed his army, and Flos spoke of a feast of Manticores for the Nomads. (Book 15, Ch7.08K L737,L840)
