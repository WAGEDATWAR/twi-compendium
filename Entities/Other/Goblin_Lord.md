# Goblin Lord
> Type: person | Also called: Reiss; the Goblin Lord | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.29
> See also: Az'kerash, Zel Shivertail, Rags, Garen Red Fang, Osthia Blackwing, Hobgoblin
## Overview
- Ryoka first sees the Goblin Lord standing in a mass of Goblins in magical armor, only a bit taller than an average Goblin and smaller than the Hobs, with white pupils in black eyes that glow. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L367-369)
- With a raised hand he reshapes the Goblin ranks into a shield wall against Zel's charge. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L370)
- Ryoka concludes that the Goblin army is raising the dead and that the Goblin Lord is another [Necromancer]. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L385)
## Rise of the army
- The Goblin Lord pursues Zel Shivertail's army south until the Goblins give up the chase, then gathers to grow stronger before sweeping north to subjugate the northern tribes. (Book 2, Ch2.29 L472)
- {belief} Scouts put his army at about the equivalent of a Walled City's standing army, with over a hundred Hobgoblins and at least ten mages or shamans. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L44)
- Gnolls in Krshia's apartment discuss that an army strong enough to defeat Zel Shivertail has slain the Stone Spears tribe to the last and has over a hundred Hobgoblins. (Book 2, Ch2.33 L275,L287)
- {event} His army attacks a Human city in black armor, and Rags's coalition crushes one black-armored warband against the walls. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L23-24,L29,L37)
- Garen tells Rags that this was only one of the Goblin Lord's warbands and not his army. (Book 4, Ch3.26G L44)
- A council speaker warns that a Goblin Lord is raising an army just south of the High Passes, and Tyrion says Esthelm fell but its people retook the city after fighting off another of his armies. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L437,L457)
- {belief} Another speaker says a Goblin Lord is one step from a Goblin King and cites rumors of a "Goblin Great Chieftain" who fought alongside the last Goblin King. (Book 5, Ch4.06KM L443)
- {event} Shivertail is told the Goblin Lord has been spotted around the Blood Fields, retreating from two suppression armies each led by a [General] and nearly ten thousand strong. (Book 5, Ch4.09 L24,L27)
## The battle against Thrissiam
- Thrissiam curses the snow that erases tracks for a Goblin Lord, and Drake and Gnoll scouts sent out alone vanish one by one. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L98,L109,L122)
- Osthia sees him on a mountain ledge, human-sized and hooded, with black eyes and white pupils, among Hobgoblins, Goblin [Mages] and [Shamans], and he strikes a Drake [Mage] down with a black lance of light. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L172-176)
- The Drake [Mages] cannot cast the [Message] spell, which Garusa says would need a specialized mage or one of far greater ability, and Thrissiam says he has blocked every spell sent at him. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L185-195)
- {reveal} A second, cold voice speaks through the Goblin Lord's body, calling him its apprentice, and Thrissiam explains that the Necromancer was controlling him and is not dead. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L283-284,L305-310)
- The Goblin Lord speaks in a second, less educated voice of his own, and the other voice scolds him for failing to use his greatest weapon and tells him to unify the Goblins or perish in despair. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L356-364)
- In his own voice he is educated and thoughtful, mentions a Ring of Protection and [Ward of Repulsion], and Osthia fears this most of all. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L370-371)
- {event} He tells Osthia the Necromancer "sees when he looks" and does not look now, points to a dead Goblin child his master created and killed as a weapon, and demands that she tell him about the Necromancer. (Book 5, Ch4.16 L376-388)
- {event} He tells Az'kerash that the servants Az'kerash sent were lost a week earlier and never found Liscor, and Az'kerash decides to reprimand them. (Book 5, Ch4.26M L372-378)
## The march north
- The Goblin Lord's army, tens of thousands strong in black armor, marches north past Liscor, having crushed two Drake armies. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L12,L18-19)
- He sees almost perfectly in darkness, has orders not to attack Liscor, and senses other Goblins that he means to claim by blood or, failing that, take their bodies. (Book 6, Ch4.32G L20-22)
- The army that marched past Liscor numbers tens of thousands, not counting the undead army that marched ahead of them. (Book 6, Ch4.33 L197)
- He is a human-sized Hobgoblin in black robes with midnight eyes and white pupils, and he is a [Mage] rather than a warrior. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L81-86)
- His tent was Thrissiam's command tent, and he keeps a yellow wildflower given to him by a child he healed with a potion, "gift for gift". (Book 6, Ch4.46 L65,L93-96)
- He uses a small [Message] scroll to contact the Necromancer, whom he calls his master. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L118,L223)
- He taught himself to read before the Necromancer found him, keeps six books including Thrissiam's copy of The Triumph of Scales, and reads whatever his army loots. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L136-142)
- His camp has sentries, patrols, latrines, a smithy and black resin armor, and he copied the color-coded battalions from the Drakes. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L44-50)
- He says he can sense other Chieftains and other Goblin Lords far away, that there are none on this continent, and that he is the "brightest" because he calls other Goblins loudly. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L156-163)
- He tells his Goblins he has a master who teaches him magic and is a master of death, and that a slave learns and one day he will not be a slave. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L223-236)
- He sends eight thousand Goblins under Snapjaw and Eater of Spears against a Human army and rides out himself on a Shield Spider. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L252-253)
- {event} In the battle he changes formations to match the Human commander's cavalry and archer tactics, kills the commander with [Arc of Midnight], and wins. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L287-307,L335-341)
- He aims [Deathbolt] at the Human commander's horse before the duel. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L337-340)
- His Ring of Protection swerves arrows aimed at him. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L345)
- He tells Osthia that Goblins need a home and that he will win three battles, against rebel Goblins, against Humans, and defending a home built in the mountains. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L390-393)
- He says Goblins take from Drakes, Humans and everyone, and that someday they will be like them and have a home; his last word is "Soon". (Book 6, Ch4.46 L409-413)
- After winning outside a Human city his army pillages for supplies, and thousands of zombies and a few ghouls rise. (Book 6, Ch4.46 L375,L387)
## Invrisil
- {event} He argues with his master and then kneels in agony, rises furious, and orders an attack on Invrisil to slay Zel Shivertail, saying "We are ordered. So we will. Or die." (Book 6, Ch4.47 L415-420)
- At Invrisil he rides a Shield Spider in robes, and his Goblins chant "Goblin" three times to break Human morale. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L68-80)
- His Goblin [Necromancers] and [Shamans] send tens of thousands of zombies, ghouls and a Crypt Lord at the Humans. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L170-175)
- {event} Az'kerash's secret weapon, exploding undead Goblin children, destroys Human lines, and the Goblin Lord's own hands bleed as he clenches them while watching. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L182-186)
- {event} He casts [Deathbolt], which Reynold calls death magic like the Necromancer's, instantly lethal to anyone below Level 20, killing Rose Knights. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L196-199,L212-217)
- With over a thousand Hobs and his Shield Spider he charges toward Reynold's position. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L351)
- {reveal} Dying, Zel suggests the name Reiss to the Goblin Lord, who accepts it and swears the Necromancer will die by his hand. (Book 6, Ch4.49 L445-452)
## Afterward
- {belief} Observers elsewhere fear that a Goblin Lord so young and mighty who felled a Drake [General] may be another Velan the Kind, or worse a Curulac. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L9)
- {belief} His forces are estimated down to sixty thousand and he is marching toward the so-called Great Chieftain in the mountain. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L46,L61)
