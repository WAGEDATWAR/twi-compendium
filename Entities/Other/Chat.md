# Chat
> Type: concept | Also called: chat log; the Call | First appears: Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call
> See also: BlackMage, Wistram factions, Kent Scott
## Overview
- The chat is a magical group chat that connects people from Earth across the world through their phones, with a floating screen showing a list of connected users. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L3,L7,L10,L12)
- A user, [Level 12 Warrior], says a floating screen popped up after answering the phone. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L10)
## Limits
- BlackMage says he cannot send anyone home and does not even know what sent them to this world. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L46)
- BlackMage says the system is not built to kick or mute a user, and that splitting the chat is not possible and this is the best he can do. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L210,L249)
## What the users share
- {belief} [strider_479] suggests that levelling in this world is based on adversity rather than repetition, and [America Group] confirms that the bigger the challenge the faster they levelled. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L235-239)
- [Level 12 Warrior] claims the way to game the system is to take multiple classes because more classes give more skills, while Loran Grimnar says new classes are hard to acquire and he has no more than three. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L246-247)
- The first chat ends with a user named 伶央 joining, writing two lines in Japanese asking whether anyone is there, and disconnecting alone. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L373-378)
## The second chat
- In a later private chat the participants are Loran Grimnar (a [Warrior] in Baleros), [Humble Actor] (a [Pop Star] and [Actor] in Terandria), [strider_479] (a [Ranger] in Terandria), and BlackMage (a [Mage] at Wistram). (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L308,L325,L347-353)
- [strider_479] says she was adopted and that her birth name is a mystery even to her, and BlackMage says mages tried to scry her and could not. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L331-334)
- BlackMage says he has called [batman] five times without an answer, and [strider_479] says [batman] was smarter than they thought and warned them in secret about a spy in the group. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L318-320)
- BlackMage says the mages with him are not sure the arrival was a spell for everyone, and that reversing a spell is apparently harder than bringing people over. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L377-381)
- [strider_479] says they can find the "Kyle imposter" or at least protect themselves from him. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L375)
- BlackMage tells the others that the King of Destruction is moving and that there are a few wars going on in Terandria. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L365)
