# Firebreath Whiskey
> Type: item | Also called: none | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.25N
> See also: Drakes, The Wandering Inn, Ilvriss
## Overview
- Firebreath Whiskey is a strong spirit imported from Izril, and the Drakes' signature drink. (Book 5, Ch4.25N L315; Book 6, Ch4.36O L6)
- Olesm considered Firebreath Whiskey offensive, hard to tolerate in large doses, and usually the prelude to a fight if drunk too heavily. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L6)
- Erin described the taste as like habaneros poured into a sock and mixed with mouthwash, and said it was the only strong drink she had in stock at the time. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L139)
- The Drakes treat it as a proper Drake drink, and Ilvriss demanded it at Erin's inn, drinking shots of it. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L330,L345)
## In the inn
- Erin cut Ilvriss off from Firebreath Whiskey after he arrived drunk and drank a full mug. (Book 6, Ch4.45 L147)
- Erin kept a whole keg of Firebreath Whiskey at her inn and called it nasty. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L153)
- Venaz drank Firebreath Whiskey by the mug, which Umina noted was a Drake favourite and expensive. (Book 11, Ch6.21D L789)
- Drassi told Kevin that Firebreath Whiskey was the cheapest strong drink on offer, and Maviola grabbed a pitcher of it as the most potent drink at a table. (Book 17, ChInterlude_A_Night_in_the_Inn L615,L844)
- A cocktail called Chaldion's Eye mixes Firebreath Whiskey with an Ultasian bitter made from the Ultasa flower and lime. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L16-18)
