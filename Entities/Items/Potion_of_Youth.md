# Potion of Youth
> Type: item | Also called: Potion of Youthfulness; Potion of Reverse Aging | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.51A
> See also: Saliss of Lights, Maviola El, Octavia, Pallass
## Overview
- A Potion of Youth can make even the oldest of people young for a short time, and a vial shone with pure magic. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L291)
- Saliss of Lights makes the Potion of Youth and Onieva delivers it; Chaldion said Saliss often fails at making it, and the world was abuzz with people ready to pay far too much for one. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L291-292,L694)
- Saliss reckoned a Potion of Youth could earn him hundreds of thousands of gold coins, or tens of thousands at least, depending on the market. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L425)
- Maviola El said Saliss can restore even someone as old as her to a young body for a while. (Book 18, Ch7.30 L770)
## Sales
- A contested Potion of Youthfulness, certified authentic by Xif of Pallass, was sold at an auction for about eighteen point four thousand. (Book 13, Ch6.51A L171-172)
- At an auction in Pallass, the Potion of Youth drew everyone's attention, and the bidding was fierce. (Book 16, Ch7.17S L665,L712)
- Rie Valerund did not bid for the Potion of Youth, and on learning who bought it asked what Reinhart needed with another potion. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L906-910)
- Maviola El held three vials of Potion of Youth that she bought with her own money, each lasting a week. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L1234-1236)
## A different potion
- Saliss made a different Potion of Youth with Octavia's help, which cost a fraction of the usual ingredients, differed in colour, and gave Maviola El true youth; he gave it to her for free. (Book 19, Ch7.51 L774-778)
