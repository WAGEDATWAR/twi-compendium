# Hamburgers
> Type: item | Also called: hamburger; burgers | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.11
> See also: Erin Solstice, Ryoka Griffin, Krshia Silverfang
## Overview
- Erin Solstice makes the first hamburgers in Liscor, frying patties of Krshia's ground beef with pepper and egg, and serving them with a bun, lettuce, tomato, cheese, mayonnaise and ketchup. (Book 2, Ch2.11 L325-329)
- Erin makes the ketchup from tomatoes and the mayonnaise from egg yolk, mustard, lemon juice and vinegar, using recipes Ryoka gave her before she left. (Book 2, Ch2.11 L329-330)
- After the burgers run out, Erin makes french fries, and Relc declares he will eat at her inn every night if she keeps making the food. (Book 2, Ch2.11 L346)
## Copying
- {event} Olesm tells Erin that shopkeepers and vendors in Liscor are already selling hamburgers the morning after, and that the only part they have not figured out is how to make the red and white sauces. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L191-198)
