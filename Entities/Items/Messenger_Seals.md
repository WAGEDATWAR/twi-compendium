# Messenger Seals
> Type: item | Also called: Seals; seals; tokens | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.02R
> See also: Runners, Healing potions
## Overview
- Messenger Seals are proof of delivery for Runners. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L151; Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L81)
## Use
- When Ryoka delivers potions to the Hammers of Hammerad, she asks for their seal, a silver and copper token with a hammer on a mountain embossed on one side. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L63-65)
- Krshia shows Erin a bowl of wooden triangles with red and gold patterns, saying she keeps the twin of each seal she gives out so she knows when a Runner has delivered. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L150-151)
- A messenger must deposit seals to receive their reward, so a delivery without one is suspect. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L81)
## Types and status
- Magnolia's seal is a token half gleaming silver and half semi-translucent cerulean, which the narration calls silver-and-sapphire. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L55)
- Wealthier patrons treat seals as a sign of status; merchants and bankers use lesser gemstones, and the most elite have their own unique form of seal. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L83)
- People without such means use simple cut stone provided by their cities at nominal prices, which is cheap but can be used to prove false deliveries. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L84)
- Messengers who take goods and give false seals are not unknown; cases happen every year, so a Runner's reputation for honesty matters as much as speed. (Book 1, ChInterlude_1.00_R L85)
