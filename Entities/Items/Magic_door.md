# Magic door
> Type: item | Also called: Erin's door; Erin's magic door; the magical door | First appears: Book 4, Ch3.30
> See also: Erin Solstice, Pisces, Ceria Springwalker, Octavia, Pallass
## Overview
- Erin's magic door is an ordinary wooden door set into the wall of Octavia's shop in Celum, linked to a door lying on the back of a wagon, so that stepping through takes Erin from one place to the other. (Book 4, Ch3.30 L67-68)
- Pisces affixed the anchoring spell to an actual door and harnessed the teleportation spell to create a portal, and Ceria says the door in Octavia's shop is only the anchor point while the real artifact is elsewhere. (Book 4, Ch3.30 L142,L159)
- The door drinks more mana than a sponge, can teleport people back and forth many times, and takes a long time to recharge once it runs out. (Book 4, Ch3.30 L160)
- Pisces and Ceria could only recharge it enough for the Horns of Hammerad to make a one-way trip to the wagon, and they still needed a few mana potions. (Book 4, Ch3.30 L161)
## Limits
- Erin tells Ilvriss that her door can only go as far as Invrisil, around four hundred miles, and transport one person at a time with a day to recharge, as Typhenous and Pisces did not think the enchantment could be improved. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L156-164)
- Erin says she needs to place an anchor at a place before the door can connect to it, so someone must run to Pallass or Invrisil first. (Book 6, Ch4.43 L168)
- A speaker at the inn says the inn does not have enough mana to let the door transport more than one person at a time, and that those who come through must wait twenty-four hours. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L303)
- Later, the door's destinations are Liscor, Celum, Pallass, Esthelm, and, on request, the Bloodfields, with the staff turning a magic dial every ten minutes to check it. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L156)
- The door also has a setting for a red gemstone leading to the Goblin cave. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L805)
- Erin says the door cannot send many people to Pallass, only two or four per day. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L498)
- {belief} Hawk says that Erin added a Sage's Grass field so that the door can ferry as many as twenty people quickly and recharge fast, and that this could hurt Runners across Izril. (Book 16, Ch7.18M L419-422)
## History
- {state} The link between the door and Pallass is damaged, and Pisces and Typhenous say they cannot restore the connection safely without sending a Runner to form another link. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L147-149)
- Numbtongue finds Erin's magic door propped up beside the oars by the boats outside Liscor. (Book 9, Ch5.54 L984-986)
- Numbtongue swaps Celum's mana stone for Liscor's to use the door, which links Celum and Liscor. (Book 10, Ch6.02 L449)
- {event} A work crew moves the door along the new road so workers can use it, and Eigusha goes through to fetch workers from Liscor; Erin says the city pays her, in reduced taxes, and the crews do not. (Book 14, Ch6.64 L63,L71-72)
- Invrisil's The Players' Retreat is linked to the inn's door, and about four hundred people with the six-silver passage fee queue to enter Liscor while a [Bouncer], Redit, holds them back. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L331-332)
