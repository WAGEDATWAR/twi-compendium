# Emerald Signet
> Type: ship | Also called: The Emerald Signet; Lasc's ship | First appears: Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships
> See also: Captain Lasc, Delivan, Wil Kallinad, Therrium Sailwinds, Diamond Swords of Serept
## Overview
- The Emerald Signet is a galleon commanded by Captain Lasc, a [Storm Captain], and crewed by [Storm Sailors]. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L180,L226-231; Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L120)
- The Emerald Signet carries a crew of over three hundred hands, and the six students are passengers kept apart from the [Sailors]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L116)
- The Emerald Signet was hired to help Wil investigate Niers' list and is specialized in recovering shipwrecks, among other things. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L282)
- The ship is a good one with magical amenities to make a sea voyage more tolerable, though its hull is not ironwood and has only a few basic enchantments. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L270; Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L529)
- Wil Kallinad chose Lasc and the Emerald Signet because Lasc has traded with House Kallinad and Wil trusts him, and Lasc and the students are the only ones on the ship who know the voyage's true purpose of finding shipwrecks. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L263,L267)
## Captain Lasc and the officers
- Captain Lasc is a tall man with a long cutlass, two ruby teeth and a tattoo like a magical rune on the back of his left hand. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L226,L230)
- Lasc often works for the Kallinad family, and is lean and young for a [Storm Captain]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L394)
- Lasc's skill [Wavebreaker's Advance] drives the ship forwards so that its bow splits a colossal wave in two. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L219-220)
- Lasc has a ship-speed Skill, and he can cast a [Ship Spell: Depth Explosion]. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L638,L847)
- Lasc fights with a shortsword in his right hand and a metal gauntlet on his left, and with the gauntlet he knocks Venaz unconscious in a duel. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L603-604,L614-616)
- Delivan, the ship's First Mate, carries a sword and a miniature crossbow, and later swaps the crossbow for a wand. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L542,L764)
- Delivan has a [Dangersense], and Lasc asks him about it before approaching a wreck. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L965)
- Delivan tells Wil that a [Storm Captain] on the sea has no use for armor, which would only make him sink faster. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L617)
## Voyage
- The ship carries [Divers] with Amulets of Waterbreathing and nearly forty diving at a time, and uses a diving bell to lower men to the wrecks. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L284-286)
- {belief} A Drowned Ship follows the Emerald Signet because, its captain says, everyone has heard the rumour that it carries a map of sunken treasure. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L363-367)
- {event} The Emerald Signet is attacked by slugs that prove to be parts of one giant jellyfish; Lasc steers toward its brain and the crew fights it off. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L836-841,L1044)
- {event} The ship flees a giant crab and a war golem guarding the wrecks, the golem firing a magical beam at it, and Lasc and Delivan steer it away from the isle. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1028,L1036,L1044-1046)
- {event} Over two thirds of the crew draw blades in a mutiny, saying that they have been keeping their real employers updated, and fewer than a hundred loyalists, including Inky, stand with Lasc. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L440-446)
- Lasc orders the mutineers put over the plank or shot, and tells Wil they must move because the ship is being scried. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L482,L486)
