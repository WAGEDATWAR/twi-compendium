# Barracks wall
> Type: item | Also called: Barracks mural | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.13L
> See also: Pawn, Yellow Splatters, Soldiers
## Overview
- {event} After a Soldier dies, Pawn kneels by the paint he bought, dips a finger in bright green paint and draws on the hard-packed dirt wall of the Soldiers' barracks the star that was on the dead Soldier's chest. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L451-455)
- Pawn resolves to repaint the star elsewhere if the wall is damaged, because he will remember it for the rest of his life. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L456-457)
- The other Soldiers then paint their own marks without speaking, one for each of the twenty-five Soldiers who died. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L458-459)
- The marks on the wall include a green star, a clumsy turquoise 7, a speckled egg, a snowflake, a black cat with orange eyes, the five numbers 46252, a smiley face, a stick-person with four arms, the word "Winter", a paw print, and a blade of grass. (Book 5, Ch4.13L L462-467)
## Later
- By the time Pawn's barracks is changed, space has been cleared on one wall for a mural of the marks of fallen Soldiers, including a white paw print, eight lines and a curvy wave, and a golden quartet of stars. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L75-77)
- Yellow Splatters recognises the patterns and feels that the wall is right, because it reminds the Soldiers who they lost. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L76,L78)
- A Soldier adds a white circle with a question mark to the wall by dipping a finger in white paint. (Book 6, Ch4.40L L77)
