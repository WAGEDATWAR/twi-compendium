# Preservation runes
> Type: item | Also called: preservation spells | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.03R
> See also: Healing potions
## Overview
- Lady Magnolia's food shelves carry tiny etched runes on every cupboard, which Ryoka Griffin identifies as preservation runes; Magnolia says she paid for the best preservation spells and has an [Enchanter] check that the runes are holding every year or so. (Book 1, Ch1.03R L12-13)
- Mages charge very affordable prices for such runes, and most inns and some larger shops use them quite often. (Book 1, Ch1.03R L14)
- Erin Solstice wishes she had preservation runes, and Pisces told her fancy runes would cost twenty to sixty gold coins and require replacing her cabinets so the runes stay intact. (Book 1, Ch1.25 L62-63)
- Erin cannot afford the runes on her limited funds of just under ten gold pieces. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L11)
- In Book 7 preservation runes on a lid keep a body from decomposing. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L435)
- In Book 10 a Drake shows Erin glowing preservation runes on the floor of a meat store. (Book 10, Ch6.09 L744)
