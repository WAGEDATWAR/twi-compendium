# Lady Magnolia's carriage
> Type: item | Also called: the pink carriage; Magnolia Reinhart's carriage | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.27
> See also: Magnolia Reinhart, Reynold, Ressa
## Overview
- Lady Magnolia Reinhart's carriage first appears as a dark green coach edged with gold, with black-painted wheels, pulled by two ghostly golden horses that shimmer with magical energy. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L521-523)
- Later the carriage is large, ornamented with gold and old carved woodwork but painted eye-searing pink, and is pulled by four misty, semi-translucent horses that run faster than any living horse. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L96)
- The carriage is almost soundless thanks to the heavy magic enchanting it, which lets it survive its speed and turn nimbly. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L97)
## Enchantments
- The driver Reynold can activate [Flying Wheels], which lifts the wheels off the ground and sends the carriage over obstacles. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L114-116)
- Reynold uses [Featherfall] to land after the pink coach flies over a hilltop, and [Grounded Wheels] to give the wheels traction again. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L120-123)
- Ressa tells Reynold that [Flying Wheels] wastes magical energy and that recharging the mana stones costs time. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L127-128)
- The carriage runs on mana stones, which Reynold replaces from a pillow. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L151,L156)
- {belief} When a servant asks Reynold whether an Archmage invented the carriage, he answers "Apparently" and says the workings are all runes and spellcraft to him. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L149-150)
- The two nearly spent mana stones he removes are a ruby and a perfectly cut emerald, among the most expensive objects in the world, and the Mage's Guild can recharge them in a week or more. (Book 6, Ch4.38B L151-153)
## Use
- {event} The pink carriage plows into the Goblins' ranks at hundreds of miles per hour, and its collision with Eggsnatcher's mount is tremendous. (Book 6, Ch4.48 L331-332)
