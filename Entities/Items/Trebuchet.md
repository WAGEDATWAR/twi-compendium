# Trebuchet
> Type: item | Also called: trebuchets | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.35E
> See also: Laken Godart, Ryoka Griffin, Tessia, Helm
## Overview
- Laken's villagers build trebuchets from designs that Ryoka gave him, which he cannot read himself, and Prost says the design is simple and the size is the problem. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L90-91,L95)
- Laken says Ryoka could not remember how torsion siege weapons are made, so trebuchets, which are easier, are the choice. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L92)
- The first trial broke the fulcrum. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L97)
- On Day 80 a trebuchet arm breaks, though the shot knocks down a tree, and Laken recalls that Ryoka said the arm needed a ratio of 1:3.75, which is hard to put into practice. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L255-256)
- Tessia fires a tiny prototype that sends a rock flying across the village. (Book 6, Ch4.35E L257)
## Demonstration and use
- {event} On Day 98 a full-size trebuchet throws a stone of over a hundred pounds at least seven hundred feet. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L2,L7-8)
- Laken says a trebuchet is a piece of technology that needs no magic or class to operate, so anyone who understands how it works can fire it. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L73)
- Tessia, one of the village's two [Engineers] and the highest-leveled, pulls the rope. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L97)
- A trebuchet can be loaded with hand-sized stones that spray like a volley, and Laken tells Lord Tourant that enough trebuchets firing at once could take down a charging army. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L98,L101)
- The same trebuchet can be loaded with a heavy stone to knock down a wall. (Book 8, Ch5.21E L102)
- Laken's two trebuchets bombard a Goblin city, firing a stone every ten minutes at most because the crews must pull back the arm, drag over a boulder and adjust the aim each time. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L119)
- Rags observes that the Humans have bad aim, but the trebuchet that a Human female seems to aim hits the walls more often than the other one. (Book 8, Ch5.31G L130-131)
