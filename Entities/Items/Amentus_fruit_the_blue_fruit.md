# Amentus fruit (the blue fruit)
> Type: item | Also called: blue fruit; Amentus fruit | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.08
> See also: Healing potions
## Overview
- Erin Solstice eats the blue fruit and makes blue juice from it. (Book 1, Ch1.08 L4; Book 1, Ch1.41 L118)
## Poison
- Erin's guest tells Erin that the core of the Amentus fruit causes a painful death within hours if eaten, that the outer rind is safe to eat, and that the inner seeds are toxic. (Book 1, Ch1.09 L95)
- Ceria identifies a blue fruit with a hollow seed pod as the core of the Amentus fruit and calls it highly poisonous. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L105)
- Ceria says the seed cores are fine if unbroken but that the liquid should not touch skin, since even touching it could poison a person mildly. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L112-114)
- Ceria says that eating enough of the liquid or the seeds would kill a person within the day, though not instantly. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L117)
- Erin says she once got sick after accidentally squishing a seed pod while making blue juice, and insists the juice uses only the outer part. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L118,L121)
