# Chessboard
> Type: item | Also called: magic chessboard; Erin's chessboard | First appears: Book 2, Ch2.15
> See also: Chess, Erin Solstice, Niers Astoragon
## Overview
- Erin receives a chessboard of gloriously smooth wood, with no pieces, that looks worth thousands of dollars in her world. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L275-276)
- Erin says she sent out a hard chess puzzle and that whoever sent the puzzle in the first place sent her this expensive board in return. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L254-255)
## Magic
- {event} Erin finds the board with ghostly, transparent pieces in their starting positions, wisps rising from them, except that a white pawn has moved up to e4. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L476-480)
- {belief} Pisces guesses that the board is a duplication spell and a magically connected board. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L48)
- The ghostly wisps follow the pieces as Olesm moves them, and the pieces are cool to the touch. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L54-56)
## Lyonette's games
- {event} When Mrsha scatters the ghostly pieces, Lyonette resets the board and a piece on the other side moves, because the unseen player thinks she is Erin. (Book 3, Ch3.22L L295-309)
- Lyonette plays on, loses a knight and a bishop, and the unseen player then eases off and plays more gently, and she wins the third game. (Book 3, Ch3.22L L313-316)
- After the third game the pieces rearrange themselves, the white side is on the far end of the board, and no further move comes. (Book 3, Ch3.22L L316-317)
- Niers Astoragon, the Titan, is shown sitting in front of a very small chessboard far across the world, musing that the mystery player is gone and that someone who knows chess but has barely played it has found the board. (Book 3, Ch3.22L L333-335)
