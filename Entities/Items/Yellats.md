# Yellats
> Type: item | Also called: Yellat; yellat | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.00K
> See also: Chandrar, Flos Reimarch, Food
## Overview
- Yellats are orange, stringy and spicy vegetables that taste a bit like sweet potatoes when made spicy, and they are a staple of the Chandrarian diet. (Book 5, Ch4.00K L100; Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L122)
- Flos called Yellats a poor man's food, filling and easier to grow in Chandrar than any other plant, because they survive extended dry spells that would kill potatoes; other nations see them as a symbol of poverty. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L124)
- Reim and Germina are described as hard lands in which to grow anything but the tough Yellats. (Book 10, Ch6.12K L28)
## Uses
- Yellats are cooked with grain in a simmering soup, fried, or served as pizza toppings, and are eaten with meat or bread. (Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L45; Book 10, Ch6.13K L206; Book 11, Ch6.32 L390)
- Erin bought Yellats in Celum rather than Liscor because it was quicker. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L383)
- Yellats are known as spicy red things in Baleros cuisine, served alongside blue tubers and other plants. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L99)
- Prost asked Califor whether she was the woman who ran Yellats the length and breadth of the north after the 2nd Antinium War, and Califor did not deny it. (Book 12, Ch6.41E L1138-1140)
- Flos pulled a large, fat Yellat from a pot, curled because it had no room to grow, and was highly amused. (Book 13, Ch6.53K L92-96)
