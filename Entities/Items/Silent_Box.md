# Silent Box
> Type: item | Also called: black box | First appears: Book 13, Ch6.57
> See also: Wistram Academy, Bag of Holding, Grimalkin
## Overview
- A Silent Box is a perfectly black cube with a single door etched in pale silver on one side. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L366-368)
- The Silent Box is a mage-prison developed by Wistram, a contained space like a very old and much more advanced Bag of Holding. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L785)
## Use
- In Liscor a Minotauress among the Wistram group draws the Silent Box and tells Pisces to put his hands up and get in. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L365-368)
- After beating Pisces down, Montressa draws the Silent Box and bends over him with it, and later a Minotauress tells Ceria's team that they put him in a Silent Box. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L498,L582)
- When Montressa lifts the box, the captured half-Elf turns into motes of black light that flow into the box and its silver doorway. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L700-701)
- Beza passes the box to Montressa, and Montressa refuses to give it up, insisting that she keeps it. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L779-781)
- Ulinde tells the onlookers it is just a prison and that the capture is legal. (Book 13, Ch6.57 L702)
## Opening it
- Grimalkin explains that the box is like a Bag of Holding and not an actual dimension a person can walk into, so he only has to release the captives. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L788)
- Grimalkin touches the door and it shines; he reaches in, or the box expands to fit his hands, and four figures lie on the ground, among them Ceria. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L789)
- Grimalkin states that Silent Boxes preserve captives as they were, with time passing very slowly, so Pisces' injuries are fresh. (Book 13, Ch6.58 L806)
- Night has fallen by the time the Horns of Hammerad are freed from the Silent Box, and their wounds have been healed. (Book 13, Ch6.59 L273-274)
