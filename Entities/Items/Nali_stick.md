# Nali-stick
> Type: item | Also called: nali sugarcane; nali-sticks | First appears: Book 10, Ch6.05D
> See also: Baleros, Erin Solstice, Garden of Sanctuary
## Overview
- A nali-stick is a concentrated version of sugarcane sold as a snack, made of many fibres, which gives a wave of sweetness after chewing. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L264-265)
- Sugar is one of Baleros's chief moneymakers, and sugarcane grows on the continent above all else. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L265)
- Fresh nali-sticks growing in Erin's jungle biome are hard to find, thin brown tubes with a terribly sweet white interior. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L172,L184-185)
- Erin finds a fresh nali-stick so sweet it nearly numbs her mouth and fears it could be addictive. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L187)
- Palt says there are people who cannot live without a nali-stick a day, and that fresh ones are far sweeter than dry ones. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L188-189)
- Embria says it is a pity they grow only in Baleros, as she could see using them in field rations. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chocolate_Alchemy L192)
