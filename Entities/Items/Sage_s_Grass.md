# Sage's Grass
> Type: item | Also called: Sage's Grass water | First appears: Book 12, Ch6.39
> See also: Door, Wailant, Viceria, Octavia
## Overview
- Octavia says that ten thousand years ago Sage's Grass did not exist, because a really powerful [Sage] bred the plant, and that after he died [Alchemists] all over the world rejoiced because it is cheap, magical and a strong ingredient. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L592)
- Octavia notes that Sage's Grass water is cheap around Celum because a man named Wailant grows it, and that it is harder to get elsewhere. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L605)
- Sage's Grass glows red when planted in the meadow, and Wailant and Viceria have planted it. (Book 16, ChInterlude_The_Hangover_After L190)
- Montressa and Wailant note the ambient mana of a Sage's Grass field, which Wailant says will do more than fill the door, even if most of it goes there. (Book 16, ChInterlude_The_Hangover_After L204-205)
- Viceria uses the field's mana to float Mrsha through the air with a spell, over the Sage's Grass. (Book 16, ChInterlude_The_Hangover_After L206-208)
- Wailant says Sage's Grass dies if uprooted and that planting a field in the Garden of Sanctuary will generate mana to recharge the door for about six hundred gold. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L985)
