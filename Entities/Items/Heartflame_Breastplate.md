# Heartflame Breastplate
> Type: item | Also called: Heartflame Armor | First appears: Book 6, Ch4.44M
> See also: Zel Shivertail, Magnolia Reinhart, General Ironscales, Selys Shivertail
## Overview
- The Heartflame Breastplate is a national artifact and a symbol of the Drake people, mentioned in several stories and older myths. (Book 7, Ch5.17S L50)
- It was thought lost two centuries ago, when General Ironscales, who held it, was slaughtered and her corpse disappeared. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L362; Book 7, Ch5.17S L50)
- Legends say the breastplate can stop an enchanted lance thrust to the heart and has never been broken. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L368; Book 7, Ch5.17S L52)
## With Zel Shivertail
- Magnolia Reinhart gives Zel the breastplate, and her maid Ressa says the Reinharts did not know Regis Reinhart had acquired it until they asked. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L362-366)
- {reveal} Zel recognises the golden-red armor at once and learns that the Reinharts had it. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L362,L369)
- Zel unbuckles his own breastplate to wear it, and it burns on his chest and makes him feel warm and stronger. (Book 6, Ch4.44M L371-372)
- {state} Zel is wearing the Heartflame Breastplate, shining gold and red, in his casket when he lies dead. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L438-439)
- Tekshia identifies the armor as the Heartflame Breastplate, and Zevara says the Reinharts had it and Magnolia gave it to Zel. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L454-456,L498)
## After Zel
- The breastplate is kept on a stand in a windowless meeting room, guarded by Relc and a mage, and Selys finds the warm metal gaudy. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L440-446)
- Pisces notes that it is a breastplate that covers only the front, and theorises that this is because a fiery ward from the armor protected the wearer's back. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L457-460,L468-471)
- Selys remarks that "Drakes do not run", a saying coined by General Sserys. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L461-462)
- Wall Lord Ilvriss returns the breastplate to Selys, and it is stored in the Watch Barracks until she finds a renter. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L38,L560)
- Zevara explains that Zel's will makes Selys the inheritor of the Heartflame Breastplate. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L580)
