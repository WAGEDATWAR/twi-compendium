# Spellbooks
> Type: item | Also called: spell books; spellbook | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.40
> See also: Silverfang tribe, Teriarch, Mrsha
## Overview
- Ceria Springwalker says a single spell book costs several hundred gold coins or even a thousand, even for low-Tier spells. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L117)
- Ceria is saving up gold to buy a spell book that holds a few Tier 4 spells, since she has more or less mastered all the books she owns. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L114,L117)
## Properties
- Klbkch states that most spell books are immune to conventional harm but that sufficiently powerful magic destroys them. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L245)
- Teriarch says that not all spellbooks are simple repositories of spells and that some are written as instructional tomes to teach magic to those who struggle. (Book 2, Ch2.39 L271)
- Teriarch says his own books contain many lower-class spells but are of higher quality in content and accessibility for those studying them. (Book 2, Ch2.39 L265)
- {belief} Teriarch reasons that if the Silverfang tribe truly bought over forty spellbooks, they would have been redundant given how common low-level spells are, and that books cheap enough for one spell to destroy were probably personal references rather than proper instructional tomes. (Book 2, Ch2.39 L267)
## Reading a spellbook
- When Mrsha opens a spell tome to a random page, the symbols shine into her head and speak there, instantly comprehensible rather than read as language. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L146,L149)
- Understanding the page gives Mrsha a migraine, and the first page she opens teaches her how to conjure a sword of stone out of the ground, a spell she thinks of as [Stone Sword] or [Blade of the Earth]. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L147-150)
- A second page teaches Mrsha the spell [Grow Grass], which makes magical grass last for minutes or possibly hours, and the spell burns itself into her thoughts. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L157-160)
- Mrsha realizes the tome is far lighter than it looks and worth infinitely more than a mundane book, and that it is what the Silverfang tribe meant to offer at the Meeting of Tribes. (Book 7, Ch5.06M L150-151)
