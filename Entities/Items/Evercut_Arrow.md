# Evercut Arrow
> Type: item | Also called: Evercut arrows | First appears: Book 5, Ch1.04D
> See also: Geneva Scarlet, Roving Arrow, Healing potions
## Overview
- A Selphid guard warns that an Evercut Arrow cannot be removed without opening the patient up, and that shaking the wounded person will make it cut through his insides. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L75)
- The Roving Arrow company began using Evercut arrows a few days before Geneva meets the wounded man, and the arrows lodge inside and keep moving, slowly digging through organs and preventing healing until the patient dies. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L90)
- The Evercut arrowhead Geneva finds inside the wounded man is a tiny piece of moving orange metal. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L103)
## Later
- Talia recognises an Evercut Arrow in a triangular shard of an Ogre Chief's club that has broken off and lodged in Ser Eldein's chest, a weapon that healing potions cannot fix. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L737-738)
- {event} Eldein declares himself mortally cut and asks to die standing, and an Ogre's mace crushes his helmet and shoulder. (Book 14, ChInterlude_Talia L743,L749,L754)
