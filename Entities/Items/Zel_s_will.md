# Zel's will
> Type: item | Also called: Zel Shivertail's will | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.16S
> See also: Zel Shivertail, Selys Shivertail, Tekshia, Heartflame Breastplate, General Sserys
## Overview
- Zel Shivertail personally dictated his will about eight years before his death, and it remains valid. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L524)
- Zel divides his monetary wealth and lands among relatives, and his father is named first. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L562-563)
- Tekshia and Selys are not named in the division of money and lands, as they are distant relatives. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L564)
- Zel leaves his Aunt Tekshia the spears in his armory and any monetary investments or holdings he placed in Liscor. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L566)
## Selys's inheritance
- Zel leaves whatever personal effects were on his corpse to his niece Selys Shivertail in hopes she will one day become an adventurer. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L569-571)
- If those artifacts are lost to war or the circumstances of his death, Zel gives Selys a blade of her choosing from his armory and a sum of coins. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L571)
- {reveal} Zevara realises that this makes Selys the inheritor of the Heartflame Breastplate and any other artifacts he owned. (Book 7, Ch5.16S L580)
## The last request
- The words "Bury me with my love" in Zel's will are taboo among Drakes, and Selys thinks they were meant for General Sserys. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L909-914)
- {event} Selys and Pisces dig by night at General Sserys's grave, where she pours Zel's ashes beside his coffin and fills the hole in again. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L872,L890-898)
- {belief} Pisces does not know the word "Turnscale", and Selys confirms his guess about the meaning, saying no one knew about it and that she thinks Zel and Sserys were that. (Book 7, Ch5.18S L899-911)
