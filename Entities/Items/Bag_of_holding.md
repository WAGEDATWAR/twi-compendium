# Bag of holding
> Type: item | Also called: bags of holding | First appears: Book 8, Ch5.33B
> See also: Silent Box, Selys Shivertail, Free Queen
## Overview
- Bags of holding have limits on what they can store, can break or malfunction, and cannot contain living things. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L184)
- It is dangerous to carry multiple bags of holding on the same person. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L184)
- {event} A bag of holding delivers a dead Antinium drone made in the shape of a Queen, preserved in thick mucus, to the Free Queen. (Book 8, Ch5.33B L183-188)
- Selys is warned to keep three bags of holding out of proximity and to finish quickly, because the stronger the enchantment, the sooner something happens when they are close. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L241)
- Lesser bags of holding can be kept close together for a while. (Book 11, Ch6.17S L241)
