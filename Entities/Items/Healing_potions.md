# Healing potions
> Type: item | Also called: healing potion; low-grade healing potion | First appears: Book 1, Ch1.02R
> See also: Amentus fruit (the blue fruit), Messenger Seals, Runners
## Overview
- Healing potions are bottled green liquids that close wounds by speeding up the body's natural healing. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L215)
- Healing potions are expensive; the Liscor Watch carries them for injured members, who are not supposed to use them unless someone is badly hurt. (Book 1, Ch1.21 L199-204)
## Appearance and taste
- Ryoka Griffin delivers fifteen healing potions and five mana potions in heavily wrapped bottles to the Hammers of Hammerad, and their leader Calruz downs a thick, syrupy green liquid from one of the bottles. (Book 1, Ch1.02R L63,L72)
- Klbkch's low-grade healing potion is a shimmering, emerald-green liquid in a corked bottle. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L249-251)
- A healing potion tastes bitter and foul. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L30-31)
## Effects and limits
- Poured over Erin Solstice's cut hand, the low-grade potion closes the wound in an instant without hurting. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L256-261)
- After the potion, Erin's right palm is clean and unscarred. (Book 1, Ch1.08 L4)
- Klbkch tells Erin that after drinking a healing potion she will be weak for at least a day but will recover in full. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L63-65)
- A mage says healing potions suit only quick fixes and can leave bones fused the wrong way round or off-center. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L207)
- Ceria says healing potions just accelerate the body's natural healing and cannot fix a break as complicated as Ryoka's leg, where only time or magic will do. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L215)
- Ryoka reports being told that a stronger healing potion would grow each bone fragment out of her skin and destroy her leg beyond any healing. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L289)
- A healing potion heals all of Ryoka's and Yvlon's injuries. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L145)
## Price, grades and distribution
- Selys says the potions are very expensive, and that the stronger potions carried by Senior Guardsmen heal most big injuries in a moment, short of losing an arm or other body part. (Book 1, Ch1.21 L199-204)
- Klbkch calls the potion he gives Erin low-grade, and when Relc calls it a lesser potion of no worth, Klbkch asks whether he would have had him tell her the true value, which Relc accepts. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L250; Book 1, Ch1.07 L320-322)
## Use in Geneva's practice
- In Book 3 a young man named Thriss calls his potion weak and says the better type of healing potion gets rid of poison and other problems, but they have only cheap stuff. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L98,L233)
- Poured on a swollen shoulder, a cheap potion makes the swelling recede, though Geneva needs to add more before the flesh looks normal. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L100)
- Geneva concludes from watching the potion that it accelerates the body's natural processes, closing existing tissue rather than creating new skin. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L272)
- {belief} Geneva reasons that potions would probably do nothing for advanced breaks unless the bones were set properly, and that they cannot heal severed limbs and would inhibit reattachment if used incorrectly. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L273)
## Pain, shock and disease
- A first-person narrator in Book 6 says a wound can be healed in seconds in this world, but the pain means few can fight afterwards, and all but the best potions leave a drinker tired or nauseous. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L5)
- The agony of healing can kill some people and the shock can stop a heart; the Fool's severed innards grow together and have to be pushed into his stomach before being healed. (Book 6, Ch1.04C L6)
- Geneva states that potions do not cure diseases and instead accelerate a body's natural functions, which makes a disease worse. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L531-532)
- Geneva claims that either a [Healer] finds a cure or the potions make a disease worse until it kills everyone it infects, so plagues are far deadlier in this world. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L534)
