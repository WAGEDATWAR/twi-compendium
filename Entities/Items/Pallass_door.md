# Pallass door
> Type: item | Also called: the Pallass setting of Erin's door | First appears: Book 7, Ch5.17S
> See also: Door, Pallass, Zevara, Assembly of Crafts
## Overview
- {event} After the door to Pallass reopens, crowds of Drakes pass through the inn, waiting for Erin to switch the connection between Liscor and Pallass. (Book 7, Ch5.17S L440-441)
- Erin says the door will stay open to Pallass for the next six hours, held by [Mages] who rotate in shifts on both sides. (Book 7, Ch5.17S L458-459)
- Erin is hiding Hobgoblins in her basement that day, right after Zel Shivertail's funeral. (Book 7, Ch5.17S L449-452)
## Restrictions
- {state} A weary [Mage] on the Pallass side announces that they will not recharge the door again, and that tomorrow access will be cut to two visitors per day, with no [Mages] available unless the [Innkeeper] or guests pay for them. (Book 12, ChInterlude_Rufelt L386-387)
- {event} During the Creler crisis the door flickers open to Pallass about forty-seven minutes in, and Grimalkin steps through to the inn after Zevara switches the dial. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L1398-1404,L1408)
- Grimalkin leads an advance party of Gold-ranks and Wing Commander Embria's best through the door to the Bloodfields on his mark, at about fifty-nine minutes. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L1414,L1421-1426)
- {state} Later, the Pallass end of the door has been moved into a chamber with a miniature steel portcullis and eight [Guards], half of them with bows, and the [Guards] call out "Two to Liscor" before raising it. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L158-167)
- The [Guards] say the security is mandated by the Assembly of Crafts, and a Drake apologises that the portcullis stays. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L164,L171)
- The Bloodfields setting of the door is now on a hill far from the Bloodfields, and Lyonette has ruled that the door is not to be opened unless people with combat Skills are present. (Book 15, Ch7.06 L157)
