# Door
> Type: item | Also called: Erin's magic door; the magic door | First appears: Book 5, Ch4.12
> See also: Pallass door, Sage's Grass, Erin Solstice, Pisces
## Overview
- Erin Solstice's magic door can teleport a person across a long distance, and the Horns of Hammerad gave it to Erin as thanks for funding their group. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L110)
## Testing and capacity
- After Ryoka's testing, the mages Typhenous and Moore calculated that the door could carry a person of roughly Ryoka's weight 536 miles, given minimal change in altitude or magical ambience. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L450,L453,L457-459)
- The door exhausts its magic after such a teleport and needs roughly 8.2 hours to recharge fully. (Book 5, Ch4.12 L459)
## Link to Pallass
- {event} Hawk the Courier sets up a second door in Pallass using an attuned stone from Pisces, and Erin's door opens onto the Walled City of Pallass for the first time. (Book 7, Ch5.00 L289-299)
- Pallass tracks unauthorised teleports, and a [Commander] of Pallass arrests Jelaqua's party for entering by unauthorised spell. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L195-198)
- {state} Moore pries loose the coloured mana stone that Typhenous and Pisces attuned to the door, and the mages in Pallass report that the link between the doors is broken. (Book 7, Ch5.01 L549)
- {state} Later, Zevara tells Erin that Pallass is ready to open the door again after the Assembly of Crafts dropped its sanctions against Liscor. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L195-198)
- Without recharging, the door has enough mana to send only two people through, so Zevara sets up a request form at the Watch barracks and clears travellers with Pallass by [Message]. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L200-203)
- Watch Captain Venim waits on the Pallass side of the door when it reopens, and the Pallass sky shows through the doorway. (Book 7, Ch5.07 L212-216)
## Use at the inn
- A plain door set into Liscor's western wall opens onto Erin's inn; Klbkch opens it on patrol, calling for Miss Erin. (Book 7, Ch5.12 L484-485)
- Lyonette must keep changing the door between Celum and Liscor by placing the matching mana stone on it, and a green stone selects Liscor. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L202,L225)
- Erin has to change the door every five minutes or less to let people in and out to Celum and Liscor. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L272)
- Erin's door has a dial that selects its destination, and during the Creler crisis Zevara switches it between Pallass and the Bloodfields. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L1399-1402,L1408)
- Pisces tells Erin the door cannot be made to move like a moving castle. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L226)
- {event} During the Creler fight at the Bloodfields, the door is still not open eight minutes in; when it opens, the workers, Palt and fleeing adventurers pour through while Crelers follow them into the inn. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L1406; Book 14, Ch6.66H L157,L292-295,L323-326)
- {event} Wailant proposes planting Sage's Grass in the middle of the Garden of Sanctuary to generate mana for the door, saying more grass means faster recharging. (Book 16, Ch7.11 L984-985)
