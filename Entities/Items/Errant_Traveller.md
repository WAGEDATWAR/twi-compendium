# Errant Traveller
> Type: item | Also called: the ship Errant Traveller | First appears: Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1
> See also: Wistram, Ceria Springwalker
## Overview
- The Errant Traveller is a tall, two-masted ship berthed at South Harbor in the city of Belan, a southern port-city of Terandria, that carries would-be students to Wistram. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L3,L14)
- The ship's [Captain] checks passengers against a paper bearing a lifelike printed portrait of each person's face. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L35)
- Below decks the ship has portholes and, instead of hammocks, small cots with pillows and blankets. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L37)
- The Captain announces that the trip will take two days if the weather holds, with three meals a day, and that passengers who go above must stay out of the sailors' way. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L83)
- The Captain forbids any magic on his ship and threatens to throw offenders overboard or cut down anyone who draws a blade, lords or not. (Book 2, ChS03_Wistram_Days_Pt._1 L147)
