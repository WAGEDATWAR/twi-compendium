# Diamond Swords of Serept
> Type: item | Also called: Swords of Serept; Diamond Sword of Serept | First appears: Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships
> See also: Fetohep of Khelt, Serept of Khelt, Wil Kallinad, Emerald Signet, Therrium Sailwinds
## Overview
- The Diamond Swords of Serept are six blades, each forged by Serept of Khelt, and each shaped of a different coloured diamond. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L2-4)
- Fetohep of Khelt calls them one of the true relics of this world and a great treasure of Khelt, each fit for a [General] or [King] of the old world. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L2,L7)
- One blade is a short curved sword, a mix of dagger and sword, made of black diamond that is transparent and lustrous. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L4-5)
- Serept reigned for three thousand years before time consumed him, according to Fetohep, and no foe less than time humbled him. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L2)
- Serept was a Smithing-King who forged crystalline artifacts as a motif, taken to extremes with the Diamond Swords, quartz armor and battleaxes forged from a single ruby. (Book 19, Ch7.46K L110)
## History
- Titan Niers Astoragon's list calls the swords a Kheltian treasure stolen from Khelt around two thousand years ago, and warns Wil not to take them but to message King Fetohep, since the curse may still be extant. (Book 16, ChInterlude_Chess_and_Ships L354)
- Fetohep says the chest and the artifacts are cursed, and that the curse consumed the thieves at sea so completely that they never uttered a word of distress. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L9)
- Fetohep says his predecessor's fleets searched for the swords for hundreds of years without finding them. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1680)
## Found by Wil's group
- {event} When the students recover the casket, Fetohep appears as an apparition of sand above the ship, and offers them half of the six swords until their deaths, or else the curse of Khelt would engulf them. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._1 L1669-1674)
- Fetohep says that the blades shall return to him upon the holders' death, since in the sun they cannot escape his gaze, and any attempt to hide them meets the curse of Khelt. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L37)
- Fetohep restates the bargain for the third time as three swords, which Wil may pick and share, and as Wil alone is granted the right to touch the blades, which otherwise carry a curse that seizes whoever touches them. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L29-34)
- Fetohep tells Wil that he has the right of choosing, that each sword is different, and that he will return in a day for the answer. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L60-61)
- Word spreads that the students have found the Diamond Swords of Serept, and soon everyone is aware of it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L152)
- {event} Wil picks up a green diamond greatsword in the final battle, throws it to Venaz, and Fetohep names it the Windblade of Serpisel as Venaz clears the deck with it. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1162-1169,L1171,L1176-1178)
- Rasea Zecrew reaches for a glowing blue blade among the swords, but Fetohep rises again out of the magical sand. (Book 17, ChInterlude_Strategists_at_Sea_Pt._2 L1049-1052)
## Aftermath
- Fetohep later tells Trey Atwood that the swords were found by a group of students after a battle at sea and that he took a role in it as an apparition, since the ruler of Khelt can manifest through the swords' enchantment. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L249,L252)
- Fetohep says that each of the six swords has an unparalleled magical enchantment and that he granted half of them to the treasure seekers. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L256)
- Fetohep says that he would have given the Pact of Temius, a blue blade, to the Gnoll and the Unfilled Cup of Cexila, a red blade, to the Minotaur. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L258)
- Fetohep says that the Pact of Temius calls on the powers of nature and animals, while the Cup of Cexila drinks the blood of those it slays and heals the wielder. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L261)
- Fetohep says the three remaining blades will return to him in time, though the carriers have been forced to take shelter at Zeres. (Book 17, Ch7.21KQ L261)
- House Kallinad would pay for a relic-class artifact, one of the Diamond Swords, enough to buy a fleet of ships, and Captain Lasc and the surviving [Storm Sailors] were compensated royally. (Book 18, Ch7.32D L1324-1325)
- Drowned Folk and [Mercenaries] hired by someone else try twice to take the swords from the three [Strategists] who carry them, and fail. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L162)
- Feshi says that she holds a Diamond Sword of Serept, and her Gnoll tribe has come because of it. (Book 18, ChInterlude_Food_and_Growth L204)
