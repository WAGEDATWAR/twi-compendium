## Spell tiers
- Pisces says magic has seven or eight tiers, with a speculative ninth tier that no mage has cast or discovered. (Book 1, Ch1.22 L136-138)
- Pisces says he cannot cast beyond the third tier in any field and is mostly limited to first and second tier within his specialisations. (Book 1, Ch1.22 L135)
- A book Ryoka read says a spell a mage below Level 16 can cast is generally 1st or 0 Tier, up to about Level 24 is 2nd Tier, and up to Level 32 is 3rd Tier. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L186)
- The same book says lower-level mages can cast higher-Tier magic with enough practice and time. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L187)
- Ceria says magic comes in Tiers, and that she would call [Light] Tier 0 if she could, since it is one of the most basic magics people learn. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L179,L193)
- Ceria names [Spark] as another example of a very basic spell, like making light or making heat. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L193)
- Ryoka calls [Noise] a Tier 0 spell that Ceria taught her. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L48)
- Ceria puts [Ice Spike] and [Detect Magic] at Tier 3, [Ice Shard] at Tier 2 and [Frozen Wind] at Tier 1. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L175,L177,L191)
- Ceria says Tier 3 magic is good for fighting so long as the target is not too deadly. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L191)
- Ceria is unsure whether [Haste] is Tier 4 or Tier 5. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L179)
- Ceria says she can cast Tier 3 magic at best, and that a Tier 4 spell would need a spellbook and weeks or months at her level. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L181)
- Ceria says that learning a spell such as [Grand Fireball] would take her years even if she knew how it was supposed to go. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L183)
- Pisces knows one Tier 4 spell, [Invisibility], which nearly killed him to learn and cost him a month of studying every night for at least two hours. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L187)
- Pisces learned [Invisibility] at Level 18 and gained two levels from learning it. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L187)
- Ceria sees Illphres cast a Tier 4 spell, [Ice Lance], in seconds without a wand. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L415)
- Rievan taught the whole class [Electric Jolt], and Pisces, who knew no Tier 2 combat spells, practised it too. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L39)
- Pisces says he finds Tier 1 spells unchallenging to make work optimally, like a map with the destination circled. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L66)
- Ceria and Pisces disagree about [Stone Fist]: she says it is a good Tier 3 spell and he thought it Tier 2. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L206-208)
- Ceria's early spells [Stone Dart], [Flame Jet] and [Chameleon] are described as barely Tier 2. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L12-13)
- An old man fighting the Horns of Hammerad calls [Stone Dart] a low level spell but all he is capable of there, and says only Tier 2 spells will be necessary. (Book 4, Ch3.30 L270)
- Calvaron says there are plenty of mages in Wistram who can cast Tier 5 or even Tier 6 spells, and higher than that is out of reach without getting past Cognita. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L373)
- Ceria tells Ryoka that half-elves of old all learned Tier 2 magic before their teens, according to her grandfather. (Book 1, Ch1.34 L49)
- Ceria says items have an intensity like magic's tiers and rates the items she and Pisces study, a sword and rings among them, as Tier 1, perhaps Tier 2 on one ring at best, adding that Tier 1 is still good. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L297,L300)
- Ceria, analysing a powerful trap spell of blue flames set off by digging into a secret corridor, wonders whether it is Tier 5 or 6. (Book 3, Ch3.02H L311,L318,L320)

## Casting, control and imagination
- Ceria says wands amplify your magic, hold stored energy that may or may not restore over time, and some contain spells that can be triggered once or several times. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L162)
- Ceria says artifacts are like wands but usually with recharging mana and deeper reserves, and warns never to tell anyone you own one because some people will kill for it. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L164)
- Ceria says [Ice Spike] is a matter of shaping the energy and that she could cast it once without a wand, but the backlash would hurt. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L173,L175)
- Adding more mana to a [Light] orb can make it bigger or differently shaped, and Ryoka makes hers brighter by adding energy; Ceria calls such variations exercises that help control. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L198,L207)
- Ceria says only a really good mage can control a thrown [Light] orb at a distance. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L198)
- Ceria says mages cannot form spells from nothing: the images in their heads become the spells, so she cannot make light as bright as the sun. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L214)
- Ceria says most mages have specialties, something they understand; she understands cold because she spent winters in snow and ice. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L219)
- Ryoka recalls learning [Light] as closer to a mathematical revelation or solving a formula, and later thinks of controlling the power as pushing or pulling, or absorbing and releasing. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L190,L209)
- In Wistram, basic telekinesis, magical composition and spellcraft are lumped into one category called elementary or 'pure' magic, which matters for any mage who wants to cast spells because badly formed spells are less effective. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L35-36)
- A Wistram teacher criticises spells that are leaking pools of wasted mana and wants well-formed ones. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L12)
- The narrator notes that the more complex a spell is, the more a mage benefits from working with it, after Rievan insists that students who know a higher-Tier spell practise that instead. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L39)
- A Wistram teacher praises Pisces for diverting the whole of his electrical spell into his other palm without letting the individual threads of magic separate as the electricity desires. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L54)
- The image of the target in the caster's mind is as important as the pointing finger for the angle and trajectory a thrown spell takes. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L39)
- Wind can blow a spell off course, and past fifty feet Ceria's aim worsens. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L43)
- Pisces warns that magic, unlike a hammer or sewing needle, can slay an incautious practitioner outright even when the spell seems mastered. (Book 1, Ch1.34 L46)
- Ceria feels as if she is burning away her very soul when she reaches her limit, and her wand-hand burns with the backlash of a spell. (Book 14, Ch6.65 L838)
- Repeatedly casting even low-Tier spells is extremely draining. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L129)

## Learning spells
- Ceria says leveling in the [Mage] class is a great way to learn a new spell and much faster than teaching yourself, though a person can learn magic without a class. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L155-158)
- Ceria says magic requires study even with levels. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L226)
- Ceria says putting enough time and effort into a spell lets you master it, but there are limits. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L183)
- Ceria says that if she gained ten levels overnight learning a hard spell would be far easier, though understanding how it works would do as well. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L189)
- Ceria says that with a spellbook she can study a spell and learn it in a year or so. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L119)
- Ceria says a single spellbook costs several hundred gold or even a thousand, even for low-Tier spells. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L117)
- Ceria says she would need a few hundred gold more to buy a book with a few Tier 4 spells. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L114)
- Ceria says neither she nor Pisces can cast [Fireball], and that she used a wand when she had to cast it. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L173)
- Ryoka learned [Light] from Ceria in a single day. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L168)
- Pisces says he benefited from someone showing him how to cast [Electric Jolt] and found the way the teacher optimised it quite interesting. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L95)
- Calvaron says Wistram hands new students old spellbooks left behind by students, and the older mages sort them so nothing beyond a Tier 2 spell goes to newcomers. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L259)
- Spellbooks given to new Wistram students are mostly empty apprentice books with the first pages filled with another person's notes and spells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L258)
- Ceria has heard that spellbooks are warded against being read, and Pisces says the book they are after apparently killed the student who tried to take it out. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L93-94)
- Pisces uses [Detect Magic] to spot the most powerful spellbooks among a shelf of books. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L98)
- After days of decoding a spell from the library spellbook, Pisces suddenly blurs and appears several feet forward in an instant. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L229-231)
- Calvaron says that older students need masters because it is too hard to progress otherwise, given the limited number of spellbooks. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L149)
- Calvaron says many mages do not want to teach younger mages, so students persuade them with services, such as him getting his master almost anything he needs or Beatrice helping her master with his spells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L151)
- A mage may have several apprentices and teach all of them, or just one. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L151)
- Ceria says half-elves are gifted at magic, with more of it in their bodies, but do not learn spells any faster. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L87)
- Ceria thinks half-elves channel mana better than any other species. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L27)

## Wistram Academy: admission and structure
> See also: 02_Levels_Classes_Skills.md for the [Mage] class; individual mages go to later files.
- The isle of Wistram opens only one day a year to accept new students. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L7)
- Ceria is told to present herself at the docks in South Harbor, Belan, Terandria, and boards a ship there for the isle of Wistram. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L2-3,L7,L25)
- Attending Wistram Academy costs twenty gold pieces for a month. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L9)
- Anyone with magic who can pay the fee can enter Wistram, and applicants prove their ability to an examiner by showing spells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L10-13)
- The captain of the Errant Traveller tells boarding students to cause no trouble and cast no magic on his ship, and Ceria recalls the others being told the same. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L14,L31-32)
- The ship's captain checks arrivals against a paper list that shows each student's face in turn, which Ceria recognises as magic. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L35-36)
- Prospective students have just over a month to prove themselves and be accepted or sent home. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L25-26)
- Wistram trains Mages mainly but also students heading to related fields such as [Alchemist] or [Blacksmith], so long as magic is involved. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L76)
- New students take mandatory basic courses in their first month from mid-level [Mages]. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L34)
- New students get ink pots, quills and a low-level spellbook on their first day. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L80)
- Calvaron says the Wistram classes teach only the basics because most magic-based classes need at least ten levels in [Mage], and many take at least twenty. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L233)
- Wistram law states that mages below the third year are allowed only one personal spellbook. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L145)
- Many new students wear long robes, usually dark blue or black, bought with a low-quality wand; older students such as Calvaron and Beatrice do not. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L90)
- Ceria sees a wall end a few feet ahead of a pair of doors beside a diagonal junction of three corridors, and feels that architecture and the laws of space do not seem to matter in Wistram. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L53)
- A general central area of Wistram holds dormitories, classrooms and general storage, while the higher levels and lower recesses are off-limits to new students, with magical barriers, locked doors or Golems at stairwells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L317-318)
- Wistram has many libraries; some hold one subject, one era, or a pool where books are waterproofed for bathing readers. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L56)
- Mages in the windowless parts of the citadel can wander for hours without seeing the sun. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L3)
- Wistram's students include Humans, Drakes, a Selphid, a Centaur and Dullahans. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L90,L104; Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L268)
- Dullahans commonly study rune magic, and Beatrice, a Dullahan, is a [Runeshaper] whose armor is etched with runes. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L419-422)
- Golems clear dishes in Wistram unless students levitate them to another table. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L29)
- Mages at Wistram eat well, and students share a grand dining hall. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L442; Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L130)
- Outside her door Ceria uses [Detect Magic] and finds a hex on the flagstones that would make the ground slippery like grease and a second, hidden trap spell, which she takes to be a shock spell. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L3-9)
- Ceria thinks Wistram has few totally absolute rules, and Pisces suspects some restrictions are warnings meant to test how students react. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L26,L34)
- Wistram students trade secrets as a currency, such as unique classes or where treasure is buried, for coin or other secrets. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L196-228)

## Wistram Academy: exams, tests and duels
- The entrance exam at the end of the first month tests students on fundamental magic control, analysing enchantments, composure in battle and a final obstacle course allowing attack spells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L15-18)
- Entrance exam rankings feed bets and power games, and wealth or the favour of influential mages can also decide who passes. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L19-20)
- Of the hundreds who come each year, less than a tenth pass the entrance exams. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L5)
- Failed students are picked up by chartered ships the day after the yearly exams. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L5)
- Students from the second year onward face yearly qualification tests, which Ceria describes as proving you have learned something, such as a spell, a recitation or something made. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L235-238)
- A mage who fails to show progress may be forced to pay to stay, from a little for food and lodging to a lot. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L238)
- Pisces tells Montressa that second-year students are not tested nearly as harshly as older students such as Calvaron and Beatrice. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.5 L240-241)
- Duels are fairly common among Wistram students, with a shield spell protecting onlookers, and students duel for money, pride or anything else. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L97-99)
- Wistram also has spoken debates, decided by collective vote; voters must answer under a truth spell about who made the more convincing argument. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L99-102)
- Calvaron says the Council eases new students into life and does not allow fights on the first day. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L97)

## Wistram Academy: history, Archmages and factions
- Wistram has been around for thousands of years, founded when many nations were at war and mages wanted somewhere to learn and teach in peace. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L342)
- Wistram has always been independent: no nation controls it, it has an army of its own, and a fleet reaching its shores would face raised weather spells. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L342)
- {belief} Cessic says, on a tip he bought cheaply from Calvaron, that the Council, made up of the leaders of all the mage factions, chewed out the mage in charge of a class and told them to start teaching right away. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L342)
- The class [Archmage] exists, and the [Archmages] who ruled Wistram a few centuries ago all held it. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L349-352)
- Archmage Zelkyr was a Drake and the best of the [Archmages], famous for damaging a Walled City wall and for creating Golems. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L352)
- Zelkyr, with Cognita and an army of Golems, took undisputed control of Wistram and alone explored its higher reaches for ancient magics. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L355)
- The greatest mages in Wistram's history settled the highest floors, leaving creations, spells and rooms that are deadly traps to break into. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L358)
- After Cognita announced that Zelkyr was dead, she and the other Golems maintain Wistram, fight intruders and enforce his order that no one may venture above. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L370)
- Calvaron says that today the Archmages of Wistram are only the greatest mages there, not holders of the class, and that factions formed because no mage can control everyone. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L390)
- Factions are groups of mages with similar ideals who stick together when it comes to voting or pushing their agendas. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L390,L399)
- Calvaron advises new students not to join a faction before passing the exam. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L393-396)
- Calvaron stays independent, and factions keep each other in check so independents can function. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L415)
- Calvaron says only the Expansionists and Revivalists care about making sure there is a system for new students, though every faction usually contributes somehow. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L343)
- The Revivalists are one faction; Beatrice calls it hers and invites Ceria and Pisces to join if they pass the exam. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L389-392)
- The Isolationists want only mages already strong admitted and reaching for greater heights; Illphres belongs to them. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L402)
- The Libertarians want Wistram to take sides with nations and throw its influence around; many are from Terandria, and Rievan belongs to them. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L407-409)
- The Centrists want every mage in the world to be part of Wistram, and for Wistram to declare itself a nation. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L438)
- The Preservationists and Revivalists would protect Ceria from an Elementalist teacher, to avoid a rumour that Wistram is speciesist. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L417)
- Teaching posts are filled by mages who like it or earn their keep by it; when the old Drake Tshya died, the Preservationists insisted on teachers from all factions, which got Rievan the job, and the Centrists nominated Illphres. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L431-438)
- Pisces reports that the Libertarians want every new student they can get and tried to recruit him before the exam. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.4 L177,L182)

## Mana and mana exhaustion
- Ceria feels magic flow into her hand when she readies a spell. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L122)
- A mana potion Ceria has taken helps her recover after a fight, and in another scene she throws up a bit as one runs through her system. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L126,L285)
- A mage whose face is grey has used too much magic, and Ceria's mana potion helps her recover from the same strain. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L126)
- Ceria says she could cast [Illumination] only once before needing a mana potion, since casting it repeatedly is too exhausting. (Book 1, Ch1.40 L102)
- Halrac recognises paleness as the first symptom of mana exhaustion, and a [Mage] who is exhausted may vomit. (Book 8, Ch5.40 L418)
- Gerial says that without mana a mage team can do little, so Lir's team will often abandon a mission if something makes them use up all their potions or the enemy proves more numerous or stronger than expected. (Book 1, Ch1.00H L215)
- Pisces is still pale from mana exhaustion after a hard fight. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L90)
- Pisces is out of mana and Ceria is nearly tapped after a dungeon fight. (Book 3, Ch3.08H L160)
- Rievan teaches that mana may over-accumulate in a body from a spell or from a charged environment, but more often from improperly drinking a mana potion. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L230)
- Rievan teaches that casting any spell usually resolves excess mana in a mage, while those without mana can only rest. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L230)
- Rievan teaches that a competent mage may drain excess magic from another, which carries risk, and that high concentrations can rupture blood vessels or shut down body parts. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.3 L230)
- The occupant of the cave, tending the unconscious Ryoka, notes she is suffering from mana poisoning, which is far more difficult to cure than mere blood loss. (Book 1, Ch1.08R L211)
- Teriarch says the mana Ryoka drank would not have killed her; he says she would have had violent illness, fever and uncontrollable bodily excretion for days. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L167)
- Teriarch says a minuscule increase in diet is the only proper way to acclimatise a human body to magic, and he doubts modern mages know how to naturalise magic within themselves. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L169)
- Mana potions and alcohol do not mix well, according to a mage. (Book 1, Ch1.41 L496)
- Ryoka's mana-poisoned state followed her drinking mana potions she thought were healing potions; Ceria had given them to her by mistake. (Book 1, Ch1.07R L342; Book 1, Ch1.10R L35)
- Octavia is trying to standardise potion colours so adventurers do not use a mana potion instead of a healing potion. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L336)
- Erin wonders whether mana potions could make food magical. (Book 2, Ch2.37 L375)
- Everything contains magic: Octavia says traces are in rocks and grass, and something like a mana stone has quite a lot. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L135)
- {belief} Fals says he hears that the powerful magic coaches, which create horses out of magic, have to replace mana stones or recharge the magic quite often. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L246-248)
- A construct in a dungeon keeps a glowing mana stone in its chest. (Book 3, Ch3.07H L481)

## Healing, stamina and other potions
- Healing potions cost a gold coin or two, rising with potency, according to Ryoka's early market survey. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L123)
- A weak healing potion costs nearly a gold coin. (Book 1, Ch1.29 L134)
- The City Watch in Liscor carries healing potions, and Senior Guardsmen carry stronger ones that fix most big injuries unless a limb is lost. (Book 1, Ch1.21 L199-204)
- Klbkch gives Erin part of what he calls a low-grade healing potion, which closes a cut on her hand in an instant. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L261-262,L268)
- Healing potions only accelerate the body's natural healing; Ceria says complicated injuries such as a shattered bone need time or magic. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L215)
- A mage says that healing potions can fuse bone back together wrongly, leaving fighters with bones attached off-center. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L207)
- A stronger healing potion would start growing bone fragments out of the skin and destroy the leg, Ryoka is told. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L289)
- Geneva observes a cheap healing potion closing existing tissue at an accelerated rate rather than creating new skin. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L272)
- Geneva infers cheap potions would not manage bad breaks, cannot heal severed limbs and can inhibit reattachment if used wrongly. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L273)
- Thriss says the better type of healing potion removes poison and other problems, while his cheap potions do not. (Book 3, Ch1.00D L233)
- Geneva explains that potions do not cure disease, they accelerate natural functions, so they can make a disease worse and kill everyone it infects. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L531-534)
- Geneva says plagues are far more deadly in this world because of potions. (Book 10, Ch6.06D L534)
- Stamina potions restore energy and give a kick, but they do not heal a torn muscle and the user crashes hard when they wear off. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L176)
- Ryoka finds Octavia's stamina potion tastes awful, and Octavia prices them at a gold piece and eight silver each. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L339,L347)
- Ryoka runs for a night and a day on one of Octavia's stamina potions and Octavia warns it can only stave off exhaustion for so long. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L311,L341)
- Ryoka falls asleep once a stamina potion wears off and resolves never to overuse one again. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L225,L252)
- Octavia says she could not brew invisibility potions, and that a regeneration potion would need fifty more levels. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L172)
- Octavia identifies Teriarch's potion as a potion of haste or a higher-level version, made with top ingredients and perfectly heated. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L392-394)
- Teriarch pays Ryoka for a delivery with a Potion of Haste that shifts colours when removed from its stand. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L373; Book 2, Ch2.09 L395)
- Octavia says potions can be any colour and she is trying to standardise them, and she offers a yellow potion for a wound. (Book 2, Ch2.09 L336; Book 2, Ch2.14 L252-253)

## Alchemy
- Octavia says she is no mage and that alchemy needs only magical ingredients are rendered down by boiling and mixing and then combined. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L130)
- Octavia says everything has some magic, and an extracted magical quality can be stored in a base liquid so more ingredients can be added. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L135-139)
- Octavia says alchemy depends on order, heat and time: moon dew can be used only at night under a waxing moon, and Goblin ears must be boiled while mixing. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L142)
- Octavia says alchemy is unpredictable and dangerous, and covers more than potions, such as a tripvine bag. (Book 2, Ch2.12 L144)
- Octavia's custom-order price for testing and delivery of Ryoka's potions is eight gold, and she is not sure everything asked is possible. (Book 2, Ch2.14 L36)
- An [Alchemist] sifts saltpeter, sulfur, charcoal and wood dust together with a material that catches fire in open air for Ryoka. (Book 2, Ch2.24 L390)
- Teriarch, watching an image of Octavia and Erin, calls the white potion Octavia tosses into a smoking pot a neutralizing potion and says the carrots should have been mixed with a cold agent before the Corusdeer horns. (Book 2, Ch2.39 L189-190)
- Eir Gel from a single island off Baleros is the foundation for modern healing potions and supplies nearly the whole world. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L537,L540)
- Octavia says every [Alchemist] has their own recipe, and potions differ because one missing or extra ingredient changes the result. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L580,L598)
- Octavia's cheap booster is made from Sage's Grass, sugar water, powdered demi-Newt skins and roach shells. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L582)
