# Levels, Classes and Skills
> Scope: how levelling, classes, skills, class changes, conditions and adventurer ranks work as the books present them (Books 1-19); individual characters' level histories and Names live in their own files.
> Keys: levelling, classes, skills, ranks
> See also: 04_Cosmology_Gods_Names_Dungeons.md (Names, Name-bearers), 03_Magic.md (spell Tiers), 05_Species_Core.md, 06_Species_Other.md

## Levelling: what it is and who does it
- Klbkch explains that classes are general ways of life people take, that each new level raises proficiency at the role, and that skills may be learnt along the way. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L118-120)
- A new class is gained by meeting its requirements, which are often known but not always. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L118; Book 1, Ch1.07 L189)
- Relc says a man he knew gained four levels in [Farmer] just by growing carrots in pots beside his window. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L189)
- Early on Erin says she kept levelling every time she fell asleep, and Relc asks whether the notification woke her as she fell asleep, saying he hates that and that it is very annoying. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L166-169)
- Level-ups appear as bracketed lines, for example "[Innkeeper Level 11!]" followed by skill lines such as "[Skill – Lesser Strength Obtained!]". (Book 1, Ch1.27 L223-225)
- Narration from Rags's viewpoint says that in this world levelling up was more important than a birthday and an occasion for rejoicing, and her tribe celebrates after she tells them she has changed class. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L11,L13-14)
- Klbkch says only the thinking races can level, while animals and even Dragons cannot; Goblins can. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L195-197)
- Klbkch says all races that think are given the ability to level and take classes, and that no one knows who set this up. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L127-131)
- {belief} Klbkch recites a passage whose exact wording he says comes from The Book of Levels, originally written nearly a thousand years ago: all who Think Feel, from Feeling they Act, in Action they Level, all who Think have a Class, and in that Class they find destiny. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L131-137)
- {belief} Klbkch tells the tale that once all races fought with tooth, claw and magic, and those who forsook their natures were given the gift of [Leveling] while the rest kept the might of their natures. (Book 1, Ch1.07 L198)
- Erin's early conclusion is that the world works like a game, with levels and skills announced in her head. (Book 1, Ch1.02 L11-15)
- Krshia says leveling comes from learning and trials, so doing the same thing without change gives no experience. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L65)
- Selys says Erin has probably levelled so quickly because starting an inn by herself must be a lot harder than just working in one or taking over a business. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L66)
- Erin says she gained ten levels in a month and is Level 10, and Selys says nobody levels that fast. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L24-29)
- Selys says people normally apprentice for years before reaching Level 10 and most people of her age are barely Level 12 in their chosen profession. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L29)
- In-world, some people treat levelling as a religion and worship those with the highest levels. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L83)
- {belief} Krshia has heard it said that each person is given a maximum level, and that reaching it means the end of their life. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L83)
- {belief} Ceria says some people claim everyone has a set amount of levels in them and will run out if they gain too many across different classes; Ryoka says she does not believe this either. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L110-111)

## How common high levels are
- Krshia says the elderly people she has known have levels in their twenties or sometimes thirties, none above Level 40, and few reach their thirtieth level. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L38)
- Krshia says only a few people in each continent are above Level 50, and she does not think she knows of anyone living above Level 70, only ancient legends of heroes at that level. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L38-41)
- Klbkch says most people reach about Level 20 in one class, a few reach 30, those who reach 40 are famous, and those past 50 are known throughout the world. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L399)
- Klbkch says there are a handful of Level 30s in each town and a few dozen in a city. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L399)
- Klbkch knows of no one near Level 100, though legends tell of levels above eighty and ninety. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L403)
- Klbkch says most people grow too old to keep levelling long before passing Level 60. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L411)
- Ressa tells Lady Magnolia that, leaving out city-states' private armies and other lords' retainers, they can call upon two thousand individuals with combat-classes above Level 20, over six hundred of them above Level 30, six Named Adventurers, eight Gold-rank teams and at least three hundred and fifty irregular units. (Book 2, ChInterlude_B02_1 L122-124)
- Lyonette says her family keeps a list of rulers above Level 40 and that fewer than forty exist in the world, counting only [Kings] and [Queens]. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L99-101)
- Lyonette's father guessed the King of Destruction is probably just above Level 50 and not yet Level 60, among the highest-levelled [Kings] in the world. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L93)
- Selys says Relc is about Level 32, which is incredibly different from a Level 13 [Warrior]. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L76)
- Garia says [Enhanced Strength] only comes to warrior classes around Level 30, so getting it so early means she is a lot stronger than normal. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L237-239)
- Selys says Peslas, a Liscor [Innkeeper] above Level 30, runs one of the best inns in the city but, being old and relaxed, levels about once every couple of years. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L533-536)

## Level thresholds and what they mean
- Grimalkin says [Warriors] tend to gain a powerful Skill with their class around Level 30, in an offensive, defensive or utility direction. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L264)
- Val agrees with Ryoka's guess that there are rare skills every ten levels and says he got his [Double Step] skill at Level 30. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L163-165)
- Garia has heard that a [Greater Strength] skill exists for people of very high level but has never seen anyone with it. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L237-239)
- A Level 20 requirement is the entry to the Knights of the Petal and the prerequisite for the [Knight] class. (Book 7, Ch5.20G L677)
- Calvaron tells two new Wistram students that unless they are over Level 20 or have some new spell or talent they will be hard-pressed to pass the exam, unless they have a patron to sponsor them. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.1 L397-399)
- A man who comes up beside the narrator says Sir Richard, having reached Level 30 as a [Knight], is considered a peer of the realm in many respects, and that all those with a level higher than 30 are honored in various ways by the crown. (Book 6, Ch1.03C L446-448)
- Liscor's Watch orders a curfew just past dark for anyone without ten levels in a combat class. (Book 8, Ch5.38 L40)
- Calvaron tells new Wistram students that most magic classes require ten to twenty levels of [Mage] first, which is why schools teach basics. (Book 4, ChWistram_Days_Pt.2 L233)
- Spell Tiers loosely follow caster level: up to about Level 16 for 1st or 0 Tier, about 24 for 2nd Tier, about 32 for 3rd Tier, though lower-level mages can cast higher Tier magic with practice. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L186-187)
- Ilvriss says a [Strategist] is Level 30, or lower if one combines a military rank with [Tactician], though he calls the shortcut diluted. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L51)

## Level gaps and power
- Selys says levels can replace numbers and muscle only if there is a big difference in levels. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L75-76)
- Magnolia says the Antinium fight assuming all soldiers are equal because they do not level, and that other armies fight differently. (Book 2, Ch2.31 L379)
- Lyonette says a Level 60 [Mayor] might equal a Level 30 [King], because a [King]'s power extends across the whole kingdom. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L104)
- Klbkch says that at his peak he was a Level 44 [Swordslayer] and Level 26 [Assassin] and Ryoka notes he was then stronger than a typical Named Adventurer. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L423-424)
- Ryoka estimates a Named Adventurer is only around Level 40. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L424)
- Cervial says Silver-rank is mostly the low twenties at best and Gold-rank starts around Level 24, though equipment and skill matter. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L175-176)
- Grimalkin says Yvlon's sword already puts her at Gold-rank, and he will certify her Gold if she gains a decent Level 30 Skill. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L266)
- After the battle, Dawil, the Horns, Moore, Seborn and Halrac raise their hands to say they levelled, while Revi scowls, Yvlon asks her brother, Falene and the others if they did not level, and Ylawes says he will probably level within the next week or month at the latest. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L311-316)
- Ylawes says most of them are past Level 30, and Dawil says this lot does not level every time they go into a hairy situation before asking about Skills and saying he got nothing. (Book 7, Ch5.13 L316,L321)
- Lyonette says the better a class, the slower its holder levels; she adds that royalty also level slowly because they rarely face danger. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L93-99)

## What a class is and how one is gained
- A class announces itself with a message: Erin's first night gives "[Innkeeper Class Obtained!]", "[Innkeeper Level 1!]" and the skills [Basic Cleaning] and [Basic Cooking]. (Book 1, Ch1.01 L86-89)
- Klbkch says that for some classes the requirements are known but there are exceptions, and a [Ruler]-type class cannot simply be taken; Erin guesses you must be born into it. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L118-119)
- Krshia says only things people make their goals and dreams form classes, not everyday acts like walking, and a [Gourmet] class exists for some rich merchants and nobles. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L12-14)
- Selys says a person can qualify for a class and still not get it, because it has to become part of their life. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L17)
- Selys says need and want determine which skills people get, so people get them at different times or in different variations. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L53)
- Pisces says Erin gains no [Tactician] levels from chess unless she considers its tactical applications, because chess gives far less experience than real work as a strategist. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L144)
- Laken works out that his [Emperor] class and its level came from intent and purpose, and he reasons that exp for an [Emperor] involves growing his kingdom and gaining subjects. (Book 3, Ch3.11E L166-169)
- Rags's early Goblin classes were Level 2 [Backstabber] and Level 5 [Scavenger]. (Book 7, Ch5.19G L147)
- Selys says a [Spearmaster] who starts using a sword would get [Warrior] first, and later a specialised class such as [Swordslayer] or [Duelist] once level and skills allow. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L45)
- Selys says classes change names mainly to show specialisation or higher level, and [Tactician] can become [Leader] or [General] or stay the same depending on skills. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L49)
- Embria says a [Spearmaster] can be gained by years of training or by killing one in combat. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L662)
- Class changes announce themselves with a line like "[Conditions Met: Innkeeper → Magical Innkeeper Class!]", as when Erin reaches Level 30 as an [Innkeeper]. (Book 4, Ch3.33 L515-517)
- Klbkch says there is no one set of skills for a class, so two [Soldiers] may learn different skills at the same level. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L120-122)
- Klbkch notes that while Relc is a higher-level [Guardsman] than he, Relc lacks the [Detect Guilt] skill. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L122)
- Garia says [Farm Workers] are looked down on, yet the class gives useful skills like [Lesser Strength], and she got it on her second level. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L233-237)

## Class rarity and quality
- Flos tells the twins that many classes are narrow in purpose, so a [Farmer] cannot learn dancing skills but may learn to defend his land or lead helpers. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L262)
- Flos says some classes have more opportunity and force: a [Survivor] has many Skills but a [Lord] will have more powerful Skills, and a [King] stands above all other classes as one of the few with the strongest Skills. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L264)
- Lyonette says a [General] can affect whole armies and Skills like demoralising enemy armies, so classes that influence people are among the best. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L43-45)
- Lyonette says royal, rare and merged classes are better than normal ones and a [Princess] can give someone a unique class. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L53)
- Lyonette says a [Lord] has almost the Skills of a [General] but can also empower his estate, and a [Blademaster] has far better Skills than any [Warrior] or normal [Knight]. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L53)
- Lyonette says an [Emperor] may be better than a [King] because only the emperor's Skills affect the entire empire. (Book 3, Ch3.23L L51)
- {belief} Laken believes [General], [Steward] and [Paladin] are rare and stronger than normal classes because they are more important. (Book 5, Ch4.23E L402)
- Olesm calls [Tactician] the first of the strategist class line, which might lead to [Strategist] and then [Grandmaster]. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L49)
- Olesm says he has heard of people getting the [Layabout] or [Idler] class, which usually only applies to the aristocracy. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L58)
- Drassi is a Level 17 [Gossip], a class most people have never heard of, with the Skills [Time to Chat] and [Trusted Voice]. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L39)
- The Earther narrator says a [Clown] might not exist in this world, but classes are what people make them. (Book 2, Ch1.01C L52)
- A Level 1 [Hero] class was given to all the summoned Earthers, speeds levelling in other classes, and many later lost it. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L33,L196-199)
- Tom reports that some of the Earthers think the [Hero] class has only one level, and he reasons that a [Hero] is only a hero if other people think he is. (Book 2, Ch1.00C L202-205)
- Numbtongue's [Bard] and Headscratcher's [Berserker] are called unique classes for Goblins, and the Hobs cannot think of any Goblin who had [Bard]. (Book 8, Ch5.41 L146-148)
- Viceria says some classes shape the way people look: [Mages] tend to be thin and look younger, while [Farmers] tend to grow larger, and Garia's first class was [Farmer]. (Book 5, Ch4.17 L103-106)

## One class or many
- Selys says in theory you could have as many classes as you want, but even most Adventurers have only three or four. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L17)
- In an online chat among Earthers, a Level 12 Warrior says more classes mean more skills, while Loran Grimnar says acquiring new classes is hard and he has no more than three. (Book 2, ChInterlude_The_Call L246-247)
- Klbkch explains that gaining levels gets exponentially harder over time. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L397)
- {belief} Ryoka theorises that the system counts cumulative levels across all classes when working out how hard the next level is, so extra classes stunt a person's growth. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L434-442)
- Klbkch is sceptical that cumulative levels matter or that a maximum level of one hundred exists, as that needs unproven suppositions. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L436)
- Klbkch says the first Grand Queen reached Level 79 as a [Supreme Matriarch], a special class gained by advancing as a [Queen] for centuries, and had only one other class. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L446-451)
- Klbkch reviews his Hive's data on several hundred high-level individuals and finds their cumulative levels rarely approach one hundred and that those with more than two classes rarely pass Level 30. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L70)
- Selys, hearing the theory, worries that her many classes mean she will never reach a high level. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L575)
- Flos tells Trey and Teres in secret that the more classes one has, the weaker one becomes over time, and a person with one class will always reach a higher level than one with many. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L251)
- Flos says this is a secret of rulers, which is why they forbid their offspring from taking up common classes. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L255)
- Flos has kept the twins from taking classes such as [Servant] or [Warrior] without knowing what they will become. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L264)
- Krshia fears that a single-class rule would force Gnoll cubs to choose roles very young, which she finds troubling. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L223-228)
- Gnoll tribes are practical, and one day someone is a hunter and the next a cook, so specialisation would change tribal life. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L224)
- Flos fears that if being a student counts as a class, schools like those of Earth would be dangerous, and says he must not allow them. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L246)

## Class consolidation and merging
- Klbkch says two classes may merge in some rare cases to create a new hybrid class, for example [Warrior] and [Strategist] into [Commander]. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L111)
- Klbkch speculates his [Swordslayer] and [Commander] have not merged because [Swordslayer] advances [Warrior], or because he may have wished to keep a pure combat class. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L113-115)
- Klbkch had regarded class merging as a somewhat negative phenomenon until that day. (Book 2, Ch2.36 L118)
- Olesm says that if you gain enough warrior levels it is possible to transition into a commander class, and the narration from Klbkch's viewpoint says enough levels in [Warrior] and [Strategist] would merge into a new class, usually [Commander], that some people could level in [Commander] right away, and that as far as Klbkch could tell merging was simply a way to consolidate levels. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L112-114)
- Laken calls a sudden swap of classes "class consolidation" when Prost loses all his [Farmer] and [Builder] levels and one [Trader] level and gains Level 8 [Steward]. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L288-291)
- Laken thinks Prost's farming experience does not transfer into [Steward], which is a sacrifice, though he may keep some or all of his Skills. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L293)
- Prost's new Skills include [Talent Finder] and [Eye of Need], which help him find the people best suited to a task. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L300)
- Durene says a [Commander] could take the [Soldier] and [Sergeant] classes and turn them into one thing. (Book 5, Ch4.20E L292)
- Rags's night of [Tactician] Level 16 and [Leader] to [Chieftain] Level 15 also announces "Class Consolidation: Scavenger removed" and "Class Consolidation: Tinkerer removed", and she gains the Skills [Tribe: Fleet Foot] and [Lesser Tinkering]. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L3-9)
- In a vision, the Goblin King's [Healer] class merged into his [Chieftain] class. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L179)
- Pyrite's [Mining Chieftain] class was consolidated into [Magestone Chieftain], letting him use magic via gemstones without being a [Mage]. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L59)
- Rufelt assumes a consolidated class means Erin is at least Level 20. (Book 10, Ch6.10 L184)
- Lyonette's [Beast Tamer] class is consolidated into [Worldly Princess]. (Book 10, Ch6.08 L512)
- When Lyonette's [Princess] class is restored, five classes ([Warrior], [Barmaid], [Beast Tamer], [Tactician], [Carer]) are removed as consolidated into [Worldly Princess] at Level 11. (Book 8, Ch5.26L L579-585)
- Lyonette's [Worldly Princess] at Level 19 is described as a rare class consolidation before Level 20. (Book 16, Ch7.16L L66)
- Pebblesnatch reaches [Cook] Level 10 and consolidates [Grabber] into [Forager Cook], gaining [Natural Oils] and [One More Portion]. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L851-855)
- The narrator calls [Forager Cook] not the best consolidation, still below [Chef], but notes it came at Level 10. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L858)
- Goblin low-level classes like [Stirrer] and [Roaster] rarely consolidate into better ones. (Book 15, Ch7.05P L612)
- Paige announces that she changed her class after levelling and calls it a class consolidation. (Book 14, Ch6.68 L170)
- Politician-turned-minister Lyfelt gained [Politician] and consolidated it to [Minister]. (Book 10, Ch6.15K L193)
- Embria says no class consolidation combines [Sergeant] and [Spearmaster], so her father stays a [Spearmaster] and [Sergeant]. (Book 11, ChInterlude_Embria L662)
- Gazi says her [War Scout] class is a product of her [Infiltrator], [Fighter], [Rider] and [Archer] classes over time, reached at Level 30. (Book 15, Ch7.09K L416)
- Raelt, King of Jecrass, reaches Level 30 as [King] and the class becomes [King of Challenges], with [Duelist] consolidated away. (Book 16, Ch7.13K L1082-1084)
- A [Hammerer] says he has consolidated twice. (Book 16, Ch7.15R L589)
- Garia, a [Runner] training as a [Martial Artist], hopes to consolidate the two classes because both are about the body, though she has no idea what class she would get. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L80-82)

## Losing a class
- Lyonette's [Princess] class is lost when the system reports "Class Conditions: Princess failed", and she also loses [Detect Poison] and [Royal Tax]. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L492-495)
- Later "Class Conditions: Princess met" restores the class and the lost skills, and the class turns into [Worldly Princess]. (Book 8, Ch5.26L L575-579)
- Lyonette wonders if she lost her [Princess] class because she had no subjects or kingdom, or had given it up. (Book 8, Ch5.26L L573)
- Klbkch says each time the Queen resurrects him, he loses ten levels from his classes, an occurrence found only among the Antinium. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L417-419)
- Klbkch is currently Level 21 [Swordslayer], Level 18 [Commander], Level 11 [Diplomat] and Level 14 [Assassin], but at his peak he was Level 44 [Swordslayer] and Level 26 [Assassin]. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L423)
- Klbkch says the Rite of Anastases is a process the Queen of the Antinium may undertake to let the dead be reborn into a new body, that only a Prognugator would be considered worthy of the effort, that he lost ten levels in every class, and that his Queen laboured nearly three weeks without rest. (Book 1, Ch1.45 L56-63)

## Skills: how they are gained and what they do
- Val describes skills as a safe gamble: each level gives some good and some less useful skills, but which ones you may get can be predicted from the class, like a pool of options. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L160-163)
- Val says almost every close-range [Warrior] gets [Thick Skin] sooner or later, but one might get it at Level 5 and another much later. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L163)
- Val agrees with Ryoka's guess that there are rare skills every ten levels, and says a rare skill can also be learned, but that takes training and some luck. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L164-165)
- Klbkch says there is no one set of skills for a class; skills depend on need and inclination. (Book 1, Ch1.17 L120-122)
- Pisces says classes and Skills are acquired often in line with the user's desires, and he has no Skills for summoning flesh-based undead. (Book 11, Ch6.18H L122)
- Garia got [Lesser Strength] on her second level up as a [Farm Worker]; she says it is the first skill most warriors get, followed by the stronger [Enhanced Strength] and, for very high levels, [Greater Strength]. (Book 1, Ch1.10R L237)
- Selys says no one in Liscor has [Expert Cooking], that you would have to be a really high-level [Chef] to have it, and that it might appear at Level 40, or maybe Level 30 if you got lucky. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L99-103)
- Erin gains [Advanced Cooking] and [Advanced Crafting] on reaching Level 21 as an [Innkeeper], while [Basic Cooking] came at Level 1. (Book 2, Ch2.16 L460-463; Book 1, Ch1.01 L89)
- Erin complains that finding a [Cook] with [Advanced Cooking] is hard because people with that Skill are rare. (Book 5, Ch4.11 L404)
- Ryoka concludes that skills can be rated by rarity, which generally corresponds to level. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L101)
- Klbkch tells Pisces that [Detect Guilt] is a basic skill all guardsmen learn at Level 15. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L196)
- Skill usage can be limited: Olesm says he can use one Skill only once a day at his level and that his other skills are mostly defensive or informational. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L227)
- Laken's Skills show that some are permanent passive changes, such as Durene's [Enhanced Strength], while others must be used. (Book 3, Ch3.01E L9)
- Lyonette recalls a tutor saying a Skill improves what is already there, so [Basic Cooking] without actual cooking experience gives only basic results. (Book 3, Ch3.05L L147-148)
- Some Skills can be copied by learning the technique: Klbkch says [Triple Thrust], three literally simultaneous strikes, cannot be copied by mere technique, but others are crystallised forms that can be taught. (Book 14, Ch6.62L L299)
- Jasi's Skill summons exactly five gold pieces' worth of supplies before a performance; she hopes it upgrades, as some Skills do, and tells Rima to wait until Level 20. (Book 14, Ch6.63P L439-442)
- Skill names can carry a prefix for what they affect, such as [Tribe: Fleet Foot], [Inn: Magical Grounds] and [Army: Flicker Charge]. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L8; Book 4, Ch3.33 L517; Book 16, Ch7.13K L1085)
- Erin's [Magical Innkeeper] skills [Inn: Magical Grounds] and [Inn: Field of Preservation] arrive at Level 30. (Book 4, Ch3.33 L515-518)
- Erin imagines a Level 40 message granting [Inn: Unlimited Mana Pool] and [Kamehameha Punch] and waits for the real voice, but nothing comes. (Book 14, Ch6.67 L954-960)
- Pisces's Level 30 Skill gave him greater mastery of bone shaping, though Draugr creation was a common alternative at that level. (Book 6, Ch4.37O L157)
- Skills that work on others can be resisted: Pisces says any powerful monster or individual will resist most of an aura Skill, though it works well on low-level enemies. (Book 2, Ch2.27 L226)
- Lyonette teaches that auras have potency, which is their power, and control, which is how they are used; she tells Erin she has high potency but her control is weak. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L121-125)
- Lyonette says she consolidated her aura into something that works like a shield, while Erin's aura is too wide and works like a hammer. (Book 17, Ch7.23LM L117)
- Val says that other Runners will only become Couriers if they get really lucky and learn a good skill. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L146)
- Skills are not always known to their owners: Erin cannot recall what her new [Inn's Aura] and [Wondrous Fare] skills do right after waking. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L12-16)
- A ruler's Skill can apply to the whole kingdom, such as Flos's Skill that makes his soldiers level up quicker, and such kingdom-wide Skills are rare even for rulers. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L350)
- Flos says a [King] can issue conditional Skills like edicts, but only one edict is active, and a new one replaces the first. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L353)
- Flos says Mars's Skills are almost all specific to herself, while his affect more people. (Book 10, Ch6.14K L353)

## Conditions, aspects and other system messages
- Class changes use lines such as "[Conditions Met: Warrior → General Class!]". (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1367)
- Consolidations list each removed class, for example "[Class Consolidation: Warrior removed.]". (Book 9, Ch5.62 L1368)
- A [Condition] line can follow a level message: the Earther [Clown] receives "[Condition – Horrific Laughter Received.]" and "[Condition – Lesser Insanity Received.]" when she reaches Level 24. (Book 2, Ch1.01C L45-49)
- The [Condition] lines can be removed: the Florist turned monster later gets "[Condition – Terrible Hunger Removed!]". (Book 3, Ch3.20T L88)
- A monster-making change uses its own format: "[Rank 1 Horror – Corpse Eater.]" with Conditions "Terrible Hunger" and "Outcast Humanity", and [Aspect] lines like "Body of the Eater". (Book 3, Ch3.17T L206-210)
- When the Florist became a Horror, her [Florist] level dropped from 19 to 16 and she lost [Aroma of Spring] and [Winter Growth]; at Rank 2 she dropped to 12 and lost [Quick Growth] and gained [Gaping Bite]. (Book 3, Ch3.17T L211-213; Book 3, Ch3.17T L225-229)
- Skills lost or restored are announced the same way, as with Lyonette's [Detect Poison] and [Royal Tax]. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L494-495; Book 8, Ch5.26L L577-578)

## How experience is gained
- Ceria says mages level from casting spells, but study also gives a lot of experience, so throwing fireballs alone does not level fast. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L112)
- Val says [Warriors] level faster by killing stronger enemies, while [Runners] level by how far they run and how challenging the run is. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L153-155)
- Calruz is called a Levelist because he fights any enemy just to gain experience. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L172)
- Garen tells Rags training only works when it hurts, and Rags learns that levelling by training is possible but slow, which she finds common knowledge among non-Goblins. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L165-170)
- Belgrade says chess is important because it teaches strategic thinking and asks why so many [Tacticians] and [Strategists] play it; a reply says it makes one level, and Anand says he could understand a low-level [Tactician] benefiting but finds more than five or six levels suspect for a high-level [Strategist]. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L57-60)
- The Counter-level Effect, according to Pisces, means defenders in a war level faster than superior aggressors, and Zel Shivertail was an example. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L505-507)
- Desperate fighting in narrow corridors during the Antinium Wars led to "explosive leveling" among the defenders. (Book 2, ChS02_The_Antinium_Wars_Pt.2 L25)
- Erin objects that the Counter-level Effect only works if the defenders are losing. (Book 13, Ch6.52K L510)
- After a day of frantic effort, Erin has gained four levels and two skills. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L12)
- Lyonette, after not having been a [Princess] for a long time, levelled four times in one day and thinks it may be because [Barmaid] reached Level 15. (Book 8, Ch5.24L L489-500)
- A Dragon tells Erin he has met legends who reached higher levels than hers at her age, but never an [Innkeeper] who rose as she did. (Book 17, Ch7.27 L750-753)

## Refusing to level and levelless people
- Ryoka stopped herself from levelling, saying she does not like being controlled and thinks classes and levels are a way for a system to control people. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L75-78)
- Ryoka says she can cancel a level-up if she thinks hard enough. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L82)
- Adventurers who learn Ryoka has no levels ask whether she is a demi-human, a monster, or a fool. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L353-356)
- Val says that he meets people who choose not to level now and then, and that they overcome the problem with magic. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L167-170)
- Ryoka tells Selys and Klbkch that one can refuse to level up or take a class, and Selys says she never understood why that was possible. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L579-581)
- Ryoka suspects that choice exists precisely because of the level-cap problem. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L583)

## Adventurer ranks and levels
- Ryoka learns that a Bronze-rank adventuring team is not much better than rookies, while a Silver-rank team is more than a match for a small city's standing militia. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L32)
- Gold-rank adventurers are experienced, highly trained groups that handle most monster threats easily, and at the top of the rank teams equal small armies and can affect disputes between nations. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L33)
- Gold is as high as most ranking systems go; some countries extend ranks into platinum and adamantium, but functionally a party or individual at that renown is no longer ranked. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L32-34)
- Past Gold-rank, adventurers are called Named Adventurers because their names are known across the part of the world where they travel. (Book 1, Ch1.11R L35)
- Cervial adds that individual skill still plays a big role, and Yvlon says Calruz is only around Level 20 but could have joined a Gold-rank team. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L176-178)
- A confirmed old magical dungeon is expected to pull in Named Adventurers and Gold-rank adventurers, possibly from other continents. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L94)
- Ryoka understands that Yvlon and Cervial rely on magical equipment along with levels, while Calruz the Levelist thinks levels are all he needs. (Book 1, Ch1.12R L172-173)

## Levels in non-humans and other beings
- Antinium Workers have very few levels; Pawn is a Level 2 [Butcher] and Level 1 [Carpenter], and some Workers do not level at all. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L60-62)
- Selys thinks Antinium Soldiers do not need levels and would be an unstoppable army if they were given high levels and produced in numbers. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L74)
- Erin says Pawn told her other Workers have levelled in [Tactician], probably the Goblins too, though she has none. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L129)
- A Drake guard tells Erin that Klbkch was the only Antinium he had heard of with a level higher than 10. (Book 1, Ch1.36 L93)
- Ryoka states that the Queen's mission is to create Individual Antinium capable of levelling, which would make the Antinium stronger, and Klbkch nods. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L279-282)
- The new Antinium Individuals level far quicker than Klbkch or the Queen. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L281)
- Ksmvr says the Antinium do not level except Individuals, and that he was Level 12 when he met Ceria and is now a Level 16 [Warrior]. (Book 5, Ch4.27H L511-515)
- Ksmvr says Antinium do not level greatly, so magic was not seen as a viable field for them. (Book 4, Ch3.32 L199)
- Klbkch reflects that Individuals can level exponentially quickly by Antinium standards and are closer to Prognugators, but true Hive Soldiers are stronger to begin with. (Book 8, Ch5.39 L550-552)
- Klbkch holds the class [Diplomat] at Level 11 and uses its Level 10 Skill [Reassuring Presence] daily; no one, not even his Queen, knows he has these tertiary classes, which he understands inhibit his levelling. (Book 11, Ch6.29 L253-256)
- Garen, whose tribe is the Red Fang Tribe, has no levels in [Chieftain] or [Leader], and his idea of leadership is leading a small group of warriors into combat. (Book 2, Ch2.03G L84-86)
- A Hobgoblin [Stirrer] is described as a low-level cook not yet at the [Cook] class, which happens when a Goblin gets to Level 10. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L64)
- Erin's lessons gave some Cave Goblins the [Cook] class straight away, without waiting for Level 10. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Pebblesnatch_and_Garry L64,L69)
- A Selphid can have levels like anyone else: Calectus is a Level 36 [Honor Guard], the senior and highest-level of the Selphids with Geneva. (Book 5, Ch1.04D L145-148)
- A character says any undead that can level will eventually rebel, like Golems, and Pisces replies that Toren proved it possible, though Toren levelled quickly enough to subvert his control. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L340-343)
- Toren sees that common skeletons cannot restore themselves and have no levels, unlike him. (Book 2, Ch2.20 L48)
- Fetohep says that as a Revenant, one of the few undead who keep their will, he does not level but keeps all the Skills he had in life. (Book 13, Ch6.55K L122)
- Fierre says Vampires do not earn classes or level up, and her mother lost her classes when she became a vampire. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L261)
- {belief} Fierre says Old Bamer thinks the lack of Vampire levels is a way of keeping balance, and asks the listener to imagine a high-level [Warrior] who was a vampire. (Book 10, Ch6.01 L261)
- Fierre says Old Bamer told her Vampire Lords of old had Skills and could turn to mist, though she thinks that may be a relic of the past. (Book 18, Ch7.41 L82)
- Fierre reflects that a Vampire gains no levels, like an animal or sub-races such as Ogres or the true Giants of old, while even Goblins level. (Book 12, Ch6.42E L224)
- Erin tells a group of adventurers that Raskghar are big and strong but cannot level. (Book 8, Ch5.27 L446)
- An adventurer says the Raskghar do not have Skills and so can be prepared for. (Book 8, Ch5.34 L129)
- {belief} Krshia imagines Raskghar as primal Gnolls who cannot level but are stronger than modern Gnolls, based on legends too old to give specifics. (Book 6, Ch4.36O L163)
- A Grand Strategist says regular Raskghar are around a Level 15 Gnoll [Warrior] unless it is a full moon, when with increased abilities they are Level 20 at best, and that awakened Raskghar are different. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L520)
- A council member in the same scene says Raskghar without Skills are inherently weaker and will not level, but can go into fighting without needing to level. (Book 19, Ch7.53 L526)
- The Gnoll council in the city chooses the oldest, wisest, most experienced or highest-level Gnolls in place of a [Chieftain]. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L512)
- Krshia's authority among them comes partly from a relatively high level. (Book 9, ChInterlude_Krshia L516)

## Common class families, as the text shows them
- A [Runner] belongs to roughly three kinds: Street Runners within one city, City Runners across a region, and Couriers who go anywhere and are each at least Level 30 per Garia, with levels in other classes. (Book 1, Ch1.01R L110-115)
- Fals says few experienced Runners exist because no one lives long doing the job, and most change class after earning enough. (Book 1, Ch1.05R L88)
- A [Healer] uses herbs and minor spells, while a [Cleric] or high-level mage-healer is needed for serious wounds and such healers are very rare. (Book 1, Ch1.04R L217-224)
- A [Healer] who says he is not high-level tells Yvlon that perhaps one with a Skill, or a [Bone Surgeon] from a Gnoll tribe's rare healers, might deal with her affliction, but that as it is he can do nothing. (Book 3, Ch3.10 L7)
- The best [Healer] in Bolandus, who reached out to Geneva, is Level 37, and Geneva lectures a sixty-year-old Dullahan [Healer] over Level 30 about her treatment. (Book 17, Ch7.22D L1019; Book 19, Ch7.51 L44)
- Pisces says wizard, sorcerer, warlock and witch are titles for mages who meet certain requirements, and mage is the general term. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L51)
- Pisces says the spell that shortens the storm could easily be cast by a Level 30 mage, or by a lower-level one if specialised. (Book 1, Ch1.10 L57)
- Pisces lists his levels as fifteen in [Elementalist], eight in [Alchemist], six in [Enchanter] and ten in [Mage], and says a single level in the [Necromancer] class makes a mage killed on sight in this region. (Book 1, Ch1.11 L32, L68)
- Yvlon's longsword was made by a Level 32 [Blacksmith], and Flos asks a merchant for masterworks made by a Level 40 [Blacksmith] or better. (Book 2, Ch2.00H L45; Book 7, ChInterlude_Flos L278)
- Olesm's class [Tactician] is the first step of the strategist class line, and Pisces says it is useless in small groups unless the holder has combat skill. (Book 1, Ch1.20 L49; Book 2, Ch2.00H L269)
- Niers Astoragon is called the highest-leveled [Strategist] on the continent, yet in the same chapter he is a Level 63 [Tactician]. (Book 2, Ch2.17 L359, L364)
- Anand tells Belgrade that understanding war beyond games is key to being a [Strategist] and not simply a [Tactician], and says Belgrade will reach the class first. (Book 6, Ch4.42L L231)
- Yvlon is a [Warrior] who in her own eyes does not deserve the [Knight] class, though her armament is a [Knight]'s. (Book 2, Ch2.00H L45)
- Saliss says the owner of the Noble's Fancy in Pallass is over Level 30 and an inn near First Landing is run by a Level 50 plus [Innkeeper]. (Book 15, Ch7.02 L560)

## Misreadings, theories and secrets about the system
- {belief} Ryoka reasons that Olesm and the others level from chess and Erin does not because there is no [Gamer] class, suggesting the system's designer or the culture had not yet adapted to casual play, and tables the idea as unconfirmed. (Book 2, Ch2.18 L63-65)
- {belief} A faerie tells Ryoka that having put the chains on willingly, her classes and levels make her a plaything, and that she does not know who plays this game. (Book 2, Ch2.28 L80-82)
- {belief} Ryoka thinks levels and classes are a way for the world or some system to control people. (Book 1, Ch1.09R L78)
- Klbkch asks Ryoka and Selys to keep the cumulative-level theory quiet until it is certain. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L570-573)
- {belief} Ryoka believes most people never hit a level cap, and the few who do mistake it for old age. (Book 2, Ch2.34 L477)
- Flos treats the rule that more classes mean a lower reachable level as a secret of rulers who reach high levels. (Book 5, Ch4.03K L249-251)
- Pisces says that classes are based on what we pursue and trivial things fail to trigger them in some people. (Book 1, Ch1.27 L139)
- Anand says that a high-level [Strategist] levelling from a game like chess would make him almost suspect an error in the way we level, and another speaker replies that this would mean an error in the world. (Book 6, Ch4.41L L60-61)
