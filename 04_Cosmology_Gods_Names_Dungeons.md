## The undead as a phenomenon
- A Crypt Lord is a dead creature animated by powerful magic that eats the dead to recover its body; its blood is black and poisonous. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L22-24)
- Crypt Lords can command other dead and are estimated around Level 22, possibly 26, able to wipe out a Silver-rank team. (Book 1, Ch1.01H L128-132)
- A Lich is simply the undead version of a spellcaster and is not immortal. (Book 2, Ch2.04 L61)
- Undead cannot grow or change unless a [Necromancer] alters them, or they can level. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L318)
- Pisces can create a stronger undead but not a higher level one. (Book 1, Ch1.33 L215)
- Pisces admits he made Toren as an undead that could level, an experimental prototype. (Book 3, Ch3.24 L331)
- Ruins near Liscor hold varied undead, and adventurers who vanished in them sometimes returned shuffling, trying to eat the living. (Book 1, Ch1.32 L7-9)
- If a horde kills an entire village the dead can become zombies, but one village's zombies regenerate fully, cannot be controlled by Necromancers and guard their home. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L149,L164,L167)
- Ghouls stream out of the zombie village and attack Rags's Goblins, one leaping at Rags and wounding her. (Book 4, Ch3.31G L168-171)
- Zevara says the Necromancer would have brought giants, wights and ghosts. (Book 1, Ch1.42 L99)
- Mage Grimalkin says he heard the basics of specter-type undead that even the current Archmages struggled against. (Book 12, Ch6.39 L201)
- Toren survives a life-draining spell by stealing mana from the zombies and ghouls he commands, which fall lifeless. (Book 5, Ch4.08T L12-18)

## Dungeons: what they are
- A magical dungeon has a source of mana and essentially never runs out of monsters. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L80,L83)
- The mana in a dungeon attracts nastier monsters and may make them stronger. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L86)
- Whatever produces the mana is usually deadly, so some dungeons are never fully cleared. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L86)
- Nearby cities hire adventurers and post guards to keep the monsters on the upper levels in check. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L86)
- Not all dungeons are magical; many just have magical things in them. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L80)
- The tombs under Liscor were like a non-magical dungeon despite the undead. (Book 2, Ch2.15 L88)
- Ulrien says adventurers classify dungeons as containment, future generations or vengeance. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L67)
- A containment dungeon is built to hold a threat or a valuable natural resource, such as one built only to fight Vampires with light-based traps. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L70)
- A future-generations dungeon tests entrants with traps so artifacts reach the deserving, perhaps with a map left behind. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L73)
- A vengeance dungeon seals its best artifacts behind the nastiest traps and monster eggs, so that a thousand Crelers crawl out when someone cracks the lid, says Jelaqua. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L76)
- Mages study which empire built a dungeon and its composition, while adventurers use the simpler system. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L67)
- Ulrien says Liscor's dungeon feels like it was not meant just to hide treasure, and Jelaqua finishes that it is a huge trap and calls it a vengeance dungeon. (Book 5, Ch4.19 L61-63)
- Pisces says a dungeon is a receptacle for the past, and even vengeance dungeons are made to hold a nation's greatest treasures. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L741)
- Dungeons come in many forms, including ruined castles, old mansions or lost cities, but Liscor's is a killing field meant to channel adventurers into traps. (Book 7, Ch5.15 L147)
- Pisces reports that Olesm claims even the worst dungeons hide some kind of treasure. (Book 8, Ch5.29 L739)
- Adventurers owe ten percent of their earnings to the guild, and treasure from dungeons is also taxed by the city if the dungeon is on its lands. (Book 9, Ch5.52 L284)
- A new dungeon can hold nothing if plundered in ages past, hold a massive fortune, or be the lair of a monster. (Book 10, Ch6.05D L304)
- Diggers note that a door found in a dungeon can transport people a hundred miles, so a new dungeon's finds could be worth a great deal. (Book 13, Ch6.48T L251)
- Jelaqua says she has seen cities in Baleros fall to a Silver-rank dungeon located ten miles away. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L583)
- Narration says that Liscor's dungeon is not an infinite beast, it has an end and a limit. (Book 8, Ch5.35H L674)
